# PMAY-U — Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Urban) 2.0

## Overview

PMAY-U ("Housing for All") was launched 25.06.2015. The original mission
ran to 31.03.2022 and was **extended to 31.12.2025 only for completion of
houses sanctioned up to 31.03.2022**; its CLSS vertical closed on
31.03.2022 [S1]. **PMAY-U 2.0** was approved by the Union Cabinet on
09.08.2024: financial assistance to **1 crore urban families** to
construct, purchase or rent a house over 5 years (2024–2029), through
four verticals — **Beneficiary-Led Construction (BLC)**, **Affordable
Housing in Partnership (AHP)**, **Affordable Rental Housing (ARH)** and
the **Interest Subsidy Scheme (ISS)** [S2][S4].

**Version note (recorded change):** PMAY-U 2.0 replaces the original
four verticals (ISSR, AHP, BLC, CLSS). CLSS is succeeded by ISS from
01.09.2024 [S1][S2][S3]. Historical details are in
[superseded history](#historical-superseded).

## Objective

To address the housing requirement of urban poor and middle-income
families (EWS/LIG/MIG) through construction, purchase and rental
pathways, in pursuance of "Housing for All".

## Target Beneficiaries

Families in urban areas belonging to EWS/LIG/MIG who **do not own a
pucca house anywhere in India**; a beneficiary family comprises husband,
wife, unmarried sons and/or unmarried daughters; an adult earning member
(irrespective of marital status) may be treated as a separate household
if they own no pucca house. Preference to persons with disabilities,
SC/ST/OBCs, minorities, transgender persons and women (overriding
priority to widows) [S1].

## Key Features

- 1 crore additional urban families targeted over 5 years (2024–29) [S2]
- Four verticals: BLC, AHP, ARH, ISS [S2][S4]
- ISS: loans up to ₹25 lakh, house value up to ₹35 lakh; **4% interest
  subsidy on the first ₹8 lakh** of loan for eligible EWS/LIG/MIG
  beneficiaries (loans sanctioned & disbursed on/after 01.09.2024) [S2][S3]
- Houses in the name of the female head or joint ownership preferred
- All statutory towns (Census 2011) + towns notified subsequently;
  notified planning/development authority areas [S1]
- Demand survey / application through State ULBs and the PMAY-U
  (pmaymis) portals [S4]

## Eligibility

Summary — see [eligibility.md](eligibility.md): urban family, no pucca
house anywhere in India, income-category fit, one benefit per family.

## Benefits

Central assistance / interest subsidy per vertical. Details:

## Documents

Aadhaar, income declaration/certificate, no-pucca-house declaration,
bank account. Details: [documents.md](documents.md).

## Application

Via States/UTs & ULB demand surveys, PMAY-U MIS applicant login, or PLI
(banks/HFCs) for ISS. Details: [application.md](application.md).

## Important Conditions

- One benefit under only one vertical of the Mission [S1]
- ISS conditions: loan ≤ ₹25 lakh, house value ≤ ₹35 lakh, minimum
  tenure 5 years, sanction/disbursal on/after 01.09.2024 [S3]
- State-specific implementation rules apply

## Exclusions

Owning a pucca house anywhere in India; already having availed a
government housing scheme benefit. Structured list:
[exclusions.md](exclusions.md).

## Historical (superseded)

Original PMAY-U (2015) verticals — for reference only; **the engine must
not apply these to new applications**:

- **ISSR**: ₹1 lakh per-house slum redevelopment grant (land as resource,
  private developer participation) [S1]
- **AHP (1.0)**: ₹1.5 lakh central assistance per EWS house in projects
  with ≥35% EWS houses / ≥250 houses [S1]
- **BLC (1.0)**: ₹1.5 lakh central assistance to eligible EWS families
  [S1]
- **CLSS**: interest subsidy 6.5% / 4% / 3% on loans up to ₹6/9/12 lakh
  for EWS-LIG / MIG-I / MIG-II; MIG window 01.01.2017–31.03.2021; CLSS
  closed 31.03.2022 [S1]
