# NMMSS — National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme

## Overview

NMMSS (centrally sponsored, since 2008) awards **₹12,000 per annum
(₹1,000/month)** to meritorious students of **government and
government-aided/local-body schools** from families with parental
income up to **₹3.5 lakh/annum**, from Class 9 through Class 12
(₹12,000/year for the 9–12 span per current guidelines; earlier ₹6,000
rate recorded historically) [S1][S2]. Entry is via the state-conducted
NMMSS examination (MAT + SAT) after Class 8, with ≥55% marks in Class
VIII (relaxations for SC/ST/PwD) [S1]. One lakh fresh scholarships per
year [S2].

## Objective

To arrest the dropout rate after Class 8 and encourage meritorious
students from economically weaker sections to continue schooling to
Class 12.

## Target Beneficiaries

Class 9 entrants from government/aided/local-body schools, in the top
of the state-conducted exam, family income ≤ ₹3.5 lakh [S1].

## Key Features

- ₹12,000/year (₹1,000/month) — 4 years (Class 9–12) [S1][S2]
- Entry exam (MAT + SAT) conducted by States/UTs [S1]
- ≥55% in Class VIII to sit the exam (SC/ST/PwD relaxation per norms)
  [S1]
- Income ceiling ₹3.5 lakh/annum (parental, all sources) [S1]
- Scholarship continues to Class 12 subject to promotion and conduct
- Application on NSP during the notified window; State exam calendar
  precedes

## Application

Via [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in/) after state exam

## Important Conditions

- Scholarship is **for government/aided/local-body school students
  only** (private-school students are ineligible) [S1]
- Minimum 55% marks required for continuation into Class 10–12 (with
  relaxations) [S1]
- Selection = exam merit within the state's quota

## Exclusions

Private-school students; income above ₹3.5L; below-threshold marks;
Kendriya/Navodaya-type residential school students (per scheme norms).
