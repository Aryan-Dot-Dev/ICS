## Process

1. **Register** — on [pmvishwakarma.gov.in](https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in/)
   or at a CSC with Aadhaar e-KYC; choose the trade [S1].
2. **Verification** — district teams/CSC verify trade and self-
   employment attestation [S1].
3. **Recognition** — certificate + ID card issued [S1].
4. **Training** — basic skill training (stipend ₹500/day); advanced
   training later phases [S1].
5. **Toolkit** — ₹15,000 e-voucher at training start [S1].
6. **Loan tranche 1** — ₹1 lakh collateral-free at 5% beneficiary
   interest via participating banks [S1].
7. **Loan tranche 2** — ₹2 lakh after tranche-1 repayment [S1].
8. **Digital + marketing** — transaction incentives and marketplace
   linkage [S1].

## Verification & approval

- District-level committees verify trades; banks appraise and sanction
  the enterprise loans; MoMSME monitors via the portal.

## Deadlines

- None — rolling enrolment; benefit sequence timelines per guidelines.
