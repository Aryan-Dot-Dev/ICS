# PMEGP — Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme

## Overview

PMEGP is a central sector credit-linked subsidy scheme of MoMSME,
implemented nationally by KVIC (with KVIBs and District Industries
Centres). It supports **new micro-enterprises** in manufacturing and
services via bank credit with a **margin-money subsidy (MMS)** of
15%–35% of project cost, depending on location (urban/rural) and
applicant category. Maximum admissible project costs for MMS:
**₹50 lakh (manufacturing)** and **₹20 lakh (service)** [S1][S3].

## Objective

To generate sustainable self-employment through micro-enterprises in
rural and urban areas and to create employment for traditional artisans
and unemployed youth.

## Target Beneficiaries

Individuals above 18 years setting up new micro-enterprises (single
unit), including self-help groups, trusts and co-operative societies
(producing goods, not regulatory-staffed). Education-linked conditions
apply only to larger service-sector projects.

Concept links: [entrepreneur](../../concepts/entrepreneur.md) ·
[low-income-household](../../concepts/low-income-household.md)

## Key Features

- New units only; one unit per beneficiary; only one activity per unit
- Project cost caps for MMS: ₹50 lakh manufacturing, ₹20 lakh service [S3]
- Margin-money subsidy 15–35% of project cost by category/location [S1][S3]
- Balance as bank credit; beneficiary share 5–10%
- Online application and tracking via the PMEGP e-Portal [S1]
- Compulsory Entrepreneurship Development Programme (EDP) training after sanction (2/3/6-day by project size) — per KVIC norms
- Scheme administered by KVIC at national level with KVIB/DIC

## Eligibility

Summary — see [eligibility.md](eligibility.md): 18+; no formal education
required for projects up to ₹10 lakh (service) / ₹25 lakh (manufacturing);
VIII-pass for larger service projects, X-pass for larger manufacturing
projects; unit must be new. Exclusions (per guidelines): income-tax
payers; beneficiaries of other similar government subsidy schemes
(central/state) in the past; units already availed government subsidy.

## Benefits

Margin-money subsidy (15–35%) routed as credit-linked back-ended
subsidy; remainder bank credit. Details: [benefits.md](benefits.md).

## Documents

Aadhaar/PAN, project details, special-category certificates, education
proof where applicable, bank account. Details: [documents.md](documents.md).

## Application

Fully online on the PMEGP e-Portal; list prepared by KVIC/KVIB/DIC;
banks appraise and sanction; MMS credited to loan account after unit
setup. Details: [application.md](application.md).

## Important Conditions

- Subsidy is **back-ended**: credited to the borrower's loan account as
  margin money after the unit is established and (per guidelines)
  after EDP training
- Sanction is a bank credit decision; PMEGP only subsidises
- Non-farm activity: farm-sector projects are outside PMEGP

## Exclusions

Income-tax payers, earlier subsidy beneficiaries under similar schemes,
existing units/institutions running already, regulatory staffed
institutions. Structured list: [exclusions.md](exclusions.md).
