# Concept: Woman

## Definition

Women as a beneficiary class, with scheme-specific age and status
boundaries: adult women (18+) for PMUY and Stand-Up India; women with
eligible girl children for SSY; pregnant and lactating mothers for PMMVY;
widows (40–79) for NSAP-IGNWPS.

## Schemes linked to this concept

- [PM Ujjwala Yojana](../schemes/pmuy/scheme.md) — adult woman (18+) of an eligible poor household; no other LPG connection in household
- [Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana](../schemes/ssy/scheme.md) — parent/legal guardian opens for a girl child below 10 (maximum two girls, exceptions per rules)
- [Stand-Up India](../schemes/stand-up-india/scheme.md) — women entrepreneurs (any-of with SC/ST)
- [PMMVY](../schemes/pmmvy/scheme.md) — pregnant women and lactating mothers (19+ for first-living-child benefit)
- [NSAP–IGNWPS](../schemes/nsap/scheme.md) — BPL widows aged 40–79

## Engine notes

Gender is a hard rule only where the scheme names women (PMUY, PMMVY,
SSY, IGNWPS). In Stand-Up India it is an OR branch. See
[gender](../rules/gender.md).
