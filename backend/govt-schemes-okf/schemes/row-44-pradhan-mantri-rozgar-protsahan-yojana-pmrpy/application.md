## Process

1. Employer registers new employees with EPFO and/or ESIC and obtains their Universal Account Number (UAN).
2. Employer ensures the employee's monthly wage is below INR 15,000.
3. Employer submits the details of such new employees through the EPFO employer portal.
4. EPFO verifies the eligibility and processes the government's share of EPF and ESI contributions.
5. The government remits the employer's contribution directly to EPFO and ESIC accounts.

## Deadlines

- Scheme was applicable for employees registered up to 31 March 2019; benefits provided for three years from date of registration. No longer accepting new registrations.
