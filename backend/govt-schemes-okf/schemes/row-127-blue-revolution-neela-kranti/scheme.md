# Blue Revolution – Neela Kranti

## Overview

The Blue Revolution – Neela Kranti scheme, launched in 2015-16 and running until 2019-20, was a centrally sponsored initiative by the Department of Fisheries, Government of India, with a central outlay of ₹3,000 crore that helped mobilize a total investment of ₹5,000 crore. It laid the foundation for integrated fisheries development by focusing on increasing fish production and productivity through technological upgradation, expansion of aquaculture, and development of fisheries infrastructure. The scheme aimed to enhance the socio-economic conditions of fishers and fish farmers while promoting sustainable and responsible utilization of fisheries resources.

## Objective

- Increase fish production and productivity through technological upgradation and expansion of aquaculture
- Enhance the socio-economic conditions of fishers and fish farmers
- Promote sustainable and responsible utilization of fisheries resources
- Develop fisheries infrastructure including fishing harbours, fish landing centres, and aquaculture facilities
- Strengthen the fisheries value chain through post-harvest infrastructure and market linkages
- Encourage private investment and entrepreneurship in the fisheries sector
- Improve fish quality and safety through better handling and processing practices
- Generate employment opportunities in the fisheries and aquaculture sectors

## Important Conditions

- The scheme was limited to the period 2015-16 to 2019-20 and is no longer accepting new applications.
- Funding was subject to availability of budget and approval of project proposals by the Department of Fisheries.
- Beneficiaries had to adhere to scheme-specific guidelines and submit utilization certificates for fund release.
- Projects had to be completed within the stipulated timeline;
- delays could affect fund disbursement.
- The scheme has been subsumed under the Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY) launched in 2020-21.
