# Maharashtra State Innovation Society

## Overview

Maharashtra State Innovation Society (MSInS) is a nodal agency established by the Government of Maharashtra to promote innovation, entrepreneurship, and startup ecosystem development across the state. It operates under the Directorate of Industries and implements various schemes to support startups, MSMEs, and innovators through funding, mentorship, infrastructure, and policy advocacy. The society aims to position Maharashtra as a leading innovation hub in India by fostering collaboration between academia, industry, and government.

## Objective

- Promote innovation and entrepreneurship across Maharashtra
- Support startups and MSMEs through funding, mentorship, and infrastructure
- Strengthen industry-academia collaboration for technology transfer
- Facilitate access to markets and investment opportunities for innovators
- Develop sector-specific innovation clusters and incubation centers
- Advocate for policy reforms to improve ease of doing business for startups

## Eligibility

Eligible beneficiaries include startups registered in Maharashtra, MSMEs, individual innovators, academic institutions, and industry partners engaged in innovative activities. Specific eligibility criteria vary by scheme and are defined under individual programs implemented by MSInS.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Benefits include financial support through grants and seed funding, access to incubation infrastructure, mentorship from industry experts, assistance in intellectual property rights (IPR) filing, market linkage support, participation in investor meets and demo days, and facilitation of partnerships with corporates and government bodies.
