## Process

1. Identify eligible electric or hybrid vehicle models listed under FAME India;
2. Purchase the vehicle from a dealer registered with the FAME scheme;
3. The dealer uploads sale details and documents to the FAME portal;
4. The Department of Heavy Industry (DHI) verifies the claim;
5. Subsidy amount is transferred directly to the dealer, who passes on the benefit to the consumer as a discount.

## Deadlines

- FAME II scheme is operational from April 1, 2019, to March 31, 2024, or until exhaustion of funds, whichever is earlier; applications are accepted on an ongoing basis during this period.
