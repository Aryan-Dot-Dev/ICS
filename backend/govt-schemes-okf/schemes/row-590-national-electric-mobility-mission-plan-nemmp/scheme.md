# National Electric Mobility Mission Plan (NEMMP)

## Overview

The National Electric Mobility Mission Plan (NEMMP) 2020 is a comprehensive initiative launched by the Government of India to promote electric and hybrid vehicles, reduce dependence on fossil fuels, and address environmental concerns related to vehicular emissions. It aims to achieve national fuel security by encouraging the adoption of electric vehicles (EVs) and developing indigenous manufacturing capabilities for EV components and batteries. The plan sets a target of 6-7 million sales of hybrid and electric vehicles year on year from 2020 onwards and includes incentives for both manufacturers and consumers.

## Objective

- Achieve national fuel security by promoting electric and hybrid vehicles
- Reduce the adverse impact of vehicular emissions on the environment
- Grow domestic manufacturing capabilities across the entire EV value chain
- Establish India as a global hub for electric vehicle manufacturing
- Incentivize the adoption of 6-7 million hybrid and electric vehicles annually from 2020
- Develop charging infrastructure and battery swapping facilities
- Support research and development in battery technology and power electronics
- Create a favorable policy and regulatory ecosystem for EV adoption


## Target Beneficiaries

**manufacturers**, **consumers**, **fleet operators**, **public transport agencies**


## Key Features

- Fund size: ₹10000 crore
- Grant/assistance per entity: Up to 50% of vehicle cost for electric buses, 40% for other EVs, capped at INR 20 lakhs for cars and proportionally lower for two/three-wheelers
- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: FAME II scheme is operational from April 1, 2019, to March 31, 2024, or until exhaustion of funds, whichever is earlier; applications are accepted on an ongoing basis during this period.
