## Process

1. Submit an initial inquiry or proposal through GIZ’s partner channels or DEG’s India office.
2. Undergo preliminary screening based on eligibility and innovation criteria.
3. Submit detailed business plan, financial projections, and impact metrics.
4. Participate in due diligence meetings with GIZ and DEG representatives.
5. Receive term sheet and negotiate financing terms.
6. Sign financing agreement and fulfill pre-disbursement conditions.
7. Receive funds in tranches upon milestone verification.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted throughout the year based on fund availability and deal flow.
