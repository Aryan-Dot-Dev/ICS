# GIZ – DEG India Startup Finance

## Overview

GIZ – DEG India Startup Finance is a financing initiative by GIZ in partnership with DEG (Deutsche Investitions- und Entwicklungsgesellschaft) aimed at supporting startups in India through financial instruments. The scheme provides funding to Indian startups to scale their operations and foster innovation, leveraging GIZ’s expertise in international development and DEG’s experience in private sector financing in emerging markets.

## Objective

- Support Indian startups with access to finance for scaling operations
- Promote innovation and entrepreneurship in India
- Strengthen the startup ecosystem through strategic financial partnerships
- Enable sustainable business growth in emerging sectors
- Facilitate job creation and economic development via startup support

## Eligibility

Indian startups that are legally registered entities, demonstrate innovative business models, have potential for scalability and impact, and operate in sectors aligned with sustainable development goals. Preference may be given to startups with social impact or green technology focus.

Access to growth capital, technical assistance, mentorship, networking opportunities with investors and industry experts, and support for market expansion. The financing is structured to support long-term sustainability and impact measurement.
