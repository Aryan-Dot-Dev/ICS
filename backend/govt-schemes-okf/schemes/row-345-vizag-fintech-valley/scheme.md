# Vizag Fintech Valley

## Overview

Vizag Fintech Valley is a state-level initiative in Andhra Pradesh aimed at establishing Visakhapatnam as a leading fintech hub by fostering innovation, supporting fintech startups, and creating a conducive ecosystem for financial technology development through infrastructure, mentorship, and industry collaboration.

## Objective

- Establish Visakhapatnam as a premier fintech destination in India
- Provide infrastructure and support services to fintech startups
- Foster collaboration between industry, academia, and government
- Promote innovation in financial technologies including payments, lending, insurtech, and wealthtech
- Create employment opportunities in the fintech sector
- Attract investment and talent to the region


## Target Beneficiaries

**fintech startups**, **entrepreneurs**, **MSMEs in fintech**

- Geographic scope: Andhra Pradesh (Visakhapatnam region) (state-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year.
- Portal: https://fintechvalley.in
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

Fintech startups and companies operating in financial technology domains such as digital payments, lending, blockchain, insurtech, regtech, and wealthtech that are registered entities and willing to operate from or establish presence in Visakhapatnam.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Access to plug-and-play office infrastructure, high-speed internet, mentorship from industry experts, networking opportunities with investors and corporates, regulatory guidance, participation in fintech events and hackathons, and support for proof-of-concept development.
