# PM Mega Integrated Textile Region and Apparel (PM MITRA)

## Overview

PM Mega Integrated Textile Region and Apparel (PM MITRA) is a scheme launched by the Ministry of Textiles to develop world-class textile manufacturing infrastructure through the establishment of integrated textile parks. The scheme aims to strengthen the textile value chain from fibre to fashion by creating plug-and-play facilities and attracting investment in the sector. It focuses on reducing logistics costs, improving competitiveness, and generating employment in the textiles and apparel industry.

## Objective

- Develop integrated textile manufacturing parks with world-class infrastructure
- Attract investment in the textiles and apparel value chain
- Reduce logistics costs and improve competitiveness of domestic textile industry
- Generate employment opportunities in the textile sector
- Promote sustainable and eco-friendly manufacturing practices
- Strengthen backward and forward linkages in the textile value chain
- Enhance export potential of Indian textiles and apparel

## Eligibility

Entities interested in setting up textile manufacturing units within PM MITRA parks, including domestic and foreign investors, MSMEs, large enterprises, and joint ventures, subject to compliance with park regulations and allotment criteria defined by the Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) managing each park.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Plug-and-play infrastructure with ready-to-use factory buildings, common facilities (testing labs, design centres, logistics hubs), power, water, and effluent treatment systems; single-window clearances; fiscal incentives as per state policies; skill development centres; and logistic support for domestic and export-oriented units.
