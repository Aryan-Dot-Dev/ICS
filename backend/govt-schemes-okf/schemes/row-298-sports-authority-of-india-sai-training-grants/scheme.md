# Sports Authority of India (SAI) Training Grants

## Overview

The Sports Authority of India (SAI) Training Grants scheme provides financial assistance to athletes, coaches, and sports institutions for training, equipment, and competition participation. It aims to support the development of sporting talent across various disciplines and levels, from grassroots to elite. The scheme is implemented by SAI under the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports to enhance India's performance in national and international sports events.

## Objective

- Provide financial support for training and development of athletes
- Assist in procurement of sports equipment and kit
- Support participation in national and international competitions
- Enhance coaching standards through training and development programs
- Promote sports excellence at sub-junior, junior, and senior levels


## Target Beneficiaries

**athletes**, **coaches**, **sports institutions**


## Key Features

- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — applications can be submitted throughout the year based on training cycles and competition schedules.
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

Eligible beneficiaries include individual athletes, coaches, and sports institutions recognized by SAI or affiliated with National Sports Federations (NSFs). Athletes must be registered with SAI or recommended by NSFs. Coaches must possess recognized qualifications. Institutions must be SAI-accredited training centers or recognized sports academies.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial assistance for training expenses, sports equipment, competition travel, coaching fees, and stipends. Non-financial benefits include access to SAI facilities, expert coaching, sports science support, and exposure to national/international competitions.

## Important Conditions

- Grants are subject to availability of funds and SAI's annual budget allocation
- Beneficiaries must submit utilization certificates and progress reports
- Support may be withdrawn for non-compliance with training plans or false information
- Priority is given to Olympic, Paralympic, Asian Games, and Commonwealth Games disciplines
