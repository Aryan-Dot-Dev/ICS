## Process

1. Identify the appropriate SAI training grant scheme based on athlete level and sport.
2. Obtain recommendation from the respective National Sports Federation (NSF) or SAI regional center.
3. Submit application along with required documents to the concerned SAI office.
4. Application is reviewed by SAI's expert committee.
5. Upon approval, sanction letter is issued.
6. Funds are released in installments as per the approved training plan and utilization certificates.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications can be submitted throughout the year based on training cycles and competition schedules.
