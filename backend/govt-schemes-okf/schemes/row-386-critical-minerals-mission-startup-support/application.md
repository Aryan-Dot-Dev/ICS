## Process

1. Submit project proposal under the S&T-PRISM programme of the Ministry of Mines.
2. Proposal is reviewed by expert committees for technical feasibility and innovation potential.
3. Shortlisted applicants are invited for presentation or interview.
4. Final selection is made based on evaluation criteria.
5. Approved projects receive grant sanction and fund release.
6. Progress is monitored through periodic reports and site visits.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round as per announcements under S&T-PRISM.
