# Critical Minerals Mission – Startup Support

## Overview

The Critical Minerals Mission – Startup Support is a component of the Ministry of Mines' Science and Technology Programme, specifically under the Promotion of Research and Innovation in Startups and MSMEs in Mining, Mineral Processing, Metallurgy and Recycling Sector (S&T-PRISM). It aims to provide seed support to deserving startups and individual innovators in the critical minerals value chain, including exploration, extraction, processing, and recycling. The scheme is implemented through the release of funds to startups, MSMEs, and individual innovators to ensure timely availability of financial support for innovation in the critical minerals sector.

## Objective

- Provide seed support to deserving startups in the critical minerals sector
- Support innovation in mining, mineral processing, metallurgy, and recycling
- Ensure timely availability of financial support for individual innovators
- Promote research and development in critical minerals and energy critical elements
- Strengthen domestic capacity in critical minerals supply chain
- Encourage private sector participation in critical minerals exploration and processing

## Eligibility

Startups, MSMEs, and individual innovators engaged in mining, mineral processing, metallurgy, and recycling sector, particularly those working on critical minerals and energy critical elements, are eligible for support under the S&T-PRISM programme of the Ministry of Mines.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial support in the form of grants for research and development projects, prototype development, technology validation, and market linkage. Non-financial benefits include technical guidance, access to Ministry of Mines' scientific institutions, networking opportunities with industry and academia, and facilitation of collaborations for scaling innovations.

## Important Conditions

- Funding is subject to availability of budget under the S&T-PRISM programme
- Projects must align with national priorities in critical minerals and energy critical elements
- Grantees must submit periodic progress reports and utilization certificates
- Intellectual property generated may be subject to government-use rights
- Support is limited to seed-stage and early validation phases
