# PM Matsya Kisan Samridhi Sah-Yojana (PM-MKSSY)

## Overview

Pradhan Mantri Matsya Kisan Samridhi Sah-Yojana (PM-MKSSY) is a central sector sub-scheme under the Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY) launched in 2023-24 with a total outlay of Rs. 6000 crore, supported by the World Bank and Agence Française de Développement (AFD). It aims to address inherent weaknesses in the fisheries sector through financial and technical interventions to enhance long-term sustainability and resilience by facilitating sectoral reforms. The scheme will be implemented from FY 2023-24 to FY 2026-27 across all states and union territories.

## Objective

- Address inherent weaknesses in the fisheries sector through financial and technical interventions
- Enhance long-term sustainability and resilience of the fisheries sector
- Facilitate sectoral reforms for improved fisheries management
- Promote private sector participation and entrepreneurship in fisheries
- Support development of fisheries value chain including infrastructure and technology adoption
- Strengthen socio-economic conditions of fishers and fish farmers
- Enhance fish production and productivity through modern aquaculture practices
- Improve post-harvest management and reduce losses
