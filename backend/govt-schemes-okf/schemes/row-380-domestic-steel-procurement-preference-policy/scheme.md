# Domestic Steel Procurement Preference Policy

## Overview

The Domestic Steel Procurement Preference Policy (DMI&SP) mandates that government procurement of iron and steel products with a minimum value addition of 15%-50% must prioritize domestically manufactured products. The policy applies to all ministries and government departments and their agencies under administrative control. It covers iron and steel products with an estimated value of ₹25 crore or more, with exemptions allowed when domestic production of a specific steel grade is unavailable or insufficient to meet project demand.

## Objective

- Promote domestically manufactured iron and steel products in government procurement
- Increase value addition in domestic steel production
- Reduce reliance on imported steel in government projects
- Support self-sufficiency in steel production
- Encourage adequate capacity additions by private manufacturers and CPSEs
- Provide policy support and guidance to private manufacturers, MSME steel producers, and CPSEs
