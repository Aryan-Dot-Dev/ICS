## Process

1. Identify if the procurement falls under DMI&SP (iron and steel products with ≥15% value addition and estimated value ≥ ₹25 crore).
2. Check if domestic production of the required steel grade is available and sufficient to meet project demand.
3. If yes, give preference to domestically manufactured iron and steel products in the tender.
4. If domestic supply is inadequate or unavailable, apply for exemption as per policy provisions.
5. Submit procurement details through the respective ministry/department's procurement portal.
6. Maintain records of compliance for audit and monitoring purposes.

## Deadlines

- Based on individual procurement cycles of ministries and departments; no centralized deadline.
