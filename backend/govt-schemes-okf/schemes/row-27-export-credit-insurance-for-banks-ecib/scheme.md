# Export Credit Insurance for Banks (ECIB)

## Overview

Export Credit Insurance for Banks (ECIB) is a scheme by ECGC Limited to provide credit insurance to banks against risks in export financing. It supports Indian exporters by enabling banks to offer pre-shipment and post-shipment credit with reduced risk. The scheme enhances export competitiveness by facilitating access to working capital and term loans for export-oriented businesses.

## Objective

- Provide credit insurance cover to banks for export-related lending
- Mitigate risks of non-payment by overseas buyers in export transactions
- Encourage banks to extend more credit to exporters, especially MSMEs
- Support pre-shipment and post-shipment export finance
- Strengthen the export credit ecosystem in India
- Promote timely realization of export proceeds
- Reduce financial stress on exporters due to buyer default
- Enhance confidence in cross-border trade financing

## Eligibility

Banks and financial institutions in India that extend pre-shipment and post-shipment credit to exporters are eligible to avail insurance cover under the ECIB scheme. The exporter must be an Indian entity engaged in export of goods or services, and the transaction must involve an overseas buyer. The credit facility must be for a bonafide export transaction as per RBI guidelines.

## Benefits

Banks receive insurance cover against commercial and political risks of non-payment by overseas buyers. This enables them to lend more confidently to exporters, especially MSMEs, at competitive rates. Exporters benefit from easier access to working capital and term loans. - Claims are payable only after exhaustive recovery efforts by the bank

- Pre-existing debts or rollovers may not be eligible
