# National Horticulture Board (NHB) Schemes

## Overview

The National Horticulture Board (NHB) implements the Cluster Development Programme (CDP) to leverage geographical specialization of horticulture clusters and make them globally competitive. The scheme aims to facilitate suitable economies of scale in production and post-harvest activities, improve price realization, and empower farming communities by enhancing income through increased competitiveness across the value chain. It operates in challenge mode where interested parties identify clusters, demonstrate feasibility, and submit proposals for development of existing or induced clusters. The programme supports multi-commodity high-value clusters and peri-urban vegetable clusters under revised CDP guidelines.

## Objective

- Address the concerns of the horticulture cluster value chain from pre-production, production, post-harvest management and value addition to logistics, marketing and branding in an integrated manner.
- Facilitate the introduction of innovative technologies and practices to help enhance the global competitiveness of focus cluster crops and thereby promote exports.
- Enhance farmers’ wallet share (reduction in cost and increase in revenue).
