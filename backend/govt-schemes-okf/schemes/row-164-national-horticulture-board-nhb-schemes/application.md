## Process

1. Submit concept note in response to Expression of Interest (EoI) via online portal at https://cdp.nhb.gov.in.
2. Await responsiveness check and technical evaluation of concept notes received on or before the cut-off date.
3. If shortlisted, submit detailed business plan/ Detailed Project Report (DPR) within six weeks.
4. Post evaluation of the business case/DPR, projects will be awarded to the IA and contract will be signed between NHB and IA.
5. No deadline date extensions will be provided for DPR submission.

## Deadlines

- Last date for submission of application is 31st July 2026 (6:00 PM, Friday). Last date for submission of Detailed Project Reports (DPRs) by shortlisted applicants is 17th April 2026 (06:00 PM).
