# Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana (PMSSY)

## Overview

Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana (PMSSY) is a centrally sponsored scheme launched by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare to correct regional imbalances in the availability of affordable and reliable tertiary healthcare services and to augment facilities for quality medical education in the country. The scheme involves setting up new AIIMS-like institutions and upgrading existing government medical colleges and institutions.

## Objective

- Correct regional imbalances in availability of affordable and reliable tertiary healthcare services
- Augment facilities for quality medical education in the country
- Set up new AIIMS-like institutions
- Upgrade existing government medical colleges and institutions


## Target Beneficiaries

**general public**, **medical students**, **healthcare professionals**

- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: Not applicable — benefits are accessed through ongoing hospital services and academic admissions at the respective institutions.
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

The scheme is implemented through the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. Eligibility for benefits under PMSSY is generally for patients seeking treatment at the upgraded or newly established institutions, and for medical education opportunities at these facilities. Specific eligibility criteria for admission to courses or treatment are determined by the respective institutions.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Benefits include access to affordable tertiary healthcare services, improved medical education infrastructure, increased availability of specialist doctors, and enhanced healthcare delivery in underserved regions through the establishment of new AIIMS and upgradation of medical colleges.
