# National Highway Logistics Facilities – NHLF

## Overview

National Highway Logistics Facilities (NHLF) is a scheme under the National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) aimed at developing logistics infrastructure along national highways to improve freight movement and supply chain efficiency. The scheme focuses on creating multimodal logistics parks, warehousing, and related facilities to reduce logistics costs and enhance connectivity. It supports the broader goal of integrating highways with logistics networks under initiatives like Bharatmala and Sagarmala.

## Objective

- Develop multimodal logistics parks along national highways
- Reduce logistics costs and improve supply chain efficiency
- Enhance freight movement through integrated infrastructure
- Promote public-private partnerships in logistics infrastructure
- Support Make in India and export competitiveness
- Develop warehousing, cold storage, and value-added services
- Improve first and last-mile connectivity for industries
- Facilitate seamless intermodal transfer of cargo


## Target Beneficiaries

**logistics companies**, **MSME**, **warehouse operators**, **freight forwarders**, **transport operators**

## Eligibility

Eligible beneficiaries include companies, consortiums, or special purpose vehicles (SPVs) registered under the Companies Act, 2013 or LLP Act, 2008, with proven experience in logistics, warehousing, or infrastructure development, and meeting financial and technical criteria as specified in the tender documents issued by NHAI for each project.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Access to developed logistics parks with plug-and-play infrastructure, long-term land leases on NHAI land, connectivity to national highways and expressways, proximity to industrial clusters and ports, facilitation of customs clearance and EXIM procedures, availability of plug-and-play utilities, and opportunities for value-added services like packaging, labeling, and cold storage.

- Development must comply with NHAI guidelines and concession agreement
- Land use is restricted to logistics and allied activities
- Failure to meet project milestones may lead to penalties or termination
- Clearances from environmental, civil aviation, or other authorities may be required
