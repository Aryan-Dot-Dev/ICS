## Process

1. Identify eligible trainees meeting attendance and eligibility criteria.
2. Enroll trainees in NSQF-approved hospitality courses under HSRT.
3. Conduct training with minimum 6 hours daily for five/six days a week.
4. Maintain attendance records and ensure minimum 80% attendance for eligibility to appear in final assessment.
5. Conduct internal and external assessment by independent examiners.
6. Award certification to successful trainees.
7. Track trainees for one year post-certification for career progression and retention.
8. Submit utilization reports and progress reports to the Ministry of Tourism.
9. Ensure Aadhaar and bank account linkage for stipend transfer via PFMS.
10. Upload video clippings on 10th and concluding day of training to the Programme Monitoring Unit (Skill Development) at pmuhsrt@gmail.com.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted quarterly (upto 31st March, 30th June, 30th September, and 31st December every year) for empanelment of training providers; training batches are conducted as per institutional capacity and prior permission from the Ministry.
