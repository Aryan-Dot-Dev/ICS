# Hunar Se Rozgar Tak (HSRT) Scheme

## Overview

The Hunar Se Rozgar Tak (HSRT) Scheme is a skill development initiative by the Ministry of Tourism aimed at providing hospitality training to youth from economically weaker sections to enhance employability in the tourism sector. It offers short-term courses in various hospitality trades such as Multi Cuisine Cook, Food & Beverage Service Steward, Room Attendant, Front Office Associate, and others, aligned with the National Skill Qualification Framework (NSQF). The scheme focuses on wage and self-employment outcomes, with stipends, certification, and placement support provided to trainees.

## Objective

- To provide skill development training in hospitality trades to enhance employability
- To target persons with limited means who need skills facilitative to employment
- To ensure at least 70% of successfully certified trainees are employed (wage or self) within three months of training completion
- To promote wage employment with remuneration at least equal to minimum wages
- To support self-employment through livelihood enhancement and enterprise development
- To up-skill persons already engaged in hospitality occupations with at least 3% remuneration increase within 14 months
- To integrate soft skills like computer literacy, language, and workplace interpersonal skills into training
- To standardize training implementation across providers using Common Norms of the Ministry of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship

## Important Conditions

- Stipend is payable only to trainees with minimum 80% attendance
- Assessment and certification require one internal and one external examiner from relevant industry
- Training providers must not use the same entity as both trainer and assessor to avoid conflict of interest
- Funding is released based on outcomes achieved, not mere enrollment
- Private training providers must undergo inspection and meet infrastructure requirements for at least two HSRT courses
- Security deposit of Rs.20,000/- is required for new applicants seeking empanelment as training providers
- Blacklisted entities from MSDE, NSDA, or MoT are ineligible to apply
- Re-inspection fee of Rs.10,000/- applies if rejected due to lack of infrastructure
