# RBI Regulatory Sandbox for Fintech

## Overview

The RBI Regulatory Sandbox for Fintech is a framework that allows fintech innovators to test their products and services in a controlled environment with regulatory relaxations. It aims to foster innovation in the financial sector while ensuring consumer protection and systemic stability. The sandbox enables live testing of innovative financial technologies under RBI supervision.

## Objective

- To foster responsible innovation in fintech
- To enable live testing of innovative financial products and services
- To assess the benefits and risks of new technologies
- To determine if regulatory changes are needed to support innovation
- To provide a sandbox participants access to regulatory guidance and support

**fintech startups**, **financial technology companies**

## Eligibility

Entities must be incorporated in India and have a net worth of at least INR 25 lakh as per the last audited balance sheet. They must demonstrate a genuine need for testing in the sandbox and have a clear exit strategy. The product or service must be innovative and not already widely available in the market.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Regulatory relaxations specific to the tested product/service, access to RBI guidance during the testing period, permission to test with real customers in a controlled environment, and potential pathway to broader regulatory approval upon successful completion.

## Important Conditions

- Participation does not guarantee regulatory approval for full-scale launch
- The sandbox is not a source of funding or investment
- Regulatory relaxations are limited to the specific product/service under test
- Participants must comply with all other applicable laws and regulations
- RBI may terminate participation if risks to consumers or systemic stability are identified
