# Deen Dayal Antyodaya Yojana - Urban (DAY-NULM)

## Overview

Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana - National Urban Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NULM) is a centrally sponsored scheme launched by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs to reduce poverty and vulnerability of urban poor households by enabling access to gainful self-employment and skilled wage employment opportunities. It aims to build strong grassroots-level institutions of the poor, provide shelter to the urban homeless, support urban street vendors, and promote financial inclusion and skill development. The mission operates through a three-tier structure at national, state, and city levels, with implementation led by Urban Local Bodies (ULBs).

## Objective

- Reduce poverty and vulnerability of urban poor households by enabling access to gainful self-employment and skilled wage employment opportunities
- Build strong grassroots level institutions of the poor through social mobilisation and institution development
- Provide shelter equipped with essential services to the urban homeless in a phased manner
- Facilitate access to suitable spaces, institutional credit, social security, and skills for urban street vendors
- Ensure availability and access to permanent 24-hour shelters for urban homeless populations
- Cater to vulnerable segments of urban homeless such as dependent children, aged, disabled, mentally ill, and recovering patients
- Establish rights-based linkages with other programmes for entitlements like food, healthcare, education, social security pensions, PDS, ICDS, financial inclusion, and affordable housing
- Promote convergence with schemes of relevant line Ministries/Departments and state governments dealing with skills, livelihoods, entrepreneurship development, health, education, and social assistance
- Encourage partnership with private sector for skill training, employment, and operation of shelters for homeless
- Support formation and strengthening of Self Help Groups (SHGs) and their federations at area and city levels

## Eligibility

The primary target of DAY-NULM is the urban poor, including the urban homeless. As an interim measure, the target is the urban population identified as below poverty line by States/UTs. Coverage may be broadened to include families of disadvantaged groups like SCs, STs, women, minorities, disabled, etc., subject to a maximum of 25 percent of the urban poor population. ## Important Conditions

- Interest subsidy under SEP is provided only in case of timely repayment of loan
- SEP-G requires minimum 5 members with at least 70% from urban poor families and no more than one person from the same family in the group
- SEP-I requires applicant to be at least 18 years of age
- Project cost for SEP-I is capped at Rs. 2 lakhs and for SEP-G at Rs. 10 lakhs
- No collateral required for bank loans up to Rs. 10 lakhs under SEP, but assets created are hypothecated
- Credit guarantee cover under SEP is available through CGTMSE only if the activity is eligible
- Under PMFME convergence, seed capital loan interest must not exceed 6% per annum, with 3% retained by SHG for loan management
- Loan tenure for PMFME seed capital is preferably 18-36 months
- SHG members must have at least one year of experience in micro food processing to be eligible for PMFME seed capital
- FSSAI license and GST registration are encouraged but not mandatory at time of application for PMFME seed capital
- Funds under PMFME seed capital are disbursed as a loan from ALF/CLF to SHG members, not as a grant
- Repayment of PMFME seed capital loan is required, with EMI schedule to be calculated by ROs/COs/CRPs
