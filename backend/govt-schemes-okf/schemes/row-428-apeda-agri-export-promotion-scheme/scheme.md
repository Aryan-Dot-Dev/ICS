# APEDA Agri Export Promotion Scheme

## Overview

The APEDA Agri Export Promotion Scheme is a financial assistance program designed to support exporters of scheduled agricultural and processed food products. It provides financial assistance for activities such as market development, quality development, and export infrastructure development under the 15th Finance Commission cycle (2021-22 to 2025-26). The scheme aims to enhance India's agri-export competitiveness through targeted interventions in key product segments.

## Objective

- Development of export infrastructure
- Quality development
- Market development
- Promotion of export-oriented production
- Collection and publication of export statistics
- Training in various aspects of connected industries
- Improvement of packaging and marketing of scheduled products outside India

## Important Conditions

- Financial assistance is subject to availability of funds and approval by the competent authority.
- Beneficiaries must maintain proper records and submit utilization certificates and audited expenditure statements.
- The scheme is operational only during the 15th Finance Commission cycle (2021-22 to 2025-26).
- Applicants must ensure that the proposed activity aligns with APEDA's scheduled products and export promotion objectives.
- Submission of false information or documents may lead to rejection and blacklisting.
