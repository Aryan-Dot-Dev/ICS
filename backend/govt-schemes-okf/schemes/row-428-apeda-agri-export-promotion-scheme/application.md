## Process

1. Visit the APEDA website and navigate to the Financial Assistance Schemes section.
2. Download the scheme guidelines and application form for the specific component (Market Development, Quality Development, or Export Infrastructure Development).
3. Prepare a detailed project proposal including objectives, budget, timeline, and expected outcomes.
4. Gather required documents such as IEC code, RCMC, MSME registration (if applicable), GST/PAN details, audited financials, and category-specific documents.
5. Submit the completed application along with supporting documents via the designated portal or email.
6. Await scrutiny and evaluation by APEDA officials.
7. If approved, receive Registration-cum-Allocation Certificate (RCAC) or equivalent approval.
8. Implement the project as per the approved plan.
9. Submit periodic progress reports and utilization certificates.
10. Undergo final inspection and submit final report for closure and final disbursement.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year as per the scheme window (2021-22 to 2025-26).
