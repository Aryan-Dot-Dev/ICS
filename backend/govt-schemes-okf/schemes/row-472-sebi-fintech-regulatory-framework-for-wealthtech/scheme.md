# SEBI FinTech Regulatory Framework for WealthTech

## Overview

The SEBI FinTech Regulatory Framework for WealthTech is a regulatory initiative by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) aimed at overseeing and guiding the growth of financial technology (FinTech) companies operating in the wealth management sector. It establishes guidelines and compliance requirements for entities providing digital wealth management services, including robo-advisory, digital portfolio management, and online investment platforms. The framework seeks to ensure investor protection, market integrity, and transparency while fostering innovation in the WealthTech space. It applies to intermediaries and platforms offering wealth-tech solutions under SEBI’s regulatory purview.

## Objective

- Regulate digital wealth management services to ensure investor protection
- Establish compliance standards for FinTech platforms offering wealth-tech solutions
- Promote transparency and accountability in algorithm-driven investment advice
- Mitigate risks associated with data privacy, cybersecurity, and mis-selling in digital wealth platforms
- Encourage responsible innovation in the FinTech and wealth management sector
- Align WealthTech operations with existing SEBI regulations for investment advisers and portfolio managers

## Important Conditions

- The framework applies only to entities conducting wealth-tech activities that constitute regulated investment advice or portfolio management
- Pure technology providers or platforms offering only execution-only services may not fall under this framework
- Compliance does not guarantee immunity from enforcement action in case of violations
- The framework is subject to updates based on technological advancements and market developments
- Foreign entities offering wealth-tech services to Indian clients may also be subject to SEBI regulations
