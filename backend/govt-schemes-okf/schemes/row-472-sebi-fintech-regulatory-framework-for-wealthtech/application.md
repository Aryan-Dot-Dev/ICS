## Process

1. Determine if your wealth-tech platform falls under SEBI’s definition of an investment adviser or portfolio manager.
2. If applicable, register under SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013 or SEBI (Portfolio Managers) Regulations,
3. 3. Ensure compliance with operational, governance, technology, and disclosure norms as outlined in the framework.
4. Implement robust cybersecurity, data protection, and audit mechanisms.
5. Submit required disclosures and reports to SEBI as per periodic reporting requirements.
6. Undergo inspections or audits as conducted by SEBI or its authorized agencies.

## Deadlines

- Ongoing compliance — entities must adhere to the framework continuously; registration and reporting follow SEBI’s prescribed timelines under relevant regulations.
