# PFRDA Innovation for PensionTech Startups

## Overview

PFRDA Innovation for PensionTech Startups is a scheme launched by the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA) to support startups innovating in the pension technology space. The initiative aims to foster innovation in pension services through regulatory sandbox frameworks and digital platforms. It encourages the development of solutions that enhance accessibility, efficiency, and user experience in pension systems. The scheme is part of PFRDA’s broader efforts to modernize pension delivery and promote financial inclusion.

## Objective

- Foster innovation in pension technology (PensionTech) through regulatory sandbox support
- Encourage development of digital solutions for pension accessibility and efficiency
- Support startups in testing innovative pension products and services under regulatory supervision
- Promote financial inclusion by enabling broader access to pension schemes via technology
- Strengthen the pension ecosystem through collaboration between regulators and innovators

## Eligibility

Startups incorporated in India working on innovative pension technology solutions, including but not limited to digital onboarding, grievance redressal, pension analytics, and subscriber education platforms, are eligible to apply under the PFRDA Innovation for PensionTech Startups scheme.
