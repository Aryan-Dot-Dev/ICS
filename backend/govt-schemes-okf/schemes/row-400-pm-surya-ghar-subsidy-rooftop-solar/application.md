## Process

1. Visit the national portal at https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in
2. Register as a beneficiary using mobile number and email
3. Log in and fill the application form with property and consumer details
4. Upload required documents including proof of ownership and identity
5. Select an empaneled vendor for solar installation
6. Submit the application for technical feasibility check
7. Upon approval, proceed with installation by the selected vendor
8. After installation, submit completion report and inspection request
9. Once inspected and approved, subsidy is disbursed to the beneficiary's bank account.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to fund availability.
