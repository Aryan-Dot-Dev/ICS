## Process

1. Interested startups must submit an application through the official ISRO S-TIC portal or designated incubation centre.
2. Applications are reviewed by a selection committee based on innovation, feasibility, and relevance to space technology.
3. Shortlisted candidates may be invited for presentations or interviews.
4. Selected startups are offered incubation support, including access to ISRO facilities and mentorship.
5. The incubation period is typically for a fixed duration, during which the startup works on developing its technology or solution under ISRO guidance.
