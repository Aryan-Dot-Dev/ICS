# Space Technology Incubation Centre (S-TIC) – ISRO

## Overview

The Space Technology Incubation Centre (S-TIC) – ISRO is a program initiated by the Indian Space Research Organisation to foster innovation and entrepreneurship in space technology. It aims to support startups and incubate ideas that contribute to the advancement of space applications and related technologies. The scheme provides a platform for nurturing early-stage ventures through mentorship, infrastructure, and technical guidance from ISRO experts.

## Objective

- To promote innovation and entrepreneurship in space technology
- To provide incubation support to startups working on space-related applications
- To facilitate access to ISRO's expertise, infrastructure, and technical mentorship
- To encourage development of indigenous technologies for national space programs
- To create a pipeline of innovative solutions for ISRO's future missions

## Eligibility

Startups and entrepreneurs working on innovative ideas, technologies, or applications in the domain of space technology, including satellite technology, remote sensing, navigation, space applications, and allied fields, are eligible to apply for incubation support under S-TIC.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Selected startups receive access to ISRO's technical expertise, mentorship from scientists and engineers, infrastructure support, networking opportunities with industry and academia, and guidance on technology development, testing, and validation. The program also facilitates connections with potential investors and collaborators in the space ecosystem.

## Important Conditions

- Incubation support is non-financial and does not include direct funding
- Selection is competitive and based on alignment with ISRO's technology priorities
- Incubation period is limited and subject to periodic review
- Startups must comply with ISRO's safety, security, and confidentiality guidelines
- Intellectual property rights arising from the incubation may be subject to specific agreements
