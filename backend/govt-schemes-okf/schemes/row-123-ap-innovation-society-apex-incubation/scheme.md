# AP Innovation Society & APEX Incubation

## Overview

AP Innovation Society & APEX Incubation is a state-level initiative by the Government of Andhra Pradesh aimed at fostering innovation and entrepreneurship through incubation support, mentorship, and infrastructure for startups and innovators across the state. The program operates under the AP Innovation Society and focuses on nurturing early-stage ventures by providing access to incubation centers, technical guidance, and networking opportunities. It seeks to strengthen the entrepreneurial ecosystem in Andhra Pradesh by supporting innovation-driven enterprises in key sectors.

## Objective

- Promote innovation and entrepreneurship across Andhra Pradesh
- Provide incubation support including infrastructure and mentorship to early-stage startups
- Facilitate access to technology, markets, and funding opportunities for innovators
- Strengthen the startup ecosystem through collaboration with academia, industry, and investors
- Support sector-specific innovation in areas such as agriculture, healthcare, and manufacturing

## Eligibility

The scheme is open to startups, innovators, and entrepreneurs based in Andhra Pradesh who are working on innovative ideas, products, or services with potential for scalability and social impact. Preference may be given to ventures registered in the state or those committing to operate within Andhra Pradesh.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Selected incubatees receive access to incubation center infrastructure (office space, labs, internet), mentorship from industry experts and academics, guidance on product development and business modeling, assistance in IPR filing, networking opportunities with investors and industry partners, and support in accessing government schemes and funding programs.
