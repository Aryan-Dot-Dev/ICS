## Process

1. For the Cargo Promotion Scheme:
2. Register online with ICSL on a portal developed by ICSL.
3. Submit applications with details of cargo to be transported, planned route, plan for modal shift for 5 years, and other relevant information.
4. Commit to cargo volumes for 3 years for modal shift to IWT and undertake regular movements on NW-1, NW-2, NW-16 and transit movements using Indo Bangladesh Protocol routes.
5. Applications examined and approved by the Steering Committee.
6. Maintain accurate records, voyage log book, documents, and reports related to transportation via IWT.
7. Submit periodic reports detailing quantity of cargo transported, routes used, modal shift achieved, and other relevant data.
8. For fund disbursement, submit documents including Bill of coastal goods, Shipper’s Certificate, Receiver’s Certificate, Port’s Invoice, Terminals Invoice, Lock Gate charges, Vessel related expenses (fuel, manning, insurance), Customs Clearance & Immigration Documents (if applicable), and other valid invoices.
9. Funds disbursed within 7 days of receipt of all requisite documents (GST bills only).
10. All documents and payment requests submitted online. For internships:
11. Send applications along with CVs in prescribed format to The Secretary, IWAI, A-13, Sector-1, Noida-201301.
12. Clearly indicate area of interest and preferred division.
13. Produce a letter from Head of Department/Principal indicating status and 'No Objection' for internship.
14. Applications processed by Recruitment and Training Cell at IWAI.
15. Applications scrutinized by Head Office/respective Directorate.
16. Actual offers sent to selected interns subject to availability of slots, consent of Division, and approval of Competent Authority.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round for internships and deputation posts as per periodic employment notices. For the Cargo Promotion Scheme, the scheme is initially valid for a period of 3 years.
