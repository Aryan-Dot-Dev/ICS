# National Waterway Development – IWAI Schemes

## Overview

The National Waterway Development – IWAI Schemes focus on the development and regulation of inland waterways for shipping and navigation in India. The Inland Waterways Authority of India (IWAI), established in 1986, undertakes projects for development and maintenance of IWT infrastructure on national waterways through grants received from the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways. IWAI has developed 111 National Waterways (NWs) across India, with a total length of 20,187 km, and promotes inland water transport (IWT) through infrastructure development, navigational aids, and schemes like the Cargo Promotion Scheme to incentivize modal shift from road and rail to IWT.

## Objective

- Development and regulation of inland waterways for shipping and navigation
- Undertake projects for development and maintenance of IWT infrastructure on national waterways
- Promote modal shift of cargo from road and rail to inland water transport (IWT)
- Develop and maintain navigational channels through dredging and bandalling
- Establish terminals, jetties, and allied infrastructure on National Waterways
- Facilitate cargo and passenger movement on Indo-Bangladesh Protocol (IBP) route
- Support river cruise tourism and develop operational stretches on National Waterways
- Provide Least Available Depth (LAD) data for safe navigation

## Important Conditions

- The Cargo Promotion Scheme is currently restricted to NW-1, NW-2, and NW-16 via Indo Bangladesh Protocol routes
- Incentives under the Cargo Promotion Scheme are not applicable for short haul movements (<300 km), Ro-Ro, Ro-Pax, ODC movements, or transport by ICSL
- Financial incentives to cargo owners will not be provided beyond the period of 3 years under the Cargo Promotion Scheme
- Internship duration is minimum 1 month and maximum 3 months as decided by Competent Authority, not exceeding 6 months
- Interns not completing the requisite period as stipulated in their offer will not be issued with any certificate
- The Internship Programme may be modified as per the requirement of IWAI with approval of the Competent Authority (Chairman, IWAI)
- Internship is neither a job nor any assurance for job in IWAI
