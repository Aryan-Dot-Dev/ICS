## Process

1. Approach a Member Lending Institution (MLI) registered with CGTMSE for an MSE loan (Term Loan or Working Capital).
2. The MLI processes the loan application and sanctions/disburses the loan based on internal credit appraisal.
3. After loan sanction/disbursement, the MLI lodges an application for guarantee coverage with CGTMSE.
4. CGTMSE approves the application and issues the guarantee cover to the MLI upon receipt of the applicable guarantee fee.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — MLIs can apply for guarantee cover anytime during the loan tenure, provided the credit facility was not restructured or remained in SMA2 status in the last 1 year from application date.
