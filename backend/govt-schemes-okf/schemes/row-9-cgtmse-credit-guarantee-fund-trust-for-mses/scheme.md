# CGTMSE (Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for MSEs)

## Overview

CGTMSE (Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises) is a joint initiative of the Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises (MSME), Government of India and Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI) to facilitate collateral-free credit to Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs) by providing guarantee cover to eligible Member Lending Institutions (MLIs). It does not provide direct financial assistance but guarantees loans extended by MLIs to MSEs, thereby enhancing access to institutional credit for unserved and underserved entrepreneurs. Over the past 25 years, CGTMSE has approved 1.15 crore guarantees worth ₹9.34 lakh crore, with 276 MLIs registered under CGS-I and CGS-II as of March 31, 2025.

## Objective

- Catalyse flow of institutional credit to Micro & Small Enterprises (MSEs)
- Strengthen credit delivery system and facilitate flow of credit to the MSE sector
- Create access to finance for unserved, under-served and underprivileged MSEs
- Make availability of finance from conventional lenders to new generation entrepreneurs
- Provide collateral and/or third party guarantee free credit facilities to MSEs
- Build a vibrant and financially inclusive ecosystem for MSE contributors to the economy


## Target Beneficiaries

**Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs)**, **new generation entrepreneurs**, **unserved and underserved MSEs**

## Important Conditions

- CGTMSE does not provide any financial assistance, loans, credit facilities, or subsidies
- CGTMSE deals only with MLIs (Banks/FIs) directly and does not have agents/agencies for loan arrangement
- Guarantee cover is not available for credit facilities extended to Self Help Groups (SHGs)
- Sharing of securities is not permitted in co-financing arrangements
- Existing credit facilities can be covered only if not restructured or in SMA2 status in the last 1 year
- Websites with deceptively similar names (e.g., www.cgtmse-govt.in) are not official and pose risks
