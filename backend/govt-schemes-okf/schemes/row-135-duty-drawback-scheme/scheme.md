# Duty Drawback Scheme

## Overview

The Duty Drawback Scheme is a customs duty refund mechanism under the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC) that reimburses exporters for duties paid on imported inputs used in the manufacture of export goods. It aims to enhance the competitiveness of Indian exports by neutralizing the incidence of taxes and duties on inputs used in export production. The scheme operates under the Customs Act, 1962 and is administered by CBIC to promote export-oriented industrialization and reduce the cost burden on exporters.

## Objective

- To refund customs and central excise duties paid on inputs used in the manufacture of export goods
- To neutralize the incidence of taxes and duties on inputs used for export production
- To enhance the international competitiveness of Indian exports
- To promote export-oriented industrialization and value addition in domestic manufacturing
- To reduce the cost burden on exporters and improve realization in global markets


## Target Beneficiaries

**exporters**, **manufacturers**, **MSME**


## Key Features

- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: Claims must be filed within 12 months from the date of let export of the goods, as per Section 75 of the Customs Act, 1962.
- Portal: https://www.icegate.gov.in/
- Source data last updated: 2026
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

Exporters who have paid customs duty on imported inputs or central excise duty on indigenous inputs used in the manufacture of goods that are subsequently exported are eligible for duty drawback. The claim must be filed within the prescribed time limit from the date of export, and the exporter must maintain proper records of import, usage in production, and export.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial reimbursement of customs and central excise duties paid on inputs used in export production; neutralization of tax burden on exports; improved price competitiveness in international markets; encouragement of value addition and import substitution in export-oriented sectors; compliance with WTO principles by not constituting a subsidy but a tax remission.

## Important Conditions

- Drawback is not available if CENVAT credit or input tax credit under GST has been availed on the same inputs
- Claims are time-barred if not filed within 12 months from the date of export
- Drawback rates are subject to periodic revision based on input-output norms
- Refund may be withheld or rejected if documents are incomplete or discrepancies are found
- No drawback is allowed on goods exported under bond or letter of undertaking (LUT) without payment of IGST
