## Process

1. Determine eligibility based on import of inputs and subsequent export of finished goods.
2. Maintain records of bills of entry, warehouse documents, production records, and shipping bills.
3. File drawback claim electronically through the ICEGATE portal along with shipping bill and proof of export.
4. Submit supporting documents such as bills of entry, bank realization certificate, and consent letter if applicable.
5. Claim is processed by the jurisdictional Customs Office after verification of documents and adherence to time limits.
6. Upon approval, drawback amount is credited to the exporter's bank account.

## Deadlines

- Claims must be filed within 12 months from the date of let export of the goods, as per Section 75 of the Customs Act, 1962.
