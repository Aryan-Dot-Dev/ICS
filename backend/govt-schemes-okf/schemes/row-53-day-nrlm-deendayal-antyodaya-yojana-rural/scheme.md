# DAY-NRLM – Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana (Rural)

## Overview

DAY-NRLM (Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana – National Rural Livelihoods Mission) is a centrally sponsored scheme launched by the Ministry of Rural Development to reduce rural poverty by promoting diversified and gainful self-employment and skilled wage employment opportunities. It focuses on organizing rural poor women into Self Help Groups (SHGs) and federations, building their capacities, and enabling access to financial and livelihood resources. The scheme aims to achieve universal social mobilization, financial inclusion, and sustainable livelihoods for rural households living below the poverty line.

## Objective

- Organize rural poor women into Self Help Groups (SHGs) and their federations
- Build capacities of the poor for livelihood diversification and skill development
- Ensure financial inclusion through access to credit, savings, and insurance
- Support creation of sustainable livelihoods through farm and non-farm activities
- Promote social inclusion and empowerment of marginalized groups
- Strengthen institutional platforms of the poor at village, block, and district levels
- Facilitate convergence with other government programs for holistic development
- Enable access to rights, entitlements, and public services


## Target Beneficiaries

**rural poor**, **women**, **Self Help Groups (SHGs)**, **village organisations**, **rural youth**

## Key Features

- Grant/assistance per entity: Up to INR 15,000 per Self Help Group (SHG) as Revolving Fund
- Geographic scope: Pan-India (rural areas across all states and union territories) (central-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — SHG formation and RF/CIF applications are accepted throughout the year based on village-level readiness and validation.
- Portal: https://aajeevika.gov.in
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

Rural households living below the poverty line (BPL), with special focus on women, SC/ST, persons with disabilities, and vulnerable groups. Eligibility is determined through participatory identification of poor (PIP) at the village level, validated by Gram Sabha. All rural poor are eligible for mobilization into SHGs, with priority given to the poorest and most marginalized.

## Important Conditions

- Financial support is contingent on group grading, performance, and adherence to norms
- Benefits are accessible only through registered and validated SHGs and village organisations
- Funds must be used for approved livelihood and income-generating activities
- Misutilization of funds may lead to recovery and exclusion from further support
