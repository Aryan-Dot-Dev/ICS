## Process

1. Mobilization of rural poor into Self Help Groups (SHGs) through village-level outreach.
2. Formation and training of SHGs on group dynamics, savings, and inter-loaning.
3. Validation of SHGs by Village Organisation and Gram Sabha.
4. Opening of bank account and initiation of regular savings.
5. Application for Revolving Fund (RF) through Village Organisation.
6. Sanction and disbursement of RF by Block Level Federation.
7. Utilization of RF for internal lending and income-generating activities.
8. Eligibility for Community Investment Fund (CIF) based on group performance and grading.
9. Access to CIF for livelihood investments through village organisation.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — SHG formation and RF/CIF applications are accepted throughout the year based on village-level readiness and validation.
