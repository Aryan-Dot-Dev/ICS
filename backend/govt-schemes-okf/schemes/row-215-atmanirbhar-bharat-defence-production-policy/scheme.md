# Atmanirbhar Bharat Defence Production Policy

## Overview

The Atmanirbhar Bharat Defence Production Policy is a strategic initiative by the Ministry of Defence to enhance self-reliance in defence manufacturing. It aims to reduce import dependence, promote indigenous design and development, and strengthen the domestic defence industrial base through policy reforms, financial incentives, and streamlined procurement procedures. The policy encourages private sector participation, MSMEs, and startups in defence production and supports innovation via indigenisation and research & development initiatives.

## Objective

- Reduce import dependence in defence procurement
- Promote indigenous design and development of defence equipment
- Strengthen the domestic defence industrial base
- Encourage private sector participation in defence production
- Support MSMEs and startups in defence manufacturing
- Streamline defence procurement procedures
- Foster innovation through indigenisation and R&D initiatives
- Enhance export competitiveness of defence products

## Eligibility

Eligible entities include Indian companies, startups, MSMEs, and private sector organisations engaged in defence manufacturing, design, development, or supply of defence equipment, systems, or services. Preference is given to entities contributing to indigenisation, technology absorption, and Make in India initiatives under the Atmanirbhar Bharat framework.

## Benefits

Benefits include preferential treatment in defence procurement, simplified procedures for vendor registration, access to defence testing and evaluation facilities, support for technology transfer and joint ventures, facilitation of exports, and participation in defence exhibitions and seminars.
