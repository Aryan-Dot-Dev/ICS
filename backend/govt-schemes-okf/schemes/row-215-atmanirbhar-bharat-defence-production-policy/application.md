## Process

1. Identify defence production opportunities through the Ministry of Defence or defence PSUs.
2. Register as a vendor on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM) or defence-specific procurement portals.
3. Submit Expression of Interest (EOI) or tender response for defence contracts.
4. Undergo technical evaluation and quality assurance processes.
5. Upon selection, enter into contract for supply, development, or production of defence equipment.
6. Avail benefits under linked schemes like DPEPP for financial incentives.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year based on defence procurement cycles and tender notifications.
