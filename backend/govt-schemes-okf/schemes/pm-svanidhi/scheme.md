# PM SVANidhi — PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi

## Overview

PM SVANidhi (launched 01.07.2020) provides **collateral-free working-
capital loans** to urban street vendors, with an escalating ladder:
**₹15,000 (first loan) → ₹25,000 (second, on repayment) → ₹50,000
(third)**, plus **7% interest subsidy** on timely/early repayment
(paid via DBT) and **cashback up to ₹1,200/year** on prescribed digital
transactions [S1][S3].

**Version note (recorded change — August 2025 restructuring):** the
Union Cabinet approved restructuring and extension of the scheme's
lending period **beyond 31.12.2024 to 31.03.2030**; first-tranche loans
increased to **₹15,000** (from ₹10,000) and second tranche to
**₹25,000** (from ₹20,000); third tranche remains ₹50,000; credit
guarantee and interest-subsidy support extended (guidelines reference
2027/2033/2035 horizons) [S2][S3][S4]. The engine must use the
restructured limits, not the 2020 ones (see
[Historical](#historical-superseded)).

## Objective

To formalise street vendors, provide affordable working capital, and
incentivise digital transactions for urban micro-entrepreneurs.

## Target Beneficiaries

Street vendors engaged in vending in urban areas as per definition
under the Street Vendors Act framework — holding a **Certificate of
Vending (CoV)** or **Letter of Recommendation (LoR)** from the ULB, or
identified in ULB surveys [S1][S4].

## Key Features

- Loan ladder ₹15,000 → ₹25,000 → ₹50,000 (progressive, repayment-gated)
  [S3]
- Interest subsidy @7% p.a. on timely/early repayment (DBT) [S1]
- Digital cashback up to ₹1,200/year on prescribed transactions [S1]
- Credit guarantee cover for second and third loans (per guidelines)
- UPI-linked PM SVANidhi credit card concept for tranche-2 repayers
  (per scheme updates) — verify current offering
- Lending institutions: Scheduled Commercial Banks, RRBs, Cooperative
  Banks, NBFCs, MFIs, SHG banks

## Eligibility

Summary — see [eligibility.md](eligibility.md): street vendor with
CoV/LoR/survey ID, urban vending, 18+.

## Benefits

Working-capital loans + interest subsidy + cashback. Details:

## Documents

CoV/LoR or survey proof, Aadhaar, bank account. Details:

## Application

Online on [pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in](https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in/),
via ULB, lending institutions, or the PM SVANidhi app. Details:
[application.md](application.md).

## Important Conditions

- Tranche progression requires **full repayment** of the previous loan
- Interest subsidy requires timely/early repayment [S1]
- Vending must be in ULB-permitted areas per the Act framework

## Exclusions

Vendors without vending documentation; non-street-vendor urban
informal workers; rural vendors. Structured list:
[exclusions.md](exclusions.md).
