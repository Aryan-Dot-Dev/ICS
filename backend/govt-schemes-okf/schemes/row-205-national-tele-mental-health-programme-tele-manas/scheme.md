# National Tele Mental Health Programme (Tele-MANAS)

## Overview

The National Tele Mental Health Programme (Tele-MANAS) is a comprehensive mental health care service launched by the Government of India to provide universal access to equitable, accessible, affordable and quality mental health care through 24x7 tele-mental health services. It operates as a digital component of the National Mental Health Programme (NMHP) across all Indian States and UTs, offering toll-free helpline services, video consultations, e-prescriptions, and linkages to in-person services. The programme aims to scale up mental health service reach, extend services to vulnerable and hard-to-reach populations, and provide integrated medical and psychosocial interventions.

## Objective

- To provide universal access to equitable, accessible, affordable and quality mental health care through 24X7 tele-mental health services as a digital component of the National Mental Health Programme (NMHP) across all Indian States and UTs with assured linkages.
- To exponentially scale up the reach of mental health services to anybody who reaches out, across India, any time, by setting up a 24x7 tele-mental health facility in each of the States and UTs of the country.
- To implement a full-fledged mental health-service network that, in addition to counselling, provides integrated medical and psychosocial interventions including video consultations with mental health specialists, e-prescriptions, follow-up services and linkages to in-person services.
- To extend services to vulnerable groups of the population and difficult to reach populations

## Important Conditions

- Personal data such as name, age, gender, district, and state are queried during calls but are not mandatory.
- Audio or video call recordings are not stored;
- only anonymized data is retained for 3 years for research and analysis.
- In case of emergency or risk to life, personal data may be shared with police or mental health specialists in compliance with law.
