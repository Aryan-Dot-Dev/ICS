## Process

1. Dial the toll-free number 14416 or 1-800 891 4416 to access the Tele MANAS helpline.
2. The call will be an IVRS-based audio call with timely auto-call back.
3. A trained counsellor will attend the call and assess the level of care required.
4. If needed, the counsellor will provide care within their capabilities or refer the caller to a mental health specialist.
5. For specialist care, a video consultation can be initiated with consent, and a link will be sent via SMS to join the call.
6. If medication is needed, psychiatrists can issue e-prescriptions.
7. For urgent in-person intervention, the caller will be referred to the nearest in-person service or an audio-visual consultation arranged via eSanjeevani.

## Deadlines

- 24x7 service — available round the clock, throughout the year.
