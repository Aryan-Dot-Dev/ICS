## Process

1. States/UTs submit project proposals under the three UCF verticals: Cities as Growth Hubs, Creative Redevelopment of Cities, and Water and Sanitation (including TOD infrastructure development).
2. Proposals undergo appraisal at national level by the National Apex Committee (NAC) and National Project Management Unit.
3. Independent Review and Monitoring Agencies (IRMA) evaluate projects for technical and financial viability.
4. Upon approval, funds are released in tranches: 30% first tranche upon sanction, 50% second tranche after utilization of at least 70% of first tranche, and 20% third tranche after utilization of at least 70% of second tranche and 100% of first tranche.
5. State/UT level implementation involves State High Powered Steering Committee (SHPSC), State Level Technical Committee (SLTC), and ULB/Implementing Agency.
6. Project Development and Management Consultant (PDMC) or Project Preparation and Support Unit assists in planning and execution.
7. Monitoring occurs at national and state levels through NAC, NAC, IRMA, SHPSC, SLTC, and ULB.

## Deadlines

- The Mission will be implemented from Financial Year (FY) 2025–26 to FY 2030–31, extendable by three years. Applications are accepted during the Mission period as per annual guidelines and fund allocation cycles.
