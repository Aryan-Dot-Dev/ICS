# Solar Parks & Ultra Mega Solar Power Projects

## Overview

Solar Parks & Ultra Mega Solar Power Projects is a scheme launched by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) on 12-12-2014 to support the development of solar parks and ultra mega solar power projects across India. The scheme aims to facilitate large-scale solar power generation by providing developed land with necessary infrastructure such as transmission systems, water access, road connectivity, and communication networks. It targets the establishment of at least 25 solar parks with a cumulative capacity exceeding 20,000 MW within five years starting from 2014-15. SECI acts as an implementing agency for this scheme, facilitating grant disbursement and project monitoring.

## Objective

- Support States in setting up solar parks at various locations to create required infrastructure for solar power projects
- Facilitate and speed up installation of grid-connected solar power projects for large-scale electricity generation
- Provide suitable developed land with clearances, transmission systems, water access, road connectivity, and communication networks
- Achieve over 20,000 MW of solar power installed capacity through solar parks and ultra mega solar power projects
- Promote solar power development in difficult terrains and hilly states by allowing smaller parks where contiguous land is scarce
- Enable central financial assistance for detailed project reports and project cost components
- Ensure timely release of grants linked to project milestones
- Monitor and oversee the development of solar parks through SECI as the central implementing agency

## Important Conditions

- The grant of up to Rs. 20 lakhs/MW or 30% of project cost (including grid-connectivity), whichever is lower, is subject to appraisal and sanction by SECI
- The scheme allows smaller parks only in Himalayan Region, other hilly States, and States with acute shortage of non-agricultural land
- The implementing agency must be designated by the State Government
- Grant release is strictly linked to achievement of prescribed milestones
- As of the evidence, grant of Rs. 141.50 Crore has been released to SECI by 31st December 2014
