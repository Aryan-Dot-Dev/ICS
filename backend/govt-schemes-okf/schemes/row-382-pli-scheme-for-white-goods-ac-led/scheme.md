# PLI Scheme for White Goods (AC & LED)

## Overview

The PLI Scheme for White Goods (AC & LED) targets manufacturers of components/sub-assemblies of Air Conditioners & LED Lights and offers 4–6% incentive on incremental sales to boost localization, reduce imports, and build a domestic supply chain.

## Objective

- Boost localization of manufacturing
- Reduce imports of white goods
- Build a domestic supply chain for AC and LED components
- Offer 4–6% incentive on incremental sales


## Target Beneficiaries

**manufacturers of components/sub-assemblies of Air Conditioners & LED Lights**
