# DSIR Recognition for In-house R&D Units

## Overview

The Department of Scientific and Industrial Research (DSIR) operates a scheme for granting recognition and registration to in-house R&D units established by corporate industry. The scheme aims to benchmark industrial R&D, provide eligibility for fiscal incentives, and facilitate access to R&D funding from other government departments. Recognition is granted to units engaged in innovative R&D activities related to the firm's line of business, excluding routine activities like market research or quality control.

## Objective

- Grant recognition & registration to in-house R&D units established by corporate industry
- Benchmark industrial R&D across the government setup
- Enable eligibility for fiscal incentives for R&D implemented through DSIR
- Facilitate access to R&D funds from other government departments such as DST, DBT, DeitY, MoEF, MNRE, MoFPI, CSIR, ICMR, ICAR, TDB
- Promote innovative research & development activities related to the line of business of the firm
- Encourage development of new technologies, design & engineering, process/product improvements, and new methods of analysis & testing
- Support research for increased efficiency in use of resources such as capital equipment, materials & energy
- Promote pollution control, effluent treatment & recycling of waste products

## Important Conditions

- Market research, work & methods study, operations & management research, testing & analysis of routine nature for operation, process control, quality control and maintenance of day-to-day production, maintenance of plant are not considered as R&D activities.
- Recognition is subject to evaluation by DSIR based on submitted documents and may involve discussions with the chief of the R&D unit and executives of the firm.
- The Chairman of the Screening Committee can relax any of the given conditions for consideration of recognition.
- For Biotech start-ups: relaxation in 3 years of existence for granting short-term fresh recognition if established in Incubation Centre or Technology Parks.
- For Deep Tech start-ups: relaxation in 3 years of existence for granting short-term fresh recognition if preferably established in Incubation Centre or Technology Parks.
