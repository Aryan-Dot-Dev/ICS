# National Technical Textiles Mission (NTTM)

## Overview

The National Technical Textiles Mission (NTTM) is a flagship initiative launched by the Ministry of Textiles, Government of India, to position India as a global leader in technical textiles. It aims to enhance domestic manufacturing, promote innovation, increase exports, and develop skilled human resources in the technical textiles sector. The mission focuses on research and development, market development, export promotion, and skill development across various sub-segments of technical textiles.

## Objective

- Position India as a global leader in technical textiles
- Enhance domestic manufacturing and reduce import dependence
- Promote innovation and research & development in technical textiles
- Increase exports of technical textiles products
- Develop skilled manpower for the technical textiles sector
- Support market development and brand promotion
- Encourage sustainable and eco-friendly technical textiles
- Strengthen industry-academia collaboration

## Eligibility

Eligible beneficiaries include Indian companies, startups, MSMEs, research institutions, universities, and industry associations engaged in technical textiles manufacturing, research, innovation, or skill development activities. Specific eligibility varies by component and is defined under each sub-mission.

## Benefits

Benefits include financial support for R&D projects, market development assistance, export promotion incentives, skill development training, infrastructure support for common facility centers, and recognition for innovation. The mission also facilitates technology transfer, industry-academia collaboration, and access to testing and certification facilities.
