# HRIDAY – Heritage City Development and Augmentation Yojana

## Overview

HRI Day India is a website providing online banking help and financial guides for various Indian banks. It offers step-by-step instructions for banking tasks such as activating debit cards, checking account balances, downloading statements, and managing online banking services. The site covers multiple banks including SBI, IOB, Canara Bank, PNB, and others, with guides on net banking, mobile apps, and account-related procedures.

## Objective

- Provide easy, accurate instructions for banking tasks
- Help users activate debit cards and enable online transactions
- Assist in resetting passwords and managing bank accounts without confusion
- Offer guidance on downloading bank statements online
- Support users in accessing net banking and mobile banking services
