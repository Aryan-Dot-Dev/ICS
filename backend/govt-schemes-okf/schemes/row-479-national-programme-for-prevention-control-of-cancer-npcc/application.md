## Process

1. States/UTs submit annual Programme Implementation Plans (PIPs) under the National Health Mission (NHM) for cancer-related activities under NPCC.
2. Proposals are appraised by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare based on norms, disease burden, and gap analysis.
3. Upon approval, funds are released in installments as per NHM financial management guidelines.
4. Implementation is monitored through regular reporting, field visits, and performance reviews.
5. Utilization certificates and audit reports are submitted by states for subsequent fund releases.

## Deadlines
