# National Programme for Prevention & Control of Cancer (NPCC)

## Overview

The National Programme for Prevention & Control of Cancer (NPCC) is a public health initiative under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare aimed at reducing cancer incidence and mortality through prevention, early detection, diagnosis, treatment, and palliative care. It focuses on strengthening infrastructure, capacity building, and awareness generation across the country. The programme supports screening for common cancers such as cervical, breast, and oral cancer at various levels of the healthcare system.

## Objective

- Prevent cancer through health promotion and risk factor reduction
- Strengthen early detection and screening facilities at district and sub-district levels
- Ensure access to equitable, affordable, and quality cancer treatment services
- Develop human resources and build capacity in oncology and palliative care
- Establish and strengthen population-based and hospital-based cancer registries
- Promote research and surveillance on cancer trends and outcomes
- Provide palliative care and pain management services
- Generate awareness about cancer prevention, early signs, and treatment options
