## Process

1. State governments or eligible entities submit project proposals to the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying (DAHD).
2. Proposals are appraised by the Project Sanctioning Committee (PSC) under DAHD.
3. Upon approval, sanction letters are issued specifying the financial assistance and terms.
4. Funds are released in installments against utilization certificates and physical progress reports.
5. Projects are monitored through periodic inspections and third-party evaluations.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — proposals can be submitted throughout the year subject to availability of funds and approval by the Project Sanctioning Committee.
