# National Programme for Dairy Development (NPDD)

## Overview

The National Programme for Dairy Development (NPDD) is a centrally sponsored scheme launched by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying (DAHD) to strengthen dairy infrastructure, enhance milk quality, and increase organized milk procurement across India. It focuses on creating and strengthening infrastructure for production, procurement, processing, and marketing of milk and milk products through financial and technical assistance to state governments and implementing agencies.

## Objective

- Enhance quality of milk and milk products
- Increase share of organized milk procurement
- Upgrade dairy processing infrastructure
- Strengthen value-added milk product manufacturing
- Improve access to markets for milk producers
- Promote dairy entrepreneurship and employment generation


## Target Beneficiaries

**state governments**, **milk unions**, **milk producer companies**, **dairy cooperatives**, **private dairy enterprises**

## Eligibility

State governments, milk unions, milk producer companies, dairy cooperatives, and private dairy enterprises are eligible to apply for financial assistance under NPDD for setting up or strengthening dairy infrastructure projects related to milk production, procurement, processing, and marketing.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial assistance for setting up new dairy projects and strengthening existing ones, including infrastructure for milk chilling, processing, value addition, packaging, and marketing. Technical support for quality improvement, training, and capacity building. Assistance for creating forward and backward linkages in the dairy value chain.
