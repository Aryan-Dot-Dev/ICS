## Process

1. Visit EESL website and fill the registration form via the provided link (e.g., Registration of Technology Service Provider, Channel Partners, or Strategic Partners).
2. Submit the printed form along with all requisite documents in physical form as per EESL prescribed format.
3. Include a refundable security deposit of INR 1 Lakh via Demand Draft/NEFT/RTGS in favor of Energy Efficiency Services Limited.
4. Submit self-declaration for blacklisting on company letterhead.
5. Provide To Whom It May Concern letter, PPP MII and Land Border Sharing Certificate, Compliance Matrix, EFT form, and cancelled cheque.
6. Submit two copies of Contract/Definitive Agreement and Non-Disclosure Agreement on stamp paper after registration.
7. Send all documents via registered post to the specified address (e.g., GM (CDP) or Head (Cluster Contract) at EESL, Ikon Tower, Noida).
8. Await evaluation by EESL and approval by competent authority.
9. Receive Letter of Registration upon approval.
10. Execute Non-Disclosure Agreement within 7 days of registration letter issuance.
