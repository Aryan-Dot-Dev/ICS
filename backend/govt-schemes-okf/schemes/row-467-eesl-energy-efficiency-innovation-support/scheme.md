# EESL Energy Efficiency Innovation Support

## Overview

EESL Energy Efficiency Innovation Support is a program by Energy Efficiency Services Limited (EESL) to facilitate business development through partnerships with technology service providers, channel partners, and strategic partners. It enables EESL to aggregate demand for consultancy services and energy efficiency projects across sectors like lighting, buildings, industry, electric mobility, smart metering, and agriculture. The program operates via registration processes where partners submit proposals, documents, and a refundable security deposit, and are evaluated for approval to collaborate on projects such as DEEP, Urja Bachat Sewa, and PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana in Odisha.

## Objective

- Facilitate business development through partnerships with technology service providers, channel partners, and strategic partners
- Aggregate demand for consultancy services and energy efficiency projects across sectors
- Create a project pipeline for consultancy services covering Energy Audit, GHG Accounting, Safety & Performance Audit, Water Audit, RE as a Service, Cooling as a Service, Lighting as a Service
- Promote market transformation in energy efficiency under MSME sector
- Support implementation of innovative energy-efficient technologies in PAT industries
- Extend consultancy in areas like solar power, building energy efficiency, e-mobility, and agriculture demand side management
- Enable EESL to remunerate registered partners with success fees or designated charges upon project completion
- Ensure transparency, scalability, and flexibility in business models to incentivize all stakeholders

## Important Conditions

- Security deposit is refundable only after completion of 2 years and 3 months claim period from date of registration letter
- Parties must not have been blacklisted by any Central/State Government or Public Sector Undertaking
- EESL reserves the right to reject applications without assigning any reason
- Success fees are contingent upon receipt of payment from end consumer
- Technology Solution Partners from countries sharing land border with India must be registered with Competent Authority as per Department of Expenditure order
- EESL may suspend, de-list, or ban partners for malpractices, false documentation, or non-performance
- All documents must be submitted as a single PDF file named after the company;
- non-compliance may lead to rejection
