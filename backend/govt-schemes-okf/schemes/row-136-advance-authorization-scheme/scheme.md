# Advance Authorization Scheme

## Overview

The Advance Authorization Scheme allows duty-free import of inputs that are physically incorporated in an export product, enabling exporters to import raw materials, intermediates, components, consumables, parts, and accessories without payment of basic customs duty, additional customs duty, anti-dumping duty, safeguard duty, or social welfare surcharge, subject to fulfilling export obligations within a specified time frame.

## Objective

- To facilitate duty-free import of inputs required for export production
- To enhance competitiveness of Indian exports by reducing input costs
- To promote value addition and export-oriented manufacturing
- To support exporters in meeting international quality and delivery standards
- To encourage import of high-quality inputs for superior export products


## Target Beneficiaries

**exporters**, **manufacturers**, **merchant exporters**


## Key Features

- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — applications can be submitted throughout the year.
- Portal: https://dgft.gov.in/CP/?opt=advance-authorization

## Eligibility

Any manufacturer exporter or merchant exporter tied to a supporting manufacturer is eligible to apply for Advance Authorization. The applicant must have a valid Importer Exporter Code (IEC) and must be engaged in the manufacture and export of goods. The scheme is applicable to exports under the Foreign Trade Policy, 2023.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Duty-free import of inputs (basic customs duty, additional customs duty, anti-dumping duty, safeguard duty, social welfare surcharge exempted), no requirement to pay duty on inputs if export obligation is fulfilled, ability to import inputs without upfront cash outflow for duties, flexibility in sourcing inputs globally, and validity of authorization for 12 months from date of issue.

## Important Conditions

- Export obligation must be fulfilled within the validity period of the authorization (usually 12 months)
- If export obligation is not met, duty exemption is withdrawn and duties along with interest must be paid
- Only inputs that are physically incorporated into the export product are eligible
- Inputs consumed in the manufacturing process but not part of the final product are not covered
- The scheme does not cover capital goods, fuel, or lubricants
- Authorization is specific to a particular export product and cannot be transferred
- Advance Authorization is subject to audit and verification by DGFT
