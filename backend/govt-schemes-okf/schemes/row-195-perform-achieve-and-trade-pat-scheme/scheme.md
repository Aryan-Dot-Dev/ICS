# Perform Achieve and Trade (PAT) Scheme

## Overview

The Perform, Achieve and Trade (PAT) Scheme is a flagship initiative of the Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) under the National Mission for Enhanced Energy Efficiency (NMEEE, targeting energy-intensive industries through mechanism where energy consumption norms and excess energy savings are converted into tradable Energy Savings Certificates (ESCerts). The scheme operates in cycles of three years each, with targets assigned to Designated Consumers (DCs) from notified sectors such as aluminum, cement, iron & steel, thermal power plants, etc., and has been implemented across multiple cycles since its inception.

## Objective

- Improve energy efficiency in energy intensive industries
- Reduce specific energy consumption (SEC) through target-based approach
- Generate tradable Energy Savings Certificates (ESCerts) from excess energy savings
- Contribute to reduction in greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions
- Promote market-based mechanism for energy efficiency
- Establish systems for measurement, monitoring, and verification of energy efficiency results
- Leverage private sector participation in energy efficiency initiatives


## Target Beneficiaries

**Designated Consumers**, **energy intensive industries**


## Key Features

- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: Based on PAT cycle notifications; each cycle spans three years with specific target year and verification periods as per official notifications (e.g., PAT Cycle VII notified in October 2021 for period 2022-23 to 2024-25).
- Source data last updated: 2026
- Import confidence (source): high


## Eligibility

Industrial units from notified energy intensive sectors (Aluminum, Cement, Chlor-Alkali, Fertilizer, Iron & Steel, Paper & Pulp, Railways, Thermal Power and Textile) that meet the threshold level of annual energy consumption as specified in the notifications issued under the Energy Conservation Act, 2001, and are designated as Designated Consumers (DCs) by the Central Government.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial benefits through trading of Energy Savings Certificates (ESCerts) on power exchanges; recognition for achieving energy efficiency targets; contribution to national energy savings and reduction in GHG emissions; facilitation of investment in energy efficient technologies and processes; access to technical guidance and user manuals developed by BEE for effective implementation.

## Important Conditions

- Only notified energy intensive sectors and units meeting threshold energy consumption are eligible
- Energy savings must be verified and monitored through accredited energy auditors
- ESCerts have a validity period and must be traded within the compliance period
- Non-compliance may attract penalties under the Energy Conservation Act, 2001
- Scheme operates in fixed cycles;
- benefits are cycle-specific
