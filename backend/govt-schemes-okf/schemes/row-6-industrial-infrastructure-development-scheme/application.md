## Process

1. Approach the Department of Industries & Commerce, Haryana for advice on land availability and procedure for allotment.
2. Obtain guidance on conversion of land use and required clearances from departments such as Commercial Taxes, Labour, Town & Country Planning, and Pollution Control Board.
3. Avail incentives and concessions available to the industry.
4. Facilitate coordination with related government departments for regulatory clearances and enforcement.
5. Proceed with setting up of industries on developed land provided under the scheme.
