# Industrial Infrastructure Development Scheme

## Overview

The Industrial Infrastructure Development Scheme under the Haryana Enterprises & Employment Policy-2020 aims to develop robust industrial infrastructure by providing developed land and facilities for industrial ventures. It focuses on creating Industrial Model Townships (IMTs), Industrial Estates, and Theme Parks to support industrial growth and attract investment. The scheme is part of the state's strategy to enhance ease of doing business and promote balanced regional development through infrastructure-led geographical dispersal of industries.

## Objective

- Develop world-class industrial infrastructure to lower operating costs for industries
- Attract indigenous and foreign investments through improved infrastructure
- Support social development and generate new employment opportunities
- Facilitate infrastructure-led geographical dispersal of industries to backward areas
- Leverage benefits from national industrial corridors like DMIC, AKIC, and KMP
- Provide developed land for industrial ventures through Industrial Model Townships (IMTs), Industrial Estates, and Theme Parks
