# Faster Adoption of Manufacturing of Hybrid and Electric Vehicles (FAME)

## Overview

Faster Adoption and Manufacturing of Hybrid and Electric Vehicles (FAME) is a Government of India scheme aimed at promoting electric mobility by providing financial incentives for the adoption of hybrid and electric vehicles. The scheme supports the development of charging infrastructure and encourages domestic manufacturing of electric vehicles and their components. FAME-II, the current phase, focuses on incentivizing electric buses, three-wheelers, four-wheelers used for public transport or registered for commercial purposes, and two-wheelers. It also aims to create demand for electric vehicles through upfront incentives and to establish a robust charging ecosystem.

## Objective

- To incentivize the adoption of hybrid and electric vehicles in India
- To develop charging infrastructure for electric vehicles
- To encourage domestic manufacturing of electric vehicles and components
- To reduce dependence on fossil fuels and curb vehicular emissions
- To create demand for electric vehicles through upfront incentives
- To support pilot projects for electric buses and other commercial vehicles


## Target Beneficiaries

**public transport operators**, **fleet operators**, **institutional buyers**, **individual consumers**, **manufacturers**


## Key Features

- Fund size: ₹10000 crore
- Grant/assistance per entity: Incentives range from INR 10,000 per kWh for two-wheelers (up to 15 kWh), INR 20,000 per kWh for three-wheelers (up to 10 kWh), INR 10,000 per kWh for four-wheelers (up to 15 kWh), and up to INR 20 lakhs per electric bus (for buses above 12 meters).
- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: The scheme is valid until March 31, 2024, or until the exhaustion of funds, whichever occurs earlier.

## Eligibility

Eligibility varies by vehicle segment: for electric two-wheelers, three-wheelers, and four-wheelers, vehicles must be approved by the Automotive Research Association of India (ARAI) or other designated testing agencies and meet specified performance and localization criteria. For electric buses and trucks, eligibility is restricted to vehicles used for public transport or commercial purposes.…

## Benefits

Upfront incentive at the point of purchase for eligible electric vehicles; support for setting up charging infrastructure; promotion of domestic manufacturing through localization requirements; reduction in operating costs due to lower electricity and maintenance expenses; contribution to environmental sustainability by reducing tailpipe emissions.

## Important Conditions

- Incentives are available only for vehicles manufactured by OEMs enrolled under FAME-II
- Subsidy is passed on to the consumer and cannot be claimed directly by end users
- Funds are limited and subject to availability;
- the scheme may close early if the budget is exhausted
- Only vehicles with specified battery capacity and performance criteria are eligible
- Charging infrastructure subsidies are subject to separate guidelines and approvals
