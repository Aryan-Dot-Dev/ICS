# ISRO Technology Transfer Programme

## Overview

The ISRO Technology Transfer Programme facilitates the transfer of ISRO-developed technologies to Indian industries, startups, and academic institutions for commercialization and societal benefit. It aims to bridge the gap between space research and industrial application by enabling access to ISRO's intellectual property, know-how, and technical expertise. The programme supports innovation in sectors such as agriculture, healthcare, telecommunications, and manufacturing through licensing, joint development, and technical collaboration.

## Objective

- Promote the utilization of ISRO-developed technologies in non-space sectors
- Encourage innovation and entrepreneurship through technology transfer
- Strengthen industry-academia collaboration in research and development
- Accelerate the commercialization of space technologies for societal applications
- Enhance the competitiveness of Indian industries through access to advanced space technologies

## Eligibility

Indian industries, startups, MSMEs, academic institutions, and R&D organizations are eligible to apply for technology transfer under the programme. Applicants must demonstrate the capability to absorb, develop, and commercialize the transferred technology for end-user applications.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Access to ISRO's patented technologies, technical know-how, and expertise; support for prototype development and testing; assistance in regulatory compliance and certification; opportunities for joint development and co-branding; potential for scaling up production with ISRO's technical guidance.

## Important Conditions

- Technology transfer is subject to export control regulations and national security considerations
- ISRO retains the right to reject applications without assigning any reason
- Recipients must comply with all terms of the Technology Transfer Agreement (TTA)
- ISRO does not guarantee market success or commercial viability of transferred technologies
