## Process

1. Identify the desired technology from ISRO's Technology Transfer Portal.
2. Submit a formal application expressing interest in technology transfer.
3. Provide details of the applicant organization, intended use, and commercialization plan.
4. ISRO evaluates the application based on technical merit, capability, and alignment with programme goals.
5. Upon approval, negotiate terms of transfer including licensing, royalties, and support.
6. Execute a Technology Transfer Agreement (TTA) and proceed with implementation.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year.
