# Punjab Skill Development Mission

## Overview

The Punjab Skill Development Mission (PSDM) is a state-level initiative aimed at enhancing employability and entrepreneurship by providing skill training to youth across Punjab. It focuses on aligning training with industry demands and promoting self-employment through various vocational programs. The mission operates under the Department of Technical Education & Industrial Training, Punjab, and collaborates with training partners to deliver certified courses. It seeks to bridge the skill gap and support inclusive growth, particularly for marginalized and rural communities.

## Objective

- Provide industry-relevant skill training to enhance employability
- Promote self-employment and entrepreneurship among trained youth
- Align training programs with current and future industry needs
- Ensure inclusion of socially and economically disadvantaged groups
- Strengthen institutional capacity for skill delivery across Punjab
- Monitor and evaluate training outcomes for continuous improvement


## Target Beneficiaries

**youth**, **unemployed**, **rural populations**, **SC/ST**, **women**, **persons with disabilities**

## Eligibility

Eligibility varies by course but generally includes Punjab residents aged 18–35 years, with preference given to unemployed youth, school dropouts, and individuals from SC/ST, women, and economically weaker sections. Specific educational qualifications depend on the trade or course selected.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Benefits include free or subsidized skill training, certification recognized by NCVT/SCVT, placement assistance, post-training support for self-employment, and stipends in certain schemes. Trainees also gain access to industry exposure, soft skills training, and entrepreneurship development modules where applicable.
