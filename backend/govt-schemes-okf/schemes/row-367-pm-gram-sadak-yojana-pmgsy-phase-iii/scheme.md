# PM Gram Sadak Yojana (PMGSY) Phase III

## Overview

PM Gram Sadak Yojana (PMGSY) Phase III is a centrally sponsored scheme aimed at consolidating 1,25,000 km of rural roads through upgradation of existing routes to connect habitations to Gramin Agricultural Markets (GrAMs), Higher Secondary Schools, and Hospitals. The scheme focuses on improving rural connectivity and access to socio-economic services by upgrading existing rural roads to all-weather standards. It is implemented in a mission mode to ensure timely completion and quality execution.

## Objective

- Consolidate 1,25,000 km of rural roads through upgradation
- Connect habitations to Gramin Agricultural Markets (GrAMs)
- Link rural areas to Higher Secondary Schools
- Ensure connectivity to Hospitals and health centres
- Improve all-weather road connectivity in rural areas
- Enhance access to socio-economic services for rural populations
- Promote inclusive growth through better rural infrastructure
- Reduce poverty and isolation in remote villages

## Eligibility

The scheme covers rural roads that are part of the Core Network and are required to be upgraded to provide connectivity to habitations with a population of 250+ (in general areas) and 100+ (in tribal, desert, hilly areas, and selected tribal and backward districts) as per Census 2011. Roads must be existing and part of the PMGSY Core Network to qualify for upgradation under Phase III.

## Important Conditions

- Only roads included in the PMGSY Core Network are eligible for upgradation
- Funding is contingent upon adherence to PMGSY-III specifications and guidelines
- States must bear their respective share of project cost as per funding pattern
- Works must be completed within the stipulated timeframe to avoid fund cancellation
- Third Party Quality Monitoring (TPQM) is mandatory for all works
