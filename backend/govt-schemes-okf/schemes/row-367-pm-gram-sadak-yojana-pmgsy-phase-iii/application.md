## Process

1. States identify eligible roads from the Core Network and prepare Detailed Project Reports (DPRs). DPRs are submitted to the respective State Level Standing Committee for approval. Approved DPRs are then forwarded to the Ministry of Rural Development for sanction. Upon sanction, funds are released to the States in instalments. States then tender and execute the works through contracted agencies. Progress is monitored via OMMAS and field inspections.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — proposals are accepted throughout the year based on annual planning cycles and fund availability.
