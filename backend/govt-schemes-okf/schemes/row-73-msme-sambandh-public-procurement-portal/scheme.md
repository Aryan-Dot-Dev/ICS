# MSME SAMBANDH (Public Procurement Portal)

## Overview

MSME SAMBANDH is a public procurement portal launched by the Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises to facilitate government procurement from MSMEs. It enables central and state government ministries, departments, and public sector undertakings to publish tenders and procurement opportunities specifically for MSMEs. The portal aims to enhance market access, promote transparency, and increase participation of MSMEs in government procurement processes.

## Objective

- Facilitate government procurement from MSMEs through a centralized online portal
- Enhance market access and visibility for MSME products and services
- Promote transparency and efficiency in public procurement processes
- Increase participation of MSMEs in government tenders and contracts
- Support the growth and competitiveness of MSMEs through assured procurement opportunities

## Eligibility

MSMEs with valid Udyam Registration are eligible to participate in procurement opportunities published on the SAMBANDH portal. The portal is accessible to all registered MSMEs across manufacturing and service sectors.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

MSMEs gain access to government procurement opportunities, increased visibility for their products/services, transparent bidding processes, and potential for assured orders from government buyers. The portal reduces information asymmetry and search costs for MSMEs seeking government contracts.

- Award of contracts is subject to competitive bidding processes
- Udyam Registration is mandatory for participation
