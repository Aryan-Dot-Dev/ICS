## Process

1. MSMEs must first obtain Udyam Registration via https://udyamregistration.gov.in
2. Visit the SAMBANDH portal at https://msme.gov.in/sambandh
3. Browse available government tenders and procurement opportunities
4. Select relevant tenders based on business category and location
5. Download tender documents and review eligibility criteria
6. Prepare and submit bids as per tender specifications
7. Track application status through the portal
8. Upon award, execute contract as per terms

## Deadlines

- Tender-specific deadlines apply; the portal hosts ongoing procurement opportunities with varying submission dates.
