## Process

1. Register on the Startup India portal (startupindia.gov.in)
2. Apply for DPIIT recognition via the 'Get Recognised' section
3. Submit incorporation documents, PAN, and business description
4. Await recognition approval (typically 2-7 business days)
5. Once recognised, apply for the Climate Action Challenge through the Startup India Programs section
6. Submit proposal detailing climate innovation, impact, and scalability
7. Await evaluation and selection by the jury panel

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications accepted year-round based on challenge cycles
