# Startup India Climate Action Challenge

## Overview

Startup India Climate Action Challenge is a program under the Startup India initiative aimed at supporting startups working on climate technology and green innovation. It provides a platform for startups to showcase solutions addressing climate change, with opportunities for funding, mentorship, and visibility. The challenge is part of broader efforts to promote sustainable entrepreneurship and align with national climate goals.

## Objective

- Identify and support innovative startups working on climate change mitigation and adaptation
- Provide a platform for green-tech startups to showcase scalable solutions
- Facilitate access to funding, mentorship, and investor connections for climate-focused ventures
- Promote sustainable entrepreneurship aligned with India’s climate action goals
- Strengthen the CleanTech and GreenTech ecosystem through targeted challenges and recognition

**startups**, **climate-tech startups**, **green-tech startups**

## Eligibility

Startups must be DPIIT-recognised, working on innovative solutions in climate technology, green technology, or related sectors such as renewable energy, energy efficiency, waste management, or sustainable agriculture. The entity should be incorporated as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or partnership firm, with turnover not exceeding INR 200 crore (INR 300 crore for DeepTech) and within 10 years…


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Recognition under Startup India, access to funding schemes like Seed Fund Scheme and Fund of Funds, mentorship via MAARG, investor connect opportunities, tax benefits under Section 80-IAC, IPR fast-tracking with 80% rebate on patent filing, easier public procurement norms, and self-certification under labour and environmental laws.
