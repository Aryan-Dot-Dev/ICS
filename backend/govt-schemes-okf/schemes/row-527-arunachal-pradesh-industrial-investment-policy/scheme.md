# Arunachal Pradesh Industrial Investment Policy

## Overview

The Arunachal Pradesh Industrial Investment Policy is a state-level initiative aimed at promoting industrial growth and investment within the state of Arunachal Pradesh. It seeks to create a conducive environment for industries by offering incentives, infrastructure support, and streamlined regulatory processes. The policy targets both new and existing industrial units to boost economic development, generate employment, and enhance the state's contribution to national manufacturing output.

## Objective

- Promote sustainable industrial development in Arunachal Pradesh
- Attract domestic and foreign investment through fiscal and non-fiscal incentives
- Generate employment opportunities for local youth
- Develop infrastructure to support industrial clusters and special economic zones
- Encourage setting up of agro-based, mineral-based, and eco-friendly industries
- Simplify regulatory procedures and ensure single-window clearance for investors
- Enhance skill development and entrepreneurship in the industrial sector
- Ensure balanced regional development by promoting industries in backward areas

## Important Conditions

- Subsidy claims are subject to availability of funds under the state budget
- Benefits are withdrawn if unit ceases production within 5 years of commencement
- No double claiming of similar subsidies from central and state schemes for the same expenditure
- Environmental and labour law compliance is mandatory throughout the subsidy period
- Transfer of ownership without government approval leads to clawback of benefits
