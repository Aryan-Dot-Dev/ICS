## Process

1. Submit application online via the Arunachal Pradesh State Portal or designated industrial investment portal.
2. Upload project report, incorporation documents, land ownership/lease details, and environmental clearances.
3. Application is scrutinized by the District Industries Centre (DIC) for preliminary eligibility.
4. Forwarded to the State Level Empowered Committee for final approval.
5. Upon approval, letter of intent is issued.
6. After commencement of commercial production, submit claim documents for subsidy disbursement.
7. Claims are verified by the Directorate of Industries before release of funds.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications accepted throughout the year subject to budget availability.
