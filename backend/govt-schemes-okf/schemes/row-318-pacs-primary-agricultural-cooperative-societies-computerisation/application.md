## Process

1. PACS submits a project proposal for computerisation to the District Central Cooperative Bank (DCCB) or State Cooperative Bank (SCB).
2. The proposal is forwarded to the National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD) for scrutiny and sanction.
3. Upon approval, funds are released in installments based on progress.
4. PACS procures the approved hardware and software through authorized vendors.
5. Installation and commissioning are carried out followed by staff training.
6. Post-implementation review is conducted to assess utilization and impact.
