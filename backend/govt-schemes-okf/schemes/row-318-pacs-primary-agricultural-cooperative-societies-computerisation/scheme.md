# PACS (Primary Agricultural Cooperative Societies) Computerisation

## Overview

The PACS (Primary Agricultural Cooperative Societies) Computerisation scheme aims to modernize and strengthen the functioning of Primary Agricultural Cooperative Societies across India by providing them with computer hardware, software, and networking facilities. The initiative seeks to improve operational efficiency, enhance service delivery to farmers, and enable better financial management and record-keeping at the grassroots level. It supports the digital transformation of cooperatives to ensure transparency, accountability, and timely access to credit and other services for rural communities.

## Objective

- To computerize the operations of Primary Agricultural Cooperative Societies (PACS)
- To improve the efficiency and effectiveness of PACS in serving farmers
- To enable real-time data management and financial accounting in cooperatives
- To enhance access to credit and other services for rural populations
- To strengthen the cooperative movement through technological upgradation
- To ensure transparency and accountability in PACS operations

## Eligibility

Primary Agricultural Cooperative Societies (PACS) that are functionally active and willing to adopt computerisation for their operations are eligible under the scheme. Societies must have a minimum membership base and demonstrate viability for sustained operations post-computerisation.
