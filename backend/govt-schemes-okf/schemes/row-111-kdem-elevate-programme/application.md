## Process

1. Visit the official Elevate Karnataka portal at https://elevate.karnataka.gov.in
2. Register on the portal by creating a login using email and mobile number
3. Fill in the online application form with details about the startup, founders, product, and traction
4. Upload required documents including incorporation certificate, product details, and team profiles
5. Submit the application and await screening by the evaluation committee
6. Shortlisted startups will be invited for a pitch presentation or interview
7. Final selection is based on innovation, scalability, team strength, and market potential
8. Selected startups will receive a grant disbursement letter and further instructions for fund utilization

## Deadlines

- Applications are accepted in periodic cycles; specific dates are announced on the portal. Interested startups are advised to check the website regularly for upcoming application windows.
