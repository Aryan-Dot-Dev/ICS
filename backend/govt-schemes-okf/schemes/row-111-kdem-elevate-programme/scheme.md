# KDEM Elevate Programme

## Overview

The KDEM Elevate Programme is a state-level initiative by the Karnataka government to support and nurture startups through financial assistance, mentorship, and market access. It aims to identify high-potential early-stage startups and provide them with seed funding and incubation support to scale their operations. The programme focuses on innovation-driven enterprises across sectors and offers a structured pathway from idea to market readiness. Selected startups receive grants, mentorship, and access to networking opportunities to enhance their growth trajectory.

## Objective

- Identify and support high-potential early-stage startups in Karnataka
- Provide seed funding to validate and scale innovative business ideas
- Offer mentorship and guidance from industry experts and mentors
- Facilitate market access and networking opportunities for startups
- Strengthen the startup ecosystem in Karnataka through structured incubation

## Key Features

- Grant/assistance per entity: Up to INR 50 lakh
- Geographic scope: Karnataka only (state-level)
- Deadline: Applications are accepted in periodic cycles; specific dates are announced on the portal. Interested startups are advised to check the website regularly for upcoming application windows.
- Portal: https://elevate.karnataka.gov.in
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

Startups must be registered in Karnataka, have a minimum viable product (MVP) or prototype, be incorporated as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm, and not have received more than INR 50 lakh in funding prior to application. The startup should be working on an innovative product, service, or process with scalability potential.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Selected startups receive a grant of up to INR 50 lakh, access to mentorship from domain experts, incubation support at recognized centers, assistance in fundraising, and opportunities to participate in investor meets and demo days. The programme also provides legal, accounting, and technical support to help startups comply with regulatory requirements and improve operational efficiency.
