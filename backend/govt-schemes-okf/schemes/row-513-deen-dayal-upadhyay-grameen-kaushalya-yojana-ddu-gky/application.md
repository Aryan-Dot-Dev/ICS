## Process

1. Identify eligible candidates from NRLM/SECC 2011 lists at the village level.
2. Mobilize and counsel youth to enroll in DDU-GKY training programmes.
3. Enroll candidates through registered Project Implementing Agencies (PIAs).
4. PIAs submit candidate details and training plans to the State Skill Development Mission (SSDM).
5. Obtain approval from SSDM and commence training as per approved under DDU-GKY norms.
6. Ensure placement of candidates post-training and report outcomes for reimbursement.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year based on training cycles and PIA availability.
