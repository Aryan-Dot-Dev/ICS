# Deen Dayal Upadhyay Grameen Kaushalya Yojana (DDU-GKY)

## Overview

Deen Dayal Upadhyay Grameen Kaushalya Yojana (DDU-GKY) is a placement-linked skill training programme under the Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India, aimed at transforming rural poor youth into an economically independent and globally relevant workforce. The scheme focuses on enhancing employability through skill development in various sectors, ensuring job placements for trained candidates, and promoting sustainable livelihoods in rural areas.

## Objective

- To enhance employability of rural poor youth through skill training
- To ensure placement of trained candidates in wage employment
- To promote convergence with other government programmes for sustainable livelihoods
- To strengthen institutional capacity for skill development at state and district levels
- To monitor and evaluate training outcomes and placement tracking
- To foster public-private partnerships in skill development initiatives


## Target Beneficiaries

**rural poor youth**, **SC/ST**, **women**, **minorities**, **persons with disabilities**

## Eligibility

The scheme targets rural poor youth aged 15-35 years from families covered under the National Rural Livelihoods Mission (NRLM) or Socio Economic and Caste Census (SECC) 2011, including SC/ST, women, minorities, and persons with disabilities, who are seeking skill training and wage employment opportunities.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Benefits include free skill training in industry-relevant trades, placement assistance with minimum wage guarantee, post-placement support for retention, accommodation and food during training, travel support, and certification recognized by the National Council for Vocational Training (NCVT) or State Council for Vocational Training (SCVT).

## Important Conditions

- Training must be conducted only by registered and approved Project Implementing Agencies (PIAs)
- Placement is mandatory for reimbursement;
- training without placement outcomes may not be funded
- Beneficiaries must be from rural poor households as per SECC 2011 or NRLM
- Training duration and trades must align with DDU-GKY approved norms and market demand
