# India Game Developer Conference (IGDC) Support

## Overview

The India Game Developer Conference (IGDC) Support scheme is a government initiative under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) aimed at supporting the growth of the game development industry in India. The scheme provides financial and non-financial support to game developers, startups, and MSMEs in the gaming sector to foster innovation, enhance competitiveness, and promote India as a global hub for game development. It includes financial assistance for participation in the India Game Developer Conference and related activities to strengthen the domestic gaming ecosystem.

## Objective

- To promote innovation and growth in the Indian game development sector
- To support Indian game developers and startups in participating in the India Game Developer Conference (IGDC)
- To enhance the global competitiveness of Indian gaming products and services
- To foster a robust ecosystem for game development in India through financial and technical support
- To encourage collaboration between Indian game developers and international stakeholders
- To promote skill development and capacity building in the gaming sector

## Important Conditions

- Reimbursement is only for testing and certification charges incurred at TEC/NCCS designated/recognized labs or NABL-accredited labs (if no TEC-designated CAB is available for a particular test facility)
- Product certificate (MTCTE/Voluntary certification) must be issued after the scheme implementation guidelines or notified date
- Reimbursement claims must be submitted within 3 months of issue of product certificate by TEC
- Reimbursement will not be considered for control tests related to product R&D
- Maximum reimbursement per entity is capped at INR 50 lakhs for the scheme duration
- The scheme is operational for two years from date of implementation or until funds of INR 25 crores are fully utilized, whichever occurs earlier
