## Process

1. Visit the online portal at https://tec.gov.in/tcrs
2. Register as an applicant (Start-up or MSE)
3. Fill in the application form with details of the applicant and the game development project
4. Upload required documents including Certificate of Incorporation, DPIIT/Udyam Registration, audited financial statements, test reports, product certificates, invoices, and undertakings
5. Submit the application online before the 20th of the last month of the quarter
6. Await screening by the Telecom Centres of Excellence (TCOE)
7. Upon clearance, the application is forwarded to the Technical Evaluation Committee for evaluation
8. After technical evaluation, the proposal is sent to the Administrative Committee for approval
9. Upon approval, funds are disbursed by the Telecom Centres of Excellence (TCOE) within 7 days of receipt from Digital Bharat Nidhi (DBN)

## Deadlines

- Applications can be submitted through the online portal on or before the 20th of the last month in the quarter.
