# APEDA Export Facilitation for Organic Products

## Overview

APEDA Export Facilitation for Organic Products is a scheme under the Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority (APEDA) aimed at promoting the export of organic products from India. It supports exporters through certification, market access, and participation in international trade fairs. The scheme focuses on enhancing the global competitiveness of Indian organic produce by providing technical and financial assistance.

## Objective

- Promote export of organic agricultural and processed food products
- Facilitate certification under National Programme for Organic Production (NPOP)
- Provide market access through participation in international trade fairs
- Offer financial assistance for infrastructure and quality development
- Strengthen supply chain and export readiness for organic products
- Enhance brand visibility of Indian organic products in global markets

## Eligibility

Exporters of APEDA-scheduled organic products with valid Import-Export Code (IEC) and Registration-cum-Membership Certificate (RCMC) from APEDA are eligible. Farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), MSMEs, and agri-startups engaged in organic production and processing can apply. Applicants must comply with NPOP standards and have a valid organic certification.
