## Process

1. Obtain Import-Export Code (IEC) from DGFT.
2. Apply for Registration-cum-Membership Certificate (RCMC) from APEDA via https://apeda.gov.in/Registration-Procedure.
3. Ensure organic certification under NPOP.
4. Submit application for financial assistance under the relevant FAS component (Market Development, Quality Development, or Export Infrastructure).
5. Upload required documents including IEC, RCMC, organic certificate, project report, and financials.
6. Await scrutiny and approval by APEDA.
7. Upon approval, submit bank guarantee and receive sanction letter.
8. Implement project and submit utilization report with expenditure details.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to fund availability.
