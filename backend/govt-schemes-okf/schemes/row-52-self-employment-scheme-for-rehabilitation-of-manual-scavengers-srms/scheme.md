# Self Employment Scheme for Rehabilitation of Manual Scavengers (SRMS)

## Overview

The Self Employment Scheme for Rehabilitation of Manual Scavengers (SRMS) is a Central Sector Scheme launched in January 2007 by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India, aimed at rehabilitating manual scavengers and their dependents by providing them with alternative livelihood opportunities through skill development and financial assistance for self-employment ventures. The scheme focuses on identifying and surveying manual scavengers, providing them with training, and facilitating access to loans and subsidies for setting up sustainable self-employment units. It operates through a network of State Channelising Agencies (SCAs) of the National Safai Karamcharis Finance and Development Corporation (NSKFDC), which is the nodal agency for implementation. The scheme has been revised and strengthened over time, with the latest revision effective from 2021-22 to enhance outreach and effectiveness.

## Objective

- To rehabilitate manual scavengers and their dependents by providing alternative livelihoods
- To eliminate the practice of manual scavenging through skill development and training
- To provide financial assistance in the form of loans and subsidies for setting up self-employment units
- To strengthen the institutional mechanism for identification, survey, and rehabilitation of manual scavengers
- To ensure convergence with other social welfare schemes for holistic rehabilitation
- To monitor and track the progress of beneficiaries post-rehabilitation

## Eligibility

The scheme covers manual scavengers as defined under the Prohibition of Employment as Manual Scavengers and their Rehabilitation Act, 2013, and their dependents. Eligible individuals include those who have been engaged in manual scavenging, identified through surveys conducted by State Governments or ULBs, and are willing to undergo training and take up self-employment ventures. ## Important Conditions

- Financial assistance is subject to the availability of funds under the scheme
- Beneficiaries must not have availed benefits under any other central/state government self-employment scheme for the same activity
- The subsidy component is contingent upon timely repayment of the loan amount as per schedule
- Projects must be economically viable and technically feasible to qualify for assistance
- Beneficiaries are required to undergo mandatory skill training before fund disbursement
