## Process

1. Identification of manual scavengers through surveys conducted by State Governments or Urban Local Bodies (ULBs).
2. Motivation and counseling of identified beneficiaries to opt for rehabilitation under SRMS.
3. Selection of suitable self-employment venture based on skills, local market demand, and training undergone.
4. Submission of project proposal to the concerned State Channelising Agency (SCA) of NSKFDC.
5. Appraisal of the project by the SCA for technical feasibility, financial viability, and beneficiary capacity.
6. Sanction of loan and subsidy components by the SCA upon approval.
7. Disbursement of funds in stages as per project progress.
8. Monitoring and handholding support provided by SCA/NSKFDC during implementation and initial operation.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds and identification of beneficiaries.
