# She Means Business – Digital Skills for Women

## Overview

She Means Business – Digital Skills for Women is a global initiative by Meta (Facebook) aimed at empowering women entrepreneurs through digital skills training, mentorship, and networking opportunities. The program focuses on helping women start, grow, and scale their businesses using digital tools and platforms. It provides free online courses, community support, and access to resources tailored to women-led enterprises.

## Objective

- Empower women entrepreneurs with digital skills
- Support women in starting and growing their businesses
- Provide access to mentorship and peer networks
- Enable use of digital tools for business growth
- Foster inclusive economic participation of women
