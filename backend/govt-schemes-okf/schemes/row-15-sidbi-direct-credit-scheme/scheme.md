# SIDBI Direct Credit Scheme

## Overview

The SIDBI Direct Credit Scheme provides direct lending products to MSMEs through various loan offerings such as project funding, machinery loans, working capital, and green finance. These schemes aim to fill credit gaps in the MSME sector by offering customized, innovative, and scalable financial solutions. The scheme supports MSMEs in expansion, modernization, technology upgradation, and sustainable growth through collateral-free or secured lending options with quick sanctions and competitive interest rates.

## Objective

- Fill existing credit gaps in the MSME sector
- Provide demonstrative and innovative lending products
- Support expansion, modernization, and technology upgradation of MSMEs
- Enable scalable and globally competitive growth
- Enhance financial accessibility through tailored credit solutions
- Promote sustainable development and green finance initiatives
- Support MSME clusters through infrastructure development
- Facilitate access to capital for deeper integration into domestic and global value chains

## Eligibility

Eligibility varies by product but generally includes MSME units in operation for a minimum period (e.g., 2 years for project funding, 3 years for machinery loan), mandatory Udyam & GST registration, no defaults to banks/FIs, audited accounts, and cash profit in recent financial years. ## Benefits

Benefits include quick sanction (in 48 hours for some products), digital and paperless processing, competitive interest rates, collateral-free options (e.g., GST Sahay), up to 100% financing with security (e.g., machinery loan), flexible repayment tenures, moratorium periods, end-to-end digital processes, and support for women entrepreneurs and socially backward sections. ## Important Conditions

- Loan amount subject to credit assessment and eligibility
- Collateral may be required depending on the product and risk profile
- Tenure and interest rates vary by product and borrower profile
- Some products are pilot-based (e.g., GST Sahay for JAKs, FPS Sahay)
- Default to banks/FIs disqualifies applicant
- Minimum vintage of business operations required (varies by product)
- Staff and senior citizens may get additional interest on fixed deposits, not applicable to loan schemes
- Fixed deposit schemes are separate from direct credit lending
