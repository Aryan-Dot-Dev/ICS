# National Programme for Bovine Breeding & Dairy Dev (NPBBD)

## Overview

The National Programme for Bovine Breeding & Dairy Development (NPBBD) is a centrally sponsored scheme aimed at enhancing milk production and productivity of bovines through genetic improvement, conservation of indigenous breeds, and development of dairy infrastructure. It focuses on strengthening semen stations, promoting artificial insemination, and supporting breed improvement programmes across the country. The scheme also supports the establishment of dairy cooperatives and milk processing units to ensure better returns for farmers.

## Objective

- To enhance milk production and productivity of bovines through genetic improvement
- To conserve and develop indigenous bovine breeds
- To strengthen semen production and distribution infrastructure
- To promote artificial insemination and natural service coverage
- To support dairy cooperative societies and milk processing units
- To provide training and capacity building for stakeholders in bovine breeding

## Eligibility

Eligible beneficiaries include individual farmers, livestock rearers, dairy cooperative societies, self-help groups, NGOs, and state livestock development boards involved in bovine breeding and dairy development activities, subject to verification by the State Implementing Agency.

## Benefits

Financial assistance for setting up semen stations, strengthening existing semen stations, procurement of breeding bulls, implementation of progeny testing and pedigree selection programmes, establishment of embryo transfer technology (ETT) labs, training of technicians and farmers, and support for dairy cooperative infrastructure and milk processing units.
