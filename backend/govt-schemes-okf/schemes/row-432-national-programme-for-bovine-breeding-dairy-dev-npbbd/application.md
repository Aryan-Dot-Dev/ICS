## Process

1. Identify the relevant component under NPBBD for which assistance is sought.
2. Prepare a detailed project proposal as per the guidelines issued by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying.
3. Submit the proposal to the State Implementing Agency (SIA) or State Livestock Development Board.
4. The SIA forwards the proposal to the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Government of India for appraisal and approval.
5. Upon approval, funds are released in installments based on utilization and submission of progress reports.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — proposals can be submitted throughout the year subject to availability of funds and approval timelines.
