# Micro & Small Enterprises Cluster Development Programme (MSE-CDP)

## Overview

The Micro & Small Enterprises Cluster Development Programme (MSE-CDP) is a Government of India initiative aimed at enhancing productivity, infrastructure development, common facility centers, market access, and technology upgradation for MSE clusters. It provides integrated support through cluster development to strengthen competitiveness and ensure sustainable growth of micro and small enterprises. The scheme focuses on creating common facility centers, improving infrastructure, and facilitating technology adoption within clusters.

## Objective

- Enhance productivity of micro and small enterprises through cluster development
- Develop infrastructure and establish common facility centers for MSE clusters
- Improve market access for products and services of MSE clusters
- Facilitate technology upgradation and adoption within clusters
- Strengthen competitiveness and ensure sustainable growth of MSE sector

## Eligibility

Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs) engaged in viable economic activities, forming clusters or willing to form clusters for mutual benefit, with focus on sectors having potential for growth and employment generation. The scheme supports both new and existing clusters of MSEs across manufacturing and service sectors.
