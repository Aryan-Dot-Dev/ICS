## Process

1. Identify and form a cluster of micro and small enterprises with common economic interests.
2. Prepare a Detailed Project Report (DPR) outlining cluster profile, proposed interventions, expected outcomes, and budget.
3. Submit the DPR along with required documents to the nodal agency (Office of Development Commissioner, MSME) or State-level implementing agencies.
4. Project appraisal and approval by the Steering Committee.
5. Release of funds in installments based on project milestones and utilization certificates.
6. Implementation of approved interventions by the cluster Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV).
7. Monitoring, evaluation, and submission of progress reports.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — proposals can be submitted throughout the year subject to availability of funds and appraisal cycles.
