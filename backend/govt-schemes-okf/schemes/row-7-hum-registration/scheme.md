# HUM Registration

## Overview

The HUM Registration scheme is a state-level initiative by the Government of Haryana aimed at registering and recognizing entities under the Haryana Udyami Mitra (HUM) program. It serves as a formal enrollment mechanism for beneficiaries to access various state government schemes, subsidies, and support services. The registration enables individuals and enterprises to be part of Haryana's entrepreneurial and MSME development ecosystem.

## Objective

- To create a centralized database of beneficiaries for effective implementation of state schemes
- To facilitate access to financial and non-financial support under Haryana government programs
- To promote ease of doing business through digital registration and verification
- To enhance transparency and accountability in disbursement of government benefits
- To support inclusive growth by enabling marginalized groups to register and avail benefits

## Eligibility

Eligibility criteria for HUM Registration include individuals, startups, MSMEs, and enterprises based in Haryana, particularly those engaged in entrepreneurial activities, manufacturing, services, or agri-allied sectors. Priority may be given to women entrepreneurs, SC/ST categories, and persons with disabilities as per state government directives.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Registered beneficiaries gain access to state government schemes, subsidies, financial assistance, skill development programs, market linkages, and priority in government procurement. They also receive updates on new initiatives, eligibility for incubation support, and facilitation in obtaining clearances and approvals.
