## Process

1. Visit the official Haryana government portal or designated HUM registration platform.
2. Click on the HUM Registration or Udyami Mitra enrollment link.
3. Fill in the online application form with personal, business, and enterprise details.
4. Upload required documents such as identity proof, address proof, business registration, and PAN.
5. Submit the application and await verification by the concerned authority.
6. Upon approval, receive a unique HUM registration number and certificate.
