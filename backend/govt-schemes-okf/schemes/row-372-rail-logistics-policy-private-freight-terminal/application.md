## Process

1. Identify suitable railway siding or land for terminal development.
2. Submit a detailed project report (DPR) including technical, financial, and operational plans to the concerned Divisional Railway Manager (DRM).
3. Obtain in-principle approval from Railway Board after technical scrutiny.
4. Execute a Lease Agreement for land use.
5. Acquire necessary statutory clearances (environmental, local bodies, etc.).
6. Construct terminal infrastructure as per approved plans.
7. Commission operations and sign a Freight Terminal Agreement for traffic handling.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to availability of land and technical feasibility.
