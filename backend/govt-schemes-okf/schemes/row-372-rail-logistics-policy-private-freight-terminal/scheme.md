# Rail Logistics Policy – Private Freight Terminal

## Overview

The Rail Logistics Policy – Private Freight Terminal is a policy initiative by the Ministry of Railways aimed at enhancing freight logistics efficiency by encouraging private sector participation in developing and operating freight terminals. It seeks to decongest existing railway infrastructure, improve first and last-mile connectivity, and boosts across the Indian Railways network.

## Objective

- To enhance freight logistics efficiency through private sector participation
- To develop and operate private freight terminals for improved rail connectivity
- To decongest existing railway infrastructure and improve first/last-mile logistics
- To promote multimodal integration and shift freight from road to rail
- To attract private investment in rail logistics infrastructure
- To improve cargo handling capacity and reduce transit times
- To support Make in India and Atmanirbhar Bharat initiatives through better logistics


## Target Beneficiaries

**private operators**, **logistics companies**, **MSMEs**, **industries**

- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to availability of land and technical feasibility.

## Eligibility

Private companies, consortiums, SPVs, or logistics providers registered under the Companies Act, 2013 or LLP Act, 2008, with net worth as per RBI guidelines, and capable of meeting technical and financial criteria for developing and operating private freight terminals on railway land or siding.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Access to railway land for terminal development on long-term lease, priority in path allocation for freight trains, customs clearance facilitation, single-window clearance through Indian Railways, potential for revenue sharing, and integration with Dedicated Freight Corridors (DFCs) and industrial corridors.

- Private entities must bear all capital and operational expenditures
