# Agricultural Infrastructure Fund (AIF) — Application

## Channels

```yaml
application:
  mode:
    - online
  official_portal: "https://agriinfra.dac.gov.in/"
  implementing_agency: "Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India"
  deadline: "Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round."
```

## Process

1. Visit the AIF portal at https://agriinfra.dac.gov.in
2. Register as a beneficiary through the Beneficiary Registration section
3. Prepare a Detailed Project Report (DPR) using the template provided
4. Approach a participating lending institution (Commercial Banks, Cooperative Banks, RRBs, Small Finance Banks, NCDC, NBFCs etc.) with the DPR
5. The lending institution processes the loan application under the AIF scheme
6. Upon sanction, the loan is disbursed by the lending institution
7. Interest subvention and credit guarantee claims are submitted through the AIF portal

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round.
