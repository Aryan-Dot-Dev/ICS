## Process

1. Get nominated by the PMRF granting institute after meeting eligibility criteria.
2. Receive a link to the PMRF portal to submit application.
3. Submit academic background, transcripts, research experience/internship, CV, research proposal/statement of purpose, list of publications, and details of recommenders.
4. Await evaluation by a National Panel for each broad area.
5. Final selection by the National Coordination Committee (NCC) based on ranks and seat allocation.

## Deadlines

- Application windows vary: for December 2024 annual review, submission was 01st Nov 2024 to 07th Nov 2024; for May 2025 review, submission was 08-May-2025 to 14-May-2025 (with extension to 20-May-2025 for 1 scholar). Specific cycles have defined timelines; otherwise, admissions occur twice a year (May and December cycles).
