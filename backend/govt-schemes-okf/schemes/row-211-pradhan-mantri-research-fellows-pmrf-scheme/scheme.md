# Pradhan Mantri Research Fellows (PMRF) Scheme

## Overview

The Pradhan Mantri Research Fellows (PMRF) Scheme is a flagship initiative by the Ministry of Education (MoE) to support PhD scholars in Science and Engineering with attractive fellowships and research grants. It aims to attract top talent into doctoral research, build outstanding research ecosystems, and focus on innovative and challenging research problems. The scheme was announced in the Budget 2018-19 and is implemented through the National Coordinating Institute (NCI) and National Coordination Committee (NCC).

## Objective

- Support the very best talents in doctoral research in India with attractive fellowships
- Build and nurture outstanding research ecosystems in the country
- Focus on extremely challenging and innovative research problems
- Attract the best talent into research to realize the vision of development through innovation
- Improve the quality of research in various higher educational institutions

**PhD scholars in Science and Engineering**

## Eligibility

Candidates can apply through Direct Entry or Lateral Entry channels. For Direct Entry: (a) completed or pursuing final year of 4/5-year undergraduate or 5-year integrated M.Tech./2-year M.Sc./5-year UG-PG dual degree in Science and Technology from IISc/IITs/NITs/IISERs/IIEST and centrally funded IIITs with CGPA/CPI ≥ 8.0, OR (b) same from any other recognized Institute/University with CGPA ≥ 8…


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Attractive monthly fellowship (Year 1: ₹70,000; Year 2: ₹70,000; Year 3: ₹75,000; Year 4: ₹80,000; Year 5: ₹80,000), annual research grant of ₹2 lakhs per year (up to ₹10 lakhs for five years), mandatory TAship duty (teaching ~1 hour/week in non-PMRF institutes), annual review process, and certification upon completion.

## Important Conditions

- Scholars who upgrade from M.S./M.Tech.
- are not eligible for Direct Entry
- Integrated PhD students are not eligible for Direct Entry at the time of completion of Masters requirement
- A candidate who defers admission in a PhD programme cannot be considered for Direct Entry at the time of joining
- Research grant amount must be refunded if unspent
- Guideline ceases to apply after 31st Dec 2028
- No retrospective payments for thesis submissions on or after November 1, 2022
- TAship duty is mandatory;
- failure to complete may lead to fellowship reduction or discontinuation
- Can apply for Lateral Entry at most twice
