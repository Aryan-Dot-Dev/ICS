# Stand-Up India

## Overview

Stand-Up India (launched 05.04.2016) facilitates **composite bank loans
between ₹10 lakh and ₹1 crore** — inclusive of term loan and working
capital — to promote entrepreneurship among Scheduled Castes (SC),
Scheduled Tribes (ST) and women. The scheme mandates that every bank
branch support at least one SC/ST borrower **and** at least one woman
borrower for setting up a **greenfield enterprise** [S1][S2]. Handholding
support is available via the [Standup Mitra](https://www.standupmitra.in/)
portal (mentoring, facilitation, and connecting applicants to banks and
support agencies).

## Objective

To promote entrepreneurship among SC/ST and women; to facilitate greenfield
enterprise creation in manufacturing, services, trading and agri-allied
sectors; and to enable branch-level inclusion through mandated loan
support.

## Target Beneficiaries

- **SC or ST entrepreneurs**, and **women entrepreneurs** (aged 18+),
- setting up a **new (greenfield)** enterprise in manufacturing,
  processing, services, trading or agri-allied activity,
- where the applicant is not a defaulter to any bank/financial
  institution.

## Key Features

- Composite loan (term loan + working capital) between ₹10 lakh and
  ₹1 crore [S1]
- Margin money: up to 15% may be provided via central/state schemes or
  beneficiary contribution (per scheme norms on standupmitra.in)
- RuPay debit card for working capital withdrawal
- Handholding: mentoring, business-plan help, connections to
  training/market support through Standup Mitra [S1]
- Delivered by all bank branches; loans priced per bank policy
- Credit guarantee cover available (CGFSIL) subject to eligibility

## Eligibility

Summary — see [eligibility.md](eligibility.md): SC/ST or woman
entrepreneur, 18+, greenfield activity, not a bank defaulter.

## Benefits

Composite loan ₹10L–₹1Cr with handholding. Details:
[benefits.md](benefits.md).

## Documents

KYC, caste certificate (SC/ST), project report, business plan,
defaulter-status declarations. Details: [documents.md](documents.md).

## Application

Online at Standup Mitra, or directly at any bank branch; Udyami helpline
available. Details: [application.md](application.md).

## Important Conditions

- **Greenfield only**: the venture must be a first-time/new activity by
  the borrower — expansion of an existing business is not covered [S1]
- Bank credit appraisal applies; sanction is the bank's decision
- The SC/ST and woman branches of eligibility are alternative (any-of)

## Exclusions

Existing-business expansions, non-greenfield projects, defaulters.
