# Indian Institute of Food Processing Technology (IIFPT) Incubation

## Overview

The Indian Institute of Food Processing Technology (IIFPT) Incubation program supports entrepreneurs and startups in the food processing sector by providing infrastructure, mentorship, and technical guidance to develop innovative food products and technologies. It aims to foster innovation, enhance product development capabilities, and promote entrepreneurship in food processing through incubation support. The program is hosted by IIFPT, a premier institute under the Ministry of Food Processing Industries, Government of India.

## Objective

- To provide incubation support to food processing startups and entrepreneurs
- To offer infrastructure and technical facilities for product development and testing
- To provide mentorship and guidance in technology, business planning, and regulatory compliance
- To promote innovation in food processing technologies and value-added products
- To facilitate market linkages and commercialization of incubated products
- To support skill development and capacity building in food processing entrepreneurship

## Eligibility

The incubation program is open to individuals, startups, entrepreneurs, and MSMEs engaged in or planning to enter the food processing sector. Applicants must have an innovative idea, prototype, or early-stage venture related to food processing, value addition, preservation, or packaging. Preference may be given to those focusing on indigenous foods, waste utilization, or nutritional enhancement.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Incubatees receive access to IIFPT's pilot plant facilities, laboratories, and processing equipment for product development and testing. They benefit from technical mentorship by IIFPT scientists, guidance on FSSAI regulations and standards, support in prototype development, assistance with business planning and IPR, and networking opportunities with industry experts and potential investors.
