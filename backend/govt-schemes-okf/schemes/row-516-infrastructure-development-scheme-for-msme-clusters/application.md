## Process

1. Identify the cluster and form a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV).
2. Prepare a detailed project report including infrastructure needs, common facility requirements, and technology upgradation plans.
3. Submit the application through the Implementing Agency (State/Central Govt. organizations, NGOs) who need to provide land and 10% (5% in NER, J&K and Hill Areas) of Hard Intervention.
4. The Implementing Agency will forward the application to the Office of Development Commissioner (MSME) for scrutiny.
5. Upon approval, financial assistance of up to 80% (95% in NER, J&K and Hill Areas) of Hard Intervention cost, entire cost of Soft Intervention, Technical Agency fee and Implementing Agency fee is given by GoI.
