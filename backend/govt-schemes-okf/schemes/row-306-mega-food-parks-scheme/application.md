## Process

1. Form a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) as a Body Corporate registered under the Companies Act (State Government, State Government entities and Cooperatives are exempt from forming a separate SPV).
2. Ensure the SPV meets the conditions of the Scheme Guidelines.
3. Submit proposal for financial assistance.
4. Upon approval, funds are released to the SPV.
5. Develop the Mega Food Park with supply chain infrastructure including collection centers, primary processing centers, central processing centers, cold chain, and 25-30 fully developed plots for food processing units.

## Deadlines

- The scheme has been discontinued by the Government with effect from 01.04.2021 with provision for committed liabilities for ongoing projects only.
