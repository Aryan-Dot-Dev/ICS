# Green Ship Building Incentive Scheme

## Overview

The Green Ship Building Incentive Scheme is part of the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways' initiatives to promote sustainable shipbuilding in India. It aims to incentivize the construction of environmentally friendly vessels through financial assistance and policy support. The scheme aligns with national goals such as Maritime India Vision 2030 and Maritime Amrit Kaal Vision 2047 to enhance green shipping and reduce carbon emissions in the maritime sector.

## Objective

- Promote construction of green and environmentally sustainable ships
- Support India's maritime decarbonization goals
- Enhance competitiveness of Indian shipbuilding industry
- Encourage adoption of clean fuel and hybrid propulsion systems
- Strengthen domestic shipbuilding capacity and ancillary industries
- Align with Maritime India Vision 2030 and Maritime Amrit Kaal Vision 2047

Financial assistance for green shipbuilding projects, including subsidies for vessels built with green technologies; support for skill development and technology upgradation; promotion of indigenous manufacturing; enhancement of global competitiveness; and contribution to sustainable blue economy growth.

## Important Conditions

- Financial assistance is subject to budget availability and may be released in subsequent financial year if budget is not available
- Only works commenced after receipt of in-principle approval are eligible for assistance
- Projects must be completed within six years from final approval unless extension is approved by NSbM
- Applicant shipyards must have been operational for at least three years prior to September 24, 2025
- Shipyards must hold at least three of five qualifying asset categories (waterfront, dry berthing, wet berthing, basic facility, material handling)
