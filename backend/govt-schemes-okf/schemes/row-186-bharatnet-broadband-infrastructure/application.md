## Process

1. Identification of Gram Panchayats for inclusion in the BharatNet project.
2. Submission of proposals by state governments or implementing agencies to the Department of Telecommunications.
3. Technical and financial appraisal of the project by BBNL and DoT.
4. Approval of the project and allocation of funds.
5. Execution of civil works, laying of optical fibre, and installation of equipment.
6. Testing, commissioning, and handover of network infrastructure.
7. Monitoring and maintenance for service continuity.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — projects are sanctioned based on annual budget allocations and implementation readiness.
