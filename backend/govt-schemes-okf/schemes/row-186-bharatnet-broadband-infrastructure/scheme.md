# BharatNet Broadband Infrastructure

## Overview

BharatNet is a flagship initiative of the Government of India aimed at providing broadband connectivity to all Gram Panchayats (approximately 2.5 lakh) across the country. It seeks to bridge the digital divide by creating a robust, scalable, and affordable broadband infrastructure in rural and remote areas. The project is implemented in phases and leverages optical fibre technology to enable high-speed internet access for e-governance, education, health, and other socio-economic applications.

## Objective

- Provide broadband connectivity to all Gram Panchayats in the country
- Enable delivery of e-governance, e-health, e-education, and other public services
- Bridge the digital divide between urban and rural areas
- Support national digital initiatives like Digital India
- Create a scalable and future-ready broadband infrastructure
- Promote inclusive growth through affordable internet access

## Eligibility

The scheme targets Gram Panchayats as the primary unit for connectivity. Implementation is carried out by Bharat Broadband Network Limited (BBNL), a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) under the Department of Telecommunications. State governments and local bodies facilitate right-of-way and infrastructure sharing. Central and state funding is utilised based on a defined cost-sharing model.

## Benefits

High-speed broadband connectivity enabling access to digital services such as telemedicine, online education, e-agriculture, and digital banking. Supports implementation of government-to-citizen (G2C) and government-to-government (G2G) services. Enhances socio-economic development in rural areas by reducing information asymmetry and enabling digital entrepreneurship.
