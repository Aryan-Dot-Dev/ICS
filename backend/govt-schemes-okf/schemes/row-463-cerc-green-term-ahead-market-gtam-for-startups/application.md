## Process

1. Register as a participant in the Green Term Ahead Market (GTAM) through the power exchange platform approved by CERC.
2. Complete the necessary KYC and compliance requirements as per the exchange regulations.
3. Submit required documents including proof of startup registration, project details, and bank guarantees.
4. Participate in the bidding process for term-ahead renewable power contracts.
5. Upon successful bid, execute power purchase agreements and fulfill delivery obligations as per contract terms.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — GTAM sessions are conducted regularly as per the power exchange calendar
