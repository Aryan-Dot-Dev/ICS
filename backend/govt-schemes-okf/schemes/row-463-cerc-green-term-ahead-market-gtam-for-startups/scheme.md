# CERC Green Term Ahead Market (GTAM) for Startups

## Overview

The CERC Green Term Ahead Market (GTAM) for Startups is a market mechanism enabling startups to participate in renewable energy trading through term-ahead contracts. It facilitates price discovery and risk management for green power procurement. The scheme aims to promote renewable energy adoption among innovative enterprises by providing access to competitive, transparent bidding processes. Startups can bid for renewable power delivery over specified future periods, supporting India's clean energy transition goals.

## Objective

- Enable price discovery for renewable energy through term-ahead market mechanisms
- Facilitate risk management for green power procurement by startups
- Promote renewable energy adoption among innovative enterprises
- Provide access to competitive and transparent bidding processes for clean power
- Support India's clean energy transition through market-based mechanisms
- Encourage participation of new entrants in the renewable energy trading ecosystem

## Eligibility

Startups engaged in renewable energy projects or clean technology innovation that are registered under relevant government schemes and meet the technical and financial criteria specified in the Central Electricity Regulatory Commission (Connectivity and General Network Access to the inter-State Transmission System) Regulations, 2022 and its amendments.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Access to transparent price discovery for renewable power, ability to hedge against price volatility in green energy markets, opportunity to secure long-term power purchase agreements at competitive rates, facilitation of market entry for innovative clean technology firms, and support for meeting renewable purchase obligations through market instruments.
