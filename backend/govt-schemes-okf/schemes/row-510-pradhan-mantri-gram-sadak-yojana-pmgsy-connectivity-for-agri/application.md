## Process

1. State Governments identify eligible habitations and prepare Detailed Project Reports (DPRs) for new connectivity and upgradation works.
2. DPRs are scrutinized by the State Level Standing Committee (SLSC) and forwarded to the Ministry of Rural Development for clearance.
3. Upon approval, the Ministry allocates funds and issues sanction orders.
4. State Rural Roads Development Agencies (SRRDA) invite tenders through e-procurement systems for civil works.
5. Contracts are awarded to qualified contractors following transparent bidding procedures.
6. Works are executed under technical supervision of SRRDA and National Quality Monitors (NQMs).
7. Completed roads undergo joint inspection before being handed over to Panchayati Raj Institutions for maintenance.

## Deadlines

- Annual project proposals are submitted by States typically between October and December for the ensuing financial year; specific dates vary by State.
