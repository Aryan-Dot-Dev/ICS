# Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana (PMGSY) – Connectivity for Agri

## Overview

Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana (PMGSY) is a centrally sponsored scheme launched in 2000 to provide all-weather road connectivity to unconnected habitations in rural areas, thereby improving access to markets, education, and healthcare. The scheme focuses on constructing and upgrading rural roads to ensure year-round connectivity, with a special emphasis on habitations with a population of 500+ in plain areas and 250+ in special category states (North-East, Himalayan, tribal and desert areas). PMGSY aims to boost rural economic growth by enhancing agricultural productivity and non-farm employment opportunities through improved infrastructure.

## Objective

- Provide all-weather road connectivity to eligible unconnected habitations
- Upgrade existing rural roads to prescribed technical standards
- Ensure sustainable maintenance of constructed roads through community participation
- Facilitate access to socio-economic services such as education, healthcare, and markets
- Promote inclusive growth by connecting marginalized and remote rural populations
- Enhance agricultural productivity and rural non-farm employment through better connectivity

## Important Conditions

- Funding is contingent upon submission of utilization certificates and progress reports
- Cost overruns beyond sanctioned amounts are borne by the State Government
- Roads must be maintained by Panchayati Raj Institutions for a minimum of five years after construction
- Deviation from approved alignment or specifications requires fresh sanction
- The scheme does not cover urban roads or State Highways
