# iCreate – International Centre for Entrepreneurship and Technology

## Overview

iCreate – International Centre for Entrepreneurship and Technology is a state-level (Gujarat) technology business incubator supported by the Government of Gujarat and the Government of India. It operates as a public-private partnership to foster next-generation entrepreneurship by supporting tech-based startups through incubation, mentorship, funding, and market access. iCreate provides end-to-end support from idea to scale-up, including prototype development, seed funding, and accelerator programs, with a focus on sectors like EV, agritech, healthtech, and renewable energy.

## Objective

- Facilitate 'Next Generation Entrepreneurship' by supporting tech-based startups
- Empower Indian youth to pursue entrepreneurial dreams instead of seeking jobs
- Provide incubation, mentorship, and funding support at every stage of the entrepreneurial journey
- Leverage world-class innovation to address national priorities through centres of excellence
- Build a vibrant entrepreneurial ecosystem involving corporates, government, investors, and institutional partners
- Support prototype development and market readiness through rapid prototyping labs and validation
- Enable global collaboration via international accelerator programs like India Israel Innovation Accelerator (i3A)
- Disburse government grants and funding schemes available from central and state governments

## Important Conditions

- Funds are disbursed in tranches subject to progress review (e.g., NIDHI-PRAYAS, TEC grant)
- Equity may be taken by iCreate in certain programs (e.g., NIDHI-SSS: 7.5% equity at par)
- Some grants are interest-bearing debt to be repaid (e.g., NIDHI-SSS)
- NIDHI-EIR support discontinues if recipient raises external funding
- Government of Gujarat grants require startup to be registered as LLP or private limited company in Gujarat
- iCreate Idea Catalyst (iIC) batch timings are fixed (e.g., Batch 4 in July 2026)
- Spark-up Idea Fund is only for students
- NIDHI-SSS is for slightly mature startups (1-3 years old) with revenue preference
- Some programs require physical incubation at iCreate Campus
- Past batches do not guarantee future availability;
- check latest announcements
