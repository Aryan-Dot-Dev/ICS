## Process

1. Interested Second Level Fund Managers (SLFMs) express interest by contacting Dr Jyoti Sharma, Head, RDI Cell at sharma[dot]jyoti[at]nic[dot]in.
2. Existing NBFCs planning to establish AIFs are encouraged to contact DST for potential participation.
3. Industry stakeholders seeking funding must not submit proposals directly to DST.
4. All disbursements are routed exclusively through designated SLFMs via separate advertisement.
5. For more details, visit https://rdifund.anrf.gov.in/.
6. Applications are evaluated based on project viability, innovation, and alignment with strategic technology goals.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted throughout the year.
