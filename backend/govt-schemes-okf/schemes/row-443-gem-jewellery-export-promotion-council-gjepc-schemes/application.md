## Process

1. Login to www.gjepc.org and click on Registration
2. Fill New Registration form and submit
3. Once user registration is completed, user will receive an email with credentials
4. Login to www.gjepc.org
5. Start filling forms starting from Application form for Associate Membership
6. Payment of fees will be initiated through challan form via net banking/Debit card/Credit Card/NEFT
7. Print all forms from - Print Application/Challan form
8. Submit the following documents to Head office or respective regional office: Application form for Associate Membership, Application form for RCMC, challan form duly signed, Signature Slip, Self-attested IEC, Self-attested PAN and Partnership Deed/MoA & AoA, Self-attested MSME(UAN)/SSI/IM if applicable, Self-attested GST certificate (if applicable), Self-attested copy of PAN, Passport and Aadhar

## Deadlines
