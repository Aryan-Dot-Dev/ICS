# Gem & Jewellery Export Promotion Council (GJEPC) Schemes

## Overview

The Gem & Jewellery Export Promotion Council (GJEPC) is the apex body driving India's export-led growth in the gem and jewellery sector since 1966. It promotes Brand India, organizes major trade shows, facilitates government interaction, and supports MSMEs through various initiatives including Common Facility Centres and Jewellery Parks. GJEPC also runs CSR programs like Jewellers for Hope and health schemes such as Swasthya Ratna and Swasthya Kosh for industry workers.

## Objective

- Promote Brand India through international advertisements and publications
- Organize India's biggest trade shows: IIJS Premiere and IIJS Signature
- Facilitate better interaction between industry and Government of India on trade-related issues
- Undertake image-building exercises through international advertisements and audio-visuals
- Represent trade-friendly policies for inclusion in the EXIM Policy
- Uphold Diamond Integrity by executing the Kimberly Process Certification Scheme as the Nodal Agency
- Spread Education by running training institutes in six cities
- Provide MSMEs with affordable modern machines and tools at Common Facility Centres
- Provide health insurance for employees of member companies through Swasthya Ratna
- Pay back to society through Jewellers for Hope and Gem & Jewellery National Relief Foundation

## Important Conditions

- Membership fees vary based on export performance and are subject to GST at 18%
- One time admission fees of Rs. 7500/- plus GST is payable for new membership (not applicable for Associate Micro Membership)
- Associate Micro Membership is only for those with domestic turnover up to Rs. 40 lakhs and does not require GST certificate
- Documents must be self-attested and submitted to Head office or respective regional office
- Payment must be made via challan form through net banking, debit card, credit card, or NEFT
