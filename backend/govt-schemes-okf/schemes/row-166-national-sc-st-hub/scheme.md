# National SC/ST Hub

## Overview

The National SC/ST Hub (NSSH) is a central sector scheme launched by the Ministry of MSME, Government of India in October 2016, aimed at enhancing the capacity of SC/ST entrepreneurs and promoting an 'entrepreneurship culture' among the SC/ST population. It empowers SC/ST entrepreneurs to participate in public procurement and fulfill the mandated target of 4% procurement from SC/ST enterprises under the Public Procurement Policy. The scheme is implemented by the National Small Industries Corporation (NSIC) and includes various sub-schemes such as Special Credit Linked Capital Subsidy Scheme (SCLCSS), Capacity Building Training Programs, Special Marketing Assistance Scheme (SMAS), and multiple reimbursement schemes for bank loan processing, testing fees, export promotion council membership, and more.

## Objective

- Capacity enhancement of SC/ST entrepreneurs
- Promoting 'entrepreneurship culture' amongst the SC/ST population
- Empowering SC/ST population to participate in the public procurement process
- Fulfilling the mandated target of 4% procurement from SC/ST enterprises under Public Procurement Policy
- Facilitating credit linkages for SC/ST entrepreneurs
- Providing handholding and mentoring support
- Sharing of SC/ST MSEs data with CPSEs to enhance procurement from SC/ST entrepreneurs
- Undertaking outreach activities for the promotion of the scheme among SC/ST population

## Important Conditions

- Units availing subsidy under SCLCSS shall not be allowed to avail any other subsidy for procurement of the same plant & machinery and equipment from any other scheme of Central/State Government and vice-versa
- Financial assistance will not be admissible for candidates with less than 75% attendance in capacity building training programs
- The SC/ST entrepreneur and their ward will be eligible for a maximum of 2 courses in total in a financial year under the training fee reimbursement scheme
- Clubbing these components with any other scheme is not permitted;
- any SC-ST MSE availing reimbursement under all the above-mentioned components cannot avail reimbursement from any other government or private agency
- Industries covered under the RED category as per Classification of industries for consent management are not eligible for subsidy under SCLCSS
- Fabricated and second-hand plant and machinery shall not be eligible for consideration for subsidy under SCLCSS
