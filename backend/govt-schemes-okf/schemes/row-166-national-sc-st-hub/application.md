## Process

1. Identify the relevant sub-scheme (e.g., SCLCSS, reimbursement schemes, capacity building).
2. Ensure eligibility (SC/ST ownership, Udyam Registration, etc.).
3. Gather required documents (Udyam Registration, PAN card, caste certificate, proof of payment, bank statements, etc.).
4. Submit application either online via the NSSH website or through the concerned nodal agency (e.g., bank for SCLCSS, NSIC/NSSH office for reimbursements).
5. For reimbursement schemes, submit claim with supporting documents to the implementing agency (NSIC/NSSH) through PFMS.
6. Await verification and disbursement of funds to the beneficiary's bank account.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — claims can be submitted throughout the year; for SCLCSS, timeline for acceptance is one year from the reference date (date of release/disbursement of last installment of term loan).
