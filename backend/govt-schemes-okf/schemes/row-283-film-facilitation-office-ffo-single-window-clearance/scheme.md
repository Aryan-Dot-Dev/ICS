# Film Facilitation Office (FFO) – Single Window Clearance

## Overview

The Film Facilitation Office (FFO) – Single Window Clearance is a government initiative under the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting that provides a single point of contact for filmmakers seeking permissions and clearances for shooting films in India. It streamlines the process of obtaining various no-objection certificates (NOCs) and approvals from central and state government agencies, thereby reducing delays and administrative burden for film production activities across the country.

## Objective

- To provide a single window clearance system for film shooting permissions
- To coordinate with various central and state government agencies for issuing NOCs
- To reduce time and effort required for obtaining multiple clearances
- To facilitate smooth filming operations across India
- To promote ease of doing business in the media and entertainment sector


## Target Beneficiaries

**filmmakers**, **production houses**, **media and entertainment professionals**


## Key Features

- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — applications can be submitted throughout the year as per project requirements.
- Portal: https://ffo.gov.in
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

Filmmakers, production houses, or any entity intending to shoot films in India that require permissions from central or state government authorities for locations, drone usage, road closures, or other regulatory clearances.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Single point of contact for all film shooting-related permissions; coordination with ministries and departments such as Ministry of Defence, Ministry of Home Affairs, Ministry of Civil Aviation, Archaeological Survey of India, and state governments; time-bound processing of applications; reduced procedural complexity; support for both Indian and foreign film productions.

## Important Conditions

- FFO does not grant permissions itself but facilitates coordination with relevant agencies
- Final approval rests with the respective concerned authorities (e.g., MoD, MHA, ASI, etc.)
- Processing time depends on response time from individual agencies
- Certain clearances (e.g., drone flights, border areas) may have additional restrictions or lead times
