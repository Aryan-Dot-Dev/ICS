# Cold Storage Infrastructure Subsidy (NHB)

## Overview

The Cold Storage Infrastructure Subsidy (NHB) is a scheme under the National Horticulture Board aimed at supporting the development of cold storage infrastructure to reduce post-harvest losses in horticulture produce. It provides financial assistance for setting up cold storage facilities to enhance the shelf life and marketability of fruits and vegetables. The scheme is part of broader initiatives to strengthen the horticulture value chain and improve farmers' income through better storage and value addition.

## Objective

- Reduce post-harvest losses of horticulture produce through development of cold storage infrastructure
- Enhance shelf life and marketability of fruits and vegetables
- Support farmers in realizing better prices by enabling storage and staggered market release
- Promote private investment in cold chain infrastructure
- Strengthen the horticulture value chain from production to consumption
- Encourage adoption of modern and energy-efficient cold storage technologies

## Important Conditions

- Subsidy is released only after verification of project milestones and utilization of funds
- Physical inspection may be conducted before and after project completion
- Beneficiary must maintain proper books of accounts and submit periodic reports
- Project must be completed within the stipulated timeline as per approval
- Any deviation from approved DPR may lead to rejection or recovery of subsidy
- Farmer component must be maintained at minimum 40% of total project cost throughout
