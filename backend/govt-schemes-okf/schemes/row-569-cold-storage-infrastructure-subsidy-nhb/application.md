## Process

1. Submit Expression of Interest (EoI) online at https://cdp.nhb.gov.in for cluster-based projects including cold storage.
2. Await shortlisting based on concept note evaluation.
3. Submit Detailed Project Report (DPR) within the stipulated timeline after shortlisting.
4. DPR undergoes technical and financial appraisal by NHB or authorized agencies.
5. Upon approval, receive sanction letter and proceed with financial closure.
6. Implement the project as per approved DPR.
7. Submit utilization reports and completion certificates for subsidy disbursement in milestones.

## Deadlines
