## Process

1. Register on e-Sushrut Clinic website: https://ehmis-lite.in/AHIMSG5/hissso/logoutLogin
2. Sign up using your credentials to create an account
3. Generate or Validate Healthcare Professional Registry (HPR) ID: New Users: Generate your HPR ID using your Aadhaar ID; Existing Users: Validate your HPR ID using your Aadhaar-linked mobile number or HPR-linked mobile number
4. Generate or Validate Health Facility Registry (HFR) ID: New Facilities: Generate your HFR ID by providing essential details such as facility name, location, operational status, services offered, ownership details etc.; Existing Facilities: Validate your facility details like HFR ID, Facility Name, State and District.
5. Complete Onboarding & Get Started: Fill in the required details on e-Sushrut Clinic to complete the onboarding process
