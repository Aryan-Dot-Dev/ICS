## Process

1. Identify project need and prepare detailed project report (DPR)
2. Submit proposal to the concerned wing of the Department of Water Resources (e.g., Flood Management Wing, Ground Water Wing, River Basin Management Wing)
3. Undergo technical appraisal by Central Water Commission or relevant technical body
4. Obtain investment clearance from Planning Commission or Ministry of Water Resources
5. Receive sanction order and release of funds in installments
6. Implement project under monitoring and evaluation framework
7. Submit utilization certificates and completion reports.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — proposals are accepted throughout the year based on budget availability and project readiness.
