# National Water Resource Development Programme

## Overview

The National Water Resource Development Programme is a comprehensive initiative under the Department of Water Resources, River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation, Ministry of Jal Shakti, aimed at enhancing water security through infrastructure development, conservation, and management across river basins. It encompasses activities such as hydropower and irrigation project investigations, flood control, river management, and ground water regulation. The programme supports national water security goals by integrating technical, financial, and community-based interventions.

## Objective

- Enhance physical access to water for irrigation and expand cultivable area under assured irrigation
- Improve on-farm water use efficiency and introduce sustainable water conservation practices
- Abate pollution and rejuvenate river systems, particularly the Ganga, through integrated conservation missions
- Provide financial assistance for flood control and river management projects to state governments
- Conduct aquifer mapping, rejuvenation, and regulation of ground water extraction
- Promote awareness about optimal water resource development and water conservation through IEC activities
- Manage shared river systems with neighbouring countries for hydrological observations and flood forecasting
- Support investigation and development of water resource projects across river basins for hydropower and irrigation

State governments, union territories, local bodies, NGOs, research institutions, and central agencies involved in water resource development, irrigation, flood control, ground water management, and river basin planning are eligible to participate in the programme based on project proposals and technical feasibility.

## Benefits

Financial assistance for project implementation, technical support for planning and execution, capacity building through training and workshops, improved irrigation efficiency, enhanced ground water sustainability, flood mitigation, pollution abatement in rivers, increased water availability for drinking and agriculture, and promotion of community participation in water conservation initiatives.
