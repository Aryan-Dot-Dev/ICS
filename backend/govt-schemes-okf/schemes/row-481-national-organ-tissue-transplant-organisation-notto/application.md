## Process

1. Visit the NOTTO website at https://notto.abdm.gov.in/register
2. Select preferred language (English, Assamese, Bengali, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Urdu)
3. Enter Aadhaar number (12 digits) and mobile number linked to ABHA
4. Verify Aadhaar via OTP authentication with UIDAI
5. Read and accept the terms and conditions, including consent to share Aadhaar-demographic data with NHA for ABHA creation
6. Submit the Form 7 for Organ or Tissue Pledging as per THOTA 1994 and Rules 2014
7. Receive a downloadable pledge certificate with QR code for verification
8. Optionally verify certificate at https://notto.abdm.gov.in/verify-certificate by scanning the QR code

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Registrations are accepted year-round.
