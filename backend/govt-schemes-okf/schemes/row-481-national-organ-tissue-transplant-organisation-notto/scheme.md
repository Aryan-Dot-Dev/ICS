# National Organ & Tissue Transplant Organisation (NOTTO)

## Overview

The National Organ & Tissue Transplant Organisation (NOTTO) is a government initiative under the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) that facilitates organ and tissue donation pledges through a national registry. It enables individuals aged 18 and above to voluntarily register as organ donors via Aadhaar-linked ABHA ID verification. The platform supports multilingual access and provides tools for verifying pledge certificates and viewing donation statistics across states and organs.

## Objective

- Establish a national registry for organ and tissue donation pledges
- Enable voluntary registration of organ donors through Aadhaar-based authentication
- Promote awareness about organ donation and its life-saving potential
- Facilitate verification of donation pledge certificates via QR code scanning
- Provide real-time dashboard analytics on donation registrations by state, organ type, age, and gender
- Ensure data privacy and compliance with the Aadhaar Act and ABDM guidelines
- Support integration with the National Digital Health Ecosystem (NDHE)

## Eligibility

Any person not less than 18 years of age, who voluntarily authorizes the removal of any of his organ and/or tissue after death, can register as a donor. Registration requires Aadhaar number and mobile number linked to ABHA, with consent to share demographic information with the National Health Authority (NHA) for ABHA creation and use in the National Digital Health Ecosystem.

## Important Conditions

- Registration is purely voluntary and does not guarantee organ retrieval at time of death
- Final organ donation consent is subject to family approval at the time of death
- Users must have an Aadhaar number and mobile number linked to ABHA to register
- Consent can be revoked at any time, but may not be actionable if organs have already been retrieved
- Data shared with NHA is governed by the Aadhaar Act, 2016 and ABDM policies
