# CSIR Technologies for MSMEs

## Overview

CSIR Technologies for MSMEs is a scheme under the Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) aimed at facilitating the transfer and adoption of CSIR-developed technologies by Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) to enhance their competitiveness, innovation capacity, and industrial growth through technology licensing, technical support, and collaborative R&D.

## Objective

- Facilitate transfer of CSIR-developed technologies to MSMEs
- Enhance technological capabilities and competitiveness of MSMEs
- Promote industrial application of CSIR R&D outcomes
- Support innovation and value addition in MSME sector
- Strengthen industry-science linkages for sustainable growth


## Target Beneficiaries

**MSME**

## Key Features

- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — applications can be submitted throughout the year based on technology availability and mutual agreement.
- Portal: https://www.csir.res.in
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) as defined under the MSMED Act, 2006, that are interested in adopting CSIR-developed technologies for commercialization or process improvement.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Access to CSIR-developed technologies through licensing, technical guidance and support for technology absorption, assistance in scaling up lab-scale processes to industrial scale, joint R&D opportunities, and facilitation of IPR-related matters.

## Important Conditions

- Technology transfer is subject to mutual agreement and IPR terms negotiated with CSIR laboratories
- Availability of specific technologies depends on ongoing R&D and lab priorities
- Financial and technical support is case-specific and not standardized
- MSMEs must demonstrate adequate absorptive capacity and commercialization potential
