## Process

1. Identify relevant CSIR technology through the CSIR technology portal or CSIR laboratories.
2. Contact the concerned CSIR laboratory or Technology Business Development Group (TBDG).
3. Discuss technology suitability, terms of transfer, and potential collaboration.
4. Negotiate and sign a technology transfer agreement or MoU.
5. Implement the technology with technical support from CSIR as per the agreement.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications can be submitted throughout the year based on technology availability and mutual agreement.
