# Atal Mission for Rejuvenation and Urban Transformation (AMRUT 1.0)

## Overview

Atal Mission for Rejuvenation and Urban Transformation (AMRUT 1.0) is a centrally sponsored scheme launched by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) to provide basic infrastructure services such as water supply, sewerage, septage management, storm water drainage, green spaces, and non-motorized urban transport in 500 selected cities across India. The mission aims to ensure that every household has access to a tap with assured water supply and a sewerage connection, thereby improving the quality of life in urban areas, particularly for the poor and disadvantaged.

## Objective

- Ensure that every household has access to a tap with assured supply of water and a sewerage connection
- Increase the amenity value of cities by developing greenery and well-maintained open spaces (e.g., parks)
- Reduce pollution by switching to public transport or constructing facilities for non-motorized transport (e.g., walking and cycling)
- Strengthen institutional and financial capacity of Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) for effective service delivery
- Promote reforms in urban governance and service delivery through incentive-based mechanisms

## Eligibility

The scheme covers 500 cities selected based on criteria including: all cities and towns with a population of over one lakh with notified Municipalities, including Cantonment Boards; all capital cities of States/Union Territories; cities classified as Heritage Cities by MoHUA under the HRIDAY scheme; and cities located on the stem of major rivers (between 5-50 km).
