# SAMPADA Scheme (Food Processing)

## Overview

The SAMPADA Scheme (Food Processing), now known as Pradhan Mantri Kisan SAMPADA Yojana as Pradhan Mantri Kisan SAMPADA Yojana (PMKSY), is an umbrella Central sector scheme approved by the Government of India on 03.05.2017 with a total allocation of Rs. 6000 crore for the implementation period 2016-2020, later extended till 31.03.2026 with an additional allocation of Rs. 4600 crore. It aims to create modern infrastructure with efficient supply chain management from farm gate to retail outlet, boost food processing sector growth, provide better returns to farmers, double farmers' income, create employment opportunities in rural areas, reduce agricultural wastage, increase processing levels, and enhance exports of processed foods. The scheme comprises seven component schemes including Mega Food Parks, Integrated Cold Chain, Agro-Processing Clusters, Creation of Backward and Forward Linkages, Creation/Expansion of Food Processing & Preservation Capacities, Food Safety and Quality Assurance Infrastructure, and Human Resources and Institutions.

## Objective

- Creation of modern infrastructure with efficient supply chain management from farm gate to retail outlet
- Provide a big boost to the growth of food processing sector in the country
- Help in providing better returns to farmers
- Double farmers' income
- Create huge employment opportunities especially in rural areas
- Reduce wastage of agricultural produce
- Increase the processing level of agricultural produce
- Enhance the export of processed foods

## Important Conditions

- Certain sub-schemes like Mega Food Park, Creation of Backward & Forward Linkages, Human Resource & Institution - Promotional Activities, Skill Development & HACCP have been discontinued in the 15th Finance Commission cycle with provision of committed liability only.
- Land must be owned or on registered leasehold with duration not less than 15 years.
- Surety Bond is required for private PIAs but not for Central/State Government Departments.
- Benefits are subject to compliance with terms in the approval letter and submission of utilization certificates.
- Funds are released in two instalments linked to specific milestones and documentation.
