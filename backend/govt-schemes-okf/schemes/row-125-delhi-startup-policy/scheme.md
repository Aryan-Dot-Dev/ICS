# Delhi Startup Policy

## Overview

The Delhi Startup Policy is a state-level initiative launched by the Government of Delhi to foster innovation, support early-stage startups, and promote entrepreneurship within the National Capital Territory. It aims to create a conducive ecosystem for startups through financial assistance, infrastructure support, regulatory simplification, and access to mentorship and markets. The policy focuses on sectors such as technology, manufacturing, services, and social enterprises, with special emphasis on women entrepreneurs and marginalized communities.

## Objective

- Promote innovation and entrepreneurship in Delhi
- Provide financial and non-financial support to early-stage startups
- Create incubation and co-working infrastructure
- Simplify regulatory compliance for startups
- Enhance access to markets and mentorship
- Support underrepresented groups including women and SC/ST entrepreneurs
- Strengthen industry-academia collaboration
- Position Delhi as a preferred startup destination in India


## Target Beneficiaries

**startups**, **women entrepreneurs**, **SC/ST**, **MSME**

## Key Features

- Fund size: ₹200 crore
- Grant/assistance per entity: Up to INR 10 Lakhs for product development; up to INR 2 Lakhs for patent/reimbursement
- Geographic scope: Delhi only (state-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds
- Portal: https://investdelhionline.com
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

Startups incorporated as a Private Limited Company, Limited Liability Partnership (LLP), or Registered Partnership Firm in Delhi, with at least one founder domiciled in Delhi, working on innovative products, services, or processes, and not older than 7 years from date of incorporation. The startup must have annual turnover not exceeding INR 25 Crore in any preceding financial year.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial support in the form of grants, reimbursement of patent and certification costs, access to subsidized incubation and co-working spaces, mentorship from industry experts, assistance in market access and government procurement, facilitation of funding linkages with investors and financial institutions, and regulatory handholding through single-window clearance.

## Important Conditions

- Grant is disbursed in tranches based on milestone achievement and submission of utilization certificates
- Startups receiving support under other central or state government schemes for the same purpose may not be eligible for duplicate benefits
- The grant must be utilized strictly for the approved purpose within the stipulated timeline
- Failure to achieve milestones or submit required reports may lead to recovery of funds
- The policy is subject to budgetary allocation and may be revised or withdrawn by the Government of Delhi
