## Process

1. Register on the Delhi Startup Policy portal (investdelhionline.com)
2. Fill the online application form with startup details and innovation description
3. Upload required documents including Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, GST registration, and address proof
4. Submit pitch deck or prototype details
5. Await preliminary screening by the Delhi Startup Policy Committee
6. Present before the evaluation committee for final assessment
7. Upon approval, sign agreement and submit utilization plan
8. Receive grant in tranches based on milestone completion

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds
