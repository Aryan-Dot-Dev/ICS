# Marketing Support & Services for Handicrafts

## Overview

The Marketing Support & Services for Handicrafts scheme aims to promote and market handicraft products through various initiatives such as participation in domestic and international exhibitions, organizing craft bazaars, and providing marketing assistance to artisans and handicraft exporters. It focuses on enhancing market access, brand building, and export promotion for the handicraft sector. The scheme supports individual artisans, self-help groups, NGOs, cooperatives, and private enterprises engaged in handicraft production.

## Objective

- Promote handicraft products in domestic and international markets
- Enhance market access for artisans and handicraft producers
- Support participation in national and international trade fairs and exhibitions
- Organize craft bazaars and marketing events to showcase handicrafts
- Provide financial assistance for marketing and publicity of handicrafts
- Strengthen export promotion of handicrafts through market development assistance
- Support branding and quality improvement initiatives for handicraft products

## Eligibility

Individual artisans, self-help groups, NGOs, cooperatives, and private enterprises engaged in the production of handicrafts are eligible to apply for benefits under the Marketing Support & Services for Handicrafts scheme. Applicants must be involved in handicraft activities and possess valid registration or recognition as handicraft producers where applicable.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial assistance for participation in domestic and international exhibitions, craft bazaars, and trade fairs; reimbursement of stall rent, travel expenses, and publicity costs; support for market development activities; assistance in branding and product promotion; facilitation of buyer-seller meets; and support for e-commerce initiatives and digital marketing of handicraft products.
