# National Industrial Corridor Programme (NICP)

## Overview

National Industrial Corridor Development Programme (NICP) is India's most ambitious infrastructure programme aiming to develop futuristic industrial cities in India which can compete with the best manufacturing and investment destinations in the world. The programme aims to create employment opportunities and economic growth leading to overall socio-economic development. It involves the development of 11 industrial corridors with 32 projects in 4 phases, including greenfield industrial smart cities and logistics hubs.

## Objective

- Develop futuristic industrial cities in India to compete with global manufacturing and investment destinations
- Create employment opportunities and economic growth leading to socio-economic development
- Develop industrial corridors with modern infrastructure and plug-and-play facilities
- Attract domestic and global investments in manufacturing and services
- Boost logistics and connectivity to make trade faster and cost-efficient
- Support employment generation and economic growth
- Develop state-of-the-art world-class infrastructure to promote local commerce and enhance investment
- Integrate next-generation technologies across infrastructure sectors to create smart cities

## Important Conditions

- The equity participation of NICDIT in any SPV shall not exceed 50% of the paid-up equity capital
- In private developer-led projects, transfer or sale or disinvestment of equity share would be permissible after a period of five years from the date of completion and operationalization of the project
- O&M Corpus Fund drawdown shall require approval of the SPV Board (including the nominee directors of NICDIT and the State Nodal Agency)
- Any surplus remaining in the O&M Corpus Fund after five years shall be transferred to the SPV’s general reserves
- The land transferred to the SPV shall be treated as equity contribution in all cases
- The ownership of land shall be transferred to the SPV within 3 months from the date of the approval of the project
- In the event of delay in transfer of land to the SPV within stipulated period, the approval shall be deemed to have been annulled.
- However, in case of bonafide reasons or genuine circumstances, an additional period of 3 months may be granted for such transfer by the NLSC
