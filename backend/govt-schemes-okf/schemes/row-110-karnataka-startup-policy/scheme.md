# Karnataka Startup Policy

## Overview

The Karnataka Startup Policy is a state-level initiative launched by the Government of Karnataka to foster innovation and entrepreneurship within the state. It aims to create a conducive ecosystem for startups by providing financial incentives, infrastructure support, regulatory simplification, and access to markets and mentorship. The policy focuses on sector-agnostic growth with special emphasis on technology-driven and social impact startups. It operates under the Department of IT, BT and S&T, Government of Karnataka.

## Objective

- To promote innovation and entrepreneurship across Karnataka
- To create 20,000 startups by 2025
- To generate 6 lakh direct and indirect employment opportunities
- To achieve INR 50,000 Crore in economic output from startups by 2025
- To establish at least one startup incubator in each district
- To promote women, SC/ST, differently abled, and rural entrepreneurship
- To develop sector-specific startup parks and innovation zones
- To simplify regulatory compliance and provide single-window clearance

## Important Conditions

- Financial support is subject to availability of funds under the annual budget allocation
- Startups must maintain primary operations in Karnataka for a minimum of 3 years after receiving support
- Funding is disbursed in tranches based on milestone achievement or audit compliance
- Benefits under the policy cannot be combined with similar state-level incentives for the same expenditure
- The policy is valid until 2025 unless extended by the Government of Karnataka
