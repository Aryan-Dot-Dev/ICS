# Integrated Wool Development Programme

## Overview

The Integrated Wool Development Programme (IWDP) is a centrally sponsored scheme under the Ministry of Textiles aimed at the holistic development of the wool sector in India. It focuses on improving productivity, quality, and value addition across the wool value chain, from sheep rearing to finished products. The programme supports activities such as breed improvement, processing, marketing, and skill development to enhance the competitiveness of Indian wool and wool products in domestic and international markets.

## Objective

- Improve the productivity and quality of indigenous wool through breed improvement and animal health management
- Strengthen the wool processing sector by modernizing processing units and introducing value addition
- Enhance marketing and promotion of wool and wool products through branding, market linkages, and participation in trade fairs
- Develop skills and provide training to stakeholders in the wool value chain
- Support the establishment of common facility centres for wool processing
- Encourage the use of wool in technical textiles and diversified applications
- Ensure sustainable and remunerative returns for sheep rearers and wool-based artisans
