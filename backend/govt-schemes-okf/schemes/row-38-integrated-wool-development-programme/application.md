## Process

1. Identify the relevant component of the IWDP under which the activity falls (e.g., breed improvement, processing, marketing).
2. Prepare a detailed project proposal including objectives, budget, implementation plan, and expected outcomes.
3. Submit the proposal to the State Wool Development Board or the designated implementing agency in the state.
4. The proposal is reviewed by the State Level Committee for technical and financial feasibility.
5. Upon approval, the implementing agency sanctions the subsidy amount.
6. The beneficiary implements the project as per the approved plan.
7. After completion, the beneficiary submits utilization certificates, bills, and completion report.
8. The subsidy is released after verification of documents and field inspection by the implementing agency.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — proposals can be submitted throughout the year subject to availability of funds and approval by the State Level Committee.
