# National Hydrology Project (NHP) Data Access for Startups

## Overview

The National Hydrology Project (NHP) Data Access for Startups initiative provides access to hydrological data and resources to support innovation in water technology. It aims to enable startups to leverage NHP's data infrastructure for developing solutions in water management, forecasting, and related domains. The program is part of the broader National Hydrology Project implemented by the Department of Water Resources, River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation under the Ministry of Jal Shakti.

## Objective

- Provide startups with access to hydrological data generated under the National Hydrology Project
- Support innovation in water technology through data-driven solutions
- Facilitate the use of NHP's data systems for applications in water resource management
- Promote entrepreneurship in the water sector by lowering barriers to data access

## Eligibility

Startups registered in India working on water technology, hydrology, or related domains are eligible to access data under the National Hydrology Project. Specific eligibility criteria may include validation of the startup's focus on water-related innovation and compliance with data usage policies.

## Benefits

Access to hydrological datasets, hydro-meteorological data, water resources information systems, and related tools developed under the National Hydrology Project. Startups can utilize this data for research, product development, and validation of water technology solutions.
