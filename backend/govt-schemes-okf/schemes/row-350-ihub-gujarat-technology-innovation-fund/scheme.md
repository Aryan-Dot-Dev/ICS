# iHub Gujarat – Technology Innovation Fund

## Overview

iHub Gujarat – Technology Innovation Fund is a state-level initiative under the Government of Gujarat aimed at fostering technology-driven innovation and entrepreneurship. The scheme provides financial and non-financial support to startups and innovators through a dedicated innovation fund. It focuses on scaling early-stage ventures with high growth potential in technology sectors. The program is managed by iHub Ahmedabad, a Section 8 company promoted by the Government of Gujarat.

## Objective

- Promote technology innovation and entrepreneurship in Gujarat
- Provide financial support to early-stage startups through grants
- Strengthen the startup ecosystem by connecting innovators with mentors and investors
- Support scalable and sustainable technology-based solutions
- Encourage job creation and economic growth through innovation


## Target Beneficiaries

**startups**, **technology innovators**, **early-stage ventures**

## Key Features

- Fund size: ₹50 crore
- Grant/assistance per entity: Up to INR 25 Lakhs
- Geographic scope: Gujarat only (state-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds.
- Portal: https://ihubahemdabad.in
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

Startups and technology innovators registered in Gujarat with a minimum viable product (MVP) or prototype, working in sectors such as AI, IoT, biotech, agritech, cleantech, or deep tech. Applicants must be incorporated as a private limited company, LLP, or partnership firm under Indian laws, and demonstrate potential for scalability and social impact.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial grants for product development and market access, mentorship from industry experts, access to prototyping and lab facilities, networking opportunities with investors and corporates, regulatory guidance, and support for intellectual property rights (IPR) filing.

## Important Conditions

- Grants are disbursed only upon achievement of pre-defined milestones
- Funding is subject to availability of funds under the Technology Innovation Fund
- Startups must maintain proper utilization certificates and financial records
- Intellectual property developed using grant funds may be subject to sharing or licensing norms as per Gujarat government guidelines
- The scheme does not cover working capital, salaries, or general administrative expenses
