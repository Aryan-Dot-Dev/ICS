## Process

1. Government or corporate sponsor approaches NPCI or its authorized partner to issue e-RUPI vouchers.
2. Sponsor provides beneficiary details (name, mobile number) and purpose/amount of voucher.
3. NPCI generates a unique e-RUPI QR code or SMS string.
4. Sponsor distributes the e-RUPI to beneficiary via SMS or messaging platform.
5. Beneficiary shows the QR code or shares the SMS string at merchant point-of-sale.
6. Merchant validates the e-RUPI through their acquiring bank or PSP.
7. Upon validation, merchant provides the service/goods and settles the amount with NPCI.
