## Process

1. Prepare a detailed project proposal including objectives, breed details, infrastructure plan, budget, and expected outcomes.
2. Submit the proposal to the State Implementing Agency (SIA) or State Livestock Development Board.
3. The SIA forwards the proposal to the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying (DAHD) for appraisal.
4. Upon approval by DAHD, funds are released in installments to the SIA.
5. The beneficiary implements the project and submits progress reports and utilization certificates.
6. Final release is made after verification of completion and outcomes.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — proposals can be submitted throughout the year based on annual action plans of states.
