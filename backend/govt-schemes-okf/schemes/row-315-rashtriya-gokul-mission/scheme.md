# Rashtriya Gokul Mission

## Overview

The Rashtriya Gokul Mission (RGM) is a flagship initiative under the National Programme for Bovine Breeding and Dairy Development, launched to conserve and develop indigenous bovine breeds through genetic upgradation and enhancement of milk production and productivity. It aims to enhance bovine productivity and increase milk production in a sustainable manner using advanced breeding technologies. The mission focuses on the propagation of high genetic merit bulls of indigenous breeds and the establishment of integrated cattle development centres.

## Objective

- Conservation and development of indigenous bovine breeds
- Enhancement of milk production and productivity of bovines
- Upgradation of nondescript bovine population using elite indigenous breeds
- Distribution of disease-free high genetic merit bulls for natural service
- Establishment of integrated indigenous cattle development centres (Gokul Grams)
- Promotion of indigenous bovine breeding through professional farm management and superior nutrition
- Organization of milk yield competitions and bovine fairs to motivate farmers
- Strengthening of bull mother farms to conserve indigenous breeds

## Important Conditions

- Funds are released only after submission of utilization certificates and progress reports
- Subsidy is limited to indigenous breeds;
- exotic or crossbred animals are not eligible for breed procurement support
- Projects must be completed within the stipulated timeline as per administrative approval
- Beneficiaries must maintain records of bovine progeny and milk yield for monitoring
