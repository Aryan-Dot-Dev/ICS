# Technology Centre Systems Programme (TCSP)

## Overview

The Technology Centre Systems Programme (TCSP) is a central sector scheme under the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME) aimed at establishing and upgrading Technology Centres (TCs) across India to provide technological support to MSMEs. The programme focuses on enhancing the competitiveness of MSMEs through access to advanced manufacturing technologies, skill development, testing, calibration, and advisory services. TCSP seeks to bridge the technology gap for MSMEs by creating world-class infrastructure and fostering innovation in manufacturing clusters.

## Objective

- Establish new Technology Centres and upgrade existing ones to provide advanced technological support
- Enhance the competitiveness of MSMEs through access to high-end manufacturing technologies
- Provide skill development training to youth and technicians in relevant trades
- Offer testing, calibration, and certification services to ensure quality compliance
- Promote innovation and R&D collaboration between TCs, MSMEs, and academic institutions
- Support cluster-based development by locating TCs in key manufacturing hubs
- Ensure sustainable operations of Technology Centres through revenue-generating activities

## Eligibility

MSMEs, manufacturing units, startups, technical institutions, and industry clusters located in proximity to Technology Centres can avail services. Eligibility for availing subsidized services is based on MSME classification as per MSMED Act, 2006, and specific service offerings of individual Technology Centres.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Access to advanced manufacturing technologies and machinery at subsidized rates, skill development training programs, testing and calibration facilities, technical consultancy, design and prototyping support, assistance in adopting Industry 4.0 technologies, and support for product development and process optimization.

## Important Conditions

- Subsidized rates are applicable only for services availed directly from Technology Centres under TCSP
- Priority may be given to MSMEs from the host state or region where the TC is located
- Availability of advanced machinery and training slots is subject to utilization and maintenance schedules
- Certain high-end services may have limited slots and require advance booking
