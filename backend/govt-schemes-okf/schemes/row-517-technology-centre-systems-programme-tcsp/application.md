## Process

1. Identify the nearest Technology Centre offering the required service.
2. Contact the TC to discuss service requirements and obtain a quotation.
3. Submit service request along with necessary documentation.
4. Avail the service upon approval and payment of subsidized user charges.
5. For long-term collaboration can be explored through MoUs or service agreements.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — services are available throughout the year based on TC schedules and capacity.
