## Process

1. Submit a detailed project proposal for mineral exploration to the NMET Secretariat.
2. The proposal must include objectives, methodology, budget, timeline, and expected outcomes.
3. Proposals are reviewed by the Technical Committee constituted by NMET.
4. Upon approval, a sanction letter is issued specifying the grant amount and terms.
5. Funds are released in stages based on progress reports and utilization certificates.
6. Final report and audit report must be submitted upon project completion.

## Deadlines
