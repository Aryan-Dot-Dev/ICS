# Indian Railways Station Redevelopment Programme

## Overview

The Indian Railways Station Redevelopment Programme (IRSRD) aims to modernize and upgrade railway stations across India through public-private partnerships, leveraging commercial development of station land and airspace to fund infrastructure improvements. The initiative focuses on enhancing passenger amenities, improving safety and accessibility, and transforming stations into world-class transit hubs while maintaining operational efficiency.

## Objective

- Modernize railway stations with improved passenger amenities and infrastructure
- Enhance safety, security, and accessibility at railway stations
- Develop stations as multi-modal transit hubs with commercial integration
- Leverage land value capture through commercial development to fund redevelopment
- Improve operational efficiency and passenger experience
- Create sustainable and environmentally friendly station environments

## Eligibility

The programme invites private sector participation through competitive bidding for station redevelopment projects. Eligible bidders include companies, consortia, and special purpose vehicles (SPVs) with relevant experience in infrastructure development, real estate, urban planning, and construction. ## Benefits

Passengers benefit from upgraded facilities including waiting areas, sanitation, food courts, retail spaces, and improved circulation. Private developers gain long-term lease rights for commercial development of station premises and airspace. Indian Railways achieves infrastructure modernization without direct budgetary expenditure through land value capture mechanisms.
