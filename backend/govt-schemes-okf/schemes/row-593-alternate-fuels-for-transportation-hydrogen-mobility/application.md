## Process

1. Industry stakeholders seeking funding must not send proposals directly to DST.
2. Disbursements are routed exclusively through designated Second-Level Fund Managers (SLFMs).
3. Interested SLFMs (Alternate Investment Funds, Development Finance Institutions, NBFCs, or Focused Research Organizations) must express interest by writing to Dr Jyoti Sharma, Head, RDI Cell at sharma[dot]jyoti[at]nic[dot]in.
4. Existing NBFCs planning to establish AIFs are encouraged to contact DST regarding potential participation.
5. Fund flow from RDIF to SLFMs occurs via Alternate Investment Fund (AIF) mechanisms or loans at low rates with extended tenors.
6. SLFMs prepare fund requirement showing estimated receipts from repayment or exit proceeds and forward to RDIF.
7. Upon receipt of demand, RDIF releases funds to the designated bank account of the SLFM within a specified time frame.
