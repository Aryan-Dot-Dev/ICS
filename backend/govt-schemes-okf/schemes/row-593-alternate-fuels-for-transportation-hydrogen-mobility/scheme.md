# Alternate Fuels for Transportation (Hydrogen Mobility)

## Overview

The Alternate Fuels for Transportation (Hydrogen Mobility) scheme under the Department of Science & Technology (DST) aims to promote hydrogen as a clean fuel for transportation to reduce carbon emissions and support India's green mobility goals. It focuses on research, development, and deployment of hydrogen-based technologies in the transport sector, including fuel cell vehicles and refueling infrastructure. The scheme supports innovation through funding and collaboration with industry and research institutions to advance hydrogen mobility solutions.

## Objective

- Promote hydrogen as a clean alternative fuel for transportation
- Support research and development in hydrogen fuel cell technologies
- Develop hydrogen refueling infrastructure across India
- Encourage private sector participation in hydrogen mobility projects
- Reduce carbon emissions in the transport sector through green mobility solutions
- Align with national goals of Atmanirbhar Bharat and sustainable development
