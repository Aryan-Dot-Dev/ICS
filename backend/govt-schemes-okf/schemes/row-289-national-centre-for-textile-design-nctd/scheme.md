# National Centre for Textile Design (NCTD)

## Overview

The National Centre for Textile Design (NCTD) is an autonomous body under the Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms, Ministry of Textiles, Government of India. It functions as a premier institute for design development, training, and research in the textile and handicrafts sector. NCTD aims to enhance the competitiveness of Indian textiles through innovative design interventions, skill upgradation, and market linkage support. It serves designers, weavers, artisans, exporters, and textile industries by providing design services, forecasting trends, and promoting traditional and contemporary textile arts.

## Objective

- To provide design development and forecasting services to the textile and handicrafts sector
- To upgrade skills of designers, weavers, and artisans through training programmes
- To promote traditional Indian textiles and contemporary design innovation
- To strengthen market linkages for handloom and handicraft products
- To conduct research and documentation on textile heritage and emerging trends
- To support export promotion through design intervention and product development

## Eligibility

Eligibility varies by programme; generally open to individual designers, weavers, artisans, self-help groups, cooperatives, NGOs, exporters, and textile/handicraft units seeking design support, training, or market linkage services from NCTD.

## Benefits

Access to professional design services, trend forecasting, prototype development, training in design software and traditional techniques, product development support, market linkage assistance, participation in design exhibitions and buyer-seller meets, and documentation of traditional designs.
