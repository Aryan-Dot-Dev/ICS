## Process

1. Download the application form from the official MSME Commissionerate website.
2. Fill in entrepreneur and enterprise details including name, address, Udyam registration, constitution, and product details.
3. Attach required documents such as Udyam Udyam certificate, audited balance sheets, constitution proof, photographs, and category-specific documents.
4. Submit a write-up of up to 300 words justifying the enterprise’s achievements.
5. Obtain GM Certificate from District Industries Center verifying eligibility.
6. Submit the completed application in duplicate to the MSME Commissionerate before the deadline.
7. Await evaluation by the appointed committee.
8. If selected, receive award, trophy, and certificate.
