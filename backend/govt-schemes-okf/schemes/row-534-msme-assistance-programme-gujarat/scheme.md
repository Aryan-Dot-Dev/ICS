# MSME Assistance Programme Gujarat

## Overview

The MSME Assistance Programme Gujarat is an award-based recognition scheme for Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) in Gujarat, administered by the Office of the MSME Commissionerate. It aims to identify and honor outstanding MSMEs across categories such as Women Entrepreneur, Young Entrepreneur, SC/ST Entrepreneur, and General through a formal application process involving documentation, verification, and committee evaluation. The scheme does not provide direct financial grants but offers recognition via awards, trophies, and certificates.

## Objective

- To recognize and promote excellence among MSMEs in Gujarat
- To encourage women, young, SC/ST, and general category entrepreneurs
- To validate Udyam registration and operational continuity of enterprises
- To assess enterprise performance based on investment, turnover, employment, and net profit
- To verify first-generation entrepreneur status and product/service achievements
- To ensure compliance with statutory clearances and tax obligations
- To promote transparency and authenticity in award selection process
