# National Digital Library of India (NDLI)

## Overview

National Digital Library of India (NDLI) is a virtual repository of learning resources sponsored and mentored by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, through its National Mission on Education through Information and Communication Technology (NMEICT). It provides a single-window access to learning resources across disciplines, academic levels, and languages, supporting learners from school to lifelong education. NDLI offers user group-specific services such as examination preparatory content, resources for researchers, and general learners, with interface support for 10 widely used Indian languages. It is developed, operated, and maintained from the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur.

## Objective

- To make learning resources available through a single-window to learners
- To provide filtered and federated searching for focused and efficient resource discovery
- To offer user group-specific services such as examination preparatory content for school and college students and job aspirants
- To support researchers and general learners with tailored services
- To hold content in any language and provide interface support for 10 most widely used Indian languages
- To support all academic levels including researchers and life-long learners across all disciplines
- To enable learning from global best practices and facilitate inter-linked exploration from multiple sources
- To democratize education and promote integrated digital learning across India
