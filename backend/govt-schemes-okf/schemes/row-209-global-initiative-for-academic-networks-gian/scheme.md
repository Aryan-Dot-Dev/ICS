# Global Initiative for Academic Networks (GIAN)

## Overview

Global Initiative of Academic Networks (GIAN) is a Government of India program in Higher Education aimed at tapping the talent pool of scientists and entrepreneurs internationally to encourage their engagement with Indian higher education institutes, augment academic resources, accelerate quality reform, and elevate India's scientific and technological capacity to global excellence. It facilitates short to semester-long courses by foreign faculty in Indian institutions, promoting international academic collaboration and knowledge exchange.

## Objective

- Increase footfalls of reputed international faculty in Indian academic institutes
- Provide opportunity for faculty to learn and share knowledge in cutting-edge areas
- Provide opportunity for students to seek knowledge from international faculty
- Create avenue for collaborative research with international faculty
- Increase participation of international students in academic institutes
- Enable interaction between students of different institutes in niche areas
- Provide opportunity for technical persons from Indian industry to update knowledge
- Motivate international experts to work on India-related problems
- Develop high-quality course material in niche areas for wider use
- Document and develop new pedagogic methods in emerging topics

## Key Features

- Grant/assistance per entity: Up to US$ 8000 for short duration courses (12-14 hours) and up to US$ 12000 for longer duration courses (20-28 hours) for foreign faculty honorarium and travel.
- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: Proposals may be submitted throughout the year. Three months required for review to final approval. Preferably, six months lead time from date of holding the course.
- Portal: https://www.gian.iitkgp.ac.in/GREGN/
- Source data last updated: 2023-24
- Import confidence (source): high


## Eligibility

All Government (State or Central) higher education Institutions / University which are in top 200 in NIRF overall rankings and having at least NAAC ‘A’ grade (3.0 and above).

International faculty engagement, knowledge exchange, collaborative research opportunities, course development, pedagogic innovation, global exposure for students and faculty, access to international expertise, creation of digital repository for future viewing via National Digital Library, potential for MOOC creation, honorarium and travel support for foreign faculty, honorarium for host faculty…

## Important Conditions

- Foreign faculty not entitled to travel allowance in virtual mode;
- only honorarium paid
- Honorarium for foreign faculty in virtual mode limited to $3000 for one week course (≥12 hrs) and $5000 for 2 week course (≥20 hrs)
- Contingencies and other expenses (excluding travel and honorarium of foreign faculty) should not exceed 35% of total fund provided for a course
- A maximum of Rs. 5000/- per course may be earmarked to local coordinator for office expenses and honorarium from contingency expense
- All approved courses must be completed by 31st December 2020 (per Local Coordinator manual, though this may be outdated)
- Dollar conversion rate fixed at Rs. 83 for payment to host institute for F.Y.
- 2023-24, subject to review for subsequent years
- GIAN to MOOC conversion of completed courses discontinued for both in-person and virtual mode
