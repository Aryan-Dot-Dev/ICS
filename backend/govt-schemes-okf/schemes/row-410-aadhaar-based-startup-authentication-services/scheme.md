# Aadhaar-Based Startup Authentication Services

## Overview

The Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) provides Aadhaar-based authentication services that enable startups to verify identities digitally using Aadhaar e-KYC and authentication APIs. This service supports digital onboarding, reduces fraud, and enhances trust in digital transactions for startups operating in India. It is part of the broader Digital India initiative to promote secure and inclusive digital infrastructure.

## Objective

- Enable secure and paperless identity verification for startups
- Facilitate digital onboarding of customers using Aadhaar e-KYC
- Reduce identity fraud and enhance trust in digital services
- Support inclusive access to digital infrastructure for all residents

## Important Conditions

- Usage of Aadhaar authentication is subject to compliance with the Aadhaar Act, 2016 and regulations framed thereunder
- Entities must maintain stringent data security and privacy standards as specified by UIDAI
- Authentication services are provided only for legitimate, lawful purposes as defined by UIDAI
- Misuse of Aadhaar data can lead to penalties and blacklisting under the Aadhaar Act
