# Goa Startup Policy

## Overview

The Goa Startup Policy is a state-level initiative by the Government of Goa to foster a robust startup ecosystem by providing financial and non-financial support to startups operating in Goa. It aims to position Goa as a preferred startup destination in India and a top 25 startup hub in Asia by 2025. The policy includes multiple incentive schemes such as seed capital, salary reimbursement, IPR reimbursement, and co-working space subsidies, administered by the Startup and IT Promotion Cell (SITPC).

## Objective

- To make Goa an aspirational geographical and human resources base for high value start-ups
- To invite the best entrepreneurial minds to make Goa their professional base, and thereby build a robust start-up eco-system in the state
- To provide assistance to the Goan entrepreneurs and local start-ups
- To endeavour to create at least 100 successful start-ups in Goa in the next 5 years, targeting generation of employment for atleast 5000 Goans
- To ensure continuous evolution of policy measures and creation of a responsive policy framework for accelerating growth of the start-up ecosystem
- To infuse technology-enabled real-world problem-solving skills into the Goan education system through collaboration between industry, academia and through the introduction of DIY modules as part of school curriculum and Massive Online Open Courses as part of University curriculum

## Important Conditions

- Benefits under all schemes are not considered an entitlement;
- SITPC reserves the sole right to accept or reject applications
- Only expenditure incurred after notification of Goa Start-up Policy 2021 and paid digitally is considered for reimbursement
- Bank accounts of directors and/or employees must be linked to Aadhaar
- Digital payment is mandatory unless SITPC makes an exception per guidelines
- Certain schemes have annual caps (e.g., 100 startups for Seed Capital, 200 for IPR/trademark, 20 for co-working space subsidy)
- Local startup definition requires at least 50% equity held by Goans continuously since inception
- Fresher definition limited to graduates/postgraduates within 2 years of completion
- Salary reimbursement requires employment period of at least 12 months
- R&D salary reimbursement capped at INR 2 lakh despite overall cap of INR 5 lakh
- Co-working space subsidy limited to 2 years (co-working), 1 year (incubator), 3 months (accelerator) per startup
- Trademark reimbursement only available after successful trademark registration
