# Account Aggregator (AA) Framework for FinTech Startups

## Overview

The Account Aggregator (AA) Framework is an RBI-approved initiative that enables secure, consent-driven sharing of financial data between Financial Information Providers (FIPs) and Financial Information Users (FIUs) via Account Aggregators (AAs). It forms a foundational pillar of India's Digital Public Infrastructure, promoting financial inclusion and innovation across lending, insurance, investments, and personal finance. Sahamati acts as the collective body supporting the AA ecosystem through technical standards, certification, governance, and member services.

## Objective

- Enable secure, consent-driven sharing of financial data across financial institutions
- Promote financial inclusion by expanding access to credit for underserved segments
- Strengthen the Account Aggregator ecosystem through technical standards and certification
- Support innovation in financial services via open finance principles
- Ensure data privacy and user control over personal financial information
- Facilitate interoperability among FIPs, AAs, and FIUs through standardized APIs
- Drive adoption of Account Aggregator framework across banking, insurance, securities, and pension sectors

## Important Conditions

- All NBFC-AAs must be classified under Fee Category B for membership fees
- Entities must align their use cases with Fair Use Template parameters to avoid rejection of consent requests
- AA does not store, process, or sell customer data;
- data is encrypted in transit
- Only asset-based data (bank accounts, deposits, mutual funds, insurance policies, pension funds) is currently accessible via AA
- Membership fees apply based on entity category and regulator (e.g., Category A: Rs. 5 Lakh, Category B: Rs. 1.25 Lakh, Category C: Rs. 25,000)
- Certification is mandatory for participation and must be renewed upon major version changes in ReBIT Technical Specifications or Sahamati Certification Framework
