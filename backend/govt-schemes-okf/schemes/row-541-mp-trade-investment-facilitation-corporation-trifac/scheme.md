# MP Trade & Investment Facilitation Corporation (TRIFAC)

## Overview

MP Trade & Investment Facilitation Corporation (TRIFAC) is a state-level agency established by the Government of Madhya Pradesh to promote trade, investment, and industrial development in the state. It acts as a nodal agency for facilitating investments, providing support to industries, and enhancing Madhya Pradesh's competitiveness in domestic and global markets. TRIFAC focuses on attracting domestic and foreign investment, supporting MSMEs, and improving the ease of doing business in the state.

## Objective

- Promote and facilitate domestic and foreign direct investment in Madhya Pradesh
- Support the growth and competitiveness of MSMEs and industries in the state
- Enhance ease of doing business through policy advocacy and regulatory support
- Develop infrastructure and logistics to support industrial growth
- Promote exports and international trade linkages for MP-based enterprises
- Provide facilitation services for land allocation, statutory clearances, and project approvals
