# Kisan Credit Card for Fisheries & Animal Husbandry

## Overview

The Kisan Credit Card (KCC) scheme was introduced by the Government of India in 1998 to provide timely and affordable credit support to farmers, including fishers and livestock farmers. It enables cultivators, livestock farmers, and fishers to access short-term loans for crop production, allied activities, and household requirements. Under KCC, farmers are issued a credit card linked to their bank account, which allows them to withdraw funds as needed. The scheme has been extended to fisheries and animal husbandry sectors to support fishers and fish farmers with working capital for fisheries-related activities.

## Objective

- Provide timely and affordable credit support to farmers
- Enable access to short-term loans for crop production and allied activities
- Support household requirements of farmers
- Extend credit facilities to fishers and livestock farmers under KCC
- Issue credit cards linked to bank accounts for flexible fund withdrawal

Cultivators, livestock farmers, and fishers engaged in agricultural and allied activities are eligible for the Kisan Credit Card scheme. The scheme is available to farmers who require credit for crop production, fisheries, animal husbandry, and household needs.
