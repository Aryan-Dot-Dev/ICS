# PM E-Bus Sewa – Electric Bus Deployment

## Overview

PM-eBus Sewa is a scheme launched by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) to promote electric bus deployment in Indian cities. The scheme aims to support the adoption of electric buses for public transport, reduce emissions, and improve urban mobility. It involves central assistance for procurement and operation of electric buses through a sanctioning and steering committee mechanism.

## Objective

- Promote adoption of electric buses for urban public transport
- Reduce vehicular emissions and improve air quality in cities
- Support sustainable urban mobility through central financial assistance
- Enable cities to transition to clean energy-based transport systems
- Strengthen institutional capacity for electric bus operations and maintenance


## Target Beneficiaries

**Urban Local Bodies**, **State Transport Undertakings**, **Special Purpose Vehicles**

## Eligibility

Urban Local Bodies (ULBs), State Transport Undertakings (STUs), and Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs) formed by ULBs/STUs are eligible to apply under the PM-eBus Sewa scheme for deployment of electric buses in cities.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial support for procurement and operation of electric buses, reduction in operational costs due to lower energy and maintenance expenses, environmental benefits through zero tailpipe emissions, improved public transport quality and reliability, and contribution to national climate goals under India's EV mission.
