# Bulk Drug Parks Scheme

## Overview

The Bulk Drug Parks Scheme aims to develop world-class infrastructure for bulk drug manufacturing in India by establishing dedicated parks with common facilities. It seeks to reduce manufacturing costs, enhance competitiveness, and ensure a steady supply of critical drugs. The scheme supports the creation of integrated infrastructure including common effluent treatment plants, solvent recovery systems, and logistics hubs. It is designed to promote self-reliance in pharmaceutical manufacturing and reduce dependence on imports.

## Objective

- To create world-class infrastructure for bulk drug manufacturing
- To reduce the cost of production through common facilities
- To ensure availability of critical drugs and reduce import dependence
- To promote environmental sustainability via common effluent treatment
- To attract investment in the pharmaceutical sector
- To generate employment in the manufacturing sector

## Eligibility

Entities eligible for setting up Bulk Drug Parks include State Government agencies, Government-owned corporations, joint ventures with equity participation from State Governments, and private sector companies selected through a transparent bidding process. The implementing agency will be a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) formed by the State Government or its nominee.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial support in the form of grant-in-aid for common infrastructure facilities. Non-financial benefits include plug-and-play infrastructure, common effluent treatment plants, solvent recovery systems, steam and power supply, water supply, logistics support, and safety and security services. The scheme enables economies of scale and reduces capital expenditure for individual units.

## Important Conditions

- Grant is released only after achievement of physical progress milestones
- Land must be in possession of the State Government or SPV before fund release
- All statutory clearances (environmental, etc.) must be obtained by the SPV
- The scheme is subject to periodic review and modification by the Government
- Beneficiary allotment within the park must follow transparent and non-discriminatory procedures
