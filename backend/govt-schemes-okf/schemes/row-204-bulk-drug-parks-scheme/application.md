## Process

1. State Governments submit proposals for setting up Bulk Drug Parks to the Department of Pharmaceuticals.
2. Proposals are evaluated based on predefined criteria including land availability, water and power connectivity, environmental clearance status, and industrial ecosystem.
3. Selected proposals are accorded 'In-Principle' approval.
4. After obtaining necessary statutory clearances, the State Governments form a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV).
5. The SPV submits a Detailed Project Report (DPR) for final approval.
6. Upon approval, funds are released in installments based on progress milestones.
7. The SPV undertakes implementation of common infrastructure facilities.
8. Facilities are made operational and allotted to bulk drug manufacturers on lease or ownership basis.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — proposals can be submitted throughout the year as per notifications issued by the Department of Pharmaceuticals.
