# Medical & Wellness Tourism Policy

## Overview

The Medical & Wellness Tourism Policy is a scheme under the Ministry of Tourism aimed at promoting India as a destination for medical and wellness tourism. It seeks to leverage India's traditional healthcare systems and modern medical facilities to attract international patients. The policy focuses on infrastructure development, service quality enhancement, and global marketing of medical and wellness tourism offerings.

## Objective

- Promote India as a global destination for medical and wellness tourism
- Enhance infrastructure and service quality in medical and wellness tourism sectors
- Increase foreign tourist arrivals for medical and wellness purposes
- Strengthen coordination between central and state governments for tourism development
- Leverage traditional Indian systems like Ayurveda, Yoga, and Naturopathy
- Improve global visibility of India's medical and wellness tourism offerings

## Important Conditions

- In case of more applications, priority would be given to those service providers who have not availed financial assistance in the past, under the MDA Scheme.
- Incomplete applications are liable for rejection.
- The claim for financial assistance must be submitted within 45 days from the date of return to India.
- Claim received after 45 days of return to India, or wherein the deficiencies in the claim as intimated are not fully completed within 45 days from the date of information/documents sought, will be rejected.
- Financial support under the Scheme would be subject to availability of budget during the year under the MDA Scheme.
- For reimbursement of air fare under the MDA Scheme, all tourism service provider shall travel only by Air India from India to stations abroad directly connected by Air India.
- For travel from India to stations abroad, NOT directly connected by Air India, but are directly connected by other private airlines, travel by shorter route would be permissible on economy class only.
- Tickets should be booked directly from Air India or from their online portal.
- In the case of travel by other private airlines for those sectors where Air India is not available, tickets should be booked directly from the online portal of the private airline or directly through the office of the private airline and not through an agency.
- The TSP shall not be under investigation or charged/ prosecuted/debarred/black listed by Ministry of Tourism, Govt.
- of India or any other Government Agency.
- The TSP should not have claimed/received any financial assistance for the Promotional Activity for which reimbursement is being claimed, from the Central / State Government or any Government Agency.
