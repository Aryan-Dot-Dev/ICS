# Atal Bhujal Yojana (Groundwater Management)

## Overview

Atal Bhujal Yojana (ABY) is a central sector scheme launched by the Government of India to improve groundwater management in water-stressed areas through community participation and sustainable practices. The scheme focuses on demand-side management, aquifer recharge, and strengthening institutional frameworks for groundwater governance. It aims to arrest the rate of decline of groundwater levels and improve water conservation practices in identified over-exploited and water-stressed areas.

## Objective

- Strengthen the institutional framework for participatory groundwater management
- Promote behavioral changes at the community level for sustainable groundwater use
- Implement demand-side management practices in identified water-stressed areas
- Undertake aquifer recharge and water conservation activities
- Improve the availability and reliability of groundwater data and monitoring systems
- Enhance the capacity of stakeholders for effective groundwater governance
- Ensure convergence with other government schemes for integrated water resource management

## Eligibility

The scheme is implemented in identified water-stressed areas of seven states: Gujarat, Haryana, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, and Uttar Pradesh. Benefits are extended to gram panchayats, water user associations, and local communities in these areas who participate in groundwater management activities through community-led water security plans.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial and technical support for preparation and implementation of community-led Water Security Plans (WSPs), capacity building and training for stakeholders, support for IEC (Information, Education, and Communication) activities, technical assistance for groundwater monitoring and data management, and convergence with other schemes for integrated water resource management.

## Important Conditions

- Scheme is limited to identified over-exploited and water-stressed areas in the seven participating states
- Funds are released based on achievement of Disbursement-Linked Indicators (DLIs)
- Requires 50% beneficiary contribution in kind or cash for certain activities
- Implementation is contingent on state government participation and fund allocation
