## Process

1. After the Detailed Energy Audit, the prospective borrower shall submit the Detailed Project Report (DPR) to SIDBI for its vetting by EEC.
2. If required, the sample format of the DPR shall be made available by EEC.
3. The Borrower shall submit application to SIDBI BOs along with a copy of the DPR vetted by EEC.
4. Eligible amount of capital subsidy under CLCSS, TEQUP, etc. shall also be sanctioned along with the loan as per prevalent guidelines.
5. Disbursement shall be made after compliance of terms & conditions of the sanction stipulated in the LoI and documentation as per the extant guidelines for DCS.
