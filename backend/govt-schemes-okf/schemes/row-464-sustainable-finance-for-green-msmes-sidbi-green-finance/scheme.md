# Sustainable Finance for Green MSMEs (SIDBI Green Finance)

## Overview

Sustainable Finance for Green MSMEs (SIDBI Green Finance) is a loan scheme aimed at supporting MSMEs in adopting green technologies and sustainable practices. It provides financial assistance for projects related to energy efficiency, renewable energy, pollution control, and other environmentally friendly initiatives. The scheme is part of SIDBI's broader efforts to promote sustainable development within the MSME sector through targeted lending and capacity building.

## Objective

- Promote energy saving in MSMEs in India
- Reduce greenhouse gas emissions, especially CO2, to mitigate climate change
- Support MSMEs in development, up-scaling, demonstration, and commercialization of innovative technology-based projects
- Enhance awareness of climate control benefits among MSE clusters
- Provide adequate and affordable green finance for clean production and energy-efficient technologies

- Loan amount is capped at ₹150 lakh per borrower
- Promoter must contribute minimum 10% of project cost
- Repayment tenure capped at 60 months for loans beyond ₹50 lakh
