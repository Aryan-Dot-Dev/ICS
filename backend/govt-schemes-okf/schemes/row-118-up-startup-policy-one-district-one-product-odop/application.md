## Process

1. Visit the official UP Startup Policy portal at startup.up.gov.in.
2. Register as a beneficiary by filling the online application form.
3. Submit details of the business idea, ODOP product linkage, and required documents.
4. Await preliminary screening by the district-level committee.
5. Present the business plan before the state-level evaluation committee.
6. Receive approval and sanction letter.
7. Submit utilization reports and claim disbursement of funds as per milestones.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds.
