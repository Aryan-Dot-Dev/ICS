# Special Economic Zone (SEZ) Scheme

## Overview

The Special Economic Zone (SEZ) Scheme is a Government of India initiative aimed at promoting exports, attracting foreign investment, and generating employment through the establishment of SEZs across the country. It provides a duty-free enclave for export-oriented units with streamlined regulatory and tax incentives. The scheme supports both manufacturing and services sectors and is governed by the SEZ Act, 2005 and SEZ Rules, 2006.

## Objective

- Promote exports of goods and services
- Attract domestic and foreign investment
- Generate employment opportunities
- Develop infrastructure facilities
- Enhance foreign exchange earnings


## Target Beneficiaries

**developers**, **co-developers**, **units**, **manufacturers**, **service providers**


## Key Features

- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — applications can be submitted throughout the year.
- Portal: https://www.sezindia.nic.in
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

Any person, including a company, partnership, proprietorship, or cooperative society, can make an application for setting up an SEZ or for developing infrastructure facilities in an SEZ. The applicant must be a resident or non-resident as per FEMA regulations and must comply with the SEZ Act, 2005 and SEZ Rules, 2006.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Units in SEZs are entitled to duty-free import/domestic procurement of goods for development, operation, and maintenance; exemption from central and state taxes and levies as approved by the Board of Approval; exemption from minimum alternate tax under Section 115JB of the Income Tax Act; external commercial borrowing up to USD 500 million per year without maturity restriction; single window…

## Important Conditions

- Approval is subject to compliance with environmental and security clearance requirements
- The SEZ must be developed within 5 years from the date of Letter of Approval
- Minimum land area requirements apply: 50 hectares for sector-specific SEZs and 1000 hectares for multi-product SEZs
- Units must be positive net foreign exchange earners within 3 years of commencement of operations
- Certain restricted sectors like arms, ammunition, explosives, atomic substances, and narcotics are not permitted
