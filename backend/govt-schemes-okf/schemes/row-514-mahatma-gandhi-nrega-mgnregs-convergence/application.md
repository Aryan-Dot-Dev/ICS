## Process

1. Apply for a Job Card at the Gram Panchayat office with household details.
2. Gram Panchayat verifies and issues Job Card within 15 days.
3. Submit application for work (written or oral) to Gram Panchayat/Programme Officer.
4. Work is allocated within 15 days of application.
5. Attend work site and mark attendance via muster roll.
6. Wages are paid within 15 days of work completion via DBT/ABPS.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications for work can be made throughout the year as per demand.
