# Logistics Efficiency Enhancement Programme (LEEP)

## Overview

The Logistics Efficiency Enhancement Programme (LEEP) is a scheme under the Department of Commerce, Ministry of Commerce and Industry, aimed at improving logistics efficiency and reducing logistics costs in India. It focuses on enhancing trade facilitation, streamlining customs procedures, and supporting infrastructure development to boost exports and integrate Indian businesses into global value chains. The programme supports MSMEs, startups, and other stakeholders through policy interventions, digital initiatives, and capacity building.

## Objective

- Enhance logistics efficiency and reduce logistics costs across the supply chain
- Improve trade facilitation through streamlined customs and regulatory procedures
- Support infrastructure development for seamless movement of goods
- Promote digital transformation in trade via platforms like Trade Intelligence & Analytics (TIA)
- Strengthen India’s position in global trade by enhancing competitiveness of Indian goods and services
- Facilitate MSME and startup participation in global value chains
- Encourage adoption of best practices in logistics and supply chain management
- Support capacity building and skill development in logistics sector

## Important Conditions

- LEEP is primarily a policy and facilitation programme;
- direct financial assistance is not provided under this scheme.
- Benefits are subject to compliance with Department of Commerce guidelines and ongoing scheme updates.
- Access to certain digital platforms or services may require registration on allied portals like TIA or DGFT services.
- The scheme does not guarantee market access or export incentives;
- it enables efficiency improvements.
- Eligibility and benefits may vary based on specific sub-initiatives under LEEP.
