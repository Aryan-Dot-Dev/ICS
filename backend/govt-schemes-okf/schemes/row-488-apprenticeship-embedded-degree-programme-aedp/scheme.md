# Apprenticeship Embedded Degree Programme (AEDP)

## Overview

The Apprenticeship Embedded Degree Programme (AEDP) integrates academic learning with on-the-job training to enhance employability and bridge the skill gap. It is designed to provide students with a degree while gaining practical industry experience through apprenticeships. The programme aims to align education with industry needs and promote workforce readiness. It supports the Skill India Mission by combining formal education with structured training.

## Objective

- Integrate academic learning with on-the-job training
- Enhance employability through industry-aligned skills
- Bridge the gap between education and industry requirements
- Promote workforce readiness under the Skill India Mission
- Provide degree qualifications alongside practical apprenticeship experience

## Eligibility

Candidates must be enrolled in a recognized degree programme and meet the educational qualifications specified for the apprenticeship trade, including minimum 10+2 or equivalent as per the trade requirements.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Participants gain hands-on industry experience, earn a stipend during training, receive a degree upon completion, and improve job prospects through certified skills and NSQF alignment.


Structured benefit objects: [benefits.md](benefits.md).

## Important Conditions

- Stipend reimbursement under NAPS is limited to 25% of stipend or INR 1,500 per month, whichever is lower
- Training duration must not exceed the academic programme timeline
- Establishments must be registered on the portal to host apprentices
