# Apprenticeship Embedded Degree Programme (AEDP) — Application

## Channels

```yaml
application:
  mode:
    - online
  official_portal: "https://apprenticeshipindia.org"
  implementing_agency: "Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship"
  deadline: "Rolling basis"
```

## Process

1. Register as a candidate on the apprenticeshipindia.org portal.
2. Complete profile with educational and personal details.
3. Search for apprenticeship opportunities under AEDP or related schemes.
4. Apply to establishments offering embedded degree apprenticeships.
5. Upon selection, sign the apprenticeship contract.
6. Begin on-the-job training while pursuing academic studies.
7. Complete assessments and submit required documentation.
8. Receive degree and certification upon successful completion.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis
