# National Livestock Mission (NLM)

## Overview

The National Livestock Mission (NLM) is a centrally sponsored scheme launched by the Government of India to ensure sustainable development of the livestock sector, focusing on improving productivity, supporting entrepreneurship, and promoting breed improvement, fodder development, and risk management. It aims to enhance livestock production through capacity building, technology adoption, and infrastructure development, with special attention to small and marginal farmers, livestock rearers, and entrepreneurs in rural areas. The mission operates through four sub-missions: Livestock Development, Pig Development in the Northeastern Region, Feed and Fodder Development, and Skill Development, Technology Transfer, and Extension.

## Objective

- Ensure sustainable development and growth of the livestock sector
- Increase availability of fodder and feed to reduce scarcity
- Promote entrepreneurship and breed improvement in livestock
- Transfer technology and build capacity of stakeholders
- Provide risk management and disease control support
- Enhance production and productivity of livestock and poultry
- Support development of small ruminants, pigs, and poultry in rural areas
- Strengthen extension services and skill development in animal husbandry

## Important Conditions

- Subsidy is subject to availability of funds and state-wise allocation under the mission
- Beneficiaries must not have availed similar benefits for the same component from other central or state schemes
- Projects must be completed within the stipulated time frame;
- delay may lead to cancellation of sanction
- Utilization certificates and completion reports are mandatory for release of subsequent installments
- Benefits are not applicable for commercial livestock farming exceeding prescribed unit sizes under the scheme guidelines
