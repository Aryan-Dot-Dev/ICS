## Process

1. Identify the relevant component under the National Livestock Mission (e.g., poultry, piggery, fodder, breed improvement).
2. Contact the State Implementing Agency (SIA) or District Animal Husbandry Office for guidance and application forms.
3. Prepare a detailed project report including cost estimates, land details, and beneficiary information.
4. Submit the application along with required documents to the concerned District Animal Husbandry Officer or State Nodal Agency.
5. Application undergoes scrutiny and field verification by the concerned authorities.
6. Upon approval, sanction letter is issued and funds are released in installments as per progress.
7. Beneficiary must complete the project within the stipulated time and submit utilization certificates and completion reports.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds and state-specific allocation windows.
