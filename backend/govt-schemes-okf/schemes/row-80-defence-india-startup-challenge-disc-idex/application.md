## Process

1. Register on the iDEX website and login using credentials.
2. Complete the application form providing full details of a Single Point of Contact, Entity Details (for Startups/MSMEs), and Proposal Details including problem statement selection, funding level, technical and financial details, patents/research papers, and tentative business plan.
3. Upload required documents.
4. Submit the application.
5. Applications are screened by a duly constituted high-powered committee of defence and technology experts.
6. Shortlisted applications undergo evaluation by the High Powered Screening Committee (HPSC) on parameters including relevance, comprehensiveness, innovation, feasibility, capability, and viability.
7. Final winners are declared based on HPSC recommendations.
