# Defence India Startup Challenge (DISC) – iDEX

## Overview

Defence India Startup Challenge (DISC) is a sub-scheme under the Innovations for Defence Excellence (iDEX) initiative, launched by the Ministry of Defence in partnership with Atal Innovation Mission. It aims to support startups, MSMEs, and individual innovators in creating prototypes and commercializing products/solutions relevant to national defence and security. The challenge provides grant funding and additional support such as access to defence testing facilities, mentorship, and accelerator programs through iDEX partners.

## Objective

- Help create functional prototypes of products/technologies relevant for national security (prototyping)
- Spur fast-moving innovation in the India Defence sector
- Help new tech products/technologies find a market and early customer (commercialization) in the form of the Indian Defence Establishment
- Facilitate co-creation of novel, indigenized, and innovative technologies
- Create pathways for incorporation of cutting-edge technologies to be used by Armed Forces, DPSUs and other Nodal Agencies
- Transform defence innovation in India with the ultimate goal of delivering military-grade products that solve critical defence needs
- Create a culture of innovation in Indian defence & aerospace by engaging innovators through encouraging co-creation & co-innovation

## Important Conditions

- Individual innovators must establish a legal entity (public limited company, private limited company, partnership firm, LLP, one-person company, or sole proprietorship) prior to execution of the iDEX Grant Agreement
- A Startup/MSME can hold a maximum of five (5) active iDEX contracts at any given time, including one (1) contract under an ADITI Challenge
- Entities with common founders/promoters/related parties are collectively subject to the five-contract limit
- An entity holding three (3) or more active iDEX contracts where at least two (2) contracts have not received technical concurrence for Milestone 3 closure is ineligible for new challenges
- Grant amount may be increased beyond prescribed limit in special cases by the high-powered committee
- Funding is subject to milestone-based disbursement and cannot be used for prohibited expenditures like cost of land/buildings, establishment of R&D centres, writing books/reports, investments, interest on loans, bad debts, contributions/donations, fines/penalties, advocacy, losses from other businesses, or unapproved capital/operating expenditures
