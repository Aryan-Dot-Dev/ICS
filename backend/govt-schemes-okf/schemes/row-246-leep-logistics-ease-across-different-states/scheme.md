# LEEP – Logistics Ease Across Different States

## Overview

LEEP – Logistics Ease Across Different States is a government initiative aimed at improving logistics efficiency and reducing costs across Indian states by identifying bottlenecks, streamlining procedures, and enhancing infrastructure coordination. The scheme focuses on creating a unified logistics ecosystem through policy reforms, digital integration, and stakeholder collaboration to boost trade competitiveness.

## Objective

- Reduce logistics costs and improve efficiency across states
- Identify and address bottlenecks in the logistics value chain
- Enhance coordination between central and state agencies
- Promote digitalization and seamless movement of goods
- Strengthen infrastructure planning and multimodal integration
- Benchmark state-level logistics performance through LEEP index


## Target Beneficiaries

**MSME**, **logistics providers**, **state governments**, **industry stakeholders**


## Key Features

- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — assessments and updates are conducted periodically as part of ongoing logistics performance monitoring.
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

The scheme involves participation from state governments, logistics service providers, industry associations, and other stakeholders engaged in logistics and supply chain operations across India.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Improved logistics efficiency, reduced transit times and costs, enhanced supply chain reliability, better infrastructure utilization, data-driven policy insights, and increased competitiveness for businesses engaged in inter-state and international trade.
