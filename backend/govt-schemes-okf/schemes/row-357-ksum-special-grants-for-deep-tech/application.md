## Process

1. Apply through the KSUM website during the official call for applications.
2. Application is scrutinised by KSUM officials as part of initial screening; shortlisted applicants are notified via email.
3. Shortlisted startups present their idea and fund utilisation plan before the Expert Committee.
4. The Expert Committee evaluates based on Team, Product Innovation, Marketing, Financials, and Grant Utilisation Plan.
5. Approval recommendations are submitted for approval by the CEO/Chairman, KSUM.
6. Submit required documents (CA-certified No Dues & Margin Money Certificate with UDIN, revenue/investment proof, incorporation documents, PAN, KSUM Unique ID, bank details, balance sheet for last two years, stamp paper, etc.).
7. Execute agreement with KSUM at designated offices before fund release.
8. Grant is disbursed in milestone-based tranches (generally 50% first tranche, balance after progress review and utilisation certificate).
9. Submit interim progress report and utilisation certificate for second tranche release.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted year-round as per demand, with calls for proposals issued periodically and published through official channels.
