# KSUM Special Grants for Deep Tech

## Overview

KSUM Special Grants for Deep Tech is a state-level initiative by Kerala Startup Mission to support deep technology startups through financial assistance in the form of grants and seed funding. The scheme aims to help innovators move from idea validation to prototype development and early market readiness by reducing financial risk in early stages. It focuses on first-time founders, student innovators, women-led startups, and technology-driven ventures to ensure inclusive access to capital and build a strong pipeline of future-ready startups from Kerala.

## Objective

- Support innovators and startups at the critical phase when ideas are taking shape and products are yet to reach the market
- Provide non-dilutive grants and seed funding to help founders move from idea validation to prototype development and early market readiness
- Reduce financial risk in early stages to empower startups to focus on building robust solutions, validating demand, and preparing for scale
- Ensure inclusive access to capital for first-time founders, student innovators, women-led startups, and technology-driven ventures
- Strengthen the pipeline of future-ready startups from Kerala

## Important Conditions

- Founder salaries, purchase of fixed assets (land, building, high-end equipment, costly software above ₹2 lakhs), rent, and major infrastructure setup are not permitted under the grant
- Startups must bring margin money (20% for Productisation Grant, 30% for Market Acceleration Grant, 50% for Scale-up Grant)
- The grant is not generally given for service startups and SMEs working in trade and commerce
- Startups shall not have any other pending dues with any of the Government agencies, KSUM, Other incubators in the state and shall not be blacklisted by any Govt.
- agency in India
- The startup director should have a good CIBIL Score, preferably more than 750
