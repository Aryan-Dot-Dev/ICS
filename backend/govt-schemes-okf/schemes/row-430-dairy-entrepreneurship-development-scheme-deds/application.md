## Process

1. Identify the eligible component under DEDS (e.g., small dairy farm, calf rearing, vermicomposting, etc.).
2. Prepare a detailed project report including cost estimates, location details, and beneficiary information.
3. Approach a commercial bank, regional rural bank, or cooperative bank for loan sanction under the scheme.
4. Submit the project report and loan application to the bank along with required documents.
5. The bank forwards the proposal to NABARD for sanction and subsidy reservation.
6. After loan disbursement, set up the unit as per the approved plan.
7. Notify the bank upon completion for inspection and verification.
8. Upon successful verification, the subsidy amount is released by NABARD to the beneficiary's loan account.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds.
