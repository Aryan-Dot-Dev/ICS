# Dairy Entrepreneurship Development Scheme (DEDS)

## Overview

The Dairy Entrepreneurship Development Scheme (DEDS) is a centrally sponsored scheme implemented by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying (DAHD), Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Government of India. It aims to generate self-employment opportunities in the dairy sector by providing financial assistance for setting up small dairy farms and related activities. The scheme promotes the establishment of modern dairy farms, calf rearing units, vermicomposting, and other allied activities to enhance milk production and processing.

## Objective

- To generate self-employment opportunities in the dairy sector
- To promote setting up of modern dairy farms for production of clean milk
- To encourage heifer calf rearing for conservation and development of good breeding stock
- To bring about structural changes in the unorganized sector for initial processing of milk at the village level
- To upgrade traditional technology to handle milk on a commercial scale
- To provide value addition to milk through processing and marketing

## Important Conditions

- Subsidy is available only for new units and not for expansion or replacement of existing units
- Beneficiaries who have availed benefits under any other central or state government scheme for the same component are not eligible
- The unit must be inspected and verified by the bank and NABARD before subsidy release
- Subsidy is released only after successful verification and is credited to the beneficiary's loan account
- The beneficiary must maintain the unit for a minimum period of 5 years from the date of subsidy release
