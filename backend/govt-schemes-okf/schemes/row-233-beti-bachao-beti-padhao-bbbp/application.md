## Process

1. Identify the district-level BBBP implementation unit (usually under District Magistrate or Women and Child Development Officer).
2. Awareness and mobilization activities are conducted at the community level through Anganwadi workers, ASHA, and school teachers.
3. For financial benefits under linked schemes (e.g., Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana), visit a authorized bank or post office with the girl child’s birth certificate and parent/guardian ID proof.
4. Open SSY account and deposit funds as per rules.
5. Monitor progress through district-level BBBP task force meetings and periodic reviews.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline for account opening under linked schemes like Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana. BBBP activities are ongoing throughout the year.
