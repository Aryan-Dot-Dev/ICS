# Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (BBBP)

## Overview

Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (BBBP) is a joint initiative of the Ministry of Women and Child Development, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, and Ministry of Education, launched to address the declining child sex ratio and promote the education and empowerment of the girl child. The scheme focuses on preventing gender-biased sex-selective elimination, ensuring survival and protection of the girl child, and promoting her education and participation. It operates through a multi-sectoral approach involving awareness campaigns, enforcement of PC&PNDT Act, and incentivizing girl child education.

## Objective

- Prevent gender-biased sex-selective elimination
- Ensure survival and protection of the girl child
- Ensure education and participation of the girl child


## Target Beneficiaries

**girl child**, **families with girl child**, **communities in low child sex ratio districts**

## Eligibility

The scheme targets families and communities in districts identified with low Child Sex Ratio (CSR) as per Census data. There is no individual beneficiary registration; benefits are delivered through district-level interventions and awareness campaigns. Eligibility for specific incentives under BBBP-linked schemes (like Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana) is governed by those respective schemes.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Non-financial benefits include awareness campaigns, community mobilization, training of functionaries, and monitoring of CSR. Financial benefits are delivered through linked schemes such as Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY), which offers tax-free interest and tax deduction under Section 80C.
