## Process

1. NHAI invites bids for HAM projects through competitive bidding.
2. Interested applicants must purchase the bid documents by paying the prescribed fee.
3. Applicants must submit their pre-qualification or bid documents online via the NHAI portal https://nhai.gov.in.
4. Submission includes technical and financial proposals along with required documents.
5. Bids are opened and evaluated as per the evaluation criteria.
6. The selected concessionaire is issued a Letter of Award (LOA).
7. The concessionaire signs the Concession Agreement with NHAI.
8. Financial closure is achieved within the stipulated period.
9. Construction begins as per the agreed schedule.
