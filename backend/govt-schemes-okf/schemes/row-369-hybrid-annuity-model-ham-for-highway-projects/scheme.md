# Hybrid Annuity Model (HAM) for Highway Projects

## Overview

The Hybrid Annuity Model (HAM) for Highway Projects is a public-private partnership (PPP) model used by the National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) for the development, operation, and maintenance of national highway projects. Under HAM, the government provides 40% of the project cost as construction support in five equal instalments during the construction period, while the remaining 60% is paid as annuity payments over the operation period along with interest. The model aims to reduce the financial burden on private developers and ensure timely project completion.

## Objective

- To develop national highway projects through public-private partnership
- To reduce the upfront financial burden on private concessionaires
- To ensure timely completion and quality execution of highway projects
- To provide a stable return to private investors through annuity payments
- To transfer construction risk to the government while retaining traffic risk with the concessionaire
- To promote private sector participation in road infrastructure development

## Eligibility

Private companies, consortia, or special purpose vehicles (SPVs) incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 or earlier, with relevant experience in highway construction, operation, and maintenance, meeting the technical and financial criteria specified in the bid documents, and capable of providing the required equity and guarantees as per NHAI guidelines.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Reduced equity requirement for private developers (only 40% of project cost as construction support), annuity payments with interest over the operation period, lower financial risk compared to BOT models, government bears construction cost overruns, and the model attracts investment due to assured returns.

## Important Conditions

- The 40% construction support is released in five equal instalments linked to milestones
- Annuity payments are subject to satisfactory operation and maintenance as per standards
- The concessionaire bears traffic risk;
- revenue shortfall affects returns
- The concession period typically includes construction period plus 15 years of operation
- NHAI retains the right to terminate the concession for material breach
