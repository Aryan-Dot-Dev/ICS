# EV Charging Infrastructure Development Scheme

## Overview

The Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) is a statutory body under the Ministry of Power, Government of India, established on 1st March 2002 under the Energy Conservation Act, 2001. Its primary mission is to reduce the energy intensity of the Indian economy by developing policies and strategies focused on self-regulation and market principles. BEE promotes energy efficiency across sectors through schemes like Perform, Achieve and Trade (PAT), Standards & Labelling, and initiatives supporting electric vehicle charging infrastructure. It acts as the Central Nodal Agency for deploying public EV-charging infrastructure as per guidelines issued on 14th January 2022.

## Objective

- Develop policies and programmes on efficient use of energy and its conservation with stakeholder involvement
- Plan, manage and implement energy conservation initiatives as envisaged in the EC Act
- Assume leadership and provide policy framework for national energy efficiency efforts
- Demonstrate energy efficiency delivery mechanisms through Public-Private Partnership (PPP)
- Establish systems to measure, monitor and verify energy efficiency results at individual and national levels
- Leverage multi-lateral, bi-lateral and private sector support for programme implementation
- Promote awareness of energy savings and energy conservation among targeted consumer groups

## Important Conditions

- EV charging infrastructure deployment requires state-level designation of SNAs;
- currently 27 states have designated SNAs.
- As of 31st March 2023, 28 states have announced EV policies, of which 25 are notified and 3 are under consultation.
- Installation and operation must comply with technical standards and safety norms issued by BEE and relevant authorities.
