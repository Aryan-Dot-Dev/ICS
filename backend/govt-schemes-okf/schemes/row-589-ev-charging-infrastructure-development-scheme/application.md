## Process

1. States must designate a State Nodal Agency (SNA) to coordinate deployment of public EV-charging infrastructure as per MoP guidelines dated 14th January
2. 2. SNAs oversee the installation and operation of EV charging stations in public areas.
3. BEE supports SNAs through policy measures, technical guidance, and coordination with central agencies.
4. Entities interested in setting up EV charging infrastructure can approach their respective SNA for approvals and guidelines.
5. Compliance with technical standards and safety norms is required for installation and operation.
