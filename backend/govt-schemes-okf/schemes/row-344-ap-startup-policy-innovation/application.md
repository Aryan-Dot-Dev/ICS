## Process

1. Register the startup on the official AP Innovation Portal (https://apinnovation.ap.gov.in).
2. Log in and fill out the online application form for the AP Startup Policy & Innovation benefits.
3. Upload the required documents including Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, GST details, pitch deck, and details of innovation.
4. Obtain a recommendation letter from a recognized incubation center in Andhra Pradesh.
5. Submit the completed application online.
6. The application is scrutinized by the State Level Committee.
7. Upon approval, the startup receives an approval letter and can proceed to claim benefits.
8. For financial claims, submit bills and vouchers along with utilization reports to the implementing agency for reimbursement or disbursement.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds.
