# AP Startup Policy & Innovation

## Overview

The AP Startup Policy & Innovation is a state-level initiative by the Government of Andhra Pradesh aimed at fostering a vibrant startup ecosystem through financial support, infrastructure, mentorship, and regulatory facilitation. It focuses on promoting innovation, entrepreneurship, and job creation across key sectors such as IT, biotechnology, agriculture, and manufacturing. The policy provides a comprehensive framework for startups at various stages, from ideation to scaling, with dedicated funds and incubation support.

## Objective

- Promote innovation and entrepreneurship across the state
- Provide financial assistance to startups at various stages of growth
- Establish and strengthen incubation centers and innovation hubs
- Facilitate access to mentorship, networking, and market linkages
- Encourage sector-specific startups in thrust areas like IT, biotech, agri-tech, and manufacturing
- Support inclusive entrepreneurship by focusing on women, SC/ST, and differently abled individuals
- Create a conducive policy and regulatory environment for startups
- Generate employment and drive economic growth through startup-led innovation

## Key Features

- Fund size: ₹200 crore
- Grant/assistance per entity: Up to INR 10 Lakhs as seed grant; up to INR 2 Lakhs for Indian patent and INR 5 Lakhs for international patent reimbursement; up to INR 1 Lakh for certification reimbursement; INR 10,000 per seat per month for incubation support (max 12 months)
- Geographic scope: Andhra Pradesh only (state-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds.
- Portal: https://apinnovation.ap.gov.in
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

The applicant must be a startup registered in Andhra Pradesh as a Private Limited Company, Limited Liability Partnership (LLP), or Registered Partnership Firm. ## Important Conditions

- Financial benefits are subject to the availability of funds under the state budget
- Startups must retain at least 51% Indian promoter shareholding to remain eligible
- Benefits such as seed grant and incubation support are one-time and non-recurring
- Patent and certification reimbursements are contingent upon successful grant or certification
- The startup must continue to operate in Andhra Pradesh for a minimum of 3 years after availing benefits
- Any misrepresentation or submission of false documents will lead to rejection and blacklisting
