# Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units (CGFMU)

## Overview

Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units (CGFMU) is a Central Sector Scheme administered by NCGTC that provides guarantee coverage for loans up to Rs.10 lakh sanctioned by Banks, NBFCs, MFIs, or other financial intermediaries to eligible micro units. It also covers overdraft loan amounts of Rs.10,000 sanctioned under PMJDY accounts. The scheme aims to facilitate collateral-free credit access for micro enterprises by sharing lending risk with financial institutions.

## Objective

- Provide guarantee coverage for loans up to Rs.10 lakh to eligible micro units
- Cover overdraft loan amounts of Rs.10,000 under PMJDY accounts
- Facilitate collateral-free credit access for micro enterprises
- Strengthen flow of institutional credit to micro units
- Reduce barriers to credit access for underserved sections

- Scheme is administered by NCGTC as a trustee company for the Credit Guarantee Fund
