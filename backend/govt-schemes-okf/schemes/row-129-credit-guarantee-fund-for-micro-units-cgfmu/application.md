## Process

1. Eligible Lending Institutions (ELIs) must register with NCGTC by submitting a signed and stamped undertaking along with Board Resolution.
2. Upon successful registration, login credentials are created for the ELI on NCGTC’s portal.
3. The ELI can then apply for guarantee cover on loans extended to eligible micro units via the NCGTC portal.
4. Guarantee fee is payable monthly by the 7th day of each month for the outstanding loan amount.
