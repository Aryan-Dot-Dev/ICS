# Pradhan Mantri Micro Food Processing Enterprises (PM-FME)

## Overview

The Pradhan Mantri Micro Food Processing Enterprises (PM-FME) Scheme is a centrally sponsored scheme launched by the Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI) to provide financial, technical, and business support for the upgradation of existing micro food processing enterprises. It aims to enhance the competitiveness of individual micro enterprises, promote the 'One District One Product' (ODOP) approach, and support the formalization of the sector through credit-linked subsidies, seed capital, and capacity building. The scheme covers various components including individual micro enterprises, groups (FPOs/SHGs/Cooperatives), and common infrastructure.

## Objective

- Provide credit-linked capital subsidy @35% of eligible project cost with a maximum of Rs. 10.0 lakhs per unit for upgradation or setting up of new units for individual micro enterprises
- Provide credit-linked capital subsidy @35% of eligible project cost with a maximum of Rs. 3.00 Crore for groups (FPOs/FPCs/Cooperatives/SHGs and their federation/Govt. Agencies) for establishing food processing lines with common infrastructure/value chain/incubation centers
- Provide seed capital @ Rs. 40,000/- per member of SHG for working capital and purchase of small tools, maximum upto Rs. 4 Lakhs per SHG
- Provide 50% financial grant for Branding and Marketing Support to groups of FPOs/SHGs/Cooperatives or a SPV of micro food processing enterprises
- Provide training on Food Processing Entrepreneurship Development Programme to PMFME beneficiaries under the Capacity Building component
- Promote the One District One Product (ODOP) approach to reap benefits of scale in procurement, availing common services, and marketing of products
- Support the formalization and mainstreaming of unorganized home-based or micro food processing units
- Enhance competitiveness and promote value addition in the micro food processing sector

## Important Conditions

- Subsidy is credit-linked and released in installments based on term loan disbursement and project progress
- Promoter's contribution (minimum 10%) and term loan (maximum 55%) are required as part of the means of finance
- Land development, building, and civil construction costs are not eligible for subsidy under the scheme
- The scheme does not cover working capital requirements directly (except seed capital for SHGs)
- Beneficiaries must comply with monitoring, reporting, and utilization requirements post-sanction
- The subsidy amount is capped at Rs. 10.0 lakhs per unit for individual enterprises and Rs. 3.00 Crore for groups regardless of actual eligible project cost
- Seed capital for SHGs is provided as grant to SHG Federation by SRLM/SULM through SNA for loan to SHG members
- Marketing and branding support is limited to 50% financial grant for groups only
