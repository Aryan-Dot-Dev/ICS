# Meity's Data Centre Policy

## Overview

Meity's Data Centre Policy is a strategic initiative under the Digital India programme aimed at establishing a robust ecosystem for data centres in India. The policy focuses on attracting investments, ensuring reliable power and connectivity, and promoting sustainable and secure data infrastructure. It seeks to position India as a global hub for data storage, processing, and cloud services by leveraging the country's growing digital demand and strategic location.

## Objective

- Attract domestic and foreign investment in data centre infrastructure
- Ensure reliable power supply and high-speed connectivity for data centres
- Promote energy efficiency and adoption of renewable energy in data centres
- Enhance data security and compliance with global standards
- Generate employment and support local economic development
- Position India as a preferred destination for global data hosting and cloud services
- Support the growth of digital services, AI, IoT, and emerging technologies
- Streamline regulatory clearances and single-window approvals for data centre projects

## Important Conditions

- Incentives are subject to availability of funds and approval of the empowered committee
- Projects must achieve commercial operation date (COD) within 24 months of approval
- Subsidy is clawed back if the data centre is not operational for commercial use within the stipulated time
- Benefits are not transferable to third parties without prior approval
- Compliance with MeitY's data localisation and security guidelines is mandatory
