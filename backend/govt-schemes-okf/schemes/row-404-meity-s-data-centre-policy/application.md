## Process

1. Submit a detailed project report (DPR) along with land details, power and connectivity plans, and environmental clearances.
2. Apply online through the MeitY Data Centre Policy portal or designated nodal agency website.
3. Undergo technical and financial appraisal by the empowered committee.
4. Receive in-principle approval based on eligibility and viability.
5. Sign memorandum of understanding (MoU) with MeitY or its agency.
6. Implement the project as per approved timelines.
7. Submit completion report and utilization certificate for disbursement of incentives.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to budget availability.
