## Process

1. Identify suitable feedstock and site for CBG plant setup.
2. Prepare Detailed Project Report (DPR) including technical, financial, and environmental aspects.
3. Apply online via the SATAT portal or through the concerned OMC.
4. Submit application along with DPR, land documents, feedstock agreement, and other required documents.
5. Application is reviewed by the OMC and forwarded to the Steering Committee for approval.
6. Upon approval, sign off-take agreement with OMC.
7. Install CBG plant as per approved DPR and technical standards.
8. Commission plant and commence CBG production for off-take by OMC.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year.
