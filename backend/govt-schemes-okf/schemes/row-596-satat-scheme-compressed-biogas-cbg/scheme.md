# SATAT Scheme – Compressed Biogas (CBG)

## Overview

The SATAT Scheme (Sustainable Alternative Towards Affordable Transportation) aims to promote Compressed Biogas (CBG) as a green transport fuel by setting up CBG plants and ensuring off-take by Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs). It supports entrepreneurs in establishing CBG production units using biomass and waste feedstock, contributing to waste-to-wealth initiatives and reducing dependence on fossil fuels. The scheme fosters a market-driven ecosystem for CBG production, procurement, and utilization in the transportation sector.

## Objective

- Promote production of Compressed Biogas (CBG) from biomass and waste sources
- Ensure off-take of CBG by Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) for use as automotive fuel
- Reduce dependence on imported natural gas and crude oil
- Support sustainable waste management and circular economy
- Generate employment and entrepreneurial opportunities in rural and industrial areas
- Contribute to national goals of carbon reduction and clean energy transition

Any entrepreneur, MSME, cooperative society, self-help group, or industrial unit can apply to set up a CBG plant under the SATAT Scheme. ## Important Conditions

- Subsidy is limited to one project per entrepreneur/entity
- Off-take guarantee by OMCs is subject to plant performance and gas quality standards
- Project must be commissioned within 18 months of approval to avail subsidy
- CBG must meet specifications as per IS 16087:2016 for use as automotive fuel
