# Bihar Industrial Investment Promotion Policy

## Overview

The Bihar Industrial Investment Promotion Policy is a state-level initiative aimed at promoting industrial development and Industrial Investment Promotion Policy is a state-level scheme designed to attract investment, promote industrial growth, and generate employment in Bihar by offering fiscal incentives, infrastructure support, and land acquisition, and infrastructure development to create a conducive environment for industries. The policy aims to position Bihar as a preferred destination for domestic and international investors by offering a range of fiscal and non-fiscal incentives.

## Objective

- Attract domestic and foreign direct investment into Bihar
- Promote sustainable industrial development and employment generation
- Develop world-class industrial infrastructure and logistics
- Enhance competitiveness of Bihar-based industries through fiscal incentives
- Support MSMEs and ancillary units for inclusive industrial growth
- Encourage export-oriented and high-technology industries
- Ensure balanced regional development across all districts of Bihar
- Simplify regulatory procedures and ensure single-window clearance
