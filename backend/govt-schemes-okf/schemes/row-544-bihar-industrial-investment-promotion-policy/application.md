## Process

1. Submit online application via the Udyog Bihar portal (https://udyog.bihar.gov.in).
2. Obtain acknowledgment and project registration number.
3. Submit detailed project report (DPR) along with required documents.
4. Application scrutinized by District Industries Centre (DIC).
5. Forwarded to State Level Committee for approval.
6. Upon approval, submit claim for subsidy with proof of investment and commencement of production.
7. Subsidy disbursed after verification by authorized agency.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications accepted throughout the year as per policy validity
