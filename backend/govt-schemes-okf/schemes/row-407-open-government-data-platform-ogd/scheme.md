# Open Government Data Platform (OGD)

## Overview

The Open Government Data (OGD) Platform India, accessible at data.gov.in, is a national initiative under the Digital India program to proactively publish government datasets in open, machine-readable formats for public use, innovation, and transparency. It serves as a centralized repository where ministries, departments, and state governments share non-sensitive data across sectors such as health, education, agriculture, and finance. The platform enables citizens, researchers, developers, and startups to access, visualize, and reuse government data for societal and economic benefit.

## Objective

- Proactively publish government datasets in open formats
- Enable innovation and entrepreneurship through open data reuse
- Enhance transparency and accountability in governance
- Support evidence-based policy making and research
- Facilitate development of data-driven applications and services
- Promote collaboration between government, academia, and industry
- Ensure data accessibility in machine-readable formats
- Create a unified national data repository

## Eligibility

Any individual, organization, startup, researcher, or developer can access and use the datasets available on the OGD Platform (data.gov.in) without restriction, subject to the terms of the National Data Sharing and Accessibility Policy (NDSAP) and applicable licenses.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Free access to thousands of government datasets in machine-readable formats (CSV, JSON, XML, etc.), data visualization tools, APIs for developers, metadata cataloging, multilingual interface, and the ability to create, share, and reuse data-driven applications and insights for commercial or non-commercial purposes.
