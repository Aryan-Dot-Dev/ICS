# UPI Innovation Fund (NPCI)

## Overview

The UPI Innovation Fund (NPCI) is a scheme under the National Payments Corporation of India aimed at fostering innovation in the Unified Payments Interface (UPI) ecosystem. It supports startups and innovators developing solutions that enhance UPI's functionality, security, and reach. The fund is part of NPCI's broader initiative to strengthen India's digital payments infrastructure through collaboration with fintech entities.

## Objective

- Promote innovation in UPI-based payment solutions
- Enhance security and user experience in digital payments
- Support development of scalable UPI-integrated technologies
- Encourage financial inclusion through UPI advancements
- Strengthen the UPI ecosystem via partnerships with fintech startups

## Eligibility

Startups and innovators working on UPI-related technologies, including but not limited to payment gateways, UPI-enabled apps, security solutions, and value-added services on the UPI platform, are eligible to apply. Entities must be registered in India and demonstrate alignment with NPCI's vision for digital payments.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Selected beneficiaries receive financial support, technical mentorship from NPCI experts, access to UPI sandbox environments, potential integration with NPCI's product suite, and visibility through NPCI's innovation network. Non-financial benefits include guidance on compliance, scalability, and market readiness.
