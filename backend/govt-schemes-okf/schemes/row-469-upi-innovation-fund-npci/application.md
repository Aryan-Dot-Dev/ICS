## Process

2. Review the UPI Innovation Fund guidelines and eligibility criteria.
3. Submit a detailed proposal outlining the innovation, its impact on UPI, and implementation plan.
4. Await evaluation by the NPCI innovation committee.
5. If shortlisted, participate in technical discussions and due diligence.
6. Receive approval and sign agreement for fund disbursement.
