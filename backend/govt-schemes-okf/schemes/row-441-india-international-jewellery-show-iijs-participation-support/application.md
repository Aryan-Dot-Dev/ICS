## Process

1. Visit https://registration.gjepc.org/login.php and log in using existing username and password (same as used for membership registration/renewal). New members must register first.
2. Fill out the space application form for either IIJS Prime Assure Scheme (6 shows) or IIJS Bharat – Premiere 2026 (single show).
3. Submit the application form along with advance payment on or before 30th April 2026 to avail the Discount Benefit Offer and retain preference status.
4. Failure to submit or incomplete submission by 30th April 2026 results in automatic rejection without notice and loss of preference for space allotment at IIJS Bharat - Premiere 2026 (single show) or for the IIJS Prime Assure Scheme across IIJS Bharat - Premiere 2026 & 2027, IIJS Bharat - Signature 2027 & 2028, and IIJS Bharat - Tritiya 2027 & 2028.

## Deadlines

- The space application for all 6 IIJS Bharat shows for the year 2026–28 opens on 13th April 2026. Applications must be submitted on or before 30th April 2026 to avail the Discount Benefit Offer and preference status. Late or incomplete submissions after this date result in automatic rejection.
