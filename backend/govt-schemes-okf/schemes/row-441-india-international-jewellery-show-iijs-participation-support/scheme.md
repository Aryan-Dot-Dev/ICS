# India International Jewellery Show (IIJS) Participation Support

## Overview

The India International Jewellery Show (IIJS) Participation Support scheme, administered by the Gem and Jewellery Export Promotion Council (GJEPC), provides financial and logistical assistance to GJEPC members for participation in IIJS Bharat shows, including IIJS Bharat - Premiere, Signature, and Tritiya. The scheme aims to enhance export opportunities, ensure long-term business planning, and offer preferential benefits such as rate locks, discounted participation fees, and exclusive facilities for committed exhibitors across multiple financial years.

## Objective

- Facilitate long-term business planning for exhibitors through multi-year participation commitments
- Ensure economy of participation via discounted rates and preferential allotment
- Provide rate lock protection against price fluctuations for two financial years
- Offer exclusive benefits such as complimentary hotel rooms and food coupons at IIJS Bharat - Tritiya
- Enable priority access to hotel bookings, networking events, and sponsorship opportunities
- Support global sourcing and market access for gem and jewellery exporters
- Strengthen IIJS Bharat shows as a premier global B2B platform


## Target Beneficiaries

**GJEPC members**, **exporters**, **MSME**


## Key Features

- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: The space application for all 6 IIJS Bharat shows for the year 2026–28 opens on 13th April 2026. Applications must be submitted on or before 30th April 2026 to avail the Discount Benefit Offer and preference status. Late or incomplete submissions after this date result in automatic rejection.

## Important Conditions

- Submission of application does not guarantee stall allotment;
- allotment is subject to availability of space.
- Exhibitors who cancel participation during the Prime Assure period (2026–28) or are not allotted space will not be considered Prime Assure Exhibitors for remaining shows and will not be eligible for any Prime Assure benefits.
- The IIJS Prime Assure Scheme is optional and not mandatory for participation in any IIJS Bharat edition.
- Due to renovation at Hall 1 of Bombay Exhibition Centre (BEC), Goregaon, during financial year 2026–27, IIJS Bharat – Premiere applicants at Hall 1 will be allowed to apply only for Retention / Change of Location & Less area (More area option not available).
- No counter sale will be permitted during the Exhibition;
- violators may be debarred from participating in current and future exhibitions organized by GJEPC.
