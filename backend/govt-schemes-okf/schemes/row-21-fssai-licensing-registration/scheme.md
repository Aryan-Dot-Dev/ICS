# FSSAI Licensing & Registration

## Overview

FoSCoS (Food Safety Compliance System) is the online platform operated by the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) for food business operators to apply for, renew, and manage FSSAI licenses and registrations. The system ensures compliance with food safety standards under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006. It supports licensing for manufacturers, traders, retailers, importers, and other food-related entities across India.

## Objective

- Ensure food safety and hygiene standards across the food supply chain
- Provide a unified online platform for FSSAI licensing and registration
- Enable real-time tracking and monitoring of food business compliance
- Facilitate ease of doing business for food operators through digital services
- Strengthen regulatory oversight and enforcement of food safety laws


## Target Beneficiaries

**food business operators**, **manufacturers**, **traders**, **retailers**, **importers**, **MSME**


## Key Features

- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — applications can be submitted throughout the year; license/registration must be renewed periodically as per validity (1 to 5 years).
- Portal: https://foscos.fssai.gov.in
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

Any person or entity engaged in food business activities including manufacturing, processing, packaging, storage, distribution, sale, or import of food products must obtain FSSAI license or registration based on turnover and scale of operation. Legal compliance to operate food business, consumer trust and brand reputation, access to government tenders and markets, facilitation of food exports, eligibility for government schemes and subsidies, and ability to display FSSAI logo on products.

## Important Conditions

- License type (Basic, State, Central) depends on annual turnover and nature of food business
- Non-compliance may lead to penalties, fines, or closure of business under FSS Act, 2006
- Annual returns must be filed by licensed food businesses
- System undergoes daily maintenance from 11:30 PM to 3:00 AM;
- avoid operations during this window
