## Process

1. Visit the FoSCoS portal at https://foscos.fssai.gov.in
2. Sign up or log in using existing credentials
3. Select 'Apply for License/Registration'
4. Choose the appropriate license type (Basic, State, or Central) based on turnover and business scale
5. Fill in the online application form with business and product details
6. Upload required documents
7. Pay the applicable fee online
8. Submit the application and track status via the portal
9. Upon approval, download the license/registration certificate.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications can be submitted throughout the year; license/registration must be renewed periodically as per validity (1 to 5 years).
