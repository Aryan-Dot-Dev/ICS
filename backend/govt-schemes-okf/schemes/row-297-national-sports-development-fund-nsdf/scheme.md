# National Sports Development Fund (NSDF)

## Overview

The National Sports Development Fund (NSDF) was established in 1998 under the Charitable Endowments Act, 1890, to mobilize resources from government and non-government sources for the promotion of sports and games in India. It aims to support excellence in sports by providing financial assistance to athletes, coaches, and sports institutions for training, equipment, and participation in national and international competitions. The fund operates under the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports and accepts contributions from various stakeholders including public sector undertakings and private entities.

## Objective

- Mobilize resources from government and non-government sources for sports development
- Provide financial assistance to athletes for training and competition
- Support coaches and sports personnel in skill upgradation
- Assist sports institutions in infrastructure and equipment development
- Promote excellence in sports at national and international levels
- Encourage public-private partnership in sports development


## Target Beneficiaries

**athletes**, **coaches**, **sports institutions**


## Key Features

- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — applications can be submitted throughout the year as per the needs of athletes and institutions.
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

Individual athletes, coaches, sports persons, and sports institutions recognized by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports or respective National Sports Federations are eligible to apply for assistance from the NSDF. Applications must be recommended by the concerned sports federation or state sports council.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial assistance for training, equipment, participation in national and international competitions, exposure trips, and sports science support. Non-financial benefits include facilitation of access to training facilities, coordination with sports authorities, and support for career development of athletes.

- All applications must be routed through recognized sports federations or state councils
- Priority is given to Olympic, Asian Games, and Commonwealth Games disciplines
- Funding is not provided for commercial or profit-oriented sports activities
