# Digital MSME Scheme

## Overview

The Digital MSME Scheme aims to promote the adoption of digital technologies among Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) to enhance their competitiveness, productivity, and market reach. It supports MSMEs in integrating digital tools such as cloud computing, AI, IoT, and digital marketing into their operations. The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MoMSME) and focuses on enabling digital transformation across various sectors. It encourages MSMEs to leverage technology for innovation, scalability, and sustainability.

## Objective

- Promote adoption of digital technologies in MSMEs
- Enhance productivity and competitiveness through digital tools
- Support integration of cloud computing, AI, IoT, and digital marketing
- Enable MSMEs to access digital infrastructure and services
- Foster innovation and scalability in MSME sector
- Improve market access and customer reach via digital platforms

## Key Features

- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds.
- Portal: https://digital.msme.gov.in
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

The scheme is open to all Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) registered under the MSME Act, 2006, including manufacturing and service enterprises. Enterprises must have a valid Udyam Registration Certificate. There are no restrictions based on sector, location, or ownership profile, as long as the enterprise meets the MSME classification criteria.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial assistance in the form of subsidy for adopting digital technologies; access to digital infrastructure and services; improved operational efficiency and productivity; enhanced market reach through e-commerce and digital marketing; better data management and analytics capabilities; support for innovation and technology upgradation; reduced operational costs through automation.

## Important Conditions

- Subsidy is limited to 50% of project cost, maximum INR 10 Lakhs per unit
- Only digital technologies from empanelled vendors are eligible for subsidy
- Subsidy is released post-implementation and verification
- Enterprises must bear 50% of the project cost
- Funds are subject to availability and allocated on first-come-first-served basis
