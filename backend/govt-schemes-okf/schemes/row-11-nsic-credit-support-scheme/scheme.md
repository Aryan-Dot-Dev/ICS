# NSIC Credit Support Scheme

## Overview

The NSIC Credit Support Scheme provides credit support to MSMEs for financing the purchase of raw material Assistance Scheme (RMA) by NSIC provides financial assistance to MSMEs for the procurement of raw materials (both indigenous and imported) against the security of a Bank Guarantee. The scheme enables MSMEs to focus on manufacturing quality products by facilitating bulk purchases, cash discounts, and extended credit up to 180 days. It is designed to enhance the competitiveness of MSMEs by reducing working capital constraints and improving procurement efficiency.

## Objective

- Facilitate procurement of raw material with credit support up to 180 days
- Help MSMEs avail economics of purchases like bulk purchase and cash discount
- Enable MSMEs to focus better on manufacturing quality products
- Provide financial assistance for raw material procurement against Bank Guarantee security

## Eligibility

MSME units with Udyam Registration are eligible to apply for Raw Material Assistance. The scheme applies to units engaged in manufacturing and services; traders are not eligible. Units must have commenced commercial production but may not have completed one year of existence for provisional registration with a monetary limit of Rs. 5.00 Lacs.

## Important Conditions

- Security in the form of Bank Guarantee from Approved Banks is mandatory
- Concessional interest rates apply only if timely repayments are made;
- otherwise, normal rates apply
- Penal charge on delayed payment beyond 180 days is 1.25% per quarter over and above normal rate
- Processing fee applicable on new sanctions and renewals
- The scheme does not cover traders
- Units engaged in manufacture of medicine and drugs except Ayurvedic, Unani, Siddha, and Homeopathic medicines are not eligible
- Wholesale trading, retail trading, or commission agents are not eligible
- Units blacklisted or with proprietor/partner/director/Karta convicted of criminal offence are not eligible
