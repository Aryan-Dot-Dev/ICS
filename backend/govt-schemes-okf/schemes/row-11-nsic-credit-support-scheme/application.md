## Process

1. Apply for Raw Material Assistance only on the prescribed application forms.
2. Download the application forms from the link provided on the NSIC website.
3. Fill and submit the forms to the nearest NSIC Branch Office.
4. Submit the application form along with required documents.
5. Preliminary appraisal and unit inspection are carried out by NSIC.
6. Sanction of limit to the unit.
7. Signing of agreement between NSIC and the unit.
8. Disbursement of assistance to the unit.
