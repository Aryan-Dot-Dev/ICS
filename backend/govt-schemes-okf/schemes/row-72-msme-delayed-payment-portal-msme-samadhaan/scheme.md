# MSME Delayed Payment Portal (MSME SAMADHAAN)

## Overview

MSME SAMADHAAN (MSME Delayed Payment Portal) is an online platform launched by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises to facilitate the resolution of delayed payment disputes faced by micro and small enterprises (MSEs) when dealing with central ministries, departments, central public sector enterprises (CPSEs), and state governments. The portal enables MSEs to file applications for the recovery of delayed payments along with applicable compound interest under the MSMED Act, 2006. It serves as a monitoring and redressal mechanism to ensure timely payments to MSEs as mandated by law.

## Objective

- Provide a facilitative mechanism for settlement of delayed payment disputes involving micro and small enterprises
- Enable MSEs to file online applications for recovery of delayed payments and compound interest
- Monitor and track the status of delayed payment cases across central and state government entities
- Ensure compliance with the provisions of the MSMED Act, 2006 regarding timely payments to MSEs
- Reduce litigation and promote amicable settlement of payment disputes through a structured online process

## Eligibility

Any micro or small enterprise (MSE) that has supplied goods or services to a central ministry, department, central public sector enterprise (CPSE), or state government and is facing delayed payment beyond the agreed period or 45 days (as per MSMED Act, 2006) can file an application on the portal. The enterprise must have a valid Udyam Registration Number.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

The portal provides a structured online mechanism for filing delayed payment claims, facilitates the possibility of receiving compound interest on delayed amounts due date Act, 2006, and enables tracking of the case status. It reduces the need for litigation by providing a government-backed facilitative forum for dispute resolution.

## Important Conditions

- Only micro and small enterprises (not medium enterprises) are eligible to file applications
- The portal covers delayed payments from central government entities, CPSEs, and state governments — private sector buyers are not included
- The claim must be for goods/services already supplied;
- advance payments or future dues are not eligible
- The remedy is facilitative in nature;
- final payment depends on the respondent entity's action
