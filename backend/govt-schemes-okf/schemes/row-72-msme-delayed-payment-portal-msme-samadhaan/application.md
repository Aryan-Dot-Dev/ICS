## Process

1. Visit the MSME SAMADHAAN portal at https://samadhaan.msme.gov.in
2. Click on 'File Application' or 'Register Grievance'
3. Enter Udyam Registration Number and validate the enterprise
4. Fill in the details of the buyer (central ministry/department/CPSE/state government), invoice details, amount due, and date of supply
5. Upload supporting documents such as invoices, delivery challans, and correspondence
6. Submit the application and note the application number for tracking
7. Monitor the status of the application through the portal using the application number

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications can be filed at any time as delayed payment issues arise
