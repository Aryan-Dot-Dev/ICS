# PADMA Scheme

## Overview

The PADMA Scheme (Programme to Accelerate Development for MSME Advancement) is a five-year block-level economic transformation policy notified by the Haryana Government on August 17, 2023, aiming to develop MSME industrial clusters across all 143 blocks of the state's 22 districts. It focuses on creating enabling infrastructure, knowledge creation, awareness generation, and handholding support to accelerate entrepreneurship, strengthen industrial infrastructure, and generate sustainable employment at the grassroots level. The scheme includes multiple sub-schemes such as Capital Investment Subsidy, Cluster Infrastructure Development, Designing & Branding, Entrepreneurship Acceleration, and Interest Subsidy, each targeting specific aspects of MSME development.

## Objective

- Set up a new MSME cluster in each block, on about 4000 acres of land
- Enable setting up of 10,000+ new enterprises
- Attract new investment of INR 25,000-30,000 Crores
- Creation of 3,00,000 new employment (direct & indirect)
- Encouraging holistic socio-economic development and a robust industrial ecosystem at block level
- Empower the state’s youth to set up new enterprises by providing them opportunities to grow and be a part of the formal economy
- Promotion of locally relevant products and provide an opportunity to state farmers to broaden their horizon – from producers to processors
- Designing and implementing developmental interventions focused on micro and small enterprises at block level, by leveraging cluster approach, to give a thrust to sustainable employment and entrepreneurship opportunities

## Key Features

- Fund size: ₹1000 crore
- Grant/assistance per entity: Up to Rs. 30 lakh for women/SC/SHG owned micro and small units inside PADMA cluster; Rs. 25 lakh for other units inside; Rs. 15 lakh for women/SC/SHG and Rs. 12.50 lakh for others outside PADMA cluster
- Geographic scope: Haryana only (state-level)
- Deadline: The scheme shall commence with effect from the date of re-notification of PADMA policy i.e., 17.08.2023 and shall remain in operation for a period of 5 years up to 16.08.2028 or till the validity of the PADMA policy. Applicants shall have to apply on the prescribed form along with complete documents within 03 months from the start of commercial production or from the date of notification of the scheme or from the date of re-notification of PADMA policy, whichever is later.

## Important Conditions

- The unit availing incentive under this scheme will not be eligible to obtain incentives under any capital subsidy/similar scheme of other policies of the State Government.
- The unit shall remain in regular production for a period of 05 years from date of commercial production.
- The land for the project shall be arranged by the promoter either by purchase or on registered lease deed of at least 10 years.
- Maximum number of cases eligible per PADMA cluster to avail the benefit under the Capital Investment Subsidy Scheme: Maximum up to 20 units in first 5 PADMA clusters, up to 10 units in next 10 PADMA clusters (6th to 15th), up to 5 units in rest (above 16th), and up to 100 units outside PADMA block across the state on first come first basis.
- For PADMA Entrepreneurship Acceleration Scheme: Benefit provided to maximum 10 startup units per PADMA cluster and 50 startups outside the PADMA cluster during the policy period on first come first basis.
- For PADMA Interest Subsidy Scheme: The PADMA interest subsidy shall be allowed for 05 consecutive financial years, from the date of re-notification of said policy or date of loan sanctioned/disbursed, whichever is later.
- For PADMA Designing, Branding, Marketing & Exports Promotion Scheme: The unit may avail the benefit every year till 05 consecutive financial years, from the date of re-notification of said policy or as extended by State Government from time to time.
- The grant shall not be used for procurement/purchase of land under PADMA Cluster Infrastructure Development Scheme.
- IA shall have to ensure setting up of 20 manufacturing MSMEs (having FCI of at least INR 25 Crore in total) in the PADMA Cluster under PADMA Cluster Infrastructure Development Scheme.
- IA shall submit the progress reports to the Directorate of MSME mentioning the project progress and expenditure as and when required.
- IA shall be exclusively responsible for the day-to-day running of the PADMA enabling infrastructural facilities.
- IA may sell/lease industrial plots to MSMEs engaged in manufacturing & allied activities of PADMA approved products across the entire value chain of one sector or any activity approved by SLSC-PADMA.
