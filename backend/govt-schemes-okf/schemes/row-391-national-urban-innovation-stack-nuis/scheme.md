# National Urban Innovation Stack (NUIS)

## Overview

The National Urban Innovation Stack (NUIS) is a digital infrastructure initiative designed to support urban innovation by providing a unified platform for data, services, and applications across Indian cities. It aims to enable seamless integration of urban systems, foster innovation in smart city solutions, and improve governance through technology-driven approaches. NUIS serves as a foundational layer for urban stakeholders including startups, municipal bodies, and technology developers to build and deploy scalable urban applications.

## Objective

- Create a unified digital infrastructure for urban innovation across Indian cities
- Enable interoperability of urban data and services through standardized APIs
- Support startups and innovators in developing scalable smart city solutions
- Improve urban governance and service delivery via technology integration
- Foster collaboration between government, academia, and private sector in urban development


## Target Beneficiaries

**startups**, **technology developers**, **municipal corporations**, **urban local bodies**

## Eligibility

Eligible beneficiaries include startups, technology companies, urban local bodies, municipal corporations, and academic institutions working on urban innovation solutions that can integrate with the NUIS platform. Entities must demonstrate alignment with national urban development goals and possess the technical capability to build or deploy applications on the stack.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Access to standardized urban data sets and APIs, technical support for integration with NUIS, opportunities for pilot projects in smart cities, visibility through government urban innovation networks, and potential for scaling solutions across multiple cities. Financial support may be available through linked innovation funds or challenge grants administered via partner agencies.
