## Process

1. Visit the NUIS portal at nuisnet.gov.in to understand platform capabilities and documentation.
2. Register your entity or solution on the portal if required for access.
3. Review API documentation, data sets, and integration guidelines available on the platform.
4. Develop or adapt your urban solution to be compatible with NUIS standards.
5. Submit your solution for validation or listing through the portal’s innovation submission interface.
6. Await review by the NUIS governance team for approval and potential inclusion in the urban innovation registry.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round.
