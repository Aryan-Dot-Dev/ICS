# National Urban Livelihood Mission (NULM)

## Overview

Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana-National Urban Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NULM) is a centrally sponsored scheme launched by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs to reduce poverty and vulnerability of urban poor households by enabling access to self-employment and skilled wage employment opportunities. It aims to build strong grassroots-level institutions of the poor, provide shelter for the urban homeless, support street vendors, and promote financial inclusion and skill development. The mission operates through a three-tier structure at national, state, and city levels, with implementation led by Urban Local Bodies (ULBs).

## Objective

- Reduce poverty and vulnerability of urban poor households by enabling access to self-employment and skilled wage employment opportunities
- Build strong grassroots level institutions of the poor through social mobilisation and institution development
- Provide shelter equipped with essential services to the urban homeless in a phased manner
- Facilitate access to suitable spaces, institutional credit, social security, and skills for urban street vendors
- Ensure universal coverage of urban poor for skill development and credit facilities
- Promote convergence with schemes of relevant line ministries and state governments
- Encourage partnership with private sector for skill training, employment, and shelter operations
- Cater to especially vulnerable segments such as dependent children, aged, disabled, mentally ill, and recovering patients

## Eligibility

The primary target of DAY-NULM is the urban poor, including the urban homeless. As an interim measure, the target is the urban population identified as below poverty line by States/UTs. Coverage may be broadened to include families of disadvantaged groups like SCs, STs, women, minorities, disabled, etc., subject to a maximum of 25 percent of the urban poor population. ## Important Conditions

- Interest subsidy is released only on timely repayment of loan
- For group enterprises under SEP, minimum 3 members required with at least 70% from urban poor families and no more than one person from the same family
- Seed capital under PMFME convergence is repayable as a loan with interest not exceeding 6% per annum
- Funding for City Livelihood Centres and shelters is subject to availability and approval
- Training under EST&P requires third-party certification and assessment
- Shelter provision for urban homeless is to be achieved by 2022 as per guidelines
- Innovative & Special Projects (I&SP) component limited to up to 5% of total central funds
