# PLI for Large Scale Electronics Manufacturing

## Overview

The Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Scheme for Large Scale Electronics Manufacturing is a Government of India initiative aimed at boosting domestic manufacturing and exports in the electronics sector. It provides financial incentives to eligible companies based on incremental sales of goods manufactured in India over a period of five years. The scheme targets large-scale electronics manufacturing units to enhance India's position in global electronics value chains.

## Objective

- To enhance India's manufacturing capabilities in electronics
- To attract large investments in electronics manufacturing
- To reduce dependence on electronics imports
- To promote exports of manufactured electronics goods
- To create employment opportunities in the electronics sector
- To integrate India into global electronics supply chains


## Target Beneficiaries

**large-scale electronics manufacturers**, **domestic companies**, **foreign companies with manufacturing units in India**

## Eligibility

Eligible applicants include companies engaged in manufacturing of specified electronic goods in India, with a minimum threshold of investment and incremental sales as defined under the scheme guidelines. Both domestic and foreign companies setting up new manufacturing units or expanding existing ones in India are eligible to apply.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial incentives in the form of production-linked incentives on incremental sales of manufactured goods over the base year. Non-financial benefits include facilitation of clearances, single-window approvals, and support for setting up manufacturing units through coordination with central and state agencies.

## Important Conditions

- Incentive is disbursed only after verification of claims and achievement of threshold incremental sales
- Approval is contingent upon achieving committed investment within the stipulated timeframe
- Scheme period is limited to five years from the date of approval
- Only companies manufacturing in the specified target segments are eligible
