## Process

1. Submit application online through the designated portal
2. Provide details of proposed investment, manufacturing capacity, and employment generation
3. Upload required documents including incorporation certificates, project reports, and bank details
4. Application reviewed by the Project Management Agency (PMA)
5. Shortlisted applicants invited for presentation before the Empowered Committee
6. Final approval granted by the Empowered Committee based on evaluation criteria
7. Signing of approval letter and commencement of production
8. Annual submission of claims for incentive based on verified incremental sales
