# Food Testing Infrastructure – NABL Accreditation Support

## Overview

NABL (National Accreditation Board for Testing and Calibration Laboratories) provides voluntary accreditation services to testing laboratories, calibration laboratories, medical testing laboratories, proficiency testing providers, reference material producers, and biobanks in accordance with international standards such as ISO/IEC 17025, ISO 15189, ISO/IEC 17043, ISO 17034, and ISO 20387. The scheme ensures technical competence, international recognition, and facilitates acceptance of test/calibration results across borders through Mutual Recognition Arrangements (MRA with ILAC and APAC). NABL is self-financing and charges fees to conformity assessment bodies to cover operational costs.

## Objective

- To provide voluntary accreditation services to conformity assessment bodies in accordance with international standards
- To enhance stakeholders' confidence in the technical competence of accredited bodies
- To facilitate international acceptance of test and calibration results through MRA with ILAC and APAC
- To strengthen the accreditation system by empanelling competent assessors and fostering APAC/ILAC MRA
- To create awareness among stakeholders and initiate new programs supporting accreditation activities
- To pursue organisational excellence and provide high quality, value-driven services

## Important Conditions

- Accreditation is voluntary and not mandatory unless required by regulation or contract
- NABL does not provide consultancy or undertake conformity assessment activities itself to maintain impartiality
- The selection of assessors for training is at the sole discretion of NABL based on current/anticipated requirements
- Only online applications are accepted;
- hard copies are not considered
- Accreditation requires periodic surveillance and re-assessment to maintain validity
