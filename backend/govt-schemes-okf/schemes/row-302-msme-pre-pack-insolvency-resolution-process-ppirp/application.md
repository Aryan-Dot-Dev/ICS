## Process

1. The corporate debtor, in consultation with its financial creditors, prepares a resolution plan.
2. The resolution plan is approved by the Committee of Creditors (CoC) with a vote of not less than sixty-six percent of financial creditors by value.
3. The corporate debtor files an application for initiation of PPIRP before the Adjudicating Authority (National Company Law Tribunal) under section 30(6) of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code,
4. 4. The application is accompanied by the resolution plan, information memorandum, evaluation matrix, and a declaration under section 29A of the Code.
5. The Adjudicating Authority examines the application for compliance with section 30(2) and 29A of the Code.
6. If satisfied, the Adjudicating Authority approves the resolution plan, making it binding on all stakeholders.
7. The successful resolution applicant then implements the plan, including infusion of funds and payment of performance guarantee.

## Deadlines

- The application for initiation of PPIRP must be filed before the Adjudicating Authority after the resolution plan is approved by the Committee of Creditors. There is no fixed window; the process can be initiated at any time subject to eligibility. However, once initiated, the resolution process must be completed within 90 days from the date of application, extendable by the Adjudicating Authority for a further 60 days in exceptional circumstances, making a maximum of 150 days.
