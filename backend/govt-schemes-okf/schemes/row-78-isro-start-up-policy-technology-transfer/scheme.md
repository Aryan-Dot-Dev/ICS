# ISRO Start-up Policy & Technology Transfer

## Overview

The ISRO Start-up Policy & Technology Transfer initiative aims to foster innovation and entrepreneurship in the space sector by enabling startups to access ISRO's expertise, facilities, and technologies. It supports the development of space-related products and services through collaboration, mentorship, and technology licensing from ISRO centres. The policy encourages startups to contribute to India's space economy by leveraging ISRO's intellectual property and infrastructure.

## Objective

- Promote private sector participation in space activities
- Facilitate technology transfer from ISRO to startups
- Provide access to ISRO facilities and expertise for testing and validation
- Encourage innovation in space technologies and applications
- Support the growth of a self-reliant space ecosystem in India

**startups**

## Eligibility

Startups registered in India working on space technologies, applications, or allied sectors that can benefit from ISRO's expertise, facilities, or intellectual property are eligible to apply under the ISRO Start-up Policy.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Access to ISRO facilities for testing and validation, mentorship from ISRO scientists, guidance on technology development, opportunities for technology transfer and licensing of ISRO-developed technologies, and support in navigating regulatory and technical requirements for space activities.

## Important Conditions

- Access to facilities is subject to availability and prior commitments
- Technology transfer is governed by ISRO's IP policies and may involve licensing fees or royalties
- Collaboration does not guarantee equity investment or direct funding from ISRO
- Startups must comply with safety, security, and regulatory protocols at ISRO facilities
