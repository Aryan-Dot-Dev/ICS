## Process

1. Identify the relevant ISRO centre or unit based on the technology or domain of interest.
2. Submit a proposal detailing the startup's technology, objectives, and proposed collaboration with ISRO.
3. Proposal is reviewed by the ISRO centre for technical feasibility and alignment with policy goals.
4. If approved, discussions are held to define the scope of collaboration, including access to facilities, mentorship, or technology transfer.
5. A formal agreement (MoU or similar) is executed outlining terms of engagement, IP rights, and responsibilities.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted throughout the year.
