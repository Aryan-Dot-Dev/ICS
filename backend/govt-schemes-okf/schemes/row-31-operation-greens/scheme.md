# Operation Greens

## Overview

Operation Greens is a scheme announced in the Union Budget 2018-19 with an outlay of Rs.500 crore to promote Farmer Producers Organizations (FPOs), agri-logistics, processing facilities and professional management for the development of Tomato, Onion and Potato and later expanded to 2018. The scheme has two components: (I) Long Term Intervention-Integrated Value Chain Development Projects and (II) Short-Term Interventions. It aims to stabilize supply and prices of TOP crops and reduce post-harvest losses.

## Objective

- Promote Farmer Producers Organizations (FPOs)
- Develop agri-logistics
- Develop processing facilities
- Promote professional management
- Stabilize supply and prices of Tomato, Onion and Potato (TOP) crops
- Reduce post-harvest losses
