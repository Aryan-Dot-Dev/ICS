# Multimodal Logistics Park (MMLP) Scheme

## Overview

The Multimodal Logistics Park (MMLP) Scheme is a government initiative aimed at developing integrated logistics infrastructure to enhance freight movement efficiency across India. It focuses on creating multimodal hubs that connect various transport modes such as road, rail, and waterways to reduce logistics costs and improve supply chain performance. The scheme is implemented by NHIDCL under the Ministry of Road Transport & Highways and supports the Bharatmala Pariyojana and Act East Policy objectives. MMLPs are envisioned as key enablers for economic growth in strategic regions, particularly the North Eastern Region and border areas.

## Objective

- Develop integrated multimodal logistics infrastructure to reduce logistics costs
- Enhance connectivity between road, rail, and inland waterways for seamless freight movement
- Support the Act East Policy by improving regional connectivity in the North Eastern Region
- Promote economic growth in remote and strategic regions through improved infrastructure
- Facilitate international trade by strengthening links with neighbouring countries
- Develop automated parking, multi-modal logistics parks, and bus ports as part of IT-enabled infrastructure
- Act as a nodal agency for infrastructure development in border and international connectivity roads

## Eligibility

Entities involved in logistics, transport, warehousing, or industrial operations seeking to establish or upgrade multimodal logistics parks in India, particularly in the North Eastern Region, border areas, and strategic corridors under Bharatmala Pariyojana, are eligible to participate or benefit from the scheme through collaboration with NHIDCL.
