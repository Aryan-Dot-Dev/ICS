# Modular Employable Skills (MES) Scheme

## Overview

The Modular Employable Skills (MES) Scheme, under the Skill Development Initiative (SDI) scheme, was launched in 2007 by the erstwhile Directorate General of Employment and Training (DGE&T) to provide vocational training through Modular Employable Skills. It aimed to enhance employability by offering short-term, industry-relevant courses. The scheme was closed effective 31.03.2017.

## Objective

- To provide vocational training through Modular Employable Skills (MES) to enhance employability
- To offer short-term, industry-relevant courses for skill development
- To reduce unemployment among educated youth by providing employable skills
- To raise the quantitative and qualitative levels of industrial production through systematic training
- To cultivate a technical and industrial attitude in the younger generation

## Eligibility

Open to individuals seeking vocational training, particularly youth, school dropouts, and workers looking to upgrade skills; specific eligibility criteria varied by course and were defined under the Skill Development Initiative Scheme (SDIS) guidelines.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Participants received industry-relevant vocational training, certification upon completion, improved employability, and access to placement support. The scheme also facilitated self-employment and wage employment opportunities through skill certification.
