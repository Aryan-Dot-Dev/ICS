## Process

1. Vocational Training Providers (VTPs) applied for registration through the prescribed procedure.
2. Applications were evaluated by the concerned authorities.
3. Upon approval, VTPs signed contracts with the government.
4. Registered VTPs could then enroll candidates for training.
5. Candidates were registered by VTPs.
6. Training was conducted as per the course structure.
7. Assessment was carried out by Assessing Bodies.
8. Post-assessment, certificates were issued.
9. Training cost reimbursement was claimed by VTPs after submission of required documents.
