# Atal Tinkering Labs (ATL)

## Overview

Atal Tinkering Labs (ATL) is an initiative under the Atal Innovation Mission (AIM) by NITI Aayog to foster curiosity, creativity, and imagination in young minds by establishing tinkering labs in schools across India. The program aims to inculcate skills such as design mindset, computational thinking, adaptive learning, and physical computing among students from grades 6 to 12. ATL provides a workspace where students can shape their ideas through hands-on do-it-yourself mode and learn innovation skills.

## Objective

- To create workspaces where young minds can learn innovation skills
- To foster curiosity, creativity, and imagination in students
- To inculcate skills such as design mindset, computational thinking, adaptive learning, and physical computing
- To provide a platform for students to develop solutions to local problems
- To promote STEM education through experiential learning

## Eligibility

Schools (Government, Government-aided, or private) seeking to establish an Atal Tinkering Lab must apply through the online application portal. Eligible institutions include those managed by Government, local bodies, or private trusts/societies. The school must have minimum required infrastructure and commit to running the lab for at least 3-4 hours per week beyond regular school hours.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Selected schools receive grant-in-aid for establishment and operation of ATL, including one-time establishment charge of up to INR 10 lakh and operational expenses of up to INR 10 lakh for a maximum period of 5 years. Schools also get access to mentorship, training programs, innovation challenges, and opportunities to participate in national-level exhibitions and competitions.

## Important Conditions

- Grant is disbursed only upon achieving predefined milestones and submission of utilization certificates
- The lab must be operational for a minimum of 3-4 hours per week beyond regular school hours
- Failure to utilize funds or operate the lab may lead to withdrawal of grant and blacklisting
- The grant is non-transferable and can be used only for the designated ATL activities
