# Gujarat Startup Policy

## Overview

The Gujarat Startup Policy is a state-level initiative aimed at fostering innovation and entrepreneurship by providing support to startups through various incentives, incubation, and financial assistance. It seeks to build a robust startup ecosystem in Gujarat by offering recognition, mentorship, infrastructure, and funding opportunities. The policy aligns with the broader 'Atmanirbhar Bharat' vision and leverages Gujarat's strong industrial base and infrastructure to promote innovation-driven growth.

## Objective

- Promote entrepreneurship and innovation in the state
- Strengthen the startup ecosystem through incubation and mentorship
- Provide financial and non-financial support to early-stage startups
- Encourage innovation in thrust sectors and technology-driven ventures
- Facilitate access to funding, mentorship, and market linkages
- Support women, SC/ST, and young entrepreneurs through inclusive provisions
- Enhance ease of doing startups via regulatory simplification and single-window clearance
- Promote research, development, and innovation in collaboration with academic and R&D institutions
