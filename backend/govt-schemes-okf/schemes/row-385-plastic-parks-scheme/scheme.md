# Plastic Parks Scheme

## Overview

The Plastic Parks Scheme aims to set up need-based Plastic Parks with common infrastructure facilities to promote investment, boost domestic manufacturing, and enhance competitiveness in the plastic industry. It provides financial assistance for developing common infrastructure in Plastic Parks, including internal roads, drainage, water supply, power distribution, and effluent treatment plants. The scheme supports the establishment of plastic processing units and ancillary industries within these parks to create employment and promote sustainable growth in the sector.

## Objective

- To set up need-based Plastic Parks with common infrastructure facilities
- To promote investment in the plastic processing sector
- To enhance competitiveness and domestic manufacturing in the plastics industry
- To create employment opportunities through cluster-based development
- To ensure sustainable and environmentally responsible growth of plastic parks

- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — proposals can be submitted throughout the year as per the scheme guidelines.
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

State Governments or their agencies (including State Industrial Development Corporations) are eligible to apply for setting up Plastic Parks under the scheme. The implementing agency must be a Government of India undertaking or a State Government entity.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial assistance for development of common infrastructure facilities such as internal roads, drainage, water supply, power distribution, street lighting, and effluent treatment plants. The scheme enables plug-and-play facilities for plastic processing units, promotes cluster-based development, and supports sustainable industrial growth with shared infrastructure.

## Important Conditions

- Financial assistance is limited to common infrastructure only;
- individual unit setup is not covered
- State Government must contribute at least 50% of the project cost
- The Plastic Park must be dedicated primarily to plastic processing and allied industries
- Projects must comply with environmental and safety regulations
