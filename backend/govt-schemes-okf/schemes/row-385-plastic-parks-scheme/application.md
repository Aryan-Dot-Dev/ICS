## Process

1. State Government or its agency identifies a suitable location for the Plastic Park.
2. Prepares a Detailed Project Report (DPR) including land details, infrastructure plan, and financials.
3. Submits the proposal to the Department of Chemicals and Petrochemicals, Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers.
4. The proposal is appraised by a Technical Committee.
5. Upon approval, financial assistance is sanctioned and released in installments based on progress.
6. The implementing agency executes the project and utilizes the grant for common infrastructure development.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — proposals can be submitted throughout the year as per the scheme guidelines.
