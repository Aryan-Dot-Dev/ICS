## Process

1. Identify the suitable loan product based on business type and needs.
2. Visit the NEDFi website or nearest branch to access the loan application form.
3. Fill out the application form with details of the entity, promoters, and project.
4. Gather required documents including KYC (identity, address), business proof (trade license, MOA, AOA, GST, ITR), and project-specific documents.
5. Submit the application along with documents to NEDFi (online or at branch).
6. NEDFi officials conduct due diligence and verification of submitted information.
7. Application is forwarded to the appropriate sanctioning authority (e.g., Branch Credit Committee, Corporate Credit Committee).
8. Upon approval, a sanction letter is issued with terms and conditions.
9. Complete security creation and documentation as per sanction terms.
10. Loan is disbursed to the borrower’s account post-fulfillment of all conditions.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round.
