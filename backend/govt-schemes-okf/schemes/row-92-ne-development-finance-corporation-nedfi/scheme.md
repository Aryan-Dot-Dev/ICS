# NE Development Finance Corporation (NEDFi)

## Overview

NEDFi (North Eastern Development Finance Corporation Limited) is a public financial institution established to provide financial assistance and support to micro, small, medium, and large enterprises in the North Eastern Region of India. It offers a range of loan products including project finance, working capital loans, loans for women entrepreneurs, artisans, doctors, professionals, and specialized sectors like tea industry. NEDFi also acts as the nodal agency for disbursing Government of India subsidies and incentives in the region under schemes such as NEIIPP 2007 and NEIDS 2017, and provides consultancy, advisory, and capacity-building services.

## Objective

- Provide financial assistance to micro, small, medium, and large enterprises for setting up industrial, infrastructure, and agri-allied projects in the North Eastern Region
- Support microfinance through MFI/NGOs for on-lending to needy individuals in rural areas for income-generating activities
- Offer consultancy and advisory services to state governments, private sectors, and other agencies
- Conduct sector or state-specific studies under the Techno-Economic Development Fund (TEDF)
- Act as the designated nodal agency for disbursal of Government of India incentives to industries in the North-East under NEIIPP 2007
- Promote entrepreneurship and livelihood enhancement through CSR initiatives and skill development programs
- Strengthen institutional capacity and provide monitoring and evaluation support for funded projects
- Facilitate market linkage for artisans and handicrafts through initiatives like NE-SHILP and NEDFi Haat

## Important Conditions

- Loans are exclusively for units/businesses located in the North Eastern Region of India
- Certain loans (e.g., working capital loan) are applicable only for existing businesses
- Promoter’s contribution is mandatory (minimum 15%-35% of project cost depending on scheme)
- Subsidy must not be lien-marked with any other bank/institution;
- otherwise, NOC from lender is required
- Loan amount is capped (e.g., max ₹15 lakhs project cost for women entrepreneurs, max ₹1 lakh for artisans)
- No Detailed Project Report required for loans up to ₹25 lakhs (varies by scheme)
- Interest rates are subject to change and start from specified minimum rates
- Processing fees and other charges are applicable as per NEDFi’s charge structure
- Certain loans have restrictions on end-use (e.g., MFIs must use funds only for income generation of ultimate borrowers)
- Beneficiaries must comply with RBI norms and NEDFi’s KYC/AML policies
