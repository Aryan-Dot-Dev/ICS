## Process

1. Constitute a CSR Committee as per Section 135 of the Companies Act,
2. 2. Formulate a CSR policy and approve projects aligned with Schedule VII of the Act.
3. Ensure expenditure of at least 2% of average net profits on CSR.
4. Monitor and report CSR spending annually in the Board’s Report.
5. File CSR-related forms (e.g., CSR-2) on the MCA portal.

## Deadlines

- Annual filing — CSR-2 form to be filed within 30 days from the close of the financial year.
