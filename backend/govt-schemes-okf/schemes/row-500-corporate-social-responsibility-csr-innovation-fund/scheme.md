# Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Innovation Fund

## Overview

The Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Innovation Fund is a scheme under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs aimed at promoting CSR activities and innovation in corporate compliance. The scheme is part of the broader CSR framework governed by the Companies Act, 2013 and related circulars, encouraging companies to allocate funds toward social welfare and sustainable development initiatives.

## Objective

- Promote corporate social responsibility initiatives across India
- Encourage innovation in CSR projects and implementation
- Facilitate compliance with CSR provisions under the Companies Act, 2013
- Support scalable and impactful social welfare programs
- Strengthen partnerships between corporates and implementing agencies

## Eligibility

Companies meeting the CSR applicability criteria under Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013 (net worth of ₹500 crore or more, or turnover of ₹1000 crore or more, or net profit of ₹5 crore or more during any financial year) are required to constitute a CSR Committee and spend at least 2% of their average net profits of the preceding three financial years on CSR activities.

## Benefits

Compliance with legal CSR obligations, enhanced brand reputation, contribution to sustainable development, access to government-recognized CSR projects, and potential tax benefits under applicable provisions of the Income Tax Act.

## Important Conditions

- CSR expenditure must align with activities listed in Schedule VII of the Companies Act, 2013
- Unspent CSR funds must be transferred to a specified fund (e.g., Schedule VII funds) within six months of the financial year end
- Penalties apply for non-compliance with CSR spending and reporting requirements
- Direct contributions to political parties are not considered valid CSR expenditure
