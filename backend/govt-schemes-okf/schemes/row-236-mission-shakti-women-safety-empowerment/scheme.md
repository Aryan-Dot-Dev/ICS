# Mission Shakti – Women Safety & Empowerment

## Overview

Mission Shakti is a centrally sponsored scheme launched by the Ministry of Women and Child Development to strengthen interventions for women safety, security, and empowerment. It integrates the erstwhile schemes of Umbrella Scheme for Protection and Development of Women and aims to address issues affecting women through a life-cycle continuum approach. The scheme focuses on convergence, capacity building, and strengthening institutional mechanisms at national, state, and district levels.

## Objective

- Ensure safety and security of women in public and private spaces
- Promote gender equality and women's empowerment
- Provide access to rights and entitlements for women
- Strengthen institutional mechanisms for implementation
- Enhance convergence of schemes and programs for women
- Build capacity of functionaries and stakeholders
- Create awareness on women's rights and legal provisions
- Establish One Stop Centres and Women Helplines across districts

## Eligibility

The scheme targets all women and girls in India, with special focus on vulnerable and marginalized groups including those affected by violence, belonging to SC/ST/OBC communities, residing in rural and tribal areas, and economically weaker sections. Implementation is carried out through State Governments and UT Administrations based on need assessment and gap analysis.
