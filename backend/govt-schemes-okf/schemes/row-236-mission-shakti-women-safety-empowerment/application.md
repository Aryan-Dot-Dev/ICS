## Process

1. State Governments/UT Administrations prepare Annual Action Plans based on gap analysis and needs assessment.
2. Plans are submitted to the Ministry of Women and Child Development for appraisal.
3. Ministry appraises and approves the plans.
4. Funds are released in instalments based on utilization of previous grants and submission of required documents.
5. States/UTs implement the scheme components through designated nodal agencies.
6. Monitoring and evaluation are conducted through periodic reviews and third-party audits.
