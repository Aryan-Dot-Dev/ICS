# Textile Upgradation Fund Scheme (TUFS)

## Overview

The Textile Upgradation Fund Scheme (TUFS) is a Government of India initiative launched in 1999 to facilitate technological upgradation of the textile industry by providing financial assistance for modernization and expansion of textile units. It aims to enhance productivity, quality, and competitiveness of the Indian textile sector through capital investment in machinery and equipment. The scheme supports both new and existing units across various segments of the textile value chain, including spinning, weaving, processing, and garmenting.

## Objective

- To facilitate technological upgradation of the textile industry
- To enhance productivity, quality, and competitiveness of textile units
- To promote investment in modernization and expansion of textile machinery
- To support the textile value chain from fibre to garment
- To reduce energy consumption and improve environmental performance
- To encourage adoption of state-of-the-art technology in textile processing

## Important Conditions

- Only new machinery (indigenous or imported) is eligible;
- second-hand or used machinery is not covered
- The scheme is subject to availability of funds and may be discontinued or modified without prior notice
- Interest reimbursement is capped at 5% per annum and limited to the loan tenure or 5-7 years, whichever is less
- Capital subsidy under Modified TUFS is available only for specific segments like processing, weaving, and garmenting
- Benefits are disbursed only after successful installation and verification of machinery
