# Gems & Jewellery Sector PLI – Design Innovation Grant

## Overview

The Gems & Jewellery Sector PLI – Design Innovation Grant is a scheme under the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) framework aimed at boosting design innovation in the gems and jewellery sector. It provides financial incentives to manufacturers based on incremental sales of value-added products, encouraging domestic value addition and reducing import dependence. The scheme supports the development of a competitive domestic supply chain and promotes innovation-driven manufacturing in the sector.

## Objective

- Boost domestic manufacturing and value addition in the gems and jewellery sector
- Encourage design innovation and development of high-value products
- Reduce import dependence by promoting local production
- Create employment opportunities in the sector
- Strengthen the domestic supply chain through incentivized investment
- Support MSMEs and startups in adopting advanced design and manufacturing practices

## Eligibility

Manufacturers of gems and jewellery products who are registered under the Companies Act, 2013 or as a partnership firm, and who undertake incremental investment in eligible product categories with a focus on design innovation and value addition, are eligible to apply under the scheme.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial incentives based on incremental sales of eligible products, support for design innovation, technical assistance for upgrading manufacturing capabilities, and facilitation of market access for value-added gems and jewellery products.
