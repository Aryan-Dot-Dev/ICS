# ESOP Tax Deferment for Startup Employees

## Overview

The ESOP Tax Deferment for Startup Employees scheme allows eligible startups to defer tax on Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOPs) and sweat equity shares, enabling employees to pay tax only upon sale of shares or exit from the company, thereby reducing early financial burden and promoting employee retention and wealth creation in startups.

## Objective

- Defer tax on ESOPs and sweat equity shares for startup employees
- Reduce early-stage financial burden on employees receiving equity compensation
- Promote employee retention and long-term engagement in startups
- Align tax treatment with actual realization of gains from equity holdings
- Support wealth creation for employees in high-growth startups

## Eligibility

The employer must be a DPIIT-recognised startup under Section 140 of the Income Tax Act, 2025, incorporated on or after 1st April 2016 and before 1st April 2030, with turnover not exceeding ₹300 crore in the relevant tax year, and engaged in innovation, development, or improvement of products, processes, or services, or having a scalable business model with high potential for employment…


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Tax deferment on ESOPs and sweat equity shares until the earlier of: expiry of 60 months from the end of the relevant tax year, sale of shares by the employee, or exit from the company. Employees are not required to pay tax at the time of exercising the option or receiving sweat equity shares, thereby improving cash flow and aligning tax liability with actual financial benefit realization.

## Important Conditions

- Tax deferment is available only for ESOPs and sweat equity shares issued by DPIIT-recognised startups
- The deferment period expires on the earliest of: 60 months from the end of the relevant tax year, sale of shares, or exit from the company
- If the employee exits the company, tax becomes due on the perquisite value of unexercised ESOPs or untransferred sweat equity shares
- The scheme does not exempt tax permanently — it only defers the tax liability
- Startups must obtain separate IMB approval for tax benefits under Section 140
