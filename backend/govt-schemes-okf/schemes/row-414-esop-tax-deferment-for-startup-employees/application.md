## Process

1. Obtain DPIIT recognition for the startup via the Startup India portal or National Single Window System (NSWS).
2. Obtain certification from the Inter-Ministerial Board (IMB) for eligibility under Section 140 of the Income Tax Act,
3. 3. Issue ESOPs or sweat equity shares to employees in compliance with company law and scheme guidelines.
4. Employees must declare the deferment claim in their income tax return at the time of filing, specifying the nature of the benefit and the deferment period.
5. Tax is assessed and payable only upon the occurrence of the trigger event: sale of shares, exit from the company, or expiry of the 60-month deferment period.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications can be made at any time during the startup's eligibility period.
