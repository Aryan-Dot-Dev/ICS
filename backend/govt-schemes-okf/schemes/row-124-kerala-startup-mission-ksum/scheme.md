# Kerala Startup Mission (KSUM)

## Overview

Kerala Startup Mission (KSUM) is a nodal agency of the Government of Kerala for entrepreneurship development and incubation activities in Kerala, India. It provides a comprehensive ecosystem of support including funding schemes, incubation, acceleration, mentorship, market access, and investor connections to nurture startups from idea to scale-up stages. KSUM aims to foster innovation, promote inclusive entrepreneurship, and strengthen Kerala's position as a leading startup destination through structured programs and strategic partnerships.

## Objective

- Foster innovation and support entrepreneurs across all stages of their journey
- Create a highly sustainable startup ecosystem under the aegis of the Govt. of Kerala
- Provide access to markets, capital, partnerships, and growth opportunities
- Nurture technology-driven and innovative solutions with strong commercialization potential
- Enable inclusive access to capital for first-time founders, student innovators, women-led startups, and technology-driven ventures
- Strengthen representation within Kerala’s startup ecosystem for Backward Classes and women/transgender founders
- Facilitate structured collaboration between corporates and startups to accelerate innovation adoption
- Empower women and transgender-led startups to transform their MVP into market-ready products

## Important Conditions

- Grants are not given for service startups and SMEs working in trade and commerce
- Startups shall not have any other pending dues with Government agencies, KSUM, or other incubators
- Startups shall not be blacklisted by any Govt.
- agency in India
- Founder salaries, purchase of fixed assets (land, building, high-end equipment, costly software above ₹2 lakhs), rent, and major infrastructure setup are not permitted under most grants
- Only one idea can be applied for at a time;
- two grants will not be sanctioned simultaneously
- If a startup has availed prior grants (Idea, Productization, Market Acceleration), only the remaining eligible amount can be claimed for subsequent grants
- KSUM Unique ID is mandatory for fund disbursement (though not always for application)
- Company incorporation and active MCA status are mandatory before fund disbursal
- Women/Transgender Productization Grant is not available to startups that have already received Scale-up Grant
- NIDHI Seed Support is not meant for Indian subsidiaries of MNCs/foreign companies
