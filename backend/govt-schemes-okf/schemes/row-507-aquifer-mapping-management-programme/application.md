## Process

1. There is no application process for external beneficiaries. The programme is implemented by CGWB through field surveys by professional hydrogeologists from regional offices, data compilation, gap analysis, data generation and integration, aquifer map preparation, and management plan formulation. Outputs are shared via DO from Secretary (WR, RD & GR) to Chief Secretaries and Central Ministries, State Ground Water Coordination Committees (SGWCC), District Authorities (DM/DC), and Public Interaction Programmes (PIP) at grassroots level.
