# MSME Development Policy Odisha

## Overview

The Odisha MSME Development Policy, 2016 is a state-level initiative by the Government of Odisha to promote the growth and competitiveness of Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) through a comprehensive framework of enabling, supporting, and financial incentives. It aims to encourage new manufacturing capacity, provide a conducive ecosystem for MSMEs in potential sectors, maximize employment generation for youth, facilitate access to domestic and export markets, and make focused efforts for revival of sick enterprises and sustainable, inclusive growth. The policy integrates state and central government schemes, develops infrastructure like MSME parks and product-specific clusters, and offers financial support including capital investment subsidies, seed capital assistance, and trade mark assistance.

## Objective

- Encourage new manufacturing capacity based on improved competitiveness
- Provide a conducive eco-system for promotion and growth of MSMEs in potential sectors
- Provide opportunities to local entrepreneurial talent
- Maximize avenues of employment generation for the youth
- Facilitate MSMEs in accessing domestic and export markets
- Make concerted efforts for revival of sick enterprises
- Make focussed efforts for sustainable, inclusive & balanced growth

## Important Conditions

- Incentives are not available if similar incentives are availed under any other State or Central Government policies
- Enterprises must file claims complete in all respects within 2 years of commencement of production to remain eligible (as amended in 2023)
- One-time relaxation allows MSMEs that commenced production but missed the deadline to file claims by 30.06.2023 to become eligible
- Government may consider condonation of delay beyond 2 years due to force majeure on case-to-case basis via Empowered Committee recommendation
- Capital Investment Subsidy upper limits vary: Rs.1 crore (general), Rs.1.5 crore (SC/ST), Rs.1.25 crore (women)
- Assistance is subject to availability of funds and approval by the respective Level Committee (DLC/SDLC/SLC)
