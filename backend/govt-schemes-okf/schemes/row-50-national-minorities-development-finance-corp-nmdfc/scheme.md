# National Minorities Development & Finance Corp (NMDFC)

## Overview

The National Minorities Development & Finance Corporation (NMDFC) is a Government of India undertaking under the Ministry of Minority Affairs, incorporated on 30th September 1994 as a not-for-profit company under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013. It functions as a National Level Apex Body to provide concessional finance to notified minority communities (Muslims, Christians, Sikhs, Buddhists, Parsis, and Jains) for self-employment and income generation activities, with preference given to artisans and women. NMDFC implements its schemes through State Channelizing Agencies (SCAs) and offers various loan schemes including Term Loan, Education Loan, Virasat (for artisans), and Micro Finance, along with promotional and fellowship schemes.

## Objective

- Provide concessional finance to minorities for self-employment/income generation
- Promote economic empowerment of weaker sections amongst minorities
- Extend loans through State Channelizing Agencies (SCAs) for income generation activities
- Support artisans and women beneficiaries under NMDFC programmes
- Implement schemes such as Term Loan, Education Loan, Virasat, and Micro Finance
- Disburse fellowships like Maulana Azad National Fellowship (MANF) for minority students
- Strengthen marketing and promotional support for minority artisans
- Facilitate refinancing of loans through partner banks like Canara Bank

## Eligibility

## Important Conditions

- Loans exceeding ₹1 lakh require religious certificate and appropriate security/Guarantee
- Manufacturing of loan application does not guarantee approval;
- subject to SCA and NMDFC verification
- In case of dormant status, beneficiaries may approach nearest Canara Bank branch for loan under their scheme, which can later be refinanced through NMDFC
- Maulana Azad National Fellowship (MANF) scheme has been discontinued from 2022-23 onward;
- existing fellows continue till end of tenure
- Exception claims for MANF arrears not entertained after 31st December 2024 for scholars registered/joined on or before 31.12.2018
