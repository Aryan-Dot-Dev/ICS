## Process

1. Identify the relevant scheme (Term Loan, Education Loan, Virasat, Micro Finance, etc.) based on eligibility and purpose.
2. Check annual family income to determine Credit Line-1 (up to ₹3.00 lakhs) or Credit Line-2 (up to ₹8.00 lakhs).
3. Approach the State Channelizing Agency (SCA) nominated by your State Government.
4. Prepare required documents: self-attested income certificate, residence proof (with AADHAAR/Ration Card/Voter ID/Passport/Phone/Electricity Bill), religious certificate (if loan > ₹1 lakh), and brief project report.
5. For loans exceeding ₹1 lakh, arrange security: self-guarantee & post-dated cheques (up to ₹1 lakh); guarantee of one Govt./PSU/Bank employee or income tax payer & post-dated cheques (₹1-5 lakhs); guarantee of two Govt./PSU/Bank employees or two income tax payers/public representatives or collateral (₹5-10 lakhs).
6. Submit application form and documents to the SCA.
7. SCA verifies documents and forwards approved proposals to NMDFC for sanction.
8. Upon approval, loan is disbursed by SCA to beneficiary.
9. Repay loan as per agreed schedule (typically 3-5 years) after moratorium period.
