# Procurement and Marketing Support Scheme (PMS) for MSMEs

## Overview

The Procurement and Marketing Support Scheme (PMS) for MSMEs aims to enhance the marketability of products and services by supporting market access initiatives such as participation in national and international trade fairs, exhibitions, and buyer-seller meets. It provides financial assistance for adopting modern packaging techniques, bar codes, e-commerce platforms, and developing retail outlets. The scheme also includes capacity building, vendor development programs, and national workshops/seminars to educate MSMEs on business development, packaging, and export readiness.

## Objective

- Promote new market access initiatives like organizing/participation in National/International Trade Fairs/Exhibitions/MSME Expo
- Create awareness and educate MSMEs about importance/methods/process of packaging in marketing, latest packaging technology, import-export policy and procedure, GeM portal, MSME Conclave, latest developments in international/national trade
- Support adoption of modern packaging techniques, bar codes, and e-commerce platforms
- Facilitate market linkages through Vendor Development Programs (VDP) for effective implementation of Public Procurement Policy
- Provide capacity building through cluster-based interventions for specific packaging and branding requirements
- Develop retail outlets for promoting Geographical Indication (GI) products
- Conduct national workshops/seminars on business development, technology, EXIM policy, and market research

## Important Conditions

- An MSE would be allowed reimbursement for a maximum of 2 (two) events in a year
- The budgetary support for participation of implementing agency will be max.
- 10% of total allocation to the event or actual whichever is less
- The Implementing Agency will take the approval of the competent authority for detailed publicity proposal
- For Bar Code: Eligible only for Micro enterprises which have UDYAM Registration and also have registration with GS1 India for use of Bar Codes
- For e-commerce platform: Eligible for Micro Enterprises with valid Udyam Registration, financial assistance for selling up to 10 new products
- For retail outlets: Only products with valid Geographical Indications (GI) would be sold, seller must have MoU/agreement with GI holder
