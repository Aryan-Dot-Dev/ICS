## Process

1. Apply online through the URL: https://my.msme.gov.in/MyMsme/Reg/COM_ViewEvent.aspx
2. Submit claims for reimbursement to the respective Implementing Agency (MSME Development Institutes, NSIC, etc.)
3. For Bar Code reimbursement, apply to the Director/HOO of the regional MSME Development Institute with prescribed application form (Annexure - A)
4. For e-commerce platform claims, submit to NSIC for reimbursement
5. For retail outlet proposals, submit to MSME-Development Institutes (MSME-DIs)
6. Submit claims online or through system in place within 30 days from the date of conclusion of the event
7. Ensure submission of required documents including attested copies of payment receipts, licenses, UDYAM Registration, affidavit, and undertaking

## Deadlines

- Applicant units shall have to submit their claim online (or system in place) in the prescribed format within 30 days from date of conclusion of the event.
