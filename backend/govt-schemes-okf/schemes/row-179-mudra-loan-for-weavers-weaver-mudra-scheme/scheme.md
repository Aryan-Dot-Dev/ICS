# MUDRA Loan for Weavers (Weaver MUDRA Scheme)

## Overview

The MUDRA Loan for Weavers (Weaver MUDRA Scheme) is part of the Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY), which provides loans up to ₹20 lakh to non-corporate, non-farm small/micro enterprises, including weavers. Launched on April 8, 2015, PMMY aims to fund the unfunded by extending credit through last-mile financiers such as banks, NBFCs, and MFIs. The scheme supports income-generating activities in manufacturing, processing, trading, services, and agri-allied sectors, with special focus on weaker sections including SC/ST/OBC and women entrepreneurs.

## Objective

- To provide loans up to ₹20 lakh to non-corporate, non-farm small/micro enterprises
- To fund the unfunded segment by extending credit through last-mile financiers
- To support income-generating activities in manufacturing, processing, trading, services, and agri-allied sectors
- To prioritize lending to SC/ST/OBC and women entrepreneurs
- To create an inclusive, sustainable, and value-based entrepreneurial culture
- To strengthen formal credit channels for micro enterprises at the grassroots level
- To monitor PMMY implementation via a dedicated portal
- To facilitate credit guarantees for loans granted under PMMY

## Important Conditions

- MUDRA does not lend directly to the micro entrepreneurs / individuals.
- There are no agents or middleman engaged by MUDRA for availing of Mudra Loans.
- The borrowers are advised to keep away from persons posing as Agents/ facilitators of MUDRA/PMMY.
- Loans are only for non-farm income generating activities.
- Credit need must be up to 20 lakh.
