## Process

1. Application processes vary by sub-scheme. For PAT, designated consumers are notified by the Central Government based on energy consumption norms and must comply with notified norms, rules and regulations. For Standards & Labelling, manufacturers can file applications online through the BEE web portal for star labelling of appliances. For SME programmes, interested entities can apply through designated channels as per specific programme guidelines. For ECBC, implementation is at state and local level through integration in municipal bye-laws.
