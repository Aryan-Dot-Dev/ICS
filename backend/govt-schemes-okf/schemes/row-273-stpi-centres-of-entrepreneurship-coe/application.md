## Process

1. Submit proposals for incubation at STPI Centres of Entrepreneurship (CoEs).
2. Proposals are accepted based on pre-defined criteria.
3. Selected startups receive mentorship from eminent personalities from Academia and Industry.
4. Undergo evaluation of Proof of Concept (POC), product development, and market connect.
5. Interact with angel investors/VCs for funding opportunities.
6. Benefit from collaborative ecosystem involving Government, Industry, Academia, and Investors.
