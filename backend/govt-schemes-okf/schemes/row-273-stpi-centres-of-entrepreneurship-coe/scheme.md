# STPI Centres of Entrepreneurship (CoE)

## Overview

STPI Centres of Entrepreneurship (CoEs) are domain-specific specialized incubation facilities established by Software Technology Parks of India (STPI) under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) to support startups in emerging technologies. These CoEs provide infrastructure, mentoring, funding opportunities, networking, and technical support to nurture innovative startups in areas such as IoT, AI, FinTech, AR/VR, ESDM, and others. The initiative aligns with the Start-up India vision to build a vibrant startup ecosystem and position India as a 'Product Nation'.

## Objective

- Deliver a vibrant platform & 360 degree support to start-ups
- Foster Entrepreneurship, Innovation, and knowledge exchange
- Build Leadership in Emerging Technologies
- Help start-ups create world-class IT / EDSM products indigenously
- Generating direct & indirect employment
- Spreading awareness about the CoE through various media & channels
- Acceptance of Proposals
- Selection on the basis of pre-defined criteria
- Mentorship from eminent personalities from Academia and Industry to nurture the ideas
- Evaluation of the POC Product development Market-connect and interaction with angel investors/VCs for funding
- Collaborative ecosystem for Start-ups
