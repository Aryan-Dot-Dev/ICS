# Animal Husbandry Infrastructure Development Fund (AHIDF)

## Overview

The Animal Husbandry Infrastructure Development Fund (AHIDF) is a central sector scheme under the Prime Minister's AtmaNirbhar Bharat Abhiyan stimulus package of Rs.15000 crore. It incentivizes investments by individual entrepreneurs, private companies, MSME, Farmers Producers Organizations (FPOs), and Section 8 companies to establish infrastructure in dairy processing, meat processing, animal feed plants, breed improvement technology, animal waste to wealth management, and veterinary vaccine and drugs manufacturing facilities. The scheme provides loans up to 90% of project cost, 3% interest subvention, and credit guarantee cover for eligible entities.

## Objective

- To help increasing of milk and meat processing capacity and product diversification thereby providing greater access for unorganized rural milk and meat producers to organized milk and meat market
- To make available increased price realization for the producer
- To make available quality milk and meat products for the domestic consumer
- To fulfill the objective of protein enriched quality food requirement of the growing population of the country and prevent malnutrition in one of the highest malnourished children population in the world
- Develop entrepreneurship and generate employment
- To promote exports and increase the export contribution in the milk and meat sector
- To make available quality concentrated animals feed to the cattle, buffalo, sheep, goat, pig and poultry to provide balanced ration at affordable prices


## Target Beneficiaries

**individual entrepreneurs**, **private companies**, **MSME**, **Farmers Producers Organizations (FPOs)**, **Section 8 companies**

## Important Conditions

- Interest subvention will not be allowed for the loan sanctioned for procurement of land, working capital, old machineries and vehicle for personal use.
- The Eligible Entities will not be able to get the interest subvention if the Eligible Entities is defaulter of re-payment of loan amount in any given year.
- Interest subvention will not be provided for the duration an account is NPA even if the beneficiary returns to standard category by the bank.
- Interest subvention will be provided only up to 8 years of repayment period from the date of first disbursement.
- Beyond that no interest subvention will be provided.
- Government entities and cooperatives are not eligible under AHIDF.
- Farming loans are not eligible under the Scheme.
