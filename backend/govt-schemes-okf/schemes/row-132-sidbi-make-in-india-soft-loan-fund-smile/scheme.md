# SIDBI Make in India Soft Loan Fund (SMILE)

## Overview

SIDBI Make in India Soft Loan Fund for Enterprises (SMILE) is a soft loan scheme by the Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI) aimed at providing financial assistance to MSMEs and service enterprises for setting up new projects, expansion, modernization, diversification, technology upgradation, and rehabilitation of sick units. The scheme supports both manufacturing and service sectors, with a focus on promoting Make in India initiatives by facilitating access to affordable credit for capital investment and operational needs.

## Objective

- Provide soft loans for setting up new projects in manufacturing and service sectors
- Support expansion, modernization, diversification, and technology upgradation of existing units
- Facilitate rehabilitation of sick units
- Promote Make in India initiative by enhancing domestic manufacturing capabilities
- Provide financial assistance for capital investment and operational needs of MSMEs
- Encourage technology adoption and industrial upgradation
- Support service sector enterprises for service expansion and quality improvement
