# EDII Tamil Nadu Incubation Scheme

## Overview

The EDII Tamil Nadu Incubation Scheme is a state-level initiative aimed at fostering innovation and entrepreneurship by providing incubation support to early-stage startups in Tamil Nadu. The scheme offers mentorship, infrastructure, and guidance to help startups validate their ideas and scale operations. It is implemented by the Entrepreneurship Development Institute of India (EDII) Tamil Nadu chapter, leveraging its expertise in entrepreneurial development.

## Objective

- To nurture innovative startup ideas through structured incubation support
- To provide access to mentorship, infrastructure, and networking opportunities
- To enhance the survival and growth rate of early-stage ventures in Tamil Nadu
- To promote inclusive entrepreneurship by supporting diverse founder profiles

## Eligibility

Startups incorporated in Tamil Nadu with a viable innovative idea or prototype, preferably in early stages (pre-revenue or early revenue), led by entrepreneurs committed to building scalable ventures.

## Benefits

Access to incubation infrastructure, mentorship from industry experts and academics, networking with investors and peers, guidance on business modeling, legal compliance, and fundraising, and support in prototype development and market validation.
