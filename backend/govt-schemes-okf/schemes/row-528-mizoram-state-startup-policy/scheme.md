# Mizoram State Startup Policy

## Overview

The Mizoram State Startup Policy is a state-level initiative aimed at fostering innovation and entrepreneurship within Mizoram. It seeks to create a conducive conditions by providing support mechanisms for startups through policy interventions, infrastructure development, and access to funding and mentorship. The policy targets early-stage ventures and aims to position Mizoram as an emerging hub for innovation in Northeast India.

## Objective

- Promote innovation and entrepreneurship in Mizoram
- Create a supportive ecosystem for startups through policy and infrastructure
- Facilitate access to funding, mentorship, and incubation support
- Encourage youth and local talent to engage in entrepreneurial activities
- Position Mizoram as a hub for innovation in Northeast India
