# Repair Renovation Restoration (RRR) of Water Bodies

## Overview

The Repair Renovation Restoration (RRR) of Water Bodies scheme is a centrally sponsored component under the Har Khet Ko Pani (HKKP) of Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY). It aims to restore and revive water bodies such as tanks, ponds, and lakes to enhance irrigation potential, improve water storage, recharge groundwater, and increase availability of drinking water. The scheme supports states in undertaking repair, renovation, and restoration of water bodies with central financial assistance, particularly focusing on special category areas and drought-prone regions.

## Objective

- Restore and revive water bodies to enhance irrigation potential
- Improve water storage capacity in tanks and ponds
- Recharge groundwater through water body restoration
- Increase availability of drinking water in rural areas
- Expand cultivable area under assured irrigation
- Improve on-farm water use efficiency and promote water conservation
- Support special category areas including drought-prone, flood-prone, and tribal regions
- Strengthen water security through community-based water management
