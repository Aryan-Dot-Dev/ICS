## Process

1. State governments identify water bodies requiring repair, renovation, and restoration.
2. Project proposals are prepared with detailed project reports (DPRs) including scope, cost estimates, and expected benefits.
3. Proposals are submitted to the Department of Water Resources, River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation (DoWR, RD & GR) for appraisal.
4. The DoWR, RD & GR appraises the proposals for technical feasibility and alignment with scheme objectives.
5. Upon approval, central assistance is sanctioned and released in instalments.
6. State governments implement the projects through concerned departments or agencies.
7. Progress is monitored through periodic reporting and field inspections.
8. Utilization certificates and completion reports are submitted for final settlement.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — proposals can be submitted throughout the year as per the financial year cycle.
