# Workshed Scheme for Handloom Weavers

## Overview

The Workshed Scheme for Handloom Weavers provides financial assistance for the construction of individual and common worksheds to improve productivity and working conditions for handloom weavers. It is a component of the National Handloom Development Programme (NHDP) and aims to support weavers by providing infrastructure that enhances their earning potential and reduces drudgery. The scheme targets individual worksheds for BPL/SC/ST/Women/differently-abled weavers and common worksheds for groups of weavers, with funding patterns varying based on beneficiary category.

## Objective

- To provide need-based financial assistance for construction of individual worksheds
- To support common worksheds for groups of handloom weavers
- To improve productivity and working conditions of handloom weavers
- To reduce drudgery and enhance earning potential of weavers
- To provide infrastructure support for skill upgradation and capacity building
- To ensure availability of concessional credit and other support mechanisms
- To promote holistic development of handloom clusters
- To empower weavers through entrepreneurial support and training

## Eligibility

Individual Worksheds for BPL/SC/ST/Women/differently-abled are funded 100% by GoI. For others, Individual worksheds are funded in the ratio 75:25 by GoI:Beneficiary. Common Workshed is funded in the ratio 90:10 by GoI:Beneficiary. Solar Lighting System for Common Workshed is funded in the ratio 90:10 by GoI:Beneficiary. ## Important Conditions

- Land cost is excluded from GoI funding for individual and common worksheds
- Inter-component diversion within approved cost requires SLPC approval and intimation to O/o DC (HL)
- For NGO implementing agencies, registration on NITI Aayog Darpan Portal is mandatory
- State Directorate must submit proposal copy to WSCs at least two weeks before SLPC meeting
- Funds released in two installments: 50% advance, balance on 70% UC of first installment
