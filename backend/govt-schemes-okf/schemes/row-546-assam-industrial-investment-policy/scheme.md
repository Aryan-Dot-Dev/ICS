# Assam Industrial & Investment Policy

## Overview

The Assam Industrial & Investment Policy is a state-level initiative aimed at promoting industrial growth, attracting investment, and fostering entrepreneurship across Assam. It provides a comprehensive framework for incentives, infrastructure development, and ease of doing business to support MSMEs, startups, and large industries. The policy emphasizes sector-specific thrust areas, skill development, and sustainable industrialization to boost the state's economic competitiveness.

## Objective

- Promote industrial development and attract domestic and foreign investment
- Generate employment opportunities through promotion of MSMEs and large-scale industries
- Develop infrastructure and logistics support for industrial clusters
- Encourage innovation, technology adoption, and skill development
- Ensure sustainable and eco-friendly industrial growth
- Simplify regulatory procedures and improve ease of doing business
- Support sector-specific growth in areas like food processing, textiles, bamboo, and tourism


## Target Beneficiaries

**MSME**, **startups**, **large industries**, **entrepreneurs**

## Eligibility

Eligibility varies based on the type of enterprise and sector. Generally, MSMEs, startups, and industrial units registered in Assam under the relevant acts (such as MSMED Act, Companies Act, etc.) are eligible. Specific incentives may have additional criteria related to investment threshold, employment generation, location (e.g., backward districts), and sectoral focus.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Benefits include capital investment subsidies, interest subsidies on term loans, power tariff concessions, stamp duty and registration fee exemptions, employment generation incentives, quality certification reimbursement, patent registration support, and assistance for technology upgradation. Special packages are available for thrust sectors and units located in notified backward areas.
