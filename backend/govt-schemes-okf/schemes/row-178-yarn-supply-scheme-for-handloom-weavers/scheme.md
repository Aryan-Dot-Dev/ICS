# Yarn Supply Scheme for Handloom Weavers

## Overview

The Yarn Supply Scheme for Handloom Weavers is a component of the Raw Material Supply Scheme (RMSS) under the National Handloom Development Programme (NHDP). It aims to provide subsidized yarn to handloom weavers through a structured supply mechanism involving yarn depots and warehouses. The scheme supports weavers by ensuring easy access to raw materials at subsidized prices, thereby reducing input costs and improving productivity.

## Objective

- To provide easy access to raw material at subsidized prices
- To reduce the input cost burden on handloom weavers
- To ensure timely availability of yarn for uninterrupted production
- To support the livelihood and sustainability of handloom weaving activities
- To enhance the quality and competitiveness of handloom products through consistent raw material supply

## Eligibility

Handloom weavers, handloom agencies, Self Help Groups (SHGs), Producer Companies, and other eligible handloom entities as recommended by the State Government and approved by the Development Commissioner (Handlooms).


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial assistance in the form of transport subsidy and price subsidy on yarn supply. Non-financial benefits include assured supply of quality yarn through a network of yarn depots and warehouses, reduced drudgery in procuring raw materials, and improved productivity and income for weavers.
