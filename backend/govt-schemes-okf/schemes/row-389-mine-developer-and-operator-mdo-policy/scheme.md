# Mine Developer and Operator (MDO) Policy

## Overview

The Mine Developer and Operator (MDO) Policy is a framework under the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957 that allows private sector participation in mining operations through a contractual model where the MDO is responsible for developing and operating mines on behalf of the mineral concession holder. The policy aims to enhance efficiency, bring in technical expertise, and ensure optimal utilization of mineral resources by leveraging private sector capabilities in mine development and operations. It is designed to promote transparency, accountability, and sustainable mining practices while reducing the burden on government agencies for operational management.

## Objective

- To enhance efficiency and productivity in mining operations through private sector participation
- To bring in technical, managerial, and financial expertise for mine development and operations
- To ensure optimal utilization of mineral resources and reduce operational risks
- To promote transparency and accountability in mining concession management
- To facilitate sustainable mining practices and compliance with environmental and safety regulations
- To reduce the administrative and operational burden on government agencies
- To encourage investment in the mining sector through a clear and structured contractual framework

## Important Conditions

- The MDO does not acquire ownership of the mineral concession;
- rights remain with the concession holder
- The MDO is liable for compliance with all applicable laws, including environmental, forest, and labour regulations
- The MDO agreement must be consistent with the terms of the mineral concession and applicable mining laws
- Early termination of the MDO agreement may attract penalties or liabilities as per contractual terms
- The policy does not guarantee minimum work volume or revenue for the MDO
