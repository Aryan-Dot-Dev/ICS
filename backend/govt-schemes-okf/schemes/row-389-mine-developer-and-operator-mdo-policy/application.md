## Process

1. Identify a mineral concession holder seeking to engage an MDO for mine development and operations.
2. The mineral concession holder invites bids or enters into negotiations with eligible MDO entities.
3. The MDO submits a technical and financial proposal detailing its capability, work plan, and proposed financial terms.
4. The concession holder evaluates the proposal based on technical competence, experience, and financial viability.
5. Upon selection, a formal MDO agreement is executed outlining roles, responsibilities, financial terms, timelines, and performance metrics.
6. The MDO commences mine development activities, including land acquisition, clearances, infrastructure setup, and mining operations.
7. Regular monitoring and reporting are conducted by the concession holder or an appointed third party to ensure compliance with the agreement and regulatory requirements.
8. Payments to the MDO are made as per the agreed schedule, based on milestones achieved or mineral dispatch.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted as and when mineral concession holders invite bids or seek MDO engagement.
