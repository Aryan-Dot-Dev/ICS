## Process

1. Log in to the GST portal at https://www.gst.gov.in.
2. Navigate to the 'Services' section.
3. Select 'Returns' > 'Quarterly Return Monthly Payment (QRMP)'.
4. Opt into the QRMP scheme for the desired quarter.
5. Confirm selection and save the choice.
6. For the first two months of the quarter, pay tax using Form PMT-06.
7. At the end of the quarter, file GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B returns quarterly.

## Deadlines

- Tax payment via PMT-06 is due monthly by the 25th of the following month. Quarterly returns (GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B) must be filed by the 22nd or 24th of the month following the quarter, depending on the taxpayer's category and state/UT. The option to opt into or opt out of the QRMP scheme must be exercised before the last day of the opening month of a quarter.
