# 5G Use Case Labs & Testbeds Initiative

## Overview

The 5G Use Case Labs & Testbeds Initiative is a program under the Department of Telecommunications, Ministry of Communications, aimed at fostering innovation and development of 5G use cases through dedicated labs and testbeds. It supports startups, MSMEs, and other entities in testing and validating 5G applications across various sectors. The initiative provides infrastructure and technical support to accelerate 5G adoption and innovation in India.

## Objective

- Establish 5G use case labs and testbeds for innovation and experimentation
- Support startups and MSMEs in developing and testing 5G applications
- Accelerate 5G technology adoption across key sectors like healthcare, manufacturing, and agriculture
- Provide technical infrastructure and expertise for 5G use case validation
- Foster collaboration between industry, academia, and government for 5G innovation
- Create a pipeline of scalable 5G solutions for commercial deployment

## Eligibility

Startups, MSMEs, enterprises, and academic institutions working on 5G use cases are eligible to apply. Entities must be registered in India and demonstrate a clear 5G innovation proposal with potential for scalability and impact.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Access to 5G testbed infrastructure, technical mentorship, connectivity support, and validation facilities. Financial support may be provided for prototype development and testing. Participants gain exposure to industry experts and potential funding opportunities.
