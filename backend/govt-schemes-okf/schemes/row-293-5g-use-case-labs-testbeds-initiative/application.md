## Process

1. Visit the official 5G initiative portal at https://dot.gov.in/5g
2. Review eligibility criteria and guidelines
3. Prepare a detailed proposal outlining the 5G use case, objectives, and expected outcomes
4. Submit the application through the designated portal
5. Await evaluation by the expert committee
6. If selected, sign an MoU and proceed with lab/testbed access
7. Develop and test the 5G solution
8. Submit final report and outcomes for review

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round.
