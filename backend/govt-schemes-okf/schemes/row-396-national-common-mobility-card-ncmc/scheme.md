# National Common Mobility Card (NCMC)

## Overview

The National Common Mobility Card (NCMC) is a contactless smart card initiative under the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) that enables seamless travel across different modes of public transport (metro, bus, suburban railways) and facilitates retail payments. It aims to integrate urban transport systems by providing a unified, interoperable payment solution for commuters. The card supports offline transactions and is designed to promote digital inclusion and ease of mobility in urban areas.

## Objective

- Enable seamless travel across metro, bus, and suburban rail systems using a single card
- Facilitate retail payments alongside transit transactions
- Promote digital and contactless payments in urban transport
- Ensure interoperability of transport systems across cities
- Support offline transaction capability for areas with limited connectivity
- Enhance user convenience and reduce dependency on cash
- Integrate with national mobility goals under Smart Cities Mission

## Eligibility

The National Common Mobility Card (NCMC) is available to all residents and commuters in India who wish to use a unified contactless card for public transport and retail payments. There are no specific eligibility restrictions based on income, age, or occupation; the card is issued by authorized banks and transit agencies upon application and KYC compliance.
