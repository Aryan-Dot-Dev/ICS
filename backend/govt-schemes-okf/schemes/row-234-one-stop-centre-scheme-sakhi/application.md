## Process

1. Women affected by violence can directly walk into the nearest One Stop Centre or be referred by any individual or organization.
2. Upon arrival, the centre provides immediate medical, legal, and psychological first aid.
3. A case is registered and a needs assessment is conducted.
4. Based on assessment, coordinated with police, hospitals, legal services, and shelter homes as required.
5. Follow-up and tracking of the case is ensured for timely resolution and rehabilitation.

## Deadlines

- Services are available 24x7, 365 days a year on a walk-in basis
