# One Stop Centre Scheme (Sakhi)

## Overview

The One Stop Centre Scheme (Sakhi) is a centrally sponsored scheme under the Ministry of Women and Child Development aimed at providing integrated support and assistance to women affected by violence, both in private and public spaces, under one roof. It facilitates immediate emergency response, medical, legal, psychological, and counselling support to survivors of gender-based violence. The scheme operates through One Stop Centres (OSCs) set up across the country to ensure access to essential services for women in distress.

## Objective

- Provide integrated support and assistance to women affected by violence under one roof
- Facilitate immediate emergency response and access to medical, legal, psychological, and counselling services
- Ensure coordination with existing mechanisms such as police, hospitals, and legal aid services
- Create a safe and secure environment for women to seek help without fear or stigma
- Strengthen the response system for addressing gender-based violence through convergence of services

**women affected by violence**, **survivors of gender-based violence**, **women in distress**

## Eligibility

Any woman affected by violence, including domestic violence, sexual assault, trafficking, or any other form of gender-based violence, irrespective of age, class, caste, education, marital status, race, or culture, is eligible to access services at the One Stop Centre. The scheme also extends support to girls below 18 years of age in cases of sexual assault.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Services provided include emergency medical aid, police assistance (FIR filing, NCR recording), legal aid and counselling, psycho-social counselling, temporary shelter for up to 5 days, video conferencing facility for court proceedings, and referral linkages to other services such as skill training, employment, and long-term rehabilitation. All services are provided free of cost.

## Important Conditions

- Temporary shelter is provided for a maximum of 5 days
- Services are contingent on the operational status of the One Stop Centre in the respective district
- The scheme does not provide long-term residential care beyond initial emergency support
