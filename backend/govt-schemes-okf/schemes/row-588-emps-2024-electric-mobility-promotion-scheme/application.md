## Process

1. OEMs must apply for registration with the Ministry of Heavy Industries (MHI) by submitting Annexure-I (Manufacturer Registration Form) along with required documents including Company Registration Certificate, GST Registration, PAN Card, Testing/Homologation Certificate, Pre-registration of EV model, MOA/AOA, sales/service network proof, and photographs of the vehicle.
2. Submit hard copy of the application to Deputy Secretary (E-Mobility), Udyog Bhavan, New Delhi-110011.
3. MHI processes the application and confirms registration if found in order.
4. OEMs upload production data of EVs for which they seek incentives.
5. After sale, OEMs submit reimbursement claims within 120 days, accompanied by an undertaking (Annexure-III), board resolution (Annexure-IV), and photo ID proof.
6. Claims are verified by MHI and reimbursed upon validation.

## Deadlines

- The scheme was implemented from 1st April 2024 to 30th September 2024 and has been subsumed under PM E-DRIVE, which runs from 1st October 2024 to 31st March 2026.
