# EMPS 2024 – Electric Mobility Promotion Scheme

## Overview

The Electric Mobility Promotion Scheme 2024 (EMPS-2024) was a subscheme under the PM Electric Drive Revolution in Innovative Vehicle Enhancement (PM E-DRIVE) Scheme, implemented from 1st April 2024 to 30th September 2024, aimed at promoting electric mobility through demand incentives for electric two-wheelers (e-2W) and three-wheelers (e-3W). It provided financial support based on battery capacity, with incentives of ₹5,000 per kWh for vehicles registered in FY 2024-25, subject to a cap of 15% of the ex-factory price. The scheme has been subsumed into the PM E-DRIVE Scheme, which runs from 1st October 2024 to 31st March 2026 with a total outlay of ₹10,900 crore.

## Objective

- Faster adoption of electric vehicles (EVs)
- Setting up of charging infrastructure
- Development of EV manufacturing eco-system in the country

## Important Conditions

- Incentive is not extended when EVs are purchased by Central or State Government departments or agencies
- Each individual beneficiary can avail incentive for not more than one EV of a particular category
- All EV models must submit revalidation certification to MHI annually to continue eligibility
- If OEM changes design or ex-factory price during the scheme tenure, fresh PM E-DRIVE eligibility certificate is required
- If revalidation is not done within one month of expiry, registration under the scheme is liable to be cancelled
