## Process

1. Complete One Time Registration (OTR) on the Uttar Pradesh Scholarship Portal.
2. Log in using registered credentials.
3. Fill the online application form for the respective scholarship category (pre-matric or post-matric).
4. Upload required documents including caste certificate, income certificate, mark sheets, and bank details.
5. Submit the application online.
6. Take a printout of the submitted application.
7. Submit the hard copy with attached documents to the educational institution.
8. Institution verifies and forwards the application online.
9. University/affiliating agency verifies student details and course eligibility.
10. NIC conducts scrutiny of applications.
11. District committee locks verified data.
12. Funds are transferred to beneficiaries' accounts after verification.

## Deadlines

- Application window: July 10, 2025 to January 14, 2026 for student registration/online application for session 2025-26. Hard copy submission deadline: July 02, 2025 to January 21, 2026. Final printout: July 12, 2025 to January 17, 2026.
