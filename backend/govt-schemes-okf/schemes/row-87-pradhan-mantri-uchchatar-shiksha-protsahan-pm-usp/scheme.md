# Pradhan Mantri Uchchatar Shiksha Protsahan (PM-USP)

## Overview

The Uttar Pradesh Scholarship Portal provides financial assistance to students from various categories including SC, ST, General, Minority, and OBC for pre-matric (classes 9-10) and post-matric (classes 11-12 and beyond) education. The scheme covers tuition fees, maintenance allowances, and other educational expenses through direct benefit transfer to eligible students.

## Objective

- Provide financial assistance to students from disadvantaged sections for pre-matric education
- Support post-matric education through scholarships and fee reimbursement
- Ensure timely disbursement of scholarship funds via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
- Promote education among SC, ST, Minority, and OBC communities
- Reduce dropout rates by alleviating financial burden on students

## Important Conditions

- Aadhaar seeding in bank account is mandatory for scholarship disbursement
- One Time Registration (OTR) is compulsory from academic session 2025-26 before applying
- Applications must be submitted online first, followed by hard copy submission to the institution
- Incomplete or erroneous applications must be corrected by students within the stipulated time
- Scholarship is subject to verification of student attendance, course continuation, and eligibility by educational institutions and authorities
