## Process

1. Submit a detailed project proposal through the NMCG portal or designated channel.
2. Proposal undergoes preliminary screening for eligibility and alignment with Namami Gange objectives.
3. Shortlisted applications are evaluated by a technical committee for innovation, feasibility, and impact.
4. Selected applicants are invited for presentation or detailed discussion.
5. Final approval is granted by the NMCG steering committee.
6. Funds are released in installments based on project progress and utilization certificates.
