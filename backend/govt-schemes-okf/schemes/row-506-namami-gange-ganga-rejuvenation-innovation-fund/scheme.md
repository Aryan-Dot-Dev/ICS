# Namami Gange – Ganga Rejuvenation Innovation Fund

## Overview

The Namami Gange – Ganga Rejuvenation Innovation Fund is a financial mechanism under the National Mission for Clean Ganga (NMCG) aimed at supporting innovative technologies and solutions for the rejuvenation of the Ganga River. It focuses on funding projects related to pollution abatement, water quality monitoring, and sustainable interventions along the river basin. The fund is part of the broader Namami Gange Programme, which integrates institutional development and infrastructure investments to achieve a clean and ecologically healthy Ganga.

## Objective

- Support innovative technologies for Ganga rejuvenation
- Promote research and development in water quality management
- Encourage private sector participation in pollution abatement
- Fund pilot projects for sustainable river conservation
- Strengthen institutional capacity for monitoring and evaluation
- Facilitate technology transfer and scaling of successful models
- Ensure ecological and economic sustainability of interventions
