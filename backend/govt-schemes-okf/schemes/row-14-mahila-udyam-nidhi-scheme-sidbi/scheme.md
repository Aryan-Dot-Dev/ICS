# Mahila Udyam Nidhi Scheme (SIDBI)

## Overview

The Mahila Udyam Nidhi Scheme (SIDBI) is a financial assistance program designed to support women entrepreneurs in establishing or expanding their micro and small enterprises. It provides term loans and working capital assistance to promote self-employment and income generation among women. The scheme aims to enhance access to credit for women who face barriers in obtaining conventional financing. It is implemented by SIDBI as part of its commitment to inclusive financial development.

## Objective

- To provide financial assistance to women entrepreneurs for setting up new projects
- To support expansion, modernization, and technology upgradation of existing women-owned enterprises
- To promote self-employment and sustainable livelihoods among women
- To enhance access to institutional credit for women in the MSME sector
- To encourage entrepreneurship among women from economically weaker sections
