# Drone Didi Scheme for Women SHGs

## Overview

The Press Information Bureau (PIB) is the nodal agency of the Government of India under the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting. It serves as the interface between the government and media, disseminating information about government policies, programmes, and activities through press releases, features, backgrounders, and media outreach. PIB also provides accreditation to media persons, facilitates media facilitation, and offers feedback mechanisms to government departments based on media coverage.

## Objective

- Disseminate information about Government of India policies, programmes and activities through media
- Provide feedback from media to government departments on how policies are received by the public
- Advise the Government on its information/media strategy
- Provide explanation and background on official pronouncements
- Grant accreditation to media persons based in New Delhi
- Organize media outreach programmes like special media interactive sessions
- Provide financial assistance to media persons under the Journalist Welfare Scheme (JWS)

## Eligibility

Eligibility for PIB accreditation varies by category: working journalists must be employed with a news organization in Delhi/NCR; freelance journalists require minimum 15 years of experience and 5 years of PIB association; veteran journalists must be 65+ years with 30 years of experience; digital news publishers must comply with IT Rules 2021, have a website active for at least one year, and…

## Benefits

Accreditation provides easy access to government information, official press conferences, and government offices. It facilitates media outreach, feedback services, and access to PIB's dissemination network. Accredited journalists may also benefit from the Journalist Welfare Scheme (JWS) for financial assistance.

- Freelance journalists must not be associated with any news syndicate to be eligible
