## Process

1. Applicants must file their application online in the prescribed format available on the PIB website (https://www.pib.gov.in).
2. Applications for accreditation can be submitted any time.
3. Eligibility criteria, category, and relevant information are available in the Guidelines for Accreditation on PIB’s website.
4. Duly filled applications meeting all criteria are placed before the Central Media Accreditation Committee (CMAC) for consideration.
5. Accreditation is granted upon submission of supporting documents, completion of formalities, recommendation by CMAC, and police verification.
6. For queries, contact designated officers: Shri Abhishek Dayal, ADG (PF), Room No. 202, National Media Centre (Tel: 011-23488048, 23488041); Shri B.H. Agrawal, Deputy Director (PF), Room No. 322, Press Lounge, National Media Centre (Tel: 011-23488375); Shri Ankit Rana, SO (PRS), National Media Centre, Room No. 320, Raisina Road, New Delhi (Tel: 011-23488371, 23488345).

## Deadlines
