# National Electricity Fund (NEF)

## Overview

The National Electricity Fund (NEF) is an interest subsidy scheme approved by the Government of India to promote capital investment in the distribution sector by providing interest subsidy linked with reform measures on loans taken by public and private power utilities for various capital works under distribution projects. The scheme aims to improve the quality, reliability, and affordability of power supply through a financially sustainable and operationally efficient distribution sector. It supports pre-paid smart metering, system metering, and distribution infrastructure works for loss reduction and modernization.

## Objective

- Promote capital investment in the distribution sector
- Provide interest subsidy linked with reform measures on loans taken by power utilities
- Improve the quality, reliability, and affordability of power supply to consumers
- Achieve financially sustainable and operationally efficient distribution sector
- Reduce AT&C losses to pan-India levels of 12-15%
- Reduce ACS-ARR gap to zero by 2024-25
- Support pre-paid smart metering, system metering, and distribution infrastructure works
- Enable DISCOMs to access funds for loss reduction and modernization

## Eligibility

Public and private power utilities taking loans for various capital works under distribution projects are eligible for the National Electricity Fund (Interest Subsidy) Scheme, subject to meeting pre-qualifying criteria and achievement of basic minimum benchmarks by the DISCOM, evaluated on the basis of action plans.
