# Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation Fast Track

## Overview

The Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO) Fast Track scheme is designed to expedite regulatory processes for pharmaceutical products, including drugs, biologics, and medical devices, by streamlining approvals, reducing timelines, and enhancing transparency in the evaluation and licensing procedures. It aims to facilitate faster market access for innovative and essential medical products while maintaining stringent safety, efficacy, and quality standards. The scheme leverages digital platforms like the SUGAM portal for online submissions and integrates with national regulatory frameworks to ensure uniformity and accountability across central and state drug control organizations.

## Objective

- Expedite approval processes for new drugs, biologics, and medical devices
- Ensure uniform implementation of the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and associated rules
- Enhance transparency, accountability, and efficiency in regulatory services
- Facilitate faster market access for essential and innovative medical products
- Strengthen coordination between central and state drug control organizations
- Promote adoption of digital systems like SUGAM for seamless submission and tracking
- Ensure safety, efficacy, and quality of medical products manufactured, imported, and distributed in India

## Important Conditions

- Applications are subject to strict scrutiny;
- deficiencies may lead to rejection or requests for compliance
- Approval is contingent upon meeting all regulatory requirements under Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and Rules 1945
- The fast track process does not compromise on safety, efficacy, or quality standards
- EMD exemption available for MSMEs and DPIIT-recognized startups under GeM GTC for tender-related services
- Guidelines for similar biologics and medical devices are subject to periodic revision;
- applicants must refer to latest versions
- Post-approval changes require separate submission via SUGAM portal
