## Process

1. Access the SUGAM portal at https://sugam.cdsco.in for online submission.
2. Determine the appropriate application form (e.g., Form 40 for Registration Certificate, Form 8/8A for Import License) based on the product type.
3. Prepare and upload required documents including covering letter, application form, power of attorney, TR-6 challan of fees paid, import permissions, wholesale/manufacturing licenses, authorization letters, product specifications, stability data, and labels.
4. Submit the application through the SUGAM portal.
5. Undergo technical review by Drugs Inspector, Assistant Drugs Inspector, Assistant Drugs Controller, Deputy Drugs Controller, and Joint Drugs Controller.
6. Respond to any queries raised during review.
7. Receive final approval or rejection from the Drug Controller General of India (for Registration Certificate) or Joint Drugs Controller (for Import License).
8. Upon approval, download the certificate or license from the portal.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted year-round via the SUGAM portal with no fixed annual deadline.
