## Process

1. State Departments of Agriculture/Horticulture act as nodal agencies for area expansion under NMEO-OP in partnership with processors.
2. Processors submit oil palm area expansion target and component-wise Annual Action Plan (AAP) to the State for inclusion in the state AAP, approved by State Level Sanctioning Committee (SLSC).
3. The Department of Agriculture/Horticulture of State Governments undertakes the area expansion programme under NMEO-OP with close partnership with processors.
4. Funds are released to State Departments of Agriculture/Horticulture through respective State Governments using DBT for transfer to State/Agencies/Farmers/Processors/SAUs.
5. Private sector and NGOs can be involved through State Department of Agriculture, Horticulture & ICAR.
6. Government of India releases funds to agencies for 100% funded components/activities at Central level.
7. Release of Central and State share is through an escrow account mechanism; Secretary, Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare authorized to release up to two instalments of 25% each to States without insisting on release of State’s share.
8. A Tripartite Agreement between Centre, State and the Bank is signed for this purpose (Annexure-XIV).
