# National Mission on Edible Oils – Oil Palm (NMEO-OP)

## Overview

National Mission on Edible Oils – Oil Palm (NMEO-OP) is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme launched by the Government of India to promote oil palm cultivation for achieving self-reliance in edible oils, with special focus on North-Eastern States and A&N Islands. The mission aims to bring an additional 6.5 lakh hectares under oil palm plantation by 2025-26, targeting 3.28 lakh ha in North-Eastern states and 3.22 lakh ha in the rest of India. It provides financial assistance for planting material, maintenance, drip irrigation, pump sets, bore-wells, vermi-compost units, machinery, and viability gap payments linked to assured procurement by industry.

## Objective

- Increase area of oil palm to 10 lakh hectares from 3.5 lakh ha during 2019-20 by 2025-26 (additional 6.50 lakh ha)
- Increase Crude Palm Oil production from 0.27 lakh tonnes during 2019-20 to 11.20 lakh tonnes by 2025-26 and 28 lakh tonnes by 2028-29
- Increase consumer awareness to maintain consumption level of 19.00 kg/person/annum till 2025-26
- Provide Viability price of FFBs to farmers linked with assured procurement by the industry
- Central government to compensate farmers through viability gap payment if price paid by industry is below viability price up to October 2037
- Boost oil palm cultivation through financial assistance for planting material, maintenance, drip irrigation, pump sets, bore-wells, vermi-compost units, and machinery
- Ensure fund flow monitoring to reach targeted beneficiaries in time to achieve targeted results
- Promote inter-cropping during gestation period of 4 years for economic return to farmers

## Key Features

- Fund size: ₹11040 crore
- Grant/assistance per entity: 85% cost of planting material @ Rs. 12,000/ha for first year plantation; Rs. 10,000/ha/year for maintenance and intercropping inputs for four years; 50% cost of pump-sets limited to Rs. 27,000 (SC/ST/Small & Marginal Farmers, Women, NE states) and Rs. 22,500 (others); 50% cost of bore-well/water harvesting limited to Rs. 50,000 (plain) and Rs. 1.50 lakh (hilly); 50% cost of vermi-compost units limited to Rs. 15,000/unit; machinery/tools as per SMAM norms; Viability Gap Payment up to October 2037

## Eligibility

Farmers in general states, North-Eastern states, and UTs including Andaman & Nicobar Islands are eligible. The scheme covers 20 states identified by ICAR-IIOPR Report 2020 as having potential for oil palm cultivation. ## Important Conditions

- No permanent posts will be created by the Central or State Governments under NMEO-OP
- The oil palm development programme of NFSM (OP) along with committed liability of the approved programme of 2020-21 and 2021-22 will be subsumed under the NMEO-OP
- Fund flow will be monitored to ensure benefit of the Mission reaches targeted beneficiaries in time to achieve targeted results
- States will be allowed to make allocation to SC/ST farmers proportionate to their population in the States/Districts
- Adoption of cluster approach is advocated for ensuring participation of all farmers belonging to small/marginal, SC/ST/Minority/Women categories in a cluster
- No area restriction is applicable for individual farmers for availing subsidy/intervention
