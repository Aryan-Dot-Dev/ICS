## Process

1. Prospective eligible beneficiaries should apply on prescribed form (available with Channel Partners) to District Office of Channel Partners where they reside.
2. Applicant should clearly mention felt needs, choice of vocation, and training requirements in the application form.
3. Applicant must fulfil eligibility criteria (belong to Backward Class and have annual family income upto Rs. 3.00 lakh).
4. Submit documents as proof such as Caste and Income Certificate issued by Competent Authority (Tehsildar/Sub Divisional Magistrate/District Collector).
5. Self-certified annual family income certificate duly endorsed by gazetted officers of State Government or Branch Manager of the Bank giving the loan is admissible.
6. Eligible persons can apply online at the registration portal on NBCFDC website; requests are forwarded to concerned Channel Partners (SCAs/Banks) with intimation to the applicant.
