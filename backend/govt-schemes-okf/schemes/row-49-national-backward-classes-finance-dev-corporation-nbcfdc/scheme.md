# National Backward Classes Finance & Dev Corporation (NBCFDC)

## Overview

The National Backward Classes Finance & Development Corporation (NBCFDC) is a Government of India Undertaking under the Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, established to promote economic and developmental activities for the benefit of Backward Classes. It provides concessional financial assistance through State Channelizing Agencies (SCAs) and Banks for income-generating activities, skill development, and self-employment ventures. The corporation offers various loan schemes including Individual Loan Scheme, Group Loan Scheme, and education loans, targeting OBC members with annual family income up to Rs. 5.00 Lakh.

## Objective

- To promote economic & development activities for the benefit of Backward Classes
- To assist individuals or groups belonging to Backward Classes by way of loans and advances for economically and financially viable schemes and projects
- To extend loans to the Backward Classes for pursuing general/professional/vocational/technical education or training at graduate and higher level
- To provide for gainful employment and skill development to the target group and to assist in the upgradation of technical and entrepreneurial skills of Backward Classes for proper and efficient management of production units


## Target Beneficiaries

**Backward Classes**, **OBC**, **SC/ST**, **Minorities**, **Person with Disabilities (PwD)**, **Self Help Groups (SHGs)**

## Eligibility

Members of Backward Classes as notified by Central/State Govt. from time to time. Applicant's annual family income should be upto Rs.5.00 Lakh. For Group Loan Scheme, at least 60% members of SHG must belong to Backward Classes, with other members from weaker sections including SC/ST/Minorities/PwD. For skill training under PM DAKSH, eligibility is based on OBC/EBC status and income criteria.

## Important Conditions

- Loans are provided only for income-generating activities with potential for sustainable income generation
- Beneficiary contribution of 10-15% of project cost is required depending on the scheme
- Timely payment rebate of 1% per annum applies only if installments are paid on time
- Education loans require admission in duly accredited/recognized institute and minimum 50% marks in qualifying examination
- SHGs under Group Loan Scheme must have at least 60% members from Backward Classes
- Channel Partners are responsible for loan recovery and utilization certificate submission within 90 days
- Utilization Certificate submission beyond 90 days attracts higher interest rates (3-8% p.a.
- based on delay)
- Unutilized funds must be refunded by Channel Partners after 180 days
