## Process

1. Submit an application to IRDAI with details of the innovative product, service, or technology to be tested.
2. Include a comprehensive testing plan, objectives, duration, resources, and risk mitigation framework.
3. Disclose customer protection measures, data privacy safeguards, and exit strategy.
4. Await evaluation by IRDAI’s Sandbox Evaluation Committee.
5. If approved, enter into a sandbox agreement outlining terms, conditions, and regulatory relaxations.
6. Conduct the test under IRDAI supervision.
7. Submit periodic reports and a final evaluation report upon completion.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year as per IRDAI notifications.
