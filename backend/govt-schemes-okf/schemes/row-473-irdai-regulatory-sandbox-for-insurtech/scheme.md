# IRDAI Regulatory Sandbox for InsurTech

## Overview

The IRDAI Regulatory Sandbox for InsurTech is a framework established by the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI) to enable innovative insurance products, services, and business models to be tested in a controlled environment with regulatory relaxations. It aims to foster innovation in the insurance sector by allowing startups and insurers to experiment with new technologies under supervision. The sandbox provides a safe space for testing without immediately requiring full compliance with all regulatory norms, subject to safeguards and oversight.

## Objective

- To encourage innovation in the insurance sector by facilitating the testing of new products and technologies
- To allow InsurTech startups and insurers to experiment with innovative solutions under regulatory supervision
- To assess the viability and safety of new insurance models before full market rollout
- To promote financial inclusion and customer-centric insurance solutions through technology
- To provide a mechanism for regulators to gain insights into emerging risks and trends in InsurTech

## Important Conditions

- Participation does not guarantee eventual regulatory approval for market launch
- Regulatory relaxations are limited in scope and duration, strictly defined in the sandbox agreement
- Applicants must bear all costs associated with testing and compliance with sandbox conditions
- IRDAI may terminate sandbox participation early if risks to policyholders or systemic concerns arise
- Successful testing does not exempt entities from obtaining necessary approvals for commercial operations
