# State Mini Cluster Development Scheme

## Overview

The State Mini Cluster Development Scheme is a component of the Haryana Enterprises & Employment Policy-2020, aimed at supporting the development of industrial clusters in the state. It focuses on fostering entrepreneurship, enhancing MSME competitiveness, and promoting balanced regional development through cluster-based interventions. The scheme provides financial and infrastructural support to MSMEs for technology upgradation, market access, and common facility creation.

## Objective

- Promote cluster development to enhance competitiveness of MSMEs
- Facilitate access to modern technology through common facility centres
- Strengthen market linkages for ancillary units of large industries
- Enhance credit flow to MSMEs via scheme incentives
- Support entrepreneurship and skill development for first-generation youth
- Revive sick MSME units through institutional and policy support
- Develop robust industrial infrastructure for MSMEs
- Promote geographical dispersal of industries to backward regions

## Eligibility

## Benefits

Financial assistance for setting up Common Facility Centres (CFCs), technology upgradation, market access enhancement, credit linkage support, infrastructure development, skill development, entrepreneurship promotion, and institutional strengthening. Includes subsidies for plant and machinery, testing labs, raw material banks, design centres, and training facilities within clusters.

## Important Conditions

- Financial support is subject to availability of funds under the state budget
- Benefits are contingent upon satisfactory progress and submission of utilization certificates
- CFCs must be operated and maintained by the SPV for a minimum period of 5 years
- Only clusters with minimum 10 MSME units are eligible for support
- Land for CFC must be provided by the SPV or State Government on lease basis
