## Process

1. Identify and form a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) or consortium of MSMEs in a defined cluster.
2. Prepare a Detailed Project Report (DPR) outlining cluster interventions, technology needs, market linkages, and financial requirements.
3. Submit the DPR to the Directorate of MSME, Haryana or Haryana Enterprises Promotion Centre (HEPC) for appraisal.
4. Obtain approval from the State Level Steering Committee under HEEP
5. 5. Execute the project upon sanction and submit utilization reports for fund release in stages.
6. Undergo monitoring and evaluation by the implementing agency for compliance and impact assessment.
