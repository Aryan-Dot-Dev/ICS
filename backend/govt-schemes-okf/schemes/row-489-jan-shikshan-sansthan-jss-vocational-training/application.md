## Process

1. NGOs/voluntary organizations apply to State Resource Centres (SRCs) or NIOS for accreditation as a Jan Shikshan Sansthan (JSS).
2. Upon accreditation, JSS submits annual action plan and budget to the State Directorate of Employment & Craftsmen Training or State Skill Development Mission.
3. After approval, funds are released in installments by the State Government based on utilization certificates.
4. JSS identifies beneficiaries, conducts training, and submits progress reports.
5. Central Ministry monitors performance through periodic reviews and third-party evaluations.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications for accreditation and funding are accepted throughout the year subject to budget availability and state-specific timelines.
