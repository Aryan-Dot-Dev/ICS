# Jan Shikshan Sansthan (JSS) Vocational Training

## Overview

Jan Shikshan Sansthan (JSS) Vocational Training is a centrally sponsored scheme implemented by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship to provide vocational skills to non-literate, neo-literate, and persons with rudimentary level of education up to 8th standard, with priority to women, SC, ST, minorities, and other disadvantaged groups. The scheme aims to enhance employability and promote self-employment through need-based, life-enriching, and income-generating vocational training programs delivered at the doorstep of beneficiaries.

## Objective

- To provide vocational skills to non-literate, neo-literate, and school dropouts up to 8th standard
- To prioritize training for women, SC, ST, minorities, and other disadvantaged groups
- To link vocational training with life skills and awareness programs
- To promote self-employment and wage employment through market-relevant skills
- To strengthen the network of Jan Shikshan Sansthans for sustainable skill delivery
- To converge with other skill development programs for optimal resource utilization

## Important Conditions

- Only accredited Jan Shikshan Sansthans (JSSs) are eligible to receive funds
- Training must conform to norms set by NIOS or State Open Schools for certification
- Priority must be given to women, SC, ST, minorities, and disadvantaged groups in beneficiary selection
- Funds are released subject to submission of utilization certificates and progress reports
- JSSs must maintain proper records and are subject to audit by CAG or State Government
