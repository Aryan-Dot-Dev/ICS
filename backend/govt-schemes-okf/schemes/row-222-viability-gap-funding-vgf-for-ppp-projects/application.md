## Process

1. Project Sponsoring Authority (PSA) identifies the PPP project and prepares feasibility studies, project agreements, etc. with expert assistance.
2. PSA submits proposal to the Public Private Partnership Appraisal Committee (PPPAC) Secretariat in the specified format (six copies, hard and soft) along with pre-feasibility/feasibility report and term-sheet of proposed project agreements.
3. PPPAC Secretariat circulates documents to all members and convenes a meeting within three weeks to consider the proposal for 'in principle' approval.
4. Planning Commission appraises the project and forwards its Appraisal Note to PPPAC Secretariat; other involved ministries/departments forward written comments within stipulated time.
5. PPPAC Secretariat forwards all comments to PSA for written response.
6. PSA submits concession agreement, supporting agreements/documents, PPPAC Memo, and project report for consideration by PPPAC.
7. PPPAC evaluates the Appraisal Note, comments, and PSA's response, and either recommends approval (with or without modifications) or requests necessary changes for further consideration.
8. Once cleared by PPPAC, the project is put up to the competent authority (same as for PIB approval) for final approval.
9. After final approval, financial bids are invited.
10. Post-award, claims for VGF disbursement are submitted based on project milestones and verification.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round as per the appraisal and approval procedure of PPPAC.
