# Viability Gap Funding (VGF) for PPP Projects

## Overview

The Viability Gap Funding (VGF) Scheme is a flagship initiative of the Government of India under the Department of Economic Affairs (DEA) that provides financial assistance to financially unviable but socially/economically desirable Public Private Partnership (PPP) projects. Launched to address infrastructure deficits, the scheme supports PPPs by bridging the gap between project costs and expected revenues, thereby attracting private investment in infrastructure and social sectors. The revamped scheme, launched in 2020, has a total outlay of Rs. 8,100 crores till 2024-25 and includes enhanced support for social sector projects such as health, education, water supply, wastewater treatment, and solid waste management.

## Objective

- Promote PPPs to bring private efficiencies and attract private capital in infrastructure development
- Provide financial assistance to financially unviable but socially/economically desirable PPP projects
- Attract more PPP projects and facilitate private investment in social sectors (Health, Education, Wastewater, Solid Waste Management, Water Supply etc.)
- Support infrastructure development in sectors with long gestation periods and limited financial returns
- Increase the quality and quantity of bankable PPP projects through financial viability enhancement

## Important Conditions

- VGF grant is only available for PPP projects that are financially unviable but socially/economically desirable
- Social sector projects under Sub Scheme-1 require at least 100% Operational Cost recovery;
- Sub Scheme-2 requires at least 50% Operational Cost recovery
- The Central Government's VGF share is subject to the total outlay of Rs. 8,100 crores till 2024-25
- Post-revamp, only projects approved after 2020 are eligible under the enhanced social sector provisions
- VGF disbursement is contingent upon project progress, verification, and compliance with scheme guidelines
- The scheme does not cover projects under the purview of Ministry of Defence, Department of Atomic Energy, and Department of Space (as per general PPP guidelines, though not explicitly restated for VGF)
- State Government/Sponsoring Central Ministry/Statutory Entity support is additional and not guaranteed;
- it must be committed separately
