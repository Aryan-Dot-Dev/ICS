# Raw Material Supply Scheme (RMSS) for Handlooms

## Overview

The Raw Material Supply Scheme (RMSS) for Handlooms is a central sector scheme implemented by the Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms under the Ministry of Textiles. It aims to ensure easy access to raw materials at subsidized prices for handloom weavers by providing yarn through a network of yarn depots and warehouses. The scheme operates via a Yarn Passbook system and Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) mechanism to supply yarn at subsidized rates, thereby reducing input costs and improving productivity and earnings of handloom weavers.

## Objective

- To ensure availability of raw materials at subsidized prices to handloom weavers
- To reduce the cost of production and improve the competitiveness of handloom products
- To streamline yarn supply through a structured distribution network of yarn depots and warehouses
- To provide financial assistance via price and transport subsidies on yarn procurement
- To enhance productivity and earnings of handloom weavers through assured raw material availability
- To support handloom agencies and weavers in both cooperative and non-cooperative sectors
- To promote inclusive growth by benefiting disadvantaged sections including SC/ST and women weavers

## Eligibility

Eligible beneficiaries include handloom weavers, self-help groups, producer companies, primary handloom weavers’ cooperative societies, and other handloom agencies engaged in handloom production and recommended by State Directorates of Handlooms and Textiles and approved by the Development Commissioner (Handlooms).

## Benefits

Financial benefits include price subsidy and transport subsidy on yarn supplied under the scheme. Non-financial benefits include assured availability of yarn through a network of 511 yarn depots and 46 warehouses, streamlined supply via Yarn Passbook and Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT), and reduced drudgery in procuring raw materials. ## Important Conditions

- Subsidy is limited to yarn only and does not cover other raw materials like dyes or chemicals
- Benefits are subject to availability of funds and approval by the Development Commissioner (Handlooms)
- Yarn supply is contingent on verification through the Yarn Passbook system and submission of Utilization Certificates
- Only handloom agencies recommended by State Directorates and approved by DCHL are eligible
