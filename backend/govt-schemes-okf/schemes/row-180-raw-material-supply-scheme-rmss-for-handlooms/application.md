## Process

1. Eligible handloom agencies or weavers apply through the State Directorate of Handlooms and Textiles.
2. The State Directorate forwards the proposal to the Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms (DCHL) for approval.
3. Upon approval, the agency is enrolled in the Yarn Passbook system.
4. Yarn is supplied through authorized yarn depots at subsidized rates.
5. Subsidy amount is transferred directly to the beneficiary’s bank account via DBT.
6. Utilization Certificate (UC) must be submitted for further fund releases.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — proposals are accepted throughout the year as per State Directorate recommendations and DCHL approval cycles.
