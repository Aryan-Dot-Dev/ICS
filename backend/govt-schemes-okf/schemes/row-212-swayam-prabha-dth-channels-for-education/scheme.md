# SWAYAM PRABHA DTH Channels for Education

## Overview

SWAYAM PRABHA is a group of 40 DTH channels devoted to telecasting high-quality educational programmes on a 24x7 basis using the GSAT-15 satellite. It aims to reach the unreached segment of India, particularly in rural, tribal, and remote locations, by providing free educational content from leading institutions like IITs, UGC, CEC, IGNOU, and others. The content is repeated multiple times a day to allow flexible viewing, and the service is available through DD Free Dish and Dish TV.

## Objective

- To reach the unreached segment of India with quality education
- To provide free 24x7 educational DTH channels
- To telecast high-quality educational programmes using GSAT-15 satellite
- To offer content from IITs, UGC, CEC, IGNOU, and other reputed institutions
- To allow students to choose viewing time as per convenience through repeated content
- To support education in rural, tribal, and remote areas via DTH presence under PM e-Vidya
- To provide curriculum-based content for school education and competitive exams
- To facilitate multi-mode access to digital/online teaching-learning contents

## Important Conditions

- MPEG2 set-top boxes must be replaced with MPEG4-compatible boxes to receive channels after June 7, 2023
- Separate STB (Set-Top-Box) is required for each TV set to receive DD Free Dish
- Service is available only via DD Free Dish and Dish TV;
- not available on TataSky, Airtel, or Videocon
- No certification or credit transfer is provided for viewing the channels
