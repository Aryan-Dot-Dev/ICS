# NSIC Marketing Support Scheme

## Overview

The NSIC Marketing Support Scheme, known as the Marketing Facilitation Scheme (MFS), supports Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs) in marketing their goods and services through consortium-based tender participation and facilitation services. It enables MSEs to participate in government procurement by acting as a facilitator between buyers and suppliers, recommending the L1 bidder and charging a facilitation fee. The scheme is designed to enhance market access and competitiveness for MSEs, particularly those registered under the Single Point Registration Scheme (SPRS).

## Objective

- Promote the products of Micro and Small Entrepreneurs
- Facilitate MSEs to market goods/services individually or collectively through consortium
- Review and update the scheme to enhance effectiveness (last reviewed in 2024)
- Facilitate tender participation for MSEs with government and PSU buyers
- Ensure streamlined and transparent procurement process for buyers
- Support MSEs in accessing government supply chains without acting as an intermediary
- Enable MSEs to supply up to 25% of requirement by matching L1 price under price band policy
- Reserve 358 items for exclusive purchase from MSE sector

## Eligibility

The scheme covers only Micro & Small Enterprises registered with NSIC under its Single Point Registration Scheme (SPRS). It also covers MSEs who apply for SPRS registration along with all required documents and whose factory is inspected before empanelment under MFS. Units engaging in trading activities without value addition, packing, or branding are not eligible.

## Important Conditions

- Scheme does not cover units engaging in trading activities without value addition, packing, or branding
- NSIC’s role is strictly facilitative — limited to recommending L1 bidder, not involved in invoicing or payment
- Facilitation fee of 5% (inclusive of GST) is charged on the award value
- SC/ST-owned units are exempt from enlistment fee
- No fee for formation/renewal of consortia, but enlistment fee applies during participation
- Annual fee structure based on SPRS monetary limit
- Units must be registered or applying for SPRS with factory inspection completed
