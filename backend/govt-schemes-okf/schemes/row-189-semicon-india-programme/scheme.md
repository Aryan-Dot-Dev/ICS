# Semicon India Programme

## Overview

The Semicon India Programme is a comprehensive initiative launched by the Government of India to develop a sustainable semiconductor and display manufacturing ecosystem in the country. It aims to position India as a global hub for electronics manufacturing and design through financial incentives, infrastructure development, and capacity building across the semiconductor value chain. The programme encompasses multiple schemes including the Scheme for Setting up of Semiconductor Fabs in India, Scheme for Setting up of Display Fabs in India, Scheme for Setting up of Compound Semiconductors/Silicon Photonics/Sensors Fab (including ATMP/OSAT) in India, and the Design Linked Incentive (DLI) Scheme.

## Objective

- To provide financial support to companies investing in semiconductor, display and design ecosystem
- To reduce reliance on imports and enhance domestic value addition in electronics manufacturing
- To create employment opportunities and develop skilled manpower in advanced technology sectors
- To encourage innovation and R&D in semiconductor technologies and applications
- To attract significant investments in semiconductor manufacturing and related facilities
- To strengthen India's position in the global semiconductor supply chain
- To develop a complete ecosystem supporting semiconductor manufacturing from design to fabrication and testing

## Important Conditions

- Incentives are subject to approval based on technical and financial evaluation
- Projects must achieve committed milestones for continued disbursement of incentives
- Land for the project must be acquired or possessed within specified timelines
- Environmental and other statutory clearances must be obtained as per regulations
- Incentives may be clawed back if project is not implemented as per approved plan
- Foreign investment proposals are subject to extant FDI policy and security clearance
- Benefits under Design Linked Incentive Scheme are capped at ₹15 Crore per application for Product Design Linked Incentive
- Deployment Linked Incentive under DLI Scheme is limited to 5 years from approval date
