# EESL EV Aggregation Scheme for Bulk Procurement

## Overview

The EESL EV Aggregation Scheme for Bulk Procurement is a government initiative under the National Electric Mobility Mission Plan (NEMMP) 2020, aimed at accelerating the adoption of electric vehicles (EVs) in India through bulk procurement by government departments and public sector undertakings. The scheme leverages EESL's innovative business model to aggregate demand, reduce costs, and deploy EVs and charging infrastructure at scale without upfront capital expenditure from beneficiary agencies. It supports India's goals of reducing carbon emissions, decreasing fossil fuel dependence, and promoting sustainable transportation.

## Objective

- Accelerate the adoption of electric vehicles in government fleets
- Reduce upfront capital expenditure for beneficiary agencies through innovative financing
- Aggregate demand to achieve economies of scale and lower procurement costs
- Deploy EV charging infrastructure alongside vehicle procurement
- Support India's National Electric Mobility Mission Plan (NEMMP) 2020 targets
- Reduce carbon emissions and fossil fuel consumption in the transportation sector
- Create a sustainable business model for EV adoption without subsidies or capex from users

## Important Conditions

- The scheme does not provide direct subsidies or grants;
- payment is based on usage or savings
- Beneficiaries must commit to a minimum contract period for cost recovery by EESL
- Availability of EV models and charging infrastructure depends on supplier empanelment and lead times
- Performance and payment terms are defined in the bilateral agreement with EESL
