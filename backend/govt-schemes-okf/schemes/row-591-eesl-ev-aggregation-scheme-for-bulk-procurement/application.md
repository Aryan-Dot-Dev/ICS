## Process

1. Express interest via the EESL website or designated portal for the EV aggregation scheme.
2. Enter into discussions with EESL to assess vehicle and charging infrastructure requirements.
3. Sign a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) or agreement outlining scope, volume, and financial terms.
4. EESL procures EVs and charging equipment through competitive bidding.
5. EESL handles delivery, installation, and commissioning of vehicles and chargers.
6. Beneficiary agency begins usage and makes payments as per the agreed service model (e.g., per km or per kWh).
7. EESL provides operations and maintenance support throughout the contract period.
