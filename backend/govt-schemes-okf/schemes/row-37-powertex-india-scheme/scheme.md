# Powertex India Scheme

## Overview

The Powertex India Scheme is a comprehensive initiative launched by the Ministry of Textiles to support the growth and modernization of the powerloom sector in India. It aims to enhance the competitiveness of powerloom units through technology upgradation, skill development, market promotion, and infrastructure support. The scheme focuses on improving productivity, quality, and value realization for powerloom weavers and entrepreneurs across the country.

## Objective

- Modernize and upgrade technology in the powerloom sector
- Improve productivity and quality of powerloom products
- Provide skill development and training to workers
- Enhance market access and promote exports
- Support infrastructure development for powerloom clusters
- Encourage adoption of eco-friendly and sustainable practices
- Facilitate access to credit and financial assistance
- Strengthen institutional support and monitoring mechanisms


## Target Beneficiaries

**powerloom units**, **MSME**, **weavers**, **entrepreneurs in the textile sector**

## Eligibility

Powerloom units, cooperative societies, self-help groups, NGOs, and entrepreneurs engaged in powerloom activities are eligible to apply. The units must be registered and operational, with preference given to those located in identified powerloom clusters and those adopting modern, energy-efficient looms.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial subsidies for technology upgradation, skill development training, market development assistance, infrastructure support for common facility centers, interest subsidy on loans, and support for product design and diversification. The scheme also provides assistance for obtaining quality certifications and participating in national and international trade fairs.
