# Startup GST Exemption & Composition Scheme

## Overview

The Startup GST Exemption & Composition Scheme is a tax benefit initiative under the Goods and Services Tax (GST) framework designed to reduce compliance burden and tax liability for eligible startups. It allows qualifying startups to opt for the GST Composition Scheme, which offers lower tax rates and simplified return filing. The scheme aims to support early-stage enterprises by providing tax relief and easing administrative requirements.

## Objective

- Reduce tax compliance burden for startups
- Lower GST liability through composition scheme rates
- Simplify return filing and record-keeping requirements
- Encourage formalization of startup businesses under GST
- Support cash flow management for early-stage enterprises

## Eligibility

Startups with an aggregate annual turnover not exceeding INR 1.5 Crore in the preceding financial year are eligible to opt for the GST Composition Scheme. The scheme applies to suppliers of goods (and certain services as notified) who are registered under GST and opt to pay tax at a fixed percentage of turnover instead of normal GST rates.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Eligible startups benefit from reduced GST rates (1% for manufacturers and traders, 5% for restaurants), quarterly return filing (GSTR-4) instead of monthly, simplified compliance with minimal record-keeping, and lower tax outflow compared to regular GST rates. The scheme also eliminates the need to issue tax invoices and maintain detailed input tax credit records.

## Important Conditions

- Startups opting for the Composition Scheme cannot claim input tax credit (ITC)
- Cannot make inter-state supplies or supply goods through e-commerce operators liable to collect tax at source (TCS)
- Must pay tax under reverse charge for certain notified supplies
- Cannot issue tax invoices;
- must issue bill of supply instead
- Eligibility is lost if aggregate turnover exceeds INR 1.5 Crore in any financial year
