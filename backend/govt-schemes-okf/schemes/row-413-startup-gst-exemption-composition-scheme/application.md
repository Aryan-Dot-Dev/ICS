## Process

1. Log in to the GST Portal (https://www.gst.gov.in) using valid credentials.
2. Navigate to Services > Registration > Application to Opt for Composition Scheme.
3. Fill in Form CMP-02 to opt in for the Composition Scheme.
4. Provide details such as business type, turnover, and declaration of eligibility.
5. Submit the application electronically.
6. Upon successful submission, an acknowledgment is generated.
7. The option is effective from the beginning of the financial year in which it is opted, subject to compliance with conditions.

## Deadlines

- The option to opt for the Composition Scheme must be exercised before the commencement of the financial year for which it is sought, or within 30 days of obtaining GST registration. Once opted, it remains valid unless withdrawn or disqualified.
