# WEP – Women Entrepreneurship Platform (NITI Aayog)

## Overview

The Women Entrepreneurship Platform (WEP) by NITI Aayog is a unified access portal that aggregates government schemes, mentorship, incubators, accelerators, and financial resources to support women entrepreneurs across India. It serves as a one-stop information hub connecting women-led ventures with over 300+ resources, 944+ mentors, 835+ government initiatives, and 576+ incubators & accelerators. WEP enables registration, profile completion, mentorship matching, and access to curated schemes and partner networks to foster entrepreneurial growth.

## Objective

- Provide a unified platform for women entrepreneurs to access government schemes and resources
- Connect women entrepreneurs with mentors, incubators, accelerators, and industry experts
- Facilitate access to financial resources, grants, and schemes tailored for women-led businesses
- Enable networking and peer support through the WEP community
- Offer personalized guidance via Smart Match mentorship programs
- Support skill development through workshops, videos, and knowledge resources
- Promote financial inclusion and growth opportunities for women entrepreneurs
- Showcase and recognize achievements of women entrepreneurs through awards and outreach
