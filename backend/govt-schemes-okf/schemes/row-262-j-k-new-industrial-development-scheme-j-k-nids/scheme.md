# J&K New Industrial Development Scheme (J&K NIDS)

## Overview

The J&K New Industrial Development Scheme (J&K NIDS) is a state-level initiative launched by the Government of Jammu and Kashmir to promote industrial development, attract investment, and generate employment in the Union Territory. The scheme offers financial incentives and support to eligible industrial units for setting up new enterprises or expanding existing ones. It focuses on thrust sectors such as manufacturing, agro-processing, tourism-related industries, and services. The scheme aims to boost economic growth and industrialization in Jammu and Kashmir through structured fiscal and policy support.

## Objective

- Promote industrial investment in Jammu and Kashmir
- Generate employment opportunities for local youth
- Develop infrastructure and support services for industries
- Encourage setting up of new industrial units in the UT
- Support expansion and modernization of existing industrial units
- Boost export-oriented and import-substituting industries
- Develop thrust sectors including manufacturing, agro-processing, and tourism


## Target Beneficiaries

**MSME**, **large industries**, **new industrial units**, **expanding units**

## Important Conditions

- Subsidy disbursement is contingent upon commencement of commercial production within 2 years of approval
- Benefits are available only for new investment in plant and machinery
- Units must employ a minimum percentage of local domiciled workforce
- Incentives are subject to annual budgetary allocations and fund availability
- Misrepresentation of facts leads to rejection and recovery of disbursed amount with interest
