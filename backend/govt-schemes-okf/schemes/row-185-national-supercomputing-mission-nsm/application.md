## Process

1. Identify research problem of national relevance requiring HPC resources.
2. Affiliate with an academic or R&D institution participating in NSM.
3. Submit proposal through institutional channels for access to NSM supercomputing facilities.
4. Proposal reviewed for scientific merit and alignment with NSM objectives.
5. Upon approval, allocate computing time on systems like PARAM Shakti or PARAM Rudra.
6. Utilize allocated resources for simulations, modeling, and analysis.
7. Submit outcomes and publications as part of mission deliverables.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — proposals are accepted throughout the year based on availability and review cycles.
