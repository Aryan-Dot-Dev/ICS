## Process

1. Farmers may contact to nearest Office of Agricultural Department or District Agriculture Officer/ District Horticulture Officer for more information and obtaining benefits under PMKSY. Farmers can apply for the scheme either in MIS system (online) or in offline mode. Subsidy is released to the beneficiary electronically through the mechanism of Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT). Aadhaar details of the beneficiary are required to access the benefit of the programme. Aadhaar details need to be linked through a web based registration process.
