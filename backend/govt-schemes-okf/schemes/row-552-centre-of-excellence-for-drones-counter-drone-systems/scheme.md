# Centre of Excellence for Drones & Counter Drone Systems

## Overview

The Centre of Excellence for Drones & Counter Drone Systems is a scheme under the Department of Science & Technology (DST) aimed at fostering innovation and development in drone technology and counter-drone systems. It supports research, development, and deployment of advanced drone technologies for national security and civilian applications. The scheme focuses on building indigenous capabilities in unmanned aerial systems and anti-drone technologies through collaboration with academic institutions, startups, and industry partners.

## Objective

- Promote indigenous development of drone and counter-drone technologies
- Support research and development in unmanned aerial systems
- Enhance national security capabilities through advanced drone technologies
- Foster collaboration between academia, startups, and industry
- Develop scalable solutions for civilian and defense applications
- Strengthen India's self-reliance in critical drone technologies

## Eligibility

Eligible entities include startups, MSMEs, research institutions, and industry partners engaged in drone technology, counter-drone systems, or related R&D activities. Preference is given to entities with innovative proposals aligned with national security and technological self-reliance goals.
