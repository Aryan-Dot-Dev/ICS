# Soil Health Card Scheme

## Overview

The Soil Health Card Scheme is a government initiative under the Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, aimed at providing farmers with soil health cards to assess nutrient status and optimize fertilizer use. It empowers farmers with data-driven insights to improve yields, conserve resources, and promote sustainable agricultural practices. The scheme involves regular soil testing every three years and provides crop-specific fertilizer recommendations. It is implemented across India through state agricultural departments.

## Objective

- To issue need-based Soil Health Cards to farmers to address nutrient deficiencies in fertilization practices
- To strengthen functioning of soil testing laboratories through capacity building and involvement of agriculture students
- To promote soil test based nutrient management for enhancing nutrient use efficiency
- To improve soil health and fertility through balanced use of fertilizers
- To create awareness among farmers about soil health and balanced fertilizer use
- To reduce cost of cultivation and improve farm income through optimized fertilizer application

## Eligibility

All farmers across India are eligible to receive a Soil Health Card under the scheme. The soil health card is provided for each farm holding, and farmers can get their soil tested at designated soil testing laboratories to receive the card.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Farmers receive a proper soil health record, become aware of soil management practices, and can plan future crops and land use accordingly. The card provides information on lacking nutrients, guiding farmers on which crops to invest in and which fertilizers to use, ultimately leading to increased crop yield. Regular updates are provided every three years to track changes in soil health over time.
