## Process

1. Visit the nearest soil testing laboratory or authorized collection center.
2. Collect soil sample from the farm as per guidelines (depth, location, etc.).
3. Submit the soil sample along with farmer details (name, address, land details).
4. Pay applicable fee (if any) for testing.
5. Laboratory analyzes the sample for nutrient status (N, P, K, organic carbon, pH, etc.).
6. Soil Health Card is generated based on test results.
7. Card is issued to the farmer with crop-specific fertilizer recommendations.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — soil samples can be submitted throughout the year; cards are issued after testing.
