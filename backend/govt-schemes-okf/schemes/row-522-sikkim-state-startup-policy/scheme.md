# Sikkim State Startup Policy

## Overview

The Sikkim State Startup Policy is a state-level initiative aimed at fostering innovation and entrepreneurship within Sikkim by providing support mechanisms for startups. It seeks to create a conducive ecosystem for new ventures through policy interventions, financial incentives, and institutional support. The policy targets early-stage enterprises and aims to promote sustainable economic growth by encouraging innovation-driven entrepreneurship across key sectors.

## Objective

- Promote innovation and entrepreneurship in the state
- Create a supportive ecosystem for startups through policy and infrastructure
- Provide financial and non-financial assistance to early-stage ventures
- Encourage sector-specific innovation in areas like pharmaceuticals, eco-tourism, and food processing
- Generate employment opportunities through startup-led growth
- Facilitate access to mentorship, incubation, and market linkages
- Strengthen institutional mechanisms for startup registration and support

## Eligibility

Startups incorporated in Sikkim, working on innovative products, services, or processes, with potential for scalability and job creation, and aligned with the state's priority sectors such as pharmaceuticals, eco-tourism, food processing, and organic farming.

## Benefits

Access to seed funding, mentorship, incubation support, market access assistance, regulatory facilitation, tax benefits, and linkages with investors and industry networks. The policy also offers support for intellectual property rights (IPR) filing and participation in national and international startup events.
