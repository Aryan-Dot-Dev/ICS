## Process

1. State Forest Departments prepare Annual Plans of Operations (APOs) based on the GIM guidelines and submit them to the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC).
2. The APOs are appraised by the National Mission Directorate and the National Steering Committee (NSC).
3. Upon approval, funds are released in installments to the State Governments.
4. States implement the projects through Joint Forest Management Committees (JFMCs), Panchayati Raj Institutions (PRIs), and other local bodies.
5. Progress is monitored through periodic reporting, field visits, and third-party evaluations.
6. Utilization certificates and audited statements of expenditure are submitted for subsequent fund releases.

## Deadlines

- Annual cycle — APOs are typically submitted by States before the start of the financial year (April), with mid-year reviews possible.
