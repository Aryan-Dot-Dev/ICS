# National Mission for a Green India (GIM)

## Overview

The National Mission for a Green India (GIM) is one of the eight missions under the National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC) aimed at protecting, restoring, and enhancing India's forest cover and responding to climate change through a combination of adaptation and mitigation measures. It focuses on increasing forest/tree cover on 5 million hectares of forest/non-forest lands and improving the quality of forest cover on another 5 million hectares. The mission emphasizes ecosystem services such as biodiversity, water, biomass, and carbon sequestration, while also supporting forest-dependent communities through livelihood improvement.

## Objective

- Increase forest/tree cover to the extent of 5 million hectares (mha) on forest/non-forest lands
- Improve the quality of forest cover on another 5 million hectares (mha)
- Improve ecosystem services including biodiversity, hydrological services, and carbon sequestration
- Increase forest-based livelihood income for about 3 million households
- Enhance annual CO2 sequestration by 50-60 million tonnes by 2020
- Strengthen cross-sectoral convergence with other missions and programs
- Promote decentralized, participatory governance involving local communities
