# BIRAC Industrial Biofoundry Support

## Overview

BIRAC Industrial Biofoundry Support is a scheme under the Bioeconomy & Biofuels category aimed at providing infrastructure and technical support for scaling up bio-based innovations. It enables startups and SMEs to access advanced biofoundry facilities for strain engineering, fermentation, and downstream processing to accelerate product development and commercialization. The scheme supports the transition from lab-scale to pilot-scale operations, reducing the time and cost associated with scaling biotechnological processes. It is implemented through selected BioNEST incubators and partner institutions equipped with biofoundry capabilities.

## Objective

- Provide access to advanced biofoundry infrastructure for strain development and fermentation
- Support scale-up of bio-based products from lab to pilot scale
- Reduce time and cost in technology translation and commercialization
- Enable startups to generate data for regulatory approvals and investment readiness
- Strengthen India’s bio-manufacturing capabilities in healthcare, agri-tech, and industrial biotech
- Foster collaboration between academia, industry, and research institutions
- Promote sustainable and affordable bioproduct development

## Important Conditions

- Support is subject to availability of biofoundry infrastructure at partner institutions
- Startups must bear consumable and operational costs as per facility usage
- Intellectual property generated remains with the startup unless otherwise agreed
- Facility access does not guarantee commercial success or funding
- All work must comply with biosafety and ethical guidelines
- BIRAC does not guarantee follow-on funding or market access
