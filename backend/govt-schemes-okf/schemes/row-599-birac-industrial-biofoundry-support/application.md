## Process

1. Identify a BIRAC-supported BioNEST incubator or partner institution with biofoundry capabilities.
2. Discuss your technology and scale-up requirements with the facility manager.
3. Submit a formal application through the host institution detailing the project scope, objectives, timeline, and resource needs.
4. The institution evaluates the proposal based on technical readiness, scalability potential, and alignment with biofoundry capabilities.
5. If approved, sign a service agreement outlining access terms, timelines, and cost structure.
6. Begin work at the biofoundry facility with technical support from the host team.
7. Submit periodic progress reports and utilization certificates as required.
8. Upon completion, receive a completion certificate and data package for further development or fundraising.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round subject to facility availability and host institution approval cycles.
