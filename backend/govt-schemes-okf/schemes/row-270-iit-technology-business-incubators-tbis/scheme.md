# IIT Technology Business Incubators (TBIs)

## Overview

The IIT Technology Business Incubators (TBIs) at IIT Bombay provide incubation support to technology-based startups, offering infrastructure, mentorship, and networking opportunities to foster innovation and entrepreneurship. The program aims to convert research and ideas into viable entrepreneurial ventures through structured support systems.

## Objective

- Foster technology-based entrepreneurship
- Provide infrastructure and mentorship to startups
- Convert research activity into entrepreneurial ventures
- Support innovation and startup growth
- Facilitate industry-academia collaboration


## Target Beneficiaries

## Eligibility

Technology-based startups and entrepreneurs seeking incubation support at IIT Bombay's Technology Business Incubator (TBI) are eligible to apply. Specific criteria may include innovation quotient, scalability, and alignment with IIT Bombay's focus areas.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Incubatees receive access to IIT Bombay's research infrastructure, mentorship from faculty and industry experts, networking opportunities, legal and IP support, seed funding assistance, and access to investor networks. The TBI also provides office space, laboratory facilities, and administrative support.
