# Elevate 100 Programme

## Overview

The Elevate 100 Programme is a flagship initiative by the Government of Karnataka aimed at identifying, nurturing, and scaling high-potential startups across the state. It provides selected startups with financial support, mentorship, market access, and networking opportunities to foster innovation and entrepreneurship. The programme focuses on early-stage and growth-stage startups with scalable business models and aims to position Karnataka as a leading startup destination in India.

## Objective

- Identify and support 100 high-potential startups in Karnataka annually
- Provide financial assistance to selected startups for product development and market scaling
- Offer mentorship and guidance from industry experts and successful entrepreneurs
- Facilitate market access and customer acquisition through curated platforms
- Enable networking opportunities with investors, corporates, and ecosystem stakeholders
- Strengthen Karnataka’s position as a premier startup hub in India
- Encourage innovation in key sectors such as technology, healthcare, agritech, and manufacturing
- Support job creation and economic growth through scalable entrepreneurial ventures

## Important Conditions

- The grant is disbursed in tranches based on achievement of pre-defined milestones
- Startups must submit periodic progress reports and utilization certificates
- Funds must be used strictly for the purposes outlined in the approved project plan
- The startup must maintain its registration and primary operations in Karnataka during the grant period
- Misuse of funds may lead to recovery of the grant amount and blacklisting from future government schemes
- The decision of the selection committee is final and binding
- Startups that have previously received benefits under Elevate 100 are not eligible to reapply
