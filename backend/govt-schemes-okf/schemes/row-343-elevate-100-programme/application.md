## Process

1. Visit the official Elevate Karnataka portal at https://elevate.karnataka.gov.in.
2. Register on the portal by providing basic details of the startup and founders.
3. Log in and fill out the online application form with information about the startup, innovation, business model, traction, and team.
4. Upload required documents including Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, pitch deck, and founder details.
5. Submit the application before the deadline.
6. Applications are screened by an expert committee based on innovation, scalability, team strength, and potential impact.
7. Shortlisted startups are invited for a pitch presentation or interview.
8. Final selection is made by the apex committee, and results are communicated via email and the portal.

## Deadlines

- Applications are invited annually; the exact dates vary each year. Interested startups are advised to monitor the official portal for announcements regarding the application window.
