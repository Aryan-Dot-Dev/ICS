## Process

1. Visit the official PMFME portal at https://pmfme.mofpi.gov.in
2. Check eligibility under Individual Micro Enterprises or Group category (FPOs/SHGs/Cooperatives)
3. Prepare project report with details of proposed food processing unit
4. Submit application through the portal with required documents
5. Application is scrutinized by State/Nodal Agency
6. Upon approval, subsidy is disbursed through linked bank loan
7. Implement project as per approved plan
8. Submit utilization reports and completion certificate
