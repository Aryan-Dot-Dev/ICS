# PM Micro Food Processing Enterprises Scheme (PM-FME)

## Overview

PM Micro Food Processing Enterprises Scheme (PM-FME) is a centrally sponsored scheme launched by the Ministry of Food Processing Industries to provide financial, technical and business support for upgradation of existing micro food processing enterprises. The scheme aims to enhance the competitiveness of existing individual micro enterprises and support FPOs/SHGs/cooperatives through credit-linked subsidy, seed capital, and capacity building. It adopts the One District One Product (ODOP) approach to leverage economies of scale in procurement, services and marketing.

## Objective

- Provide credit-linked capital subsidy @35% of eligible project cost with a maximum of Rs. 10.0 lakhs per unit for upgradation or setting up of new units
- Provide credit-linked capital subsidy @35% of eligible project cost with a maximum of Rs. 3.00 Crore for groups (FPOs/FPCs/Cooperatives/SHG and its federation/Govt. Agencies) establishing food processing line with common infrastructure/value chain/incubation centers
- Provide seed capital @ Rs. 40,000/- per member of SHG for working capital and purchase of small tools, maximum upto Rs. 4 Lakhs per SHG
- Provide 50% financial grant for Branding and Marketing Support to groups of FPOs/SHGs/Cooperatives or a SPV of micro food processing enterprises
- Provide training on Food Processing Entrepreneurship Development Programme to PMFME beneficiaries under the Capacity Building component

## Important Conditions

- Subsidy is credit-linked and requires term loan from bank
- Individual unit subsidy capped at Rs. 10.0 lakhs (35% of project cost)
- Group subsidy capped at Rs. 3.00 Crore (35% of project cost)
- Seed capital for SHGs limited to Rs. 40,000 per member (max Rs. 4 Lakhs per SHG)
- Marketing grant limited to 50% of approved cost for groups
- Land and building cost not eligible for subsidy under individual unit component
- Beneficiary must contribute minimum 10% of project cost as promoter's contribution
