## Process

1. Visit the official MeitY website or the designated portal for the ESDM scheme.
2. Register and create an account on the portal.
3. Fill out the online application form with details of the proposed project, including project report, financials, and implementation plan.
4. Upload required documents such as company registration, GSTIN, project report, bank details, and other relevant certificates.
5. Submit the application and await scrutiny by the nodal agency.
6. Upon preliminary approval, submit detailed project report and financial closure documents.
7. Upon final approval, enter into an agreement with the implementing agency and fulfill compliance requirements.
8. Submit periodic progress reports and utilization certificates for disbursement of funds in installments.
