# ESDM – Electronic System Design and Manufacturing Scheme

## Overview

The ESDM – Electronic System Design and Manufacturing Scheme is a Government of India initiative under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) aimed at promoting domestic manufacturing and value addition in the electronics system design and manufacturing sector. The scheme aims to enhance India's capabilities in electronics manufacturing, reduce import dependence, and promote innovation and investment in the ESDM sector. It supports the development of electronic systems and components through incentives, infrastructure support, and policy interventions to strengthen the domestic electronics manufacturing ecosystem.

## Objective

- Promote domestic manufacturing and value addition in the electronics system design and manufacturing sector
- Reduce dependence on imports of electronic goods and components
- Promote innovation, research, and development in electronics design and manufacturing
- Attract investment in the electronics manufacturing sector through financial and fiscal incentives
- Develop skilled manpower and infrastructure for electronics manufacturing
- Promote export competitiveness of Indian electronics products
- Support the development of semiconductor and electronic component manufacturing ecosystem

## Eligibility

The scheme is open to Indian companies, including MSMEs, startups, and large enterprises, engaged in or planning to engage in electronic system design and manufacturing activities. Applicants must be registered entities in India with a valid GSTIN and must propose projects involving value addition in electronics manufacturing, such as design, development, prototyping, testing, and manufacturing…

## Benefits

The scheme provides financial incentives in the form of subsidies or incentives on capital expenditure, interest subsidies on loans, reimbursement of certain expenses related to technology acquisition, skill development, and quality certification.
