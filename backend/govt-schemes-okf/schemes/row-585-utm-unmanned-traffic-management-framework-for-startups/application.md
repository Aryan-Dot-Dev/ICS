## Process

1. Visit the DGCA website or eGCA portal to express interest in the UTM Framework.
2. Submit company profile and technical details of the UTM solution.
3. Await review by DGCA’s drone policy division.
4. Upon approval, engage in discussions for test environment access and regulatory guidance.
5. Conduct trials under DGCA supervision as per agreed parameters.
6. Submit test results and compliance reports for evaluation.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round.
