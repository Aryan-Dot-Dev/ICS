## Process

1. Visit the nearest bank branch or Bank Mitra (Business Correspondent) outlet.
2. If Aadhaar card/number is available, no other documents are required; if address has changed, self-attestation of current address is sufficient.
3. If Aadhaar is not available, submit any one of the following officially valid documents (OVD): voter ID, driving licence, PAN card, passport, or NREGA card. If these documents do not contain address, they can still serve as proof of identity and address together.
4. If no officially valid documents are available, the bank may open a 'small account' based on self-attested photograph and thumb impression/signature in the presence of a bank official, subject to certain transaction limits.
5. The account will be opened and a RuPay Debit card issued, which can be used immediately for transactions.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Accounts can be opened throughout the year.
