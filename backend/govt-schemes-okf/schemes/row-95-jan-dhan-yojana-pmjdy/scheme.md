# Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)

## Overview

Pradhan Mantri Jan-Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) is a National Mission for Financial Inclusion launched by the Government of India to ensure universal access to banking facilities, with at least one basic banking account for every household. The scheme provides access to savings, remittance, credit, insurance, and pension services, and promotes financial literacy and Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) of government subsidies. It aims to bring the unbanked population into the formal financial system through the use of technology, RuPay debit cards, and banking correspondents.

## Objective

- Universal access to banking facilities with at least one basic banking account for every household
- Financial literacy and access to credit, insurance and pension facilities
- Channeling all Government benefits (Centre/State/Local Body) to beneficiary accounts and pushing Direct Benefits Transfer of Direct Benefits Transfer (DBT)
- Addressing technological issues like poor connectivity and online transactions
- Providing indigenous RuPay Debit cards with inbuilt accident insurance cover
- Creating a pipeline for implementation through Jan Dhan-Aadhar-Mobile (JAM) linkage
- Ensuring availability of basic banking services within 5 KM distance of every village
- Promoting use of technology and lessons learnt from earlier financial inclusion efforts

## Benefits

## Important Conditions

- Overdraft facility is available only after six months of satisfactory account operation
- Overdraft facility of up to Rs. 2,000 has no conditions attached;
- higher amounts require satisfactory transaction history and Aadhaar linkage (or declaration if Aadhaar not available)
- Age limit for overdraft facility is 18-65 years
- Accidental insurance cover on RuPay card is Rs. 1 lakh for existing accounts and Rs. 2 lakh for new accounts opened after 28.8.18
- Small accounts (opened without valid documents) have transaction limits: total credit not exceeding Rs. 1 lakh in a year, total debit not exceeding Rs. 10,000 in a month, and balance not exceeding Rs. 50,000 at any point;
- such accounts are valid for 12 months and can be extended if the holder applies for a valid document within that period
