# PM Vishwakarma

## Overview

PM Vishwakarma (launched 17.09.2023) supports traditional artisans and
craftspeople in **18 notified trades** (carpenter, blacksmith, potter,
cobbler/shoemaker, mason ( sculptor/stone-breaker family), goldsmith,
tailor, barber, boat maker, armourer, hammer & toolkit maker, locksmith,
fishing-net maker, doll & toy maker, marble sculptor, broom maker,
basket maker, washerfolk (Dhobi)) with end-to-end support: **PM
Vishwakarma certificate and ID card**, **₹15,000 toolkit e-voucher**,
**skill training with ₹500/day stipend**, **collateral-free enterprise
development loans** (₹1 lakh first tranche; ₹2 lakh second tranche after
repayment — total ₹3 lakh at concessional interest with 5% cap borne by
beneficiary and Government covering 8%), digital-transaction incentives,
and marketing support [S1][S2].

## Objective

To recognise and upscale the guru-shishya tradition-based traditional
trades, improve quality/scale/market reach of artisans' products, and
integrate them into MSME value chains and formal credit.

## Target Beneficiaries

Self-employed traditional artisans/craftspeople in the 18 notified
trades, aged 18+, engaged in the trade on the date of registration,
not government/salaried employees, not having taken similar credit-
linked subsidy loans in the past 5 years (per eligibility on the portal)
[S1][S2].

## Key Features

- Recognition: certificate + ID card (UV/embedded QR)
- Toolkit e-voucher ₹15,000 [S2]
- Basic + advanced skill training with ₹500/day stipend [S2]
- Collateral-free Enterprise Development Loan: **₹1 lakh (18-month
  tenure) + ₹2 lakh (30-month tenure) after first-tranche repayment**
  [S1][S2]
- Concessional interest: beneficiary pays 5%; Government covers the
  difference (8%) [S2]
- Incentive for digital transactions; marketing support (e-marketplace
  linkage, exhibitions)

## Eligibility

Summary — see [eligibility.md](eligibility.md): artisan in a notified
trade, 18+, self-employed, Indian citizen; prior similar-scheme loans
and salaried persons excluded.

## Benefits

Toolkit voucher, stipend, concessional credit, marketing. Details:
[benefits.md](benefits.md).

## Documents

Aadhaar-linked registration, trade details, bank account; trade
verification at CSC. Details: [documents.md](documents.md).

## Application

Online registration via
[pmvishwakarma.gov.in](https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in/) or CSCs; verification
by district teams; training + benefits sequence. Details:
[application.md](application.md).

## Important Conditions

- Benefits follow a **sequence**: registration → verification → training
  (stipend) → toolkit voucher → loan eligibility
- Second loan tranche **only after** full repayment of the first [S2]
- Government covers 8% interest; the artisan pays 5% [S2]

## Exclusions

Salaried/government employees; minors; prior similar-scheme loan
beneficiaries (past 5 years); trades outside the 18 trades. Structured
list: [exclusions.md](exclusions.md).
