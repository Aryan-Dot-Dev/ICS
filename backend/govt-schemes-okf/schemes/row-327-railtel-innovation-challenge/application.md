## Process

1. Prepare application with all required documents as per EOI requirements.
2. Pay empanelment fee via RTGS and attach UTR details.
3. Submit the complete application (single PDF for documents, Excel sheet for Supplier/Customer form, payment details) to baapplications@railtelindia.com.
4. Application undergoes scrutiny for eligibility and compliance.
5. Shortlisted applicants may be contacted for clarification or additional documentation.
6. Final selection leads to issuance of Letter of Empanelment (LOE).
7. Empanelment is valid for 3 years from LOE issuance date.
