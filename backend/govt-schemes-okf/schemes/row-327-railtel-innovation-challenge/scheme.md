# RailTel Innovation Challenge

## Overview

RailTel Innovation Challenge is a program by RailTel Corporation of India Limited, a Navratna PSU under the Ministry of Railways, aimed at fostering innovation by partnering with startups and technology providers. The initiative seeks to empanel business associates who can deliver innovative solutions in areas such as telepresence, cloud services, AI/ML, IoT, and smart city projects. Through this challenge, RailTel intends to collaborate with empaneled partners to co-create and deploy technology solutions on a revenue-sharing or service-based model, leveraging its nationwide ICT infrastructure.

## Objective

- To empanel business partners capable of delivering innovative technology solutions
- To expand RailTel's market share in telecom and IT offerings such as telepresence, cloud services, and AI/ML
- To support RailTel's objective of multi-fold growth in turnover and profitability
- To enable partners to acquire new business opportunities from RailTel's customers across industries
- To foster collaboration in system integration, digital services, and smart city projects
- To promote sustainable business models through revenue-sharing or service-based partnerships
- To leverage RailTel's nationwide infrastructure including optic fiber network, data centers, and NOCs
- To enhance service delivery in sectors like health, education, security, agriculture, and urban development

## Important Conditions

- Empanelment fee is non-refundable unless application is not converted into empanelment
- False information/documents lead to cancellation of empanelment without fee refund
- RailTel reserves the right to accept or reject any application without assigning any reason
- Empanelled partners must inform RailTel proactively if bidding opportunities are identified
- Validity of empanelment is 3 years from LOE issuance, after which re-application is required
- RailTel does not guarantee confirmed business during the empanelment period
