## Process

1. State Implementing Agency (SIA) or Implementation Support Agency (ISA) prepares project proposal in coordination with State Nodal Department (SND) and respective Regional Office of TRIFED.
2. Project proposal is submitted to TRIFED for approval, including detailed profiling of constituent VDVKs, geographical and resource mapping, activities for awareness generation and handholding support, revival plan of defunct VDVKs, assessments of production potential, infrastructure, market prospects, and post-implementation plan.
3. Upon approval, funds are released in tranches based on milestones.
4. SIA/ISA is delegated administrative and financial powers to execute VDPE activities.
5. TRIFED Regional Offices assist in proposal formulation, field-level coordination, verification visits, compliance checks, and monitoring & evaluation.
6. VDPEs must undergo formation prerequisites including infrastructure assessment, compliance gaps, capacity-building needs, and market linkages, documented in a Detailed Project Report (DPR).
7. VDPEs are established through legal registration under recognized structures such as Societies, Co-operatives, Producer Companies, or MSMEs.
8. Operationalization includes market linkage, branding, full-scale operations, monitoring & evaluation, and SRoI assessment.
