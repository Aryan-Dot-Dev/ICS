# Tribal Innovation & Entrepreneurship Fund (TIEF)

## Overview

Tribal Innovation & Entrepreneurship Fund (TIEF) is a scheme under TRIFED aimed at fostering entrepreneurship among tribal communities by supporting the establishment of Van Dhan Producer Enterprises (VDPEs) through resource allocation, capacity building, and market access. It seeks to transform Van Dhan Vikas Kendras (VDVKs) into profit-oriented legal entities to enhance tribal income and economic resilience. The scheme integrates tribal communities into formal economic systems by promoting collective processing, value addition, and marketing of forest, farm, and non-farm products.

## Objective

- Formalizing and strengthening VDVKs, ensuring socio-economic empowerment
- Promoting entrepreneurship among Tribals
- Increasing the livelihood opportunities for individual members of VDPEs
- Developing sustainable value chains for Minor Forest Produce (MFP)
- Integrating tribal communities into formal economic systems
- Enhancing tribal income through collective processing and market access
- Supporting establishment of Van Dhan Producer Enterprises (VDPEs) as profit-oriented legal entities
- Facilitating access to institutional finance and scaling operations

## Important Conditions

- VDPEs must be formed by federating four or more geographically proximate VDVKs;
- relaxation may be granted by TRIFED if justified and feasible.
- At least one-third of women's representation on the Board of Directors (BoD), with at least 50% of women from the tribal community.
- At least 70% of BoD members must be from the tribal community.
- BoD must convene at least two formal meetings annually.
- All governance structures must operate in alignment with the legal form adopted by the VDPE.
- TRIFED and concerned district/state authorities conduct periodic reviews and audits to uphold transparency and compliance.
- Fund release is contingent upon verified completion of deliverables and milestones.
- VDPEs are not standalone manufacturing/storage/procurement facilities but holistic enterprises under one legal umbrella.
