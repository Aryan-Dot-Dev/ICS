# MNRE Off-grid Solar Power Schemes

## Overview

The MNRE Off-grid Solar Power Schemes support decentralized solar energy solutions for areas without reliable grid access. These schemes aim to promote solar electrification in rural and remote regions through financial assistance and implementation support. They focus on standalone solar systems, solar pumps, and other off-grid applications to enhance energy access and sustainability.

## Objective

- Provide electricity access to unelectrified households and villages
- Promote solar-powered irrigation through solar water pumps
- Support decentralized renewable energy applications in remote areas
- Reduce dependence on diesel generators and fossil fuels in off-grid settings
- Enhance livelihoods and productivity through reliable solar power supply

## Eligibility

Beneficiaries include individuals, farmers, cooperatives, panchayats, and government institutions in off-grid or poorly electrified areas. Systems must comply with MNRE technical standards and be installed by authorized channel partners. Priority is given to SC/ST, women, and border regions.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial subsidies for solar home lights, solar street lights, solar pumps, and mini-grids; reduced electricity costs; reliable power supply; environmental benefits through reduced emissions; and support for agricultural productivity via solar-powered irrigation.
