## Process

1. State Implementing Agency (SIA) or Implementation Support Agency (ISA) prepares project proposal in coordination with State Nodal Department (SND) and TRIFED Regional Office (RO).
2. Submit proposal to TRIFED for approval.
3. Upon approval, receive first tranche (10% for SIA, 10–20% for ISA) to initiate activities like staff deployment and awareness generation.
4. Submit Utilization Certificate (UC) and Detailed Project Report (DPR) for second tranche (40% for SIA, 30–40% for ISA) to fund capacity building, infrastructure setup, and revival of defunct VDVKs.
5. Achieve full utilization of second tranche, undergo verification, and submit supporting documents for final tranche (50% for SIA, remaining balance for ISA) to support market linkage, operationalization, and monitoring & evaluation.
6. TRIFED conducts periodic physical verifications, audits, and performance evaluations throughout the lifecycle.
