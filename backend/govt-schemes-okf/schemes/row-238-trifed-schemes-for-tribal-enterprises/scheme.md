# TRIFED Schemes for Tribal Enterprises

## Overview

TRIFED Schemes for Tribal Enterprises aim to promote tribal entrepreneurship by transforming Van Dhan Vikas Kendras (VDVKs) into Van Dhan Producer Enterprises (VDPEs) through capacity building, market access, and financial support. The schemes focus on value addition of Minor Forest Produce (MFP), sustainable livelihood generation, and integration of tribal communities into formal economic systems. TRIFED acts as the nodal agency under the Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Government of India, implementing initiatives like Van Dhan Yojana, PTP-NER, and Tech for Tribals to enhance tribal income and economic resilience.

## Objective

- Promote sustainable livelihood systems for tribal people through marketing development and remunerative pricing
- Empower tribal producers to embrace entrepreneurship as a way of life
- Strengthen tribal value chains by transforming VDVKs into legally registered Van Dhan Producer Enterprises (VDPEs)
- Enhance market access through institutional partnerships, exhibitions, and e-commerce platforms
- Provide capacity building and skill upgradation via collaborations with IITs, TISS, and other reputed institutions
- Ensure socio-economic empowerment through collective processing, value addition, and marketing of forest and farm products
- Facilitate access to institutional finance and scale operations for tribal enterprises
- Promote tribal products in domestic and international markets through brand development and strategic alliances

## Important Conditions

- Fund release is contingent upon achievement of key implementation milestones and verified completion of deliverables
- VDPEs must be formed by federating four or more geographically proximate VDVKs, with relaxation only after TRIFED review based on feasibility and justification
- At least 70% of Board of Directors must be from the tribal community, and at least one-third must be women (with 50% of women from tribal community)
- All direct financial assistance for working capital must be carried out only through bank account transfers
- Van Dhan Vikas Kendras and SHGs must open bank accounts as per scheme guidelines
- The scheme is an ongoing intervention covering 27 States and 308 Tribal pre-dominant districts under Van Dhan program and MSP for MFP Scheme
