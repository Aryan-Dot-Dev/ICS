# Haryana State Start-ups Scheme

## Overview

The Haryana State Start-ups Scheme is a flagship initiative of the Government of Haryana under the Department of Industries and Commerce, aimed at fostering innovation, entrepreneurship, and job creation by providing a comprehensive framework of fiscal and non-fiscal incentives to startups and incubators. It supports the creation of a vibrant startup ecosystem through financial assistance, regulatory simplifications, mentorship, incubation, and access to funding and networking opportunities. The scheme is aligned with the Haryana State Startup Policy 2022 and targets DPIIT-recognized startups operating within or through government-supported infrastructure in Haryana.

## Objective

- Foster innovation & entrepreneurship
- Create jobs and stimulate economic growth
- Encourage investment in startups
- Develop a robust startup ecosystem
- Establish Haryana as a preferred destination for startups
- Support 5000 startups in the next 5 years
- Attract major investment
- Provide financial incentives including grants, subsidies, and tax benefits

## Important Conditions

- Benefits/Incentives can be availed under only one policy in the State (viz.
- Haryana State Startup Policy 2022 or HEEP 2020 or MSME or IT & ESDM Policy or any other State Policy).
- All Government-owned/Supported Incubators in Haryana must register with Startup Haryana Cell under DITECH to be eligible for benefits.
- Private incubators must have at least 10,000 Sq.
- of built-up space, owned or leased, with lease period minimum 5 years if leased.
- Seed Grant applications are evaluated quarterly;
- applications received in a quarter are considered in the subsequent quarter by the Evaluation & Approval Committee.
- Lease Rental Subsidy is available for a maximum of 1 year only.
- Patent Cost Reimbursement requires submission of CA-certified Utilization Certificate, invoices, tickets/boarding passes (if travel), and progress reports.
- Cloud Storage Reimbursement is limited to Haryana-based Data Centres only.
- Net SGST Reimbursement is capped at 100% of Fixed Capital Investment and available for 7 years.
