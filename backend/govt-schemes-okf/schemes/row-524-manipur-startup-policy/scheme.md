# Manipur Startup Policy

## Overview

The Manipur Startup Policy is a state-level initiative aimed at fostering innovation and entrepreneurship within Manipur. It seeks to create a supportive ecosystem for startups by providing financial, infrastructural, and regulatory assistance. The policy focuses on promoting technology-driven ventures and encouraging youth participation in entrepreneurial activities. It aligns with national startup goals while addressing regional challenges and opportunities.

## Objective

- Promote innovation and entrepreneurship in Manipur
- Support technology-driven startups with financial and infrastructural aid
- Encourage youth and women participation in startup ecosystem
- Simplify regulatory processes for startup establishment and operation
- Create incubation and mentorship facilities for early-stage ventures
- Facilitate access to funding through government and private partnerships
- Strengthen industry-academia collaboration for research and development
- Monitor and evaluate startup performance for policy improvement

## Eligibility

Startups registered in Manipur as private limited companies, LLPs, or partnership firms, incorporated within the last 7 years, with annual turnover not exceeding INR 25 crores, and working on innovative products, services, or processes with potential for scalability and employment generation.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial support in the form of seed funding, grants, or equity-based assistance; access to incubation centers with infrastructure and mentorship; regulatory facilitation including single-window clearance; tax and duty exemptions as per state provisions; participation in state-organized startup events and networking opportunities; guidance on intellectual property rights and legal compliance.
