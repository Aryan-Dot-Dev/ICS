## Process

1. Register on the official Manipur Startup Policy portal.
2. Submit the online application form with business details and innovation description.
3. Upload required documents including Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, and pitch deck.
4. Await preliminary screening by the State Startup Committee.
5. Present the business model before the selection panel for evaluation.
6. Receive approval and disbursement of funds upon successful selection.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year.
