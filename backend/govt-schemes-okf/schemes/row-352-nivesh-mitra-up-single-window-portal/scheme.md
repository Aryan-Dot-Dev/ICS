# Nivesh Mitra – UP Single Window Portal

## Overview

Nivesh Mitra – UP Single Window Portal is a state-level initiative by the Government of Uttar Pradesh to provide a unified online platform for entrepreneurs and businesses to apply for various approvals, licenses, and incentives from multiple government departments. It streamlines the application process by enabling a single Common Application Form, online payment, and real-time tracking of applications across departments such as MSME, Industrial Development Authorities, Pollution Control Board, and others. The portal aims to reduce procedural delays, enhance transparency, and improve the ease of doing business in Uttar Pradesh.

## Objective

- Provide a single online platform for submitting applications to multiple government departments
- Enable a Common Application Form to avoid repetitive data entry
- Facilitate online payment of fees and charges through integrated payment gateway
- Ensure real-time tracking and status updates of applications
- Reduce procedural delays and enhance transparency in approvals
- Integrate services from departments like MSME, Industrial Development, Pollution Control, and others
- Support grievance redressal and refund processing for online transactions
