# NFDC Production Grants

## Overview

The NFDC Production Grants scheme is part of the Development, Communication and Dissemination of Filmic Content (DCDFC) scheme by the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting, under which NFDC produces and co-produces feature films, children's films, and documentary films in various Indian languages. It encourages debutant filmmakers by undertaking 100% production of their first feature film, children's film, and documentary film, and supports co-production of good quality films in partnership with filmmakers from India and abroad. The scheme aims to foster excellence in cinema, promote cultural diversity, and preserve India's cinematic heritage.

## Objective

- Encourage debutant filmmakers by undertaking 100% production of their first feature film, children's film, and documentary film
- Support co-production of good quality films in partnership with filmmakers from India and abroad
- Foster excellence in cinema and promote the diversity of Indian culture
- Preserve the rich heritage of filmic content and ensure its wider reach within and across borders
- Develop talent and facilitate the growth of Indian cinema in all languages through productions and co-productions
- Promote Indian culture through cinema in India and overseas
- Build a lean and flexible organization responsive to the needs of the Indian film industry

## Eligibility

Under the DCDFC scheme, NFDC produces & co-produces films, children's films and documentaries under its extant guidelines for production, whereby it encourages debutant filmmakers by undertaking 100% production of their first Feature Film, Children’s Film and Documentary Film and Co-production of good quality films in partnership with filmmakers both from India and abroad.

## Benefits

100% production support for debut feature films, children's films, and documentary films; co-production opportunities with Indian and international filmmakers; access to NFDC's production guidelines and expertise; participation in Film Bazaar and other NFDC initiatives for global exposure; preservation and promotion of India's cinematic heritage.
