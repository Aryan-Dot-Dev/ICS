# Modified Electronics Manufacturing Clusters (EMC 2.0)

## Overview

The Modified Electronics Manufacturing Clusters (EMC 2.0) scheme is a government initiative under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) aimed at strengthening India's electronics manufacturing ecosystem by developing world-class infrastructure and common facilities in electronics manufacturing clusters. The scheme supports the creation of plug-and-play infrastructure, common facility centers, and other enabling infrastructure to attract investments in electronics manufacturing. It aims to enhance India's competitiveness in global electronics value chains and promote domestic value addition.

## Objective

- To create world-class infrastructure for electronics manufacturing
- To provide plug-and-play facilities and common facility centers
- To attract investments in electronics manufacturing sectors
- To enhance domestic value addition in electronics manufacturing
- To promote exports and reduce imports of electronic goods
- To generate employment opportunities in the electronics sector
- To promote innovation and R&D in electronics manufacturing
- To support the development of semiconductor and display manufacturing ecosystems

## Important Conditions

- Funding is subject to availability of funds under the scheme
- Projects must comply with all statutory and regulatory clearances
- Land must be free from encumbrances and litigation
- The scheme supports only electronics manufacturing and related activities
- Beneficiaries must comply with environmental and labor laws
- Funding is released in milestones based on project progress verification
- The scheme does not cover land cost, only infrastructure development
- Private sector participation must be through a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV)
