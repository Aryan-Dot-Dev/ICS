## Process

1. State Government/UT Administration or its agency identifies land and prepares a Detailed Project Report (DPR) for the Electronics Manufacturing Cluster (EMC).
2. The proposal is submitted to the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) through the State Government/UT Administration.
3. MeitY appraises the proposal through an Expert Committee or Project Appraisal Committee.
4. Upon approval, an In-Principle Approval (IPA) is issued.
5. The State Government/UT Administration acquires the land and begins site development.
6. Detailed Project Report (DPR) is submitted for final approval.
7. Upon final approval, the Viability Gap Funding (VGF) is released in milestones linked to project progress.
8. The State Government/UT Administration or its agency implements the project and develops the infrastructure.
9. The developed infrastructure is made available to electronics manufacturers on a plug-and-play basis.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds and appraisal cycles.
