# National Mission on Interdisciplinary Cyber-Physical Systems (NM-ICPS)

## Overview

The National Mission on Interdisciplinary Cyber-Physical Systems (NM-ICPS) is a mission-mode initiative under the Department of Science and Technology (DST) aimed at fostering innovation, research, and development in cyber-physical systems and related interdisciplinary domains. It seeks to strengthen India's capabilities in strategic technologies, promote technological self-reliance, and align with the Atmanirbhar Bharat vision by supporting academia, industry, startups, and research institutions in cutting-edge areas such as AI, robotics, quantum computing, and IoT. The mission encourages private sector participation and aims to build a robust ecosystem for innovation and entrepreneurship in deep tech sectors.

## Objective

- Encourage private sector involvement in high-impact R&D
- Promote strategic technologies including quantum computing, robotics, space, AI, and biotechnology
- Foster self-reliance and economic security through indigenization of critical technologies
- Ensure flexibility in sectoral support for public interest
- Strengthen India’s capabilities in strategic technologies and promote technological self-reliance
- Align with the nation’s long-term innovation and Atmanirbhar Bharat vision
- Catalyze private sector participation in high-impact R&D
- Support technologies where indigenization is essential for strategic or economic reasons

## Important Conditions

- Grants and short-term loans are not supported under the scheme
- Disbursements are routed exclusively through designated Second-Level Fund Managers (SLFMs);
- direct proposals to DST are discouraged
- The entire fund must be returned to the Consolidated Fund of India (CFI) after 50 years without any interest
- Funding is contingent on approval by the Empowered Group of Secretaries (EGoS) for exceptions beyond standard coverage
