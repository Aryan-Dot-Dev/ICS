# Pradhan Mantri National AIDS Control Programme

## Overview

The Pradhan Mantri National AIDS Control Programme (PMNACP) is a centrally sponsored scheme implemented by the National AIDS Control Organisation (NACO) under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India. It aims to prevent and control HIV/AIDS through targeted interventions, awareness campaigns, and provision of antiretroviral therapy (ART) and other healthcare services. The programme focuses on high-risk groups, general population awareness, and strengthening healthcare infrastructure for HIV management across the country.

## Objective

- Reduce new HIV infections through targeted prevention strategies
- Provide comprehensive care, support, and treatment to people living with HIV
- Strengthen institutional capacity for effective programme implementation
- Enhance strategic information management for monitoring and evaluation
- Promote community participation and reduce stigma and discrimination
