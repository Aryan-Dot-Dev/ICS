## Process

1. Individuals seeking ART must visit a designated ART centre with HIV-positive test report.
2. Registration is done at the ART centre after counselling and baseline investigations.
3. Eligibility for free ART is assessed based on clinical and immunological criteria.
4. Upon registration, monthly ART drug kits are dispensed free of cost.
5. Regular follow-up visits are scheduled for monitoring and adherence counselling.
6. For targeted interventions, high-risk groups access services through NGOs or TI sites after outreach and mobilization.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — services are available throughout the year at designated centres.
