# Stree Shakti Package for Women Entrepreneurs

## Overview

The Stree Shakti Package for Women Entrepreneurs is a scheme by the State Bank of India (SBI) aimed at supporting women entrepreneurs by providing financial assistance and other benefits to promote women entrepreneurship in India. The scheme offers financial support in the form of loans and other benefits to help women start and expand their businesses.

## Objective

- To promote women entrepreneurship in India
- To provide financial assistance to women entrepreneurs for starting and expanding businesses
- To offer concessional interest rates and relaxed collateral requirements for women borrowers
- To support women in achieving financial independence and business growth

## Benefits

The scheme offers concessional interest rates, reduced margin requirements, relaxed collateral norms, and extended repayment periods. It also provides access to SBI's digital banking platforms like YONO for easy application and tracking. Additional benefits include waiver of certain processing fees and access to financial literacy and business development support through SBI's initiatives.
