# Impact Investors Council (IIC) India Fund

## Overview

The Impact Investors Council (IIC) India Fund is a social entrepreneurship initiative aimed at catalyzing impact investing in India by supporting enterprises that generate measurable social and environmental outcomes alongside financial returns. It functions as a platform to mobilize capital toward high-impact ventures, particularly in underserved sectors. The fund seeks to bridge the gap between traditional investment and social development by promoting scalable, innovative business models. It operates under the IIC’s broader mission to strengthen the impact investing ecosystem in India through collaboration, research, and capital deployment.

## Objective

- Mobilize capital for enterprises delivering measurable social and environmental impact
- Support scalable and innovative business models in underserved sectors
- Strengthen the impact investing ecosystem through collaboration and knowledge sharing
- Promote transparency, accountability, and standardization in impact measurement
- Facilitate access to funding for early-stage and growth-stage impact ventures
- Advocate for policy enablers that support social entrepreneurship and impact investing

## Eligibility

Enterprises must demonstrate a clear intent to generate positive social or environmental impact alongside financial returns, operate within India, and have a scalable business model. Preference is given to ventures in sectors such as agriculture, healthcare, education, clean energy, and financial inclusion.
