# Broadcast Audience Research Council (BARC) Data Access for Startups

## Overview

Broadcast Audience Research Council (BARC) India is a Joint Industry Body founded by stakeholder bodies representing Broadcasters, Advertisers, and Advertising and Media Agencies. It provides transparent, accurate, and inclusive TV audience measurement data to power efficient media spends and content decisions in India's broadcasting sector. BARC India operates the world's largest television audience measurement system, covering approximately 257,000+ individuals across 58,000+ households. The scheme offers data access for startups through its products and insights, though no specific startup-focused grant or financial support program is detailed in the evidence.

## Objective

- Provide transparent, accurate, and inclusive TV audience measurement system
- Power efficient media spends and content decisions in the television sector
- Generate Big Data driven insights for Broadcasters, Advertisers and Agencies
- Maintain a robust and future-ready technology backbone
- Ensure the selected panel represents a true microcosm of India's TV viewing landscape
