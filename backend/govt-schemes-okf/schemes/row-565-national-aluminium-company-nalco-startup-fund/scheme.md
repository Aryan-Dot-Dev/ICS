# National Aluminium Company (NALCO) Startup Fund

## Overview

The National Aluminium Company (NALCO) Startup Fund is a scheme under the PSU Support category aimed at supporting startups through financial assistance and other benefits. The scheme is managed by NALCO, a Government of India Enterprise, and focuses on promoting innovation and entrepreneurship in alignment with national objectives. It provides financial support to eligible startups, particularly those engaged in innovation and technology-driven ventures, and facilitates their growth through funding and non-financial benefits.

## Objective

- To promote innovation and entrepreneurship in India
- To provide financial support to early-stage startups
- To encourage technology-driven ventures aligned with national priorities
- To strengthen the startup ecosystem through PSU-led initiatives
- To facilitate access to funding and mentorship for emerging enterprises

## Eligibility

Startups incorporated in India, working on innovative products, services, or processes, with a focus on technology and innovation, and meeting the eligibility criteria as defined under the Startup India initiative or as specified by NALCO for the fund.
