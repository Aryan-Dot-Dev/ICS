## Process

1. Interested startups must register on the NALCO portal or designated platform.
2. Submit a detailed proposal outlining the innovation, business model, and alignment with NALCO’s sectors.
3. Undergo evaluation by NALCO’s expert committee based on innovation, feasibility, and impact.
4. Shortlisted startups may be invited for presentations or due diligence.
5. Selected startups receive funding and enter into a formal agreement with NALCO.

## Deadlines
