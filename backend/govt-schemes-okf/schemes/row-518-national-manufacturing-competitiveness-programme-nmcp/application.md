## Process

1. Identify the relevant NMCP component (e.g., PMS, Design Clinic, Lean Manufacturing) based on the required intervention.
2. Ensure the enterprise has a valid Udyam Registration Certificate.
3. Contact the concerned Implementing Agency (e.g., MSME Development Institute, NSIC, KVIC, Coir Board) or apply online through the designated portal.
4. Submit the application form along with required documents such as Udyam Registration, project report, quotations, and affidavits.
5. The Implementing Agency scrutinizes the application for eligibility and technical feasibility.
6. Upon approval, the beneficiary implements the approved activity.
7. Submit claim for reimbursement with supporting documents (bills, receipts, proof of participation) within the stipulated time (usually 30 days post-event or activity completion).
8. The Implementing Agency verifies the claim and processes the subsidy reimbursement.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications can be submitted throughout the year, subject to availability of funds and approval cycles of Implementing Agencies.
