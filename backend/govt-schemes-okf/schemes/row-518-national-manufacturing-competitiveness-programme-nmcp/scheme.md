# National Manufacturing Competitiveness Programme (NMCP)

## Overview

The National Manufacturing Competitiveness Programme (NMCP) is a scheme under the Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises aimed at enhancing the competitiveness of the MSME sector through various interventions. It focuses on improving technology, quality, design, packaging, and market access for MSMEs. The programme supports clusters and individual enterprises in adopting modern practices to increase productivity and sustainability.

## Objective

- Enhance competitiveness of MSMEs through technology upgradation
- Improve quality and design capabilities of MSME products
- Promote adoption of modern packaging techniques
- Support market access initiatives including participation in trade fairs
- Facilitate cluster-based development of MSMEs
- Encourage energy efficiency and sustainable manufacturing practices
- Strengthen backward and forward linkages for MSMEs
- Develop skilled manpower in the MSME sector

## Eligibility

Manufacturing / Service sector MSMEs having valid Udyam Registration (UR) Certificate are eligible to avail benefits under the NMCP scheme components such as Procurement and Marketing Support (PMS), Design Clinic, Lean Manufacturing Competitiveness Scheme, etc., as per the specific guidelines of each sub-scheme.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial assistance in the form of subsidies for various interventions: up to 80% subsidy on built-up space rent for trade fair participation (100% for SC/ST/Women/PH/Aspirational Districts), 80% financial assistance on one-time and annual recurring fees for Bar Code adoption (up to Rs. 50,650), financial assistance on annual membership/subscription fees for e-commerce platform adoption,…

## Important Conditions

- Subsidy is provided on reimbursement basis after successful completion of the activity
- Beneficiary must bear the non-subsidized portion of the cost upfront
- Claims must be submitted within 30 days of event completion or activity conclusion
- Maximum of two trade fair participations allowed per enterprise per year under PMS
- Bar Code adoption support is limited to micro enterprises only and for up to 100 bar codes
- e-Commerce platform support is available only for micro enterprises selling up to 10 new products
- Retail outlet development requires 50% contribution from the retailer and sale of only GI-tagged products
- Cluster-based interventions require formation of an approved cluster with SPV or consortium
- Any misuse of Ministry's logo or support may lead to blacklisting for three years
- Financial assistance is subject to availability of budgetary allocation under the scheme
