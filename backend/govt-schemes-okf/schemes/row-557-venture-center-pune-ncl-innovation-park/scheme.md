# Venture Center Pune – NCL Innovation Park

## Overview

Venture Center Pune – NCL Innovation Park is India's largest technology incubator hosted by CSIR’s National Chemical Laboratory, supporting science-based startups since December 2006. It provides a comprehensive innovation ecosystem including mentoring, funding, scientific assistance, incubation, and specialized resource centers. As an approved incubator by DST-NSTEDB, it collaborates with BIRAC, DST, Atal Innovation Mission, MeitY, and the Defence Innovation Organisation to nurture startups across lifecycle stages.

## Objective

- Nurture and propel early-stage ideas, startups, and entrepreneurs
- Provide mentoring expertise, funding, infrastructure support, and open-access scientific and prototyping facilities
- Offer business, IP & regulatory advisory services and specialized resource centers
- Support startups in difficult domain areas such as life sciences, engineering, materials sciences, agriculture, and clean energy
- Validate technologies and de-risk new ventures through proof of concept and prototyping
- Facilitate market entry and scaling via investor readiness programs and commercialization support
- Foster a stimulating environment through proximity to academic and research organizations
- Build a strong peer network of like-minded, driven entrepreneurs
