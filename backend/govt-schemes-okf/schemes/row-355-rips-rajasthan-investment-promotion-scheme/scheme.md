# RIPS (Rajasthan Investment Promotion Scheme)

## Overview

The Rajasthan Investment Promotion Scheme (RIPS) is a state-level initiative by the Government of Rajasthan to promote industrial investment and economic development in the state. It offers financial incentives and concessions to eligible industrial units for setting up new enterprises or expanding existing ones. The scheme aims to generate employment, boost exports, and promote balanced regional development across Rajasthan.

## Objective

- Promote industrial investment in Rajasthan
- Generate employment opportunities in the state
- Boost exports from Rajasthan-based industries
- Encourage balanced regional development across districts
- Attract both domestic and foreign direct investment
- Support setting up of new industrial units and expansion of existing ones


## Target Beneficiaries

**MSME**, **large industries**, **manufacturing units**, **service enterprises**, **tourism units**

Eligible units include new industrial units setting up operations in Rajasthan or existing units undertaking substantial expansion. ## Benefits

Benefits under RIPS include exemption from electricity duty, stamp duty, land conversion charges, and entry tax on capital goods. Financial incentives consist of interest subsidy, employment generation subsidy, and capital subsidy. Additional benefits include reimbursement of VAT/CST, payroll tax subsidy, and special packages for mega projects and thrust sectors.
