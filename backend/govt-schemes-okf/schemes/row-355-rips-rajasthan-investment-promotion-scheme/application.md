## Process

1. Submit application online through the Rajasthan Single Sign-On (RajSSO) portal or designated nodal agency.
2. Upload required documents including project report, investment details, and clearance certificates.
3. Application is scrutinized by the District Level Committee (DLC) or State Level Committee (SLC) based on investment scale.
4. Upon approval, letter of intent or eligibility certificate is issued.
5. After commencement of commercial production, submit claims for subsidies with supporting documents.
6. Claims are verified and subsidies are disbursed through direct benefit transfer.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications can be submitted throughout the year as per scheme guidelines.
