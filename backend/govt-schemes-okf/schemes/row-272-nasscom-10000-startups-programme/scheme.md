# NASSCOM 10000 Startups Programme

## Overview

The NASSCOM 10000 Startups Programme is a joint initiative by NASSCOM and the Government of India aimed at nurturing and scaling 10,000 technology startups across India. It provides end-to-end support including mentorship, funding access, infrastructure, and market linkages to early-stage startups. The programme focuses on fostering innovation in emerging technologies and building a sustainable startup ecosystem through incubation, acceleration, and industry connect.

## Objective

- Nurture and scale 10,000 technology startups in India
- Provide mentorship and guidance from industry experts
- Facilitate access to funding through investor connects
- Offer infrastructure and incubation support
- Enable market access and industry linkages
- Promote innovation in emerging technologies such as AI, IoT, and blockchain
- Build a sustainable and inclusive startup ecosystem
- Support startups through all stages of growth from idea to scale

## Eligibility

The programme is open to early-stage technology startups incorporated in India, working on innovative products or services in domains such as software, hardware, AI, IoT, fintech, healthtech, and other emerging technologies. ## Benefits

Selected startups receive mentorship from industry leaders, access to NASSCOM’s network of investors and corporates, incubation support, office infrastructure, technical and business workshops, pitch events, demo days, and opportunities for global market access. The programme also facilitates connections with potential customers, partners, and venture capital firms.
