# Design Registration Facilitation for Startups

## Overview

The Design Registration Facilitation for Startups scheme under the Intellectual Property India portal supports startups in securing design protection by providing guidance on the design application workflow, eligibility criteria, fee structures, and procedural steps. It aims to simplify the design registration process for innovative startups through e-filing services, facilitator support, and clear documentation requirements.

## Objective

- Facilitate design registration for startups through simplified application procedures
- Provide access to e-filing services and IP facilitators for design protection
- Ensure clarity on eligibility, fees, and documentation for design applications
- Promote innovation by supporting startups in securing intellectual property rights
- Streamline the design application workflow from preparation to registration and publication

## Eligibility

Startups, as defined under the Startup India initiative, are eligible to apply for design registration. The applicant must be a recognized startup by DPIIT, incorporated as a private limited company, registered partnership, or limited liability partnership, with turnover less than INR 100 Crore and not older than 10 years from incorporation.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Startups benefit from facilitation in design registration, access to IP Mitras (facilitators) under the SIPP scheme, guidance on design application workflow, clarity on fee structures for natural persons/startups/small entities, and support through e-filing services. The scheme enables startups to secure legal protection for their designs, enhancing market competitiveness and innovation valuation.
