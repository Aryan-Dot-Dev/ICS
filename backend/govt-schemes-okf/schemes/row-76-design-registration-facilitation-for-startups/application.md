## Process

1. Check design availability by searching existing registered designs on the official website.
2. Assess novelty and identify the correct Locarno class-subclass.
3. Choose the goods/articles class and confirm eligibility and application category.
4. Decide applicant type (Individual/Startup/MSME/Others), understand applicable official fee, provide applicant details, design representation, and Power of Attorney (if applicable).
5. Prepare required information and documents, choose application type.
6. File new application (Form 1) via e-Filing portal: login, upload documents, pay prescribed fee.
7. Track application status online.
8. Respond to examination objections if required.
9. Application published in Design Journal upon acceptance.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year with no fixed deadline.
