# Drone Shakti Initiative (MoCA)

## Overview

The Drone Shakti Initiative (MoCA) is a government scheme launched by the Ministry of Civil Aviation to promote the drone and UAV ecosystem in India. It aims to foster innovation, support startups, and create employment opportunities in the drone sector through various initiatives including drone schools, pilot certification, and skill development programs. The initiative focuses on expanding drone applications across sectors such as agriculture, logistics, and surveillance, while ensuring regulatory compliance and safety standards. It also emphasizes skill development through partnerships with institutions like AASSC, IGRUA, and RGNAU to train certified pilots, trainers, and assessors.

## Objective

- Promote the growth of the drone and UAV ecosystem in India
- Support startups and MSMEs in the drone sector through incubation and financial support
- Develop skilled manpower through certified training programs
- Expand drone applications in agriculture, logistics, surveillance, and other sectors
- Ensure regulatory compliance and safety in drone operations
- Promote indigenous drone manufacturing and innovation
- Facilitate ease of doing business for drone operators through simplified regulations
- Create employment opportunities in the drone ecosystem
