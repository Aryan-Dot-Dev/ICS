## Process

1. Identify eligibility under the Drone Shakti Initiative based on entity type (startup, MSME, drone operator, training partner, etc.).
2. Ensure compliance with DGCA regulations under the Bharatiya Vayuyan Adhiniyam, 2024, including obtaining UIN and DAN where applicable.
3. For training partners, affiliate with recognized bodies such as AASSC, IGRUA, or RGNAU.
4. Apply for relevant government schemes such as the Credit Guarantee Scheme for Startups (CGSS), Seed Fund Scheme, or Fund of Funds for Startups (FFS) through the respective implementing agencies.
5. Submit required documents including incorporation certificates, PAN, business plan, compliance certificates, and training affiliation proofs.
6. Submit application via the respective scheme portal or through the Ministry of Civil Aviation's designated channels.
7. Await verification and approval from the concerned agency.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round.
