## Process

1. MSMEs must first confirm their eligibility as an open access consumer under the Electricity Act, 2003 and relevant CERC/SERC regulations.
2. They must apply for open access approval to the designated nodal agency (typically the State Transmission Utility or State Load Despatch Centre).
3. Submit required documents including MSME registration, proof of identity, address, and details of the proposed power procurement arrangement.
4. Upon approval, sign a Long-Term Access (LTA) or Medium-Term Open Access (MTOA) agreement with the transmission utility.
5. Coordinate with the chosen power generator/trader for scheduling and energy accounting.
6. Commence power draw under open access after meeting all technical and commercial conditions.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications can be submitted throughout the year as per the procedures of the respective State Transmission Utility or Load Despatch Centre.
