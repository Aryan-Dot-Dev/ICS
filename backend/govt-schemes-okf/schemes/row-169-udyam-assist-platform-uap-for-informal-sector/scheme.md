# Udyam Assist Platform (UAP) for Informal Sector

## Overview

The Udyam Assist Platform (UAP) is an initiative by the Ministry of MSME, Government of India, designed to facilitate the online registration of Informal Micro Enterprises (IMEs) through an Assist Process. It enables Designated Agencies (DAs) such as banks, NBFCs, and MFIs to share data of their IME customers to generate Udyam Registration Numbers (URN) and Udyam Assist Certificates (UAC). The platform aims to formalise informal enterprises outside the GST ecosystem and integrate them into digital and financial systems.

## Objective

- Provide Udyam Registration to Informal Micro Enterprises (IMEs) not registered with GST authorities
- Enable access to Priority Sector Lending (PSL) for loans to Udyam-registered IMEs
- Facilitate integration of IMEs into digital payment ecosystems via merchant QR codes
- Create a central digital identity (URN) for future access to governance, financial services, and digital marketplaces
- Generate quality data for better policy interventions for the informal sector
- Provide access to knowledge and skills development for IMEs

## Important Conditions

- Informal Micro Enterprises (IMEs) cannot register directly on the Udyam Assist Platform;
- they must approach a Designated Agency (DA) for assistance.
- Mere sharing of data by DAs does not entitle IMEs to URN/UAC;
- issuance is subject to validation as per program methodology.
- The Udyam Assist Platform does not provide direct financial benefits;
- it enables access to financial services through registration.
- Data shared by DAs must be with the consent of the IME customers.
- The platform is governed by the guidelines of the MSME Formalisation Project and Udyam Assist Platform terms and conditions.
