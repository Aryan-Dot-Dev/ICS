## Process

1. Patent offices interested in accessing TKDL must negotiate and sign an access agreement with the TKDL Unit.
2. Upon agreement, access credentials are provided to authorized patent examiners.
3. Authorized users can search the TKDL database through the secure access portal.
4. Search results are used to assess novelty and inventive step in patent applications related to traditional knowledge.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — access is granted upon execution of formal access agreements.
