# Traditional Knowledge Digital Library (TKDL)

## Overview

The Traditional Knowledge Digital Library (TKDL) is a collaborative initiative between the Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR), Ministry of Science and Technology, and the Department of Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha and Homoeopathy (AYUSH), Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India. It aims to prevent the misappropriation of India's traditional knowledge by providing access to patent examiners at international patent offices. The database contains transcribed and digitized traditional knowledge from ancient Indian texts in five international languages (English, French, German, Spanish, Japanese) to facilitate prior art searches.

## Objective

- Prevent the grant of erroneous patents on India's traditional knowledge
- Provide access to traditional knowledge in patent application languages to international patent offices
- Digitize and transcribe traditional knowledge from ancient Indian texts
- Facilitate prior art search for patent examiners worldwide
- Protect India's traditional knowledge from biopiracy
- Promote awareness and documentation of traditional knowledge systems

## Eligibility

TKDL is not a beneficiary-oriented scheme; it is a defensive database accessible to patent examiners under international agreements. Access is granted to patent offices that have signed access agreements with TKDL, including the European Patent Office (EPO), United States Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO), Japan Patent Office (JPO), United Kingdom Intellectual Property Office (UKIPO), and…


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Prevents erroneous patent grants on traditional knowledge, provides prior art evidence to patent examiners, supports legal opposition and revocation of patents, enhances global awareness of India's traditional knowledge, and safeguards indigenous rights without financial compensation to individuals.

- TKDL does not provide financial benefits or direct support to individuals or communities
- The database is not publicly accessible;
- access is limited to authorized patent examiners
- TKDL functions as a prior art repository and does not grant intellectual property rights
