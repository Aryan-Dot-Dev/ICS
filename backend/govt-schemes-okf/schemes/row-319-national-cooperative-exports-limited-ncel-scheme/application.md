## Process

1. Register as an FPO or cooperative society under the relevant Act.
2. Engage with NAFED as the National Implementing Agency for export promotion.
3. Submit product details and quality compliance documents.
4. Undergo capacity building and training on export procedures.
5. Receive support for branding, packaging, and market linkage.
6. Export facilitation through NAFED’s international trade network and portals like e-Samridhi and NAFEX.
