## Process

1. Apply for In-principle Approval as per Annexure-I.
2. Submit application for release of Financial Assistance as per Annexure-II-1 to II-4.
3. Competent Authority scrutinizes application; if deficiencies are found, applicant is given opportunity to rectify.
4. Upon satisfactory compliance, Financial Assistance is released.
5. Claims for stage payments can be submitted during construction based on progress, supported by documentary evidence.

## Deadlines

- Applications for contracts signed between April 1, 2016, and March 31, 2026, are eligible. No rolling basis mentioned; tied to contract dates within validity period.
