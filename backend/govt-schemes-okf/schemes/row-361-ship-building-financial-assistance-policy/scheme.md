# Ship Building Financial Assistance Policy

## Overview

The Shipbuilding Financial Assistance Policy (SBFAP) provides financial incentives to Indian shipyards for constructing vessels under shipbuilding contracts, with assistance released in stages tied to milestones such as keel laying, launching, delivery, and post-delivery. The policy aims to support the domestic shipbuilding industry, enhance global competitiveness, and promote indigenous manufacturing. It applies to all Indian shipyards and covers contracts signed between April 1, 2016, and March 31, 2026.

## Objective

- Provide financial assistance to shipyards for construction of vessels under shipbuilding contracts
- Support the growth and competitiveness of the Indian shipbuilding industry
- Promote indigenous manufacturing and self-reliance in shipbuilding
- Encourage construction of vessels in Indian shipyards through fiscal incentives
- Strengthen the maritime sector and support the Blue Economy

All Indian shipyards are eligible. ## Important Conditions

- Financial assistance shall only be released in Indian Rupees
- If budget is not available, assistance will be released on priority in subsequent financial year
- Applicant must not have availed monetary support under any other Central or State Government policy/scheme for the same vessel
- Major portion of hull must be constructed and assembled in India
- Assembly, installation, commissioning of major machinery, and tests/trials must be carried out in India
- Repeat orders require L1 price from initial competitive bidding to be considered as Fair Price
- Exchange rate for foreign currency conversion must be from State Bank of India TT Buying Rates on Date of Contract
