## Process

1. Identify rural poor households through SECC 2011 or participatory identification of poor (PIP).
2. Mobilize and form Self Help Groups (SHGs) of 10–20 members with facilitator support.
3. Train SHGs in group management, savings, and internal lending.
4. Open a joint bank account and begin regular savings.
5. Apply for Revolving Fund (RF) through the SHG federation after 3–6 months of functioning.
6. Upon RF utilization, apply for Community Investment Fund (CIF) via the federation for livelihood investments.
7. Access skill training and placement under Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grameen Kaushalya Yojana (DDU-GKY) or Rural Self Employment Training Institutes (RSETIs).
8. Link with banks for credit under SHG-Bank Linkage Programme with interest subvention.
9. Participate in producer collectives and market linkages for value chain development.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted throughout the year based on household mobilization and SHG readiness.
