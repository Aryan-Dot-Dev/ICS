# National Rural Livelihood Mission (NRLM)

## Overview

The National Rural Livelihood Mission (NRLM), also known as Aajeevika, is a flagship poverty alleviation program of the Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India. It aims to reduce poverty by enabling poor households to access gainful self-employment and skilled wage employment opportunities through the mobilization of rural poor into Self Help Groups (SHGs) and their federations. The mission focuses on building strong institutional platforms of the poor, enhancing livelihoods through skill development, access to credit, and market linkages, and promoting social inclusion and empowerment, particularly of women and marginalized groups.

## Objective

- Mobilize rural poor households into Self Help Groups (SHGs) and their federations
- Enable access to formal credit, livelihoods, and social security entitlements
- Enhance livelihoods through skill development, capacity building, and technology transfer
- Promote financial inclusion and linkage with banks
- Support farm and non-farm livelihood activities
- Strengthen institutions of the poor and promote social inclusion
- Facilitate market linkages and value addition for rural producers
- Empower women and vulnerable sections through leadership and decision-making roles

## Eligibility

The program targets rural poor households, particularly those identified through the Socio Economic and Caste Census (SECC) 2011. Eligibility is based on poverty and social vulnerability criteria, with priority given to women, SC/ST, persons with disabilities, and other marginalized groups. ## Important Conditions

- Benefits are contingent upon SECC 2011 or PIP-based identification of poverty
- SHGs must maintain regular savings and internal lending to qualify for RF/CIF
- CIF is released only to federated SHGs (Village Organizations/Cluster Level Federations)
- Interest subvention is available only to women SHGs on loans up to INR 3 lakh
- Funds are subject to availability and utilization norms as per NRLM guidelines
