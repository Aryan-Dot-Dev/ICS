# Punjab Startup and Entrepreneurship Policy

## Overview

The Punjab Startup and Entrepreneurship Policy is a state-level initiative launched by the Government of Punjab to foster innovation, support early-stage startups, and build a vibrant entrepreneurial ecosystem across the state. It aims to provide financial, infrastructural, and regulatory support to startups in key sectors such as IT, agro-processing, manufacturing, and biotechnology. The policy emphasizes ease of doing business, access to funding, and collaboration between industry, academia, and government.

## Objective

- Promote innovation and entrepreneurship across Punjab
- Provide financial assistance and incentives to early-stage startups
- Create incubation and co-working infrastructure
- Facilitate access to mentorship, networking, and market linkages
- Encourage sector-specific startups in IT, agro-processing, biotech, and manufacturing
- Simplify regulatory compliance and approval processes for startups
- Establish a Punjab Startup Fund for seed and early-stage investments
- Strengthen industry-academia collaboration for research and innovation

## Eligibility

Startups incorporated as Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm under relevant Indian laws, with headquarters or significant operations in Punjab, working on innovative products, services, or processes, and recognized by the Department of Industries, Government of Punjab, or an approved incubator.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial support through seed funding and grants, access to state-funded incubators and co-working spaces, mentorship from industry experts, fast-track approvals and single-window clearance, assistance in intellectual property rights (IPR) filing, participation in state-organized startup events and exhibitions, and facilitation of linkages with investors and markets.

## Important Conditions

- Financial support is subject to availability of funds under the Punjab Startup Fund
- Startups must maintain operations in Punjab for a minimum period of 3 years after receiving support
- Funding may be withdrawn if milestones are not met or if false information is provided
- Benefits under the policy may be revised or withdrawn by the state government
- Only startups recognized by the Department of Industries or approved incubators are eligible
