## Process

1. Obtain a valid Importer Exporter Code (IEC) from DGFT.
2. Identify the designated Special Notified Zone (SNZ) for diamond trading as notified by DGFT.
3. Ensure the business entity is located or registered to operate within the SNZ.
4. Comply with the procedural guidelines for diamond imports and exports under the Foreign Trade Policy.
5. Submit required documents for customs clearance through the ICEGATE portal or as specified by DGFT.
6. Avail benefits of streamlined procedures within the SNZ for diamond trading activities.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications and operations are permitted as long as the entity complies with SNZ regulations and holds a valid IEC.
