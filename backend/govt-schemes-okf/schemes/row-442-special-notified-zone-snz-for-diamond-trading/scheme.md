# Special Notified Zone (SNZ) for Diamond Trading

## Overview

The Special Notified Zone (SNZ) for Diamond Trading is a scheme under the Foreign Trade Policy aimed at promoting diamond trading by establishing designated zones with specific regulatory and procedural benefits. It facilitates diamond imports and exports by providing a streamlined customs and regulatory environment, encouraging value addition and job creation in the gems and jewellery sector. The scheme supports India's position as a global hub for diamond trading and processing.

## Objective

- Promote diamond trading and value addition in India
- Establish Special Notified Zones for diamond imports and exports
- Provide a streamlined customs and regulatory environment for diamond trade
- Encourage employment generation in the gems and jewellery sector
- Enhance India's competitiveness in the global diamond market
- Facilitate ease of doing business for diamond traders and processors


## Target Beneficiaries

**diamond traders**, **jewellery manufacturers**, **exporters**, **importers**, **MSMEs in gems and jewellery**


## Key Features

- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications and operations are permitted as long as the entity complies with SNZ regulations and holds a valid IEC.
- Portal: https://dgft.gov.in
- Source data last updated: 2026
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

Entities engaged in diamond trading, including importers, exporters, and processors, holding a valid Importer Exporter Code (IEC) issued by DGFT, and operating within the designated Special Notified Zones as notified by the Directorate General of Foreign Trade.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Simplified customs procedures for diamond imports and exports, reduced procedural delays, enhanced monitoring and security for high-value goods, facilitation of re-export of diamonds after value addition, and access to infrastructure and support services within the SNZ.


Structured benefit objects: [benefits.md](benefits.md).

## Important Conditions

- Benefits are available only within the geographically defined Special Notified Zones
- Entities must comply with all provisions of the Foreign Trade Policy and customs regulations
- Misuse of SNZ facilities for unauthorized trade may lead to penalties or withdrawal of benefits
- The scheme is subject to periodic review and modification by DGFT based on trade policy updates
