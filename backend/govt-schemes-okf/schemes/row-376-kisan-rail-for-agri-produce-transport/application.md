## Process

1. Identify the agricultural produce to be transported and ensure it meets the eligibility criteria for Kisan Rail.
2. Contact the nearest railway freight office or authorized logistics partner.
3. Submit details of the consignment including origin, destination, quantity, and type of produce.
4. Complete the booking process through the Indian Railways freight portal or authorized agent.
5. Prepare the goods for loading as per railway guidelines.
6. Track the shipment using the railway consignment note.
7. Collect the delivered goods at the destination station upon arrival.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — services are available throughout the year based on demand and operational feasibility.
