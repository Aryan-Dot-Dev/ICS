# Kisan Rail for Agri Produce Transport

## Overview

Kisan Rail for Agri Produce Transport is a scheme introduced by Indian Railways to facilitate the transportation of agricultural produce via dedicated freight trains, aiming to reduce post-harvest losses and improve farmers' income by ensuring timely and efficient movement of perishable goods across the country. The scheme leverages the existing rail network to connect farms with markets, supporting the agricultural supply chain.

## Objective

- To provide a seamless and cost-effective logistics solution for agricultural produce
- To reduce wastage and spoilage of perishable goods during transit
- To connect farmers directly with national and regional markets
- To promote the use of rail transport for agri-logistics over road transport
- To support the doubling of farmers' income by improving market access
- To enhance the efficiency of the agricultural supply chain through dedicated freight services

## Eligibility

Farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), cooperatives, agri-businesses, and other entities involved in the production and transportation of agricultural produce are eligible to utilize Kisan Rail services for transporting their goods.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Kisan Rail offers subsidized freight rates for agricultural commodities, ensures faster and safer transport of perishable goods, provides end-to-end logistics support including loading/unloading assistance, reduces dependency on road transport, minimizes post-harvest losses, and enables farmers to realize better prices by accessing distant markets in a timely manner.

## Important Conditions

- Subsidy is applicable only for notified routes and specific agricultural commodities
- Prior booking is required;
- services are subject to wagon and route availability
- The consignor is responsible for first-mile and last-mile connectivity
- Perishable goods must be properly packed to meet railway safety standards
- The scheme does not cover non-agricultural freight
