# Agri Export Zones (AEZ)

## Overview

Agri Export Zones (AEZ) is a scheme aimed at promoting agricultural exports by creating a cluster-based industries, value addition, and infrastructure development in the agriculture sector. The scheme focuses on integrating the entire value chain from production to export, providing support for post-harvest management, processing, and marketing of agricultural products. It encourages private sector participation and public-private partnerships to develop export-oriented agri-based industries. The initiative seeks to boost India's share in global agricultural trade by improving quality, reducing wastage, and enhancing competitiveness of agri-exports.

## Objective

- To promote export-oriented production and processing of agricultural products
- To develop infrastructure for post-harvest management and value addition
- To integrate farmers, processors, and exporters in a cluster-based approach
- To enhance quality and compliance with international standards
- To reduce transaction costs and improve supply chain efficiency
- To encourage private investment and public-private partnerships in agri-export
- To increase India's share in global agricultural exports through brand promotion
