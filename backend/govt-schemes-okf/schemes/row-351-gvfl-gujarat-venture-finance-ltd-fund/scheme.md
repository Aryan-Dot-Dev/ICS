# GVFL – Gujarat Venture Finance Ltd Fund

## Overview

GVFL – Gujarat Venture Finance Ltd Fund is a venture capital firm established in 1990 that provides financial and managerial support to innovative startups across various sectors in India. It manages multiple funds tailored to different startup growth stages, including pre-seed, seed, Series A, and Series B investments. GVFL focuses on sectors such as biotechnology, deep tech, clean tech, defence tech, healthcare, consumer brands, gaming, fintech, and agri-tech, offering capital along with strategic guidance and network access.

## Objective

- To deliver high returns to investors while supporting innovative entrepreneurs
- To promote entrepreneurship and venture capital activity in India
- To provide financial and managerial support to high-growth companies
- To nurture innovation across sectors including biotech, deep tech, and clean tech
- To build scalable and sustainable businesses through strategic guidance and network access
