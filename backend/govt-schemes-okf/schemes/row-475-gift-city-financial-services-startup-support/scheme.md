# GIFT City Financial Services Startup Support

## Overview

GIFT City Financial Services Startup Support is a program designed to foster innovation and growth in the financial technology and banking, financial services, and insurance (BFSI) sectors by providing startups with access to GIFT City's infrastructure, regulatory sandbox, and ecosystem benefits. The scheme aims to attract and nurture FinTech startups by offering them a conducive environment for development, testing, and scaling of financial products and services within India's first international financial services centre.

## Objective

- Promote innovation in financial technology and services
- Provide startups with access to GIFT City's world-class infrastructure
- Enable testing of financial products in a regulated sandbox environment
- Facilitate mentorship, networking, and collaboration with industry experts
- Support regulatory compliance and approvals for FinTech innovations
- Attract domestic and international FinTech ventures to operate from GIFT City


## Target Beneficiaries

**startups**, **FinTech startups**, **BFSI startups**

## Eligibility

Startups operating in the financial technology (FinTech), banking, financial services, and insurance (BFSI) sectors that are incorporated as private limited companies, LLPs, or partnerships and are working on innovative financial products, services, or processes. Preference may be given to entities seeking to leverage GIFT City's regulatory sandbox or international financial services platform.
