# Self-Certification for Labour Laws (Startups)

## Overview

Self-Certification for Labour Laws (Startups) is a regulatory simplification initiative under the Startup India initiative that allows DPIIT-recognised startups to self-certify compliance with specified labour and environmental laws, reducing the need for routine inspections and lowering compliance burden. Startups can self-certify through the Startup India portal or mobile app, with no inspections conducted for a specified period unless a credible complaint is filed. The scheme aims to foster innovation by enabling startups to focus on core business activities rather than regulatory formalities.

## Objective

- Reduce the regulatory burden on Startups thereby allowing them to focus on their core business and keep compliance cost low
- Make compliance for Startups friendly and flexible through simplifications in the regulatory regime
- Allow Startups to self-certify compliance with labour and environment laws through a simple online procedure
- Ensure inspections are conducted only on receipt of credible and verifiable complaints
- Promote ease of doing business for startups in India

## Benefits

Startups shall be allowed to self-certify compliance with 6 labour laws and 3 environmental laws through a simple online procedure. In the case of labour laws, no inspections will be conducted for a period of 5 years. Startups may be inspected only on receipt of a credible and verifiable complaint of violation, filed in writing and approved by at least one level senior to the inspecting officer.…

## Important Conditions

- Self-certification under labour laws is valid for a period of 5 years from recognition
- Inspections may be conducted only on receipt of credible and verifiable complaints
- Startups must be DPIIT recognised to avail this benefit
- The benefit applies only to startups falling under the 'white category' for environment laws as defined by CPCB
