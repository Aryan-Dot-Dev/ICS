# Self-Certification for Labour Laws (Startups) — Application


## Process

1. Register on the Startup India portal.
2. Log in to your dashboard.
3. Navigate to the 'Get Recognised' section.
4. Fill out the DPIIT recognition application form with business details.
5. Upload required documents including Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, and business description.
6. Submit the application for review.
7. Upon recognition, access the Shram Suvidha Portal of the Ministry of Labour and Employment.
8. Register at the Shram Suvidha Portal and log in.
9. Click the link 'Is Any of your Establishment a Startup'.
10. Follow the instructions to complete self-certification for labour laws.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round.
