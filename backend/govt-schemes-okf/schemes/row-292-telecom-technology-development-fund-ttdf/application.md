## Process

1. Submit a detailed project proposal outlining objectives, methodology, timeline, and budget.
2. Include technical specifications, expected outcomes, and plan for technology transfer or commercialization.
3. Provide details of the applicant organization, including registration, technical team, and past experience.
4. Attach supporting documents such as certificates of incorporation, audited financial statements, and letters of collaboration (if applicable).
5. Submit the application through the designated portal or as specified in the official notification.
6. Await evaluation by the expert committee constituted by the Department of Telecommunications.
7. If approved, sign the grant agreement and commence project execution.
8. Submit periodic progress reports and utilization certificates as per scheme guidelines.
