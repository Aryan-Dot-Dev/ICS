# Telecom Technology Development Fund (TTDF)

## Overview

The Telecom Technology Development Fund (TTDF) is a scheme under the Department of Telecommunications, Ministry of Communications, Government of India, aimed at promoting research, development, and innovation in telecom technologies. It supports indigenous development of telecom equipment and solutions to reduce import dependence and strengthen India's position in global telecom standards. The fund encourages collaboration between industry, academia, and research institutions to advance 5G, 6G, and other emerging telecom technologies.

## Objective

- Promote indigenous research and development in telecom technologies
- Support innovation in 5G, 6G, and future telecom standards
- Reduce import dependence on telecom equipment through domestic manufacturing
- Strengthen India's participation in global telecom standard-setting bodies
- Encourage industry-academia collaboration for technology development
- Provide financial support for proof-of-concept, prototyping, and field trials
- Facilitate technology transfer and commercialization of telecom innovations
- Enhance competitiveness of Indian telecom manufacturers in global markets
