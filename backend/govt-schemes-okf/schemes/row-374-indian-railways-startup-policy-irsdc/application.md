## Process

1. Visit the IRSDC portal at https://irsdc.gov.in
2. Register as a startup on the portal
3. Submit a detailed proposal outlining the innovation, its relevance to Indian Railways, and implementation plan
4. Proposal undergoes initial screening by IRSDC
5. Shortlisted startups are invited for presentation and evaluation
6. Selected startups enter the pilot phase with relevant railway units
7. Performance is monitored during the pilot
8. Based on outcomes, further collaboration or scaling is considered.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year.
