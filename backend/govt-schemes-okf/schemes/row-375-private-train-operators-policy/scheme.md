# Private Train Operators Policy

## Overview

The Private Train Operators Policy is a scheme under the Ministry of Railways that allows private entities to operate passenger trains on Indian Railways network. It aims to introduce competition, improve service quality, and enhance passenger experience through private sector participation in train operations. The policy enables private operators to run trains under specified terms and conditions, contributing to modernization and efficiency in rail services.

## Objective

- Introduce private sector participation in passenger train operations
- Enhance service quality and passenger experience
- Increase competition and efficiency in rail services
- Modernize rolling stock and infrastructure through private investment
- Expand train connectivity and frequency on existing routes
- Generate employment and stimulate economic activity
- Leverage private expertise for innovation in rail services
- Optimize utilization of railway infrastructure


## Target Beneficiaries

**private train operators**, **railway passengers**

## Eligibility

Private entities meeting the criteria set by the Ministry of Railways, including financial capability, technical expertise, and compliance with safety and operational standards, are eligible to apply for operating private trains under the policy. Specific eligibility conditions are defined in the policy documents and tender notices issued by the Railway Board.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Private train operators benefit from the opportunity to operate trains on Indian Railways network, access to railway infrastructure, potential for revenue generation, and contribution to improved passenger services. Passengers benefit from enhanced service quality, modern amenities, increased train frequency, and competitive fares resulting from private participation.

## Important Conditions

- Operations are subject to the terms and conditions of the concession agreement
- Private operators must comply with all safety, operational, and regulatory standards of Indian Railways
- The policy does not guarantee exclusivity on routes
- Charges for use of railway infrastructure and services apply as per agreement
- Performance is subject to periodic review and penalties for non-compliance
