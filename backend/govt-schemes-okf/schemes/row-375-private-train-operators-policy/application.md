## Process

1. Monitor tender notices issued by the Railway Board for private train operations.
2. Check eligibility criteria and requirements specified in the tender documents.
3. Prepare and submit the bid as per the instructions in the tender notice.
4. Submit required documents including financial statements, technical proposals, and compliance certificates.
5. Await evaluation and selection process by the Railway Board.
6. Sign the concession agreement upon selection.
7. Fulfill pre-operational requirements such as rolling stock procurement and safety certifications.
8. Commence operations as per the agreed schedule and terms.
