# Social Alpha Innovation Platform

## Overview

Social Alpha is a full-stack lab-to-market platform that supports science- and technology-led innovations addressing critical social, economic, and environmental challenges. It enables mission-driven entrepreneurs to de-risk, deploy, and scale deep-tech innovations across sectors such as energy, health, agriculture, and climate through incubation, seed funding, mentorship, market access, and deployment partnerships. Since 2016, Social Alpha has supported 450+ startups, awarded 200+ catalytic grants, made 100+ seed investments, and unlocked 150 million+ in follow-on capital, with impact reaching 3.5 million+ people across 400+ districts in 28 states and union territories.

## Objective

- De-risk, deploy, and scale deep-tech innovations across energy, resources, materials, food systems, and health
- Support mission-driven entrepreneurs from lab to market through a full-stack architecture
- Unlock capital from philanthropy, venture capital, and strategic investors for social innovations
- Enable market adoption and real-world deployment of science and technology solutions
- Provide catalytic grants and seed investments to de-risk innovations at early stages
- Facilitate market access and procurement pathways for startups
- Build deployment partnerships for real-world scaling and follow-on investment
- Nurture entrepreneurs through structured programs like Techtonic, Entrepreneur-in-Residence, and incubation labs

## Important Conditions

- Preference is given to start-ups that have not received funding from any Government of India or State Govt.
- Indian Subsidiaries of MNCs/foreign companies are not eligible for NIDHI Seed Support Scheme
- Shareholding by Indian promoters must be at least 51%
- Applicants must declare they have not received NIDHI-seed support from any other DST-supported incubator
- Financial support under NIDHI-SSS shall not be used for repayment of loans, creation of personal assets, inter-corporate deposits, speculative purposes, or personal benefit of promoters
- Program-specific eligibility applies (e.g., SPIN requires space-tech focus, Techtonic requires sustainability/agri/health focus)
- Some programs (e.g., Techtonic: Innovations for Sustainability) have closed applications as of the latest update
