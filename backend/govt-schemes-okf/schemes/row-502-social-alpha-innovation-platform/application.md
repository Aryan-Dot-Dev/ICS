## Process

1. Visit the Social Alpha website and navigate to the relevant program page (e.g., Techtonic, NIDHI-SSS, EIR, or Namma Bengaluru Challenge).
2. Review eligibility criteria and program details.
3. Click on 'Apply Now' or 'View Details' to access the application form.
4. Fill in the application with details about your startup, including innovation focus, stage of development, team information, and financial needs.
5. Upload required documents such as Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, pitch deck, and declaration of no prior NIDHI-seed support from other DST incubators (if applicable).
6. Submit the application and await review by the respective selection committee (e.g., NIDHI-SSMC, SpIN Selection Committee, or Social Alpha portfolio team).
7. Shortlisted candidates may be invited for interviews, pitches, or due diligence.
8. Selected startups will receive formal communication and onboarding into the program.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted year-round for most programs (e.g., EIR, NIDHI-SSS). Specific programs like Techtonic and Namma Bengaluru Challenge have defined cycles (e.g., quarterly reviews).
