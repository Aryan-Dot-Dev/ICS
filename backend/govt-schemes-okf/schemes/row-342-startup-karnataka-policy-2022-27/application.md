## Process

1. Register on the Startup Karnataka portal (startup.karnataka.gov.in)
2. Create a profile and provide details about the startup
3. Apply for specific schemes under the policy such as innovation grant, patent reimbursement, or seed fund
4. Upload required documents including incorporation certificate, DPIIT recognition, project report, and bank details
5. Submit the application for review by the Karnataka Innovation and Technology Society (KATS)
6. Await evaluation and approval, post which funds are disbursed in stages based on milestones

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds
