# Startup Karnataka Policy 2022-27

## Overview

Startup Karnataka Policy 2022-27 is a state-level initiative by the Government of Karnataka to foster a vibrant startup ecosystem by providing financial support, infrastructure, mentorship, and regulatory facilitation to startups across various sectors. The policy aims to position Karnataka as a global innovation hub by supporting early-stage and growth-stage startups through grants, incubation, and access to funding networks.

## Objective

- To promote innovation and entrepreneurship across all sectors in Karnataka
- To provide financial assistance and infrastructure support to startups
- To encourage participation of underrepresented groups including women, SC/ST, and differently abled entrepreneurs
- To strengthen incubation and mentorship networks across the state
- To facilitate market access and industry linkages for startups
- To create a conducive regulatory environment for startup growth
- To establish Karnataka as a preferred destination for domestic and global investment in startups

**startups**, **women entrepreneurs**, **SC/ST entrepreneurs**, **differently abled entrepreneurs**, **rural startups**, **agri-startups**

## Key Features

- Fund size: ₹200 crore
- Grant/assistance per entity: Up to INR 50 lakhs per startup for product development and innovation support
- Geographic scope: Karnataka only (state-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds
- Portal: https://startup.karnataka.gov.in
- Source data last updated: 2022
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

Startups incorporated in Karnataka as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm under the Companies Act, 2013 or LLP Act, 2008, with operations primarily in Karnataka, working on innovative products, services, or processes, and recognized by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) or recommended by a recognized incubator in Karnataka.

## Important Conditions

- Financial support is subject to availability of funds under the policy
- Startups must maintain primary operations in Karnataka to avail benefits
- Funds disbursed under grants must be utilized for the approved purpose only
- Benefits under the policy are subject to audit and may be recovered if misused
- Startups receiving support must submit periodic progress reports and utilization certificates
