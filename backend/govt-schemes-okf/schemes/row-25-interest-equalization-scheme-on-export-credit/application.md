## Process

1. Exporter avails pre- or post-shipment rupee export credit from a bank in India.
2. Bank charges interest on the export credit as per prevailing rates.
3. Bank claims reimbursement of interest equalization from the Reserve Bank of India at the rate of 3% per annum (or 5% for MSME exporters) on the outstanding export credit.
4. RBI reimburses the claimed amount to the bank.
5. Bank passes on the benefit to the exporter by reducing the effective interest rate on the export credit.

## Deadlines

- Claims can be submitted by banks to the RBI on a quarterly basis within the stipulated time frame after the end of each quarter. There is no fixed deadline for exporters to avail the benefit, as it is passed on by banks upon disbursement of export credit.
