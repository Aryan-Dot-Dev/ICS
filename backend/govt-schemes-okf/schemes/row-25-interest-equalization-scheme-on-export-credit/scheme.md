# Interest Equalization Scheme on Export Credit

## Overview

The Interest Equalization Scheme on Export Credit is a scheme administered by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) to provide interest equalization support on pre- and post-shipment rupee export credit extended by banks to exporters. The scheme aims to enhance the competitiveness of Indian exports by reducing the effective cost of credit for exporters. It is administered by the RBI under the authority of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, and is part of the RBI's developmental and regulatory policies to support export promotion.

## Objective

- To enhance the competitiveness of Indian exports in the global market
- To reduce the effective cost of rupee export credit for exporters
- To support MSME exporters in accessing affordable credit
- To promote exports in sectors such as manufacturing, agriculture, and services
- To align with the Foreign Trade Policy of India for export promotion
- To provide interest equalization support on pre- and post-shipment rupee export credit

## Important Conditions

- The benefit is available only on rupee export credit, not on foreign currency credit
- The benefit is available only for exports under the Foreign Trade Policy of India
- MSME exporters must possess a valid MSME registration to avail the additional 2% benefit
- The benefit is available only on the outstanding amount of export credit and not on overdue amounts
- Banks must submit claims to the RBI within the stipulated quarterly timeframe
- The benefit is not available on deferred credit or usance bills beyond the usance period
