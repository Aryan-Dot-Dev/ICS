# NHAI InvIT – Infrastructure Investment Trust for Highways

## Overview

NHAI InvIT – Infrastructure Investment Trust for Highways is a financial instrument established by the National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) to monetize its operational highway assets through the issuance of units to investors. It enables NHAI to raise long-term capital by transferring revenue-generating road projects into a trust structure, thereby unlocking value from its infrastructure portfolio. The InvIT was listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange and National Stock Exchange on 28 October 2022, with bonds issued at a 7.9% semi-annual coupon, yielding 8.05% annually, and a minimum investment of ₹10,000 for retail investors.

## Objective

- Monetize operational highway assets of NHAI through infrastructure investment trust structure
- Raise long-term capital for NHAI by leveraging revenue-generating road projects
- Provide retail and institutional investors with an opportunity to invest in highway infrastructure
- Enhance liquidity and unlock value from NHAI’s asset base
- Support NHAI’s asset recycling model under the National Monetisation Pipeline
- Ensure regular income distribution to unit holders through toll and annuity revenues
- Promote private participation in highway infrastructure via SEBI-regulated InvIT framework
- Strengthen NHAI’s financial position for future road development projects

## Eligibility

The NHAI InvIT is open to all eligible investors including retail investors, institutional investors, pension funds, insurance companies, and mutual funds who comply with SEBI (Infrastructure Investment Trusts) Regulations, 2014. There are no restrictions based on entity type, geography, or investor category, subject to KYC and AML norms as per applicable securities laws.

- Unit price is subject to market fluctuations and liquidity conditions on the stock exchange
- The InvIT structure is governed by SEBI (Infrastructure Investment Trusts) Regulations, 2014, and any regulatory changes may affect operations
- Early redemption or buyback of units is subject to trust deed provisions and SEBI guidelines
