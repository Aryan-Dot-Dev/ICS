## Process

1. Investors must have a demat and trading account with a registered stockbroker.
2. Units of NHAI InvIT can be purchased through the stock exchange (BSE/NSE) during market hours using the scrip code.
3. For anchor allocation in the IPO, eligible institutional investors submitted bids through Book Running Lead Managers as per the offer document.
4. Post-listing, units are traded freely on the exchange like any other equity security.
5. Income distributions are made periodically to unit holders based on cash flows from the underlying highway projects.
