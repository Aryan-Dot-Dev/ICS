# North East Venture Fund (NEVF)

## Overview

The North East Venture Fund (NEVF) is a scheme managed by SIDBI to support startups and early-stage enterprises in the North Eastern region of India. It aims to promote entrepreneurship and innovation by providing financial assistance through venture capital investments. The fund focuses on high-growth potential startups in sectors such as technology, manufacturing, and services. NEVF operates as a Fund of Funds, investing in SEBI-registered Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs) that in turn invest in eligible startups.

## Objective

- Promote entrepreneurship and innovation in the North Eastern region
- Provide financial assistance to high-potential startups through venture capital
- Support technology-driven and innovation-led enterprises
- Strengthen the startup ecosystem in underserved regions
- Facilitate access to capital for early-stage ventures
- Encourage private sector participation via Fund of Funds structure
- Generate employment and economic growth in the North East
- Enable startups to scale and achieve market readiness

## Eligibility

Startups and early-stage enterprises based in the North Eastern region of India with innovative business models, high growth potential, and compliance with SIDBI's investment criteria. Enterprises must be registered as private limited companies, LLPs, or partnership firms and demonstrate scalability and market readiness.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Access to venture capital funding without immediate equity dilution in some cases, mentorship and strategic guidance from fund managers, networking opportunities with investors and industry experts, validation of business model through institutional investment, and potential follow-on funding from other sources.
