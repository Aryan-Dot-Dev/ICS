# Mukhya Mantri Antyodaya Parivar Utthan Yojana (MMAPUY)

## Overview

Mukhya Mantri Antyodaya Parivar Utthan Yojana (MMAPUY) is an umbrella mission launched in July 2021 by the Government of Haryana to identify economically disadvantaged families and provide a tailored package of welfare measures from various schemes to uplift them. The scheme aims to eradicate poverty by raising income levels through targeted interventions like skill development, job placements, financial aid, and entrepreneurial support. It seeks to ensure sustainable livelihoods and a dignified quality of life for underprivileged families, with an initial economic threshold of Rs. 1 lakh per annum and a subsequent target of Rs. 1.80 lakh per annum.

## Objective

- Eradicate poverty by raising income levels of economically disadvantaged families in Haryana
- Provide sustainable livelihoods through skill development, job placements, financial aid, and entrepreneurial support
- Ensure a dignified quality of life for underprivileged families
- Foster equitable growth and reduce income disparities across the state
- Identify poorest of the poor families and tailor a package of measures from various welfare schemes for each family
- Reach a minimum economic threshold of Rs. 1 lakh initially and Rs. 1.80 lakh per annum subsequently
