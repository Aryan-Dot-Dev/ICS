# Eklavya Model Residential Schools (EMRS)

## Overview

Eklavya Model Residential Schools (EMRS) is a central sector scheme aimed at providing quality education to Scheduled Tribe (ST) students in remote areas through the establishment and management of residential schools from Class VI to XII. The scheme focuses on holistic development, including academic excellence, preservation of tribal culture and heritage, and overall personality development of tribal children. EMRS schools are designed to be at par with Navodaya Vidyalayas in terms of infrastructure and academic standards.

## Objective

- To provide quality upper primary, secondary and senior secondary level education to ST students
- To ensure access to education in remote tribal areas
- To promote holistic development including academic, physical and cultural growth
- To preserve and promote tribal art, culture, language and heritage
- To bridge the educational gap between tribal and non-tribal populations
- To provide free education including lodging, boarding, uniforms, and textbooks
- To ensure student-teacher ratio conducive to effective learning
- To prepare students for higher education and professional careers

## Eligibility

The scheme targets Scheduled Tribe students studying in classes VI to XII. Admission is primarily based on merit and availability of seats, with priority given to students from Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTGs), displaced families, and those affected by left-wing extremism. Schools are set up in blocks with 50% or more ST population and at least 20,000 tribal persons.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Free education, lodging, boarding, school uniforms, textbooks, stationery, and medical care. Students receive holistic development through co-curricular activities, sports, life skills training, and exposure to tribal culture and traditions. The schools follow CBSE curriculum and aim for all-round development of tribal children.
