## Process

1. Identify a defence technology requirement aligned with TDF objectives.
2. Prepare a project proposal detailing innovation, objectives, budget, and timeline.
3. Submit the proposal through the designated DRDO channel.
4. Proposal undergoes technical and financial evaluation by DRDO experts.
5. Upon approval, enter into a formal agreement with DRDO.
6. Receive funds in tranches based on project milestones.
7. Submit periodic progress reports and utilization certificates.
8. Complete project and submit final report and financial statement.
