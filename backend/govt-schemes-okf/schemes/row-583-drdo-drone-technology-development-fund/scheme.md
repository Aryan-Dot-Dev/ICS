# DRDO – Drone Technology Development Fund

## Overview

The DRDO – Drone Technology Development Fund, referred to as the Technology Development Fund (TDF) Scheme, supports the development of defence and dual-use systems, subsystems, components, or technologies for products that are Indian Designed, Developed and Manufactured (IDDM). It aims to promote Micro, Small, Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) including start-ups to develop innovative products for the armed forces under the 'Make in India' initiative. The scheme is implemented by DRDO to foster indigenous technological capabilities and self-reliance in defence technologies.

## Objective

- Promote development of defence and dual-use systems, subsystems, components, or technologies
- Support Indian Designed, Developed and Manufactured (IDDM) products
- Encourage innovation among MSMEs and start-ups for defence applications
- Strengthen indigenous defence technological capabilities
- Advance the 'Make in India' initiative in the defence sector

## Eligibility

Indian industries, specially MSMEs, including start-ups, are eligible for the Technology Development Fund (TDF) Scheme to undertake indigenisation of defence products, subsystems and components, or to develop new technologies as required by DRDO, Services and DPSUs.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial support for project development, access to DRDO's technological expertise, infrastructure, testing facilities, and support for technology transfer and commercialisation of defence technologies developed under the scheme.

## Important Conditions

- Funding is limited to 90% of project cost;
- beneficiary must contribute 10%
- Maximum project cost under TDF is capped at Rs 10 Cr
- Funds must be utilized strictly for the approved project objectives
- Beneficiary must comply with reporting and audit requirements
- Technology developed may have dual-use applications but must align with defence requirements
