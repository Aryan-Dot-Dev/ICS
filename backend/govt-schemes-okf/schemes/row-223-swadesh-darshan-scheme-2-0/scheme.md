# Swadesh Darshan Scheme 2.0

## Overview

Swadesh Darshan Scheme 2.0 is a centrally sponsored scheme launched by the Ministry of Tourism, Government of India, aimed at developing theme-based tourist circuits across the country. It focuses on integrated development of tourism infrastructure, promoting sustainable and responsible tourism, and enhancing visitor experience through planned interventions. The scheme supports the creation of world-class tourism destinations by improving connectivity, facilities, and services at identified circuits.

## Objective

- Develop theme-based tourist circuits in a planned and prioritized manner
- Enhance tourism infrastructure and visitor facilities at identified destinations
- Promote sustainable and responsible tourism practices
- Increase domestic and international tourist footfalls
- Generate employment opportunities through tourism-led economic growth
- Preserve and promote cultural and natural heritage
- Strengthen linkages between tourism and other sectors like handicrafts, local cuisine, and adventure sports

## Eligibility

State Governments and Union Territory Administrations are eligible to submit project proposals under the Swadesh Darshan Scheme 2.0. Projects must be aligned with the identified thematic circuits and adhere to the scheme guidelines, including norms for sustainable tourism, environmental clearances, and land availability.
