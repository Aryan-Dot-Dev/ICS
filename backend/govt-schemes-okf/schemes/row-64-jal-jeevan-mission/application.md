## Process

1. States/UTs must submit proposals through the Institutional Mechanism involving State Water and Sanitation Mission (SWSM) and District Water and Sanitation Mission (DWSM). Proposals are appraised and approved by the National Jal Jeevan Mission (NJJM) under the Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation. Funds are released in installments based on utilization certificates and progress reports. The process includes preparation of Detailed Project Reports (DPRs), community contribution planning, and convergence with other schemes. Implementation is monitored via the JJM-Management Information System (MIS).

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — proposals are accepted and processed throughout the year based on fund availability and approval cycles.
