# Nagaland Startup Policy – StartupNaga

## Overview

The Nagaland Startup Policy – StartupNaga is a state-level initiative aimed at fostering innovation and entrepreneurship in Nagaland. It seeks to create a supportive ecosystem for startups by providing financial, infrastructural, and mentorship support. The policy targets early-stage ventures and aims to promote sustainable economic growth through innovation-driven enterprises.

## Objective

- To promote innovation and entrepreneurship in Nagaland
- To provide financial and non-financial support to early-stage startups
- To create a conducive ecosystem for startup growth and scalability
- To encourage youth and women participation in entrepreneurial activities
- To facilitate access to mentorship, incubation, and market linkages
- To position Nagaland as a hub for innovative startups in the Northeast

## Eligibility

Startups registered in Nagaland with innovative ideas, scalable business models, and potential for job creation are eligible. Preference is given to ventures led by youth, women, and entrepreneurs from marginalized communities.
