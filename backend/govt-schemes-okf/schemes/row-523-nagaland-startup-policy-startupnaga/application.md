## Process

1. Visit the official Nagaland Startup Policy portal or designated nodal agency.
2. Register as a startup and create a profile.
3. Submit the application form with business plan, pitch deck, and incorporation documents.
4. Undergo screening and evaluation by the selection committee.
5. Present the idea before the panel for final approval.
6. Upon selection, sign the agreement and receive disbursement of funds.
