# NGO Darpan – CSR Fund Access Portal

## Overview

NGO Darpan – CSR Fund Access Portal is a digital platform operated by NITI Aayog that enables Non-Profit Organizations (NPOs) to obtain a unique Darpan ID through self-declared affidavit-based registration. The portal facilitates access to government grant applications by linking NPOs to official portals of various Ministries and Departments for submitting online grant applications. It does not issue physical certificates but provides a Darpan registration ID that allows NPOs to start applying for grants and exemptions after registration. The portal also includes important advisories regarding the prohibition of misuse of NITI Aayog's name, logo, or National Emblem.

## Objective

- Provide a unique Darpan ID to NPOs based on self-declared affidavit
- Enable NPOs to access online grant application portals of various Government Ministries and Departments
- Facilitate registration and document submission for NPOs seeking government grants and exemptions
- Maintain a directory of registered NPOs with status tracking (active, blacklisted)
- Prevent misuse of NITI Aayog's name, logo, or National Emblem by NPOs

## Eligibility

Any Non-Profit Organization (NPO) including Trusts, Societies, and Section 8 Companies can register by creating an account, filling in organizational details, uploading necessary documents, and submitting for review to receive a Darpan Unique ID based on self-declared affidavit.

## Important Conditions

- The Darpan ID generated upon registration does not confer any right, endorsement, affiliation, or recognition by NITI Aayog.
- NPOs are strictly prohibited from using the NITI Aayog name/logo or the National Emblem in any manner;
- unauthorized use may lead to blacklisting of Darpan ID and legal action.
- NITI Aayog does not issue any physical Darpan certificate;
- only the registration ID is provided.
