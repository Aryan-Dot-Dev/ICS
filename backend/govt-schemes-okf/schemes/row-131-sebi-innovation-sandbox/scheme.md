# SEBI Innovation Sandbox

## Overview

The SEBI Innovation Sandbox is a regulatory framework designed to allow fintech startups and innovators to test new products, services, or business models in a controlled environment under SEBI supervision. It aims to foster innovation in the securities market while ensuring investor protection and market integrity. Participants can experiment with relaxed regulatory requirements for a limited period, subject to oversight and reporting obligations.

## Objective

- Promote innovation in the securities market
- Enable testing of new financial products and services in a live environment
- Ensure investor protection during experimentation
- Provide regulatory flexibility for emerging technologies
- Gather insights to inform future policy and regulation
- Facilitate collaboration between regulators and innovators

## Eligibility

Entities must be incorporated in India, have a minimum net worth as specified by SEBI, and propose an innovative product, service, or business model related to securities markets. The innovation must address a genuine market need and include safeguards for investor protection and risk management.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Access to a regulated testing environment with relaxed compliance requirements, guidance from SEBI officials, potential pathway to full regulatory authorization, and enhanced credibility through SEBI association. Non-financial benefits include regulatory mentorship and market access opportunities.

## Important Conditions

- Participation is time-limited and subject to SEBI approval
- Relaxed regulations apply only to the approved scope of testing
- SEBI may terminate participation if risks to investors or market integrity are identified
- Successful testing does not guarantee future regulatory approval
- Participants bear all costs associated with the innovation and testing
