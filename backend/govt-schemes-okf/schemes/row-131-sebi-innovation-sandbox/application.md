## Process

1. Submit an application detailing the innovative product, service, or business model to be tested.
2. Include information on the technology, target users, risk mitigation measures, and investor protection mechanisms.
3. SEBI reviews the application for eligibility and potential risks.
4. If approved, enter into a sandbox agreement outlining the scope, duration, and conditions of testing.
5. Conduct the test under SEBI supervision with periodic reporting.
6. Submit a final report on outcomes and lessons learned.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year.
