## Process

1. Visit the KSUM website and navigate to the relevant scheme page.
2. Download guidelines and check eligibility.
3. Apply online during the call for applications via the KSUM portal.
4. Submit the application form with business profile and seed proposal.
5. Upload required documents (incorporation, PAN, KSUM Unique ID, bank details, stamp paper, etc.).
6. Application scrutiny by KSUM officials.
7. Shortlisting based on eligibility and proposal quality.
8. Pitch before the Expert Committee (varies by scheme).
9. Selection based on evaluation criteria (team, product innovation, marketing, financials, fund utilization plan).
10. Execute agreement with KSUM.
11. Fund disbursement in installments based on milestone completion and utilization certificate submission.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted year-round with periodic calls for proposals published through official channels.
