# Kerala Technology Startup Policy

## Overview

Kerala Technology Startup Policy is implemented through Kerala Startup Mission (KSUM), the nodal agency of the Government of Kerala for entrepreneurship development and incubation activities in Kerala, India. KSUM provides a comprehensive ecosystem of support including funding schemes, incubation, acceleration, market expansion, investor connections, and infrastructure to nurture startups across all stages of their journey. The policy aims to drive real change for startups by enabling access to markets, capital, partnerships, and growth opportunities, fostering a sustainable and inclusive startup ecosystem in the state.

## Objective

- Foster innovation and support entrepreneurs to drive sustainable economic growth
- Create a highly sustainable startup ecosystem under the aegis of the Govt. of Kerala
- Provide financial assistance through grants, loans, and reimbursements at various startup stages
- Enable access to markets, capital, partnerships, and growth opportunities
- Nurture creativity, experimentation, and entrepreneurial thinking among students via IEDC platforms
- Support women and transgender-led startups through inclusive funding and ecosystem programs
- Strengthen Kerala’s startup investment ecosystem via Fund of Funds and investor education initiatives
- Facilitate structured collaboration between corporates and startups to accelerate innovation adoption

**startups**, **women entrepreneurs**, **student innovators**, **technology-driven ventures**, **first-time founders**, **OBC/OEC graduates**, **transgender-led startups**

## Important Conditions

- Grants are not generally given for service startups and SMEs working in trade and commerce
- Startups must not have any other pending dues with government agencies, KSUM, or other incubators
- Founder salaries, purchase of fixed assets (land, building, high-end equipment, costly software above ₹2 lakhs), rent, and major infrastructure setup are not permitted under most grants
- KSUM Unique ID is mandatory for fund disbursement (though not always for application)
- Company incorporation and active MCA status are required before fund release
- Preference is given to startups that have not availed funding from any Government of India/State Govt.
- Shareholding by Indian promoters must be at least 51%
- Some grants (e.g., NIDHI Prayas) require physical product development;
- pure software projects are not eligible
- Women/transgender-focused schemes require ≥51% shareholding by women/transgender founders
