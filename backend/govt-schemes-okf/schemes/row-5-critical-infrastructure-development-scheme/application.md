## Process

1. Identify the industrial estate or area where the unit is to be set up or expanded.
2. Verify eligibility based on location in HSIIDC-developed area and block categorization (especially Category D for backward areas).
3. Submit application through the HSIIDC or Department of Industries & Commerce, Haryana.
4. Provide project details including land allotment, infrastructure requirements, and cost estimates.
5. Await verification and approval by the competent authority.
6. Upon approval, receive subsidy disbursement as per scheme guidelines after infrastructure development is completed and verified.
