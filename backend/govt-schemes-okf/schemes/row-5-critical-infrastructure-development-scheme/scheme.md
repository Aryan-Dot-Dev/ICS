# Critical Infrastructure Development Scheme

## Overview

The Critical Infrastructure Development Scheme under the Enterprises Promotion Policy 2015 aims to support the development of essential industrial infrastructure such as roads, water supply, drainage, and power supply in industrial areas to facilitate industrial growth and attract investment in Haryana. It is designed to improve the basic infrastructure in industrial estates and parks to make them investment-ready and support the establishment and expansion of industries.

## Objective

- Develop critical infrastructure in industrial areas
- Improve road connectivity, water supply, drainage, and power supply in industrial estates
- Facilitate balanced regional development by improving infrastructure in backward areas
- Attract investment by making industrial plots investment-ready
- Support the establishment and expansion of industries through infrastructure support
- Enhance ease of doing business by providing basic infrastructure facilities
- Promote industrial growth in Haryana through infrastructure development

## Eligibility

Industries and enterprises setting up or expanding in Haryana State Industrial and Infrastructure Development Corporation (HSIIDC) developed industrial estates, parks, and areas are eligible for the scheme. The scheme applies to units in industrially backward blocks (Category D) as per block categorization under HEEP 2020, where special incentives are provided to hasten industrial development.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

The scheme provides financial assistance for the development of critical infrastructure including internal roads, water supply, sewerage/drainage, and power supply in industrial areas. It supports the creation of investment-ready industrial plots by ensuring basic infrastructure is in place, thereby reducing the initial setup cost for industries and promoting industrial dispersal across the state.
