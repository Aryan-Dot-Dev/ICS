## Process

1. Identify the required service (e.g., standard formulation, NSF & FI approval, vegan logo, FSMP, import NOC, lab recognition, Food Analyst exam, FBO training, RTI, CPGRAMS grievance).
2. Visit the relevant portal or division as specified (e.g., FoSTaC portal for FBO training, FoSCoS for licensing, FSSAI website for RTI/CPGRAMS, labs@fssai.gov.in for lab recognition).
3. Prepare and submit the required application form (e.g., Form-I for NSF & FI, Form-A for lab recognition, Annexure for claims, Form for vegan logo, dossier for FSMP).
4. Attach required documents (e.g., scientific rationale, source of ingredients, certificate of analysis, NABL accreditation, educational qualifications, experience proof, identity/address proof).
5. Pay applicable fees via FSSAI ePayment portal or other specified modes (e.g., https://epay.fssai.gov.in/).
6. Submit application via email, online portal, or physical submission as directed (e.g., labs@fssai.gov.in for lab recognition, online for Food Analyst exam).
7. Await processing and approval as per indicated timeframes (e.g., 04-06 months for NSF & FI, 06-09 months for claims, 04-06 months for vegan logo, 09-12 months for FSMP, 2-10 days for import NOC/NCR, 01-09 months for lab recognition, 01 year for Food Analyst certification).
8. Receive approval, certificate, notification, or service delivery upon successful completion.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round.
