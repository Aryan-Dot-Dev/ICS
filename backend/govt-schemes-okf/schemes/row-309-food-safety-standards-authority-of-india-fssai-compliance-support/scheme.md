# Food Safety & Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) Compliance Support

## Overview

The Food Safety & Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) Compliance Support scheme provides guidance and assistance to food businesses for adhering to food safety regulations, including licensing, registration, standards formulation, and compliance with fortification and labeling requirements. It aims to ensure availability of safe and wholesome food through regulatory oversight, capacity building, and stakeholder engagement. The scheme supports food business operators, regulators, and consumers via training, advisories, and digital platforms like FoSCoS and Food Safety Connect.

## Objective

- Establish science-based standards for articles of food
- Regulate manufacture, storage, distribution, sale, and import of food
- Ensure availability of safe and wholesome food for human consumption
- Promote coordination of work on food standards with international bodies
- Build capacity of food safety officials and food business operators through training
- Provide digital platforms for licensing, compliance, and grievance redressal
- Support fortification of staple foods to address micronutrient deficiencies
- Enable consumers to report misleading claims and food safety concerns

## Important Conditions

- No upper age limit for appearing in Food Analyst Examination
- Experience gained before or after the period from graduation to closing date of application, and during academic programmes (e.g., Post Graduate, Doctoral), will not be considered for Food Analyst eligibility
- Candidates must select applicable discipline (Chemical or Biological) while filling application form for Computer Based Test (CBT) and Practical Examination for Food Analyst, and cannot change it till completion of examination
- Only those candidates who qualify the practical examination and fulfill all prescribed eligibility criteria will be eligible to undergo the mandatory two-week Food Analyst Training
- All training-related expenses for Food Analyst Training shall be borne by FSSAI
- Candidates who successfully complete Food Analyst Training shall be issued Food Analyst Certificates by hand at FSSAI Training Centres
- No fees for trainings conducted by Staff Training Unit for FSSAI Officials
- Residential trainings at NPC, ISTM, FOIR, etc., are nominated via email at training@fssai.gov.in
- The standard fee for filing an RTI application is ₹10;
- applicants below poverty line (BPL) are exempt upon providing BPL certificate
- CPGRAMS portal is accessible 24/7 and connected to all Ministries and Departments of Government of India and States
- Time taken in redressal of public grievances received through CPGRAMS portal is 21 days
