## Process

1. Identify the appropriate NCLT bench based on geographical jurisdiction.
2. File the relevant petition or application (e.g., Company Petition, Company Appeal, Insolvency Petition) under the applicable provision of Companies Act, 2013 or Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code,
3. 3. Submit the application along with required documents at the NCLT registry or through e-filing portal.
4. Pay the prescribed court fees.
5. Serve notice to the opposite party as per rules.
6. Attend hearings as scheduled by the Tribunal.
7. Await judgment or order from the NCLT bench.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications can be filed throughout the year as per cause of action arising.
