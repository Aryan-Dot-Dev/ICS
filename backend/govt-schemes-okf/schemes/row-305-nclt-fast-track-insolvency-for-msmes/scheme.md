# NCLT Fast Track Insolvency for MSMEs

## Overview

The National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) is a quasi-judicial body constituted under section 408 of the Companies Act, 2013, effective from 1st June 2016, to adjudicate company law and insolvency matters. It functions as the Adjudicating Authority under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), 2016, and aims to streamline corporate dispute resolution by consolidating functions of erstwhile bodies like CLB, BIFR, and AAIFR. NCLT operates through multiple benches across India, handling cases related to oppression, mismanagement, amalgamations, winding up, and Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP).

## Objective

- To provide a unified forum for adjudication of disputes under Companies Act and Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code
- To promote corporate governance and legal compliance while safeguarding stakeholder interests
- To facilitate revival and rehabilitation of financially distressed companies through timely insolvency resolution
- To maximize asset value and promote entrepreneurship and availability of credit
- To balance stakeholder interests including alteration in government dues payment priority
- To establish Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India and handle connected matters
- To reduce NPAs substantially as an ancillary benefit of IBC implementation
- To resolve insolvency of individual debtors (personal guarantors) and restore their financial viability

## Important Conditions

- Jurisdiction is bench-specific;
- cases must be filed before the bench having territorial and subject-matter jurisdiction
- Certain procedural defects (e.g., incorrect case type, missing documents, improper indexing, lack of OCR, unsigned pages) may lead to objections and rejection
- During office relocation, helpdesk numbers may be temporarily non-functional
- NCLT does not provide financial assistance;
- outcomes depend on judicial orders
- Parties must comply with timelines prescribed under IBC for insolvency proceedings
