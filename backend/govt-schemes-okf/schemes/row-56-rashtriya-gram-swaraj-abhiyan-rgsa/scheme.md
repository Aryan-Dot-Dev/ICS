# Rashtriya Gram Swaraj Abhiyan (RGSA)

## Overview

Rashtriya Gram Swaraj Abhiyan (RGSA) is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme approved on 13.04.2022 for implementation from 01.04.2022 to 31.03.2026, co-terminus with the XV Finance Commission period. The scheme focuses on re-imagining Panchayati Raj Institutions as vibrant centers of local self-governance and economic growth, with special emphasis on localization of Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) at the grassroots level through a thematic approach and collaborative efforts of Central Ministries and State line departments. It ensures basic orientation training for Elected Representatives (ERs) of Panchayats within 6 months of election and refresher training within 2 years, with a funding pattern of 60:40 (Central:State) for general states, 90:10 for NE Hilly States and UT of J&K, and 100% Central share for other UTs.

## Objective

- Re-imagine Panchayati Raj Institutions as vibrant centers of local self-governance and economic growth
- Focus on localization of Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) at the grassroots level
- Adopt a thematic approach through concerted and collaborative efforts of Central Ministries and State line departments
- Ensure basic orientation training for Elected Representatives (ERs) of Panchayats within 6 months of election
- Provide refresher training for ERs within 2 years
- Strengthen Panchayati Raj Institutions through capacity building and training
- Promote local self-governance and grassroots democracy
- Support economic development at the village level through empowered PRIs


## Target Beneficiaries

## Important Conditions

- Funding is contingent upon submission and approval of Annual Action Plans (AAPs)
- Release of subsequent installments is subject to utilization of previous funds and submission of Utilization Certificates
- The scheme is co-terminus with the XV Finance Commission period (up to 31.03.2026)
- NE Hilly States and UT of J & K have a different funding ratio (90:10 Central:State)
- Other UTs receive 100% Central share
- Training must include basic orientation within 6 months of election and refresher within 2 years
