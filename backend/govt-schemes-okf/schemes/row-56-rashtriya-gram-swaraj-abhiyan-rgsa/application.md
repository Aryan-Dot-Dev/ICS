## Process

1. States and Union Territories are required to submit Annual Action Plans (AAPs) through the MIS portal for approval. The process involves:
2. Preparation of State Annual Action Plan (AAP) by the State Government/UT Administration.
3. Submission of AAP via the MIS portal on the RGSA website.
4. Review and approval of AAP by the Ministry of Panchayati Raj.
5. Release of funds based on approved AAP and utilization certificates.
6. Implementation of activities at the district, block, and gram panchayat levels.
7. Monitoring and reporting of progress through the MIS portal.
8. Submission of Utilization Certificates and progress reports for subsequent fund releases.

## Deadlines

- Annual Action Plans (AAPs) are to be submitted for each financial year. The scheme is implemented from 01.04.2022 to 31.03.2026. Specific submission deadlines for AAPs are communicated annually by the Ministry of Panchayati Raj.
