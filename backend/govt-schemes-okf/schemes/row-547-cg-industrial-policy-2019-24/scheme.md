# CG Industrial Policy 2019-24

## Overview

The CG Industrial Policy 2019-24 is a state-level initiative by the Government of Chhattisgarh aimed at promoting industrial development, attracting investment, and supporting MSMEs and startups within the state. It outlines fiscal and non-fiscal incentives, infrastructure support, and sector-specific thrust areas to boost manufacturing and services. The policy emphasizes ease of doing business, skill development, and sustainable industrial growth across Chhattisgarh.

## Objective

- Promote industrial investment and economic growth in Chhattisgarh
- Support MSMEs and startups through incentives and infrastructure
- Enhance ease of doing business via single-window clearance
- Develop sector-specific industrial parks and clusters
- Generate employment and foster skill development
- Encourage sustainable and eco-friendly industrial practices

## Benefits

Benefits include fiscal incentives such as capital subsidy, interest subsidy, power tariff concession, VAT/GST reimbursement, and non-fiscal benefits like land allotment at concessional rates, single-window clearance, and infrastructure support. Specific entitlements vary by sector, investment size, and location (e.g., backward districts).
