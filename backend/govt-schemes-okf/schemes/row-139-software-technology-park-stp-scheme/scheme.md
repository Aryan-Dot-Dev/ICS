# Software Technology Park (STP) Scheme

## Overview

The Software Technology Park (STP) Scheme is a 100% export-oriented scheme for the development and export of computer software, including export of professional services using communication links or physical media. It integrates the government concept of 100% Export Oriented Units (EOU) and Export Processing Zones (EPZ) with the concept of Science Parks/Technology Parks. The scheme provides single-point contact services for member units to conduct export operations at a pace commensurate with international practices.

## Objective

- To promote the development and export of computer software
- To provide single-window clearance and statutory services to exporters
- To enable duty-free import of hardware and software for STP units
- To allow 100% foreign equity and free repatriation of capital and profits
- To support re-export of capital goods and donation of computers after two years
- To provide 100% depreciation on computers and peripherals over five years
- To permit sales in Domestic Tariff Area (DTA) with GST refund on capital goods
- To simplify export performance norms to 'Positive Net Foreign Exchange Earnings'

## Eligibility

Any entity engaged in the development and export of computer software, including professional services via communication links or physical media, can set up an STP unit anywhere in India. Jurisdictional STPI authorities can clear projects costing less than Rs.100 million with Indian Investment. 100% foreign equity is permitted.

## Important Conditions

- Units must maintain 100% export orientation for software and professional services
- Benefits like duty-free import and GST refund are contingent on compliance with export performance norms
- Donation of computers and peripherals is permitted only after two years of import
- 100% depreciation benefit is spread over five years
- Sales in Domestic Tariff Area (DTA) are permitted but capital goods purchased from DTA are eligible for GST refund only
- Re-export of capital goods is permitted under specified conditions
