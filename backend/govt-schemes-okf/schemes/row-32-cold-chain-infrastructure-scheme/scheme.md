# Cold Chain Infrastructure Scheme

## Overview

The Cold Chain Infrastructure Scheme is a component of the Pradhan Mantri Kisan SAMPADA Yojana (PMKSY) aimed at developing integrated cold chain and value addition infrastructure to reduce post-harvest losses, enhance processing levels, and boost exports of perishable products. It supports the setting up of multi-product food irradiation units and cold chain projects across India. The scheme provides financial assistance in the form of grants-in-aid for infrastructure development in the food processing sector.

## Objective

- Reduce post-harvest losses of horticultural and non-horticultural produce
- Enhance processing and value addition of agricultural products
- Provide seamless cold chain and preservation infrastructure from farm gate to consumer
- Boost exports of processed and perishable food products
- Create modern infrastructure with efficient supply chain management
- Generate employment opportunities in rural areas
- Increase farmers' income through better returns and reduced wastage

## Important Conditions

- Land on registered leasehold must have a lease duration of not less than 15 years
- Surety Bond is not required for Central/State Government Departments
- Original Surety Bond must be submitted to the Ministry through speed post
- Blacklisting/debarring may occur for misuse of grant-in-aid (e.g., M/s Paras Spices, M/s Vision Fresh and Frozen)
