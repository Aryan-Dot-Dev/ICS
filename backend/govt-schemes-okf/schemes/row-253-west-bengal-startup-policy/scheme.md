# West Bengal Startup Policy

## Overview

The West Bengal Startup Policy is a state-level initiative aimed at fostering innovation and entrepreneurship within the state of West Bengal. It seeks to create a conducive ecosystem for startups by providing support in areas such as funding, mentorship, infrastructure, and regulatory ease. The policy focuses on promoting technology-driven and innovation-led enterprises across various sectors. It is implemented by the Department of MSME & Textiles, Government of West Bengal.

## Objective

- Promote innovation and entrepreneurship in West Bengal
- Provide financial and non-financial support to startups
- Create incubation and co-working infrastructure
- Facilitate access to funding and investment
- Simplify regulatory compliance for startups
- Encourage sector-specific innovation (e.g., IT, biotech, agri)
- Develop skilled manpower for startup ecosystem
- Establish West Bengal as a preferred startup destination

## Key Features

- Grant/assistance per entity: Up to INR 25 lakhs for seed funding; up to INR 2 lakhs for domestic patent and INR 10 lakhs for international patent reimbursement
- Geographic scope: West Bengal only (state-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year

- Startups must maintain operational presence in West Bengal to continue availing benefits
- Misrepresentation of facts may lead to rejection and blacklisting
- Certain benefits like seed funding may require matching contribution from the startup
