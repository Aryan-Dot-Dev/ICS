## Process

1. Visit the official MSME West Bengal website or designated startup portal.
2. Register as a startup by providing basic entity and promoter details.
3. Fill out the online application form under the West Bengal Startup Policy.
4. Upload required documents including Certificate of Incorporation, business plan, and proof of innovation.
5. Submit the application for review by the designated committee.
6. Upon approval, receive acknowledgment and further instructions for benefit disbursement.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year
