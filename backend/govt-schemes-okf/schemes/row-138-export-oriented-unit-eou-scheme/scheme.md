# Export Oriented Unit (EOU) Scheme

## Overview

The Export Oriented Unit (EOU) Scheme is a policy initiative under India's Foreign Trade Policy that allows units to undertake export-oriented production with benefits such as duty-free import of capital goods, raw materials, and consumables, as well as exemptions from central excise duty and service tax. The scheme aims to boost exports by providing fiscal incentives and simplifying procedural requirements for units committed to exporting their entire production. EOUs are permitted to sell up to 50% of their output in the Domestic Tariff Area (DTA) under certain conditions, subject to payment of applicable duties.

## Objective

- To promote export-oriented production and increase India's foreign exchange earnings
- To provide duty-free import of capital goods, raw materials, and consumables for export production
- To exempt EOUs from central excise duty and service tax on goods and services used in the production process
- To allow EOUs to sell a limited portion of their output in the Domestic Tariff Area (DTA) under specified conditions
- To simplify procedural compliance and reduce transaction costs for export-oriented units
- To enhance competitiveness of Indian exports through fiscal incentives and streamlined regulations

## Important Conditions

- EOUs must achieve positive net foreign exchange earnings cumulatively over five years from commencement of production
- Sale in Domestic Tariff Area (DTA) is limited to 50% of annual export earnings or equivalent value, subject to payment of applicable duties
- Failure to meet export obligations may result in withdrawal of benefits and recovery of duties exempted
- Units must not engage in any activity listed in the negative list of exports under the Foreign Trade Policy
- Benefits are contingent upon adherence to the Legal Undertaking (LUT) or bond executed with customs authorities
