# Synd Mahila Shakti Scheme (Syndicate Bank)

## Overview

The Synd Mahila Shakti Scheme is a loan product offered by Syndicate Bank (now merged with Canara Bank) specifically designed to support women entrepreneurs. It provides financial assistance to women for starting or expanding business ventures, with a focus on promoting women's economic empowerment and entrepreneurship. The scheme is part of the bank's priority sector lending initiatives aimed at fostering inclusive growth.

## Objective

- To promote entrepreneurship among women
- To provide financial assistance for setting up and expanding women-owned businesses
- To enhance economic empowerment and self-reliance of women
- To support women-led micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs)
- To encourage women's participation in economic development

## Eligibility

The scheme is available to women entrepreneurs who are Indian citizens and wish to start or expand a business venture. Applicants must be sole proprietors, partners in a partnership firm, or directors of a private limited company where women hold majority stake. The business activity should be viable and fall under permissible sectors as per bank guidelines.

Financial support for business setup, expansion, or working capital needs; concessional interest rates compared to standard loans; simplified documentation process; quick loan approval and disbursal; no collateral requirement for loans up to a certain limit (as per bank's policy); and support for various business activities including manufacturing, services, and trading.
