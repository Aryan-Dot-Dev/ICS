# Medical Devices Park Scheme

## Overview

The Medical Devices Park Scheme aims to provide world-class infrastructure and common facilities for the manufacturing of medical devices in India. It seeks to reduce import dependence, promote domestic manufacturing, and create an ecosystem for innovation and export in the medical devices sector. The scheme supports the development of medical device parks with plug-and-play facilities, testing labs, and other shared infrastructure to attract investment and boost the industry.

## Objective

- To provide world-class infrastructure and common facilities for medical device manufacturing
- To reduce import dependence on medical devices and promote domestic manufacturing
- To create an ecosystem that supports innovation, research, and development in medical devices
- To enhance export competitiveness of Indian medical devices
- To attract investment in the medical devices sector through plug-and-play facilities
- To generate employment and support the growth of MSMEs and startups in the healthcare domain

## Eligibility

Eligible entities include companies, consortiums, or special purpose vehicles (SPVs) registered under the Companies Act, 2013 or any other relevant law, engaged in or planning to engage in the manufacturing of medical devices. The implementing agency will evaluate proposals based on technical feasibility, financial viability, and potential for employment generation and export promotion.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial support in the form of grant-in-aid for creation of common infrastructure facilities such as roads, water supply, power distribution, effluent treatment, and testing labs. Non-financial benefits include facilitation of clearances, single-window approval, and access to shared testing and certification facilities. The scheme also provides plug-and-play infrastructure to reduce setup time…

## Important Conditions

- The grant is limited to common infrastructure facilities only;
- individual factory sheds or plant machinery are not covered
- Beneficiaries must ensure that the park is used exclusively for medical device manufacturing activities
- Failure to utilize the grant for the approved purpose may lead to recovery of funds with interest
- The scheme is subject to availability of budgetary allocations and may be revised or discontinued by the government
