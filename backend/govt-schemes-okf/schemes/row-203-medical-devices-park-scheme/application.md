## Process

1. Submit a detailed project report (DPR) along with the application form to the implementing agency.
2. The DPR must include details on land availability, project layout, proposed facilities, financial plan, and employment generation.
3. Applications are evaluated by a technical committee appointed by the Department of Pharmaceuticals Department of Pharmaceuticals.
4. Shortlisted proposals undergo site verification and due diligence.
5. Upon approval, a formal sanction letter is issued.
6. Funds are released in stages based on milestone completion verified by the agency.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds.
