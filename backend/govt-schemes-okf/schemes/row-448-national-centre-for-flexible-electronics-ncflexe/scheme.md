# National Centre for Flexible Electronics (NCFlexE)

## Overview

The National Centre for Flexible Electronics (NCFlexE) is a specialized research and development centre established at IIT Kanpur to advance flexible electronics technology in India. It focuses on innovation, prototyping, and commercialization of flexible electronic systems, supporting startups, MSMEs, and academic researchers in the electronics manufacturing sector. The centre provides infrastructure, technical expertise, and collaborative platforms to foster indigenous development and reduce import dependence in flexible electronics.

## Objective

- Develop indigenous capabilities in flexible electronics design and manufacturing
- Provide prototyping and testing facilities for flexible electronic devices
- Support technology transfer and commercialization of research outcomes
- Foster collaboration between academia, industry, and startups
- Skill development and training in flexible electronics technologies
- Reduce import dependence through domestic innovation and production

## Eligibility

Startups, MSMEs, academic institutions, and research organizations working in the field of flexible electronics, wearable technology, bendable sensors, and related domains are eligible to collaborate with NCFlexE. Entities must demonstrate innovative potential and alignment with the centre’s technological focus areas.

## Benefits

Access to state-of-the-art flexible electronics fabrication and characterization facilities, technical mentorship from IIT Kanpur experts, collaborative R&D opportunities, prototyping support, testing and validation services, training programs, and assistance in scaling lab-scale innovations to pilot production.
