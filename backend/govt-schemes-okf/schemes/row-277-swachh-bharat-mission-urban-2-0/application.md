## Process

1. ULBs must maintain complete and accurate data on the Swachhatam Portal.
2. ULBs must update contact details of their representatives on the Swachhatam Portal.
3. ULBs must regularly submit monthly Management Information System (MIS) data.
4. For star rating certification, ULBs fill Common City Profile on SBM Urban Portal.
5. Fill MIS on SBM Urban Portal.
6. Check self-assessment scores on SBM Urban Portal.
7. Apply for Star Rating as per eligibility on SBM Urban Portal.
8. Upload documents & declarations.
9. Undergo Third Party Verification through Desktop Assessment and Field Assessment.
10. Receive Final Star Rating Certification (Valid for 1 Year).
11. For Swachh Shehar Jodi, mentor and mentee cities finalize pairs by 5th September 2025, conduct As-Is Joint Assessment & Action Plan by end of September 2025, sign MOUs by October 2025, implement Action Plan for 100 days, and assess progress during Swachh Survekshan 2025-26.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications for star rating and project proposals are accepted year-round. Specific timelines exist for initiatives like Swachh Shehar Jodi (announcement 17th July 2025, city pair finalization by 5th September 2025, action plan by end September 2025, MOU signing October 2025, 100-day implementation, assessment during Swachh Survekshan 2025-26).
