# Swachh Bharat Mission – Urban 2.0

## Overview

Swachh Bharat Mission – Urban 2.0 (SBM-U 2.0) is a flagship initiative of the Government of India launched on 1st October 2021 by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) with the vision of achieving 'Garbage Free' status for all cities by 2026. The mission focuses on 100% scientific solid waste management, sustained Open Defecation Free (ODF) status, and behavioral change through Jan Andolan. It aims to institutionalize 'swachh' behavior, contribute to SDG 2030, and drive urban transformation through infrastructure development, capacity building, digital innovations, and citizen engagement.

## Objective

- Achieve 'Garbage Free' status for all cities by 2026
- Ensure 100% scientific solid waste management
- Sustain Open Defecation Free (ODF) status and prevent slippage
- Institutionalize 'swachh' behavior through Jan Andolan
- Promote waste segregation, collection, transportation, and processing
- Remediate legacy dumpsites and manage construction & demolition waste
- Advance used water management and faecal sludge treatment
- Strengthen capacity building, skill development, and knowledge management
- Leverage digital innovations for e-governance and citizen engagement
- Foster healthy competition among cities through Swachh Survekshan

The Government of India in partnership with States/UTs and ULBs is committed to make all cities 'Garbage Free' under SBM-Urban 2.0. ## Important Conditions

- Star rating certification is valid for 1 year only
- ULBs must achieve minimum qualifying marks in each parameter for ODF++ certification (e.g., 200 aggregate marks with minimum qualifying marks per parameter)
- M4 and M8 (Mechanized cleaning of sewer/septic tanks and Sustainable financing mechanism) not applicable for ULBs with <30,000 population in ODF++ scoring
- ULBs formed after 31st Dec’24 require formal request to MoHUA for inclusion in assessment
- Additional penalties apply for mismatch between MIS claims and third-party field assessment
- Cities failing level 1 in any applicable ward-level component will not achieve star rating
- Fund utilization under Swachh Shehar Jodi restricted to soft and process-based interventions
- PPP projects require adherence to DBFOT model and payment security mechanisms
