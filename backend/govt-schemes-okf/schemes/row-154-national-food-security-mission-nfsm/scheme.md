# National Food Security Mission (NFSM)

## Overview

The National Food Security and Nutrition Mission (NFSNM) is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme launched in October 2007 to increase production of rice, wheat, pulses, coarse cereals, commercial crops, and nutri-cereals through area expansion, productivity enhancement, and sustainable practices. It aims to restore soil fertility, enhance farm-level economy, improve seed replacement rates, and strengthen seed sector infrastructure. The mission has been continued under Krishonnati Yojana till 31.03.2026 and comprises seven components: NFSNM-Rice, NFSNM-Wheat, NFSNM-Pulses, NFSNM-Coarse Cereals (Maize & Barley), NFSNM-Sub Mission on Nutri Cereals (Shree Anna), NFSNM-Commercial Crops, and NFSNM-Seed Components.

## Objective

- Increasing production of Rice, Wheat, Pulses, Coarse Cereals (Maize & Barley), Commercial Crops (Cotton, Jute & Sugarcane) and Nutri-Cereals (Shree Anna) through area expansion, adoption of different cropping patterns and productivity enhancement in a sustainable manner.
- Restoring soil fertility and productivity at farm level.
- Enhancing farm level economy to enhance farmer’s income.
- Enhancing post-harvest value addition at the farm gate for better price realization to farmers through efficient market linkages.
- Enhancing the Seed Replacement Rate (SRR) and Varietal Replacement Rate (VRR).
- Improving the infrastructure of the Seed Sector in the country.

## Eligibility

Farmers cultivating rice, wheat, pulses, coarse cereals (maize & barley), commercial crops (cotton, jute, sugarcane), and nutri-cereals (shree anna) in identified districts of the country are eligible for benefits under the mission. The mission prioritizes districts with low productivity yet high potential, especially focusing on food-grain crops cultivated in rain-fed regions.

## Important Conditions

- The mission is implemented through States/UTs, and States/UTs shall maintain designated scheme accounts through the Public Financial Management System (PFMS).
- The release of the Central Share to States shall be in accordance with prevailing guidelines of the Department of Expenditure, Ministry of Finance, Government of India.
- States/UTs should adopt the fund flow mechanism as communicated by the Government of India and ensure that the accounts thus maintained are audited annually by the Designated Authority/Agency of the State/UT.
