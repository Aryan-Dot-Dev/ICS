# Three-Year Tax Holiday for DPIIT Startups (80IAC)

## Overview

The Three-Year Tax Holiday for DPIIT Startups (80IAC) provides eligible startups with a 100% income tax exemption for three consecutive financial years out of their first ten years of incorporation. This benefit is granted under Section 80-IAC of the Income-tax Act, 1961, and requires certification from the Inter-Ministerial Board (IMB) set up by DPIIT. The scheme aims to reduce the tax burden on early-stage startups to encourage innovation and entrepreneurship.

## Objective

- Provide tax relief to eligible startups during their initial years of operation
- Encourage innovation and entrepreneurship by reducing financial burden
- Support job creation and economic growth through startup ecosystem development
- Promote long-term sustainability of startups by improving cash flow in early stages
- Align tax policy with the Startup India initiative to foster a conducive environment for new ventures

## Benefits

100% deduction of profits and gains from eligible business for three consecutive assessment years out of ten years beginning from the year of incorporation. This results in complete income tax exemption for the selected three years. The benefit is available only after obtaining certification from the Inter-Ministerial Board (IMB) established by DPIIT.

## Important Conditions

- The benefit requires separate certification from the Inter-Ministerial Board (IMB);
- DPIIT recognition alone is not sufficient.
- The three-year tax holiday must be claimed within the first ten years from incorporation.
- If the startup's turnover exceeds INR 100 crore in any financial year, it loses eligibility for future tax benefits under this section.
- The deduction cannot be claimed for more than three consecutive assessment years.
- The scheme applies only to eligible businesses as defined under section 80-IAC;
- not all startup activities qualify.
