## Process

1. Obtain DPIIT recognition for the startup via the Startup India portal.
2. Apply for certification from the Inter-Ministerial Board (IMB) through Form 1 of the Income-tax Rules,
3. 3. Submit the application along with required documents such as Certificate of Incorporation, details of business, PAN, and audited financial statements.
4. Upon IMB approval, claim the deduction under section 80-IAC while filing the income tax return for the relevant assessment years.

## Deadlines

- Applications for IMB certification can be made at any time; there is no fixed deadline. However, the tax benefit must be claimed in the income tax return for the relevant assessment year within the time allowed under section 139(1) of the Income-tax Act.
