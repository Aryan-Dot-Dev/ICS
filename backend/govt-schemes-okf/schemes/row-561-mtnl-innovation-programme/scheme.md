# MTNL Innovation Programme

## Overview

The MTNL Innovation Programme is a scheme under the PSU Support category aimed at fostering innovation within Mahanagar Telephone Nigam Limited (MTNL), a Government of India enterprise. The programme encourages innovation among employees and stakeholders through initiatives such as innovation challenges, idea submissions, and recognition of innovative projects. It aligns with MTNL's vision to become a total solution provider and global telecom company by promoting innovation in telecom and IT-related services.

## Objective

- Foster a culture of innovation within MTNL and its subsidiaries
- Encourage employees to submit innovative ideas for telecom and IT services
- Recognize and reward innovative contributions by employees and stakeholders
- Support innovation in telecom services, IT services, and customer service delivery
- Promote innovation in areas such as network optimization, customer service, and digital services
- Encourage participation from employees across departments and levels
- Recognize innovations that improve service quality, reduce costs, or enhance customer experience
- Promote innovation in sustainability and green telecom initiatives

## Eligibility

Open to serving and retired employees of MTNL, employees of MTNL subsidiaries (such as MSITS Ltd.), officers on deputation to MTNL or its subsidiaries, and individuals associated with MTNL through deputation or contractual arrangements, subject to internal guidelines and approval by the Competent Authority.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Recognition through certificates, certificates of appreciation, certificates of merit, and potential monetary rewards or awards as per the Innovation Programme guidelines; opportunities to present innovations at MTNL forums; possible implementation of innovative ideas within MTNL operations; recognition in MTNL communications and internal platforms.

## Important Conditions

- Participation is subject to internal guidelines and approval by the Competent Authority (CMD, MTNL)
- Innovations must be relevant to MTNL's business and service areas
- Financial support, if any, is subject to availability of funds and approval
- Applications must be submitted through proper channel and by the specified deadline
- Innovations must not violate any existing policies, policies, or intellectual property rights
- MTNL reserves the right to modify, cancel, or amend the Innovation Programme guidelines
