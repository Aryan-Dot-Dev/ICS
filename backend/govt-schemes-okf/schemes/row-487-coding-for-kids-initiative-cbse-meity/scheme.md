# Coding for Kids Initiative – CBSE & MeitY

## Overview

The Coding for Kids Initiative – CBSE & MeitY is a government initiative aimed at introducing coding education to school students in collaboration with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) and the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY). The initiative seeks to promote digital literacy and computational thinking among school-going children across India. It is part of MeitY's broader efforts to promote digital literacy and future-ready skills in the education sector.

## Objective

- Introduce coding and computational thinking to school students
- Promote digital literacy in school education
- Prepare students for future-ready skills in technology
- Strengthen collaboration between CBSE and MeitY for digital education initiatives
