# Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance (RAMP)

## Overview

The Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance (RAMP) scheme is a World Bank-assisted initiative under the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MoMSME) aimed at strengthening MSME performance through improved access to finance, market linkages, technology upgradation, and institutional support. It focuses on enhancing competitiveness, resilience, and sustainability of MSMEs across India, particularly in the post-pandemic recovery phase. The scheme supports both existing and new MSMEs through financial and non-financial interventions delivered via state-level implementation units.

## Objective

- Improve access to finance and markets for MSMEs
- Strengthen institutional capabilities at the state and central levels
- Promote technology upgradation and innovation among MSMEs
- Enhance sustainability and resilience of MSMEs
- Support formalization and growth of MSMEs
- Facilitate market linkages and integration into value chains
- Build capacity of MSMEs through skill development and training
- Support women-led and SC/ST-owned MSMEs through targeted interventions

## Important Conditions

- Financial support is subject to availability of funds under the scheme
- Benefits are available only for approved project proposals
- Interest subvention is applicable only on term loans from scheduled banks
- Grant components require submission of utilization certificates and are subject to audit
- MSMEs must maintain Udyam registration validity to continue availing benefits
- Benefits may be withdrawn in case of misuse or non-compliance with scheme guidelines
