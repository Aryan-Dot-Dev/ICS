## Process

1. Mobilization and counseling of potential candidates by training partners or implementing agencies.
2. Enrollment of eligible candidates at designated RPL centers.
3. Conduct of pre-assessment bridge training (if required) to address skill gaps.
4. Final assessment by empaneled assessment agencies as per NSQF-aligned qualification packs.
5. Certification and disbursement of reward money upon successful completion.
