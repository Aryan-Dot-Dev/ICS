# Affordable Rental Housing Complexes (ARHC) Scheme

## Overview

Affordable Rental Housing Complexes (ARHCs) is a sub-scheme under Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana-Urban (PMAY-U) launched by the Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs to provide dignified affordable rental housing to urban migrants and poor from EWS/LIG categories near their workplaces. The scheme operates through two models: Model-1 involves converting existing government-funded vacant houses into ARHCs via Public-Private Partnership or public agencies, while Model-2 involves construction, operation, and maintenance of ARHCs by public/private entities on their own vacant land. Projects are to be operated for a minimum period of 25 years and aim to unlock vacant housing stock, promote investment in rental housing, and improve living conditions for urban migrants/poor.

## Objective

- Provide ease of living to urban migrants/poor by ensuring access to dignified affordable rental housing close to their workplace
- Unlock existing vacant housing stock and make it available in urban space
- Propel new investment opportunities and promote entrepreneurship in the rental housing sector
- Create a conducive ecosystem for public/private entities to leverage investment in rental housing
- Prevent growth of slums by providing affordable rental accommodation
- Improve productivity of urban migrants/poor by reducing commute time and improving living conditions
- Support the vision of 'AatmaNirbhar Bharat' and the larger goal of 'Housing for All' under PMAY-U

## Important Conditions

- Projects under ARHCs will be applicable for consideration and funding till PMAY (U) Mission period i.e. March 2022, though approved projects will be funded for another 18 months to enable completion
- ARHCs must be exclusively used for rental housing for a minimum period of 25 years
- A maximum of 33% dwelling units in double bedroom form is permissible in any ARHC project
- Technology Innovation Grant (TIG) is released only for projects using identified innovative technologies
- Beneficiaries are restricted to urban migrants/poor from EWS/LIG categories
