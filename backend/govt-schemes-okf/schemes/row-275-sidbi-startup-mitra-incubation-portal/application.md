## Process

1. Visit the Startup India portal at https://www.startupindia.gov.in/
2. Navigate to the Startup Mitra – Incubation Portal section
3. Register or log in to your Startup India profile
4. Complete the application form with details about your startup, including innovation, business model, and team
5. Upload required documents such as Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, and pitch deck
6. Submit the application for review
7. Await confirmation and further instructions from the incubation partner
