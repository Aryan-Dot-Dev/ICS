# SIDBI Startup Mitra – Incubation Portal

## Overview

SIDBI Startup Mitra – Incubation Portal is a platform under the Startup India initiative designed to support startups by providing access to incubation services, mentorship, and resources. It aims to nurture early-stage startups through structured incubation programs, connecting them with incubators, accelerators, and industry experts. The portal facilitates application to various incubation schemes and offers guidance on accessing government and private funding, regulatory support, and market linkages.

## Objective

- To provide access to incubation and acceleration support for startups
- To connect startups with mentors, industry experts, and investors
- To facilitate entry into government incubation and funding schemes
- To support prototype development, market entry, and commercialization
- To strengthen the startup ecosystem through collaboration and knowledge sharing

## Eligibility

DPIIT-recognised startups with technology-enabled business ideas are eligible to apply for incubation support through the SIDBI Startup Mitra portal. Startups must be incorporated as a Private Limited Company, Registered Partnership Firm, Limited Liability Partnership, or Cooperative Society.
