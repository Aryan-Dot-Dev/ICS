## Process

1. Athletes are identified through talent search and development programs conducted by the High-Powered Committee.
2. Selected athletes are notified via official induction notifications (e.g., Induction Notification of Khelo India Athletes - [discipline]).
3. Athletes must submit required documents for verification and inclusion in the Khelo India athlete database.
4. Upon approval, athletes receive annual financial assistance and access to training facilities.
5. Continued support is subject to performance review and retention protocols.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — inductions and notifications are issued periodically throughout the year based on talent identification cycles.
