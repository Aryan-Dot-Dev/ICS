# Khelo India – Sport Tech Fund

## Overview

Khelo India – Sport Tech Fund is a component of the Khelo India National Programme for Development of Sports, aimed at reviving sports culture at the grassroots level by building a strong framework for all sports in India and establishing the nation as a sporting superpower. The programme includes verticals such as State Level Khelo India Centres, Annual Sports Competitions, Talent Search and Development, and support for sports academies, among others. It is implemented by multiple agencies including the Sports Authority of India, Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports, and Lakshmi Bai National Institute of Physical Education (LNIPE). The initiative provides financial assistance to talented athletes, infrastructure development, and opportunities for participation in national and international competitions.

## Objective

- Revive sports culture in India at the grass-root level
- Build a strong framework for all sports played in the country
- Establish India as a great sporting nation
- Inspire young talent and provide top-notch infrastructure and training
- Inculcate a strong spirit of participation in sports
- Enable players to demonstrate their true potential
- Support National/Regional/State Sports Academies
- Promote sports among persons with disabilities
- Promote rural and indigenous/tribal games
- Enhance physical fitness of school-going children

## Important Conditions

- Financial assistance is contingent on continued performance and adherence to retention protocols.
- Athletes may be weeded out based on performance, injury, or failure to meet benchmarks.
- Support is limited to priority sports disciplines as determined by the High-Powered Committee.
- The programme does not guarantee permanent employment or long-term sponsorship beyond the 8-year assistance period.
- Funding for centres and infrastructure is subject to budgetary allocations and utilization certificates.
