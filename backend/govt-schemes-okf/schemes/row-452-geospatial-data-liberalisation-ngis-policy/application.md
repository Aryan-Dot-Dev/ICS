## Process

1. No prior approval or license is required for the acquisition, generation, preparation, dissemination, distribution, publication, or updating of digital geospatial data and maps within India.
2. Entities can directly access geospatial data from government sources such as the Survey of India, National Remote Sensing Centre (NRSC), and other authorized agencies.
3. Private entities can generate their own geospatial data using drones, satellites, or other technologies without seeking prior permission.
4. Data can be used, shared, published, or commercialized freely for any lawful purpose.
5. Entities must comply with applicable laws related to national security, privacy, and data protection, but no separate geospatial licensing is required.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. The policy is in effect and applications or data access requests can be made at any time.
