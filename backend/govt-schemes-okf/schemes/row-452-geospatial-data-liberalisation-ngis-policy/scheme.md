# Geospatial Data Liberalisation – NGIS Policy

## Overview

The Geospatial Data Liberalisation – NGIS Policy is a policy initiative by the Department of Science and Technology (DST) aimed at liberalizing access to geospatial data in India. The policy seeks to deregulate the acquisition and use of geospatial data, promote innovation in the geospatial sector, and encourage private sector participation in mapping and geospatial services. It aims to democratize access to high-resolution geospatial data for startups, industries, and researchers, fostering innovation in sectors like urban planning, agriculture, disaster management, and infrastructure development.

## Objective

- Liberalize access to geospatial data by removing prior approval and licensing requirements
- Promote innovation and entrepreneurship in the geospatial sector
- Enable private sector participation in mapping and geospatial data services
- Democratize access to high-resolution geospatial data for startups, academia, and industry
- Support applications in urban planning, agriculture, disaster management, and infrastructure development
- Align with the vision of Atmanirbhar Bharat and technological self-reliance
- Facilitate the growth of the Indian geospatial economy and space economy

## Important Conditions

- The policy does not apply to classified or sensitive geospatial data related to national security, defense, or critical infrastructure.
- Users must comply with other applicable laws such as data privacy, security, and sector-specific regulations.
- The policy does not provide financial grants or subsidies;
- it only deregulates access to geospatial data.
- The policy is subject to periodic review and updates by the Department of Science and Technology.
