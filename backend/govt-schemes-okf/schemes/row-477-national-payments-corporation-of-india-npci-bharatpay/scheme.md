# National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) BharatPay

## Overview

National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) BharatPay is a digital payments initiative by NPCI, an RBI-regulated entity, aimed at enabling seamless and secure digital transactions across India. It encompasses platforms like UPI, RuPay, BHIM, and NACH to promote financial inclusion and modernize retail payments. The scheme supports interoperable, real-time payments for individuals, merchants, and businesses through a robust, scalable infrastructure.

## Objective

- To create a robust payment and settlement infrastructure in India
- To promote financial inclusion through accessible digital payment systems
- To enable interoperable, real-time retail payments across banks and platforms
- To support indigenous payment systems like RuPay and UPI for domestic and global use
- To ensure safety, security, and sustainability of national retail payment systems
- To foster innovation in digital payments through partnerships and technology adoption

## Eligibility

Eligibility varies by product: For UPI, any individual with a bank account linked to a UPI-enabled app can participate; for RuPay, banks issuing cards must be NPCI members; for BHIM, users need a bank account and mobile number; for NACH, institutions must register as corporate or retail users with participating banks.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Benefits include instant 24x7 fund transfers, low transaction costs, wide acceptance across merchants and banks, interoperability, enhanced security via UPI PIN and biometric authentication, support for bill payments, merchant QR codes, international usage through UPI One World, and access to transaction history and complaint redressal mechanisms.

## Important Conditions

- Transaction limits apply as per RBI and bank guidelines
- UPI PIN must not be shared;
- NPCI does not store or access UPI PIN
- Dispute resolution for failed transactions lies with the user’s bank
- International usage via UPI One World is subject to country-specific enablement
- Merchant QR codes must adhere to NPCI brand guidelines
