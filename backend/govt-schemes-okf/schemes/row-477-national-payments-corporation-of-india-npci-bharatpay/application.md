## Process

1. For UPI: Download a UPI-enabled bank app, register using mobile number linked to bank account, set UPI PIN, and link bank account.
2. For RuPay: Approach a member bank to issue a RuPay debit/credit card.
3. For BHIM: Download BHIM app, verify mobile number, link bank account, set UPI PIN.
4. For merchants: Register with a bank or payment gateway to accept UPI/QR payments.
5. For NACH: Corporates/institutions must register with their bank, submit mandate forms, and complete onboarding via NPCI’s NACH portal.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Users and entities can onboard anytime.
