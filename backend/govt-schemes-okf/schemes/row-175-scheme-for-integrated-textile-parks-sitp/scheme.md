# Scheme for Integrated Textile Parks (SITP)

## Overview

The Scheme for Integrated Textile Parks (SITP) is a Government of India initiative under the Ministry of Textiles aimed at developing world-class infrastructure facilities for textile units through public-private partnerships. It supports the establishment of integrated textile parks with plug-and-play facilities to enhance productivity, global competitiveness, and attract investment in the textiles sector. The scheme focuses on creating synergies by co-locating various stages of textile production within a single park.

## Objective

- To provide world-class infrastructure facilities for textile units
- To enhance productivity and global competitiveness of the Indian textiles industry
- To attract investment in the textiles sector through plug-and-play facilities
- To promote backward and forward linkages by integrating various stages of textile production
- To generate employment opportunities in the textiles sector
- To encourage adoption of modern technology and sustainable practices in textile manufacturing


## Target Beneficiaries

**MSME**, **private entrepreneurs**, **industry associations**, **state governments**

## Key Features

- Grant/assistance per entity: Up to INR 40 crore per textile park (INR 60 crore for hilly/tribal/difficult areas)
- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) formed by industry associations, private entrepreneurs, or state government agencies with minimum 10 acres of land, clear title, and commitment to develop the park with at least 50% area allotted to textile units. The SPV must contribute at least 25% of the project cost as equity and ensure that no individual unit occupies more than 25% of the developed area.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial support in the form of grant-in-aid for infrastructure development, common facilities like power, water, effluent treatment, roads, and security. Enables economies of scale, shared services, reduced logistics costs, and access to plug-and-play infrastructure for textile units. Promotes cluster-based development and enhances export competitiveness.

## Important Conditions

- Grant is released in installments based on verification of pre-defined milestones
- Land must be in possession of SPV with clear title before application
- At least 50% of the developed area must be allotted to textile units
- No individual textile unit can occupy more than 25% of the total developed area
- SPV must contribute minimum 25% of project cost as equity
- Projects must be completed within the stipulated timeline as per approved DPR
- Grant-in-aid is subject to availability of funds under the scheme
