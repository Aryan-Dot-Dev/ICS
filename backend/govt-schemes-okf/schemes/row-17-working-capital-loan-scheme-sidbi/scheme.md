# Working Capital Loan Scheme (SIDBI)

## Overview

The Working Capital Loan Scheme by SIDBI provides collateral-free working capital support to MSMEs through digital platforms like DigiCash and GST Sahay, enabling quick sanction within 48 hours. It is designed to address short-term liquidity needs for raw material purchases, credit sales, and operational expenses. The scheme leverages invoice-based financing and digital public infrastructure for seamless, paperless processing. It targets existing SIDBI customers who are GST and Udyam registered with at least two years of business operations and positive cash accruals.

## Objective

- To provide cash flow-based financing for purchase of raw materials or credit sales
- To enable 'on tap' working capital through invoice-based financing via GST Sahay App
- To offer collateral-free credit with quick disbursement and attractive interest rates
- To support GST and Udyam registered micro and small enterprises with 2+ years of operations
- To facilitate digital, straight-through processing using OCEN and Account Aggregator frameworks
- To improve liquidity and cash flow management for MSMEs
- To enable financing against purchase or sales invoices with up to 90% of invoice value processed
