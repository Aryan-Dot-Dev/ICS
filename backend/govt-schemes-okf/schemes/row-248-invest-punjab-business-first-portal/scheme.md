# Invest Punjab – Business First Portal

## Overview

Invest Punjab – Business First Portal is a state-level initiative by the Punjab Bureau of Investment Promotion (Invest Punjab) that serves as a single point of contact for investors seeking to set up businesses in Punjab. It provides state-level regulatory clearances, approvals for fiscal incentives, and facilitates the entire investment lifecycle from proposal to implementation. The portal operates under the 'Business First' philosophy, aiming to reduce compliance burden, ensure time-bound digital approvals, and foster a conducive environment for growth, innovation, and employment generation.

## Objective

- To act as a single point of contact for facilitation of investors looking to set up a business in Punjab
- To provide state-level regulatory clearances and approvals for fiscal incentives
- To offer a unified and transparent interface for all regulatory and fiscal services throughout the investment lifecycle
- To guide investors through the entire investment lifecycle – from initiating the proposal to setting up the business
- To maintain integrity while serving investors promptly and transparently
- To build long-term relationships with the state's industries
- To support the MSME sector and facilitate its growth as the backbone of Punjab’s economy
- To position Punjab as a global investment destination and boost high-quality jobs in the State

## Important Conditions

- Services are limited to regulatory clearances and facilitation;
- direct financial assistance is not provided
- Approval timelines are subject to departmental compliance and document completeness
- Certain approvals may require physical verification or site inspection
- The portal facilitates access to incentives but does not guarantee fiscal benefit disbursement
- Services are primarily for setting up new businesses or expansions in Punjab
