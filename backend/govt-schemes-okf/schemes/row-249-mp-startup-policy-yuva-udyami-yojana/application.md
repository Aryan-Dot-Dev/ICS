## Process

1. Visit the official MP e-District portal at https://mpedistrict.gov.in
2. Register as a new user by providing personal and contact details
3. Log in to the portal using credentials
4. Select 'MP Startup Policy & Yuva Udyami Yojana' from the list of available services
5. Fill in the online application form with business details, project report, and financial projections
6. Upload required documents including identity proof, address proof, business plan, and bank details
7. Pay the applicable processing fee, if any
8. Submit the application and note the application number for tracking
9. Await verification and approval by the designated authority
10. Upon approval, funds are disbursed in stages as per milestones

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds.
