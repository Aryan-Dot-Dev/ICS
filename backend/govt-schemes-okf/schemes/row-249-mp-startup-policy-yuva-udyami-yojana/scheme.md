# MP Startup Policy & Yuva Udyami Yojana

## Overview

The MP Startup Policy & Yuva Udyami Yojana is a state-level initiative by the Government of Madhya Pradesh aimed at fostering entrepreneurship and supporting startups through financial assistance, incubation, and policy incentives. The scheme targets youth and aspiring entrepreneurs to promote innovation and self-employment across the state. It is implemented under the broader framework of the Madhya Pradesh Public Services Guarantee Act, 2010, leveraging the e-District platform for service delivery.

## Objective

- Promote entrepreneurship among youth in Madhya Pradesh
- Provide financial and non-financial support to startups
- Facilitate ease of doing business through single-window clearance
- Encourage innovation and technology-driven ventures
- Create employment opportunities through startup ecosystem development
- Support women and marginalized communities in entrepreneurship
- Strengthen incubation and mentorship infrastructure
- Link startups with funding sources and market access

## Eligibility

The scheme is open to individuals aged 18 to 45 years who are residents of Madhya Pradesh and possess a viable business idea or early-stage startup. Applicants must be first-time entrepreneurs or those who have not previously availed benefits under similar state startup schemes. ## Benefits

Benefits include financial assistance in the form of seed funding, interest subsidies on loans, credit guarantee support, access to incubation centers, mentorship from industry experts, facilitation in regulatory approvals, assistance in patent filing, and support for market linkage and product commercialization. ## Important Conditions

- Financial assistance is subject to availability of funds under the state budget
- Benefits cannot be availed concurrently with other similar central or state startup schemes
- Misrepresentation of facts or submission of false documents leads to rejection and blacklisting
- The startup must remain operational for a minimum of 2 years post-funding to avoid clawback
- Funding is disbursed in stages and contingent upon achieving predefined milestones
