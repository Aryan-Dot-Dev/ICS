# National Centre of Excellence for Green Port & Shipping (NCoEGPS)

## Overview

The National Centre of Excellence for Green Port & Shipping (NCoEGPS) is an initiative under the Sagarmala Programme aimed at promoting innovation, research, and development in green technologies for ports and shipping. It focuses on reducing carbon emissions, enhancing energy efficiency, and supporting sustainable practices in maritime operations. The Centre serves as a hub for collaboration between industry, academia, and government to advance green port and shipping solutions across India.

## Objective

- Promote research and innovation in green port and shipping technologies
- Support development of low-carbon and energy-efficient solutions for maritime sector
- Facilitate collaboration between industry, academia, and government stakeholders
- Provide incubation and testing support for green technologies in ports and shipping
- Advance policy and regulatory frameworks for sustainable port operations
- Enhance skill development and capacity building in green maritime practices
