# Maharashtra Industrial Development Corporation (MIDC) Schemes

## Overview

MIDC Maharashtra Industrial Development Corporation is the premier industrial infrastructure development agency of the Government of Maharashtra, established in 1962 under the M.I.D.C. Act, 1961. It plays a pivotal role in Maharashtra's industrial and economic growth by providing world-class industrial infrastructure, formulating equitable economic development policies, and supporting industries throughout their investment lifecycle. MIDC has built 300 industrial areas covering approximately 101,171.41 hectares of land and developed specialized parks for sectors including IT, BT, SEZs, Wine (Grape Processing), Silver Zone, and Gems.

## Objective

- Set up industrial areas for planned and systematic industrial development
- Function as a special planning authority in development of industrial areas
- Promote equitable economic development through right policies
- Support industries through the entire life-cycle of investment
- Develop industrial infrastructure as the singular focus
- Preserve natural resources and conserve the ecosystem
- Re-invent the paradigm of investment promotion through Magnetic Maharashtra 2.0
- Catapult Maharashtra to be the first trillion dollar economy in India

## Important Conditions

- Land allotment is subject to conditions in the offer letter;
- failure to pay EMD within stipulated period may lead to extension (max 15 days) with interest.
- Balance Occupancy Premium (BOP) must be paid within 30 days of allotment letter;
- extendable up to 150 days with delayed payment charges.
- Building plan approval must be obtained within 35 days from application submission on BPAMS portal.
- Construction and commencement of production must occur within zone-specific time periods (A/B: 3 years, C: 5 years, D/D+: 7 years from actual possession).
- Stamp duty exemption under PSI 2019 is available only for IT and BT manufacturing units in IT and BT Parks in A and B areas.
- Venture Capital facility for SC/ST entrepreneurs available under Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Special Package Scheme of Incentives.
