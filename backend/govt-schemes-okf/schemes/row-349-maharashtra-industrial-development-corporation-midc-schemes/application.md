## Process

1. Fill Up & Submit Application for land allotment via 'Direct' or 'e-Bidding' process.
2. Pay Processing Fee (based on area) and Application Advance (5% EMD) online via NEFT/RTGS/Net Banking/Credit Card/Debit Card/UPI.
3. Application processed by Land Allotment Committee (LAC); IT park applications processed by head office.
4. LAC meeting held after DPR scrutiny; decision is final; approved minutes published on MIDC website.
5. System generated Offer Letter issued upon LAC approval; applicant must pay 25% EMD (Direct) or 20% EMD (e-Bidding H1 bidder) within 15 days.
6. System generated Allotment Letter issued upon EMD payment; applicant must pay Balance Occupancy Premium (BOP) within 30 days (extendable up to 150 days).
7. Execute Agreement to Lease (A to L) after BOP payment, pay necessary stamp duty.
8. MIDC deputes surveyor for plot handover/takeover document signing.
9. Submit building plan with details to BPAMS portal via appointed Architect; Special Planning Authority scrutinizes plan; challan generated; after payment, commencement certificate issued within 35 days.
10. Complete construction and commence production within specified time period (A/B Zone: 3 years, C Zone: 5 years, D/D+ Zone: 7 years from actual possession).
11. Apply online via SWC portal for Occupancy Certificate after site inspection by SPA (if required).
12. Execute final lease upon receipt of Occupancy Certificate.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications accepted year-round for Direct and e-Bidding allotment.
