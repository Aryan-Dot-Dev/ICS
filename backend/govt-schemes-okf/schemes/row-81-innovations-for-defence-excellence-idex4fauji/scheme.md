# Innovations for Defence Excellence (iDEX4Fauji)

## Overview

Innovations for Defence Excellence (iDEX) is a flagship scheme launched by the Government of India in April 2018 under the Department of Defence Production, Ministry of Defence, to foster innovation and technology development in defence and aerospace by engaging startups, MSMEs, individual innovators, R&D institutes, and academia. It provides grants, funding, and other support to carry out R&D with potential for future adoption in Indian defence and aerospace needs. iDEX operates through the Defence Innovation Organisation (DIO), a Section 8 company formed by HAL and BEL, and runs challenges such as Defence India Startup Challenge (DISC), Open Challenge, and ADITI to support prototype development and commercialization.

## Objective

- Facilitate rapid development of new, indigenized, and innovative technologies for the Indian defence and aerospace sector to meet needs in shorter timelines
- Create a culture of engagement with innovative startups to encourage co-creation for defence and aerospace sectors
- Empower a culture of technology co-creation and co-innovation within the defence and aerospace sectors
- Boost innovation among start-ups and encourage them to be part of the Indian defence and aerospace ecosystem
- Facilitate scale-up, indigenization, and integration in manufacturing facilities for successfully piloted technologies
- Interface with military top brass about key innovative technologies and encourage their adoption into defence establishments
- Organize various challenges/hackathons to shortlist potential technologies and entities for defence and aerospace use
- Enable and fund pilots using innovation funds dedicated to the purpose

## Benefits

Grants/funding, mentorship, access to defence testing facilities and experts, entry to accelerator programs run by iDEX partners, support in technology and business development, and facilitation of scale-up, indigenization, and integration in manufacturing facilities for successfully piloted technologies.

## Important Conditions

- Individual innovators post-selection need to register as Startup/MSME
- Grant disbursement is based on milestone completion
- No establishment funding provided to any DIO-affiliated incubators
- Funding is subject to due diligence and financial compliance guidelines
- Government may put restrictions on transfer/licensing of technology/IPR developed under the program on considerations of national security or other strategic reasons
