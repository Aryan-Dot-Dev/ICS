## Process

1. Provide full details of a Single Point of Contact for the applying entity – these are the full contact details of a single person who will act as the point of contact between iDEX and the applicant.
2. Provide Entity Details – these are the details of the Startup/MSME applying (Individual Innovators do not have to fill these).
3. Provide Proposal Details – these are the details of the proposal in response to the iDEX Challenge problem statements/focus areas. Applicants must select the problem statement they are addressing, what level of funding they want and give technical and financial details of their proposal. This will include mentioning relevant patents and research papers by the applicant, if any as well as a tentative business plan.
4. Submit the application online via the iDEX portal.
5. Applications will be screened by a duly constituted high-powered committee of defence and technology experts for screening and selection of applications for the iDEX Challenge.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round.
