# NABARD RIDF (Rural Infrastructure Development Fund)

## Overview

The Rural Infrastructure Development Fund (RIDF) is a scheme administered by NABARD to provide financial support for rural infrastructure projects. It aims to bridge the gap in rural infrastructure by financing projects related to agriculture, irrigation, soil conservation, and other rural development initiatives. The fund is contributed by commercial banks and managed by NABARD for disbursement to state governments and other implementing agencies.

## Objective

- To provide loans to state governments and state-owned corporations for ongoing rural infrastructure projects
- To support projects in agriculture and allied sectors, irrigation, soil conservation, drinking water, rural roads, and bridges
- To encourage private sector participation in rural infrastructure through public-private partnerships
- To augment resources for rural infrastructure where normal bank credit flow is inadequate
- To promote sustainable and inclusive rural development

## Eligibility

State governments, state-owned corporations, local self-governments, NGOs, and cooperatives are eligible to avail loans under RIDF for rural infrastructure projects. Projects must be technically feasible, economically viable, and socially acceptable, and should fall under the approved list of activities under the fund.

## Benefits

Long-term financing at concessional interest rates for rural infrastructure projects; support for completion of ongoing projects; contribution to rural employment generation; improvement in agricultural productivity and rural livelihoods; enhanced access to basic rural infrastructure.

- Projects must be from the approved list of activities under RIDF
- State government contribution is mandatory as per prescribed ratio
- Utilization certificates and progress reports are mandatory for subsequent disbursements
- Interest rates and terms are revised periodically by NABARD
