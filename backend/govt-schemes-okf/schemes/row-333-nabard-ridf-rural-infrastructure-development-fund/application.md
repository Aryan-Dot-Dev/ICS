## Process

1. Identify eligible rural infrastructure project under approved RIDF activities.
2. Prepare detailed project report (DPR) including technical, financial, and economic aspects.
3. Submit application through the state government nodal officer to NABARD.
4. NABARD appraises the project for technical feasibility and financial viability.
5. Upon approval, loan agreement is executed.
6. Funds are released in stages based on project progress and utilization certificates.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — proposals can be submitted throughout the year based on annual allocations.
