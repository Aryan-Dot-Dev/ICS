# ECGC Export Credit Guarantee Schemes

## Overview

ECGC Export Credit Guarantee Schemes provide financial assistance to Indian exporters by offering credit guarantees against risks of non-payment by overseas buyers due to commercial or political reasons. The scheme supports export growth by mitigating payment risks and enhancing access to working capital finance from banks. It covers both pre-shipment and post-shipment stages of export transactions.

## Objective

- Provide credit guarantee covers to protect exporters against payment defaults by overseas buyers
- Facilitate easier access to export finance from banks and financial institutions
- Promote Indian exports by mitigating commercial and political risks
- Support MSME exporters through tailored guarantee products
- Enhance competitiveness of Indian goods and services in international markets


## Target Beneficiaries

**exporters**, **banks**, **MSME**


## Key Features

- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — applications accepted throughout the year based on export transactions.
- Portal: https://www.ecgc.in
- Source data last updated: 2025
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

Indian exporters, including MSMEs, engaged in export of goods or services, who have obtained an export order and require credit protection against buyer default. Banks lending to such exporters can also avail guarantees to secure their export credit exposure.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Protection against commercial and political risks, improved access to bank finance at better terms, enhanced creditworthiness of exporters, coverage for both pre-shipment and post-shipment risks, and support for export bill discounting and collection.
