## Process

1. Identify eligible sanitation project (e.g., IHHL, CSC, SWM, LWM, GOBARdhan) as per SBM-G Phase II guidelines.
2. Gram Panchayat or implementing agency submits proposal through the Swachh Bharat Mission Gramin portal or to the District Water and Sanitation Committee.
3. Project is appraised by the Block/ District level officials for technical and financial feasibility.
4. Upon approval, funds are released in installments linked to physical progress.
5. Implementation is carried out by Gram Panchayat, SHG, NGO, or CSR partner.
6. Operation and maintenance plan is established, often with CPSEs/NGOs for 10 years.
7. Project is monitored via Swachh Survekshan Grameen and reported through MIS.
8. Upon completion, village is assessed for ODF Plus status (Aspiring, Rising, Model).
