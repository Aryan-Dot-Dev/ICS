# Bengal Silicon Valley Startup Hub

## Overview

The Bengal Silicon Valley Startup Hub is a state-level initiative by the Government of West Bengal to foster innovation and entrepreneurship by providing infrastructure, mentorship, and support services to startups in the state. It aims to create a conducive ecosystem for technology-driven ventures through incubation, networking, and access to resources. The hub focuses on enabling early-stage startups to scale by offering shared facilities and expert guidance.

## Objective

- To provide incubation facilities and infrastructure support to startups
- To offer mentorship and guidance from industry experts
- To facilitate networking and collaboration among entrepreneurs
- To promote innovation and technology-driven ventures in West Bengal
- To enhance the startup ecosystem through access to shared resources and services
