# National Beekeeping & Honey Mission (NBHM)

## Overview

The National Beekeeping & Honey Mission (NBHM) is a Central Sector Scheme under the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, implemented through the National Agricultural Cooperative Marketing Federation of India Ltd. (NACFED). It aims to promote holistic growth of the beekeeping sector for income and employment generation, particularly for small and marginal farmers, tribals, and women. The mission focuses on scientific beekeeping, honey production, processing, value addition, and market linkage through integrated interventions.

## Objective

- Promote holistic growth of beekeeping sector for income and employment generation
- Support small and marginal farmers, tribals, and women through scientific beekeeping
- Enhance honey production and productivity through adoption of improved technologies
- Develop infrastructure for collection, processing, storage, and marketing of honey and beehive products
- Promote value addition and diversification of beehive products (wax, pollen, propolis, royal jelly, venom)
- Strengthen market linkages and brand promotion for domestic and export markets
- Provide training and capacity building to beekeepers and stakeholders
- Encourage pollination support services to enhance agricultural and horticultural crop yields

## Eligibility

Individual farmers, beekeepers, self-help groups (SHGs), joint liability groups (JLGs), farmer producer organizations (FPOs), cooperatives, and tribal communities engaged in or willing to take up beekeeping activities are eligible. Preference is given to small and marginal farmers, SC/ST, and women beneficiaries.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial assistance for setting up beekeeping colonies, procurement of equipment (hives, extractors, processing units), training and capacity building, infrastructure support for honey processing and storage, market development support, branding and packaging assistance, quality certification support, and technical guidance on scientific beekeeping practices.

## Important Conditions

- Subsidy is reimbursement-based after procurement and submission of bills
- Beneficiaries must procure items only from NACFED-empaneled suppliers
- Assistance is subject to availability of funds under the scheme
- Beneficiaries must maintain beekeeping colonies for minimum 3 years post-subsidy
- Duplicate benefits under other central/state schemes for same activity not permitted
