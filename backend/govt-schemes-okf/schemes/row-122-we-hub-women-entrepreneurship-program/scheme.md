# WE-Hub Women Entrepreneurship Program

## Overview

WE Hub is a Government of Telangana initiative aimed at empowering women entrepreneurs by providing access to technical, financial, government, and policy support to start, scale, sustain, and accelerate their businesses. It focuses on fostering innovation and leadership among women in both urban and rural areas to drive social empowerment through economic enablement. The hub offers a range of services including business consultation, mentorship, marketing assistance, compliance and tax support, funding assistance, and community networking. It works with startups, SMEs, and students, aligning its efforts with 12 of the 17 UN Sustainable Development Goals.

## Objective

- Build a powerful community of 1 million women entrepreneurs across India
- Transform the entrepreneurial landscape by fostering innovation and leadership among women
- Counter biases and stereotypes faced by women entrepreneurs
- Enable access to technical, financial, government, and policy support
- Support women to start, scale, sustain, and accelerate their businesses
- Promote social empowerment through economic enablement
- Engage with communities and institutions to advance 12 of the 17 UN SDGs
- Provide skill development and entrepreneurial training for students

## Eligibility

Women entrepreneurs must hold at least 51% stake in their business, which must be legally registered in India. For specific programs like WE Reach, applicants must be women or groups of 2-3 women from Tier 2/Tier 3 regions of Telangana who want to set up a for-profit enterprise or are running an informal enterprise and wish to formalize it.
