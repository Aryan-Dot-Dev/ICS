# Farmer Producer Organisation (FPO) Equity Grant Fund

## Overview

The Farmer Producer Organisation (FPO) Equity Grant Fund is a scheme under the Central Sector Scheme for Formation and Promotion of 10,000 Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) implemented by the Small Farmers' Agri-Business Consortium (SFAC). It provides matching equity grants to Farmer Producer Companies (FPCs) to improve availability of working capital and support business development. The scheme aims to strengthen the equity base of FPOs and promote sustainable agribusiness through financial support and handholding.

## Objective

- To provide matching equity grants to Farmer Producer Companies (FPCs) to improve availability of working capital
- To support development of business activities of FPOs
- To promote formation and growth of Farmer Producer Organizations/Farmer Producer Companies across the country
- To establish an ecosystem for FPOs/FPCs to make them sustainable and viable in the long run
- To increase incomes of small and marginal farmers through aggregation and development of agribusiness

## Eligibility

Farmer Producer Companies (FPCs) registered under Part IXA of the Companies Act or under Co-operative Societies Act of the concerned States, formed for the purpose of leveraging collectives through economies of scale in production and marketing of agricultural and allied sector, and seeking equity grant for working capital and business development activities.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial support in the form of matching equity grant to strengthen the equity base of FPOs; non-financial benefits include handholding support from Cluster Based Business Organizations (CBBOs), capacity building, market linkages, credit linkages, and technical guidance for sustainable agribusiness development.

## Important Conditions

- Equity Grant is subject to maximum of Rs. 15.00 lakh per FPO
- Grant is released in 3 tranches within a period of 3 Years
- Requires matching equity contribution from FPO shareholders
- FPC must be registered under Part IXA of Companies Act or Co-operative Societies Act
- Business plan for 18 months is mandatory
