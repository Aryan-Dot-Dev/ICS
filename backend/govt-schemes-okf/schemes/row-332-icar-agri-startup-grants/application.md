## Process

1. Visit the official ICAR website or designated portal for agri-startup grants.
2. Check eligibility and ensure the startup is DPIIT-recognized or endorsed by an ICAR institute.
3. Prepare a detailed project proposal including innovation description, objectives, methodology, budget, and expected outcomes.
4. Submit the application online along with required documents.
5. Applications are screened by an ICAR committee for technical feasibility and innovation quotient.
6. Shortlisted startups may be invited for a presentation or interview.
7. Final selection is made based on merit, and approved grantees receive a sanction letter.
8. Funds are disbursed in installments as per milestones.
9. Grantees are required to submit progress reports and utilization certificates.
