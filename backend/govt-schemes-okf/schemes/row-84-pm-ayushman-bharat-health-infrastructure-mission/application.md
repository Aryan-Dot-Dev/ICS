## Process

1. State Governments/UTs submit project proposals to the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare.
2. Proposals are appraised by the Expenditure Finance Committee or Standing Finance Committee.
3. Upon approval, administrative sanction is issued.
4. Funds are released in installments as per the agreed schedule.
5. States/UTs implement the projects through designated agencies.
6. Utilization certificates and progress reports are submitted for subsequent fund releases.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — proposals can be submitted throughout the year based on annual budget allocations and planning cycles.
