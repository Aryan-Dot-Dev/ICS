# PM Ayushman Bharat Health Infrastructure Mission

## Overview

The PM Ayushman Bharat Health Infrastructure Mission (PM-ABHIM) is a centrally sponsored scheme launched to strengthen public health infrastructure and pandemic preparedness across India. It aims to develop critical care hospital blocks, expand diagnostic services, and enhance surveillance and research capabilities at national, state, and district levels. The mission focuses on building resilient health systems through investments in both urban and rural health facilities.

## Objective

- Strengthen public health infrastructure at primary, secondary, and tertiary levels
- Establish critical care hospital blocks in districts with population over 5 lakhs
- Expand diagnostic services through a network of laboratories
- Enhance disease surveillance and outbreak response capabilities
- Develop institutional capacity for research and pandemic preparedness
- Support the creation of block public health units in rural areas
- Upgrade existing health and wellness centers
- Establish integrated public health labs at district level

## Eligibility

State Governments and Union Territories are eligible to receive financial assistance under the scheme. Funds are allocated based on population, disease burden, and health infrastructure gaps. Implementation is carried out through State Health Societies and designated nodal agencies.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial support for construction and equipping of critical care blocks, diagnostic laboratories, block public health units, and integrated public health labs. Non-financial benefits include technical guidance, training programs, and monitoring support from central agencies. The scheme enhances access to quality healthcare services, especially in underserved and rural areas.
