## Process

1. Application process varies by component: For NIDHI-PRAYAS 2.0, innovators apply to designated PRAYAS Centres/Advance PRAYAS Centres via the NIDHI PRAYAS Portal using a common application template; for NIDHI Seed Support Program, incubators apply once per year through an online portal (call announced annually), and startups apply to listed incubators/TBIs; for NIDHI-EIR, startups apply through NIDHI-EIR Centres at selected TBIs; for NIDHI-TBI, proposals are submitted to NSTEDB via their online portal with required enclosures; for NIDHI-Accelerator, TBIs apply through the department’s online portal http://onlinedst.gov.in.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round for most components; however, specific calls like NIDHI Seed Support Program are announced once per year, and Indo-German InnoXchange Programme had a deadline of 10th April, 2026.
