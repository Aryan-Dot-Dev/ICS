# National Initiative for Developing and Harnessing Innovations (NIDHI)

## Overview

National Initiative for Developing and Harnessing Innovations (NIDHI) is an umbrella programme conceived and developed by the Technology Translation and Innovation (TTI) Division, Department of Science & Technology, Government of India, for nurturing ideas and innovations (knowledge-based and technology-driven) into successful startups. The programme works in line with national priorities and goals, focusing on building an innovation-driven entrepreneurial ecosystem with the objective of socio-economic development through wealth and job creation. It supports startups through scouting, supporting, and scaling of innovations via components like PRAYAS, EIR, TBI, Accelerator, Seed Support, and Centres of Excellence.

## Objective

- Nurture ideas and innovations into successful startups
- Build an innovation-driven entrepreneurial ecosystem
- Focus on socio-economic development through wealth and job creation
- Support scouting, supporting, and scaling of innovations
- Align with national priorities and goals
- Promote indigenous innovation and Atmanirbhar Bharat Mission
- Strengthen the pipeline of high-quality innovative startups
- Establish networks between innovators, academia, mentors, and incubators

## Eligibility

Eligibility varies by component: For NIDHI-PRAYAS, innovators/startups can apply through NIDHI-PRAYAS Centres at selected TBIs; for NIDHI Seed Support Program, startups must be registered in India, preferably DPIIT-registered, with at least 3 months association with the incubator, clarity on USP, and at least 51% shareholding by Indian promoters; for NIDHI-EIR, applicants must be Indian citizens…

## Benefits

Benefits include financial support (grants up to INR 40 lakhs for prototyping under PRAYAS 2.0, up to INR 100 lakhs under Seed Support, up to INR 30,000/month under EIR), access to infrastructure (Fab Labs, incubation space), mentoring, technical and business guidance, IP and legal assistance, industry and investor connect opportunities, networking events, demo day exposure, and co-working…

## Important Conditions

- Funding continuation depends on performance evaluation by PMC/NEAC
- Support may be terminated mid-term for non-compliance or significant shortfall in progress
- Grantee must register at PFMS and CGA website to facilitate fund release
- All NIDHI-TBIs and i-TBIs must execute a bond on non-judicial stamp paper before grant release
- Unspent grants may be refunded to Government of India
- Assets created from grants must be installed in TBI premises unless approved otherwise
- Grant release is subject to satisfactory performance and utilization certificate submission
- NIDHI-EIR support cannot be concurrent with any other remuneration or fellowship
- NIDHI-EIR recipients cannot be promoters or significant (>10%) shareholders/beneficiaries of another company
- NIDHI-EIR must be registered for pre-incubation/incubation at TBI for entire duration
- NIDHI-PRAYAS 2.0 centres must establish DST NIDHI Maker Bhavan within one year of first tranche or refund amount
- Indo-German InnoXchange Programme requires refundable deposit of INR 30,000 per participant
