# TANSIDCO MSME Support Scheme

## Overview

TANSIDCO (Tamil Nadu Small Industries Development Corporation Limited) provides industrial infrastructure support through the allotment of plots, sheds, and plug-and-play modules on outright sale, lease, or rental basis across Tamil Nadu. The scheme facilitates MSMEs and entrepreneurs by offering developed industrial spaces with basic amenities in various industrial estates. It aims to promote industrial growth by making land and built-up spaces accessible for manufacturing and service units.

## Objective

- Provide developed industrial infrastructure for MSMEs and entrepreneurs
- Facilitate ease of doing business through ready-to-use plug-and-play modules
- Promote industrial growth across districts of Tamil Nadu
- Offer flexible land and building acquisition options (outright sale, lease, rental)
- Support silver anklet manufacturing and other niche industries through dedicated modules
- Ensure transparent allotment process via GIS and online application system


## Target Beneficiaries

**MSME**, **entrepreneurs**, **manufacturing units**, **service units**

- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

Any individual, entrepreneur, MSME, or industrial unit seeking to establish or expand operations in Tamil Nadu can apply for allotment of plots, sheds, or modules. Applicants must visit the industrial estate physically and upload geo-tagged photos as part of the application. Udyam/UAM registration is encouraged but not explicitly mandatory for application initiation.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Access to developed industrial plots, sheds, and plug-and-play modules with basic infrastructure (power, water, approach roads). Options for outright purchase, 99-year lease, or rental basis. Transparent allotment process with GIS mapping, online application, and status tracking. Dedicated facilities like Silver Anklet Manufacturing Complex in Salem. Support for backward block industrial estates.


Structured benefit objects: [benefits.md](benefits.md).

## Important Conditions

- Physical visit to the industrial estate and uploading of geo-tagged photos is mandatory for application
- Allotment is subject to availability of vacant plots/sheds/modules in the selected estate
- Costs are tentative and subject to revision
- Application does not guarantee allotment;
- subject to verification and payment confirmation
- Specific modules (e.g., Silver Anklet) are exclusively for designated industrial activities
