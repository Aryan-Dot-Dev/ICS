## Process

1. Submission portal will open from 1st July
2. 2. Visit https://epms.icmr.org.in. The user manual (under Guidelines → e-PMS manual) is available on the portal.
3. The proposal submission process has three steps: o Step 1: PI registration/login https://epms.icmr.org.in/userLogin o Step 2: Verify email ID and complete/update PI profile o Step 3: Proposal submission
4. After completing the mandatory sections of the PI profile: o Go to Proposal Submission o Click on Apply Proposal o Select ‘Request for Expression of Interest for ICMR Collaborating Centre of Excellence (ICMR-CCoE) – 2026’ from the dropdown – Apply New Proposal o Fill the form step by step.
5. For queries, contact: po.epms@icmr.gov.in

## Deadlines
