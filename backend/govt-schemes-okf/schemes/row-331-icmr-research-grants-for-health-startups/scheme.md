# ICMR Research Grants for Health Startups

## Overview

The ICMR Research Grants for Health Startups is a funding initiative by the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) aimed at supporting health-related startups through financial assistance for research and innovation in biomedical and public health domains. The scheme operates under ICMR's extramural and intramural research programmes, providing grants to eligible entities for conducting research aligned with national health priorities. It encourages innovation in areas such as diagnostics, therapeutics, vaccines, and health technologies, with a focus on translating research into practical healthcare solutions.

## Objective

- Promote innovation in biomedical research and health technologies
- Support startups in developing solutions for national health priorities
- Strengthen the ecosystem for translational research in medicine and public health
- Encourage collaboration between startups and ICMR institutes
- Facilitate early-stage validation and scaling of health innovations
- Enhance capacity for research in diagnostics, treatment, and preventive healthcare

## Eligibility

Indian research institutes or organisations can submit the EoI, provided they fulfil the following criteria: a research team of an institute with cutting-edge biomedical research experience, led by a senior PI; excellent track record in health research in terms of impactful publications; demonstrated collaboration with or contribution to ICMR; willingness to contribute to ICMR’s objectives and…

## Benefits

Recognition as ICMR Collaborating Centre of Excellence (ICMR-CCoE), access to ICMR's research infrastructure, mentorship from ICMR scientists, opportunities for collaboration on national health projects, visibility in national research networks, and support for proposal development and technical guidance through ICMR's portals and helpdesks such as MedTech Mitra, Patent Mitra, and Biostatistics…
