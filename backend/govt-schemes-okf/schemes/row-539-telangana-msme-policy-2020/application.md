## Process

1. Download the application form and fill it in online.
2. Sign the filled application form duly by the applicant or authorized person.
3. Submit the signed application form along with valid documents in original at the Head Office or Zonal Office.
4. The Sub-committee (with members from TGIIC, TSPCB, SFC, Commissioner of Industries, APITCO, technical consultants) will scrutinize the DPR and related documents and recommend the extent of land for allotment.
5. The State Level Allotment Committee (formed with the same members) will approve, defer, or reject the application based on recommendations.
6. The applicant will receive the decision on the same day through their registered mobile.
