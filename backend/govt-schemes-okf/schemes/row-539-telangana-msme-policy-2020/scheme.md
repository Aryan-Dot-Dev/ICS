# Telangana MSME Policy 2020

## Overview

TGIIC (Telangana Industrial Infrastructure Corporation) is a Telangana Government initiative established in 2014 to provide infrastructure through the development of industrial areas, including developed plots/sheds, roads, drainage, water, power, and social infrastructure like worker housing. It coordinates with agencies for communication, transport, and other facilities, and undertakes projects in Public-Private Partnership mode. TGIIC develops industrial parks across all districts of Telangana and develops industrial clusters based on demand and product availability, utilizing Government of India grants under the MSE-CDP scheme for new cluster development and upgradation of existing industrial parks.

## Objective

- Identify and develop potential growth centers in Telangana
- Provide fully equipped industrial plots/sheds with infrastructure (roads, drainage, water, power)
- Provide social infrastructure such as housing for workers near industrial zones
- Coordinate with agencies for communication, transport, and other facilities
- Undertake projects in Public-Private Partnership mode
- Develop industrial parks in all districts of Telangana
- Develop industrial clusters based on demand and product availability
- Utilize Government of India grants under MSE-CDP for new cluster development and upgradation of existing industrial parks
