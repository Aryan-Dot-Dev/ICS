## Process

1. Register yourself using a valid Indian mobile number, Driving License, PAN or Aadhaar.
2. Verify yourself via OTP or other verification method.
3. Fetch your documents from linked issuers.
4. Share your documents with consent.
5. Become a DigiLocker Partner Organization by registering as an Issuer or Requester.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round.
