# DigiLocker

## Overview

DigiLocker is a flagship initiative of the Ministry of Electronics & IT (MeitY) under the Digital India programme. It provides a secure cloud-based platform for storage, sharing, and verification of digital documents and certificates. DigiLocker aims at 'Digital Empowerment' of citizens by providing access to authentic digital documents to their digital document wallet, with issued documents deemed legally at par with original physical documents under Rule 9A of the IT (Preservation and Retention of Information by Intermediaries providing Digital Locker facilities) Rules, 2016.

## Objective

- Provide access to authentic digital documents to citizens' digital document wallet
- Enable secure storage, sharing, and verification of documents & certificates
- Promote digital empowerment through paperless governance
- Facilitate real-time verification of documents by government agencies
- Reduce administrative overhead by minimizing paper use
- Support faster service delivery in areas like employment, financial inclusion, education, and health
