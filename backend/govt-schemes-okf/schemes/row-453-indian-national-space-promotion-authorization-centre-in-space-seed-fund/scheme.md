# Indian National Space Promotion & Authorization Centre (IN-SPACe) Seed Fund

## Overview

The Indian National Space Promotion & Authorization Centre (IN-SPACe) Seed Fund is a financial support initiative aimed at nurturing early-stage space startups in India. It provides seed funding to innovative ventures engaged in space technology, applications, and services. The fund is designed to promote private sector participation in the Indian space economy by supporting proof-of-concept, prototype development, and early commercialization efforts. IN-SPACe, as the regulatory and promotional body under the Department of Space, administers this scheme to bridge the funding gap for emerging space entrepreneurs.

## Objective

- To provide financial assistance to early-stage space startups for proof-of-concept and prototype development
- To promote innovation and entrepreneurship in the Indian space sector
- To enable technology demonstration and validation of space-based solutions
- To support the commercialization of indigenous space technologies
- To strengthen the pipeline of investable space ventures for future scaling
- To foster self-reliance (Atmanirbharata) in critical space technologies

## Important Conditions

- Funds must be utilized strictly for the approved project objectives and within the stipulated timeline
- Grantees are required to submit periodic progress reports and utilization certificates
- Any unspent balance must be refunded to IN-SPACe upon project completion or termination
- The grant is non-transferable and cannot be used for land acquisition or construction of permanent structures
- IN-SPACe reserves the right to reject applications without assigning any reason
- Beneficiaries must comply with all statutory and regulatory requirements applicable to space activities
