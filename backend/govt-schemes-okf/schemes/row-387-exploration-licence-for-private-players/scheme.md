# Exploration Licence for Private Players

## Overview

The Exploration Licence for Private Players is a scheme introduced under the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Amendment Act, 2023, which empowers the Central Government to auction exploration licences for critical and deep-seated minerals. The scheme allows private sector participation in reconnaissance and prospecting operations for minerals listed in the new Seventh Schedule of the Act. Exploration licences are granted through a transparent, non-discretionary online auction process, with a validity of 5 years extendable by another 2 years.

## Objective

- Promote private sector participation in exploration of critical and deep-seated minerals
- Ensure transparent and non-discretionary allocation of exploration rights through auction
- Facilitate reconnaissance and prospecting operations for minerals in the Seventh Schedule
- Strengthen domestic supply chain resilience for strategic minerals
- Enable access to geo-scientific data for informed bidding
- Encourage technological innovation in mineral exploration
- Support Atmanirbhar Bharat by reducing import dependency on critical minerals

## Eligibility

Comply with eligibility conditions specified in Section 5 of the MMDR Act: Indian National and Company Registered under Companies Act, 1956. Maximum area limit: One or more exploration licences covering a total area of more than 5000 Sq Km. Net worth requirement for Exploration Licence: Bidder should have net worth of more than or equal to Rs. 25 Crore.

## Important Conditions

- Exploration licence valid for 5 years, extendable by 2 years only
- No mining lease deed executed if not done within 3 years from letter of intent (extendable to 5 years if delay beyond bidder's control)
- Auction process annulled if fewer than 3 technically qualified bidders in Round I
- Must comply with Minerals (Evidence of Mineral Contents) Rules, 2015 for area eligibility
- Reimbursement from NMET subject to audit and compliance with SoC guidelines
