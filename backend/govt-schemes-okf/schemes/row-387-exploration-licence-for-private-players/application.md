## Process

1. State/Central Government issues notice inviting tender to commence auction process and identify/demarcate area.
2. Submission of Technical Bids and Initial Price Offer (IPO) as percentage of value of mineral despatched.
3. Top 50% technically qualified bidders (TQB) or 5 whichever is higher proceed to Round II.
4. If TQBs < 3, auction process annulled.
5. Opening of IPO and live forward auction with minimum increment of 0.05%.
6. At least one Final Price Offer (FPO) required for successful auction.
7. Bidder submitting highest FPO declared as Preferred Bidder (PB).
8. If no qualified bidder submits FPO, auction annulled.
9. For second attempt, terms remain same as first annulled attempt.
