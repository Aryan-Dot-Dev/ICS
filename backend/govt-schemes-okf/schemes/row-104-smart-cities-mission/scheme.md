# Smart Cities Mission

## Overview

The Smart Cities Mission is a centrally sponsored scheme launched by the Government of India to promote sustainable and inclusive urban development by transforming 100 cities into smart cities through the application of smart solutions. The mission focuses on core infrastructure, clean and sustainable environment, and smart technology applications to improve quality of life and drive economic growth.

## Objective

- Promote cities that provide core infrastructure and give a decent quality of life to its citizens
- Apply smart solutions to infrastructure and services in area-based development
- Use technology, information and data to improve infrastructure and services
- Ensure sustainable and inclusive development with focus on poor and disadvantaged
- Strengthen municipal finance and improve urban governance
- Encourage private sector participation through PPPs and other models


## Target Beneficiaries

**urban local bodies**, **state governments**, **municipal corporations**


## Key Features

- Fund size: ₹20000 crore
- Grant/assistance per entity: INR 100 crore central assistance per city (matched by state/ULB for total INR 200 crore)
- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: Challenge-based selection with periodic rounds; no rolling deadline. Specific timelines announced for each challenge round.
- Portal: https://smartcities.gov.in
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

Cities are selected through a competitive challenge process where states nominate cities based on predefined criteria including urban population, presence of municipal corporation, and readiness for reform. Selected cities must prepare a Smart City Proposal (SCP) detailing vision, plan for area-based development and pan-city initiatives, and financial sustainability.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Selected cities receive central financial support of INR 100 crore per city over five years, matched by equal contribution from state/ULB. Benefits include improved urban infrastructure (water supply, sanitation, mobility), enhanced service delivery through technology, increased investment, job creation, and better quality of life for residents.
