## Process

1. Identify metro rail project alignment and prepare Detailed Project Report (DPR).
2. Form a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) as a 50:50 joint venture between Government of India and respective State Government.
3. Secure approvals from MoHUA, including legal compliance under Metro Railway Acts and safety certification.
4. Obtain sanction from the Union Cabinet detailing financing pattern, institutional arrangements, and conditions.
5. Sign tripartite Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between GoI, State Government, and SPV.
6. Arrange financing: central assistance (equity/subordinate debt), state contribution, and loans from bilateral/multilateral agencies.
7. Implement project under SPV with oversight from High Powered Committee chaired by Chief Secretary of State Government.
8. Ensure compliance with reforms such as Urban Transport Fund (UTF), fare revision mechanisms, and integrated ticketing.
9. Monitor progress via National Apex Committee (NAC) and State Level Technical Committee (SLTC).
10. Release central assistance in tranches (30%, 50%, 20%) based on physical and financial progress.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round based on project readiness and sanction timelines.
