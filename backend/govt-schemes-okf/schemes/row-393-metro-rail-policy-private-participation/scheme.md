# Metro Rail Policy – Private Participation

## Overview

The Metro Rail Policy – Private Participation is a framework under the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) that enables private sector involvement in metro rail projects through public-private partnerships (PPPs), equity participation, and financial structuring. It aims to leverage private investment for urban rail infrastructure while ensuring policy compliance, safety standards, and institutional oversight. The policy supports metro rail implementation across Indian cities by defining roles of central and state governments, special purpose vehicles (SPVs), and financing mechanisms including grants, loans, and subordinate debt.

## Objective

- Enable private sector participation in metro rail projects through PPP models
- Ensure standardized safety and operational compliance via Metro Railway Acts
- Promote indigenization and local manufacturing in metro rail procurement
- Facilitate integrated transport planning including first and last mile connectivity
- Establish dedicated urban transport funds for asset replacement and operational sustainability
- Strengthen corporate governance through adherence to DPE, DEA, and CVC guidelines
- Ensure financial sustainability via value engineering, minimum land acquisition, and technological innovation

## Important Conditions

- Central assistance for equity and subordinate debt from GoI is ring-fenced to 20% of total project cost excluding private investment, land, R&R, and taxes
- Cost escalation due to price or exchange rate variations is borne by the State Government
- GoI will not share cost for procurement of additional rolling stock in second and subsequent years of operation
- State/local taxes are not included in project cost to be shared by GoI
- SPV must ensure safety certification through Commissioner of Metro Railway Safety
- Maha-Metro (or equivalent SPV) must adhere to DPE, DEA, and CVC guidelines for corporate governance
- Integrated ticketing and national common mobility card must be prioritized by State Government and SPV
- Dedicated Urban Transport Fund (UTF) must be established by State Government in consultation with MoHUA
