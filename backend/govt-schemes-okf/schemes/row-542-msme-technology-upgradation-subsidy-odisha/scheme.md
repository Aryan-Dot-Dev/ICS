# MSME Technology Upgradation Subsidy – Odisha

## Overview

The MSME Technology Upgradation Subsidy – Odisha is a state-level scheme under the Odisha MSME Development Policy, 2016, aimed at encouraging technology upgradation among Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises to enhance competitiveness, productivity, and quality. It provides financial support for adopting modern technology and improving production processes. The scheme is part of the broader financial support framework of the policy, focusing on technology intervention for MSMEs in Odisha.

## Objective

- Encourage MSMEs to undertake technology upgradation for enhanced competitiveness
- Improve productivity and reduce production costs through modern technology adoption
- Promote environment-friendly production via technology intervention
- Increase awareness of Government of India schemes like CLCSS, QMS, QTT for technology upgradation
- Monitor credit delivery under technology upgradation schemes through the Directorate of Industries

## Eligibility

MSMEs in Odisha are eligible to avail the subsidy for technology upgradation under the Odisha MSME Development Policy, 2016, provided they meet the general eligibility criteria for MSMEs as defined in the policy, including being engaged in manufacturing or service activities and having commenced production after the policy's effective date.
