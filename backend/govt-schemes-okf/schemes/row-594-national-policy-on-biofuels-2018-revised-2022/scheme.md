# National Policy on Biofuels 2018 (Revised 2022)

## Overview

The National Policy on Biofuels 2018 (Revised 2022) aims to promote the use of biofuels in India by setting indicative blending targets for ethanol and biodiesel, encouraging advanced biofuels, and supporting the development of a sustainable biofuel ecosystem through policy interventions, financial incentives, and institutional mechanisms.

## Objective

- Achieve 20% ethanol blending in petrol and 5% biodiesel blending in diesel by 2030
- Promote production and use of advanced biofuels such as 2G ethanol, bio-CNG, and drop-in fuels
- Reduce dependence on imported crude oil and enhance energy security
- Support farmers by providing remunerative prices for surplus biomass and agricultural residues
- Encourage research, development, and deployment of innovative biofuel technologies
- Create employment opportunities in rural areas through biofuel value chains
- Ensure environmental sustainability by reducing greenhouse gas emissions
- Establish a national biofuel coordination committee for effective policy implementation

## Important Conditions

- Financial support is subject to availability of funds and approval by the empowered committee
- Projects must adhere to sustainability criteria and avoid competition with food security
- Benefits under the policy are contingent upon meeting blending obligations set by oil marketing companies
- Advanced biofuel projects require techno-economic clearance and may have longer gestation periods
- Support under Pradhan Mantri JI-VAN Yojana is limited to second-generation (2G) ethanol projects using non-food feedstocks
