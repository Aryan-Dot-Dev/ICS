## Process

1. Identify the appropriate scheme under the National Policy on Biofuels (e.g., Pradhan Mantri JI-VAN Yojana for 2G ethanol).
2. Prepare a detailed project report (DPR) including techno-economic analysis, feedstock availability, and environmental impact assessment.
3. Obtain necessary statutory clearances from pollution control boards, forest department, and other relevant agencies.
4. Apply through the designated nodal agency (e.g., Indian Renewable Energy Development Agency - IREDA for JI-VAN Yojana) via their online portal.
5. Submit the DPR along with required documents such as incorporation certificates, land documents, and promoter details.
6. Await technical and financial appraisal by the nodal agency.
7. Upon approval, sign the agreement and fulfill conditions for disbursement of financial support.
