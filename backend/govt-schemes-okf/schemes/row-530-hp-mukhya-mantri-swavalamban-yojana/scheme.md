# HP Mukhya Mantri Swavalamban Yojana

## Overview

Mukhya Mantri Swavalamban Yojana (MMSY) is a state-level scheme by the Government of Himachal Pradesh aimed at promoting self-employment and entrepreneurship among the youth. It provides financial support in the form of capital and interest subsidies to eligible beneficiaries for setting up industrial, service, trade, or shop units. The scheme targets bonafide Himachali youth aged 18 to 45 years and supports projects in sectors like manufacturing, service, agriculture, animal husbandry, sericulture, and mining.

## Objective

- Promote self-employment and entrepreneurship among youth
- Provide financial support for setting up industrial and service units
- Generate employment opportunities in the state
- Encourage investment in priority sectors like agriculture, animal husbandry, sericulture, and mining
- Support skill development and access to resources for aspiring entrepreneurs
- Foster economic growth through establishment of new enterprises

## Eligibility

The scheme is exclusively for bonafide Himachali candidates. Applicants must be between 18 and 45 years of age at the time of application. They must possess a minimum level of education and have a viable business proposal. The unit must be set up in Himachal Pradesh and the applicant must not have engaged any private agency or middleman for aviling benefits under the scheme.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Beneficiaries are eligible for capital investment subsidy at 25% (30% for women) on plant & machinery and technical civil works, subject to a maximum investment ceiling of Rs. 40 lakh in plant & machinery and total project cost not exceeding Rs. 60 lakh. Additionally, interest subsidy at 5% per annum for 3 years is available on term loans up to Rs. 40 lakh sanctioned by financial institutions.…

## Important Conditions

- Plant and machinery for which payment has been made in cash are not eligible for subsidy
- Subsidy will not be available for the same component twice under any other incentive scheme
- Units must commence commercial production within 6 months (service/trade/shop) or 1 year (manufacturing) from date of approval
- Investment subsidy is released only after physical verification and keeping in TDR for 3 years;
- refund required if unit ceases commercial production within 3 years
- MMSY has not engaged any private party/agency/middlemen/franchise;
- beneficiaries dealing with such agencies do so at their own risk
