## Process

1. Submit application online via the MMSY portal along with Aadhar Card, Bonafide Himachali certificate, and brief project profile including land details.
2. Obtain approval from the District Level Committee (DLC).
3. After approval, apply for loan from a financial institution.
4. Upon loan disbursement by the bank, apply for capital investment subsidy to the General Manager, District Industries Centre (DIC) with required documents.
5. Submit physical inspection report, undertaking, and engineer’s certificate.
6. After verification, the GM DIC issues sanction order for subsidy release.
7. Interest subsidy and CGTMSE fee reimbursement are applied for after commencement of commercial production.
