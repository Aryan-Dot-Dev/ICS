# Delhi Industrial Policy 2021

## Overview

The Delhi Industrial Policy 2021 is a state-level initiative aimed at promoting industrial development, attracting investment, and enhancing the competitiveness of industries in Delhi. It focuses on creating a conducive environment for manufacturing and service sectors through infrastructure development, skill enhancement, and policy support. The policy emphasizes sustainable and inclusive industrial growth aligned with national objectives like Make in India and Atmanirbhar Bharat.

## Objective

- Promote sustainable and inclusive industrial development in Delhi
- Enhance infrastructure and logistics support for industries
- Encourage adoption of advanced technologies and innovation
- Generate employment opportunities through industrial growth
- Attract domestic and foreign investment into Delhi
- Support MSMEs and cluster-based development
- Ensure compliance with environmental and safety standards
- Integrate Delhi’s industrial policy with national initiatives like Make in India

## Eligibility

Industrial units engaged in manufacturing or service activities within the National Capital Territory of Delhi, including MSMEs, large industries, and service enterprises, subject to compliance with zoning regulations, environmental norms, and other statutory requirements as per Delhi government policies.

## Benefits

Financial incentives such as capital subsidy, interest subsidy, and reimbursement of taxes; infrastructure support including land allocation and common facility centers; skill development and training programs; technology upgradation assistance; marketing and export promotion support; and facilitation of single-window clearance for approvals.
