# National AYUSH Mission

## Overview

The National Ayush Mission (NAM) is a centrally sponsored scheme launched by the Ministry of Ayush to promote AYUSH systems of healthcare through cost-effective services, strengthen educational systems, ensure availability of quality raw materials, and sustainably manage medicinal plants. It aims to improve access to AYUSH services, particularly in vulnerable and underserved areas, by upgrading AYUSH hospitals and dispensaries, co-locating AYUSH facilities at primary health centres, and supporting cultivation and conservation of medicinal plants.

## Objective

- Provide cost-effective AYUSH services with universal access through upgrading AYUSH hospitals and dispensaries
- Strengthen institutional capacity at the state level through upgrading AYUSH educational institutions
- Ensure availability of quality raw materials for AYUSH systems by supporting cultivation and conservation of medicinal plants
- Promote sustainable management of medicinal plants resources
- Support setting up of up to 50-bedded integrated AYUSH hospitals
- Co-locate AYUSH facilities at Primary Health Centres (PHCs), Community Health Centres (CHCs), and District Hospitals (DHs)
- Support AYUSH wellness centres including yoga and naturopathy centres
- Develop and promote AYUSH education and research

## Important Conditions

- Funding is contingent on submission of utilization certificates and progress reports
- Cost-sharing ratio varies: 60:40 (Centre:State) for most states, 90:10 for Northeastern and Himalayan states
- Projects must align with scheme guidelines and national AYUSH priorities
- Land for infrastructure must be free from encumbrances and available for government use
