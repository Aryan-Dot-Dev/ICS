# MahaDBT – Direct Benefit Transfer for Entrepreneurs

## Overview

MahaDBT – Direct Benefit Transfer for Entrepreneurs is a state-level scheme in Maharashtra that facilitates the direct transfer of financial benefits to eligible entrepreneurs through the MahaDBT portal. The scheme aims to streamline the disbursement of subsidies, grants, and other financial assistance by linking beneficiary bank accounts with Aadhaar for transparent and efficient delivery. It supports various entrepreneur-focused initiatives under departments like Skill Development, Employment and Entrepreneurship, and others, ensuring timely and traceable benefit transfers.

## Objective

- Ensure direct and transparent transfer of benefits to eligible entrepreneurs
- Eliminate intermediaries and reduce leakage in subsidy disbursement
- Link beneficiary bank accounts with Aadhaar for secure and real-time transfers
- Provide a unified digital platform for applying, tracking, and managing benefits
- Support skill development, employment generation, and entrepreneurship across Maharashtra
- Enable real-time tracking of application status and benefit disbursement
- Promote financial inclusion through Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts
- Streamline renewal and re-application processes for ongoing support

## Important Conditions

- Beneficiary must have an Aadhaar-seeded bank account to receive benefits
- Applications may be rejected if Aadhaar is not mapped to bank account via NPCI mapper
- Benefits are discontinued if the beneficiary fails to meet renewal criteria (e.g., attendance, academic progress)
- Only two children per family are eligible for certain schemes
- Benefits are not available for distance education, virtual learning, or part-time courses
- Applicants must not have availed similar benefits from government or private ITIs previously
- Management quota admissions are not eligible for fee reimbursement
- Beneficiary must attempt every semester or annual exam
- Non-satisfactory educational progress due to misconduct or attendance failure leads to ineligibility
- Account must be active and not closed, frozen, or under litigation
- Aadhaar must be seeded and active in NPCI mapper for successful transaction
