## Process

1. States/UTs conduct a gap analysis of existing infrastructure at HWCs under NHM or other schemes.
2. Prepare a proposal for telemedicine rollout including infrastructure, HR, and budget requirements.
3. Submit proposal to MoHFW under NHM for financial and technical support.
4. Upgrade HWCs (spokes) with required telemedicine infrastructure and train existing manpower (CHO, MPW, ASHA).
5. Establish HUBs at medical colleges or zonal levels with MBBS doctors (contractual) and specialists (daily roster).
6. Deploy the centrally hosted e-Sanjeevani application.
7. Integrate with ABHA, MyHealthRecord, and CPHC-IT application.
8. Launch services and monitor via district/state/centre-level dashboards.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — proposals can be submitted year-round under NHM; service access is available 24x7.
