# e-Sanjeevani Telemedicine Platform for Health Startups

## Overview

eSanjeevani is India's National Telemedicine Service, providing accessible and affordable healthcare through a hub-and-spoke model connecting patients at Ayushman Bharat Health & Wellness Centres (spokes) with doctors and specialists at medical college hubs. It enables remote consultations via smartphones or at wellness centres, supporting Universal Health Coverage by bridging the digital health divide. The platform is ABDM compliant, integrates with Ayushman Bharat Health Account (ABHA), and supports electronic health records, e-prescriptions, and SNOMED CT terminology.

## Objective

- Provide quick and easy access to doctors and medical specialists from smartphones
- Enable access to quality health services remotely via Ayushman Bharat Health & Wellness Centres
- Achieve digital health equity and Universal Health Coverage (UHC)
- Link and manage health records through ABHA ID integration
- Support telemedicine services in Health and Wellness Centres under NHM
- Ensure interoperability and standardization of electronic health records
- Facilitate assisted telemedicine through Community Health Officers at spokes
- Promote affordable and accessible healthcare as envisioned by the Prime Minister

## Eligibility

Patients can access eSanjeevani by visiting the nearest Ayushman Bharat Health & Wellness Centre or using the platform via smartphone with internet connectivity. Healthcare providers (MBBS doctors, specialists, Medical Officers, Community Health Officers) can onboard as providers after training and registration through the NHM-supported hub-and-spoke model.

## Benefits

Affordable and accessible healthcare, remote consultations reducing travel burden, integration with ABHA for health record linkage, e-prescription functionality, support for SNOMED CT and DICOM standards, seamless integration with CPHC-IT application, SMS alerts, comprehensive dashboard for monitoring, and lifetime archival of health records via MyHealthRecord (PHRMS).

## Important Conditions

- ABHA ID must be provided in all consultations for health record linkage
- eSanjeevani complies with EHR Guidelines (2016) and Telemedicine Practice Guidelines (2020)
- Hubs require contractual MBBS doctors and specialist availability on daily roster basis
- States must utilize or upgrade existing IT infrastructure under NHM or other schemes before proposing new infrastructure
- The application is centrally hosted;
- States may propose server requirements in consultation with CDAC-Mohali
