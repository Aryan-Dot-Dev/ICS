# National Water Awards (NWA) Innovation Support

## Overview

The National Water Awards (NWA) Innovation Support is a recognition-based scheme under the Department of Water Resources, River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation, Ministry of Jal Shakti, aimed at promoting innovation in water technology and management. It seeks to identify, encourage, and reward innovative solutions in water conservation, efficiency, and sustainability across sectors. The scheme supports grassroots innovators, startups, and organizations contributing to national water security goals through recognition and visibility.

## Objective

- Identify and promote innovative practices and technologies in water conservation and management
- Encourage grassroots innovation and community-led water security initiatives
- Recognize excellence in water use efficiency, reuse, and recycling across industries and sectors
- Support scaling of proven water innovations through national visibility and networking
- Align with national missions like Jal Sanchay Jan Bhagidari and Namami Gange for holistic water security
- Foster a culture of innovation in the water sector to address challenges of scarcity, pollution, and climate resilience

**startups**, **MSME**, **NGOs**, **research institutions**, **individual innovators**

## Eligibility

Open to individuals, startups, MSMEs, NGOs, research institutions, and organizations across India that have developed innovative solutions in water conservation, efficiency, reuse, recycling, or management. Projects must demonstrate measurable impact, scalability, and sustainability. Preference is given to grassroots and community-driven innovations aligned with national water missions.

## Benefits

Recognition through national awards, certificate of appreciation, media coverage, inclusion in Ministry publications, opportunity to showcase innovations at national events like India International Water Week, and networking with policymakers, investors, and sector experts. ## Important Conditions

- No financial assistance is provided under this scheme
- Recognition does not guarantee future funding or procurement
- Awardees must comply with national laws and environmental regulations
- The Ministry reserves the right to verify claims and withdraw awards in case of misrepresentation
- Innovations must be original and not infringe on intellectual property rights
