## Process

1. Visit the National Water Awards portal at https://jalshakti-dowr.gov.in/nwa
2. Review eligibility criteria and innovation categories
3. Prepare a detailed proposal highlighting the innovation, impact, scalability, and sustainability
4. Submit the application online through the portal with required documents
5. Applications are evaluated by a technical committee
6. Shortlisted candidates may be invited for presentation or field verification
7. Final awardees are selected and announced at the National Water Awards ceremony.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year, with awards announced annually around World Water Day (March 22).
