# Section 80IAC – Tax Exemption for Startups

## Overview

Section 80IAC of the Income Tax Act provides a tax exemption to eligible startups for three consecutive assessment years out of their first ten years from incorporation. The scheme aims to promote innovation and entrepreneurship by reducing the tax burden on startups during their early growth phase. To qualify, startups must obtain recognition from the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) and secure approval from the Inter-Ministerial Board (IMB). The exemption applies to 100% of profits and gains from eligible business activities.

## Objective

- Provide tax relief to startups during their initial years of operation
- Encourage innovation and entrepreneurship in India
- Support early-stage startups by reducing their tax liability
- Promote sustainable business growth through fiscal incentives

## Important Conditions

- The tax exemption under Section 80IAC is contingent upon obtaining prior approval from the Inter-Ministerial Board (IMB)
- The benefit is available for only three consecutive assessment years out of the first ten years from incorporation
- If the turnover exceeds INR 100 crore in any financial year, the startup loses eligibility for future tax benefits under this section
- The startup must continue to meet the innovation and scalability criteria to maintain eligibility
