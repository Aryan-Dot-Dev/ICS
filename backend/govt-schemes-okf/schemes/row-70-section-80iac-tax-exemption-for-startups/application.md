## Process

1. Incorporate the business as a Private Limited Company or LLP.
2. Obtain DPIIT recognition by registering on the Startup India portal (https://www.startupindia.gov.in) and submitting the recognition application.
3. After receiving DPIIT recognition, apply for approval from the Inter-Ministerial Board (IMB) via Form 1 of the Income Tax Rules.
4. Submit Form 1 along with required documents to the IMB through the designated channel.
5. Upon IMB approval, claim the tax exemption under Section 80IAC while filing the income tax return for the relevant assessment year(s).

## Deadlines

- Applications for IMB approval can be made at any time; there is no fixed deadline. However, the tax exemption must be claimed in the income tax return for the relevant assessment year within the time allowed under the Income Tax Act.
