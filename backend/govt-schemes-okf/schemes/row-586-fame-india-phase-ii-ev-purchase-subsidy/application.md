## Process

1. OEMs must register on the FAME India portal and get their vehicle models approved under the scheme.
2. Consumers purchase eligible EV models from registered OEMs.
3. OEMs upload sales data and claim incentives on the FAME portal.
4. The Heavy Industries Ministry verifies the claims and disburses the subsidy amount to the OEMs.
5. The benefit is reflected as a reduced price for the end consumer.

## Deadlines
