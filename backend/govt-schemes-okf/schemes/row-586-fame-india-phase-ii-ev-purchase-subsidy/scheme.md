# FAME India Phase II – EV Purchase Subsidy

## Overview

FAME India Phase II is a Government of India scheme launched to promote faster adoption and manufacturing of electric vehicles by providing upfront purchase incentives to consumers and supporting the development of charging infrastructure. The scheme focuses on incentivizing electric two-wheelers, three-wheelers, four-wheelers, and buses through demand-side subsidies. It aims to reduce vehicular emissions and dependence on fossil fuels by making EVs more affordable.

## Objective

- Incentivize the purchase of electric vehicles to increase their adoption
- Support the development of charging infrastructure across the country
- Reduce dependence on fossil fuels and vehicular emissions
- Promote domestic manufacturing of electric vehicles and components
- Encourage the use of electric vehicles in public and shared transport


## Target Beneficiaries

**individual consumers**, **fleet operators**, **institutional buyers**, **public transport undertakings**

## Eligibility

Electric vehicles that meet the technical specifications and localization criteria as defined under FAME India Phase II are eligible for incentives. The vehicle must be registered in India and purchased from an OEM that has been approved under the scheme. Incentives are available only for vehicles that are not used for commercial transport of goods or passengers unless specifically permitted…


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Upfront reduction in the purchase price of electric vehicles through direct subsidies to buyers. The incentive is claimed by the OEM and passed on to the consumer at the point of sale. The scheme also supports the installation of charging stations and promotes indigenization of EV components.

## Important Conditions

- Incentives are available only for vehicles that meet minimum localization requirements
- Subsidy is subject to availability of funds and may be withdrawn without notice
- The scheme does not cover vehicles used for inter-state transport or goods carriage unless exempted
- Claims must be filed within the stipulated time frame after sale
