# Meghalaya Startup Policy – MeghaStart

## Overview

Meghalaya Startup Policy – MeghaStart is a state-level initiative launched by the Government of Meghalaya to foster innovation, support entrepreneurs, and build a robust startup ecosystem within the state. It aims to provide financial, infrastructural, and regulatory support to early-stage startups, particularly those led by youth, women, and marginalized communities. The policy emphasizes creating an enabling environment through incubation, mentorship, and access to funding. It aligns with national startup goals while addressing regional challenges and opportunities in Meghalaya.

## Objective

- To promote innovation and entrepreneurship among the youth of Meghalaya
- To provide financial assistance and seed funding to early-stage startups
- To establish incubation centers and mentorship networks across the state
- To support women-led and socially disadvantaged entrepreneurs
- To create linkages with national startup programs and investors
- To simplify regulatory processes and provide single-window clearance
- To encourage startups in sectors like agriculture, tourism, handicrafts, and IT
- To monitor and evaluate the impact of supported startups on employment and GDP

## Eligibility

Startups registered in Meghalaya with a minimum of one founder domiciled in the state, incorporated as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm, working on innovative products, services, or processes, and not older than 7 years from the date of incorporation. Preference is given to startups led by women, SC/ST communities, and those operating in priority sectors such as agriculture,…

## Benefits

Financial support in the form of seed grants, access to incubation facilities with infrastructure and internet, mentorship from industry experts and successful entrepreneurs, assistance in IPR filing and patenting, support for participation in national and international startup events, facilitation of market linkages and pilot projects, and guidance on compliance, taxation, and fundraising.

## Important Conditions

- Grant is disbursed in tranches based on achievement of predefined milestones
- Startups must maintain regular reporting and submit utilization certificates
- Funds cannot be used for land acquisition, construction, or purchase of vehicles
- The grant must be utilized within 18 months from the date of first disbursement
- Startups receiving support from other state or central schemes may have reduced eligibility
- Failure to show progress may lead to recovery of disbursed funds
