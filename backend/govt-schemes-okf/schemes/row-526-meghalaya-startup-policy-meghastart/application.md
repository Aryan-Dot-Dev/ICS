## Process

1. Visit the official Meghalaya Startup Policy portal or the designated nodal agency website.
2. Register as a startup and create a profile with basic details.
3. Fill out the online application form with information on the startup, innovation, team, and financial needs.
4. Upload required documents including Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, pitch deck, and founder details.
5. Submit the application for preliminary screening.
6. Shortlisted applicants will be invited for a pitch presentation before the Selection Committee.
7. Upon approval, sign the grant agreement and fulfill pre-disbursal compliance.
8. Receive the first tranche of funds and begin implementation.
9. Submit periodic progress reports and utilization certificates for subsequent tranches.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds.
