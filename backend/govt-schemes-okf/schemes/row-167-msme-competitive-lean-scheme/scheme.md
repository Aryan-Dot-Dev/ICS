# MSME Competitive (LEAN) Scheme

## Overview

The MSME Competitive (LEAN) Scheme is a Government of India initiative aimed at enhancing the competitiveness of Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) through the implementation of Lean Manufacturing practices. The scheme focuses on reducing waste, improving productivity, and increasing operational efficiency in manufacturing units. It provides financial and technical support to MSMEs for adopting Lean tools and techniques, thereby enabling them to compete effectively in domestic and global markets.

## Objective

- To enhance the competitiveness of MSMEs through Lean Manufacturing implementation
- To reduce waste and improve productivity in manufacturing processes
- To increase operational efficiency and profitability of participating units
- To promote sustainable manufacturing practices among MSMEs
- To enable MSMEs to adopt world-class manufacturing standards
- To create awareness and build capacity in Lean methodologies

## Key Features

- Grant/assistance per entity: Up to INR 40 lakhs per MSME unit
- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds.
- Portal: https://lean.msme.gov.in
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

The scheme is open to Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) engaged in manufacturing activities, as defined under the MSMED Act, 2006. The unit must be operational and have a valid Udyam Registration. Priority is given to units willing to implement Lean Manufacturing practices and commit to the required handholding and implementation period.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial assistance in the form of subsidy for implementation of Lean Manufacturing tools, access to expert consultants and trainers, handholding support during implementation, improved productivity and reduced operational costs, enhanced quality and delivery performance, better inventory management, and increased competitiveness in national and international markets.

## Important Conditions

- Financial support is limited to 80% of project cost, with beneficiary contributing 20%
- Maximum financial assistance capped at INR 40 lakhs per unit
- Only manufacturing MSMEs with valid Udyam Registration are eligible
- Subsidy is disbursed only after verification of milestone completion
- Scheme is subject to availability of funds and may be revised or withdrawn by the government
