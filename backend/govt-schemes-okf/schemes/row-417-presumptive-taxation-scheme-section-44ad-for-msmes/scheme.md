# Presumptive Taxation Scheme (Section 44AD) for MSMEs

## Overview

The Presumptive Taxation Scheme under Section 44AD of the Income-tax Act, 1961 is designed for small businesses, including MSMEs, to simplify tax compliance by allowing them to declare income at a prescribed rate (8% of turnover or 6% for digital transactions) without maintaining detailed books of accounts. It applies to eligible taxpayers whose total turnover or gross receipts do not exceed INR 2 crores in a financial year. The scheme reduces the burden of audit and record-keeping while promoting ease of taxation for small enterprises.

## Objective

- Simplify tax compliance for small businesses by eliminating the need to maintain detailed books of accounts
- Reduce the burden of tax audit under Section 44AB for eligible taxpayers
- Promote ease of doing business through a presumptive income taxation model
- Encourage digital transactions by offering a lower presumptive rate (6%) for income received via digital modes
- Broaden the tax base by bringing small traders and service providers into the tax net with minimal compliance

## Important Conditions

- The scheme is not available to Limited Liability Partnerships (LLPs)
- Professionals covered under Section 44AA(1) (e.g., doctors, lawyers, architects) are ineligible
- Businesses earning income from agency, brokerage, or commission are excluded
- If actual profits are higher than the presumptive rate, the taxpayer cannot claim actual expenses
- Once opted out, the taxpayer cannot re-enter the scheme for the next 5 assessment years
