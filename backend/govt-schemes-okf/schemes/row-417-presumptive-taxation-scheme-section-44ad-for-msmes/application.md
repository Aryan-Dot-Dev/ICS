## Process

1. Compute presumptive income at 8% of turnover (6% for digital receipts) under Section 44AD.
2. Ensure total turnover does not exceed INR 2 crores.
3. File Income Tax Return (ITR-4 Sugam) declaring the presumed income.
4. No need to maintain books of accounts or undergo tax audit.
5. Pay advance tax in full by March 15 if applicable.
6. Submit the return before the due date (usually July 31 for non-audit cases).

## Deadlines

- Income Tax Return (ITR-4) must be filed by July 31 of the assessment year for non-audit cases. Advance tax, if applicable, must be paid in full by March 15.
