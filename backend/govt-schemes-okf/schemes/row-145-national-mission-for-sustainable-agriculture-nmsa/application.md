## Process

1. State Governments prepare Annual Action Plans (AAPs) based on fund availability and past performance.
2. AAPs are submitted to the Government of India for approval by the Project Sanctioning Committee.
3. Funds are released in installments to States upon receipt of Utilization Certificates and detailed progress reports.
4. States implement components through district and village-level agencies.
5. Beneficiaries (farmers) access benefits via State-level implementing departments/agencies.
6. Monitoring and evaluation are conducted quarterly by technical personnel and reported to the National Advisory Committee.

## Deadlines

- Annual Action Plan (AAP) submission is required yearly; funds are released based on AAP approval and utilization of previous funds. Revalidation of unspent funds is permitted subject to conditions.
