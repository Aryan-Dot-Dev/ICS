# Khanij Bidesh India Ltd (KABIL) Overseas Mineral Support

## Overview

Khanij Bidesh India Ltd (KABIL) is a joint venture company established by three Central Public Sector Enterprises (CPSEs) under the Ministry of Mines to ensure supply of critical and strategic minerals to the Indian domestic market. The company focuses on identifying, acquiring, developing, processing, and commercializing overseas mineral assets of strategic and commercial importance to India. KABIL aims to build a sustainable mineral resource base for the country through overseas investments and partnerships.

## Objective

- Ensure supply of critical and strategic minerals to the Indian domestic market
- Identify, acquire, develop, process, and commercialize overseas mineral assets
- Build a sustainable mineral resource base for India through overseas investments
- Enhance mineral security and reduce import dependence
- Facilitate joint ventures and partnerships in mineral-rich countries
- Promote value addition and downstream processing of minerals
