## Process

1. Obtain DPIIT recognition for the startup via the Startup India portal.
2. Ensure the startup is engaged in specified transactions attracting TCS.
3. Furnish Form 27EQ or relevant TCS exemption certificate to the collector of tax.
4. The collector shall not collect TCS if a valid exemption certificate is furnished.
5. Maintain documentation for audit and compliance purposes.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications can be submitted throughout the financial year as and when TCS-liable transactions occur.
