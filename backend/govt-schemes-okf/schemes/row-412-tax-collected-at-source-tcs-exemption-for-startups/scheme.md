# Tax Collected at Source (TCS) Exemption for Startups

## Overview

Tax Collected at Source (TCS) Exemption for Startups is a tax benefit under the Income Tax Act, 1961 that allows eligible startups to be exempt from TCS on certain specified transactions, reducing their compliance burden and improving cash flow during early growth stages.

## Objective

- Reduce tax compliance burden on startups
- Improve working capital availability for early-stage enterprises
- Encourage formalization and digital transactions among startups
- Support ease of doing business for innovative ventures


## Target Beneficiaries

**startups**


Concept links: [entrepreneur](../../concepts/entrepreneur.md)


## Key Features

- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — applications can be submitted throughout the financial year as and when TCS-liable transactions occur.
- Portal: https://www.startupindia.gov.in/
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

Startups recognized by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) under the Startup India initiative are eligible for TCS exemption on specified goods and services as per the provisions of the Income Tax Act, 1961.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Exemption from Tax Collected at Source (TCS) on specified transactions such as sale of overseas tour packages, foreign remittances, and sale of alcohol, thereby reducing upfront tax outflow and improving liquidity for eligible startups.


Structured benefit objects: [benefits.md](benefits.md).

## Important Conditions

- TCS exemption is applicable only on specified goods and services as notified under the Income Tax Act, 1961
- Exemption is valid only for DPIIT-recognized startups
- The collector of tax must be furnished with a valid exemption certificate before the transaction
- Exemption does not apply if the startup exceeds turnover or age limits defined under Startup India policy
