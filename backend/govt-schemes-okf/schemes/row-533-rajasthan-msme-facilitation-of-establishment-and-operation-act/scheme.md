# Rajasthan MSME (Facilitation of Establishment and Operation) Act

## Overview

The Rajasthan MSME (Facilitation of Establishment and Operation) Act is a state-level initiative aimed at promoting and supporting Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) in Rajasthan by simplifying regulatory processes and providing facilitation for establishment and operation. The scheme focuses on creating a conducive business environment through policy support, infrastructure development, and access to financial and non-financial benefits. It targets entrepreneurs and existing MSME units seeking to start or scale operations within the state. The implementing agency is the Department of Industries and Commerce, Government of Rajasthan.

## Objective

- Facilitate the establishment and operation of MSMEs in Rajasthan
- Simplify regulatory and compliance procedures for MSMEs
- Promote industrial growth and employment generation through MSME development
- Provide access to financial incentives, infrastructure, and business support services
- Strengthen the MSME ecosystem via policy interventions and institutional support

## Eligibility

The scheme applies to Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises as defined under the MSME Act, 2006, engaged in manufacturing or service activities, and registered or proposing to register in the state of Rajasthan. Enterprises must comply with relevant statutory requirements and possess valid documentation such as Udyam Registration, GSTIN, and other applicable licenses.

## Benefits

Benefits include facilitation in obtaining clearances and approvals, access to industrial infrastructure and land allocation, priority in government procurement, financial incentives such as subsidies on power, interest, and capital investment, skill development support, and marketing assistance.
