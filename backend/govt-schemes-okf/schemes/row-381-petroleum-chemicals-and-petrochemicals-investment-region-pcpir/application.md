## Process

1. State government submits an in-principle application to DPIIT in the prescribed format.
2. DPIIT examines the application and forwards it to the Commerce & Industry Minister for in-principle approval.
3. Upon in-principle approval, the state government submits a detailed application for final approval along with required documents.
4. The application is reviewed by a High-level Committee chaired by the Secretary, DPIIT.
5. Final approval is granted by the Commerce & Industry Minister.
6. The NIMZ is notified in the official gazette by DPIIT.
