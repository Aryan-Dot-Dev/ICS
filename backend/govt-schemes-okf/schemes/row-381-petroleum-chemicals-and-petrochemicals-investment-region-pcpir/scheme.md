# Petroleum, Chemicals and Petrochemicals Investment Region (PCPIR)

## Overview

The Petroleum, Chemicals and Petrochemicals Investment Region (PCPIR) is a government initiative aimed at promoting investment in the chemicals and petrochemicals sector by developing integrated investment regions with world-class infrastructure. It seeks to create a conducive environment for manufacturing and export-oriented units through policy support, infrastructure development, and fiscal incentives. The scheme encourages public-private partnerships and aims to boost domestic value addition and employment generation in the sector.

## Objective

- Promote investment in chemicals and petrochemicals sector
- Develop integrated investment regions with infrastructure
- Enhance export competitiveness of domestic industries
- Generate employment in the manufacturing sector
- Encourage public-private partnerships for infrastructure development
- Support technology upgradation and innovation in the sector

## Eligibility

Eligible entities include companies, consortia, or special purpose vehicles (SPVs) proposing to set up units in the chemicals, petrochemicals, and petroleum sectors within the designated PCPIR areas. The project must involve substantial investment and contribute to value addition and employment generation.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Benefits include fiscal incentives such as tax exemptions, customs duty concessions, and reimbursement of state taxes; infrastructure support like road, rail, port, and utility linkages; single-window clearance for approvals; and facilitation of land acquisition and statutory clearances through the SPV mechanism.
