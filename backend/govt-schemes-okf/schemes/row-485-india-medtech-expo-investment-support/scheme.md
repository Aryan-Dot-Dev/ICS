# India MedTech Expo & Investment Support

## Overview

India MedTech Expo & Investment Support is a platform designed to showcase innovations in the medical technology sector, connect stakeholders, and promote investment opportunities in MedTech. The event facilitates networking between startups, investors, industry players, and government bodies to accelerate the growth of the MedTech ecosystem in India. It aims to highlight cutting-edge technologies, foster collaborations, and support the scaling of MedTech ventures through exposure and matchmaking.

## Objective

- Showcase innovations and advancements in the medical technology sector
- Facilitate networking and collaboration between startups, investors, and industry stakeholders
- Promote investment opportunities in the MedTech ecosystem
- Support the growth and scaling of MedTech startups and MSMEs
- Highlight government initiatives and policies supporting healthcare innovation
