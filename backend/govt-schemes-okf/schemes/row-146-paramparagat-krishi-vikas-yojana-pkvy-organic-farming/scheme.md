# Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY) – Organic Farming

## Overview

Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY) – Organic Farming is a sub-scheme under the National Mission on Sustainable Agriculture (NMSA) aimed at promoting organic farming through cluster-based approaches and Participatory Guarantee System (PGS) certification. It supports farmers in adopting organic practices, improving soil health, and accessing domestic and export markets for organic produce. The scheme emphasizes capacity building, input support, market linkage, and certification assistance to ensure sustainable agricultural livelihoods.

## Objective

- Promote organic farming through cluster approach and PGS certification
- Improve soil health and fertility through organic practices
- Enhance farmer income via premium prices for organic produce
- Develop domestic and export markets for organic products
- Provide financial and technical assistance for organic input production
- Strengthen certification and quality assurance mechanisms
- Encourage sustainable agricultural practices and reduce chemical dependency

## Eligibility

Farmer groups, self-help groups, producers, and other collectives engaged in agriculture and willing to adopt organic farming practices under the Participatory Guarantee System (PGS-India) are eligible. Individual farmers can also benefit through group participation. Preference is given to small and marginal farmers, women farmers, and those from SC/ST communities.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial assistance for organic input production, certification, labeling, packaging, and marketing; capacity building and training on organic practices; exposure visits; handholding support for certification; market linkage development; and access to organic premium markets. Non-financial benefits include technical guidance, quality assurance, and institutional support for PGS certification.

## Important Conditions

- Financial support is provided only to organized farmer groups, not individuals directly
- Benefits are contingent upon adherence to PGS-India standards and active participation in group activities
- Certification is subject to verification and compliance with organic production norms
- Support is limited to a maximum of 3 years per beneficiary group
