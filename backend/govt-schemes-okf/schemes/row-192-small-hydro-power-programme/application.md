## Process

1. Identify a potential small hydro site and prepare a Detailed Project Report (DPR) as per CEA guidelines.
2. Obtain necessary clearances from concerned authorities (e.g., forest, environment, irrigation).
3. Submit the DPR along with application to the State Nodal Agency (SNA).
4. SNA forwards the proposal to MNRE for appraisal.
5. MNRE appraises the proposal through the Project Appraisal Committee (PAC).
6. Upon approval, administrative sanction is issued by MNRE.
7. Funds are released in instalments based on project progress and submission of utilization certificates.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted throughout the year.
