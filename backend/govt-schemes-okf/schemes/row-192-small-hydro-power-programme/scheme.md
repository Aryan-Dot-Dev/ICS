# Small Hydro Power Programme

## Overview

The Small Hydro Power Programme (SHP) under the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) supports the development of small hydro projects (up to 25 MW capacity) to enhance renewable energy capacity in India. The scheme provides Central Financial Assistance (CFA) for project implementation, including survey and investigation, and aims to promote clean energy generation in remote and hilly areas. It is implemented in coordination with State Nodal Agencies and focuses on grid-connected and off-grid applications.

## Objective

- Promote development of small hydro power projects (up to 25 MW) for renewable energy generation
- Enhance energy access in remote, hilly, and inaccessible regions through decentralized power
- Support survey and investigation of potential small hydro sites
- Encourage private and public sector participation in small hydro development
- Reduce dependence on fossil fuels and contribute to India's clean energy targets
- Strengthen grid connectivity and stability in rural and mountainous areas


## Target Beneficiaries

**state governments**, **private developers**, **public sector undertakings**, **cooperative societies**

## Eligibility

Eligible entities include State Governments, State Power Utilities, Public Sector Undertakings, private developers, and cooperative societies. Projects must have a capacity of up to 25 MW and be technically feasible as per CEA guidelines. Preference is given to projects in hilly and remote areas. The implementing agency must submit a Detailed Project Report (DPR) and obtain necessary clearances.

## Benefits

Financial support through Central Financial Assistance (CFA) for survey, investigation, and project implementation. Technical guidance from MNRE and its institutes. Facilitation of clearances and approvals. Promotion of renewable energy in underserved regions. Contribution to national renewable energy targets and reduction in greenhouse gas emissions.

## Important Conditions

- Projects exceeding 25 MW capacity are not eligible under SHP
- CFA is subject to availability of funds and approval by the Project Appraisal Committee
- Beneficiaries must submit utilization certificates and progress reports for subsequent instalments
- Projects must be commissioned within the stipulated timeline;
- extensions require approval with penalties
