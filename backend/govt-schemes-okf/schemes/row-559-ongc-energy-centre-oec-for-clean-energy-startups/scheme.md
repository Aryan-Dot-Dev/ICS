# ONGC Energy Centre (OEC) for Clean Energy Startups

## Overview

The ONGC Energy Centre (OEC) for Clean Energy Startups is an initiative by ONGC to support and nurture startups working in the clean energy sector. It provides a platform for innovation, mentorship, and collaboration to accelerate the development of sustainable energy solutions. The programme focuses on fostering entrepreneurship in areas such as renewable energy, energy efficiency, and emerging clean technologies. OEC aims to bridge the gap between research and commercialization by offering startups access to resources, expertise, and networking opportunities.

## Objective

- To support and nurture startups working in the clean energy sector
- To provide mentorship and guidance to early-stage clean energy ventures
- To facilitate access to ONGC’s resources, expertise, and infrastructure
- To promote innovation in renewable energy, energy efficiency, and emerging clean technologies
- To create a platform for collaboration between startups, industry, and research institutions
- To accelerate the commercialization of clean energy solutions developed by startups

## Eligibility

Startups incorporated in India working on innovative solutions in the clean energy domain, including renewable energy, energy storage, energy efficiency, and related technologies, are eligible to apply. The startup must be registered under the Companies Act, 2013, or as an LLP or partnership firm, and should not be more than 5 years old at the time of application.
