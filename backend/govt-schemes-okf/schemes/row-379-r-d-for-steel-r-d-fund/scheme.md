# R&D for Steel (R&D Fund)

## Overview

The Ministry of Steel provides financial assistance through the Government funded scheme 'Promotion of Research and Development in the steel sector' to pursue R&D addressing technological issues faced by the sector and for indigenous development of processes/technologies. The thrust areas include development of new alternate processes & technologies to address challenges such as climate change (green steel production, H2 based steel production, CCUS etc.), waste utilization, and resource efficiency. The scheme supports R&D work at lab scale, bench scale, and scale-up to pilot/demonstration plants, with financial assistance varying by applicant type: up to 50% for industrial/commercial organisations, up to 70% for academic institutions and national/regional research laboratories, and up to 40% for pilot/demonstration scale projects with the balance met by industrial partners. Joint proposals with other laboratories/institutions/industry are encouraged, and individual innovators and start-ups in the steel sector are granted access to scheme-funded facilities and equipment.

## Objective

- To catalyze innovation, sustainability, and global competitiveness in the steel sector by funding cutting-edge research and development initiatives.
- To empower industry, academia, start-ups, and individual innovators to collaboratively address technological challenges.
- To enhance resource efficiency and accelerate the adoption of advanced materials and processes for a resilient and future-ready steel ecosystem.
- To promote Research & Development in the Iron & Steel Sector by providing financial support to stakeholders to address R&D needs of national importance.
- To develop innovative/path breaking technologies for utilization of iron ore fines and non-coking coal.
- To beneficiate raw materials like iron ore, coal etc. and agglomeration.
- To improve quality of steel produced through various routes of steel making including the Induction Furnace route.
- To develop commercially viable technology for utilization of steel plant and mine wastes including LD/EAF/IF Slag.
- To develop indigenous technologies for improved processes and product development aimed at reducing import dependency.
- To pursue R&D on steel intensive design of building/bridges/structures to increase usage/consumption of steel.
- To develop innovative solutions for addressing challenges faced by the iron & steel industry.
- To pursue R&D on any other subject of national importance concerning the Iron & Steel sector.

## Important Conditions

- Basic research should not be funded or should be funded in much less quantum & numbers through this scheme as there are other agencies of the Government to promote basic research like Dept.
- of Science & Technology.
- Researchers may be asked to submit their proposal jointly in collaboration with one or more industry partners in which the industry partner is willing to fund at least 15% of the research expenditure and in return may claim the right of first use of the research outcomes.
- The R&D Implementing Agency must submit beforehand the road map for commercialisation that they will take up on successful completion of the R&D project and indicate the stakeholders to be associated by them for the commercialisation activities.
- The Grantee institution may use the IPR arising out of the Ministry funded project while the patent may be filed in the name(s) of inventor(s)/ the institution(s).
- In cases of collaborative projects, the IP rights would be appropriately shared among the collaborators.
- For Intellectual Property proposed for commercial usage, the provisions of licensing may be mutually agreed upon with the grantee institution(s) possessing the IPR.
- The Ministry shall be duly informed by the grantee institution about the patents filed/granted and IPR emerging out of the R&D project on yearly basis during the project tenure and following 5 years period after completion of the project.
- In case of any commercial event like trading/ selling/ transferring /licensing of IP rights, the grantee institution shall provide information to the Ministry within a period of 6 weeks from the closure of the agreement.
- While licensing of patents/transfer or commercialization of technology, the grantee institution shall ensure that the interests of India and its citizens are fully protected.
- The institution is permitted to retain the benefits and earnings arising out of the IPR for plough back to conduct research/research related activities.
- Notwithstanding the above, the Ministry reserves the right to take over ownership of the rights of IP in case of national priority without any compensation to the grantee institution.
