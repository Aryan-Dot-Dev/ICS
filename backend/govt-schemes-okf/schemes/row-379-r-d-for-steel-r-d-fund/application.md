## Process

1. Applicants are requested to go through guidelines, formats and terms & conditions given as Annexures before submitting the R&D Proposals.
2. R&D Proposals duly signed and forwarded by Head of the Institutions/Organisations/Companies should be uploaded to the Ministry of Steel R & D portal (https://research.steel.gov.in/Applicant) as well as sent in Pdf to mosteel-rd@gov.in.
3. For further queries, contact Technical Division, Ministry of Steel at Tel: 011-26773364.
4. Submission of R&D proposals is continuous and there is no last date for submission of the proposals (open ended).

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round.
