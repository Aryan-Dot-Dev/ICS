# TIDCO Venture Capital Fund

## Overview

TIDCO Venture Capital Fund is a state-level initiative by the Tamil Nadu Industrial Development Corporation Limited (TIDCO) to support startups and emerging sector enterprises through equity investments. The fund operates as part of TIDCO's broader mandate to promote industrial growth and innovation in Tamil Nadu via joint ventures and financial support mechanisms. It targets innovative and high-growth enterprises, sunrise sectors, startups, and MSMEs with up to Rs 250 Cr revenues. The fund is SEBI-registered as a CAT-I AIF and has an anchor investment of Rs. 100 Cr from the Government of Tamil Nadu, sponsored by TIDCO, TIDEL, and managed by TNIFMC.

## Objective

- Invest in innovative and high growth enterprises
- Focus on sunrise and emerging sectors
- Support startups and MSMEs (up to Rs 250 Cr revenues)
- Address market gaps with innovation, technology, or cost efficiency
