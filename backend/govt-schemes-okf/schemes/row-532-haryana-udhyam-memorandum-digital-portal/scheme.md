# Haryana Udhyam Memorandum Digital Portal

## Overview

The Haryana Udhyam Memorandum Digital Portal (HUM) is a single portal launched by the Directorate of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprise (MSME), Haryana for registration of all types of enterprises and businesses operating in Haryana. The portal aims to create and maintain a centralised consolidated repository of all enterprises and businesses in the state to aid effective decision making. Enterprises and businesses in Haryana, irrespective of size and type, can register and obtain an Udhyam ID by following the procedure on the HUM Registration Portal.

## Objective

- Create a centralised consolidated repository of all enterprises and businesses operating in Haryana
- Enable effective decision making for the State through the repository
- Provide a single portal for registration of all types of enterprises and businesses in Haryana
- Issue Udhyam ID to registered enterprises and businesses
