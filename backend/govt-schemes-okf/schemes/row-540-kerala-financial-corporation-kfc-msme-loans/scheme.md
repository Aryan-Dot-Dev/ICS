# Kerala Financial Corporation (KFC) MSME Loans

## Overview

Kerala Financial Corporation (KFC) MSME Loans is a state-level financial assistance program providing various loan schemes to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) in Kerala. The corporation offers term loans, working capital loans, and specialized schemes for startups, agro-based MSMEs, government contractors, tourism, infrastructure, and traditional fishermen. KFC aims to boost industrial growth in Kerala by strengthening the MSME sector through financial support up to Rs.50 crore with moratorium periods up to 3 years and repayment periods up to 10 years.

## Objective

- Boost industrial growth in the state by strengthening the MSME industrial sector
- Provide financial assistance to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises in manufacturing and service sector
- Support startups in all stages of their growth from proof of concept to scaling up
- Assist agro-based MSMEs in agriculture sector
- Provide loans to government contractors and MSMEs for executing work orders
- Finance tourism sector and infrastructure development projects
- Support traditional fishermen to procure mechanized deep sea fishing vessels
- Offer working capital assistance to MSMEs at competitive rates

## Important Conditions

- Interest subvention under KAMS and CMEDP limited to first five years
- Moratorium period varies by scheme (6 months to 3 years)
- Repayment period varies by scheme (1 year to 10 years)
- Promoter's contribution required (10% minimum for most schemes)
- Minimum credit rating of 40% required for most schemes
- CIBIL score more than 650 required for CMEDP
- Age limit for promoters: 50 years general, 55 years for SC/ST/women/NRK
- Promoters should not be permanently employed
- Loan amounts vary by scheme and purpose (e.g., Rs.25 lakh for productisation, Rs.50 lakh for commercialization, Rs.100 lakh for scaling up under Startup Kerala)
- Working capital loans require minimum promoter contribution of 25%
- Term loans for industrial activities require promoter contribution of 33.33% for new units
- Financing construction activities requires promoter contribution of 33.33%
