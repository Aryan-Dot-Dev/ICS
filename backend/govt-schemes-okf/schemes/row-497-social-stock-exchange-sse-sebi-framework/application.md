## Process

1. Determine eligibility as a social enterprise or voluntary organization under SEBI-defined social welfare activities.
2. Ensure registration under applicable Indian law (e.g., Societies Act, Trusts Act, Section 8 Company).
3. Prepare required disclosures including object clause, audited financials, and governance structure.
4. Appoint a merchant banker or lead manager registered with SEBI.
5. File draft offer document with SEBI for review.
6. Address SEBI observations and obtain final approval.
7. Issue securities and list on the Social Stock Exchange platform of a recognized stock exchange (e.g., BSE or NSE).
8. Comply with ongoing reporting and disclosure requirements post-listing.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications can be submitted throughout the year as per SEBI timetable for issue of capital.
