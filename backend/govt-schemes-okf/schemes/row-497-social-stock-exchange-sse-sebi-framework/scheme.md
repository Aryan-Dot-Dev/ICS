# Social Stock Exchange (SSE) – SEBI Framework

## Overview

The Social Stock Exchange (SSE) is a platform established by SEBI to facilitate fundraising for social enterprises and voluntary organizations through the issuance of securities such as zero-coupon zero-principal instruments (ZCZPIs) and other innovative financial products. It aims to channel capital toward social welfare activities by enabling transparent, regulated, and efficient fundraising mechanisms. The SSE operates under SEBI's regulatory framework and is designed to support entities engaged in eligible social welfare activities as defined by SEBI.

## Objective

- To provide a regulated platform for social enterprises to raise capital
- To enable issuance of zero-coupon zero-principal instruments (ZCZPIs) for fundraising
- To enhance transparency and accountability in social sector funding
- To channel investments toward eligible social welfare activities
- To promote investor participation in social impact initiatives
- To establish a standardized framework for listing and trading of social securities

## Important Conditions

- Only entities engaged in eligible social welfare activities as defined by SEBI can list
- Zero-coupon zero-principal instruments (ZCZPIs) do not pay interest or principal repayment
- Continuous compliance with SEBI (SSE) disclosure norms is mandatory post-listing
- Listing does not guarantee fund raising;
- success depends on investor demand
- Tax benefits for investors are subject to provisions of Income Tax Act provisions and not guaranteed
