# Integrated Cold Chain and Value Addition Infrastructure

## Overview

The Integrated Cold Chain and Value Addition Infrastructure scheme is a component of the Pradhan Mantri Kisan SAMPADA Yojana (PMKSY) aimed at creating modern infrastructure for cold chain and value addition from farm gate to retail outlet. It supports setting up of integrated cold chain projects including irradiation units, cold storage, and value addition facilities to reduce post-harvest losses, increase processing levels, and enhance exports. The scheme provides financial assistance in the form of grants-in-aid for infrastructure development in the food processing sector.

## Objective

- Create modern infrastructure with efficient supply chain management from farm gate to retail outlet
- Provide a big boost to the growth of food processing sector in the country
- Help in providing better returns to farmers and double farmers' income
- Create huge employment opportunities especially in rural areas
- Reduce wastage of agricultural produce
- Increase the processing level of perishable commodities
- Enhance the export of processed foods

## Important Conditions

- The scheme has been discontinued for some components (Mega Food Park, Creation of Backward & Forward Linkages, Human Resource & Institution - Promotional Activities, Skill Development & HACCP) in the 15th Finance Commission cycle with provision for committed liabilities only.
- Funds are released only upon strict compliance with approval letter terms and conditions.
- Surety Bond is not required to be executed by Central/State Government Departments.
- In case of registered leasehold land, duration of lease should not be less than 15 years.
- Blacklisting/debarring may occur for misuse of grant-in-aid (e.g., M/s Paras Spices, M/s Vision Fresh and Frozen).
