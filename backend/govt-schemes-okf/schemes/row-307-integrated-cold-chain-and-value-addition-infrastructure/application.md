## Process

1. Submit Expression of Interest (EOI) as per notices issued under the scheme.
2. Attend pre-bid meetings as scheduled.
3. Submit detailed proposals in response to EOI or RFP for engagement of PMA.
4. Undergo technical and project approval committee review.
5. Receive approval letter from Inter-Ministerial Approval Committee (IMAC).
6. Fulfill compliance requirements for first instalment: submit acceptance letter, compliance documents, geo-tagged photographs, building plan, and proof of 50% equity and term loan expenditure.
7. Submit utilization certificate and related documents for second instalment.
8. Obtain NABL accreditation for food testing laboratories where applicable.
9. Submit proof of project completion and commercialization.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — EOIs are invited periodically with specific closing dates (e.g., EoI dated 20.05.2026 for irradiation units, EoI dated 27.05.2025 for multiproduct food irradiation units).
