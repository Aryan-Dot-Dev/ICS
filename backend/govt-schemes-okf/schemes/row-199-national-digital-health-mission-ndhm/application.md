## Process

1. For citizens: Visit https://abha.abdm.gov.in/register to generate an ABHA number.
2. For health facilities: Visit https://facility.abdm.gov.in/ to register on the Health Facility Registry.
3. For healthcare professionals: Visit https://hpr.abdm.gov.in/en to register on the Healthcare Professionals Registry.
4. For health tech companies: Integrate and validate software systems with ABDM APIs via the ABDM Sandbox at https://sandbox.abdm.gov.in/.
5. For states: Refer to https://abdm.gov.in/resources/for-states for strategic overview and technical guidelines, and check adoption status at https://dashboard.abdm.gov.in/abdm/.
6. For e-Sushrut Clinic registration: Visit https://ehmis-lite.in/AHIMSG5/hissso/logoutLogin, generate or validate HPR ID, generate or validate HFR ID, and complete onboarding.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round.
