# Scheme for Promotion of Manufacturing of Electronic Components (SPECS)

## Overview

The Scheme for Promotion of Manufacturing of Electronic Components and Semiconductors (SPECS) is a financial incentive scheme launched by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) to promote domestic manufacturing of electronic components and semiconductors. It aims to reduce import dependency and strengthen India's position in the global electronics value chain by providing financial support to eligible applicants setting up new manufacturing units or expanding existing ones.

## Objective

- Promote domestic manufacturing of electronic components and semiconductors
- Reduce import dependency in the electronics sector
- Attract investments in the electronics manufacturing value chain
- Enhance competitiveness of Indian electronics manufacturers globally
- Create employment opportunities in the electronics manufacturing sector
- Support Make in India and Atmanirbhar Bharat initiatives
