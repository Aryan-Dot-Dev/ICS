## Process

1. Applicants must register on the National Single Window System (NSWS) portal. 2.0 the application form along with detailed project report, incorporation documents, board resolution, and details of proposed investment.
2. The application is scrutinized by the Project Management Agency (PMA) appointed by MeitY.
3. Upon approval, a Letter of Approval is issued.
4. After commencement of commercial production, the applicant submits claims for reimbursement with supporting documents such as chartered accountant certificates, bank statements, and invoices.
5. Claims are verified by the PMA and subsidy is disbursed to the applicant's bank account.

## Deadlines

- The scheme was open for applications from 1st April 20 to 2023. No further applications are being accepted as the scheme has reached its financial closure.
