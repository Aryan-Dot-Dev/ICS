# Scheme for Agro-Marine Processing & Development (SAMPADA)

## Overview

The Scheme for Agro-Marine Processing and Development (SAMPADA) was approved by the Government of India on 03.05.2017 with a total allocation of Rs. 6000 crore for the implementation period 2016-2020, later renamed as Pradhan Mantri Kisan SAMPADA Yojana (PMKSY). It is a comprehensive package aimed at creating modern infrastructure with efficient supply chain management from farm gate to retail outlet, boosting food processing sector growth, providing better returns to farmers, doubling farmers' income, creating employment opportunities in rural areas, reducing agricultural wastage, increasing processing levels, and enhancing exports of processed foods. The scheme has seven component schemes including Mega Food Parks, Integrated Cold Chain, Infrastructure for Agro-Processing Clusters, Creation of Backward and Forward Linkages, Creation/Expansion of Food Processing & Preservation Capacities, Food Safety and Quality Assurance Infrastructure, and Human Resources and Institutions.

## Objective

- Creation of modern infrastructure with efficient supply chain management from farm gate to retail outlet
- Provide a big boost to the growth of food processing sector in the country
- Help in providing better returns to farmers
- Double farmers' income
- Create huge employment opportunities especially in rural areas
- Reduce wastage of agricultural produce
- Increase the processing level of agricultural produce
- Enhance the export of processed foods

## Important Conditions

- The scheme was extended till 31.03.2026 with an allocation of Rs. 4600 crore
- Component schemes of Mega Food Park, Creation of Backward & Forward Linkages, Human Resource & Institution - Promotional Activities, Skill Development & HACCP have been discontinued in the 15th Finance Commission cycle with provision of committed liability
- Surety Bond is not required for Central/State Government Departments
- Lease duration for registered leasehold land should not be less than 15 years
- Financial assistance is subject to compliance with terms and conditions in the approval letter
