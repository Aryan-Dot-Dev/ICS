## Process

1. Visit the PMKSY portal or relevant component scheme portal.
2. Check eligibility and guidelines for the specific component scheme.
3. Prepare required documents including project report, land documents, financial statements, etc.
4. Submit Expression of Interest (EOI) or proposal through the online portal.
5. Await scrutiny and evaluation by Technical Committee and Project Approval Committee.
6. Upon approval, submit detailed project report and bank details.
7. Receive approval letter and fulfill pre-disbursement compliances.
8. Receive first instalment (50%) after submission of acceptance letter and compliance documents.
9. Complete project and submit utilization certificate, bank statements, and other required documents.
10. Receive second instalment (50%) after verification of utilization and completion.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round as per EOI notices and scheme guidelines.
