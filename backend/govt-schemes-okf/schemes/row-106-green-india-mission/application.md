## Process

1. State Forest Departments prepare Annual Plans of Operations (APOs) based on the perspective plan under GIM.
2. APOs are submitted to the National Mission Authority for appraisal and approval.
3. Upon approval, funds are released in installments to the states.
4. States implement the projects through Joint Forest Management Committees (JFMCs), Village Forest Committees (VFCs), and other local institutions.
5. Progress is monitored through periodic reporting and field visits.
6. Utilization certificates and audited statements are submitted for subsequent fund releases.

## Deadlines

- Annual planning cycle — APOs are typically submitted before the start of each financial year for approval.
