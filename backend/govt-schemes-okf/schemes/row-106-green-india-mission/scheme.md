# Green India Mission

## Overview

The Green India Mission (GIM) is one of the eight missions under the National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC) launched by the Government of India. It aims to protect, restore, and enhance India's forest cover and respond to climate change through a combination of adaptation and mitigation measures. The mission focuses on increasing forest/tree cover on 5 million hectares of forest/non-forest lands and improving the quality of forest cover on another 5 million hectares. It emphasizes ecosystem services, biodiversity conservation, and livelihood improvement for forest-dependent communities.

## Objective

- Increase forest/tree cover to the extent of 5 million hectares (mha) and improve quality of forest/tree cover on another 5 mha of forest/non-forest lands
- Improve ecosystem services including carbon sequestration and storage, hydrological services, and biodiversity
- Increase forest-based livelihood income for about 3 million households
- Enhance carbon dioxide sequestration by 50-60 million tonnes by 2020
- Promote holistic development through convergence with other missions and schemes
- Strengthen capacity building and training for stakeholders
- Ensure monitoring and evaluation through robust mechanisms

## Important Conditions

- Funds are released subject to satisfactory utilization of previous installments and submission of utilization certificates
- Projects must conform to the approved perspective plan and annual plans of operations
- Afforestation activities must use native and ecologically appropriate species
- Land tenure and community rights must be respected;
- no activities on disputed or private land without consent
- Mission convergence with other schemes (e.g., MGNREGA, CAMPA) is encouraged for optimal resource use
