## Process

1. Obtain DPIIT recognition as a startup via the Startup India portal.
2. Prepare the patent application with Form 1 (Application for Grant of Patent) and Form 2 (Provisional/Complete Specification).
3. Fill and submit Form 28 (Declaration for Startups) to avail of startup benefits.
4. File the application online through the IP India e-filing portal or physically at the appropriate patent office.
5. Pay the prescribed fees (with 80% rebate applicable for startups).
6. Request expedited examination by filing Form 18A (Request for Expedited Examination).
7. Track application status via the IP India portal.
8. Respond to examination reports (FER) within the prescribed time.
9. Upon compliance, the patent proceeds to grant and publication.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round.
