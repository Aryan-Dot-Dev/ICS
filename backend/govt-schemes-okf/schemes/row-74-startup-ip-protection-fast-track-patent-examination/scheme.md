# Startup IP Protection – Fast Track Patent Examination

## Overview

The Startup IP Protection – Fast Track Patent Examination scheme is designed to expedite the patent examination process for startups in India. It enables eligible startups to avail of accelerated examination of their patent applications, reducing the time taken for grant. The scheme is part of the broader Intellectual Property Protection (SIPP) initiative under the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT).

## Objective

- To reduce the time taken for patent examination for startups
- To encourage innovation and IP filing among startups
- To provide expedited examination under the SIPP scheme
- To support startups in securing early patent rights
- To align with national goals of fostering innovation and entrepreneurship

**startups**

## Eligibility

Startups recognized by DPIIT are eligible to avail of the fast track patent examination facility under the Startup Intellectual Property Protection (SIPP) scheme. The applicant must be a DPIIT-recognized startup and must submit Form 28 (Declaration for Startups) along with the patent application.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Expedited examination of patent applications, leading to faster grant of patents. Startups also benefit from an 80% rebate on official fees for patents, designs, and trademarks under the SIPP scheme. The facility helps in early protection of innovations, which is crucial for attracting investment and commercialization.

## Important Conditions

- The 80% fee rebate is available only for DPIIT-recognized startups
- Form 28 must be submitted to avail of startup benefits
- Expedited examination under Form 18A is not available in physical filing mode;
- only e-filing is allowed
- The benefit is available only for a limited period as per the startup's eligibility (turnover < INR 100 Cr and age < 10 years)
- The scheme does not provide financial grant;
- only fee reduction is offered
