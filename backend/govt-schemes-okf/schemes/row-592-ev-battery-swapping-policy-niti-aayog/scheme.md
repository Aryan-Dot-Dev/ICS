# EV Battery Swapping Policy (NITI Aayog)

## Overview

NITI Aayog serves as the apex public policy think tank of the Government of India, tasked with catalyzing economic development and fostering cooperative federalism through the involvement of State Governments in economic policymaking using a bottom-up approach. It provides strategic, functional, and technical inputs to the Prime Minister and Government at all levels, focusing on areas such as agriculture, social sectors, innovation, frontier technologies, manufacturing, services, smart urbanization, mobility, clean energy, improved governance, and aspirational districts. NITI Aayog also monitors and evaluates government programmes, promotes innovation and entrepreneurship through initiatives like Atal Innovation Mission, and fosters partnerships with stakeholders including academia, industry, and international think tanks.

## Objective

- Evolve a shared vision of national development priorities, sectors, and strategies with active involvement of States
- Foster cooperative federalism through structured support initiatives with States
- Develop mechanisms to formulate credible plans at the village level and aggregate them at higher levels of government
- Ensure national security interests are incorporated in economic strategy and policy for referred areas
- Pay special attention to sections of society at risk of not benefiting adequately from economic progress
- Design strategic and long-term policy and programme frameworks, monitor their progress and efficacy, and use feedback for innovative improvements
- Provide advice and encourage partnerships between key stakeholders and national/international think tanks, educational and policy research institutions
- Create a knowledge, innovation, and entrepreneurial support system through a collaborative community of experts and practitioners
- Offer a platform for resolution of inter-sectoral and inter-departmental issues to accelerate development agenda implementation
- Maintain a state-of-the-art resource centre for research on good governance and best practices in sustainable and equitable development
- Actively monitor and evaluate programme implementation, identify needed resources to strengthen success probability and scope of delivery
- Focus on technology upgradation and capacity building for programme implementation
- Undertake other activities necessary to further the execution of the national development agenda
