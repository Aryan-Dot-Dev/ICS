# BOT Toll Highway Startup Opportunities

## Overview

The Ministry of Road Transport & Highways (MoRTH) has announced opportunities for startups and private entities to participate in Build-Operate-Transfer (BOT) toll highway projects under Public-Private Partnership (PPP) models. These opportunities involve Design, Build, Finance, Operate, and Transfer (DBFOT) models for national highway projects, enabling private sector participation in infrastructure development through toll revenue collection.

## Objective

- Promote private sector participation in national highway development
- Develop highway infrastructure through PPP models like DBFOT and BOT (Toll)
- Ensure efficient financing, construction, operation, and maintenance of highway projects
- Facilitate toll-based revenue generation for project sustainability
- Improve connectivity and reduce logistics costs through enhanced road infrastructure
- Encourage innovation in highway construction and maintenance through private sector efficiency
- Ensure timely completion and quality adherence in highway projects

**startups**, **private companies**, **PPP operators**, **infrastructure developers**

## Eligibility

Private companies, consortia, or special purpose vehicles (SPVs) registered under the Companies Act, 2013 or relevant statutes, with demonstrated technical and financial capacity to undertake highway projects on DBFOT or BOT (Toll) basis, meeting pre-qualification criteria set by MoRTH or NHAI for specific projects.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Opportunity to develop and operate national highway projects on DBFOT/BOT (Toll) basis, earn revenue through toll collection over concession period, contribute to national infrastructure development, avail tax benefits and incentives under PPP frameworks, and gain long-term operational rights over developed assets.

## Important Conditions

- Projects are awarded through competitive bidding;
- selection is not guaranteed
- Private entity bears all financial risks including cost overruns and revenue shortfalls
- Concession period is fixed;
- asset reverts to government after concession period
- Compliance with environmental, land acquisition, and statutory clearances is mandatory
- Toll rates may be subject to regulation or caps as per government policy
