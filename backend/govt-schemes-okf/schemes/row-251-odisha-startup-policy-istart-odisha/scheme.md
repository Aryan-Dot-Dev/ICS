# Odisha Startup Policy & iStart Odisha

## Overview

Odisha Startup Policy & iStart Odisha is a state-level initiative by the Government of Odisha to foster innovation, support startups, and build a robust entrepreneurial ecosystem through strategic partnerships, policy interventions, and financial incentives. The scheme provides recognition, funding, incubation support, and various benefits to startups operating in the state, with a focus on sectors like Agritech, IT, AI, Healthcare, Fintech, and Biotechnology. It aims to position Odisha among the top startup hubs in India by promoting innovation, skill development, and market access for startups.

## Objective

- To emerge amongst the top 3 'Startup Hubs' in India by 2020
- To unleash the potential of women entrepreneurship in Odisha by encouraging at least 33% of startups with Founders/Co-Founders as women
- To build a holistic Startup ecosystem in Odisha to enable the State to become one of the top three Startup destinations in the country
- To implement a supportive regulatory framework and robust governance structure that enables hassle-free, time-bound statutory clearances and monitoring
- To develop a world class 'Startup Hub' in Odisha by 2020
- To encourage incubators including sector-specific ones aligning with the State’s strengths and requirements
- To create enabling environment and supporting eco-system that facilitates at least 1,000 Startups in next five years in the state
- To create a strong institutional framework for effective implementation, monitoring and evaluation of this initiative

## Important Conditions

- Monthly allowance payment for the first three months may be made directly to a recognized eligible startup entity in its registered bank account on a monthly basis;
- three months onwards, payment may be released based on the utilization report submitted by the Startup online to the Nodal agency within 15 days of completion of the first three months
- Payment of the monthly allowance will be made for a period of one year only;
- Startup entity can avail this benefit only once
- Startup entity shall submit a final utilization certificate highlighting utilization of the monthly allowance along with supporting documents and bills within one month of the completion of 1 year
- Product Development and Marketing / Publicity Assistance: Startup will have to submit a self-contained product development plan with justification online;
- Subject to approval, 50% of the payment to a recognized startup entity may be made in advance and the remaining 50% will be made to its registered bank account upon utilization of the advance payment on the basis of submission of utilization certificate along with bills;
- The Startup entity must submit utilization certificate along with bills for the advance payment within three months of receiving the grant;
- The Startup entity must submit final utilization certificate along with bills for the entire amount within six months of release of grant
- Need based Assistance: Startup entity will have to submit a self-contained plan with justification online for the need and the manner of utilization of the assistance;
- Subject to approval, payment may be made directly to a recognized startup in its registered bank account;
- The Startup entity must submit utilization certificate along with bills for all the expenses within three months of receiving the grant
