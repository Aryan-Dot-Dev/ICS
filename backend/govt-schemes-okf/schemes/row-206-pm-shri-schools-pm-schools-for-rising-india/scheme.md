# PM SHRI Schools (PM Schools for Rising India)

## Overview

PM SHRI Schools (PM Schools for Rising India) is a centrally sponsored scheme by the Government of India aimed at developing more than 14,500 schools managed by Central Government, State/UT Governments, local bodies, KVS, and NVS. The initiative seeks to provide a safe, stimulating, and inclusive learning environment with quality infrastructure and resources, aligned with the National Education Policy 2020. It targets over 20 lakh students as direct beneficiaries and aims to scale up learnings to other schools nationwide. The scheme is implemented over a five-year period from 2022-23 to 2026-27.

## Objective

- Develop more than 14,500 PM SHRI Schools across India
- Ensure every student feels welcomed and cared for in a safe and stimulating learning environment
- Provide wide-ranging learning experiences and good physical infrastructure conducive to learning
- Nurture students to become engaged, productive, and contributing citizens for an equitable, inclusive, and plural society
- Promote understanding of various dimensions of quality in school education to inform policy, practice, and implementation
- Scale up learnings from PM SHRI Schools to other schools in the country
- Showcase implementation of the National Education Policy 2020 and emerge as exemplar schools
