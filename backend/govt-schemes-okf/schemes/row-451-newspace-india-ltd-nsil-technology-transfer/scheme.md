# NewSpace India Ltd (NSIL) Technology Transfer

## Overview

NewSpace India Limited (NSIL) Technology Transfer scheme facilitates the transfer of ISRO-developed technologies to Indian industries, academia, and government organizations through formal agreements, handholding events, and technical support. The scheme aims to enhance industrial capabilities, promote self-reliance in space technologies, and support the growth of India's space economy by enabling industry access to ISRO's intellectual property. NSIL acts as the nodal agency under the Department of Space to manage technology transfer activities, including evaluation, agreement signing, fee remittance, and handholding by ISRO units. The scheme covers a wide range of technologies across domains such as antennas, communication & navigation, RF & electronics, remote sensing, materials, mechanical systems, optics, and software.

## Objective

- Transfer technologies developed by DOS/ISRO centres/units to Indian Industries
- Contribute towards technological self-reliance and industrial growth
- Enable national development through space technology spin-offs
- Cater to emerging global commercial small satellite launch service market
- Enable space technology spin-offs for betterment of mankind through industry interface
- Facilitate collaboration between ISRO and Indian industry for technology development
- Support the growth of India's space economy through public-private partnerships
- Provide handholding and technical support during technology transfer process

## Eligibility

Department of Space has authorised NSIL to enter into Technology Transfer Agreement with suitable entrepreneurs/Industry in India. Interested parties must submit a Technology Transfer (TT) Request through the IN-SPACe portal, followed by evaluation of company profile and technical capabilities by IN-SPACe, signing of TT Agreement with NSIL & IN-SPACe, remittance of TT Fee to NSIL, and…

## Important Conditions

- Technology transfer is subject to evaluation of company profile and technical capabilities by IN-SPACe
- Remittance of TT Fee to NSIL is required before handholding
- Handholding is conducted by ISRO units (e.g., IISU) as per the technology being transferred
- Only suitable entrepreneurs/Industry in India are eligible as authorised by Department of Space
- The process requires formal agreement signing with both NSIL and IN-SPACe
