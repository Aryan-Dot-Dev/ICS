## Process

1. Visit the official StartUp J&K portal at https://jkstartupsummit.in
2. Register as a new user by providing basic details and creating a login
3. Log in and fill out the online application form with startup details, business model, and financial projections
4. 4. Upload required documents including Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, Aadhaar of founders, and a detailed pitch deck
5. Submit the application and await preliminary screening
6. Shortlisted applicants will be invited for a pitch presentation before the selection committee
7. Final selection will be based on innovation, feasibility, scalability, and team strength
8. Approved startups will receive a sanction letter and grant disbursement will begin post-agreement signing

## Deadlines

- Applications are accepted twice a year — typically in April and October. Exact dates are announced on the portal each cycle.
