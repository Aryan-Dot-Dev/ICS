# StartUp J&K Initiative

## Overview

The StartUp J&K Initiative is a state-level program launched by the Government of Jammu and Kashmir to promote entrepreneurship and innovation within the Union Territory. It aims to support early-stage startups through financial assistance, mentorship, and access to incubation facilities. The initiative focuses on creating a conducive ecosystem for youth and aspiring entrepreneurs to launch and scale their ventures.

## Objective

- Promote innovation and entrepreneurship among the youth of Jammu and Kashmir
- Provide financial support to early-stage startups for product development and market entry
- Establish and strengthen incubation centers across the region
- Offer mentorship and guidance through industry experts and successful entrepreneurs
- Facilitate access to funding networks and investor connects
- Encourage sector-specific innovation in areas like tourism, handicrafts, agriculture, and IT

## Eligibility

Applicants must be residents of Jammu and Kashmir. The startup must be registered as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm under relevant Indian laws. The venture should be innovative, scalable, and have a clear business model. Preference is given to ventures led by women, SC/ST, or differently abled individuals. The startup must not have received more than INR 25 lakhs in funding…

## Benefits

Selected startups receive financial grants, access to state-recognized incubators, mentorship from industry experts, assistance in patent filing and IPR protection, support for participation in national and international startup events, and help in connecting with angel investors and venture capital firms. ## Important Conditions

- Grant is disbursed in stages based on milestone verification
- Startup must maintain regular progress reports and financial statements
- Funds must be utilized strictly for the approved business purpose
- Misuse of funds may lead to recovery and blacklisting
- The grant is non-transferable and cannot be pledged or mortgaged
- Beneficiaries must participate in at least two mentorship sessions per quarter
