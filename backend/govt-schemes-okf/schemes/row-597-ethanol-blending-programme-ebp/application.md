## Process

1. Ethanol producers must register with the respective State Excise Authority and obtain necessary clearances.
2. Ensure compliance with BIS standards for fuel ethanol (IS 15464:2004).
3. Enter into supply agreements with Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) through tender or nomination basis.
4. Submit quality and quantity documentation as required by OMCs for each consignment.
5. Receive payment from OMCs based on the notified ethanol price under the administered price mechanism.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — ethanol supply and procurement occur throughout the year as per OMC requirements and notified blending targets.
