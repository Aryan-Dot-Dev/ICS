# Ethanol Blending Programme (EBP)

## Overview

The Ethanol Blending Programme (EBP) is a Government of India initiative aimed at reducing dependence on fossil fuels by blending ethanol with petrol. It seeks to promote the use of biofuels, support farmers through increased demand for sugarcane and other feedstocks, and contribute to environmental sustainability by lowering vehicular emissions. The programme mandates Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) to procure ethanol from domestic sources and blend it with petrol as per the notified blending targets.

## Objective

- Achieve 20% ethanol blending in petrol by 2025-26% ethanol blending in petrol by 2022 and 20% by 2025-26
- Reduce import dependence on crude oil
- Promote domestic ethanol production from sugarcane, maize, and other feedstocks
- Provide remunerative prices to farmers for ethanol-producing crops
- Lower carbon emissions and improve air quality
- Strengthen the biofuel ecosystem and encourage investment in distillation capacity

- Supply is subject to OMC procurement policies and blending targets

- Only ethanol produced from domestically approved feedstocks is eligible for procurement under EBP
