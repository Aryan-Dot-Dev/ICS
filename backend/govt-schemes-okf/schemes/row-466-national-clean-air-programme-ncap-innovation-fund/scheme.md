# National Clean Air Programme (NCAP) Innovation Fund

## Overview

The National Clean Air Programme (NCAP) Innovation Fund is a government initiative under the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC) aimed at supporting innovative solutions to combat air pollution in India. It focuses on fostering research, development, and deployment of clean technologies through financial and technical assistance. The fund encourages participation from startups, MSMEs, academic institutions, and research organizations to develop scalable and sustainable air quality improvement measures.

## Objective

- Support development and deployment of innovative technologies for air pollution mitigation
- Promote research and development in clean air solutions across sectors
- Encourage collaboration between industry, academia, and government for scalable outcomes
- Pilot and scale proven technologies in non-attainment cities under NCAP
- Strengthen indigenous capabilities in air quality monitoring and control technologies
- Facilitate technology transfer and commercialization of green innovations
- Create a pipeline of bankable projects for long-term sustainability
- Align with national climate goals and sustainable development objectives
