## Process

1. Visit the official NCAP portal at https://cpcb.nic.in/ncap to access the Innovation Fund guidelines.
2. Review eligibility criteria and prepare a detailed project proposal including problem statement, objectives, methodology, budget, and expected outcomes.
3. Ensure all required documents are ready as per the checklist.
4. Submit the proposal online through the designated submission portal or via email to the specified address.
5. Await preliminary screening by the technical evaluation committee.
6. Shortlisted applicants may be invited for presentation or field validation.
7. Final selection is based on technical merit, innovation, and alignment with NCAP goals.
8. Selected projects receive sanction letters and enter into memorandum of understanding (MoU) before fund disbursement.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — proposals are accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds and periodic review cycles.
