# National Aquifer Mapping Programme (NAQUIM)

## Overview

The National Aquifer Mapping and Management (NAQUIM) programme, initiated by the Central Ground Water Board (CGWB) in 2012 under the Ground Water Management and Regulation Plan Scheme (XII plan), aims to comprehensively map India's aquifers to support effective groundwater management. It involves delineation of aquifers in terms of horizontal and vertical extent, characterization of hydrogeological properties, assessment of resource availability and quality, preparation of aquifer maps, identification and quantification of issues, and development of management strategies for sustainability. The programme has completed mapping of the targeted 25 lakh km² area by 2023 and has expanded into NAQUIM 2.0 for higher granularity and issue-based scientific inputs.

## Objective

- Delineation of aquifers in terms of their horizontal and vertical extent
- Characterization of aquifers in terms of their hydrogeological properties
- Assessment of resource availability and quality
- Preparation of aquifer maps
- Identification and quantification of issues
- Developing management strategies to ensure sustainability of ground water resources
- Providing information in higher granularity with a focus on increasing density of dynamic data like ground water level, ground water quality etc.
- Providing issue based scientific inputs for ground water management up to panchayat level
- Putting in place a strategy to ensure implementation of the recommended strategies

## Eligibility

The NAQUIM programme is implemented by the Central Ground Water Board (CGWB) in coordination with state government counterparts and collaborates with organizations/institutes like GSI, NGRI, IMD, NRSC, IISc, IIT etc. on specific topics. It is a government-led initiative and not a scheme open for external applications by individuals or entities.

## Benefits

NAQUIM outputs support drinking water source finding and source sustainability, site selection for Artificial Recharge, safe drinking water in Arsenic affected areas, assured irrigation through ground water in areas with adequate ground water potential, implementation of water conservation and AR recommendations, ground water regulation based on NAQUIM recommendations, rejuvenation of springs,…

## Important Conditions

- NAQUIM outputs are intended for use by government agencies involved in ground water management and water supply
- The programme focuses on assessment and mapping;
- direct financial benefits to individuals or entities are not part of the scheme
- NAQUIM 2.0 activities are ongoing with priority areas including water stressed areas, coastal areas, urban agglomerates, springsheds, industrial/mining areas, command areas, deep-seated aquifers, auto-flow areas, and poor ground water quality areas
