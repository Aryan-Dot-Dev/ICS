## Process

1. The NAQUIM programme is a government-implemented initiative and does not have an application process for external beneficiaries. It is carried out by the Central Ground Water Board (CGWB) in collaboration with state governments and technical partners.

## Deadlines

- The mapping of the entire targeted area under NAQUIM was completed in the year 2023. The programme has evolved into NAQUIM 2.0 with ongoing activities.
