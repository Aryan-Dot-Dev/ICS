## Process

1. Eligible candidates (engineering/diploma graduates) must register on the NATS portal at https://mhrdnats.gov.in.
2. After registration, candidates can browse and apply for apprenticeship vacancies posted by participating establishments.
3. Upon selection by an employer, the candidate joins the organization for apprenticeship training.
4. The employer uploads the apprenticeship contract and monthly attendance/stipend details on the portal.
5. After verification, the government processes the 50% stipend reimbursement to the employer.
6. Upon successful completion of training, the apprentice receives a Certificate of Proficiency.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year based on vacancy notifications from employers.
