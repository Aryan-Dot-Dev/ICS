# National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS)

## Overview

The National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS) is a Government of India initiative under the Ministry of Education aimed at enhancing the employability of engineering and diploma graduates through structured on-the-job training. It provides apprenticeship opportunities in various industries and organizations, enabling fresh graduates to gain practical skills and industry exposure. The scheme facilitates a mutually beneficial arrangement where employers get trained manpower and graduates acquire hands-on experience relevant to their academic qualifications.

## Objective

- To enhance the employability of engineering and diploma graduates through apprenticeship training
- To provide industry-relevant practical skills to fresh graduates
- To bridge the gap between academic knowledge and industrial requirements
- To facilitate industry-institute interaction for skill development
- To increase the availability of skilled manpower in various sectors


## Target Beneficiaries

**engineering graduates**, **diploma holders**, **fresh graduates**


## Key Features

- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year based on vacancy notifications from employers.
- Portal: https://mhrdnats.gov.in
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

Engineering graduates (B.E./B.Tech) and diploma holders in engineering/technology disciplines who have passed their qualifying examination are eligible to apply for apprenticeship training under NATS. Candidates must register on the NATS portal and seek placement with participating establishments.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Apprentices receive a monthly stipend during the training period, with 50% of the stipend reimbursed to the employer by the Government of India. Upon successful completion, apprentices are awarded a Certificate of Proficiency by the Government of India, which is recognized for employment purposes across industries. The training helps in skill development and improves job readiness.

## Important Conditions

- Stipend reimbursement to employers is limited to 50% of the actual stipend paid, subject to government-prescribed maximum limits
- Apprenticeship placement is subject to availability of vacancies with participating establishments
- The Certificate of Proficiency is awarded only upon successful completion of the training period and satisfactory performance
- Employers must be registered establishments under the Apprentices Act, 1961 to participate in the scheme
