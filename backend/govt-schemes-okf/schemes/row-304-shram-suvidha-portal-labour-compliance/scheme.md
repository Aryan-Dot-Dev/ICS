# Shram Suvidha Portal – Labour Compliance

## Overview

Shram Suvidha Portal – Labour Compliance is a unified digital platform launched by the Ministry of Labour and Employment to facilitate ease of compliance and ease of doing business under the new Labour Codes. It enables digitization of processes such as registration, licence, returns, inspection notices, and enforcement by agencies like EPFO, ESIC, DGMS, DGFASLI, and CLC. The portal supports self-certification for startups under labour laws and provides access to various labour codes, notifications, and user manuals.

## Objective

- Facilitate Ease of Compliance and Ease of Doing Business as per provisions of Labour codes
- Enable effective monitoring and evaluation of labour compliances by digitizing processes of Registration, Licence, Returns, Inspection Notices, and Enforcement
- Provide a one-stop solution for labour law compliance through integration with EPFO, ESIC, DGMS, DGFASLI, and CLC
- Support self-certification for startups under labour laws to reduce regulatory burden
- Ensure transparency, accountability, and uniform implementation of labour laws across Central and State spheres

## Eligibility

Every employer of any establishment which comes into existence after the commencement of the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 and the Social Security Code, 2020 must apply for registration within 60 days in Form-I electronically through the Shram Suvidha Portal. ## Important Conditions

- Electronic registration is the standard process;
- temporary relaxation for non-electronic submission may be notified by Central Government in exceptional circumstances
- Registration number must be quoted on all documents, records, and correspondence related to the Code or Rules
- Failure to comply with registration provisions may lead to direction by the registering officer to comply within a specified timeframe
- Certificate of registration may be cancelled if obtained by providing wrong information, after giving an opportunity to show cause within 30 days
- Any change in ownership, management, or registration details must be updated within thirty days via amendment on the portal
