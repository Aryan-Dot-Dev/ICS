## Process

1. Visit the NIFTEM admission portal at www.niftem.ac.in
2. Check the Admission Prospectus for the relevant academic session for programme details and important dates
3. Commence online application from the specified date (e.g., 29 April 2026 for many programmes)
4. Submit the online application before the last date (e.g., 3 June 2026 for many programmes)
5. Upload required documents including academic certificates, scorecards, and identity proof
6. Pay the prescribed application fee online
7. Appear for entrance tests, GD/PI, or other selection processes as per the programme
8. Await merit list and counselling notification
9. Complete admission formalities upon selection

## Deadlines

- Varies by programme and announcement; for example, last date for B.Tech, B.Tech+MBA, BBA (Hons) applications was extended to 3 June 2026; for MBA and Executive MBA, extended to 30 June 2026; tender/EOI deadlines are specified in individual notices (e.g., 23 July 2026 for EOI on pilot plants). Admission deadlines are tentative and subject to change based on national level entrance test results.
