## Process

1. Project identification and feasibility studies conducted by NHIDCL or other implementing agencies.
2. Preparation of Detailed Project Reports (DPRs) including technical, economic, and environmental assessments.
3. Submission of DPRs to the Ministry of Road Transport & Highways for appraisal and approval.
4. Sanction of projects based on priority and availability of funds.
5. Tendering process for selection of contractors through competitive bidding.
6. Award of contracts and commencement of civil works.
7. Monitoring and supervision during construction.
8. Commissioning and handover for operation and maintenance.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — projects are sanctioned and implemented based on annual planning and budget cycles.
