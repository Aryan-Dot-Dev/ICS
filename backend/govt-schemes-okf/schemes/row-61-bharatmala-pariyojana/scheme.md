# Bharatmala Pariyojana

## Overview

Bharatmala Pariyojana is a flagship infrastructure initiative by the Government of India aimed at improving road connectivity across the country, particularly in border areas, coastal regions, and economic corridors. It focuses on developing National Highways, strategic roads, and interconnecting routes to enhance regional integration and support economic growth. The scheme is implemented by agencies like NHIDCL under the Ministry of Road Transport & Highways, with projects spanning multiple states and union territories.

## Objective

- Develop approximately 24,800 km of roads under Phase-I
- Improve connectivity in border, coastal, and tribal areas
- Enhance efficiency of national corridors through multi-modal integration
- Support economic corridors and feeder routes
- Strengthen infrastructure in the North Eastern Region and strategic areas
- Facilitate seamless movement of goods and passenger traffic
- Promote sustainable and environmentally conscious highway development
- Boost regional development and inclusive growth
