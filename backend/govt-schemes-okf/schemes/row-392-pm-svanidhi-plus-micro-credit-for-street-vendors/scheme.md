# PM SVANidhi Plus (Micro Credit for Street Vendors)

## Overview

PM SVANidhi Plus (Micro Credit for Street Vendors) is a special micro-credit facility launched by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs to provide collateral-free working capital loans to street vendors. The scheme aims to support urban street vendors affected by economic hardships, enabling them to resume livelihood activities through accessible credit. It offers loans in tranches with increasing amounts upon successful repayment, along with digital transaction incentives and credit card facilities for eligible beneficiaries.

## Objective

- Provide affordable working capital loans to street vendors
- Support livelihood restoration post-economic disruption
- Encourage digital transactions through cashback incentives
- Facilitate credit access for vendors from Census Towns
- Enable vendor mobility via ULB transfer facility
- Promote financial inclusion through linked Aadhaar and mobile verification
- Strengthen urban local bodies' role in vendor identification and support

## Important Conditions

- Loan availability is restricted to States/UTs that have notified the Street Vendors (Protection of Livelihood and Regulation of Street Vending) Act, 2014
- Second and third tranche loans are contingent upon timely repayment of previous tranches
- PM SVANidhi Credit Card facility is available only to vendors who have repaid the second tranche
- Beneficiaries must maintain Aadhaar-linked mobile number for authentication and future benefits
