# Pradhan Mantri JI-VAN Yojana (Biofuel)

## Overview

Pradhan Mantri JI-VAN Yojana is a central sector scheme launched to provide financial support to integrated bioethanol projects using lignocellulosic biomass and other renewable feedstocks. The scheme aims to promote the production of second-generation (2G) ethanol from non-food biomass sources such as agricultural residues, waste, and grasses. It supports the establishment of commercial and demonstration projects to reduce dependence on fossil fuels and mitigate greenhouse gas emissions. The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas.

## Objective

- To create an enabling environment for commercial production of 2G ethanol
- To support research and development in biomass pretreatment and enzyme technology
- To reduce India's dependence on imported fossil fuels
- To mitigate greenhouse gas emissions through renewable fuel blending
- To utilize agricultural and industrial waste for value-added biofuel production
- To encourage private investment in advanced biofuel technologies
