## Process

1. Check the nearest empanelled hospital from the Network Hospital List available on the FHPL website.
2. Intimate the TPA about IPD hospitalization at the earliest via email, with a copy marked to HCL and the Mediclaim Consultant at designated email IDs.
3. Visit the hospital’s Insurance/TPA Desk with Health Card, valid ID proof, doctor’s advice, and treatment-related medical documents.
4. Upon commencement of treatment, the member’s relative must complete and submit the Pre-authorization Form at the hospital’s TPA desk.
5. The hospital shall forward all required documents to the TPA for verification of eligibility.
6. Initial approval shall be provided by the TPA within six (06) working hours upon receipt of complete documents.
7. Final approval shall be issued by the TPA within 2–4 hours after receipt of the discharge summary, reports, and final bills.
8. The TPA shall settle the claim within 10 working days of receipt of complete documents, and the settlement amount shall be credited directly to the primary member’s bank account.

## Deadlines

- IPD and OPD reimbursement claims must be submitted within 60 days from the date of discharge; however, post-hospitalization claims shall be submitted within 7 days of completion of treatment or within 60 days from the date of discharge, whichever is earlier. OPD claims must be submitted within 90 days from the billing date or upon expenses exceeding Rs. 4,000/- per member, whichever occurs first.
