# Hindustan Copper Ltd (HCL) Innovation Support

## Overview

Hindustan Copper Limited (HCL) is a public sector undertaking under the Ministry of Mines, Government of India, established on 9 November 1967. It is the nation's only vertically integrated copper producing company, engaged in mining, beneficiation, smelting, refining, and marketing of copper and its by-products. The company operates mines and plants across Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Jharkhand, Maharashtra, and Gujarat, with a focus on sustainable mining and value addition.

## Objective

- To strive to be a leading metal mining company and maximize total shareholder return
- To sustainably find, develop, and mine copper ore and associated minerals
- To ensure responsible resource utilization, environmental stewardship, and social responsibility
- To achieve 12.2 MTPA mining capacity by 2030
- To progress towards Vision Plan 2030 and aspiration for Navratna status
- To implement welfare initiatives for officers, workmen, and contract personnel
- To introduce the Nursing Scheme and educational support under the Sathee Scheme
- To roll out License to operate software
- To resume operations at Kendadih, Kolihan, and Surda mines
- To advance greenfield projects in India (Sikkim, Dhobani, Pathargora) and overseas (Chile)

## Important Conditions

- Coverage shall continue for the spouse in case of death of the Primary Member during the policy period.
- The cover extends 30 days pre-hospitalization and 60 days post-hospitalization benefits.
- If medically required, any procedure requiring an admission of less than 24 hours shall be treated under IPD coverage even if it is not a part of the standard Day care list.
- In the event of Death of the insured member during hospitalization, claims shall be payable in full without any deductions, even in cases where the duration of hospitalization is less than or equal to 24 hours.
- OPD reimbursement claims may be submitted at any time during the policy period.
- However, claims shall be submitted within 90 days from the date of the bill(s) or when the cumulative OPD expenses exceed Rs. 4,000/- per member, whichever is earlier.
- In case cashless facility is availed under the policy, any non-admissible amount or deductions imposed by the Insurance Company/TPA shall be directly recovered from the beneficiary concerned.
- HCL shall have no liability or responsibility whatsoever with respect to recovery of such non-admissible amounts.
