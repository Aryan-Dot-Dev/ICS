# BSNL Startup & Innovation Challenge

## Overview

The BSNL Startup & Innovation Challenge is a scheme under the PSU Support category aimed at fostering innovation and supporting startups through BSNL's Integrated Business Solution (IBS) initiatives. It provides a platform for startups to collaborate with BSNL on technology-driven solutions, leveraging BSNL's infrastructure and expertise in areas such as cloud services, IoT, and digital transformation. The scheme encourages startups to become partners in BSNL's ecosystem, offering opportunities for empanelment, revenue sharing, and access to BSNL's enterprise services.

## Objective

- Empower businesses, especially SMEs and startups, by delivering integrated, cost-effective, and technology-driven workspace solutions
- Enhance productivity, simplify operations, ensure data security, and foster a modern, collaborative work environment
- Act as a strategic partner in a company's digital transformation journey
- Provide end-to-end solutions combining advanced technology, workspace automation, and cloud services
- Support national skill development goals through initiatives like Kaushalam
- Enable startups to access BSNL's enterprise services and infrastructure
- Foster innovation and entrepreneurship through partnership models
- Promote digital inclusion and connectivity across India

## Important Conditions

- Empanelment is initially for TWO (2) Years and may be extended to another Two years on year-to-year basis
- Startups must submit proof of DPIIT recognition to claim turnover exemption
- Partners must comply with all applicable norms and directions issued by Regulator (TRAI), Licensor (DOT) and Govt.
- Failure to generate minimum topline revenue may result in infrastructure charges not being free of cost
- Additional Bank Guarantees of Rs.20 Lacs each per SMSC deployed per zone may be required for regulatory compliance
