## Process

1. Review eligibility requirements and operational capabilities for becoming an Integrated Business Solution Partner (IBSP).
2. Ensure legal registration in India, GST & PAN compliance.
3. Meet turnover requirement of ₹1 Crore average in last 3 financial years (waived for startups).
4. Confirm PAN India operational capability and 24x7 SPOC/helpdesk.
5. Obtain required certifications (ISO/IEC, CERT-In, MeitY empanelment, Tier III/IV DC) based on service type.
6. Submit application via BSNL's IBS portal.
7. Complete KYC and documentation verification.
8. Await empanelment approval for initial period of TWO (2) Years, extendable to another Two years on year-to-year basis.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round.
