# CONCOR (Container Corporation) Logistics Startup Challenge

## Overview

The CONCOR Logistics Startup Challenge is an initiative by Container Corporation of India Limited (CONCOR) aimed at fostering innovation in the logistics sector by engaging startups to develop solutions for end-to-end logistics services. The challenge aligns with CONCOR's broader mission to enhance logistics efficiency, sustainability, and customer-centric solutions through technology integration and partnerships. It supports the government's push for ease of doing business and green logistics initiatives.

## Objective

- To identify and promote innovative logistics solutions from startups
- To enhance end-to-end logistics services through technological integration
- To support green and sustainable logistics initiatives
- To strengthen customer-centric focus areas
- To collaborate with market leaders for inclusive growth in logistics
- To provide a platform for startups to showcase and scale their solutions
- To improve first-mile and last-mile connectivity in logistics networks

## Eligibility

Startups with innovative logistics solutions, particularly in areas such as e-logistics, first-mile last-mile connectivity, green logistics, digital workflows, and technology-driven logistics services are eligible to participate in the CONCOR Logistics Startup Challenge.

## Benefits

Selected startups gain recognition, opportunities for pilot projects with CONCOR, potential collaboration or partnership, access to CONCOR's logistics network and infrastructure, visibility through CONCOR's platforms, and support for scaling their solutions in the logistics domain.
