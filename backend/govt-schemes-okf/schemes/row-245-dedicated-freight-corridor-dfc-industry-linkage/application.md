## Process

1. Identify the nearest DFC alignment and conduct feasibility study for siding connection.
2. Submit a formal application to DFCCIL with project details, land documents, and traffic projections.
3. Obtain in-principle approval from DFCCIL after technical evaluation.
4. Sign a Licensing Agreement for siding construction and maintenance.
5. Construct the siding as per DFCCIL specifications under supervision.
6. Complete safety inspection and commissioning.
7. Begin operations and avail linkage benefits.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to technical feasibility and network capacity.
