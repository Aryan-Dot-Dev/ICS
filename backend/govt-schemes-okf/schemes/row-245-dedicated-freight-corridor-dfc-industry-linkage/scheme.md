# Dedicated Freight Corridor (DFC) Industry Linkage

## Overview

The Dedicated Freight Corridor (DFC) Industry Linkage scheme, managed by the Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Limited (DFCCIL), aims to enhance logistics efficiency by connecting industrial hubs to the Dedicated Freight Corridors. It facilitates seamless freight movement, reduces transit time and cost, and supports industrial growth through improved rail connectivity. The scheme focuses on developing last-mile connectivity and industrial nodes along the DFC network.

## Objective

- Develop last-mile connectivity from industrial clusters to Dedicated Freight Corridors
- Reduce logistics cost and transit time for freight movement
- Enhance competitiveness of Indian industries through efficient rail connectivity
- Promote shift from road to rail for sustainable freight transport
- Develop industrial nodes and logistics parks along DFC routes
- Improve supply chain efficiency for domestic and EXIM cargo


## Target Beneficiaries

**industries**, **manufacturing units**, **logistics providers**, **MSME**


## Key Features

- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to technical feasibility and network capacity.
- Portal: https://dfccil.com
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

Industries, manufacturing units, and logistics providers located near or planning to locate along the Dedicated Freight Corridor network are eligible to apply for linkage development. Entities must have valid industrial registration and comply with DFCCIL's technical and safety standards for siding connections.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial assistance for siding construction, priority path allocation on DFC, reduced freight charges, assured wagon availability, end-to-end logistics solution, reduced carbon footprint, and improved supply chain reliability.
