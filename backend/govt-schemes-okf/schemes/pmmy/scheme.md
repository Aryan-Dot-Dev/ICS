# PMMY — Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana

## Overview

PMMY, launched 08.04.2015, provides loans up to **₹20 lakh** for
non-farm income-generating activity (manufacturing, processing, trading
or services) to micro and small enterprises through banks, NBFCs and
MFIs. Loans are collateral-free ("Mudra loans"). Four categories:
**Shishu** (up to ₹50,000), **Kishore** (₹50,001–₹5,00,000), **Tarun**
(₹5,00,001–₹10,00,000), and **Tarun Plus** (₹10,00,001–₹20,00,000) —
Tarun Plus announced in Union Budget 2024-25 for entrepreneurs who have
availed and successfully repaid previous Tarun loans [S1][S2][S3].

PMMY is a **credit facilitation scheme**, not a subsidy scheme — the
Government does not subsidise interest under PMMY; interest is charged by
the lending institution as per its policy (RBI-regulated).

## Objective

To fund the unfunded micro-enterprise segment, enable income-generating
self-employment, and expand access to formal credit for small business
owners including first-generation entrepreneurs.

## Target Beneficiaries

Individuals and micro/small enterprises engaged (or starting) non-farm
income-generating activity — small manufacturing units, service units,
shopkeepers, fruit vendors, truck operators, food-service units, repair
shops, machine operators, artisans, food processors, etc.

## Key Features

- Collateral-free loans up to ₹20 lakh [S1][S2]
- Category ladder: Shishu ≤ ₹0.5L; Kishore ₹0.5–5L; Tarun ₹5–10L; Tarun
  Plus ₹10–20L (repaid-Tarun borrowers only) [S2][S3]
- Delivery through all scheduled banks, NBFCs and MFIs
- No fixed scheme interest rate — as per lending institution policy
- No direct government subsidy under PMMY
- Application via lending institutions or the Government's JanSamarth
  portal [S3]

## Eligibility

Summary — see [eligibility.md](eligibility.md): any individual/enterprise
with a non-farm income-generating activity; Tarun Plus requires prior
successful Tarun repayment.

## Benefits

Loan (not grant/subsidy) per category limits. Details:

## Documents

KYC/identity, business address proof, activity/project details,
bank statements, ITR (where applicable). Details:
[documents.md](documents.md).

## Application

Through any lending institution branch, or online via JanSamarth.
Details: [application.md](application.md).

## Important Conditions

- Loan use must be for **non-farm** income-generating activity
- Repayment obligation remains with the borrower; default affects credit
  history
- Tarun Plus strictly for borrowers who repaid a prior Tarun loan [S3]

## Exclusions

Farm-sector cultivation loans, personal consumption loans and
collateralised/other-scheme credit are not Mudra loans. Structured list:
[exclusions.md](exclusions.md).
