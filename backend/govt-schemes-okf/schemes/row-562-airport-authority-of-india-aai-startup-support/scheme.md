# Airport Authority of India (AAI) Startup Support

## Overview

The Airport Authority of India (AAI) Startup Initiative is a program under the PSU Support category aimed at fostering innovation and supporting startups in the aviation and aviation-related sectors. The initiative is part of AAI's broader efforts to promote innovation and entrepreneurship through collaboration with startups, particularly in areas such as aviation technology, airport operations, and passenger experience enhancement. The initiative is highlighted on the AAI website under the 'AAI Startup Initiative' section, which is accessible via the main website and linked from various portal pages including careers, media, and innovation sections.

## Objective

- To foster innovation in the aviation and aviation-related sectors
- To support startups through collaboration and mentorship
- To enhance airport operations and passenger experience through startup innovations
- To promote entrepreneurship and technological advancement in civil aviation
- To create a platform for startups to showcase and test their solutions at AAI airports
- To strengthen the startup ecosystem in India through PSU-led initiatives

## Eligibility

Startups operating in the aviation, aviation technology, airport operations, passenger experience, or related sectors are eligible to apply. Startups must be registered entities in India and must demonstrate innovation, scalability, and relevance to AAI's operational or strategic goals. Specific eligibility criteria such as incorporation date, turnover, or sector focus are not explicitly…


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Selected startups may receive mentorship, technical guidance, access to AAI infrastructure for pilot testing, visibility through AAI platforms, and potential opportunities for collaboration or pilot projects at AAI airports. The initiative aims to provide a platform for startups to validate and scale their solutions in real-world aviation environments.

## Important Conditions

- The initiative does not guarantee funding or financial support.
- Selection is based on the relevance, innovation, and scalability of the startup's solution to AAI's needs.
- Final selection and engagement terms are at the sole discretion of the Airports Authority of India.
- Startups must comply with AAI's security, safety, and operational protocols when engaging with airport facilities.
