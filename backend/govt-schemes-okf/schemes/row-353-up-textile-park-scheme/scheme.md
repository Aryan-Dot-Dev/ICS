# UP Textile Park Scheme

## Overview

The UP Textile Park Scheme is a state-level initiative by the Uttar Pradesh government to promote textile industry development by establishing integrated textile parks with modern infrastructure and facilities. The scheme aims to attract investment, generate employment, and enhance the competitiveness of the textile sector in Uttar Pradesh through cluster-based development.

## Objective

- Develop world-class infrastructure for textile units in designated parks
- Attract domestic and foreign investment in the textile sector
- Generate employment opportunities in textile manufacturing
- Enhance productivity and value addition in textile processing
- Promote cluster-based growth for economies of scale
- Provide plug-and-play facilities to reduce setup time for textile units


## Target Beneficiaries

**MSME**, **textile manufacturers**, **entrepreneurs**, **industrial units**

## Key Features

- Grant/assistance per entity: Up to INR 5 Crore
- Geographic scope: Uttar Pradesh only (state-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — applications accepted throughout the year subject to plot availability
- Portal: https://upica.org
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

Textile manufacturing units, MSMEs, and entrepreneurs engaged in spinning, weaving, processing, garmenting, or technical textiles who wish to set up operations in UP Textile Parks. Applicants must comply with land use norms, environmental regulations, and possess necessary statutory clearances as per Uttar Pradesh industrial policies.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Allotment of developed land or built-up space in textile parks at concessional rates, access to plug-and-play infrastructure (power, water, drainage, effluent treatment), common facilities like testing labs, design centers, logistics support, and skill development centers. Financial incentives including capital subsidy, interest subsidy, and power tariff concessions as per UP Textile Policy…

## Important Conditions

- Benefits are subject to availability of funds under the state budget
- Land allotment is contingent upon meeting investment and employment commitments
- Failure to commence production within stipulated period may lead to cancellation of allotment
- Subsidy claims must be submitted within 12 months of commercial production
- Units must operate for minimum 7 years to retain subsidy benefits
