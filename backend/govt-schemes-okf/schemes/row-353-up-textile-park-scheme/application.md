## Process

1. Identify suitable textile park and plot/unit requirement
2. Submit application to Uttar Pradesh State Industrial Development Corporation (UPSIDC) or concerned development authority
3. Provide project report, land use details, and financial appraisal
4. Obtain in-principle approval from the committee
5. Execute lease agreement and pay initial deposit
6. Commence construction/installation as per approved plan
7. Apply for financial incentives post-commencement of production

## Deadlines
