# India Enterprise Architecture (InEA)

## Overview

India Enterprise Architecture (InEA) is a framework under the Digital India initiative by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) aimed at establishing a standardized, interoperable, and secure enterprise architecture for government systems and digital services. It seeks to align IT investments with national goals, improve efficiency, and enable seamless data exchange across departments. The framework supports the design, development, and deployment of scalable and future-ready digital infrastructure in line with India’s digital transformation objectives.

## Objective

- Establish a standardized enterprise architecture framework for government IT systems
- Ensure interoperability, security, and scalability of digital services across departments
- Optimize IT investments and reduce redundancy in government technology spending
- Enable seamless data exchange and integration through common standards and protocols
- Support the Digital India vision by building a future-ready, citizen-centric digital infrastructure
- Provide guidelines and reference architectures for e-governance projects and digital initiatives

The framework is applicable to all central government ministries/departments, state governments, union territories, and their agencies undertaking IT system design, development, or modernization under Digital India and related initiatives.

## Benefits

InEA provides a structured approach to IT planning, promotes reuse of components, ensures compliance with national standards (such as e-Gov standards), enhances system reliability and security, facilitates cloud adoption, and supports long-term maintainability and scalability of government digital systems.
