# Jharkhand MSME Policy 2015 (Revised)

## Overview

The Jharkhand MSME Policy 2015 (Revised) is a state-level initiative aimed at promoting the growth and competitiveness of Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) in Jharkhand through financial incentives, infrastructure support, and policy interventions. It focuses on enhancing productivity, encouraging technology adoption, and generating employment opportunities across the state. The policy provides a framework for simplifying regulatory processes and improving access to credit and markets for MSMEs.

## Objective

- Promote sustainable growth and competitiveness of MSMEs in Jharkhand
- Enhance access to institutional credit and financial support for MSMEs
- Encourage technology upgradation and modernization of MSME units
- Develop infrastructure and common facility centers for cluster-based development
- Simplify regulatory procedures and reduce compliance burden for MSMEs
- Promote entrepreneurship and skill development among youth and marginalized groups
- Enhance market access and export promotion for MSME products
- Support women, SC/ST, and differently-abled entrepreneurs through special provisions

## Eligibility

The policy applies to all Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises as defined under the MSMED Act, 2006, that are registered or in the process of registration with the District Industries Centre (DIC) or Udyog Aadhaar Memorandum (UAM) in Jharkhand. Enterprises must be engaged in manufacturing or service activities and have valid registration to avail benefits under the policy.

## Benefits

Benefits include capital investment subsidy, interest subsidy on term loans, reimbursement of ISO certification expenses, stamp duty exemption, electricity duty concession, and support for technology upgradation. ## Important Conditions

- Subsidy claims must be submitted within 12 months of commencement of commercial production
- Benefits are not available for enterprises engaged in trading or retail activities
- Double claiming of subsidies under central and state schemes for the same purpose is not permitted
- Subsidy is subject to availability of funds and approval by the State Level Committee
- Enterprises must remain in operation for a minimum of 3 years after availing subsidy to avoid clawback
