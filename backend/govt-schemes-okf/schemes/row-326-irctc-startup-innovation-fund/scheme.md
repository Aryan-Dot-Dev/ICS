# IRCTC Startup Innovation Fund

## Overview

IRCTC Startup Innovation Fund is a scheme under the PSU Support category aimed at fostering innovation and supporting startups through financial and non-financial assistance. The fund is managed by IRCTC, a Mini Ratna PSU under the Ministry of Railways, to encourage entrepreneurial ventures aligned with railway and tourism sectors. It provides funding, mentorship, and market access to eligible startups. The initiative reflects IRCTC's commitment to innovation and collaboration with the startup ecosystem.

## Objective

- To promote innovation in railway and tourism-related sectors
- To provide financial support to early-stage startups
- To mentor and guide startups through IRCTC's expertise and network
- To facilitate market access and pilot opportunities within IRCTC operations
- To strengthen the startup ecosystem in alignment with government initiatives

## Eligibility

Startups incorporated in India, working on innovative products, services, or processes in domains such as railway catering, tourism, digital ticketing, passenger experience, logistics, or allied sectors, with a focus on scalability and social impact. Preference may be given to startups led by women, SC/ST, or differently-abled entrepreneurs.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial support in the form of grants or equity-free funding, mentorship from IRCTC experts, access to IRCTC's vast user base for pilot testing, potential integration of solutions into IRCTC platforms, branding and visibility through IRCTC channels, and guidance on regulatory and compliance matters.
