# National TB Elimination Programme – Digital Initiative

## Overview

The National TB Elimination Programme – Digital Initiative, known as Nikshay, is a web-enabled patient management system for TB control under the National Tuberculosis Elimination Programme (NTEP). It is developed and maintained by the Central TB Division (CTD), Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India, in collaboration with the National Informatics Centre (NIC) and the World Health Organization Country office for India. Nikshay is used by health functionaries across the country in both public and private sectors to register TB cases, order tests, record treatment details, monitor adherence, and transfer cases between care providers. It also functions as the National TB Surveillance System and enables reporting of surveillance data to the Government of India.

## Objective

- To register TB cases under care
- To order various types of tests from Labs across the country
- To record treatment details and monitor treatment adherence
- To transfer cases between care providers
- To function as the National TB Surveillance System
- To enable reporting of various surveillance data to the Government of India
