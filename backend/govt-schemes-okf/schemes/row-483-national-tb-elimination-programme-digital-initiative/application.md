## Process

1. Visit the Nikshay portal at https://nikshay.in
2. Click on 'Login' to access the system
3. For new users, register via 'New Health Facility Registration' or 'New Informant Registration' as applicable
4. Complete the required registration form with details such as state, district, block, facility, address, basic information, designation, contact number, email, and bank details (if applicable)
5. Create login credentials (username)
6. Submit the form for approval
7. Upon approval, use the login credentials to access the Nikshay dashboard
8. Navigate to relevant modules such as case registration, test ordering, treatment monitoring, etc.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Registrations are accepted year-round.
