# National Wind-Solar Hybrid Policy

## Overview

The National Wind-Solar Hybrid Policy aims to promote large grid-connected wind-solar PV hybrid systems for optimal and efficient utilization of transmission infrastructure and land, reducing the variability in renewable power generation and achieving better grid stability. It encourages new hybrid projects as well as hybridization of existing wind or solar projects. The policy provides a framework for promoting hybrid projects through various fiscal and financial incentives, and outlines the methodology for determining the tariff for hybrid projects.

## Objective

- To promote large grid-connected wind-solar PV hybrid systems
- To optimize and efficiently utilize transmission infrastructure and land
- To reduce the variability in renewable power generation
- To achieve better grid stability
- To encourage new hybrid projects and hybridization of existing wind or solar projects
- To provide a framework for promoting hybrid projects through fiscal and financial incentives
- To outline the methodology for determining the tariff for hybrid projects

## Important Conditions

- Hybrid projects must not exceed the sanctioned capacity at the point of interconnection
- The wind and solar components must be connected at a single point to the grid
- Projects must comply with the Central Electricity Authority (CEA) technical standards for grid connectivity
- The policy does not create a new financial incentive but relies on existing wind and solar schemes for benefits
- Benefits such as accelerated depreciation and customs duty exemption are subject to the provisions of the respective wind and solar policies
