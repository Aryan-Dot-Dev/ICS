# National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC) Schemes

## Overview

The National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC) is a comprehensive framework launched by the Government of India to address climate change through eight national missions focusing on solar energy, enhanced energy efficiency, sustainable habitat, water, sustaining the Himalayan ecosystem, Green India, sustainable agriculture, and strategic knowledge for climate change. It aims to promote ecological sustainability while supporting economic development.

## Objective

- Promote understanding of climate change, adaptation and mitigation, energy efficiency and natural resource conservation
- Enhance ecological sustainability of India's development path
- Achieve sustained economic growth while addressing climate change concerns
- Promote advanced research on climate change
- Enhance and improve natural resource conservation
- Develop and deploy new technologies for climate change mitigation and adaptation
- Create awareness among stakeholders about climate change impacts and responses
- Strengthen institutional and individual capacity to address climate change

## Eligibility

The NAPCC is a policy framework and not a direct beneficiary scheme; its missions are implemented by various central ministries and state governments, with benefits flowing to sectors such as renewable energy, agriculture, water, forestry, and urban development through respective mission guidelines.

## Benefits

The NAPCC provides a coordinated approach to climate action, enabling access to financial and technical support under its eight missions, promoting renewable energy adoption, energy efficiency, sustainable agriculture, water conservation, afforestation, and climate-resilient infrastructure development across sectors.
