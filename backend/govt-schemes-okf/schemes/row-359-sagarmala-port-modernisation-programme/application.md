## Process

1. Project identification by port authorities, state governments, or implementing agencies.
2. Preparation of Detailed Project Report (DPR) with techno-economic feasibility.
3. Submission of DPR to the Sagarmala Cell or concerned nodal agency.
4. Appraisal by the Project Appraisal Committee (PAC) or Independent Expert Body.
5. Recommendation for approval by the Sagarmala Apex Committee.
6. Final approval by the National Sagarmala Apex Committee (NSAC).
7. Signing of Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) or Concession Agreement.
8. Disbursal of funds in tranches based on project progress.
9. Monitoring and evaluation by the Sagarmala Monitoring Cell.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — project proposals can be submitted throughout the year, subject to appraisal cycles and availability of funds.
