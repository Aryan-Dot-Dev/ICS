# Sagarmala Port Modernisation Programme

## Overview

The Sagarmala Port Modernisation Programme is a flagship initiative of the Government of India aimed at modernizing India's ports through port-led development, enhancing port infrastructure, improving connectivity, and promoting industrialization and coastal community development. It focuses on port modernization, new port development, port connectivity enhancement, port-linked industrialization, and coastal community development to reduce logistics costs and boost EXIM trade.

## Objective

- Modernize and develop port infrastructure
- Enhance port connectivity through rail, road, inland waterways, and coastal shipping
- Promote port-led industrialization and coastal economic zones
- Develop coastal communities through skill development and livelihood projects
- Reduce logistics cost for EXIM and domestic cargo
- Improve efficiency, productivity, and competitiveness of Indian ports
- Develop new major ports and upgrade existing ones
- Promote inland waterways and coastal shipping as sustainable transport modes


## Target Beneficiaries

**port authorities**, **state governments**, **private sector operators**, **logistics providers**, **coastal communities**


## Key Features

- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — project proposals can be submitted throughout the year, subject to appraisal cycles and availability of funds.
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

Eligible beneficiaries include major ports under the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways, state governments, state maritime boards, private sector entities through public-private partnerships (PPP), and other government agencies involved in port infrastructure and connectivity projects, subject to project appraisal and approval under the Sagarmala framework.
