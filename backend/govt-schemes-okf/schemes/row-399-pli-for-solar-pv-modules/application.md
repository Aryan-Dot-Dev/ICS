## Process

1. Eligible manufacturers must register on the ALMM portal.
2. Submit application for PLI scheme through the designated MNRE portal.
3. Provide details of manufacturing capacity, technology, and investment plans.
4. Submit required documents including incorporation certificates, GSTIN, PAN, and bank details.
5. Application is evaluated by the Project Appraisal Committee (PAC).
6. Upon approval, a Letter of Award (LoA) is issued.
7. Incentives are claimed annually based on verified incremental sales.
8. Submission of utilization certificates and audit reports is required for each claim.
