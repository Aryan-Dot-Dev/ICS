# PLI for Solar PV Modules

## Overview

The PLI for Solar PV Modules is a Production Linked Incentive scheme aimed at boosting domestic manufacturing of high-efficiency solar photovoltaic modules in India. It supports the national goal of achieving 500 GW of non-fossil fuel-based electricity generation capacity by 2030 and reducing reliance on imports. The scheme provides financial incentives based on incremental sales of domestically manufactured solar PV modules over a five-year period. It is part of broader efforts to strengthen India's solar manufacturing ecosystem under the Atmanirbhar Bharat initiative.

## Objective

- Enhance domestic manufacturing capacity of high-efficiency solar PV modules
- Reduce dependence on imported solar PV modules
- Achieve 500 GW of non-fossil fuel-based electricity generation capacity by 2030
- Support Atmanirbhar Bharat through self-reliance in solar manufacturing
- Attract investment in the solar PV manufacturing sector
- Create employment opportunities in the renewable energy sector
- Promote technological advancement and innovation in solar PV manufacturing

## Eligibility

Domestic manufacturers of solar PV modules who are registered under the Approved List of Models and Manufacturers (ALMM) and meet the eligibility criteria as per the PLI scheme guidelines issued by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE). Applicants must have a valid GSTIN, PAN, and be engaged in the manufacturing of solar PV modules in India.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial incentives on incremental sales of domestically manufactured solar PV modules over a five-year period, support for scaling up manufacturing capacity, enhancement of domestic value chain in solar energy, reduction in import bills, and strengthening of India's position in the global solar supply chain.

## Important Conditions

- Incentives are contingent upon achieving minimum domestic value addition
- Only manufacturers listed under ALMM are eligible
- Incentive disbursement is subject to annual verification of sales and production
- Scheme is subject to mid-term review and possible modification
- Benefits are available only for a fixed period of five years from the date of award
