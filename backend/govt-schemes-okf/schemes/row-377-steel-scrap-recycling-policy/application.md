## Process

1. Identify eligibility under the Steel Scrap Recycling Policy guidelines issued by the Ministry of Steel.
2. Ensure compliance with quality standards and processing norms for steel scrap as per Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) and Ministry notifications.
3. Register steel scrap processing or recycling units with relevant state pollution control boards and obtain necessary environmental clearances.
4. Maintain records of scrap collection, processing, and supply to steel plants for audit and verification.
5. Apply for recognition or benefits under linked schemes such as PLI, DMI&SP, or R&D Scheme via the respective portals (e.g., https://research.steel.gov.in/Applicant for R&D, https://www.pli.gov.in for PLI).
6. Submit required documents including mill test certificates, consignment details, and process descriptions.
7. Await verification and approval from the Ministry of Steel or designated agencies.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications and compliance are accepted year-round as per ongoing policy implementation.
