# Steel Scrap Recycling Policy

## Overview

The Steel Scrap Recycling Policy is a government initiative aimed at promoting the recycling of steel scrap to enhance resource efficiency, reduce dependence on raw material imports, and support sustainable steel production in India. It provides a framework for the collection, processing, and utilization of steel scrap in the steelmaking process, encouraging the use of secondary raw materials in electric arc furnaces and induction furnaces. The policy aligns with national goals of circular economy, waste utilization, and decarbonization of the steel sector by reducing carbon emissions associated with primary steel production.

## Objective

- Promote the use of steel scrap as a raw material in steelmaking to reduce reliance on virgin iron ore and coking coal
- Enhance resource efficiency and support circular economy in the steel sector
- Reduce carbon footprint and energy consumption in steel production through scrap-based routes
- Develop organized steel scrap collection, segregation, and processing infrastructure
- Encourage technological upgradation in scrap processing and quality improvement
- Support MSMEs and small-scale units engaged in steel scrap recycling
- Ensure availability of quality steel scrap for secondary steel producers
- Integrate scrap recycling into national steel policy goals for self-reliance and sustainability

## Eligibility

Eligible beneficiaries include steel producers, MSMEs, recycling units, and scrap processors engaged in the collection, segregation, processing, and supply of steel scrap for use in electric arc furnaces, induction furnaces, and other steelmaking routes, as per guidelines issued by the Ministry of Steel under the Steel Scrap Recycling Policy.
