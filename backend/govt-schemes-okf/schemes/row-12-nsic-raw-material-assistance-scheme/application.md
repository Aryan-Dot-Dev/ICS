## Process

1. Download the application form from NSIC’s website or obtain it from the nearest branch office.
2. Fill the application form with required details.
3. Submit the duly filled application form along with prescribed documents to the nearest NSIC branch office.
4. Preliminary appraisal and unit inspection are carried out by NSIC.
5. Sanction of limit to the unit is granted.
6. Agreement is signed between NSIC and the unit.
7. Assistance is disbursed to the unit.
