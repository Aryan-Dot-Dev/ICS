# NSIC Raw Material Assistance Scheme

## Overview

The NSIC Raw Material Assistance Scheme aims to help MSMEs by financing the purchase of raw materials (both indigenous and imported) through credit support, enabling them to focus on manufacturing quality products. It facilitates procurement with credit up to 180 days, allowing MSMEs to benefit from bulk purchase raw materials at bulk supplier rates and avail cash discounts. The scheme requires security in the form of a Bank Guarantee from approved banks and involves a process of application, appraisal, unit inspection, sanction of limit, agreement signing, and disbursement of assistance.

## Objective

- Facilitate procurement of raw material with credit support up to 180 days
- Help MSMEs avail economics of purchases like bulk purchase and cash discount
- Enable MSMEs to focus better on manufacturing quality products
- Provide financial assistance for raw material procurement against Bank Guarantee

## Important Conditions

- Trading activities are not allowed under the Raw Material Assistance Scheme
- Maximum exposure to a single unit is Rs. 1000 Lakhs for manufacturing units and Rs. 600 lakhs for service units
- Maximum exposure for infrastructure units is Rs. 800 lakhs for individual unit and Rs. 1000 Lakhs for group companies (2 or more)
- Ceiling for maximum exposure to all associate/sister concerns or connected accounts belonging to a 'GROUP' is Rs. 2000 lakh for manufacturing units, Rs. 1500 Lakh for service units, and Rs. 1000 Lakh for Infrastructure unit
- Validity of the limit sanctioned is one year, renewable for one year provided conduct of the account was satisfactory during the last year
- Credit period is 180 days, extendable on request of MSME with consent of Zonal Head, provided unit serves interest, total outstanding is within BG limit, and BG remains valid till extended period
- Payment is released to the supplier as requested by the MSME, not directly to the MSME
- Penal charges on delayed payment beyond 180 days are 1.25% per quarter on the outstanding amount
- Concessional interest rates apply only if timely repayments are made;
- otherwise, the higher rate for 'Other units' is charged
