# Central Silk Board – Bivoltine Silk Development Programme

## Overview

The Central Silk Board – Bivoltine Silk Development Programme is a Central Sector Scheme under the Ministry of Textiles, Government of India, aimed at enhancing bivoltine silk production through research, extension, seed production, cocoon production practices, marketing, credit facilitation, policy options, and stakeholder participation. It was formulated during the XII Five Year Plan (2012-13 to 2016-17) with the goal of increasing bivoltine silk production from 1685 MT to 5000 MT, representing a 197% increase. The programme focuses on mulberry sector development and includes the formation of 179 clusters across India to promote large-scale bivoltine sericulture, particularly targeting economically weaker sections in rural areas.

## Objective

- Enhance bivoltine silk (3A grade and above) production from 1685 MT to 5000 MT
- Formulate about 179 clusters all over India for bivoltine sericulture promotion
- Promote import substitute bivoltine silk generation
- Generate employment particularly for economically weaker sections in rural areas
- Strengthen research, extension, seed production, cocoon production practices, marketing, credit facilitation, policy options, and stakeholder participation in decision-making
