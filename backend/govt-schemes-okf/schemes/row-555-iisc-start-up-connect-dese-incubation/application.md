## Process

1. Submit an online application through the IISc Start-up Connect portal detailing the startup idea, team, and technology.
2. Provide documentation including incorporation certificate, team profiles, and a brief business plan or pitch deck.
3. Applications are reviewed by the IISc incubation committee based on innovation, feasibility, and alignment with IISc's strengths.
4. Shortlisted candidates are invited for an interview or presentation.
5. Final selection is made, and successful startups are onboarded into the incubation program with access to resources and mentorship.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year.
