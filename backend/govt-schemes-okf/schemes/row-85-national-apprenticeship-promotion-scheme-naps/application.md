# National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS) — Application

## Channels

```yaml
application:
  mode:
    - online
  official_portal: "https://www.apprenticeshipindia.gov.in/"
  implementing_agency: "Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE)"
  deadline: "Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round."
  contact: "Email: vs.arvind@nic.in | Helpline: Not explicitly mentioned in evidence"
```

## Process

1. Establishments register on the apprenticeshipindia.org portal.
2. Candidates register as apprentices on the portal.
3. Establishment selects a candidate and issues a contract through the portal.
4. Candidate signs the contract within 7 days of issuance.
5. The signed contract is routed to the Apprenticeship Advisor for verification/approval within 30 days.
6. Upon approval, stipend payroll is created and establishment pays the apprentice.
7. Establishment submits reimbursement claim through the portal for government share of stipend.
8. Claims are processed and funds transferred to the establishment's registered bank account.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round.
