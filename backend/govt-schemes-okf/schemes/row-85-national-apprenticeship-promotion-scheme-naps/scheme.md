# National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS)

## Overview

The National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS) is a Government of India initiative under the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE) to promote apprenticeship training and provide financial support to establishments engaging apprentices. It aims to bridge the skill gap by offering stipend support to apprentices and incentivizing industries to participate in skill development. NAPS operates through a digital portal for registration, contract management, and reimbursement processes, targeting youth and establishments across India.

## Objective

- Promote apprenticeship training across sectors
- Provide financial support to establishments engaging apprentices
- Bridge the skill gap between education and industry requirements
- Increase youth employability through on-the-job training
- Support implementation of the Apprentices Act, 1961
- Enable digital processing of apprenticeship contracts and claims
- Encourage industry participation in skill development initiatives

## Important Conditions

- Reimbursement under NAPS is not available for extended training periods due to lockdown if stipend was not paid during the pause
- Online training proposals under consideration do not qualify for NAPS reimbursement
- Backdating of termination date is not allowed for contracts enjoying NAPS benefits
- Portal will not accept termination requests if remaining training period is ≤ 45 days
- Establishments must maintain 100% profile completion to issue contracts
- Candidates must complete profiles to apply for apprenticeships
