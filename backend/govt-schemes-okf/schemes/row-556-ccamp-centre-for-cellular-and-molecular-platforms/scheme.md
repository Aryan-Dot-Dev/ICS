# CCAMP (Centre for Cellular and Molecular Platforms)

## Overview

CCAMP (Centre for Cellular and Molecular Platforms) is an initiative supported by the Department of Biotechnology, Government of India, established in 2009 to enable cutting-edge life science research and innovation. It serves as a catalyst for bioscience research and entrepreneurship by providing research, development, training, and services through state-of-the-art technology platforms. CCAMP promotes entrepreneurship and innovation through seed funding schemes, mentorship programs, and bio-incubation facilities, fostering an entrepreneur-friendly culture in academic and research environments.

## Objective

- Enable cutting-edge life science research and innovation
- Facilitate bioscience research and entrepreneurship through technology platforms
- Promote entrepreneurship and innovation via seed funding schemes and mentorship programs
- Provide bio-incubation facilities to support early-stage startups
- Bridge the gap between academic research and industry through translation and commercialization
- Support startups in healthcare, agriculture, environment, and industrial biotechnology sectors
- Nurture ideas and innovations into successful startups through various seed funding schemes
- Widen the pipeline of startups and ensure long-term operational sustainability of incubators

## Important Conditions

- Funding is scheme-specific and not guaranteed;
- meeting eligibility does not assure selection
- Some schemes require equity sharing or co-investment from private partners
- Startups must not have received excessive prior government support (e.g., SISFS bars those with >INR 10 lakh from other Central/State schemes)
- Certain schemes are restricted to specific sectors (e.g., SIIP focuses on 'Aging and Health' and 'Food and Nutrition')
- Residency at C-CAMP incubation may be mandatory for some schemes (e.g., NIDHI SEED requires 3 months of incubation)
- Funds must be used for approved activities only (e.g., proof-of-concept, prototyping, market entry)
- Intellectual property generated may be subject to sharing or reporting obligations
- Continued funding may depend on milestone achievement and progress reporting
