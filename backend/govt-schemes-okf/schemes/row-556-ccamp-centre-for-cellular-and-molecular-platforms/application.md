3. Prepare required documents including Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, business plan, pitch deck, details of technology/IP, team credentials, and financials.
4. Apply via the designated application portal (commonly https://www.ccamp.res.in/funding/content/application-form-seed-funding-schemes or scheme-specific URLs like https://www.birac.nic.in/desc_new.php?id=395 for SIIP).
5. Submit the application before the deadline (if applicable).
6. Undergo screening and evaluation by a panel of experts based on scientific strength, commercialization potential, societal impact, team strength, and implementation plan.
7. Shortlisted candidates may be invited for presentations or interviews.
8. Await final selection and funding disbursement upon agreement to terms (e.g., equity sharing, milestone reporting).
