# IN-SPACe Authorization for Private Space Launch

## Overview

IN-SPACe Authorization for Private Space Launch is a regulatory framework established by the Indian National Space Promotion and Authorization Center (IN-SPACe) to enable private sector participation in space activities. It provides authorization for private entities to conduct space launches, establish space infrastructure, and operate space-based services in India. The scheme aims to promote private investment and innovation in the space sector by streamlining regulatory processes and ensuring compliance with national and international space laws.

## Objective

- Promote private sector participation in space activities
- Provide a single-window authorization system for space launches and operations
- Ensure compliance with national and international space laws and safety standards
- Facilitate access to ISRO facilities and expertise for private entities
- Encourage innovation and competitiveness in the Indian space economy
- Streamline regulatory processes to reduce time and cost for private space ventures

## Eligibility

Private entities including companies, startups, and MSMEs registered under Indian law, seeking to undertake space activities such as launch vehicle operations, satellite manufacturing, ground station establishment, or space-based services. Applicants must demonstrate technical capability, financial viability, and adherence to safety and regulatory norms as specified by IN-SPACe.

## Benefits

Authorization to conduct space launches and operations in India, access to ISRO infrastructure and technical support, regulatory clarity and single-window clearance, facilitation of technology transfer and collaboration with ISRO, and recognition as a authorized non-governmental entity (NGE) in the space sector.

## Important Conditions

- Authorization is subject to periodic review and renewal
- Applicants must comply with the Space Activities Bill (pending) and existing space policy guidelines
- Use of ISRO facilities is subject to availability and separate agreements
- National security clearance may be required for certain sensitive space activities
- Authorization does not guarantee access to funding or market opportunities
