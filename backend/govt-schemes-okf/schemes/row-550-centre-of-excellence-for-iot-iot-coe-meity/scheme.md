# Centre of Excellence for IoT (IoT CoE) MeitY

## Overview

The Centre of Excellence for IoT (IoT CoE) under MeitY aims to foster innovation and development in the Internet of Things ecosystem in India. It supports research, prototyping, and commercialization of IoT solutions through collaboration with industry, academia, and startups. The initiative focuses on building infrastructure, skill development, and creating testbeds for IoT applications across sectors like agriculture, healthcare, and smart cities.

## Objective

- Establish state-of-the-art infrastructure for IoT research and development
- Promote innovation and entrepreneurship in IoT technologies
- Support prototyping, testing, and validation of IoT solutions
- Facilitate industry-academia collaboration for technology transfer
- Develop skilled manpower in IoT through training and workshops
- Create sector-specific IoT testbeds for real-world validation
- Encourage development of indigenous IoT products and solutions
- Support scaling of IoT innovations for market deployment
