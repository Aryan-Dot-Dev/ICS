# One Station One Product (OSOP) Scheme

## Overview

The One Station One Product (OSOP) Scheme is an initiative by Indian Railways to promote local products and artisans by providing them a platform at railway stations to sell their goods. The scheme aims to enhance the livelihood of local communities, promote indigenous crafts, and improve passenger experience by offering region-specific products at stations. It supports the vision of 'Vocal for Local' and encourages entrepreneurship at the grassroots level.

## Objective

- To provide a sales outlet for local products at railway stations
- To promote indigenous crafts and regional specialties
- To enhance income opportunities for local artisans and self-help groups
- To improve passenger experience by offering authentic local products
- To support the 'Vocal for Local' initiative of the Government of India
- To encourage entrepreneurship at the station level
- To preserve and promote traditional art forms and culinary heritage

## Eligibility

Local artisans, self-help groups, cooperatives, NGOs, and MSMEs producing indigenous or region-specific products are eligible to participate in the OSOP scheme. Preference is given to products that reflect the cultural, culinary, or artisanal heritage of the station's locality.

## Benefits

Selected participants get allocated space at designated railway stations to display and sell their products. They benefit from increased visibility, direct access to a large passenger footfall, and opportunities for business growth. The scheme provides non-financial support such as training in packaging, branding, and customer service, along with facilitation for digital payments and marketing…

## Important Conditions

- Allocation of space is subject to availability and safety regulations at the station
- Products must comply with food safety (FSSAI), quality, and legal standards where applicable
- No prohibited or hazardous items can be sold under the scheme
- Beneficiaries must maintain hygiene and adhere to station cleanliness norms
- The railway administration reserves the right to terminate allocation in case of violations or complaints
