## Process

1. Identify the nodal officer or station manager at the respective railway station.
2. Submit a proposal detailing the product, its local significance, production capacity, and business plan.
3. Provide samples and documentation such as business registration, GST details, and product photographs.
4. Await evaluation by the station-level committee.
5. Upon approval, sign an agreement for space allocation and comply with operational guidelines.
6. Begin operations after receiving clearance from railway authorities.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year based on station availability and product suitability.
