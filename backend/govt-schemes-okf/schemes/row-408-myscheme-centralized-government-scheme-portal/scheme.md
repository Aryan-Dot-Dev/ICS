# MyScheme – Centralized Government Scheme Portal

## Overview

myScheme is a National Platform that aims to offer one-stop search and discovery of Government schemes from both Central and State/UT Governments. It provides an innovative, technology-based solution to discover scheme information based on citizen eligibility. The platform helps citizens find the right Government schemes for them and guides on how to apply, eliminating the need to visit multiple Government websites.

## Objective

- Offer one-stop search and discovery of Government schemes
- Provide technology-based solution to discover scheme information based on eligibility
- Help citizens find the right Government schemes for them
- Guide on how to apply for different Government schemes
- Eliminate the need to visit multiple Government websites
- Streamline the government-user interface for government schemes and benefits
- Reduce time and effort required to find and avail a government scheme
