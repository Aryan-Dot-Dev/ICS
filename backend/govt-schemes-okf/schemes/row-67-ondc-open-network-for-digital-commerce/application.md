## Process

1. Sign up on the ONDC Portal and create a profile.
2. Explore introductory resources and attend community webinars.
3. Select your role (Buyer Network Participant, Seller Network Participant, Technology Service Provider, or Gateway) and domain via the portal.
4. Integrate with ONDC APIs and test your solution in Staging, Pre-Production, and Production environments.
5. Qualify the requirements and Go-Live on the ONDC network.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round.
