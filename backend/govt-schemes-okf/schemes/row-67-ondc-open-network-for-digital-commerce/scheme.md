# ONDC (Open Network for Digital Commerce)

## Overview

ONDC (Open Network for Digital Commerce) is an initiative of the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India, incorporated as a Section 8 company in December 2021. It aims to democratize digital commerce in India by enabling an open, interoperable network that connects buyers and sellers across platforms, thereby increasing e-retail penetration from 4.3% to its maximum potential. ONDC empowers MSMEs, startups, women entrepreneurs, and small businesses by providing equal opportunities to compete with large market players and access nationwide markets through a unified protocol.

## Objective

- Increase e-retail penetration in India from 4.3% to its maximum potential
- Enable population-scale inclusion of all types and sizes of sellers in digital commerce
- Facilitate scalable and cost-effective e-commerce through an open protocol based on open-source specifications
- Empower startups to grow collaboratively by reducing cost overheads such as acquisition, digital presence, and inventory costs
- Enable easy participation in digital commerce for businesses irrespective of size, location, or digital proficiency
- Expand the digital commerce landscape by amplifying geographic and socio-economic coverage
- Enable increased trade of locally manufactured goods in India, thereby multiplying investment and production of MSMEs
- Formalize businesses by creating active digital history and enabling easier access to finance options

## Important Conditions

- ONDC is not an application, platform, intermediary, or software but a set of specifications designed to foster open, unbundled, and interoperable Open Networks.
- The network does not endorse any buyer or seller application;
- branding and logos are owned by respective network participants.
- ONDC is not a party to any transaction made through buyer applications and does not offer terms, representations, or warranties regarding such transactions.
- Financial support under the TEAM Initiative is facilitated through Seller Network Participants, not directly by ONDC.
- The TEAM Initiative targets Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs), with 50% reserved for women-led businesses, and includes special attention to SC/ST-led enterprises.
