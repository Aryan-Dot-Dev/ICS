# SIDBI SMILE Scheme

## Overview

The SIDBI SMILE Scheme is a financial assistance program designed to support MSMEs and startups by providing access to credit and other financial products. It aims to facilitate affordable financing for micro-entrepreneurs and enterprises to promote livelihood and business growth. The scheme operates through partner institutions and digital platforms to ensure seamless credit delivery and documentation. It focuses on inclusive growth, with special emphasis on women entrepreneurs and socially backward sections.

## Objective

- To facilitate access to affordable credit to informal micro-entrepreneurs/ micro-enterprises for their livelihood enterprise promotion
- To provide door step assistance with the help of Partner Institutions in credit processes including application information, digital documentation, and collections
- To ensure faster credit delivery through seamless digital integration between OPI and SIDBI and with the help of Prayaas App/ portal
- To support women entrepreneurs and socially backward sections of society
- To promote enterprise development and scaling from sustenance to enterprise level
