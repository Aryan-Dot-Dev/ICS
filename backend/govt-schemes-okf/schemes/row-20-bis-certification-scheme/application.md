
## Process

1. Identify the relevant Indian Standard for the product or service.
2. Ensure compliance with the standard through appropriate manufacturing and quality control.
3. Apply online via the BIS portal (www.manakonline.in) for licensing or registration.
4. Submit required documents and pay applicable fees.
5. Undergo factory inspection and testing by BIS or recognized labs.
6. Upon successful evaluation, grant of licence or registration is issued.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round.
