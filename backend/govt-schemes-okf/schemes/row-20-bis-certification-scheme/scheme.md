# BIS Certification Scheme

## Overview

The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) is India's national standards body responsible for standardization, hallmarking, and conformity assessment. It develops and publishes Indian standards, implements product certification schemes including compulsory registration for electronics and IT goods, operates hallmarking schemes for gold and silver jewellery, and provides laboratory services and management system certifications. BIS also engages in consumer affairs, training, and international standardization activities.

## Objective

- Harmonious development of standardization, marking, and quality certification activities
- Provide thrust to standardization and quality control for industrial growth and consumer protection
- Develop consensus-based voluntary national standards through stakeholder consultation
- Operate product certification, hallmarking, and management systems certification schemes
- Ensure conformity of goods and services to relevant Indian standards through testing and inspection

## Eligibility

Eligibility varies by scheme: For product certification, manufacturers must comply with relevant Indian standards; for hallmarking, jewellers and assaying centres must meet BIS requirements; for laboratory recognition, outside labs must meet IS/ISO/IEC 17025 criteria; for compulsory registration, electronics and IT goods manufacturers must register under the order notified by MeitY.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Benefits include legal compliance, consumer trust, market access, quality assurance, hallmarking purity guarantee, ability to use BIS standard mark, access to government tenders, and recognition under various national and international frameworks.

## Important Conditions

- Certification is voluntary except for specific products made compulsory by government orders
- Hallmarking licences are region-specific and require renewal
- Laboratory recognition requires adherence to IS/ISO/IEC 17025 standards
- Compulsory registration applies only to notified electronics and IT goods categories
