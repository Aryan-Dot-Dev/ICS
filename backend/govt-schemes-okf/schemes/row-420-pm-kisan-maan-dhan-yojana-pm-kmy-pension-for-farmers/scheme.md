# PM Kisan Maan Dhan Yojana (PM-KMY) Pension for Farmers

## Overview

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Maandhan Yojana (PM-KMY) is a voluntary and contributory pension scheme launched by the Government of India for old age protection and social security of Small and Marginal Farmers (SMFs). It provides an assured monthly pension of Rs. 3000/- after attaining the age of 60 years, with matching contributions from the government. The scheme is administered through Common Service Centres (CSCs) and managed by Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) as the pension fund manager.

## Objective

- Provide old age protection and social security to Small and Marginal Farmers
- Ensure a minimum assured pension of Rs. 3000/- per month after attaining the age of 60 years
- Offer family pension benefits to the spouse of the subscriber
- Provide pension benefits in case of permanent disability before age 60
- Allow exit options with return of contributions and interest under specified conditions
- Enable contributions through PM-KISAN benefits or other bank accounts
- Ensure zero administrative cost to subscribers
- Facilitate enrollment through Common Service Centres (CSCs) or State Nodal Officers (SNOs)

## Eligibility

All Small and Marginal Farmers having cultivable landholding up to 2 hectares falling in the age group of 18 to 40 years, whose names appear in the land records of States/UTs as on 01.08.2019 are eligible. However, the following are ineligible: SMFs covered under any other statutory social security schemes such as National Pension Scheme (NPS), Employees’ State Insurance Corporation scheme,…

## Important Conditions

- Should not be SMFs covered under any other statutory social security schemes such as National Pension Scheme (NPS), Employees’ State Insurance Corporation scheme, Employees’ Provident Fund Organization Scheme etc.
- Farmers who have opted for Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maandhan Yojana and Pradhan Mantri Vyapari Maandhan administered by the Ministry of Labour & Employment are not eligible.
- All Institutional Land holders, former and present holders of constitutional posts, former and present Ministers/State Ministers and former/present Members of Lok Sabha/Rajya Sabha/State Legislative Assemblies/State Legislative Councils, former and present Mayors of Municipal Corporations, former and present Chairpersons of District Panchayats are not eligible.
- All serving or retired officers and employees of Central/State Government Ministries/Offices/Departments and their field units, Central or State PSEs and Attached offices/Autonomous Institutions under Government as well as regular employees of the Local Bodies (Excluding Multi Tasking Staff / Class IV/Group D employees) are not eligible.
- All Persons who paid Income Tax in last assessment year are not eligible.
- Professionals like Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Chartered Accountants, and Architects registered with Professional bodies and carrying out profession by undertaking practice are not eligible.
- In case of incorrect declaration, the beneficiary shall be liable to get back his contributions without any interest thereon and the Central Government’s matching contribution will be stopped.
- Land holding is the criteria to be eligible to get the benefit under the scheme;
- individuals without land holding in their name are not eligible.
- Any individual farmer owning more than 2 hectare of cultivable land will not be eligible to get benefit under the scheme.
