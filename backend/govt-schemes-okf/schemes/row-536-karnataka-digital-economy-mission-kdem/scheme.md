# Karnataka Digital Economy Mission (KDEM)

## Overview

The Karnataka Digital Economy Mission (KDEM) is a state-level initiative launched by the Government of Karnataka to accelerate the growth of the digital economy in the state. It aims to position Karnataka as a global leader in digital innovation by supporting startups, fostering innovation, and developing digital infrastructure. The mission focuses on creating a conducive ecosystem for digital enterprises through policy support, skill development, and industry-academia collaboration.

## Objective

- Position Karnataka as a global leader in the digital economy
- Foster innovation and support startups in emerging technologies
- Develop digital infrastructure across the state
- Promote skill development and talent creation for digital jobs
- Encourage industry-academia collaboration for R&D and innovation
- Attract investments in digital sectors and create employment opportunities
