# IIT Hyderabad Deep Tech Startup Incubator

## Overview

The IIT Hyderabad Deep Tech Startup Incubator is part of the Institute Innovation and Startup Policy, which supports innovation and entrepreneurship across all stages from germination to scaling. It provides mentorship, infrastructure, funding support, and access to incubation facilities like iTIC, CfHE, and FabCI for students, staff, and faculty. The policy aligns with the National Innovation and Startup Policy (NISP) and enables academic initiatives such as IEP projects, internships, and startup registration.

## Objective

- Synchronize various innovation and entrepreneurship initiatives to fit the NISP framework
- Enable creation of a robust innovation and startup ecosystem
- Support the entrepreneur through all stages: germination, pre-incubation, incubation, acceleration, and maturing/scaling
- Integrate education activities with enterprise-related activities
- Provide financial support for prototype building and sustenance through schemes like BUILD and Entrepreneurs-in-Residence (EIR)
- Offer mentorship, sitting space, basic business facilities, and VC funding in exchange for equity
- Facilitate technology licensing, consultancy, and paid use of institute facilities by startups
- Set the overall vision for promoting innovation and entrepreneurship through Institution’s Innovation Councils (IICs)

## Eligibility

UG & PG Students (see 5.3 for eligibility), Staff, Faculty. For student startups, registration with iTIC is required. Faculty-driven startups must be incubated in one of the institute's incubators (iTIC, CfHE, or FabCI) unless exceptional justification is provided for external incubation.

## Important Conditions

- Faculty startups must be incubated in institute incubators (iTIC, CfHE, FabCI) unless exceptional justification is provided for external incubation
- IEP registration under Core Elective category requires DUGC approval
- Semester leave requires sound proposal and commitment to complete degree within maximum allowed years
- Deferred placement eligibility requires not having appeared in regular placement season and having registered for a startup or enrolled with an incubation centre
- Startup norms for faculty do not apply to roles as mentor or consultant (no equity holding)
