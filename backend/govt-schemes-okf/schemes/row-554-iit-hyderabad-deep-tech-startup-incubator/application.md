## Process

1. For student startups: Register the startup at iTIC with oneself as (if pursuing internship in one's own startup).
2. For BUILD project: Pitch idea to obtain financial support (no academic prerequisites or approvals needed).
3. For faculty startups: Obtain approval for incubation in iTIC, CfHE, or FabCI.
4. For academic initiatives: Enroll in IEP (Innovation and Entrepreneurship Project) as free/core/additional elective or register for Minor in Entrepreneurship or Techno-Entrepreneurship Dual Degree.
5. For internships: Pursue sixth-semester internship in a startup (own or incubated at IITH) or other semesters with DUGC approval and satisfactory course plan.
6. For semester leave: Apply through advisor/program coordinator at least one month before semester commencement, evaluated by committee chaired by Dean Academics with HOD and FIC Incubation as members.
7. For deferred placement: Opt out of regular placement season and register for a startup or enroll with an incubation centre.
