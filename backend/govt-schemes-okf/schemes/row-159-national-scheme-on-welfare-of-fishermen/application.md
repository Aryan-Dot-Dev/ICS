## Process

1. State Governments/UTs submit project proposals for fisher welfare activities to the Department of Fisheries.
2. Proposals are appraised by the Department for technical and financial feasibility.
3. Approved projects are sanctioned with central share allocation.
4. Funds are released in installments based on utilization certificates and progress reports.
5. States implement the schemes through designated agencies and monitor outcomes.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — proposals can be submitted throughout the year as per budget cycle and scheme guidelines.
