# National Scheme on Welfare of Fishermen

## Overview

The National Scheme on Welfare of Fishermen is a centrally sponsored scheme under the Department of Fisheries, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying, aimed at providing financial assistance and welfare measures to fishermen and fish farmers. It supports livelihood enhancement, social security, and infrastructure development for the fisheries sector. The scheme is implemented across India and targets traditional and small-scale fishers.

## Objective

- Provide financial assistance for livelihood support to fishermen
- Enhance social security coverage for fishers and their families
- Support infrastructure development for fisheries welfare
- Promote sustainable fisheries practices through welfare interventions
- Strengthen institutional mechanisms for fisher welfare delivery
- Facilitate access to credit and insurance for fishers
- Improve living and working conditions of fisher communities

## Eligibility

Traditional and small-scale fishermen, fish farmers, and members of fisher communities engaged in capture fisheries, aquaculture, or allied activities, who are citizens of India and belong to economically weaker sections, as identified by State Governments/UTs based on socio-economic criteria.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial assistance for housing, sanitation, drinking water, community assets, and livelihood support; access to accident insurance and health coverage; support for formation of fisher cooperatives and self-help groups; skill development and training programs; and subsidies for fisheries-related equipment and infrastructure.
