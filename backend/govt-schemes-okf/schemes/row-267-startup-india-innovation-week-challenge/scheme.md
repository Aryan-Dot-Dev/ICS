# Startup India Innovation Week Challenge

## Overview

Startup India Innovation Week Challenge is a program under the Startup India initiative that invites startups to participate in innovation challenges and competitions to showcase their solutions, gain recognition, and access support such as mentorship, networking, and potential funding opportunities. It aims to foster innovation across sectors by connecting startups with industry experts, investors, and government bodies. The challenge is part of broader efforts to strengthen the startup ecosystem through structured programs and events.

## Objective

- To provide a platform for startups to showcase innovative solutions
- To connect startups with mentors, investors, and industry experts
- To promote sector-specific innovation in areas like DeepTech, AgriTech, and SpaceTech
- To offer recognition and support through cash prizes and handholding
- To strengthen the startup ecosystem via structured challenges and events

## Eligibility

Startups must be DPIIT recognised to participate in the Innovation Week Challenge. Eligibility for DPIIT recognition includes being incorporated as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or partnership firm, being within 10 years of incorporation (20 years for DeepTech), having turnover not exceeding INR 200 crore (INR 300 crore for DeepTech), and working towards innovation, development, or improvement…


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Participants gain access to mentorship, networking opportunities with investors and industry leaders, potential cash prizes, handholding support, visibility through national platforms, and eligibility for further government schemes such as Seed Fund Scheme, Fund of Funds, and Credit Guarantee Scheme. Winners may also receive support for scaling and market access.
