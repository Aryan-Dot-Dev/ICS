# Champions Portal for MSME Grievance Resolution

## Overview

The Champions Portal for MSME Grievance Resolution is a technology-driven platform launched by the Ministry of MSME to help MSMEs resolve grievances related to finance, raw materials, labor, regulatory permissions, and other issues, particularly in the context of the Covid-19 situation. It aims to capture new opportunities, identify and encourage potential MSMEs, and provide handholding and support through a unified system. The portal integrates with other grievance redressal systems like CPGRAMS and uses AI, machine learning, and data analytics to reduce human intervention and delay.

## Objective

- Grievance Redressal: resolve the problems of MSMEs including those of finance, raw materials, labor, regulatory permissions etc particularly in the Covid created difficult situation
- To help them capture new opportunities: including manufacturing of medical equipments and accessories like PPEs, masks, etc and supply them in National and International markets
- To identify and encourage the sparks: i.e. the potential MSMEs who are able to withstand the current rough weather and can become national and international champions
- To provide handholding, guide, empower, ease and encourage the MSME sector of India
- To capture and encourage ideas and collaborative, supportive ecosystem of experts and technocrats who can guide in the technical, managerial and financial issues faced by the MSMEs
- To capture the self pro-active administrative interventions by the functionaries of the Ministry of MSME
- To capture the VIP References coming from Hon. Ministers and other dignitaries

## Important Conditions

- Grievances will not be invited on telephone.
- They will be enlisted ONLY on and through the Portal.
- The matter should NOT remain unattended for more than three days and should NOT remain inconclusive after seven days.
- In case of extreme humanitarian case or a case of injustice and/or harassment or undue delay, if the decision is against the individual or unit, the unit or its promoters must be heard telephonically or in person.
- The Champion Control Room (CCR) functions from 8 AM to 10 PM every day.
- State level Champion Control rooms require at least 4-6 officials (preferably from Group A and B officials) from nominated institutions.
