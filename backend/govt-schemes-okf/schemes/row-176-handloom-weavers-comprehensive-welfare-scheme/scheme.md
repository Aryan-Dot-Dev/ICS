# Handloom Weavers Comprehensive Welfare Scheme

## Overview

The Handloom Weavers Comprehensive Welfare Scheme (HWCWS) is a component of the National Handloom Development Programme (NHDP) aimed at ensuring the welfare of handloom workers through provision of life and financial support to indigent handloom awardees. It is implemented by the Office of the Development Commissioner for Handlooms under the Ministry of Textiles. The scheme targets handloom weavers and their families, particularly those from disadvantaged sections of society, to provide social security and improve their livelihoods.

## Objective

- To ensure welfare of handloom workers through provision of life & accidental insurance cover
- To provide scholarships for school and higher education to the wards of handloom workers
- To provide financial support to indigent handloom awardees
- To enhance capabilities including social security, better healthcare, life insurance, work sheds
- To ensure easy credit flow at low interest rate
- To provide easy access to raw material at subsidized prices
- To develop a strong, competitive and vibrant handloom sector
- To provide sustainable employment to handloom weavers particularly belonging to disadvantaged sections

## Eligibility

Handloom weavers and allied workers engaged in pre-loom (winding, warping, dyeing etc.) and post-loom activities (finishing, calendaring etc.), including Self Help Groups, producer companies, and other handloom agencies, particularly those belonging to disadvantaged sections of society such as SC, ST, women, transgender, and differently-abled individuals.
