# IFFCO Nano Urea Initiative for Agri Startups

## Overview

IFFCO Nano Urea Initiative for Agri Startups is a program by IFFCO to promote nano-based fertilizers like Nano Urea and Nano DAP, which are designed to improve nutrient use efficiency, reduce environmental impact, and support sustainable agriculture. The initiative includes the establishment of nano fertilizer production units at Aonla, Kalol, and Phulpur, with commercial production already underway. It aligns with the Government of India's vision to reduce chemical fertilizer usage and promote eco-friendly agri-inputs.

## Objective

- Reduce usage of chemical fertilisers through nano-technology based agri-inputs
- Improve nutrient use efficiency and crop yield via nano fertilizers
- Minimize environmental footprint by reducing leaching and gaseous emissions
- Support sustainable crop production and soil health
- Enable precision and targeted application of nutrients to plants
- Promote smart agriculture and combat climate change through innovation
- Increase farmer income by reducing input costs and improving produce quality
- Ensure easy storage, transport, and handling of nano fertilizers compared to conventional ones
