# IndiaStack Initiatives

## Overview

IndiaStack Initiatives is a collection of open APIs and digital public goods that aim to unlock the economic primitives of identity, data, and payments at population scale. It is a vision for financial and social inclusion, serving as national plumbing for the Internet Age. Inspired by open-source software and interoperability, IndiaStack enables people and businesses to access services like Aadhaar-based identity verification, digital signatures, UPI payments, and consent-based data sharing through DEPA.

## Objective

- Unlock economic primitives of identity, data, and payments at population scale
- Promote financial and social inclusion through digital infrastructure
- Enable interoperability and open access to digital services via standardized APIs
- Empower users with control over their personal data via DEPA framework
- Support innovation in fintech, healthtech, and other sectors through open networks
- Facilitate paperless, secure, and cost-effective service delivery

## Eligibility

Eligibility varies by component: Aadhaar-based services (e-auth, e-KYC) are available to banks, licensed NBFCs, telecom companies, and government bodies; other entities may use QR code scans, e-Aadhaar, or offline XML. For eSign ASP onboarding, eligible entities include Central/State Government ministries, authorities under Central/State Act, not-for-profit organizations of national importance,…


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Non-financial benefits include secure digital identity verification, legally valid digital signatures, access to digitally signed documents via DigiLocker, consent-based data sharing, and open network participation. Financial benefits include reduced KYC costs (from ~$23 to ~$0.15), real-time low-cost payments via UPI, and access to credit and financial products through verified data trails.
