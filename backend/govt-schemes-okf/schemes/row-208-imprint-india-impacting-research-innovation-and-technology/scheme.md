# IMPRINT India (Impacting Research Innovation and Technology)

## Overview

IMPRINT India (Impacting Research Innovation and Technology) is a joint initiative of the Ministry of Education and the Ministry of Defence aimed at addressing major engineering and technology challenges relevant to national development through collaborative research between premier academic institutions and user agencies. The scheme focuses on translating research into usable technologies and products by funding high-impact, interdisciplinary projects in identified thematic areas.

## Objective

- Identify and address major engineering and technology challenges in domains relevant to national development
- Promote collaborative research between premier academic institutions (IITs, IISc, NITs, etc.) and user agencies (including defence, space, railways, etc.)
- Ensure higher funding for research into usable and affordable technology solutions
- Accelerate the translation of research into products, processes, and services for societal and strategic applications
- Strengthen industry-academia partnership in research and innovation
- Enhance the quality and relevance of research output from Indian higher educational institutions


## Target Beneficiaries

## Eligibility

The scheme invites project proposals from faculty members and researchers of premier academic institutions such as IITs, IISc, NITs, IISERs, and other centrally funded technical institutions. ## Benefits

Financial support for research and development projects, access to user agency problem statements and domain expertise, opportunity for technology transfer and product development, enhanced visibility and impact of academic research, potential for commercialization and strategic deployment of outcomes, and facilitation of industry-academia-government collaboration.
