## Process

1. Visit the T-Works website at https://t-works.telangana.gov.in
2. Explore available facilities and services based on your prototyping needs
3. Submit an inquiry or application through the online portal detailing your project requirements
4. Await review by the T-Works team
5. Upon approval, schedule access to facilities and begin prototyping with technical support
6. Iterate and refine your prototype as needed
7. Complete the project and collect your final prototype.
