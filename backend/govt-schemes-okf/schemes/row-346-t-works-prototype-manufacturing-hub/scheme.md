# T-Works Prototype Manufacturing Hub

## Overview

T-Works Prototype Manufacturing Hub is a state-level initiative by the Government of Telangana to support innovators, startups, and MSMEs in transforming ideas into tangible prototypes through access to advanced manufacturing facilities, technical expertise, and collaborative workspaces. It aims to reduce the time and cost of product development by providing end-to-end prototyping support across multiple domains including electronics, mechanical, IoT, and bio-fabrication. The hub fosters innovation by enabling rapid iteration and validation of concepts before scaling to production.

## Objective

- Provide access to advanced prototyping and manufacturing facilities for innovators
- Reduce the time and cost of developing physical prototypes
- Support startups and MSMEs in validating product ideas before scale-up
- Offer technical mentorship and expert guidance across engineering disciplines
- Promote indigenous innovation and local manufacturing capabilities
- Create a collaborative ecosystem for hardware-focused innovation in Telangana

## Eligibility

Open to individuals, startups, MSMEs, research institutions, and innovators based in Telangana who have a product idea or concept requiring prototyping support. Priority is given to those working on socially relevant, industrially viable, or technologically innovative solutions.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Access to state-of-the-art prototyping equipment (3D printers, CNC machines, PCB labs, etc.), technical support from expert engineers, design and simulation tools, material assistance, workspace for fabrication, and guidance on design for manufacturability. Users can iterate rapidly and test functionality before moving to production.
