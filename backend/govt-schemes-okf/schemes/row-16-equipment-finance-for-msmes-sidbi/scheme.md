# Equipment Finance for MSMEs (SIDBI)

## Overview

Equipment Finance for MSMEs (SIDBI) is a loan scheme designed to support micro, small, and medium enterprises in acquiring machinery and equipment for business expansion, modernization, or technology upgradation. It provides financial assistance for the purchase of plant and machinery, fixed assets, and other capital goods essential for operational efficiency and growth. The scheme targets existing MSME units with a proven track record and aims to enhance productivity and competitiveness through accessible credit.

## Objective

- Facilitate access to affordable credit for MSMEs to procure machinery and equipment
- Support technology upgradation and modernization of existing enterprises
- Enhance productivity and operational efficiency of MSMEs
- Promote business expansion and scalability through capital investment
- Strengthen the manufacturing and service capabilities of MSMEs

## Eligibility

MSME units in operation for at least three years. Mandatory Udyam & GST registration. No defaults to Bank/FIs. Cash profit in last audited financial results.

## Benefits

Quick sanction of machinery loan through automated platform using GST returns, ITRs, Bank Statement and CIBIL score. Loan amount up to Rs. 3 Crore with immediate in-principle offer. Upto 100% financing with 25% security in the form of fixed deposit. Processing fees of 0.50% plus applicable GST. MCLR Linked attractive rate of Interest. Repayment upto 60 months.
