# Craftsmen Training Scheme (CTS) – ITI

## Overview

The Craftsmen Training Scheme (CTS) was introduced by the Government of India in 1950 to ensure a steady flow of skilled workers in different trades for the domestic industry, to raise quantitatively and qualitatively the industrial production by systematic training, to reduce unemployment among the educated youth by providing them employable skills, and to cultivate and nurture a technical and industrial attitude in the minds of younger generation. The scheme operates through a vast network of Industrial Training Institutes (ITIs) spread across various States and Union Territories. It aims to meet the existing and future manpower needs of industry by shaping craftsmen through vocational training.

## Objective

- Ensure a steady flow of skilled workers in different trades for the domestic industry
- Raise quantitatively and qualitatively the industrial production by systematic training
- Reduce unemployment among the educated youth by providing them employable skills
- Cultivate and nurture a technical and industrial attitude in the minds of younger generation

## Eligibility

Age 14 years & above. Entry Criteria: 8th pass to 12th pass, most trades require 10th pass.

## Benefits

Training in 166 NSQF compliant trades with duration from 6 months to 2 years. Provides employable skills, certification through NCVT, and access to employment opportunities in industry. Includes employability skills training as part of the curriculum.
