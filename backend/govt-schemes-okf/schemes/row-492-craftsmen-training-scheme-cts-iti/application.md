## Process

1. Admission to ITIs under CTS is managed by respective State Governments / Union Territory administrations. The process involves meeting eligibility criteria (age 14+, 8th to 12th pass depending on trade), applying through state-level admission procedures, and securing a seat in the desired trade at an ITI.
