## Process

1. Eligible Lending Institutions (ELIs) must register with NCGTC as a Member Lending Institution (MLI) by submitting a signed and stamped undertaking on stamp paper of requisite value (to be stamped as an Agreement under the local Stamp Act) along with a copy of Board Resolution.
2. Submit the undertaking and documents to the Registered Office of NCGTC in Mumbai.
3. Upon successful registration, login credentials are created for the MLI on the NCGTC portal.
4. The MLI can then apply for guarantee cover on loans extended by it to eligible borrowers through the NCGTC portal.
5. After disbursement of the loan to the borrower, the MLI must submit a claim for guarantee cover to NCGTC.
6. Guarantee fee is payable by the MLI to the Trust by the 7th day of each month for the outstanding loan amount on the closing day of the previous month.

## Deadlines
