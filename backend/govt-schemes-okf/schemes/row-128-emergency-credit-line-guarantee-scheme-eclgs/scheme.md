# Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme (ECLGS)

## Overview

The Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme (ECLGS) is a Government of India initiative launched to provide liquidity support to businesses adversely impacted by the COVID-19 pandemic and other crises such as the West Asia conflict. It offers 100% guarantee coverage to Banks and NBFCs on credit extended to Business Enterprises/MSMEs based on their loan outstanding as of February 29, 2020, subject to defined criteria, to meet additional term loan or working capital requirements. The scheme has evolved through multiple versions, with ECLGS 5.0 launched in May 2026 as a targeted response to the West Asia crisis. It is administered by the National Credit Guarantee Trustee Company Ltd (NCGTC), a wholly owned entity of the Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance.

## Objective

- Provide liquidity support to businesses adversely impacted by lockdown due to COVID-19 pandemic
- Enable Banks & NBFCs to extend collateral-free loans to Business Enterprises/MSMEs
- Meet additional term loan or additional working capital requirements of eligible borrowers
- Support borrowers by encouraging MLIs to provide additional working capital term loans for business continuity and stability
- Address exceptional circumstances caused by the West Asia crisis through ECLGS 5.0

## Important Conditions

- Guarantee coverage is subject to defined criteria and loan outstanding as of February 29, 2020
- Scheme is administered through Member Lending Institutions (MLIs);
- direct borrower application is not permitted
- Guarantee fee must be paid monthly by MLIs to NCGTC Trust
- Loans must be extended for additional term loan or working capital purposes only
- ECLGS versions have varying end dates;
- e.g., ECLGS 1.0-4.0 had sanction deadline of 30.09.2021
