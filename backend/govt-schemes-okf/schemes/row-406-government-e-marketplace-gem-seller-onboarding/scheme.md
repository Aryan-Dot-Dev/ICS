# Government e-Marketplace (GeM) Seller Onboarding

## Overview

Government e-Marketplace (GeM) is a national public procurement portal that facilitates online procurement of goods and services for Central and State Government Ministries, Departments, Public Sector Units (PSUs), and affiliated entities. It aims to make public procurement transparent, efficient, and inclusive by providing a unified platform for buyers and sellers. GeM supports MSMEs, startups, and women entrepreneurs through dedicated sections and preferential treatment under public procurement policies.

## Objective

- Make public procurement process transparent, efficient and inclusive
- Act as a centralized and unified public procurement platform for government buyers and suppliers
- Drive inclusiveness and ease of doing business with the government, especially for smaller organizations such as MSMEs
- Standardize processes backed by the regulatory framework
- Enable a fully automated system through advances in technology
- Provide access to national public procurement market for sellers
- Offer special provisions and sections for startups, MSMEs and Emporium products
- Ensure fully online, paperless and contactless platform for registration and transactions

## Important Conditions

- Sellers must avoid bid rigging, cartelisation, tinkering, bypassing or any type of collusion — strict actions are taken against errant sellers
- Buyers are solely responsible for adherence to latest policy, regulations, GFRs, and public procurement manual
- Caution Money requirement for sellers has been discontinued;
- existing deposits can be withdrawn via the portal
- For TReDS integration, sellers must ensure timely onboarding and updated Udyam Registration and bank account details on both GeM and TReDS platforms
- Startup Runway 2.0 allows startups to list products irrespective of DPIIT-certification
- MSME SC/ST entrepreneurs initiative targets 4% procurement from SC/ST MSME entrepreneurs
- Womaniya initiative aligns with 3% procurement reservation for women MSME entrepreneurs
- Sellers belonging to North East states and J & K are exempted from ITR at the time of bid participation
