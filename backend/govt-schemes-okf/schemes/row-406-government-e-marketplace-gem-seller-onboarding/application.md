## Process

1. Visit the GeM portal at https://gem.gov.in
2. Click on 'Sign Up' and select 'Seller' or 'Service Provider'
3. Fill in the registration form with required details
4. Upload required documents including PAN, bank account details, and business registration documents
5. Complete PFMS verification of bank account
6. Submit the application for verification
7. Upon approval, receive GeM Seller ID and login credentials
8. Log in to the seller dashboard to start offering products/services
9. Update TReDS onboarding status in profile (Profile -> TReDS) if applicable
10. Participate in bids, reverse auctions, or direct purchase opportunities

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Registration is open year-round.
