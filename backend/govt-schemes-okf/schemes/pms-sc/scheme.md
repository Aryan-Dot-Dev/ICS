# PMS-SC — Post-Matric Scholarship for SC Students

## Overview

The Post-Matric Scholarship for SC Students (PMS-SC) is a centrally
sponsored scheme of MoSJE implemented through States/UTs: financial
support to SC students studying **post-matriculation** courses in
recognised institutions. Revised guidelines operative from 01.07.2020
introduced income ceiling **₹2.5 lakh** (parental), full course-fee
reimbursement for eligible courses (with institutional caps) directly
to institutions, and DBT maintenance allowances to students. Applications
flow through the National Scholarship Portal / State portals [S1][S2].

## Objective

To enable SC students to pursue post-matric education without financial
barriers, supporting completion of higher/professional education.

## Target Beneficiaries

SC students (certified) whose parental income does not exceed ₹2.5
lakh/annum, studying in recognised post-matric courses (Class XI to
post-doctoral, per scheme course lists) [S1][S3].

## Key Features

- Income ceiling ₹2.5 lakh parental income (revised from ₹2 lakh in
  2018; operative from the 2020 guidelines) [S3]
- Maintenance allowance by course group (DBT to student)
- Full course fee reimbursement to institutions for eligible students
  (capped per guidelines)
- Non-refundable fees covered per guidelines; hosteller/day-scholar
  rates differ
- Multi-level verification (institution → State nodal → Ministry)

## Eligibility

Summary — see [eligibility.md](eligibility.md): SC certificate, ₹2.5L
income ceiling, post-matric recognised course, full-time enrolment.

## Benefits

Maintenance allowance by group + fee reimbursement. Details:
[benefits.md](benefits.md).

## Documents

Caste certificate, income certificate, marksheets, admission proof,
bank account (student's own), Aadhaar. Details:
[documents.md](documents.md).

## Application

Via [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in/) or the State
portal (State-specific flows exist). Details:
[application.md](application.md).

## Important Conditions

- Only **one** Post-Matric Scholarship may be availed (no parallel
  central scholarships with overlap)
- Compulsory gap-year/attendance/course-continuation conditions for
  renewal
- State share/implementation rules apply (60:40 central-state sharing
  as per 2020 guidelines — NE/hilly states 90:10)

## Exclusions

Non-SC students; income above ceiling; already-holding overlapping
scholarships; discontinued courses. Structured list:
[exclusions.md](exclusions.md).
