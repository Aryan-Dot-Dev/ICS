## Process

1. State government or DISCOM prepares a Detailed Project Report (DPR) for smart metering and distribution infrastructure works as per RDSS guidelines.
2. The DPR is submitted to the nodal agency designated by the Ministry of Power for technical appraisal.
3. Upon approval, a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) is signed between the Ministry of Power, state government, and the DISCOM.
4. The project is sanctioned by the Inter-Ministerial Monitoring Committee (IMMC) based on the appraised DPR.
5. Funds are released in installments linked to physical progress and achievement of pre-defined milestones.
6. Third-party inspection agencies verify the quality and quantity of works before subsequent fund releases.
7. Performance is monitored against key indicators such as AT&C loss reduction, ACS-ARR gap, and metering coverage.

## Deadlines

- Projects under RDSS are to be completed within a stipulated timeline, generally 24-30 months from the date of sanction, with the overall scheme target to achieve objectives by 2024-25. Fund utilization and performance assessment are subject to annual review and mid-term evaluation.
