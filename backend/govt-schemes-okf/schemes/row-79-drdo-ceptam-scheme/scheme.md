# DRDO CEPTAM Scheme

## Overview

The DRDO CEPTAM Scheme is a recruitment initiative by the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) aimed at hiring personnel for technical and administrative posts such as Senior Technical Assistant-B (STA-B) and Technician-A. It is part of DRDO's efforts to strengthen its workforce in research, development, and support functions across its laboratories and establishments. The scheme is advertised periodically, with the latest notification being CEPTAM-11 for STA-B and Technician-A positions.

## Objective

- Recruit qualified candidates for technical and administrative roles in DRDO
- Strengthen DRDO's workforce in research and development establishments
- Provide employment opportunities in defence research and development
- Ensure availability of skilled personnel for DRDO's laboratories and centres
