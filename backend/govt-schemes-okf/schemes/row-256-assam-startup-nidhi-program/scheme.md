# Assam Startup – Nidhi Program

## Overview

The Assam Startup – Nidhi Program is a state-level initiative under the Assam Startup ecosystem designed to provide financial assistance to early-stage startups registered in Assam. It aims to support innovation and entrepreneurship by offering seed funding to eligible ventures. The program is managed through the Assam Startup portal and focuses on nurturing high-potential startups in their initial phases.

## Objective

- To provide financial support to early-stage startups in Assam
- To promote innovation and entrepreneurship within the state
- To assist startups in overcoming initial funding challenges
- To strengthen the Assam startup ecosystem through seed funding

## Eligibility

Startups must be registered in Assam and recognized by the Assam Startup portal. They should be in the early stage of development and demonstrate innovative potential. The startup must be a legal entity registered under the Companies Act, LLP Act, or as a partnership firm.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial support in the form of seed funding to cover initial operational and product development costs. Non-financial benefits include mentorship, networking opportunities, and access to incubation support through the Assam Startup ecosystem.
