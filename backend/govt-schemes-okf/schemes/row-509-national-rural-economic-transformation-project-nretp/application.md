## Process

1. Mobilization and formation of Self-Help Groups (SHGs) at the village level under DAY-NRLM.
2. Training and capacity building of SHGs on financial literacy, livelihood planning, and enterprise development.
3. Preparation of micro-investment plans (MIPs) by SHGs for livelihood activities.
4. Appraisal and approval of MIPs by Village Organizations (VOs) and Cluster Level Federations (CLFs).
5. Disbursement of revolving funds or community investment funds to SHGs based on approved plans.
6. Monitoring and hand-holding support by SRLMs and community resource persons.
7. Linkage with banks, markets, and value chains for enterprise growth and sustainability.

## Deadlines
