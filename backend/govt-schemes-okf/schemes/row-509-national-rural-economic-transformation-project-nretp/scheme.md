# National Rural Economic Transformation Project (NRETP)

## Overview

The National Rural Economic Transformation Project (NRETP) is a World Bank-assisted initiative under the Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana – National Rural Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NRLM) aimed at promoting rural enterprises, enhancing value chains, and supporting women’s collectives in farm and non-farm sectors. It focuses on scaling up successful livelihood interventions, strengthening producer enterprises, and facilitating access to finance and markets for rural poor households. The project operates across 13 high-poverty states and supports innovation, skill development, and digital inclusion in rural economies.

## Objective

- Promote rural enterprises and enhance value chains in farm and non-farm sectors
- Support women’s collectives and strengthen their livelihoods
- Scale up successful livelihood interventions under DAY-NRLM
- Strengthen producer enterprises and improve market linkages
- Facilitate access to finance, credit, and financial services for rural poor
- Enhance skill development and digital inclusion in rural areas
- Support innovation and entrepreneurship in rural economies
- Monitor and evaluate impact through robust MIS and learning systems

## Eligibility

The project targets rural poor households, particularly women, belonging to Self-Help Groups (SHGs) and their federations under DAY-NRLM. Benefits are extended to producer enterprises and collectives engaged in agricultural, livestock, handicrafts, and other non-farm activities. Implementation is focused on 13 high-priority states identified based on poverty and livelihood indicators.
