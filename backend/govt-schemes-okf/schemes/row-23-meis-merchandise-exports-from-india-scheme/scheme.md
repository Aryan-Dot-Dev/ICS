# MEIS (Merchandise Exports from India Scheme)

## Overview

The Merchandise Exports from India Scheme (MEIS) is an export promotion initiative by the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India. It aims to promote the manufacture and export of notified goods/products by providing financial incentives in the form of duty credit scrips to exporters. The scheme supports Indian exporters by offsetting infrastructural inefficiencies and enhancing competitiveness in global markets.

## Objective

- Promote the manufacture and export of notified goods/products
- Offset infrastructural inefficiencies and associated costs involved in exporting goods/products
- Enhance India's export competitiveness in global markets
- Provide incentives through duty credit scrips to eligible exporters
- Support MSME participation in international trade


## Target Beneficiaries

**exporters**, **manufacturers**, **MSME**


## Key Features

- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: Applications are typically submitted within the financial year of export; specific timelines are notified by DGFT from time to time.
- Portal: https://dgft.gov.in
- Source data last updated: 2026
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

Exporters of notified goods/products as per the Foreign Trade Policy (FTP) are eligible for MEIS benefits. The scheme applies to exports made under the IEC (Importer Exporter Code) granted by DGFT. Eligibility is determined based on the product description and ITC(HS) code as notified under the scheme from time to time.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

MEIS provides duty credit scrips as a percentage of the FOB value of exports, which can be used to pay basic customs duty on import of goods, payment of service tax, and settlement of other dues. The scrips are transferable and can be traded in the market. The rate of incentive varies by product and is notified periodically.

## Important Conditions

- Benefits are available only for notified products as per the Foreign Trade Policy
- The scheme is subject to periodic review and revision by DGFT
- MEIS has been discontinued for exports made on or after 1 January 2021, with RoSCTL replacing it for textiles and garments
- Incentive rates vary by product and are notified periodically
