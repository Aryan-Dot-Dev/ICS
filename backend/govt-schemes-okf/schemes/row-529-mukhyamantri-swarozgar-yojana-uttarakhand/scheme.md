# Mukhyamantri Swarozgar Yojana – Uttarakhand

## Overview

Mukhyamantri Swarozgar Yojana (MSY) is a state-level scheme in Uttarakhand aimed at generating maximum employment and subsidiary employment by providing loan facilities through nationalized/scheduled commercial banks, state cooperative banks, and regional rural banks for setting up enterprises, services, or businesses. It targets enterprising youth, returning migrants due to Covid-19, skilled/unskilled artisans, handicraftsmen, and educated urban/rural unemployed individuals. The scheme operates under guiding principles to encourage self-employment and industrial/business establishment in the state.

## Objective

- Generate maximum employment and subsidiary employment
- Link beneficiaries to enterprise, service, or business setup
- Provide loan facility through nationalized/scheduled commercial banks
- Provide loan facility through state cooperative banks/regional rural banks
- Encourage enterprising youth of the state
- Support migrants of Uttarakhand who returned due to Covid-19
- Assist skilled and unskilled artisans and handicraftsmen
- Aid educated urban and rural unemployed individuals

Enterprising youth of the state, migrants of Uttarakhand who have returned due to Covid-19, skilled and unskilled artisans and handicraftsmen, and educated urban and rural unemployed individuals are eligible to establish their own industries/businesses under Mukhyamantri Swarozgar Yojana.
