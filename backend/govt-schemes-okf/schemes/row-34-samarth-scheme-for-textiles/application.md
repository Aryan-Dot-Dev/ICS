## Process

1. Register for Empanelment under 'Samarth' (SCBTS) by clicking on Register for Empanelment under 'Samarth' (SCBTS) for new Registration.
2. Enter Organization Name, Address details, Phone, Email id (must be unique), and Captcha.
3. Click on Register button to register empanelment; verification link sent to registered mail id.
4. Click on verification link (highlighted with blue colour) for verification of registered mail id.
5. Click on Go to home page button to redirect to Home page.
6. Click on Login Here button to redirect to Login page.
7. Enter Valid Login Credential (Email id and password), enter captcha, then click on login button.

## Deadlines

- Please upload action plan (project wise) in MIS till 29-01-2024 under tab 'Notice'.
