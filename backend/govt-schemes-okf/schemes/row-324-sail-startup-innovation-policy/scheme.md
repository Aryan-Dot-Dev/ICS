# SAIL Startup & Innovation Policy

## Overview

The SAIL Startup & Innovation Policy is a PSU Support scheme by Steel Authority of India Limited (SAIL) aimed at fostering innovation and supporting startups. The scheme provides a platform for startups to collaborate with SAIL on technological solutions, pilot projects, and innovation challenges. It focuses on areas such as steel manufacturing, sustainability, digital transformation, and operational efficiency. Selected startups may receive mentorship, access to SAIL's infrastructure, and opportunities for proof-of-concept trials.

## Objective

- To identify and nurture innovative startups working in areas relevant to SAIL's operations
- To provide startups with access to SAIL's plants, infrastructure, and technical expertise for pilot projects
- To foster collaboration between SAIL and startups for developing sustainable and technologically advanced solutions
- To support innovation in steel manufacturing, digitalization, energy efficiency, and environmental sustainability
- To create a structured pathway for startups to scale their solutions through potential integration into SAIL's value chain

## Eligibility

Startups registered in India with a valid DPIIT recognition certificate, working on innovative products, services, or technologies in domains such as steel processing, automation, AI/IoT, sustainability, waste management, or related industrial applications. The startup must have a prototype or minimum viable product (MVP) ready for pilot testing.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Selected startups gain access to SAIL's manufacturing units for pilot testing, technical mentorship from SAIL experts, opportunities to present solutions to SAIL's leadership, potential for long-term collaboration or integration into SAIL's operations, and visibility through SAIL's innovation network. Financial support may be considered on a case-by-case basis for proof-of-concept validation.

## Important Conditions

- Financial support is not guaranteed and is subject to mutual agreement and availability of funds
- Intellectual property rights must be clearly defined in any collaboration agreement
- SAIL reserves the right to discontinue engagement if milestones are not met
- The scheme does not provide equity funding or debt financing as a standard offering
