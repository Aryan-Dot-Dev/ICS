## Process

1. Visit the SAIL official website and navigate to the Startup & Innovation Policy section.
2. Review the eligibility criteria and focus areas.
3. Prepare a detailed proposal including startup profile, problem statement, solution description, stage of development, and expected outcomes.
4. Submit the application via the designated portal or email address provided on the SAIL website.
5. Applications are reviewed by SAIL's innovation committee.
6. Shortlisted startups are invited for presentations or discussions.
7. Final selection is made based on innovation, feasibility, and alignment with SAIL's objectives.
8. Selected startups are notified and guided through the next steps for collaboration.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round.
