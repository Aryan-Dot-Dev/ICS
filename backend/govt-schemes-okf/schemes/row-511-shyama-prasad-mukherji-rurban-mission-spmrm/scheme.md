# Shyama Prasad Mukherji Rurban Mission (SPMRM)

## Overview

The Shyama Prasad Mukherji Rurban Mission (SPMRM) is a flagship initiative by the Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India, aimed at developing 300 Rurban clusters across the country by provisioning economic, social, and physical infrastructure facilities. It seeks to stimulate local economic development, enhance basic services, and create well-planned Rurban clusters through convergence of various government schemes and Critical Gap Funding (CGF). The mission focuses on geographically contiguous villages with populations ranging from 5,000 to 50,000, depending on terrain, and aims to bridge the rural-urban divide while preserving rural community life with urban-like amenities.

## Objective

- Stimulate local economic development with emphasis on reduction of poverty and unemployment in rural areas
- Enhance basic services and create well planned Rurban clusters
- Bridge the rural-urban divide in economic, technological, and service-related aspects
- Spread development in the region and attract investment in rural areas
- Develop 300 Rurban clusters over the next five years across all States and UTs
- Strengthen rural areas by provisioning economic, social, and infrastructure amenities for sustainable and balanced regional development
- Ensure organized development of clusters through delineation as planning areas under relevant State Planning Acts
- Promote convergence of Central, Centrally Sponsored, and State Government schemes for fund mobilization

## Important Conditions

- CGF is limited to 30% of project cost or Rs. 30 crores per cluster (plain areas) and Rs. 15 crores per cluster (desert/hilly/tribal areas), whichever is lesser
- Fund release is contingent upon utilization of 60% or more funds available in the State Nodal Account
- State must release corresponding State Share against previous central releases
- Expenditure projection for next 6 months must be submitted with fund release proposal
- State Project Management Unit (SPMU) must be set up with adequate professionals as per Framework of Implementation
- Third instalment of CGF (35%) is released only after approval of DPR by SLEC and 60% CGF utilization
- Final 5% of CGF is released only after submission of Audited Statement of expenditure, completion of Planning Notification, publication of Spatial Plan, and setup of SPMU, DPMU, and CDMU
- States must ensure Geo-tagging initiation and work verification for at least 5% of works executed
- All funds must be transferred to State Nodal Account within 15 days of receiving central share in State treasury
