## Process

1. Identification of potential Rurban clusters by the Ministry and States based on demographic, economic, tourism, and transportation criteria.
2. Selection of clusters by State Governments within identified sub-districts, considering performance parameters like decadal rural population growth, rise in land values, non-farm work participation, girls' enrollment in secondary schools, bank account penetration under PMJDY, Swachh Bharat Mission (Grameen) performance, and good governance initiatives.
3. Preparation of Integrated Cluster Action Plan (ICAP) by State Nodal Agency (SNA) with support from State Technical Support Agencies (STSAs), covering baseline studies, required interventions, resource convergence, and CGF estimation.
4. Submission of ICAP to the Ministry for approval by the Empowered Committee.
5. Preparation of Detailed Project Report (DPR) for approved components.
6. Approval of DPR by State Level Empowered Committee (SLEC).
7. Release of funds in instalments as per financial concurrence.
8. Implementation of project components through convergence of schemes and CGF.
9. Operations and maintenance for ten years post-implementation.
10. Monitoring and evaluation through quarterly reports, utilization certificates, and site visits.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round as per the five-yearly iteration of ICAPs and fund release proposals.
