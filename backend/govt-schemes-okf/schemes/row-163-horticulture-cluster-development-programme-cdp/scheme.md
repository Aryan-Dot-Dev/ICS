# Horticulture Cluster Development Programme (CDP)

## Overview

The Horticulture Cluster Development Programme (CDP) is a central sector scheme by the National Horticulture Board (NHB) under the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India. It aims to leverage geographical specialization of horticulture clusters to make them globally competitive by facilitating economies of scale in production and post-harvest activities, better price realization, and empowerment of the farming community through increased competitiveness across the value chain. The programme supports the development of Multi-Commodity High Value Clusters and Peri-urban Vegetable Clusters through a challenge mode selection process where applicants identify and demonstrate feasibility of proposed clusters. Total investment under the programme is INR 10,000 Crore (equivalent to $1.3 Billion).

## Objective

- Address the concerns of the horticulture cluster value chain from pre-production, production, post-harvest management and value addition to logistics, marketing and branding in an integrated manner.
- Facilitate the introduction of innovative technologies and practices to help enhance the global competitiveness of focus cluster crops and thereby promote exports.
- Enhance farmers’ wallet share (reduction in cost and increase in revenue).
- Leverage geographical specialization of horticulture clusters to make them globally competitive.
- Facilitate the horticulture sector in realising suitable economies of scale in production and post-harvest activities.
- Help in better price realisation and empowerment of the farming community by enhancing their income through increased competitiveness across the entire value chain.

## Important Conditions

- Submission of application physically or through any other means shall not be accepted;
- only online submission at https://cdp.nhb.gov.in is allowed.
- No deadline date extensions will be provided for DPR/ Business case submission after the six-week period.
- The farmer component shall be minimum 40% of the total project cost.
- The applicant entity must contribute a minimum of 20% of the IA’s Cost Component as equity.
- The applicant entity must secure a term loan constituting at least 20% of the IA’s Cost Component from RBI Approved Scheduled Commercial Bank or Financial Institution.
- Detailed Project Report/ Techno-Economic Viability Report merely stamped or endorsed by the Scheduled Commercial Bank without detailed Appraisal Note shall not be considered valid.
- The applicant entity, including its shareholder(s), partner(s), director(s), and key management personnel, must have a clean financial record with no defaults on debt obligations over the past three years and must not have been classified as ‘non-performing assets’ by any lender.
- Joint Ventures will not be allowed to fulfill the eligibility criteria.
