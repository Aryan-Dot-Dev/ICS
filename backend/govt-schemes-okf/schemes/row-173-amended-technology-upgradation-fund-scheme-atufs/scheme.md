# Amended Technology Upgradation Fund Scheme (ATUFS)

## Overview

The Amended Technology Upgradation Fund Scheme (ATUFS) is a credit-linked subsidy scheme launched by the Ministry of Textiles to promote technology upgradation in the textile industry. It aims to enhance productivity, quality, and global competitiveness of textile units by providing financial assistance for the adoption of indigenous, state-of-the-art, and energy-efficient technology. The scheme supports both new and existing textile units across various segments including spinning, weaving, processing, garmenting, technical textiles, and jute.

## Objective

- Promote investment in technology upgradation in the textile industry
- Enhance productivity, quality, and global competitiveness of textile units
- Encourage adoption of indigenous, state-of-the-art, and energy-efficient technology
- Support employment generation in the textile sector
- Facilitate sustainable and inclusive growth of the textile industry

## Important Conditions

- Subsidy is available only for machinery listed in the approved list under ATUFS
- Garmenting and technical textiles segments are eligible for 10% subsidy, while other segments get 15%
- Maximum subsidy per unit is capped at INR 30 crores
- Subsidy is released only after verification of installation and commissioning by PMA
- Units must not have availed benefits under any other similar scheme for the same machinery
