## Process

1. Identify eligible machinery from the approved list under ATUFS.
2. Submit a project proposal to a lending institution (bank or financial institution) along with required documents.
3. The lending institution appraises the project and sanctions the loan.
4. Submit the sanctioned loan details and documents to the Project Management Agency (PMA) for registration.
5. After installation and commissioning of machinery, submit verification documents to the PMA.
6. PMA verifies the claim and recommends subsidy release.
7. The subsidy is released by the Ministry of Textiles to the lending institution, which credits the beneficiary’s account.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds.
