## Process

1. Apply through the official Uttarakhand investment portal or designated incubation center.
2. Submit a detailed business proposal including innovation, scalability, and sector alignment.
3. Undergo screening by the selection committee based on viability and impact.
4. Shortlisted applicants are invited for pitch presentation or interview.
5. Selected startups are offered incubation space and support services at SIDCUL centers.
6. Post-selection, startups undergo mentorship, milestone tracking, and periodic review for continued support.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to availability of incubation seats and funds.
