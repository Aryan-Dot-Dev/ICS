# Uttarakhand Startup Policy & SIDCUL Incubation

## Overview

The Uttarakhand Startup Policy & SIDCUL Incubation scheme is a state-level initiative aimed at fostering innovation and entrepreneurship in Uttarakhand by providing support through incubation, mentorship, and infrastructure. It focuses on creating a conducive ecosystem for startups by leveraging SIDCUL's industrial infrastructure and offering policy incentives. The scheme targets early-stage ventures and promotes sector-specific growth in areas such as tourism, agriculture, handicrafts, and IT.

## Objective

- Promote innovation and entrepreneurship across key sectors in Uttarakhand
- Provide incubation support through SIDCUL infrastructure and mentorship
- Facilitate access to funding, networking, and market linkages for startups
- Encourage sector-specific startup growth in tourism, agriculture, handicrafts, and IT
- Strengthen the startup ecosystem via policy interventions and industry-academia collaboration

## Eligibility

Startups registered in Uttarakhand or willing to establish operations in the state, with a focus on innovative ideas or scalable business models in priority sectors such as tourism, agriculture, handicrafts, and IT. Preference may be given to ventures utilizing SIDCUL infrastructure or demonstrating potential for employment generation and regional development.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Access to SIDCUL incubation centers with infrastructure support, mentorship from industry experts, networking opportunities, assistance in securing funding, and policy facilitation for regulatory approvals. Startups may also benefit from subsidies on utilities, infrastructure usage, and sector-specific incentives as per state policy.
