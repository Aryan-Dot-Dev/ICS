# Multi State Cooperative Societies Development Fund

## Overview

The Multi State Cooperative Societies Development Fund is a financial assistance scheme aimed at supporting the development and strengthening of multi-state cooperative societies in India. It provides financial support for various developmental activities including infrastructure, technology upgradation, and human resource development. The fund is managed by the National Cooperative Development Corporation (NCDC) under the Ministry of Cooperation. The scheme aims to enhance the economic viability and operational efficiency of multi-state cooperatives across the country.

## Objective

- To provide financial assistance for the development of multi-state cooperative societies
- To support infrastructure development and modernization of cooperative enterprises
- To promote technology upgradation and adoption of best practices in cooperatives
- To enhance human resource development and training within cooperative societies
- To strengthen the financial base and economic viability of multi-state cooperatives
- To encourage diversification and expansion of cooperative business activities
- To support risk management and institutional building initiatives
- To promote inclusive growth and equitable development through cooperative models

## Eligibility

Multi-state cooperative societies registered under the Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act, 2002 are eligible to apply for financial assistance under the fund. The societies must demonstrate viable project proposals with clear developmental objectives and repayment capacity. ## Important Conditions

- Financial assistance is subject to the availability of funds under the scheme
- Priority may be given to projects in identified thrust sectors and underserved regions
- Beneficiary societies must contribute a minimum promoter's equity as per NCDC norms
- Utilization of funds must be strictly for the approved project purpose
- Regular monitoring and reporting are mandatory during and after project implementation
- The fund does not provide grants;
- assistance is extended on a repayable basis
- Societies with outstanding dues to NCDC or other government agencies may be ineligible
