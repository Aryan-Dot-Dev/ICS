# Homestay Scheme for Rural Tourism

## Overview

The Homestay Scheme for Rural Tourism is a sub-scheme under Swadesh Darshan 2.0, aimed at developing homestays in tribal areas to promote responsible tourism and enhance livelihood opportunities for tribal communities. It provides funding support for the development and renovation of tribal homestays and village community requirements, creating community-based responsible tourism through cultural exchange between tourists and indigenous people. The scheme is implemented under the Pradhan Mantri Janjatiya Unnat Gram Abhiyan (PMJUGA) and targets the development of 1000 homestays in tribal areas.

## Objective

- Develop homestays in tribal areas to promote responsible tourism
- Enhance livelihood opportunities for tribal communities
- Create community-based responsible tourism through cultural exchange
- Provide authentic tribal rural experience for tourists
- Encourage states/UTs to provide further assistance to uplift local livelihood
- Develop tourism potential in tribal villages
- Promote entrepreneurship and self-employment in tourism value chain
- Ensure convergence with existing Government of India/State/UT schemes

## Important Conditions

- Funding under the scheme cannot be utilized for land acquisition, resettlement and rehabilitation, relocation, dredging/development of bunds of a water body (man-made & natural both)
- States/UTs must ensure convergence of various central and state schemes while identification of villages and homestays
- State/UT shall ensure that the proposed land parcel for development of homestays is in possession of beneficiary with clear ownership title and available for development
- State/UT shall ensure that the identified homestay owner belongs to the tribal community
- The State/UT shall be responsible for providing requisite capacity building support (upskilling/reskilling) to beneficiaries during development phase and further responsible for marketing and promotion of developed homestays under the scheme
- The State/UT shall also partner with experienced NGO/Rural Tourism Societies etc to support in identification of tribal villages, tribal households, undertake stakeholder consultations, sensitization of homestay owners, community development, and overall project implementation through its own resources
- The State/UTs may also assist for upgrading the hospitality experience from state/UT funds where ever required and further encourage stakeholders to adopt sustainable tourism practices in tribal villages
- Technical upskilling and training of homestay owners should be done by States/UTs in collaboration with IHMs, SIHMs and Ministry of Tribal Affairs etc
- Once homestays and community spaces are operational, State Governments/UTs will feature them on their promotional websites/platforms along with photographs and testimonials and make necessary efforts to integrate feedback mechanism
- State Government will periodically collect data regarding regular operation of homestays developed under the scheme including revenue generation, occupancy, feedback received etc
