## Process

1. States/UTs shall identify suitable tribal villages with tourism potential and tribal beneficiaries for homestay development in consultation with local community.
2. States/UTs shall prioritize Dharti Aaba villages for homestay establishment.
3. States/UTs shall identify required interventions for village community to enhance tourism potential.
4. States/UTs shall prepare project proposal as per the Model Template (Annexure-I) including preference to female homestay owners, model homestay design according to local vernacular architecture, marketing plan, convergence with central and state schemes, development of community space, and capacity building plan.
5. States/UTs shall submit the project proposal on the portal (sd2.tourism.gov.in) with covering letter of State/UT Tourism Secretary as per timeline communicated by Ministry of Tourism.
6. In case of any issues related to proposal submission on the portal, States/UTs may submit the proposal on email id swadesh.darshan@gov.in and inform the same to the Ministry.
7. Proposals meeting guidelines shall be placed before Mission Directorate (MD) for review and recommendation to Central Sanctioning and Monitoring Committee (CSMC).
8. CSMC after due examination will grant approval to the project and further, the project will be sanctioned by the Ministry of Tourism.
9. State Government has to implement the project as per approved project proposal and scheme guidelines.
10. In case of any revisions in the project, approval of CSMC will be obtained.
