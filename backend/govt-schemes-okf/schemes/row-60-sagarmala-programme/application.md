## Process

1. Identification of project ideas aligned with Sagarmala objectives and National Perspective Plan (NPP).
2. Preparation of Detailed Project Report (DPR) by the project proponent.
3. Submission of proposal to the concerned nodal agency (e.g., Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways, State Government, or Port Trust).
4. Appraisal of the project by the Sagarmala Coordination and Steering Committee (SCSC) or relevant technical committee.
5. Approval by the Empowered Committee under Sagarmala.
6. Implementation of the project through EPC contracts or PPP mode.
7. Monitoring and evaluation by the Sagarmala Secretariat and concerned ministries.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — proposals are accepted throughout the year based on budget availability and project readiness.
