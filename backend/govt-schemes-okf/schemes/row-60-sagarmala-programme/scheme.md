# Sagarmala Programme

## Overview

The Sagarmala Programme is a flagship initiative of the Government of India aimed at promoting port-led development through the harnessing of India's 7,500 km long coastline, 14,500 km of potentially navigable waterways and strategic location on key international maritime trade routes. It seeks to reduce logistics costs for both domestic and EXIM cargo, enhance port infrastructure, and promote industrialization and coastal community development. The programme integrates the development of ports, industrial clusters, hinterland connectivity, and coastal shipping to drive economic growth and job creation.

## Objective

- Reduce logistics cost for EXIM and domestic cargo through optimized infrastructure investment
- Modernize and mechanize ports to achieve global standards of efficiency
- Develop new major ports and enhance capacity of existing ports
- Enhance evacuation infrastructure to and from ports via rail, road, inland waterways and pipelines
- Promote port-led industrialization and development of coastal economic zones
- Develop inland waterways and coastal shipping as sustainable and cost-effective transport modes
- Skill development and livelihood enhancement for coastal communities
- Enhance sustainability and environmental protection in port and coastal areas

## Eligibility

Eligibility varies by component and project under Sagarmala. Generally, proposals are invited from state governments, maritime boards, port trusts, central public sector enterprises, and private sector entities through competitive bidding or public-private partnership (PPP) modes. Projects must align with the National Perspective Plan (NPP) under Sagarmala and contribute to port-led development.

## Benefits

Benefits include reduced logistics costs, improved port efficiency and capacity, enhanced hinterland connectivity, promotion of coastal shipping and inland waterways, development of industrial clusters and manufacturing zones, job creation in port and coastal areas, skill development for local communities, and increased exports and economic growth through port-led development.

- Private sector participation requires adherence to PPP guidelines and transparent bidding processes
- Financial support is subject to availability of funds and project appraisal outcomes
