# Indian Oil Startup Scheme

## Overview

The Indian Oil Startup Scheme is a program by Indian Oil Corporation Limited (IOCL) aimed at supporting startups through collaboration, innovation, and potential partnerships. The scheme seeks to foster entrepreneurial ventures that align with IOCL's business domains such as energy, petrochemicals, and sustainable technologies. It provides a platform for startups to engage with IOCL for pilot projects, technology validation, and possible scaling opportunities.

## Objective

- To identify and support innovative startups in energy and allied sectors
- To foster collaboration between IOCL and startups for technology development
- To provide startups with access to IOCL's infrastructure, expertise, and network
- To promote sustainable and scalable solutions aligned with national energy goals
- To encourage innovation in areas such as biofuels, hydrogen, and digital transformation

## Eligibility

Startups must be registered entities working on innovative solutions in domains relevant to IndianOil's business, including but not limited to energy, petrochemicals, sustainability, and digital technologies. Preference is given to those with scalable models and potential for pilot integration with IOCL operations.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Selected startups may gain access to IndianOil's technical expertise, infrastructure for pilot testing, mentorship from industry experts, potential for commercial collaboration, and visibility through IOCL's innovation network. The scheme facilitates proof-of-concept validation and scaling opportunities within a corporate ecosystem.
