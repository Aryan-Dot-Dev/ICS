## Process

1. Visit the official IndianOil startup portal at https://iocl.com/startup
2. Register as a startup on the platform
3. Submit a detailed proposal outlining the innovation, its relevance to IndianOil's domains, and expected outcomes
4. Await review by the IndianOil innovation team
5. If shortlisted, participate in discussions for potential pilot or collaboration
6. Upon agreement, proceed with defined milestones and deliverables.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round.
