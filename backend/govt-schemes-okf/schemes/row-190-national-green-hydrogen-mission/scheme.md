# National Green Hydrogen Mission

## Overview

The National Green Hydrogen Mission is a flagship initiative of the Government of India aimed at making India a global hub for the production, utilization, and export of green hydrogen and its derivatives. Launched under the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE), the mission seeks to reduce dependence on fossil fuels, decarbonize key sectors such as transportation, industry, and power, and achieve energy independence by 2047. It focuses on scaling up green hydrogen production through electrolyser manufacturing, demand creation, and pilot projects in end-use sectors.

## Objective

- Make India a leading producer and supplier of green hydrogen in the world
- Achieve annual green hydrogen production capacity of at least 5 MMT by 2030
- Develop electrolyser manufacturing capacity of at least 15 GW per year by 2030
- Attract over INR 8 lakh crore in investments by 2030
- Create over 6 lakh jobs by 2030
- Reduce cumulative CO2 emissions by approximately 50 MMT per annum by 2030
- Develop enabling infrastructure such as storage, transportation, and refuelling networks
- Support research, development, and demonstration (RD&D) projects for technology innovation

Financial incentives for setting up green hydrogen production and electrolyser manufacturing facilities; support for pilot projects in end-use sectors such as mobility, shipping, and steel; funding for research and development; facilitation of land and statutory clearances; creation of green hydrogen hubs; and support for development of export infrastructure and international partnerships.
