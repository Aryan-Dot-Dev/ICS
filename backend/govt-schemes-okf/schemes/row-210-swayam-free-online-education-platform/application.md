## Process

1. Visit the SWAYAM portal at https://swayam.gov.in
2. Click on 'Register as Student'
3. Provide essential information: Name and Email ID
4. Optionally sign up using Google or Microsoft login
5. Verify account via email notification from support@swayam.gov.in
6. Browse or search for courses by category, keyword, or university
7. Select a suitable course and click to enroll
8. Access course content immediately after enrollment
9. For certification, register for the proctored exam when announced
10. Pay the exam fee (if applicable) and appear at the designated centre on the specified date.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Courses have specific enrollment windows (e.g., enrollment ends 31 Aug, 2026 for many courses), but learners can enroll in self-paced courses anytime. Exam registration has end dates (e.g., 14 Aug, 2026 for many July 2026 semester courses).
