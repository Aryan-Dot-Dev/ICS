# SWAYAM Free Online Education Platform

## Overview

SWAYAM (Study Webs of Active Learning for Young Aspiring Minds) is a Government of India initiative providing free online courses from school to post-graduation level, hosted by top institutions like IITs, IIMs, and central universities. It aims to achieve access, equity, and quality in education by bridging the digital divide and offering courses in 11 Indian languages. Learners can access video lectures, reading materials, self-assessment tests, and discussion forums at no cost, with optional proctored exams for certification available at a nominal fee.

## Objective

- Provide free access to high-quality education resources from Class 9 to post-graduation
- Bridge the digital divide for disadvantaged learners untouched by the digital revolution
- Enable credit transfer for SWAYAM courses into regular academic programmes
- Offer industry-aligned skill-based courses to enhance employability
- Support lifelong learning through curated standalone and awareness programmes
- Ensure quality content via appointed National Coordinators from premier institutions
- Facilitate multi-modal learning through video, text, self-assessment, and discussion forums
- Promote access to education in 11 Indian languages for inclusivity

## Important Conditions

- Certification requires appearing for an in-person, proctored exam at designated centres on specified dates
- Exam fee is applicable for certification (e.g., Rs. 1000 for NPTEL, Rs. 750 for IIMB)
- Credit transfer depends on university adoption of UGC/AICTE Credit Framework regulations
- Some courses have specific enrollment and exam end dates (e.g., enrollment ends 31 Aug, 2026)
- Self-paced courses do not award certificates or credits
- Assignment and quiz submission minimums (e.g., 75%) may be required to appear for university-conducted exams
