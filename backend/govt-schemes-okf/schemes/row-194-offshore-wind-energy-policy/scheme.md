# Offshore Wind Energy Policy

## Overview

The Offshore Wind Energy Policy is a government initiative aimed at promoting the development of offshore wind energy projects in India. It seeks to harness the country's offshore wind potential to contribute to renewable energy targets and reduce dependence on fossil fuels. The policy includes provisions for viability gap funding and other incentives to attract investment in offshore wind projects.

## Objective

- Promote development of offshore wind energy projects in India
- Harness offshore wind potential to meet renewable energy targets
- Reduce dependence on fossil fuels through clean energy generation
- Attract investment via financial incentives like viability gap funding
- Establish a robust offshore wind energy ecosystem in the country
