# National Drone Policy 2021

## Overview

The National Drone Policy 2021, implemented by the Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA) under the Ministry of Civil Aviation, establishes a regulatory framework for the operation of Unmanned Aircraft Systems (UAS) in India. It aims to promote safe, secure, and sustainable drone operations while fostering innovation and growth in the drone ecosystem. The policy covers registration, certification, operational permissions, and safety standards for drones, including provisions for drone corridors, beyond visual line of sight (BVLOS) operations, and drone training organizations. It applies to all categories of drones and stakeholders, including manufacturers, operators, and service providers.

## Objective

- Establish a safe and secure regulatory framework for drone operations in India
- Promote innovation, research, and development in drone technology
- Enable commercial use of drones across sectors such as agriculture, logistics, and surveillance
- Ensure airspace safety and security through standardized operational procedures
- Facilitate ease of doing business by simplifying drone registration and approval processes
- Support capacity building through drone training and skill development programs
- Encourage indigenous manufacturing and reduce dependence on imports
- Enable beyond visual line of sight (BVLOS) and beyond visual range (BVR) operations under controlled conditions

## Important Conditions

- Operations are prohibited in no-drone zones (e.g., near airports, international borders, strategic locations)
- Nano drones (<250g) used for non-commercial purposes are exempt from UIN and UAOP but must follow safety guidelines
- UAOP is valid for a maximum of 10 years and subject to renewal
- Foreign-operated drones require prior DGCA approval and must be operated through an Indian entity
- All drone operations must comply with the Aircraft Act, 1934, and Aircraft Rules, 1937
- Violations may attract penalties under the Aircraft Act, including fines and imprisonment
