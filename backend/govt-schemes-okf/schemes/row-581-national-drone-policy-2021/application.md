## Process

1. Determine the drone category based on weight (nano: ≤250g, micro: 250g–2kg, small: 2kg–25kg, medium: 25kg–150kg, large: >150kg).
2. Obtain a Unique Identification Number (UIN) via the Digital Sky platform by providing drone specifications and owner details.
3. For commercial operations, apply for an Unmanned Aircraft Operator Permit (UAOP) through the Digital Sky portal.
4. Ensure compliance with operational requirements such as pilot training, maintenance checks, and insurance.
5. Submit flight plans and obtain clearance for operations in controlled or restricted airspaces.
6. Adhere to no-drone zones, height limits (typically 400 ft AGL), and visual line-of-sight (VLOS) rules unless granted BVLOS approval.
7. Maintain operational logs and report incidents as required.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — UIN and UAOP applications can be submitted at any time through the Digital Sky platform.
