# Coal India Ltd (CIL) Innovation & Startup Fund

## Overview

The Coal India Ltd (CIL) Innovation & Startup Fund is a dedicated initiative by Coal India Limited to support innovation and entrepreneurship in the coal and allied sectors. It aims to foster startups working on technological solutions, sustainability, and operational efficiency relevant to the coal industry. The fund provides financial and non-financial support to selected startups through a structured selection and mentorship process.

## Objective

- To promote innovation in the coal and energy sector
- To support startups developing solutions for coal mining, safety, and environmental sustainability
- To encourage entrepreneurship and technological advancement in PSU-associated domains
- To provide funding and mentorship to early-stage startups with scalable ideas
- To strengthen collaboration between CIL and the startup ecosystem

## Eligibility

Startups incorporated in India working on innovative solutions in areas such as coal mining technology, safety, environmental management, energy efficiency, and allied sectors.
