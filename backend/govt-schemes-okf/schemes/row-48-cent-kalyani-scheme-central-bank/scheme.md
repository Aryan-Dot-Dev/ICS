# Cent Kalyani Scheme (Central Bank)

## Overview

The Cent Kalyani Scheme is a loan product offered by Central Bank of India specifically designed to support women entrepreneurs in establishing or expanding their business ventures. It provides financial assistance through term loans to eligible women entrepreneurs for various business purposes including working capital, asset creation, and business expansion. The scheme aims to promote women's entrepreneurship and economic empowerment by offering accessible credit facilities with favorable terms.

## Objective

- To provide financial assistance to women entrepreneurs for starting or expanding business ventures
- To promote women's entrepreneurship and economic empowerment
- To support business growth through accessible credit facilities
- To encourage self-employment and income generation among women
- To contribute to the development of women-led MSMEs
