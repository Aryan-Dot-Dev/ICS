# Support to Training & Employment Programme (STEP)

## Overview

The Support to Training and Employment Programme (STEP) is a Central Sector Scheme implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development to provide skills that enhance employability and entrepreneurship among women aged 16 years and above across India. The scheme focuses on traditional sectors such as agriculture, horticulture, food processing, handlooms, handicrafts, textile and embroidery, zari work, sericulture, fisheries, and allied sectors, aiming to empower women through skill development and self-employment opportunities.

## Objective

- To provide skills that enhance employability and entrepreneurship among women
- To provide competencies and skills that enable women to become self-employed/entrepreneurs
- To cover traditional sectors such as agriculture, horticulture, food processing, handlooms, handicrafts, textile and embroidery, zari work, sericulture, fisheries, and allied sectors

## Eligibility

Women aged 16 years and above across India are eligible to benefit from the scheme. The implementing agencies include Institutions/Organisations set up as Autonomous Bodies under Ministries/Departments of Government of India, State Governments/UT Administrations, Voluntary Organisations, Non-Governmental Organisations, Co-operatives, and other similar entities engaged in training and employment…

## Benefits

The scheme provides skill training in traditional sectors to enhance employability and entrepreneurship among women, enabling them to become self-employed or start their own enterprises. It supports capacity building and income generation through training and market linkages in identified traditional sectors.
