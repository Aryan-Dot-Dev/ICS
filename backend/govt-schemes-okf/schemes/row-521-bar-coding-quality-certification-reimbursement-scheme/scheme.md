# Bar Coding / Quality Certification Reimbursement Scheme

## Overview

The Bar Coding / Quality Certification Reimbursement Scheme provides financial assistance to micro enterprises for adopting bar codes and quality certification. It offers reimbursement of 80% of one-time registration fees and annual recurring fees for the first three years for bar code adoption, and financial assistance on annual membership or subscription fees for selling products via e-commerce platforms. The scheme aims to enhance market access and competitiveness of micro enterprises through standardized product identification and quality assurance.

## Objective

- Promote adoption of Bar Code among micro enterprises
- Provide financial assistance for bar code registration and renewal
- Support micro enterprises in selling products via e-commerce platforms
- Enhance market access and product visibility
- Encourage use of modern packaging techniques
- Facilitate participation in trade fairs and exhibitions
- Support vendor development programs
- Conduct national workshops and seminars on business development

## Important Conditions

- Only micro enterprises with valid Udyam Registration and GS1 India registration for Bar Code are eligible
- Reimbursement is limited to 80% of one-time registration fee and annual recurring fee for the first three years
- Maximum reimbursement for Bar Code is capped at Rs. 50,650/-
- For e-commerce platform, assistance is limited to 75% of paid amount, subject to maximum Rs. 25,000/- per financial year
- An MSE is allowed reimbursement for a maximum of 2 events in a year under the scheme's trade fair component
