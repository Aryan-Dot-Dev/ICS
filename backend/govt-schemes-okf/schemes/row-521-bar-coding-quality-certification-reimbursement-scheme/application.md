## Process

1. Confirm eligibility as a micro enterprise with valid Udyam Registration and GS1 India registration for Bar Code.
2. Obtain application form from the Director/HOO of the respective MSME Development Institute (MSME-DI).
3. Fill in the application form with details of the micro enterprise, amount paid for one-time and annual recurring fees to GS1 India.
4. Attach required documents: attested copy of cash receipt, license from GS1 India, pre-receipted bills in triplicate, copy of UDYAM Registration, affidavit, and undertaking.
5. Submit the application to the Director/HOO of the MSME-DI.
6. Await verification and reimbursement processing.
