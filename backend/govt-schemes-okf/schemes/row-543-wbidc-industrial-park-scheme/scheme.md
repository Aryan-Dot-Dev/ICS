# WBIDC Industrial Park Scheme

## Overview

The WBIDC Industrial Park Scheme is a state-level initiative by the West Bengal Industrial Development Corporation (WBIDC) to facilitate industrial development through the allotment of land in various industrial parks across West Bengal. The scheme provides land on lease or freehold basis for setting up industries, with support through single-window clearance via Silpa Sathi. It aims to promote investment, generate employment, and develop infrastructure in sectors such as manufacturing, logistics, MSME, and emerging industries.

## Objective

- Promote industrial investment in West Bengal through land allotment in industrial parks
- Facilitate ease of doing business via single-window clearance (Silpa Sathi)
- Develop infrastructure and plug-and-play facilities in industrial parks
- Generate employment and socio-economic development in the state
- Support sectors like MSME, logistics, manufacturing, and emerging industries
- Encourage private investment through ready industrial infrastructure and incentives

## Eligibility

Applicants must be bonafide residents of West Bengal or entities seeking to set up industries in the state. Land allotment is open to prospective investors for setting up industrial units, with application money varying based on land area (e.g., Rs. 2 lakhs for up to 2 acres, Rs. 25 lakhs for above 50 acres). ## Benefits

Benefits include access to developed industrial land with plug-and-play infrastructure, uninterrupted power and water supply, connectivity via national highways and railways, proximity to ports and airports, and support through single-window clearance for statutory approvals. Financial benefits include subsidized land prices and waivers on certain fees as per applicable policies.

## Important Conditions

- Land is allotted on 'as is where is' basis;
- investors must verify suitability
- Application fee is non-refundable
- Allotment is subject to clearance from relevant authorities and compliance with land use norms
- Freehold conversion attracts additional charges (typically 15% of base price)
- Land use must conform to the designated industrial category of the park
