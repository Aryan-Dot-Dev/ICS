## Process

1. Visit the Silpa Sathi portal (silpasathi.in) or WBIDC website for land availability and details.
2. Select the desired industrial park and land module.
3. Pay the applicable application fee online (based on land area).
4. Submit the application with required documents via the online portal.
5. Await evaluation and allotment decision by WBIDC.
6. Upon approval, execute lease/deed agreement and pay land cost as per allotment terms.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year as per availability of land.
