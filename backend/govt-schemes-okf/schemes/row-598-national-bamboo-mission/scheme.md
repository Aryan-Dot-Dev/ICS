# National Bamboo Mission

## Overview

The National Bamboo Mission (NBM) is a centrally sponsored scheme implemented by the Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare under the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare. It aims to promote holistic growth of the bamboo sector through area-based, regionally differentiated strategies, focusing on increasing bamboo cultivation, improving post-harvest management, and developing product processing and value addition. The mission supports farmers, artisans, and entrepreneurs engaged in bamboo-based activities across India.

## Objective

- To increase the area under bamboo cultivation in potential and non-forest government and private lands
- To improve post-harvest management through establishment of innovative primary processing units
- To promote product development and value addition at micro, small and medium levels
- To rejuvenate the under-developed bamboo industry in India
- To promote skill development, capacity building and awareness generation for sector growth
- To strengthen marketing of bamboo products, especially handicraft items
- To converge efforts of various agencies for development of bamboo sector
