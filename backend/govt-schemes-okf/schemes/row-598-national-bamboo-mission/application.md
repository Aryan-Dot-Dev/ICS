## Process

1. Identify the relevant activity under NBM (plantation, processing, value addition, etc.).
2. Prepare a detailed project report (DPR) including cost estimates, location, and expected outcomes.
3. Submit the proposal to the State Bamboo Mission or the nodal department of agriculture/horticulture in the respective state.
4. The proposal undergoes technical and financial appraisal by the State Level Committee.
5. Upon approval, administrative sanction is issued.
6. Beneficiary implements the project as per approved plan.
7. After verification of progress, subsidy is released in installments directly to the beneficiary's bank account.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — proposals can be submitted throughout the year subject to availability of funds and state-specific announcements.
