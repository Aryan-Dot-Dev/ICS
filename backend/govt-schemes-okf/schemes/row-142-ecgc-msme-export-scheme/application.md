## Process

1. Visit the ECGC website and navigate to the RELIEF scheme section.
2. Click on the ‘Standard Operating Procedure for filing claims under RELIEF Components I, II and III’ link.
3. Download the claim form and required documents checklist.
4. Fill out the claim form with details of the export transaction, buyer default, and loss amount.
5. Attach supporting documents such as invoice, shipping bill, proof of non-payment, and buyer insolvency proof.
6. Submit the completed application via the ECGC client portal or designated email.
7. Await verification and processing by ECGC officials.
8. Upon approval, the claim amount is disbursed to the exporter’s account.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — claims can be submitted as and when buyer default occurs, subject to policy terms and RELIEF scheme guidelines.
