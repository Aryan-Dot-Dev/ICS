# ECGC MSME Export Scheme

## Overview

ECGC MSME Export Scheme is a government initiative by the Export Credit Guarantee Corporation of India (ECGC) under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, designed to support Indian exporters, particularly MSMEs, by providing financial assistance for export-related claims arising from buyer defaults or political disruptions. The scheme operates under the RELIEF (Resilience and Logistics Intervention for Export Facilitation) program, offering timely support to exporters facing payment delays or non-payment by overseas buyers due to insolvency, bankruptcy, or geopolitical disturbances. It enables exporters to file claims for compensation through a standardized process, with ECGC acting as the insurer of last resort for export credit risks.

## Objective

- Provide financial assistance to Indian exporters facing non-payment by overseas buyers
- Support MSMEs in mitigating export credit risks due to buyer insolvency or political disruptions
- Facilitate timely settlement of export claims under the RELIEF scheme
- Strengthen export competitiveness by reducing financial vulnerability of exporters
- Ensure quick disbursal of claim amounts to affected exporters
- Promote confidence in international trade by backing exporters with government-guaranteed support

- Deadline: Rolling basis — claims can be submitted as and when buyer default occurs, subject to policy terms and RELIEF scheme guidelines.
- Portal: https://www.ecgcltd.in

## Eligibility

Indian exporters, particularly MSMEs, who have suffered losses due to non-payment by overseas buyers on account of buyer insolvency, bankruptcy, or geopolitical disruptions in regions such as West Asia, and who hold valid export credit insurance policies issued by ECGC are eligible to file claims under the RELIEF scheme.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial compensation for export losses due to buyer default, quick claim settlement process, access to ECGC’s digital portal for claim submission and tracking, support under the government-backed RELIEF initiative, and assistance in recovering dues from insolvent or defaulting overseas buyers.

## Important Conditions

- Claims are admissible only if the exporter holds a valid ECGC export credit insurance policy
- Loss must be due to buyer default, insolvency, or geopolitical events covered under RELIEF Components I, II, and III
- Claims must be filed within the stipulated time frame after the occurrence of the loss
- Support is limited to the insured amount under the ECGC policy
- Geopolitical support is time-bound and applicable only during declared periods of disruption
