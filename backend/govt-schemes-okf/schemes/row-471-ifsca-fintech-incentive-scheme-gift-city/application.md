## Process

1. Engage with the IFSCA Development team at development[at]ifsca[dot]gov[dot]in to seek information on opportunities and the process for setting up business in GIFT IFSC.
2. Request a presentation on your business plan if needed.
3. Once decided to apply, connect with the concerned regulatory team as per the relevant vertical (e.g., FinTech Sandbox) for queries on the regulatory framework or application process.
4. Fill the Common Application Form (CAF) and download vertical-specific annexures (for FinTech Sandbox: Preliminary Sandbox Application Form and Fintech Sandbox Application form).
5. Submit the final application via the SWIT Portal at https://swit.ifsca.gov.in/.
6. Await outreach from the regulatory team for any further queries within 7 working days of submission.
7. IFSCA will strive to take a final decision on all applications within 45 days.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round.
