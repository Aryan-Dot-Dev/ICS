# IFSCA FinTech Incentive Scheme (GIFT City)

## Overview

The IFSCA FinTech Incentive Scheme (GIFT City) is designed to promote and support FinTech innovation within the International Financial Services Centre at GIFT City, Gandhinagar, Gujarat. It aims to create a conducive ecosystem for FinTech startups and entities by providing regulatory support, access to infrastructure, and facilitation for setting up operations in the IFSC. The scheme encourages the development and testing of innovative financial products and services under a regulated environment, leveraging GIFT IFSC's status as India's maiden international financial services centre.

## Objective

- Promote innovation in financial technology within the IFSC
- Facilitate the setup and scaling of FinTech startups in GIFT IFSC
- Provide regulatory sandbox facilities for testing innovative financial products
- Enhance access to global markets and investors for FinTech entities
- Support development of a robust FinTech ecosystem through infrastructure and policy support
