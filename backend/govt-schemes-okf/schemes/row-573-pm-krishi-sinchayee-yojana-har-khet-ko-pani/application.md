## Process

1. Prepare District Irrigation Plans (DIPs) and State Irrigation Plan (SIP).
2. Ensure expenditure in water resource development for agriculture sector is not less than baseline expenditure (average of three prior years).
3. Submit DIPs and SIPs for approval.
4. Farmers can apply for the scheme either in MIS system (online) or in offline mode.
5. Subsidy is released to the beneficiary electronically through the mechanism of Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — no fixed deadline. Applications are accepted year-round.
