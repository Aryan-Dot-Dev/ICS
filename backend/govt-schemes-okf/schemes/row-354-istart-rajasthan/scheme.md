# iStart Rajasthan

## Overview

iStart Rajasthan is an integrated startup platform launched by the Department of IT&C, Government of Rajasthan, to foster innovation and entrepreneurship across the state. It provides a comprehensive ecosystem including incubation, mentorship, funding incentives, skill development, and market access for startups at various stages. The initiative supports idea-stage founders through programs like Business Innovation Programme (BIP), Atal Innovation Studio, and LEAP, while offering financial grants and equity support based on QRate assessment.

## Objective

- Empower idea-stage founders and foster innovation among Rajasthan’s youth
- Provide a 5-week intensive journey with mentorship and resources to turn ideas into ventures
- Support students statewide to develop their ideas into startups through Business Innovation Programme
- Drive innovation and entrepreneurship across emerging sectors like AVGC-XR, Robotics, Coding, and Agritech
- Revitalize the startup ecosystem post-pandemic and support entrepreneurs lacking business acumen
- Provide expert-led video courses, assessments, and government-branded digital certificates via LMS
- Enhance recruitment efficiency and collaboration through TalentConnect platform
- Enable startups to scale up through Scale Up Fund and Startup Equity Fund based on QRate performance

## Eligibility

The startup should be registered as Sole Proprietorship, Private Limited/ OPC, Limited Liable Partnership, Partnership Firm in Rajasthan or should have one operating office in Rajasthan.

## Important Conditions

- Financial incentives are subject to evaluation by the committee and government notification
- Additional grants for QRate score upgrade are time-bound and slab-based
- Scale Up Fund is provided as debt convertible to grant and subject to eligibility
- Startup Equity Fund requires the startup to choose between it and Scale Up Fund
- Women-led startup additional grant of INR 60,000 applies only if >50% equity with women founders
