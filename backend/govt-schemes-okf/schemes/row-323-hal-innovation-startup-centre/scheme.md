# HAL Innovation & Startup Centre

## Overview

The HAL Innovation & Startup Centre is a program by Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL), a Maharatna PSU under the Ministry of Defence, aimed at fostering innovation and supporting startups in the aerospace and defence sectors. The initiative provides a platform for startups to collaborate with HAL on technology development, testing, and potential integration into HAL's supply chain. It focuses on nurturing indigenous innovation aligned with national defence and aviation priorities.

## Objective

- Promote innovation in aerospace and defence technologies
- Support startups developing solutions for HAL's operational needs
- Facilitate technology collaboration between HAL and startups
- Enable testing and validation of startup prototypes in HAL facilities
- Integrate viable startup innovations into HAL's product lifecycle or supply chain
