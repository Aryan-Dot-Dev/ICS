# Universal Service Obligation Fund (USOF) Schemes

## Overview

The Universal Service Obligation Fund (USOF), renamed as Digital Bharat Nidhi (DBN) under the Telecommunications Act, 2023, is a Government of India initiative aimed at providing universal access to quality ICT services at affordable prices in rural and remote areas. It supports telecom service providers through subsidy mechanisms to bridge the digital divide by funding projects such as BharatNet, 4G saturation in uncovered villages, mobile tower installations in Left Wing Extremism-affected areas, and international bandwidth hiring. The fund is administered by the Department of Telecommunications, Ministry of Communications, and is financed through the Universal Access Levy (UAL) on telecom operators' adjusted gross revenue.

## Objective

- Provide widespread and non-discriminatory access to quality ICT services at affordable prices in rural & remote areas
- Promote universal access to telecommunication services in underserved rural, remote, and urban areas
- Facilitate research and development of telecommunication services, technologies, and products
- Provide support for pilot projects, consultancy services, and advisory assistance for service provision
- Encourage the adoption of new telecommunication services, technologies, and products
- Narrow the access gaps and remove barriers to information dissemination and access to knowledge
- Reduce the digital divide within the country and promote economic growth through augmenting digital connectivity and digital services in rural and remote India
- Mainstream the underserved and unserved areas or groups by bridging the 'Access Gaps'
- Address the 'Lack of a business case’ for telecom companies and the market gap or failure for ICT services in rural and remote areas

## Eligibility

Telecom service providers undertaking projects in areas where ICT services are not available due to commercial non-viability, on account of sparse population, remoteness of areas, absence of supporting infrastructure, low income of inhabitants and insurgency, as determined by the Universal Service Obligation Fund (USOF) / Digital Bharat Nidhi (DBN).

## Benefits

Subsidy support to incentivize telecom service providers to venture into commercially non-viable areas; funding for BharatNet, 4G saturation, mobile tower projects in LWE-affected areas, CTDP for NER, international bandwidth hiring, and Digital Satellite Phone Terminals; non-discriminatory access to telecommunication services; promotion of R&D in telecom technologies; support for pilot projects…

## Important Conditions

- Subsidy disbursement is subject to the terms and conditions specified in agreements with implementing agencies
- Fund utilization is exclusively for meeting the Universal Service Obligation as per the Indian Telegraph Act, 1885 (as amended)
- Projects must adhere to the Telecommunications (Administration of Digital Bharat Nidhi) Rules, 2024
- Financial support is contingent upon verification of progress and compliance with project milestones
- Certain projects have validity periods (e.g., Rural Wireline Broadband Scheme had a validity of nine years, extended by one year)
