# Mission Innovation India

## Overview

Mission Innovation India is a national initiative under the Department of Science and Technology (DST) aimed at accelerating clean energy innovation through public-private partnerships, research funding, and technology demonstration projects. It aligns with the global Mission Innovation framework to double governmental clean energy R&D investment over five years. The scheme supports startups, researchers, and industry in developing scalable solutions for renewable energy, energy efficiency, and carbon capture.

## Objective

- Accelerate clean energy innovation through increased public and private investment
- Demonstrate scalable clean energy technologies at pilot and commercial scale
- Strengthen international collaboration in clean energy research and development
- Support early-stage startups and SMEs in the clean energy sector
- Enhance capacity building and skill development in clean energy technologies
