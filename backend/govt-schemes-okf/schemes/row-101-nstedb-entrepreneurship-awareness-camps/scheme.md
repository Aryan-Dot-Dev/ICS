# NSTEDB Entrepreneurship Awareness Camps

## Overview

The NSTEDB Entrepreneurship Awareness Camps scheme is part of the National Science and Technology Entrepreneurship Development Board (NSTEDB) under the Department of Science and Technology (DST), aimed at promoting entrepreneurship awareness among students and youth. The scheme focuses on creating awareness about entrepreneurship as a career option, fostering innovation, and encouraging the establishment of startups and small enterprises. It targets students and youth in educational institutions to sensitize them about entrepreneurial opportunities and support mechanisms available through government schemes.

## Objective

- Create awareness about entrepreneurship as a career option among students and youth
- Promote innovation and entrepreneurial mindset in educational institutions
- Encourage the establishment of startups and small enterprises
- Provide information about government schemes and support mechanisms for entrepreneurs
- Foster linkages between academia, industry, and financial institutions for entrepreneurial support

## Eligibility

Educational institutions such as universities, colleges, polytechnics, and technical institutions are eligible to organize Entrepreneurship Awareness Camps under NSTEDB. The institutions must have the necessary infrastructure and faculty to conduct such programs. Preference may be given to institutions in backward regions or those serving underrepresented groups.
