# PLI Scheme for Drones & Drone Components

## Overview

The PLI Scheme for Drones & Drone Components is a Production Linked Incentive scheme aimed at boosting domestic manufacturing of drones and drone components in India. It seeks to enhance India's position as a global drone manufacturing hub by providing financial incentives on incremental sales of eligible products. The scheme is part of the broader PLI initiative to promote self-reliance and reduce imports in strategic sectors.

## Objective

- Promote domestic manufacturing of drones and drone components
- Reduce dependence on imports in the drone sector
- Attract investment in drone manufacturing value chain
- Enhance competitiveness of Indian drone manufacturers globally
- Create employment opportunities in the drone manufacturing sector
- Support innovation and technology development in drone technology
- Achieve self-reliance (Atmanirbhar Bharat) in critical drone technologies

## Eligibility

Domestic manufacturers of drones and drone components who are registered in India and have commenced commercial production are eligible. Applicants must meet minimum investment thresholds and incremental sales criteria and commit to specific production targets as outlined in the scheme guidelines.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial incentives on incremental sales of eligible drone and drone component products manufactured in India. Non-financial benefits include facilitation of clearances, single-window support, and promotion of domestic value addition in the drone ecosystem.
