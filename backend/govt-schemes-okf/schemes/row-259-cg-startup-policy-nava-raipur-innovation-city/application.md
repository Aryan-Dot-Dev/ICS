## Process

1. Visit the official CG Startup Policy portal at https://cgstartup.in
2. Register as a startup on the portal by providing basic entity and promoter details
3. Fill in the online application form with details about the business model, innovation, and funding requirement
4. Upload required documents such as incorporation certificate, PAN, pitch deck, and bank details
5. Submit the application for review by the screening committee
6. Await evaluation and possible interview or pitch presentation
7. Receive approval and disbursement of funds or allocation of incubation space upon selection
