# CG Startup Policy & Nava Raipur Innovation City

## Overview

The CG Startup Policy & Nava Raipur Innovation City is a state-level initiative by the Government of Chhattisgarh aimed at fostering a vibrant startup ecosystem through financial support, infrastructure development, and policy incentives. It focuses on promoting innovation, entrepreneurship, and job creation within the state by providing startups with access to funding, incubation facilities, and regulatory ease. The policy is anchored around the development of Nava Raipur as an innovation hub to attract and nurture early-stage ventures.

## Objective

- Promote innovation and entrepreneurship across Chhattisgarh
- Develop Nava Raipur as a dedicated innovation city for startups
- Provide financial and infrastructural support to early-stage ventures
- Create employment opportunities through startup-led growth
- Establish a conducive regulatory environment for new businesses
- Encourage sector-specific innovation in areas like agri-tech, health-tech, and manufacturing
