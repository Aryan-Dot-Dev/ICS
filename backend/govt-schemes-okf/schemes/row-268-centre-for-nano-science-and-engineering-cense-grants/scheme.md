# Centre for Nano Science and Engineering (CeNSE) Grants

## Overview

The Centre for Nano Science and Engineering (CeNSE) at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc) was established in 2010 to pursue interdisciplinary research in nanoscale systems, including nanoelectronics, MEMS/NEMS, microfluidics, nanomaterials, photonics, nano-biotechnology, solar cells, quantum and neuromorphic computing, sensors, and optoelectronics. It offers M.Tech and PhD programs, hosts state-of-the-art nanofabrication and characterization facilities, and runs the in-house technology business incubator INCeNSE, which currently incubates 6 start-ups with access to infrastructure, labs, and mentoring support. CeNSE maintains close industry interactions and supports national initiatives like the India Semiconductor Mission through specialized training programs and industry collaborations.

## Objective

- Pursue interdisciplinary research across nanoscale systems
- Offer M.Tech and PhD programs in nanoscience and engineering
- Provide access to state-of-the-art nanofabrication and characterization facilities
- Support industry collaboration and technology transfer
- Incubate deep-tech startups through INCeNSE with infrastructure and mentoring
- Align with national missions such as the India Semiconductor Mission
- Deliver training programs in semiconductor technology for students and tribal communities
- Foster innovation in deep-tech domains including quantum technologies, neuromorphic computing, and nanorobotics

## Important Conditions

- Admission to M.Tech program is offered only for the July session
- PhD admissions are typically for July session, with limited January session for topic/field-specific admissions
- MTech Fellowship is exclusively for 2nd Year MTech students of CeNSE
- Selection for fellowships is based on application review and inputs from industry sponsors
- Internship stipend is ₹5,000 per month (as per Summer Internship Program), with no accommodation provided
- Interns must join before 31st May and complete at least 8 weeks
- Getting a Minor in M.Tech program is optional;
- degree will not mention any Minor if not pursued
