# Design Clinic Scheme for MSMEs

## Overview

The Design Clinic Scheme for MSMEs is a government initiative aimed at enhancing the competitiveness of micro, small, and medium enterprises by providing access to professional design services. It supports product development, innovation, and value addition through expert design interventions. The scheme focuses on improving aesthetics, functionality, and market readiness of products manufactured by MSMEs.

## Objective

- To provide professional design expertise to MSMEs for product development and innovation
- To enhance the competitiveness of MSME products in domestic and international markets
- To promote design awareness and its strategic importance among MSMEs
- To facilitate collaboration between MSMEs and professional designers or design institutes
- To improve product quality, aesthetics, functionality, and user experience
- To support MSMEs in adopting modern design practices for sustainable growth


## Target Beneficiaries

**MSME**

## Key Features

- Grant/assistance per entity: Up to INR 60,000 per MSME (based on enterprise category)
- Geographic scope: Pan-India (central-level)
- Deadline: Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds and empaneled design partners.
- Portal: https://designclinic.in
- Import confidence (source): medium


## Eligibility

Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) registered under the MSME Act, 2006 are eligible to apply for the Design Clinic Scheme. The enterprise must be engaged in manufacturing or production of goods and seek design intervention for product development or improvement.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

MSMEs receive subsidized access to professional design services, including product conceptualization, design development, prototyping, and testing. The scheme covers a significant portion of the design consultancy fees, reducing the financial burden on MSMEs. Beneficiaries gain improved product design, enhanced market appeal, and increased potential for commercialization and export.

## Important Conditions

- The subsidy is available only for design consultancy services provided by empaneled design institutes or professionals
- Travel, material, and prototyping costs are not covered under the scheme
- Each MSME can avail the benefit only once under this scheme
- The design project must be completed within the stipulated timeline as agreed in the work order
- Funds are limited and subject to availability;
- approval does not guarantee subsidy disbursement
