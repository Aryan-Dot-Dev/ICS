## Process

1. MSMEs must first register on the official Design Clinic Scheme portal at https://designclinic.in.
2. Submit an online application detailing the product requiring design intervention and the expected outcomes.
3. Upon preliminary screening, the application is forwarded to empaneled design institutes or professionals.
4. A design problem statement is formulated and agreed upon between the MSME and the design expert.
5. The design work is carried out as per the agreed scope and timelines.
6. Final designs and deliverables are submitted, and the subsidy is disbursed after verification and approval.

## Deadlines

- Rolling basis — applications are accepted throughout the year subject to availability of funds and empaneled design partners.
