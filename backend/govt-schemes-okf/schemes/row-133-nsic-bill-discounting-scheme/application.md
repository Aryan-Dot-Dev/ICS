## Process

1. The Entrepreneurs are required to apply for Bill Discounting only on the prescribed application forms.
2. The application forms downloaded from the link given below may be filled and can be submitted to the nearest Branch Office.
3. The blank forms are also available free of charge from the Branch offices.
4. Duly filled application form is to be submitted along with the Application.
5. Preliminary appraisal and Unit inspection is carried out by NSIC.
6. Sanction of Limit to the Unit.
7. Signing of agreement between NSIC and Unit.
8. Disbursement of assistance to the unit.
