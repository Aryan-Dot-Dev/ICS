# NSIC Bill Discounting Scheme

## Overview

The Bill Discounting Scheme by NSIC provides financial assistance to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) engaged in manufacturing or service activities by discounting bills arising from genuine trade transactions with reputed buyers such as Public Limited Companies, Government Departments, and Private Limited Companies. The scheme facilitates working capital financing against the security of Bank Guarantee, with bills having a maximum usance period of 180 days. It excludes traders and focuses on enabling MSMEs to improve cash flow and manage receivables efficiently.

## Objective

- Provide financial assistance to MSMEs by discounting bills from genuine trade transactions
- Facilitate working capital financing against Bank Guarantee security
- Support MSMEs in managing receivables and improving cash flow
- Enable MSMEs to avail credit up to 180 days usance period
- Offer concessional discounting rates for MSMEs with valid SME ratings
- Ensure timely repayment benefits through structured interest and penalty charges

## Important Conditions

- Traders are excluded from the scheme.
- The concessional rate of discounting charges for good rated units is applicable only if units make timely repayments of NSIC’s dues.
- Units which have not re-paid dues within usance period shall not be eligible for concessional rate and will be charged the normal rate.
- The concession continues till the validity of the rating awarded by rating agencies and ceases on expiry unless renewed.
- Processing fees apply on new sanctions and renewals as per Micro and Small & Medium categories.
