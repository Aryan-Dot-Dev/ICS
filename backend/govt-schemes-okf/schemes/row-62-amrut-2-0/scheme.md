# AMRUT 2.0

## Overview

AMRUT 2.0 (Atal Mission for Rejuvenation and Urban Transformation 2.0) is a centrally sponsored scheme launched by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) to provide universal coverage of water supply and sewerage/septage management in all statutory towns across India. The mission aims to make cities water-secure and improve the quality of life through sustainable urban infrastructure development, with a focus on sewage treatment, reuse of treated wastewater, and rejuvenation of water bodies.

## Objective

- Provide universal coverage of water supply to all households in statutory towns
- Ensure sewerage and septage management in 500 AMRUT cities
- Promote reuse of treated wastewater for non-potable purposes
- Rejuvenate water bodies and develop green spaces and parks
- Strengthen institutional and financial capacity of Urban Local Bodies
- Improve governance and service delivery through digital interventions


## Target Beneficiaries

**Urban Local Bodies (ULBs)**, **State Governments**, **Cantonment Boards**

## Eligibility

All statutory towns as per Census 2011, including cantonment boards, are eligible under AMRUT 2.0. The scheme covers 500 cities and towns with a population of over 1 lakh, along with all state/UT capitals and cities/towns situated on the banks of major rivers, irrespective of population.


Deterministic rules: [eligibility.md](eligibility.md).


## Benefits

Financial assistance for water supply, sewerage, septage management, water body rejuvenation, and park development; capacity building and reforms implementation; incentive for water reuse and non-revenue water reduction; technical support for project preparation and monitoring; promotion of public-private partnerships in urban infrastructure.

## Important Conditions

- Funding is contingent upon implementation of mandatory and incentive-based reforms
- Central assistance is released in installments subject to utilization of previous installments
- Projects must be completed within the mission period (2021-2026)
- Land acquisition and statutory clearances are the responsibility of the State/ULB
