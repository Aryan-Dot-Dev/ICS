## Process

1. States/UTs submit State Annual Action Plans (SAAPs) for approval by the Apex Committee under MoHUA.
2. Upon approval, projects are formulated by Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) or Parastatal agencies.
3. Detailed Project Reports (DPRs) are prepared and submitted for technical sanction.
4. Works are tendered and awarded as per financial rules.
5. Projects are executed and monitored through geo-tagging and third-party inspection.
6. Utilization certificates and progress reports are submitted for subsequent fund releases.

## Deadlines

- Annual planning cycle — States/UTs submit SAAPs yearly for approval; project implementation timelines vary as per DPR approval.
